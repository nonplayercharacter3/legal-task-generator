# PARENT GUARANTY

This Parent Guaranty (this "**Guaranty**") is made as of March 1, 2024 by **Trobur Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 220 Executive Drive, Suite 300, Newark, Delaware 19702 ("**Guarantor**"), in favor of **Furux Dental Technologies, Inc.**, a New Jersey corporation with its principal place of business at 410 Carnegie Center Boulevard, Princeton, New Jersey 08540 ("**Supplier**").

## RECITALS

A. Supplier and **Trobur Dental Distribution, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Distributor**"), are parties to an Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of March 1, 2024 (as amended, restated, supplemented or otherwise modified from time to time, the "**Distribution Agreement**"). Under the Distribution Agreement, Supplier has appointed Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products to Customers in the Territory.

B. Guarantor is the sole member of Distributor. Guarantor will derive substantial direct and indirect benefit from the Distribution Agreement.

C. Section 5.6 of the Distribution Agreement requires Distributor to cause Guarantor to deliver to Supplier, on the Effective Date, a guaranty of Distributor's payment obligations under the Distribution Agreement. Guarantor is delivering this Guaranty to satisfy that requirement.

Guarantor therefore agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Guaranty have the meanings given to them in the Distribution Agreement.

1.2 **Guaranteed Obligations.** "**Guaranteed Obligations**" means all amounts that Distributor owes to Supplier under the Distribution Agreement, whenever arising, when they become due and payable. This includes (a) the purchase price of Products ordered by Distributor and delivered by Supplier under Section 5.4 of the Distribution Agreement, (b) any shortfall amount invoiced under Section 5.3(a) of the Distribution Agreement, (c) interest on late payments under Section 5.5 of the Distribution Agreement, and (d) any taxes payable by Distributor under Section 5.7 of the Distribution Agreement. Guaranteed Obligations do not include any amount that Distributor in good faith disputes under Section 5.4 of the Distribution Agreement, until that dispute is resolved by agreement of Supplier and Distributor or by final, non-appealable judgment.

## 2. GUARANTY

2.1 **Guaranty of Payment.** Guarantor absolutely, unconditionally and irrevocably guarantees to Supplier, as primary obligor and not merely as surety, the full and punctual payment of the Guaranteed Obligations when due. This is a guaranty of payment and not of collection. This Guaranty does not extend to any obligation of Distributor under the Distribution Agreement other than a payment obligation.

2.2 **Maximum Liability.** Guarantor's maximum aggregate liability under this Guaranty shall not exceed $3,000,000, plus the costs of enforcement payable under Section 6.3.

2.3 **Continuing Guaranty.** This is a continuing guaranty. It applies to all Guaranteed Obligations arising from time to time during the Term, including any Renewal Term, and remains in full force and effect until all Guaranteed Obligations have been paid in full, subject to Section 7.

2.4 **Reinstatement.** If any payment of a Guaranteed Obligation is rescinded or must be returned by Supplier on the insolvency, bankruptcy or reorganization of Distributor or otherwise, this Guaranty shall continue to be effective or be reinstated, as the case may be, as to that payment, as though the payment had not been made.

## 3. DEMAND AND PAYMENT

3.1 **Demand.** If Distributor fails to pay any Guaranteed Obligation when due, and that failure continues for 15 days after Supplier gives written notice of non-payment to Distributor under Section 13.1 of the Distribution Agreement, Supplier may make written demand on Guarantor. The demand must identify the unpaid Guaranteed Obligation, attach copies of the relevant invoices, and state the amount demanded.

3.2 **Payment.** Guarantor shall pay the amount demanded within ten business days after receipt of a demand that complies with Section 3.1, in immediately available funds, to the account Supplier designates in the demand.

3.3 **No Set-Off.** Guarantor shall make all payments under this Guaranty without set-off, counterclaim or deduction, except for any defense of payment or any defense that Distributor would have to the underlying Guaranteed Obligation, other than defenses arising from Distributor's insolvency, bankruptcy or lack of capacity.

## 4. GUARANTOR'S OBLIGATIONS UNCONDITIONAL

4.1 **Obligations Not Affected.** Guarantor's obligations under this Guaranty are not released, discharged or otherwise affected by:

(a) any amendment, modification, extension or renewal of the Distribution Agreement, including any change in the Territory, the Products, prices or the Minimum Annual Purchase Commitment;

(b) any extension of time for payment, or any waiver, indulgence or forbearance granted by Supplier to Distributor;

(c) any change in the ownership, structure or existence of Distributor, or any insolvency, bankruptcy or reorganization of Distributor;

(d) any release of, or failure to perfect or enforce, any other guaranty or security for the Guaranteed Obligations; or

(e) any other circumstance that might otherwise constitute a legal or equitable discharge of a guarantor or surety, other than payment in full of the Guaranteed Obligations.

4.2 **Waivers.** Guarantor waives (a) notice of acceptance of this Guaranty, (b) presentment, protest and notice of dishonor, (c) notice of any amendment to the Distribution Agreement or of any extension of credit to Distributor, and (d) any requirement that Supplier first proceed against Distributor or any other Person, or exhaust any other remedy, before enforcing this Guaranty. Nothing in this Section 4.2 waives the demand required by Section 3.1.

## 5. SUBROGATION AND SUBORDINATION

5.1 **Subrogation.** Guarantor shall not exercise any right of subrogation, reimbursement, contribution or indemnity against Distributor in respect of any payment under this Guaranty until all Guaranteed Obligations then due have been paid in full.

5.2 **Subordination.** Any indebtedness of Distributor to Guarantor is subordinated to the Guaranteed Obligations then due and unpaid. If Distributor is in default in paying any Guaranteed Obligation, any amount Guarantor receives from Distributor on that indebtedness shall be held in trust for Supplier and paid to Supplier on demand, up to the amount of the unpaid Guaranteed Obligations.

## 6. REPRESENTATIONS AND COVENANTS

6.1 **Representations.** Guarantor represents and warrants to Supplier that:

(a) it is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware;

(b) it has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Guaranty, and this Guaranty has been duly authorized by all necessary corporate action;

(c) this Guaranty is a legal, valid and binding obligation of Guarantor, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws and general principles of equity; and

(d) the execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty do not violate its organizational documents, any law applicable to it, or any material agreement to which it is a party.

6.2 **Financial Information.** Within 120 days after the end of each fiscal year of Guarantor during the Term, Guarantor shall deliver to Supplier, on request, its annual consolidated balance sheet and statement of income for that fiscal year, certified by its chief financial officer. Supplier shall treat that information as Confidential Information of Distributor under Section 12 of the Distribution Agreement.

6.3 **Costs of Enforcement.** Guarantor shall pay Supplier's reasonable attorneys' fees and costs incurred in enforcing this Guaranty against Guarantor, if Supplier prevails in that enforcement.

## 7. TERMINATION

7.1 **Termination.** This Guaranty terminates on the later of (a) the expiration or termination of the Distribution Agreement and (b) the payment in full of all Guaranteed Obligations arising before that expiration or termination, subject to Section 2.4.

7.2 **Replacement Guaranty.** If Supplier accepts in writing a replacement guaranty of the Guaranteed Obligations from another Person, on terms reasonably satisfactory to Supplier, this Guaranty terminates as to Guaranteed Obligations arising after the effective date of that replacement guaranty. Guarantor remains liable for Guaranteed Obligations arising before that date.

## 8. GENERAL

8.1 **Governing Law.** This Guaranty is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

8.2 **Forum.** Guarantor irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Guaranty and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

8.3 **Jury Waiver.** GUARANTOR AND SUPPLIER EACH WAIVE ANY RIGHT TO A JURY TRIAL IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS GUARANTY.

8.4 **Notices.** Notices under this Guaranty must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation copy by courier. Notices to Guarantor go to the address above, Attention: Chief Executive Officer. Notices to Supplier go to its address for notices under Section 16.4 of the Distribution Agreement. Either party may designate another address by notice. Notices are effective on receipt.

8.5 **Assignment.** Supplier may assign this Guaranty only together with, and to the same Person as, an assignment of the Distribution Agreement permitted under Section 14 of the Distribution Agreement. Guarantor may not assign or delegate its obligations under this Guaranty without Supplier's prior written consent. This Guaranty binds Guarantor and its successors and benefits Supplier and its permitted successors and assigns.

8.6 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Guaranty may be amended or waived only by a written instrument signed by Guarantor and Supplier.

8.7 **Severability.** If any provision of this Guaranty is held unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted and the remaining provisions remain in effect.

8.8 **Counterparts.** This Guaranty may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together are one instrument.

8.9 **Headings.** Headings in this Guaranty are for convenience only.
