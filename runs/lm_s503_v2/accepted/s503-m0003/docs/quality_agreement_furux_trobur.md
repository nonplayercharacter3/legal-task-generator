# QUALITY AGREEMENT

This Quality Agreement (this "**Quality Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Furux Dental Technologies, Inc.**, a New Jersey corporation with its principal place of business at 410 Carnegie Center Boulevard, Princeton, New Jersey 08540 ("**Supplier**"), and **Trobur Dental Distribution, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal place of business at 220 Executive Drive, Suite 300, Newark, Delaware 19702 ("**Distributor**"). Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier and Distributor are parties to an Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of the Effective Date (as amended from time to time, the "**Distribution Agreement**"). Under the Distribution Agreement, Supplier has appointed Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products to Customers in the Territory.

B. The Products are medical devices regulated by the FDA. Section 2.3 of the Distribution Agreement contemplates that the Parties will enter into this Quality Agreement to set out their respective responsibilities for Quality Matters.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Quality Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Distribution Agreement. In this Quality Agreement, the following terms have the following meanings:

"**Adverse Event**" means any event that reasonably suggests that a Product may have caused or contributed to a death or serious injury, or that a Product has malfunctioned and the malfunction would be likely to cause or contribute to a death or serious injury if it were to recur, in each case within the meaning of 21 C.F.R. Part 803.

"**Applicable Regulations**" means the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act and the regulations of the FDA under it, including 21 C.F.R. Parts 7, 801, 803, 806, 807, 810 and 820, and any comparable state law applicable to the storage, handling, distribution or servicing of the Products in the Territory.

"**Complaint**" means any written, electronic or oral communication that alleges deficiencies related to the identity, quality, durability, reliability, safety, effectiveness or performance of a Product after it is released for distribution.

"**Device History Record**" means the record of the manufacturing history of each lot or serial number of a Product maintained by Supplier under Applicable Regulations.

"**Nonconforming Product**" means any Product that does not conform to its specifications, labeling or Applicable Regulations.

"**Quality Contact**" means the individual designated by each Party in Exhibit 1, as updated by notice under Section 13.2.

"**Recall**" means a removal or correction of a Product that is mandated by order of the FDA or another Governmental Authority, including any mandatory recall ordered under 21 C.F.R. Part 810 or any comparable order of a Governmental Authority.

"**Serious Complaint**" means a Complaint that involves, or may involve, an Adverse Event.

"**Traceability Records**" has the meaning given in Section 5.1.

1.2 **Application of Definitions.** The definitions in this Section 1 apply to all Quality Matters between the Parties.

1.3 **Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections and Exhibits are to sections of and exhibits to this Quality Agreement unless otherwise stated.

## 2. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

2.1 **Purpose.** This Quality Agreement allocates between the Parties the responsibilities for Quality Matters relating to the Products distributed by Distributor in the Territory, so that each Party can comply with Applicable Regulations.

2.2 **Scope.** This Quality Agreement applies to all Products listed in Exhibit A to the Distribution Agreement, as updated from time to time, and to all Product units sold or delivered by Distributor to Customers, including demonstration units.

2.3 **Responsibilities Matrix.** Exhibit 2 summarizes the allocation of key quality responsibilities between the Parties. Exhibit 2 is a summary only, and the operative provisions of this Quality Agreement control its interpretation.

## 3. REGULATORY RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1 **Supplier.** Supplier is the manufacturer of record and the specification developer for each Product. Supplier is responsible for:

(a) obtaining and maintaining all FDA clearances, establishment registrations and device listings for the Products;

(b) maintaining a quality management system that complies with 21 C.F.R. Part 820 and ISO 13485:2016;

(c) design control, manufacturing, inspection, testing and release of each Product, and maintenance of the Device History Record for each lot or serial number;

(d) all Product labeling, instructions for use and unique device identification under 21 C.F.R. Part 801 and Part 830;

(e) investigation of Complaints, filing of medical device reports with the FDA, and reports of corrections and removals under 21 C.F.R. Part 806; and

(f) communications with the FDA and other Governmental Authorities concerning the Products.

3.2 **Distributor.** Distributor is responsible for:

(a) storing, handling and transporting Products within its control in accordance with Section 4;

(b) maintaining the Traceability Records under Section 5;

(c) receiving, documenting and forwarding Complaints and Adverse Event information under Sections 6 and 7;

(d) cooperating with Supplier in any Recall under Section 8; and

(e) installing and servicing scanners and milling units in accordance with Supplier's written installation and service procedures, using only Supplier-approved spare parts.

3.3 **No Modification.** Distributor shall not modify, relabel, repackage or reprocess any Product, or remove or obscure any label, serial number or unique device identifier, except as directed in writing by Supplier.

3.4 **Regulatory Inspections.** Each Party shall notify the other within two business days after it receives notice of any inspection by a Governmental Authority that relates to the Products, and shall provide copies of any resulting inspection observations relating to the Products within five business days after receipt.

## 4. STORAGE, HANDLING AND DISTRIBUTION

4.1 **Storage Conditions.** Distributor shall store Products in clean, dry, secure areas in accordance with the storage conditions stated on the Product labeling. Zirconia milling blocks shall be stored in their original sealed packaging, protected from moisture and contamination, and shall not be distributed after the expiration date stated on the label.

4.2 **Stock Rotation.** Distributor shall distribute consumables on a first-expiry, first-out basis.

4.3 **Damaged Product.** Distributor shall segregate and quarantine any Product that is damaged in transit or storage, or whose packaging integrity is compromised, and shall not distribute it pending Supplier's disposition instructions under Section 9.

4.4 **Installation.** Distributor shall install intraoral scanners and chairside milling units only through personnel who have completed Supplier's training under Section 6.6 of the Distribution Agreement, and shall complete and retain Supplier's installation checklist for each unit.

4.5 **Software Updates.** Supplier shall notify Distributor of each software or firmware update for a Product that is required for safety or regulatory compliance. Distributor shall assist Customers in installing that update within the timeframe Supplier specifies.

## 5. TRACEABILITY

5.1 **Traceability Records.** For every Product unit it sells or delivers, Distributor shall record the Product name, catalog number, lot or serial number, unique device identifier, date of shipment, and the name and address of the receiving Customer (the "**Traceability Records**").

5.2 **Retrieval.** Distributor shall be able to retrieve and provide to Supplier the Traceability Records for any lot or serial number within 24 hours after Supplier's request in connection with a Recall or a Serious Complaint, and within five business days after any other request.

5.3 **Use.** Supplier shall use the Traceability Records solely for Quality Matters, warranty administration and compliance with Applicable Regulations, subject to Section 12 of the Distribution Agreement.

## 6. COMPLAINT HANDLING

6.1 **Receipt and Forwarding.** Distributor shall document each Complaint it receives using Supplier's complaint intake form and forward it to Supplier's Quality Contact:

(a) for a Serious Complaint, within 24 hours after Distributor becomes aware of it; and

(b) for any other Complaint, within two business days after Distributor becomes aware of it.

6.2 **Samples.** Where available, Distributor shall use reasonable efforts to obtain the affected Product, components or milled restoration from the Customer and send it to Supplier for evaluation, at Supplier's cost, in accordance with Supplier's returned-goods instructions.

6.3 **Investigation.** Supplier is solely responsible for investigating each Complaint and determining root cause and corrective action. Distributor shall provide reasonable assistance, including access to Customers where the Customer consents.

6.4 **Customer Communications.** Supplier shall provide Distributor with a written response for each Complaint that Distributor forwarded, within 30 days after receipt or within such other period as the investigation reasonably requires. Distributor shall not make any statement to a Customer about the cause of a Complaint other than as approved by Supplier.

## 7. ADVERSE EVENT REPORTING

7.1 **Supplier Reporting.** Supplier is solely responsible for determining whether an event is reportable under 21 C.F.R. Part 803 and for filing all required medical device reports.

7.2 **Distributor Cooperation.** Distributor shall provide Supplier with all information within its possession or control that Supplier reasonably requests to evaluate and report an Adverse Event, within the timeframes Supplier specifies to meet its reporting deadlines.

7.3 **Trend Information.** Supplier shall provide Distributor, on request and no more than quarterly, a summary of Complaint trends for the Products in the Territory.

## 8. RECALLS

8.1 **Notice.** Each Party shall notify the other's Quality Contact within 24 hours after it becomes aware of any order, request or communication from a Governmental Authority that may result in a Recall.

8.2 **Recall Strategy.** Supplier shall develop the recall strategy for each Recall, including the depth of the Recall, the content of Customer notifications and effectiveness checks, and shall provide that strategy to Distributor in writing.

8.3 **Distributor Actions.** In each Recall, Distributor shall, within the timeframes stated in Supplier's recall strategy:

(a) stop distribution and quarantine affected Products in its inventory;

(b) identify affected Customers from the Traceability Records;

(c) deliver Supplier-approved notifications to affected Customers and document receipt;

(d) retrieve, replace or correct affected Products at Customer locations as directed by Supplier; and

(e) provide Supplier with reconciliation and effectiveness-check information.

8.4 **Governmental Communications.** Supplier shall handle all communications with Governmental Authorities concerning any Recall. Distributor shall promptly refer to Supplier any inquiry it receives from a Governmental Authority concerning a Recall.

8.5 **Records.** Each Party shall keep complete records of its actions in each Recall for the period required by Section 10.1.

## 9. NONCONFORMING PRODUCT AND RETURNS

9.1 **Notice.** Each Party shall notify the other within five business days after it identifies any Nonconforming Product in Distributor's inventory or at a Customer location.

9.2 **Disposition.** Supplier shall provide written disposition instructions for Nonconforming Product within ten business days after notice. Distributor shall not return, scrap or rework any Nonconforming Product except in accordance with those instructions.

9.3 **Returned Goods.** Distributor shall obtain a return authorization number from Supplier before returning any Product. Returned Products shall not be returned to saleable inventory unless Supplier has inspected and released them.

## 10. RECORDS AND AUDITS

10.1 **Record Retention.** Each Party shall retain the records it is required to keep under this Quality Agreement for the longer of (a) the expected design and commercial life of the relevant Product, and (b) two years after the date the relevant Product was released for distribution, or any longer period required by Applicable Regulations.

10.2 **Audits by Supplier.** Supplier may audit Distributor's facilities, procedures and records relating to the storage, handling, distribution, installation, servicing and traceability of the Products, on at least 15 business days' prior written notice, during normal business hours, no more than once per Contract Year, except that Supplier may conduct an additional audit for cause following a Recall or Serious Complaint.

10.3 **Audit Findings.** Supplier shall provide Distributor with a written audit report within 30 days after the audit. Distributor shall respond with a corrective action plan within 30 days after receipt and implement it within the agreed timeframe.

10.4 **Supplier Certifications.** Supplier shall provide Distributor, on request, copies of its current ISO 13485 certificate and a summary of the results of any FDA inspection of its manufacturing facilities relating to the Products.

## 11. TERM AND CHANGES

11.1 **Term.** This Quality Agreement takes effect on the Effective Date and remains in effect for so long as the Distribution Agreement is in effect. Sections 5, 6, 7, 8 and 10 continue to apply after expiration or termination of the Distribution Agreement for so long as Distributor holds Product inventory or Product units sold by Distributor remain in use at Customer locations, and Section 12 survives.

11.2 **Changes Notification.** Supplier shall notify Distributor in writing at least 60 days before implementing any change to a Product's specifications, labeling, packaging or storage conditions that affects Distributor's activities under this Quality Agreement, or as soon as practicable for any change required by a Governmental Authority or for safety reasons.

11.3 **Review.** The Quality Contacts shall review this Quality Agreement at least once every two Contract Years and propose any changes needed to reflect changes in Applicable Regulations or the Products.

## 12. GOVERNING LAW AND FORUM

12.1 **Governing Law.** This Quality Agreement and all disputes concerning Quality Matters are governed by the laws of the State of New Jersey, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

12.2 **Forum.** Any action arising out of or relating to this Quality Agreement or any Quality Matter shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts of Mercer County, New Jersey. Each Party irrevocably submits to the jurisdiction of those courts for that purpose and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

## 13. GENERAL

13.1 **Quality Contacts.** Each Party shall designate a Quality Contact in Exhibit 1. Routine communications on Quality Matters may be given between the Quality Contacts by email.

13.2 **Notices.** Formal notices under this Quality Agreement shall be given in the manner and to the addresses provided in Section 16.4 of the Distribution Agreement, with a copy by email to the other Party's Quality Contact. A Party may change its Quality Contact by notice to the other Party.

13.3 **Amendment.** This Quality Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. Exhibit 1 may be updated by notice under Section 13.2.

13.4 **Assignment.** This Quality Agreement may be assigned only together with, and to the same Person as, an assignment of the Distribution Agreement permitted under Section 14 of the Distribution Agreement.

13.5 **Severability.** If any provision of this Quality Agreement is held unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted and the remaining provisions remain in effect.

13.6 **Counterparts.** This Quality Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together are one instrument.
