# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO EXCLUSIVE DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 to Exclusive Distribution Agreement (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of September 15, 2026 (the "**Amendment Date**") by and between **Furux Dental Technologies, Inc.**, a New Jersey corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Trobur Dental Distribution, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Distributor**"). Supplier and Distributor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier and Distributor are parties to the Exclusive Distribution Agreement dated as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Supplier appointed Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products to Customers in the Territory.

B. Distributor has advised Supplier that Trobur Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation and the sole member of Distributor ("**Holdings**"), entered into a Unit Purchase Agreement dated August 3, 2026 with Pellmont Dental Group, LLC ("**Pellmont**"). Under that Unit Purchase Agreement, Holdings has agreed to sell all of the outstanding limited liability company units of Distributor to Pellmont (the "**Pending Sale**"). As of the Amendment Date, the Pending Sale has not closed.

C. The Parties wish to expand the Territory and make certain other amendments to the Agreement on the terms of this Amendment.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND REFERENCES

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

1.2 **References.** On and after the Amendment Date, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement," "hereunder," "hereof" or words of similar import means the Agreement as amended by this Amendment. Each reference in the Quality Agreement to the Agreement or the "Distribution Agreement" means the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

## 2. EXPANSION OF TERRITORY

2.1 **Amended Exhibit B.** Exhibit B (Territory) to the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> **EXHIBIT B – TERRITORY**
>
> The State of New Jersey; the State of Delaware; the following counties of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania: Berks, Bucks, Chester, Delaware, Lancaster, Lehigh, Montgomery, Northampton and Philadelphia; the State of Maryland; the District of Columbia; and the following counties and independent cities of the Commonwealth of Virginia: Arlington County, Fairfax County, Loudoun County, Prince William County, the City of Alexandria, the City of Fairfax and the City of Falls Church.

2.2 **Additional Territory.** The areas that Section 2.1 adds to Exhibit B are referred to as the "**Additional Territory**." Distributor's appointment under Section 2.1 of the Agreement extends to the Additional Territory starting on October 1, 2026 (the "**Additional Territory Start Date**").

2.3 **Transition of Existing Accounts.** Within 15 business days after the Amendment Date, Supplier shall deliver to Distributor a list of each Customer in the Additional Territory that bought Products from Supplier or any other Supplier reseller during the 12 months before the Amendment Date. For each such Customer, the list shall show the installed base of intraoral scanners and chairside milling units by serial number and the warranty expiration date. Supplier shall terminate or amend, effective no later than the Additional Territory Start Date, any reseller arrangement that would otherwise permit sales of Products to Customers in the Additional Territory. Supplier may fill purchase orders it accepted before the Additional Territory Start Date from Customers in the Additional Territory, provided that all such orders ship no later than November 30, 2026.

2.4 **Warranty Service in Additional Territory.** For Products installed in the Additional Territory before the Additional Territory Start Date, Distributor shall perform warranty service at Supplier's request, and Supplier shall reimburse Distributor for that service under Section 9.3 of and Exhibit D to the Agreement.

2.5 **National Accounts.** Section 2.4 of and Exhibit C to the Agreement apply to the Additional Territory in the same manner as to the rest of the Territory.

2.6 **Demonstration Units.** For the Contract Year beginning March 1, 2026 only, the limit on demonstration units in Section 6.5 of the Agreement is increased from four to six units of each model.

## 3. PRODUCT CLAIMS INDEMNITY

3.1 **Section 10.1(b).** Section 10.1(b) of the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and restated as new Section 9.6 of the Agreement, which reads as follows:

> **9.6 Product Claims Indemnity.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Distributor, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, managers, employees and agents from and against all Losses arising out of any Product Claim, including any claim of injury to a patient treated with a restoration milled from a Product or using data captured by a Product, except to the extent caused by Distributor's negligence or its modification of the Product. The procedures in Section 10.3 apply to any claim for which indemnification is sought under this Section 9.6.

3.2 **Conforming Change to Section 10.1.** Section 10.1 of the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> **10.1 By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Distributor, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, managers, employees and agents from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, judgments, settlements, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) ("**Losses**") arising out of any third-party claim to the extent arising from any allegation that a Product, or Distributor's sale of a Product in accordance with this Agreement, infringes or misappropriates any patent, copyright, trademark or trade secret of a third party.

3.3 **Section 9.5.** In Section 9.5 of the Agreement, the words "THIS SECTION 9" are replaced with "SECTIONS 9.1 THROUGH 9.4".

## 4. NOTICES

4.1 **Supplier Notice Address.** For purposes of Section 16.4 of the Agreement, Supplier's address for notices is changed to: Furux Dental Technologies, Inc., 600 College Road East, Suite 2100, Princeton, New Jersey 08540, Attention: General Counsel.

## 5. EFFECT OF AMENDMENT

5.1 **Agreement Continues.** Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect. The Parties ratify and confirm the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

5.2 **No Consent or Waiver.** This Amendment, including its Recitals, does not constitute a consent or waiver under any provision of the Agreement or the Quality Agreement. Nothing in this Amendment waives or limits any right or remedy of either Party under the Agreement, the Quality Agreement or applicable law, whether arising before or after the Amendment Date.

5.3 **Quality Agreement.** Except as provided in Section 1.2, this Amendment does not amend the Quality Agreement.

## 6. MISCELLANEOUS

6.1 **Representations.** Each Party represents to the other that it has full power and authority to enter into this Amendment, and that this Amendment has been duly authorized, executed and delivered by that Party and is a valid and binding obligation of that Party.

6.2 **Governing Law and Forum.** Sections 15.1 through 15.4 of the Agreement apply to this Amendment as if set out in full in this Amendment.

6.3 **Counterparts.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together are one instrument.

6.4 **Headings.** Headings in this Amendment are for convenience only.
