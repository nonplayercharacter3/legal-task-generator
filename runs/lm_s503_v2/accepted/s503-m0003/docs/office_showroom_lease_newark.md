## 2. PREMISES AND COMMON AREAS

2.1 **Lease of Premises.** Landlord leases the Premises to Tenant, and Tenant leases the Premises from Landlord, for the Term and on the terms of this Lease.

2.2 **Common Areas.** Tenant has the non-exclusive right, together with other tenants of the Building, to use the lobbies, corridors, elevators, restrooms, loading dock, parking areas and other areas of the Building and its land that Landlord designates for common use (the "**Common Areas**"), subject to the Building Rules and Regulations in Exhibit C.

2.3 **Parking.** Tenant may use up to 33 unreserved parking spaces in the Building's surface lot at no additional charge during the Term, including 6 spaces designated by Landlord for showroom visitors.

2.4 **Loading Dock.** Tenant may use the Building's shared loading dock and freight elevator for deliveries of equipment and supplies during the hours set out in Exhibit C. Deliveries outside those hours require 24 hours' prior notice to the Building manager.

## 3. TERM

3.1 **Term.** The term of this Lease begins on the Commencement Date and ends on the Expiration Date (the "**Term**"), unless terminated earlier or extended under this Lease.

3.2 **Landlord's Work.** Before the Commencement Date, Landlord shall complete the improvements described in Exhibit B ("**Landlord's Work**") in a good and workmanlike manner and in compliance with applicable law. Landlord's Work includes the showroom build-out, demonstration operatory rough-ins (dedicated electrical circuits, compressed air and water supply and drain lines) and the enclosed equipment room described in Exhibit B. If Landlord's Work is not substantially complete by the Commencement Date, Base Rent and Additional Rent abate one day for each day of delay.

3.3 **Option to Extend.** Tenant has one option to extend the Term for five years (the "**Extension Term**"), on the terms of this Lease, except that Base Rent for the first year of the Extension Term will be 95% of the Fair Market Rent, increasing 3% annually. Tenant may exercise the option by written notice to Landlord given no earlier than 15 months and no later than 9 months before the Expiration Date, provided Tenant is not then in default beyond any applicable cure period. "**Fair Market Rent**" means the annual base rent per rentable square foot then being agreed for comparable office and showroom space in comparable buildings in the Newark, Delaware area, for a comparable term, taking into account all relevant concessions. If the parties do not agree on Fair Market Rent within 30 days after Tenant's exercise notice, it will be determined by a panel of three MAI-designated appraisers in the customary manner, with each party bearing the cost of its own appraiser and half the cost of the third.

3.4 **Holding Over.** If Tenant remains in possession after the end of the Term without Landlord's written consent, Tenant is a tenant at sufferance, and Base Rent for the holdover period is 150% of the Base Rent in effect at the end of the Term, prorated on a daily basis.

## 4. RENT

| March 1, 2026 – February 28, 2027 | $30.24 | $297,864.00 | $24,822.00 |

4.2 **Abated Rent.** Base Rent for the months of March and April 2024 is abated in full. Tenant's obligations to pay Additional Rent are not abated during that period.

4.3 **Additional Rent.** Starting on the Commencement Date, Tenant shall pay, as additional rent, Tenant's Share of the amount by which Operating Expenses and Taxes for each calendar year exceed Operating Expenses and Taxes for calendar year 2024 (the "**Base Year**") ("**Additional Rent**"). Base Rent and Additional Rent are together "**Rent**."

4.4 **Operating Expenses.** "**Operating Expenses**" means the reasonable costs Landlord incurs to operate, maintain, repair, insure and manage the Building and Common Areas, determined in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles consistently applied. Operating Expenses exclude capital expenditures (other than the annual amortized portion of capital improvements that reduce Operating Expenses or are required by laws first effective after the Lease Date), debt service, leasing commissions, costs of tenant improvements for other tenants, costs reimbursed by insurance or other tenants, Landlord's income and franchise taxes, and management fees exceeding 3% of gross Building revenues. Controllable Operating Expenses (excluding insurance, utilities, snow removal and Taxes) used to calculate Additional Rent will not increase by more than 5% per year on a non-cumulative basis over the Base Year.

4.5 **Taxes.** "**Taxes**" means real estate taxes and assessments levied on the Building and its land, but not Landlord's income, franchise, estate or transfer taxes.

4.6 **Estimates and Reconciliation.** Landlord may estimate Additional Rent for each calendar year and Tenant shall pay one-twelfth of the estimate monthly with Base Rent. Within 120 days after the end of each calendar year, Landlord shall deliver a statement of actual Operating Expenses and Taxes for that year. Any overpayment will be credited against the next Rent due (or refunded if the Term has ended), and Tenant shall pay any underpayment within 30 days after receipt of the statement.

4.7 **Audit.** Tenant may, within 180 days after receiving an annual statement, inspect Landlord's records relating to that statement at Landlord's office during normal business hours on 15 days' notice. If the inspection shows that Landlord overstated Tenant's Share of Operating Expenses and Taxes by more than 4%, Landlord shall pay the reasonable cost of the inspection.

4.8 **Late Charge.** If any Rent is not paid within five business days after it is due, Tenant shall pay a late charge of 4% of the overdue amount. Landlord will waive the first late charge in any 12-month period if Tenant pays within five business days after written notice.

## 5. SECURITY DEPOSIT

5.1 **Deposit.** On signing this Lease, Tenant shall deposit the Security Deposit with Landlord as security for Tenant's performance. Landlord may apply the Security Deposit to cure any default by Tenant that continues beyond applicable notice and cure periods, and Tenant shall restore any amount so applied within 15 days after written demand.

5.2 **Return.** Landlord shall return any unapplied balance of the Security Deposit to Tenant within 30 days after the end of the Term and Tenant's surrender of the Premises in the condition required by Section 8.4.

5.3 **Transfer.** On a sale of the Building, Landlord shall transfer the Security Deposit to the purchaser, and Landlord is then released from liability for its return.

## 6. USE

6.1 **Permitted Use.** Tenant may use the Premises for general office purposes, for a showroom and training center for the display and demonstration of dental equipment and consumables (including intraoral scanners and chairside milling units), for the storage of demonstration units, spare parts and limited quantities of consumables incidental to those uses, and for any other lawful use consistent with a first-class suburban office building (the "**Permitted Use**"). Tenant shall not use the Premises for the treatment of patients.

6.2 **Demonstration Equipment.** Tenant may operate chairside milling units and related equipment in the Premises for demonstration and training, provided that milling equipment is operated only in the showroom and the enclosed equipment room described in Exhibit B, with the dust collection and filtration supplied by the equipment manufacturer, and that Tenant disposes of milling waste, coolant and dust in accordance with applicable law and the manufacturer's instructions.

6.3 **Compliance with Laws.** Tenant shall comply with all laws applicable to its particular use of the Premises. Landlord shall comply with all laws applicable to the Building structure, systems and Common Areas, including the Americans with Disabilities Act, and the cost of that compliance is included in Operating Expenses only to the extent permitted by Section 4.4.

6.4 **Hazardous Materials.** Tenant shall not bring any hazardous materials onto the Premises, except ordinary office and cleaning supplies and the consumables, coolants and lubricants customarily used in demonstrating dental equipment, in each case in quantities customary for the Permitted Use and stored, used and disposed of in compliance with applicable law. Tenant shall indemnify Landlord against Losses arising from any hazardous materials that Tenant or its agents bring onto the Building. Landlord represents that, to Landlord's knowledge, no hazardous materials are present in the Premises in violation of applicable law on the Lease Date, and Landlord remains responsible for any hazardous materials present in the Building before the Commencement Date.

## 7. SERVICES AND UTILITIES

7.1 **Building Services.** Landlord shall provide heating, ventilation and air conditioning to the Premises from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. on business days and 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. on Saturdays (excluding holidays listed in Exhibit C), janitorial service five nights per week, elevator service 24 hours per day, and water for restroom, kitchenette and demonstration operatory use. Tenant may obtain after-hours HVAC at Landlord's actual cost, currently $55 per hour per zone.

7.2 **Electricity.** Electricity to the Premises is separately metered. Tenant shall pay the utility provider directly for all electricity consumed in the Premises.

7.3 **Interruption.** If any interruption of Building services caused by Landlord's negligence or failure to make required repairs makes all or a material part of the Premises untenantable for more than three consecutive business days, Rent abates in proportion to the untenantable area from the fourth business day until the services are restored.

## 8. MAINTENANCE, ALTERATIONS AND SURRENDER

8.1 **Landlord's Obligations.** Landlord shall maintain and repair, in good condition, the roof, foundation, structural elements, exterior walls and windows of the Building, the Common Areas, and the Building's mechanical, electrical, plumbing, life safety and HVAC systems up to the point of connection with the Premises.

8.2 **Tenant's Obligations.** Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good condition, ordinary wear and tear and casualty excepted, and shall repair any damage to the Building caused by Tenant or its agents, employees or invitees.

8.3 **Alterations.** Tenant may not make alterations to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay. Consent is not required for cosmetic alterations, such as painting, carpeting and installation of furniture, signage within the Premises and demonstration equipment, that cost less than $25,000 in any single project and do not affect the Building structure or systems.

8.4 **Surrender.** At the end of the Term, Tenant shall surrender the Premises broom clean and in good condition, ordinary wear and tear and casualty excepted, and shall remove its demonstration equipment, furniture and other personal property. Tenant is not required to remove Landlord's Work or any alteration that Landlord approved unless Landlord stated, when granting its approval, that removal would be required.

## 9. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

9.1 **Consent Required.** Except as provided in Section 9.2, Tenant may not assign this Lease or sublet all or any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay. Landlord shall respond to any request for consent within 15 business days after receiving the request and reasonable information about the proposed assignee or subtenant.

9.2 **Permitted Transfers.** Tenant may, without Landlord's consent but on at least 10 days' prior written notice to Landlord (or, where prior notice is prohibited by confidentiality obligations, notice within 10 days after the transfer), (a) assign this Lease or sublet all or part of the Premises to an entity that controls, is controlled by or is under common control with Tenant, (b) assign this Lease to a successor by merger, consolidation or acquisition of all or substantially all of Tenant's assets, or (c) permit any sale or transfer of the equity interests in Tenant, including a transfer of all of those interests (each, a "**Permitted Transfer**"). In the case of clause (b), the successor must assume Tenant's obligations under this Lease in writing. A Permitted Transfer is not an assignment requiring consent under Section 9.1.

9.3 **No Release.** No assignment or sublease releases Tenant from its obligations under this Lease, except that on a Permitted Transfer under Section 9.2(c), the entity named as Tenant in this Lease remains the Tenant and no assignment occurs.

## 10. INSURANCE AND INDEMNITY

10.1 **Tenant's Insurance.** During the Term, Tenant shall maintain, at its expense:

(a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate, naming Landlord and its managing agent as additional insureds;

(b) umbrella or excess liability insurance with limits of at least $3,000,000;

(c) property insurance covering Tenant's furniture, demonstration equipment, inventory and alterations for their full replacement cost; and

(d) workers' compensation insurance as required by law and employer's liability insurance with limits of at least $1,000,000.

Tenant shall deliver a certificate of insurance evidencing that coverage on or before the Commencement Date and on each renewal.

10.2 **Landlord's Insurance.** Landlord shall maintain all-risk property insurance on the Building for its full replacement cost and commercial general liability insurance for the Common Areas with limits customary for comparable buildings.

10.3 **Waiver of Subrogation.** Each party waives, and shall cause its property insurers to waive, all rights of recovery against the other party for loss or damage to its property to the extent covered (or required by this Lease to be covered) by property insurance.

10.4 **Mutual Indemnity.** Subject to Section 10.3, Tenant shall indemnify Landlord against all claims, damages, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees ("**Losses**"), arising from bodily injury or property damage occurring in the Premises or caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its agents, employees or invitees, and Landlord shall indemnify Tenant against all Losses arising from bodily injury or property damage occurring in the Common Areas or caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord or its agents, employees or contractors, in each case except to the extent caused by the indemnified party's negligence or willful misconduct.

## 11. CASUALTY AND CONDEMNATION

11.1 **Casualty.** If the Premises or Building access is damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord shall restore the Building structure, systems, Common Areas and Landlord's Work with reasonable diligence, and Rent abates in proportion to the area of the Premises rendered unusable until restoration is complete. If Landlord's architect reasonably estimates that restoration will take more than 180 days after the casualty, either party may terminate this Lease by written notice given within 30 days after receiving that estimate.

11.2 **Condemnation.** If all of the Premises, or so much of the Premises or the parking areas that Tenant cannot reasonably continue the Permitted Use, is taken by eminent domain, either party may terminate this Lease as of the date of taking. Landlord is entitled to the award for the taking, except that Tenant may pursue a separate claim for its moving expenses and its trade fixtures.

## 12. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

12.1 **Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**": (a) Tenant fails to pay Rent within ten days after written notice that it is past due; (b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation under this Lease within 30 days after written notice, or, if the failure cannot reasonably be cured in 30 days, Tenant fails to begin the cure within 30 days and diligently complete it; or (c) Tenant becomes the subject of a bankruptcy proceeding not dismissed within 90 days.

12.2 **Remedies.** On an Event of Default, Landlord may terminate this Lease by written notice and recover possession of the Premises by lawful process, and may recover from Tenant (a) unpaid Rent through the date of termination, and (b) the amount by which the Rent for the remainder of the Term exceeds the fair rental value of the Premises for that period, discounted to present value at 5% per year. Landlord shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate its damages.

12.3 **Landlord Default.** If Landlord fails to perform any of its obligations within 30 days after written notice from Tenant (or such shorter period as is reasonable in an emergency), Tenant may pursue all remedies available at law or in equity.

## 13. SUBORDINATION AND ESTOPPEL

13.1 **Subordination.** This Lease is subordinate to any mortgage now or later encumbering the Building, provided that the mortgage holder agrees in a commercially reasonable subordination, non-disturbance and attornment agreement not to disturb Tenant's possession so long as no Event of Default exists. Landlord shall deliver such an agreement from the current mortgage holder within 60 days after the Lease Date.

13.2 **Estoppel Certificates.** Each party shall, within 15 business days after the other's request, deliver a certificate confirming whether this Lease is in effect, the dates to which Rent has been paid, and whether, to its knowledge, any default exists, together with any other factual matters reasonably requested by the requesting party, a lender or a prospective purchaser of the Building or of Tenant.

## 14. QUIET ENJOYMENT; ACCESS

14.1 **Quiet Enjoyment.** So long as no Event of Default exists, Tenant shall peaceably and quietly enjoy the Premises during the Term without interference from Landlord or anyone claiming through Landlord.

14.2 **Access.** Landlord may enter the Premises on 24 hours' notice (or without notice in an emergency) to inspect, make repairs or show the Premises to lenders and purchasers and, during the last nine months of the Term, to prospective tenants. Landlord shall use reasonable efforts to minimize interference with Tenant's business, including showroom demonstrations and training sessions.

## 15. SIGNAGE

15.1 **Signage.** Tenant may, at its expense, install building-standard suite entry signage and a listing on the Building directory, and one panel on the Building's monument sign facing Executive Drive, in each case in accordance with Landlord's sign criteria and applicable law.

## 16. GENERAL

16.1 **Notices.** Notices under this Lease must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the parties' notice addresses in Section 1.1, or to another address a party designates by notice. Notices are effective on receipt or refusal of delivery.

16.2 **Brokers.** Each party represents that it has dealt with no broker in connection with this Lease other than Brandywine Commercial Realty Advisors, whose commission Landlord shall pay under a separate agreement. Each party shall indemnify the other against claims by any other broker claiming through it.

16.3 **Governing Law and Forum.** This Lease is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware. Any action arising out of or relating to this Lease shall be brought in the state or federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware.

16.4 **Limitation of Landlord Liability.** Landlord's liability under this Lease is limited to its interest in the Building, including rents, insurance proceeds and sale proceeds.

16.5 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Lease may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Landlord and Tenant. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

16.6 **Severability.** If any provision of this Lease is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect.

16.7 **Successors.** This Lease binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

16.8 **Counterparts.** This Lease may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
