# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**Timais Outdoor Co. / Pesith Apparel Manufacturing, LLC**

This Master Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**" or the "**MSA**") is entered into as of March 1, 2026 (the "**MSA Effective Date**") by and between **Timais Outdoor Co.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"), and **Pesith Apparel Manufacturing, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**"). Customer and Provider are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## Recitals

A. Customer designs, markets and sells outdoor apparel, including technical waterproof shell jackets and insulated layers.

B. Provider is in the business of contract apparel manufacturing and direct-to-consumer order fulfillment.

C. Customer wishes to engage Provider to manufacture certain Products and to perform Fulfillment Services, and Provider wishes to provide them, in each case on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings given below. Other terms are defined where they first appear.

1.1 "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

1.2 "**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 9.1.

1.3 "**Contract Year**" means each consecutive twelve (12) month period beginning on the MSA Effective Date or on an anniversary of the MSA Effective Date.

1.4 "**DPA**" means the Data Processing Addendum between the Parties dated as of the MSA Effective Date, as amended from time to time.

1.5 "**Fees**" means the amounts payable by Customer for Products and Services under Schedule B, the applicable Purchase Order and any Statement of Work.

1.6 "**Fulfillment Services**" means the receipt, storage, pick, pack, labeling, shipping, returns processing and related services Provider performs for orders placed by Customer's end consumers, as further described in Schedule C and any Statement of Work.

1.7 "**Customer Materials**" means designs, patterns, tech packs, artwork, trademarks, trims, fabrics and other materials Customer furnishes or specifies, including any supplier-nominated materials.

1.8 "**Personal Data**" has the meaning given in the DPA.

1.9 "**Products**" means the finished goods listed in Schedule A, including technical waterproof shell jackets and insulated layers, and any other goods added to Schedule A by written amendment or Statement of Work.

1.10 "**Purchase Order**" means a written or electronic order for Products issued by Customer under Section 4.

1.11 "**Quality Agreement**" means the Quality Agreement between the Parties dated as of March 1, 2026, as amended from time to time.

1.12 "**Services**" means the manufacturing services and the Fulfillment Services, together with any other services described in a Statement of Work.

1.13 "**Specifications**" means the specifications for each Product set out in or referenced by Schedule A, including the materials, construction, dimensional, labeling and performance requirements stated there, and the applicable tech pack approved in writing by Customer.

1.14 "**Statement of Work**" or "**SOW**" means a document signed by both Parties that describes Services, deliverables, service levels or pricing under this Agreement.

1.15 "**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Term, as described in Section 14.1.

## 2. Structure of Agreement

2.1 **Framework.** This Agreement sets out the terms on which Provider will manufacture Products and perform Services. Each Purchase Order and Statement of Work is governed by this Agreement.

2.2 **Related Documents.** The Quality Agreement and the DPA are incorporated into this Agreement by reference. Schedules A (Products and Specifications), B (Pricing), and C (Fulfillment Services) form part of this Agreement.

2.3 **Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict or inconsistency among the documents forming this Agreement, the following order of precedence applies: the DPA (as to Personal Data), then the MSA, then the Quality Agreement, then Statements of Work. Pre-printed terms on any Purchase Order, acknowledgment, invoice or similar form have no effect.

## 3. Services

3.1 **Manufacturing.** Provider shall manufacture the Products in accordance with the Specifications, the Quality Agreement, applicable law and each accepted Purchase Order.

3.2 **Fulfillment.** Provider shall perform the Fulfillment Services at its fulfillment center located at the address stated in Schedule C, or at another facility approved in advance in writing by Customer.

3.3 **Personnel.** Provider shall assign qualified and appropriately trained personnel to perform the Services. Provider may replace personnel on reasonable notice, provided that service levels are maintained.

3.4 **Subcontracting.** Provider shall not subcontract the manufacture of any Product, or any material part of the Fulfillment Services, without Customer's prior written consent. Provider remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors as for its own.

3.5 **Facility Standards.** Provider shall comply with Customer's Supplier Code of Conduct as provided to Provider from time to time, and shall permit Customer or its designee to audit Provider's facilities on reasonable notice during normal business hours.

## 4. Forecasts, Orders and Volume Commitments

4.1 **Forecasts.** Customer shall provide Provider a non-binding rolling twelve (12) month forecast of Product requirements on or before the first business day of each calendar quarter.

4.2 **Purchase Orders.** Customer shall issue Purchase Orders stating the Product, style, colorway, size run, quantity, requested delivery date and delivery location. Provider shall accept or reject each Purchase Order within five (5) business days of receipt. A Purchase Order not rejected within that period is deemed accepted.

4.3 **Lead Times.** Unless otherwise agreed in a Purchase Order, standard production lead time is one hundred twenty (120) days from acceptance of the Purchase Order to delivery.

4.4 **Minimum Volume Commitments.** In each Contract Year during the Term, Customer shall issue Purchase Orders for not fewer than the minimum annual unit volumes for each Product category stated in Schedule B (the "**Minimum Volume Commitment**"). If Customer's accepted Purchase Orders in a Contract Year fall below the Minimum Volume Commitment, Customer shall pay Provider, within forty-five (45) days after the end of that Contract Year, a shortfall fee equal to the shortfall in units multiplied by the per-unit shortfall rate stated in Schedule B.

4.5 **Changes.** Customer may change the delivery date, delivery location or size run of a Purchase Order by written notice at least sixty (60) days before the scheduled ship date. The Parties shall negotiate in good faith any equitable adjustment to price or schedule reasonably required as a result.

## 5. Delivery, Inspection and Title

5.1 **Delivery.** Provider shall deliver Products FCA Provider's facility (Incoterms 2020) to the carrier nominated by Customer, or, for Products held for Fulfillment Services, shall transfer them into fulfillment inventory as stated in Schedule C.

5.2 **Inspection.** Customer may inspect Products and reject them in accordance with the inspection and rejection procedures of the Quality Agreement, including within the inspection and rejection period stated in Section 5.2 of the Quality Agreement.

5.3 **Title and Risk.** Title to and risk of loss of Products pass to Customer on delivery under Section 5.1. Products held by Provider in fulfillment inventory remain Customer's property, and Provider bears risk of loss for those Products while in its care, custody or control.

5.4 **Late Delivery.** If Provider fails to deliver any Products within ten (10) days after the agreed delivery date for reasons not attributable to Customer, Provider shall, at Customer's option, ship the Products by expedited freight at Provider's cost or accept cancellation of the late portion of the Purchase Order without liability to Customer.

## 6. Warranties and Remedies

6.1 **Product Warranty.** Provider warrants to Customer that, for a period of twenty-four (24) months after delivery, each Product will (a) conform to the Specifications, (b) be free from defects in materials and workmanship, (c) be manufactured in accordance with the Quality Agreement and applicable law, and (d) be delivered free of liens and encumbrances.

6.2 **Replacement and Credit.** If any Product is Nonconforming Product (as defined in the Quality Agreement), Provider shall, at Customer's election, (a) replace the Nonconforming Product at Provider's cost, including inbound and outbound freight, within thirty (30) days, or (b) issue Customer a credit equal to the Fees paid for the Nonconforming Product together with any associated freight and handling costs. The remedies in this Section 6.2 are Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for Nonconforming Product.

6.3 **Services Warranty.** Provider warrants that it will perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards and the service levels set out in Schedule C and any Statement of Work. If Provider breaches this warranty, Provider shall re-perform the affected Services at no additional charge and shall pay any service level credits stated in Schedule C.

6.4 **Mutual Warranties.** Each Party warrants that it is duly organized and in good standing, has full authority to enter into and perform this Agreement, and that its performance will not breach any agreement with a third party.

6.5 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET OUT IN THIS AGREEMENT, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 7. Fees and Payment

7.1 **Fees.** Customer shall pay the Fees set out in Schedule B, the applicable Purchase Order and any Statement of Work. Fees are exclusive of applicable sales, use, value-added and similar taxes, which Customer shall pay other than taxes on Provider's income.

7.2 **Invoicing.** Provider shall invoice Products on shipment and Fulfillment Services monthly in arrears. Customer shall pay undisputed amounts within forty-five (45) days after receipt of a correct invoice.

7.3 **Disputed Amounts.** Customer may withhold any amount disputed in good faith by written notice stating the basis of the dispute. The Parties shall work diligently to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days.

7.4 **Price Adjustments.** Prices in Schedule B are fixed for the first Contract Year. Thereafter, Provider may propose price adjustments supported by documented changes in raw material or labor costs no more than once per Contract Year, on at least ninety (90) days' written notice. Adjustments take effect only on Customer's written approval, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

## 8. Intellectual Property

8.1 **Customer Property.** Customer retains all right, title and interest in Customer Materials, Customer's trademarks and the designs of the Products. Provider shall use them solely to perform this Agreement.

8.2 **Provider Property.** Each Party retains ownership of its pre-existing materials, processes and know-how. Provider retains ownership of its general manufacturing techniques and fulfillment systems.

8.3 **Deliverables.** All patterns, samples, molds, screens, tooling and other items created specifically for Customer's Products ("**Deliverables**") become Customer's property on payment of the applicable Fees. Provider shall return or destroy Deliverables as Customer directs on expiry or termination.

8.4 **No Unauthorized Production.** Provider shall not manufacture, sell or otherwise dispose of any Products, overruns, seconds or goods bearing Customer's trademarks except to Customer or as Customer directs in writing.

## 9. Confidentiality

9.1 **Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential, including Specifications, pricing, forecasts and product roadmaps.

9.2 **Obligations.** The receiving Party shall protect the disclosing Party's Confidential Information with at least reasonable care, use it only to perform or receive the benefit of this Agreement, and disclose it only to its personnel, Affiliates and advisers who need to know it and are bound by obligations of confidence no less protective.

9.3 **Exceptions.** Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes public without fault of the receiving Party, was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure, is independently developed, or is received from a third party without restriction. A Party may disclose Confidential Information as required by law, after giving the other Party prompt notice where lawful.

9.4 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 9 continue for five (5) years after expiry or termination, and indefinitely for trade secrets.

## 10. Data Protection

10.1 **Processing.** In performing the Fulfillment Services, Provider will process Personal Data of Customer's end consumers, including names, shipping addresses, contact details and order information. The Parties' respective obligations concerning that processing are set out in the DPA.

10.2 **Compliance.** Each Party shall comply with its obligations under the DPA and applicable data protection laws.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

11.1 **Cap.** Except as provided in Section 11.3, each Party's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not exceed the fees paid or payable in the 12 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

11.2 **Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** Except as provided in Section 11.3, neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits or lost revenue, even if advised of their possibility.

11.3 **Exceptions.** Sections 11.1 and 11.2 do not apply to (a) a Party's obligations under Section 13 (Indemnification), (b) a Party's breach of Section 9 (Confidentiality), (c) a Party's gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud, or (d) Customer's obligation to pay Fees and shortfall fees.

## 12. Insurance

12.1 **Required Coverage.** During the Term and for two (2) years thereafter, Provider shall maintain, with insurers rated A- VII or better by A.M. Best:

(a) commercial general liability insurance, including products-completed operations coverage, with limits of not less than $5,000,000 per occurrence;

(b) property insurance covering Customer's goods in Provider's care, custody or control at full replacement value;

(c) cyber liability and network security insurance with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per claim;

(d) workers' compensation insurance as required by law and employer's liability insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000 per accident; and

(e) commercial automobile liability insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000 combined single limit.

12.2 **Certificates.** Provider shall name Customer as an additional insured on the policy in Section 12.1(a), and shall deliver certificates of insurance to Customer before the first shipment of Products and on each renewal of coverage. Provider shall give Customer at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of cancellation or material reduction of any required coverage.

## 13. Indemnification

13.1 **By Provider.** Provider shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all third-party claims, and resulting losses, damages, costs and reasonable attorneys' fees, to the extent arising from (a) bodily injury, death or property damage caused by a manufacturing defect in a Product, (b) Provider's negligence or willful misconduct, (c) Provider's violation of applicable law, including labor and employment laws, or (d) any claim that Provider's processes or materials not specified by Customer infringe a third party's intellectual property rights.

13.2 **By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Provider and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all third-party claims, and resulting losses, damages, costs and reasonable attorneys' fees, to the extent arising from (a) any claim that Customer Materials or Customer's trademarks infringe a third party's intellectual property rights, or (b) Customer's negligence or willful misconduct.

13.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of a claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement (provided no settlement imposes any obligation or admission on the indemnified Party without its consent), and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

## 14. Term and Termination

14.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the MSA Effective Date and continues for three years from the MSA Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"). Thereafter, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive three (3) year renewal terms (each, a "**Renewal Term**"), unless either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal no earlier than ninety (90) days and no later than sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current Initial Term or Renewal Term.

14.2 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party (a) materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after written notice describing it, or (b) becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes subject to any bankruptcy, receivership or similar proceeding not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

14.3 **Termination for Convenience.** During the Initial Term, Customer may terminate this Agreement for convenience on not less than one hundred eighty (180) days' written notice to Provider. On such termination, Customer shall pay for all Products completed or in production under accepted Purchase Orders and for raw materials purchased by Provider specifically for those Purchase Orders that cannot reasonably be cancelled, returned or used for other customers.

14.4 **Effect of Termination.** On expiry or termination, (a) Provider shall complete accepted Purchase Orders unless Customer directs otherwise, (b) Provider shall provide transition assistance for the Fulfillment Services for up to ninety (90) days at the rates in Schedule B, including transfer of fulfillment inventory to Customer's designated warehouse, (c) each Party shall return or destroy the other's Confidential Information, and (d) Provider shall return or delete Personal Data as provided in the DPA.

14.5 **Survival.** Sections 6, 8, 9, 11, 13, 14.4, 14.5 and 16, and any other provision that by its nature should survive, survive expiry or termination.

## 15. Force Majeure

15.1 Neither Party is liable for delay or failure to perform (other than payment obligations) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, epidemics, war, terrorism, government action or widespread utility failure, provided that it promptly notifies the other Party and uses commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate. If a force majeure event prevents Provider from performing for more than forty-five (45) days, Customer may cancel affected Purchase Orders and source the affected Products elsewhere without liability, and such volumes shall count toward the Minimum Volume Commitment.

## 16. General

16.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods does not apply.

16.2 **Disputes.** The Parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith discussions between senior executives for thirty (30) days. Thereafter, either Party may bring proceedings in the state or federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware, and each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of those courts.

16.3 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation copy by courier, to the addresses stated on the signature page or as later updated by notice. Notices are effective on receipt.

16.4 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, except that either Party may assign it to an Affiliate or to a successor in connection with a merger or sale of all or substantially all of its relevant assets, on written notice.

16.5 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency relationship.

16.6 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing, and no failure to exercise a right operates as a waiver.

16.7 **Severability.** If any provision is held unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible and the remainder of this Agreement continues in effect.

16.8 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.

## Schedule A: Products and Specifications

**A.1 Products**

| Product Category | Description |
|---|---|
| Technical Waterproof Shell Jackets | Three-layer waterproof breathable shell jackets, men's and women's styles, as detailed in the applicable tech packs |
| Insulated Layers | Synthetic-insulated and down-insulated jackets and vests, men's and women's styles, as detailed in the applicable tech packs |

**A.2 Performance Specifications: Technical Waterproof Shell Jackets**

(a) Waterproofing: minimum 20,000 mm hydrostatic head with fully taped seams.

(b) Breathability: minimum 15,000 g/m²/24h moisture vapor transmission rate.

(c) Durable water repellent finish: minimum spray rating of 80 after twenty (20) home launderings.

(d) Seam tape adhesion: no delamination or lifting after twenty (20) home launderings.

**A.3 Performance Specifications: Insulated Layers**

(a) Down fill power: as stated in the applicable tech pack, verified by independent laboratory testing.

(b) Synthetic insulation weight: within ±5% of the weight stated in the applicable tech pack.

(c) Down-proof construction: no visible fill migration after ten (10) home launderings.

**A.4 General Requirements**

Products shall conform to the approved tech pack for each style, including materials, trims, dimensions, size grading, labeling and care instructions, and to all applicable product safety, labeling and chemical restriction laws, including California Proposition 65 and applicable PFAS restrictions.
