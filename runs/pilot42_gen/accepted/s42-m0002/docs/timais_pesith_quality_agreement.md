# QUALITY AGREEMENT

**Timais Outdoor Co. / Pesith Apparel Manufacturing, LLC**

This Quality Agreement (this "**Quality Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2026 (the "**Quality Agreement Effective Date**") by and between **Timais Outdoor Co.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"), and **Pesith Apparel Manufacturing, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**"). Customer and Provider are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## Recitals

A. Customer and Provider are parties to a Master Services Agreement dated as of March 1, 2026 (the "**MSA**"), under which Provider manufactures Products for Customer and performs Fulfillment Services.

B. The MSA incorporates this Quality Agreement by reference. This Quality Agreement sets out the quality system, inspection, rejection and corrective action procedures that apply to Products manufactured under the MSA.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Quality Agreement have the meanings given in the MSA. In addition:

1.1 "**AQL**" means the acceptance quality limit applied under the sampling plan described in Appendix A.

1.2 "**Corrective Action Plan**" or "**CAP**" means a written plan prepared by Provider under Section 7 that identifies the root cause of a quality issue and the actions and timetable to correct it and prevent recurrence.

1.3 "**Final Random Inspection**" or "**FRI**" means the pre-shipment inspection of a finished production lot described in Section 4.3.

1.4 "**Golden Sample**" means the pre-production sample of each style and colorway approved in writing by Customer and retained by both Parties as the reference standard for that style and colorway.

1.5 "**Lot**" means all units of a single style and colorway produced under a single Purchase Order and presented for inspection together.

1.6 "**Nonconforming Product**" means any Product, or any unit of a Product, that fails the visual and workmanship criteria in Appendix A, as determined by inspection under Section 4 or Section 5.

1.7 "**Quality Manager**" means the individual designated by each Party under Section 2.2.

1.8 "**Quality Records**" means the records described in Section 8.1.

## 2. Quality System and Responsibilities

2.1 **Quality System.** Provider shall establish, document and maintain a quality management system appropriate to the manufacture of technical outerwear and insulated apparel, covering incoming materials control, in-process controls, final inspection, nonconforming material control, calibration of measuring and test equipment, and personnel training.

2.2 **Quality Managers.** Each Party shall designate a Quality Manager responsible for administering this Quality Agreement and shall notify the other Party in writing of the Quality Manager's name and contact details within ten (10) business days after the Quality Agreement Effective Date and promptly after any change.

2.3 **Quality Reviews.** The Quality Managers shall meet, in person or by video conference, at least once per calendar quarter to review inspection results, rejection rates, open CAPs, customer returns data provided by Customer, and planned changes.

2.4 **Training.** Provider shall ensure that all personnel performing sewing, seam sealing, cutting, inspection and packing operations on Products are trained on the applicable tech packs, Golden Samples and this Quality Agreement before working on Products.

## 3. Materials and Pre-Production

3.1 **Approved Materials.** Provider shall use only fabrics, membranes, seam tapes, insulation, trims and labels specified in the applicable tech pack or supplied or nominated by Customer as Customer Materials. Provider shall not substitute any material or supplier without Customer's prior written approval.

3.2 **Incoming Inspection.** Provider shall inspect incoming materials against the applicable supplier certificates and the tech pack before release to production, and shall segregate and quarantine any material that does not conform.

3.3 **Supplier Documentation.** Provider shall obtain and retain, for each lot of fabric, membrane, seam tape and insulation, the supplier's certificate of conformance and any test report supplied with the material, and shall make them available to Customer on request.

3.4 **Pre-Production Approval.** Provider shall not begin bulk production of any style or colorway until Customer has approved in writing a Golden Sample and a pre-production sample for that style and colorway. Provider shall hold a pre-production meeting with its production and quality personnel for each new style before bulk production begins.

3.5 **Changes.** Provider shall not change any manufacturing facility, production line location, process, equipment or material that may affect any Product without at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice to Customer and Customer's written approval.

## 4. In-Process and Final Inspection by Provider

4.1 **In-Process Inspection.** Provider shall conduct in-line inspections at each critical operation, including cutting, assembly, seam sealing and finishing, and shall record the results in its Quality Records.

4.2 **Laboratory Testing.** Provider shall arrange laboratory testing of Products at the frequency stated in the applicable tech pack, using an independent laboratory approved by Customer, and shall deliver copies of all test reports to Customer's Quality Manager within five (5) business days after Provider receives them.

4.3 **Final Random Inspection.** Before shipment of each Lot, Provider shall conduct a Final Random Inspection of that Lot under the sampling plan and against the criteria in Appendix A. Customer or its designated third-party inspector may attend or conduct the FRI on at least five (5) business days' notice.

4.4 **Lot Disposition.** A Lot that fails the FRI shall not be shipped. Provider shall sort and rework the Lot at its own cost and resubmit it for FRI. Provider shall give Customer written notice of any failed FRI within two (2) business days.

4.5 **Inspection Report.** Provider shall issue an inspection report for each FRI, signed by its inspector, and shall send it to Customer's Quality Manager before shipment.

## 5. Inspection and Rejection by Customer

5.1 **Customer Inspection.** Customer or its designee may inspect Products after delivery, using the sampling plan and criteria in Appendix A or by inspection of individual units.

5.2 **Rejection Period.** Customer may reject any Nonconforming Product, or any Lot in which the number of Nonconforming Product units in the inspection sample exceeds the applicable AQL acceptance number, by written notice to Provider within 30 days after delivery to Customer's designated warehouse. For Products transferred into fulfillment inventory under Schedule C of the MSA, the period begins on the date the Products are recorded as received into that inventory.

5.3 **Rejection Notice.** A rejection notice shall identify the affected Purchase Order, style, colorway and quantity and describe the nonconformity, with photographs or inspection data where available.

5.4 **Disposition.** Provider shall, within five (5) business days after receipt of a rejection notice, either accept the rejection or request a joint re-inspection. Rejected Product shall be held by Customer for Provider's collection or disposed of as Provider directs at Provider's cost. Rejected Product shall not be sold as seconds or otherwise disposed of in breach of Section 8.4 of the MSA. Replacement and credit for Nonconforming Product are provided under Section 6.2 of the MSA.

5.5 **Joint Re-Inspection.** Any joint re-inspection shall take place within ten (10) business days after Provider's request, using the sampling plan and criteria in Appendix A, and the Parties shall each bear their own costs. If the Parties cannot agree on the result, either Party may refer the matter for escalation under Section 9.

## 6. Customer Returns and Field Complaints

6.1 **Returns Data.** Customer may share with Provider data on consumer returns and complaints relating to Products. Provider shall review that data at each quarterly quality review under Section 2.3.

6.2 **Investigation.** At Customer's request, Provider shall investigate any recurring consumer complaint relating to a Product and report its findings to Customer within fifteen (15) business days.

## 7. Corrective and Preventive Action

7.1 **CAP Requirement.** Provider shall deliver a CAP to Customer within ten (10) business days after (a) any Lot fails an FRI or is rejected by Customer, (b) any laboratory test report shows a result outside the applicable Specification, or (c) Customer's written request following an investigation under Section 6.2.

7.2 **Implementation.** Provider shall implement each CAP in accordance with its timetable and shall report progress at each quarterly quality review until Customer confirms in writing that the CAP is closed.

## 8. Records and Audits

8.1 **Quality Records.** Provider shall maintain complete and accurate records of incoming material inspections, supplier certificates, in-process inspections, FRI reports, laboratory test reports, rejections, rework and CAPs for each Lot.

8.2 **Retention.** Provider shall retain Quality Records for at least three (3) years after delivery of the relevant Lot and shall make them available to Customer on request.

8.3 **Audits.** Customer may audit Provider's quality system, production facilities and Quality Records in accordance with Section 3.5 of the MSA.

## 9. Escalation

9.1 Any disagreement under this Quality Agreement that the Quality Managers cannot resolve within ten (10) business days shall be escalated to each Party's head of operations. If not resolved within a further ten (10) business days, the dispute procedures of Section 16.2 of the MSA apply.

## 10. Term and General

10.1 **Term.** This Quality Agreement begins on the Quality Agreement Effective Date and remains in effect for the Term of the MSA and thereafter for so long as any Product delivered under the MSA remains within the warranty period stated in Section 6.1 of the MSA.

10.2 **Amendment.** This Quality Agreement, including Appendix A, may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

10.3 **General.** Sections 16.1 (Governing Law), 16.3 (Notices) and 16.8 (Counterparts) of the MSA apply to this Quality Agreement.

## Appendix A: Visual and Workmanship Inspection Criteria

**A.1 Sampling Plan**

Inspection shall use single sampling under ANSI/ASQ Z1.4, General Inspection Level II, with the following AQLs:

| Defect Classification | AQL |
|---|---|
| Critical | 0 (zero tolerance) |
| Major | 2.5 |
| Minor | 4.0 |

**A.2 Critical Defects**

(a) Broken needle or other sharp metal fragment found in a unit.

(b) Missing or incorrect country of origin, fiber content or care label.

(c) Wrong Customer trademark or brand label applied.

**A.3 Major Defects**

(a) Stitching: open seams, skipped or broken stitches, run-off stitching, or stitch density outside the tech pack by more than one stitch per inch.

(b) Color: shade variation from the Golden Sample visible at arm's length under D65 light, or panel-to-panel shade variation within a unit.

(c) Labeling: size label not matching the size marked on hangtag or polybag; missing hangtag; illegible label printing.

(d) Trims: missing, broken or non-functioning zipper, snap, cord lock or toggle; incorrect trim color.

(e) Appearance: holes, fabric flaws or stains visible at arm's length; mismatched panels; garment-level misalignment of pockets or zippers exceeding 6 mm.

**A.4 Minor Defects**

(a) Stitching: uneven topstitching, loose thread ends longer than 6 mm, minor seam puckering.

(b) Color: slight shade variation visible only at close range.

(c) Labeling: label slightly crooked or off-center.

(d) Appearance: minor marks removable by normal cleaning; minor pressing or folding irregularities.

(e) Packing: incorrect fold, polybag warning missing or misplaced, carton marking errors.

**A.5 Reference Standard**

Color and appearance shall be assessed against the Golden Sample for the applicable style and colorway.
