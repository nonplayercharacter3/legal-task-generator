# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

**Timais Outdoor Co. / Pesith Apparel Manufacturing, LLC**

This Data Processing Addendum (this "**DPA**") is entered into as of March 1, 2026 by and between **Timais Outdoor Co.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**" or "**Controller**"), and **Pesith Apparel Manufacturing, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**" or "**Processor**"). Customer and Provider are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## Recitals

A. Customer and Provider are parties to a Master Services Agreement dated as of March 1, 2026 (the "**Agreement**" or the "**MSA**"). Under the Agreement, Provider manufactures Products for Customer and performs Fulfillment Services for orders placed by Customer's end consumers.

B. In performing the Fulfillment Services, Provider processes Personal Data of Customer's end consumers on Customer's behalf. The Agreement incorporates this DPA by reference.

C. This DPA sets out the Parties' obligations concerning that processing.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the Agreement. In addition:

1.1 "**Applicable Data Protection Laws**" means all laws and regulations that apply to the processing of Personal Data under the Agreement. These include the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act of 2020, and its implementing regulations (the "**CCPA**"). They also include comparable consumer privacy laws of other U.S. states and any applicable data breach notification laws, in each case as amended from time to time.

1.2 "**Consumer**" means an identified or identifiable natural person who places an order for Products through Customer's sales channels, or who receives or returns such an order.

1.3 "**Customer Personal Data**" means Personal Data that Provider processes on behalf of Customer in connection with the Agreement, including in performing the Fulfillment Services.

1.4 "**Personal Data**" means any information that identifies, relates to, describes, or is reasonably capable of being associated with a natural person or household, and any information defined as "personal information," "personal data" or a similar term under Applicable Data Protection Laws.

1.5 "**Processing**" (and "**process**" and "**processed**") means any operation performed on Personal Data, whether or not by automated means. This includes collection, recording, organization, storage, retrieval, use, disclosure, transmission, alignment, restriction, erasure and destruction.

1.6 "**Security Incident**" means any breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Customer Personal Data processed by Provider or any Subprocessor.

1.7 "**Subprocessor**" means any third party engaged by Provider to process Customer Personal Data. This includes carriers, warehouse management system vendors and hosting providers.

## 2. Scope and Roles

2.1 **Roles.** As between the Parties, Customer is the controller and a "business" under the CCPA. Provider is the processor and a "service provider" under the CCPA, processing Customer Personal Data on Customer's behalf.

2.2 **Scope.** This DPA applies to all processing of Customer Personal Data by Provider and its Subprocessors in connection with the Agreement. The subject matter, nature, purpose and duration of the processing, and the categories of Consumers and Customer Personal Data, are described in Annex 1.

2.3 **Customer Responsibilities.** Customer shall provide any notices to Consumers required by Applicable Data Protection Laws for the disclosure of Customer Personal Data to Provider. Customer shall also have a lawful basis for that disclosure.

## 3. Processing Instructions

3.1 **Documented Instructions.** Provider shall process Customer Personal Data only on Customer's documented instructions. Those instructions consist of the Agreement, this DPA, any Statement of Work and any further written instructions Customer gives that are consistent with the Agreement.

3.2 **Prohibited Processing.** Provider shall not:

(a) sell or share Customer Personal Data, as those terms are defined in the CCPA;

(b) retain, use or disclose Customer Personal Data for any purpose other than performing the Services, including for any commercial purpose of its own;

(c) retain, use or disclose Customer Personal Data outside the direct business relationship between Customer and Provider; or

(d) combine Customer Personal Data with Personal Data that Provider receives from or on behalf of any other person, or that Provider collects from its own interactions with Consumers, except as Applicable Data Protection Laws permit for a service provider.

3.3 **Unlawful Instructions.** Provider shall promptly notify Customer if, in Provider's opinion, an instruction infringes Applicable Data Protection Laws. Provider shall also notify Customer if it determines that it can no longer meet its obligations under Applicable Data Protection Laws.

3.4 **Certification.** Provider certifies that it understands and will comply with the restrictions in this Section 3.

## 4. Personnel and Confidentiality

4.1 **Access.** Provider shall limit access to Customer Personal Data to personnel who need that access to perform the Services.

4.2 **Confidentiality.** Provider shall ensure that all such personnel are bound by written obligations of confidentiality, or are under an appropriate statutory duty of confidentiality.

4.3 **Training.** Provider shall train personnel with access to Customer Personal Data on data protection and information security at onboarding and at least annually.

## 5. Security

5.1 **Security Measures.** Provider shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Customer Personal Data against Security Incidents. These shall include, at a minimum, the measures in Annex 2.

5.2 **Changes.** Provider may update its security measures from time to time, provided that no update materially reduces the overall protection of Customer Personal Data.

5.3 **Insurance.** Provider shall maintain the cyber liability and network security insurance required by Section 12.1(c) of the Agreement.

## 6. Subprocessors

6.1 **Authorization.** Customer authorizes Provider to engage the Subprocessors listed in Annex 3. Provider shall notify Customer in writing at least thirty (30) days before engaging any new Subprocessor or replacing an existing one.

6.2 **Objection.** Customer may object to a new Subprocessor on reasonable data protection grounds by written notice within that thirty (30) day period. If the Parties cannot resolve the objection in good faith within a further thirty (30) days, Provider shall not use that Subprocessor to process Customer Personal Data.

6.3 **Flow-Down.** Provider shall impose on each Subprocessor, by written contract, data protection obligations no less protective than those in this DPA. Provider remains responsible for each Subprocessor's performance of those obligations. Engagement of a Subprocessor is also subject to Section 3.4 of the Agreement.

## 7. Security Incidents

7.1 **Notification.** Provider shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours, after becoming aware of a Security Incident.

7.2 **Information.** Provider's notice shall describe, to the extent then known:

(a) the nature of the Security Incident;

(b) the categories and approximate number of Consumers and records affected;

(c) the likely consequences; and

(d) the measures taken or proposed to address the Security Incident.

Provider shall supplement the notice as further information becomes available.

7.3 **Cooperation.** Provider shall take reasonable steps to contain, investigate and remediate each Security Incident. Provider shall cooperate with Customer in any notification to Consumers or regulators.

7.4 **Notifications by Customer.** Customer shall decide whether and how to notify Consumers, regulators and other third parties of a Security Incident. Provider shall not make any such notification without Customer's prior written approval, unless required by law.

## 8. Liability and Indemnification

8.1 **Liability.** Each Party's liability arising out of or relating to this DPA is subject to Section 11 of the Agreement, except as stated in Section 8.2.

8.2 **Indemnification by Customer.** Customer, as Controller, shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Processor and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all losses, damages, regulatory fines and penalties, costs, reasonable attorneys' fees and third-party claims. This applies to all such amounts arising out of or relating to any processing of Customer Personal Data performed by Processor on Customer's behalf. Customer's obligations under this Section 8.2 shall not be subject to any limitation of liability in the Agreement.

8.3 **Procedure.** The procedure in Section 13.3 of the Agreement applies to any claim for indemnification under this DPA.

## 9. Assistance and Audits

9.1 **Consumer Requests.** Provider shall promptly forward to Customer any request from a Consumer to exercise rights under Applicable Data Protection Laws. Such rights include requests to know, access, correct or delete. Provider shall not respond to the request except on Customer's instructions. Provider shall give Customer reasonable assistance, through appropriate technical and organizational measures, in responding to such requests.

9.2 **Regulatory Inquiries.** Provider shall promptly notify Customer of any inquiry, complaint or investigation by a regulator relating to Customer Personal Data, unless prohibited by law. Provider shall reasonably cooperate with Customer in responding.

9.3 **Assessments.** Provider shall provide information reasonably requested by Customer to enable Customer to conduct any risk assessment or cybersecurity audit required by Applicable Data Protection Laws.

9.4 **Audits.** Provider shall make available to Customer, on request, information reasonably necessary to demonstrate its compliance with this DPA. This includes, where available, its most recent independent security assessment report. Customer may audit Provider's compliance with this DPA in accordance with Section 3.5 of the Agreement. Customer may also take reasonable and appropriate steps to stop and remediate any unauthorized processing of Customer Personal Data.

## 10. Return and Deletion

10.1 **On Termination.** Within thirty (30) days after expiry or termination of the Agreement, or on completion of any transition assistance under Section 14.4(b) of the Agreement, Provider shall, at Customer's election, return or delete all Customer Personal Data. This applies to Customer Personal Data in the possession or control of Provider and its Subprocessors.

10.2 **Certification.** Provider shall certify in writing that it has completed the return or deletion, unless retention is required by law.

10.3 **Retained Data.** Any Customer Personal Data retained under a legal requirement remains subject to this DPA. Provider shall process it only for the purposes of that legal requirement.

## 11. Term and General

11.1 **Term.** This DPA begins on March 1, 2026. It remains in effect for so long as Provider or any Subprocessor processes Customer Personal Data.

11.2 **Amendment.** This DPA may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. The Parties shall negotiate in good faith any amendment reasonably required to comply with changes in Applicable Data Protection Laws.

11.3 **General.** Sections 16.1 (Governing Law), 16.2 (Disputes), 16.3 (Notices) and 16.8 (Counterparts) of the Agreement apply to this DPA.

## Annex 2: Security Measures

(a) **Access control:** role-based access to warehouse management and order systems, unique user credentials, multi-factor authentication for remote and administrative access, and prompt removal of access on personnel departure.

(b) **Encryption:** encryption of Customer Personal Data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest using AES-256 or equivalent.

(c) **Network security:** firewalls, intrusion detection, regular vulnerability scanning, and patching of critical vulnerabilities within thirty (30) days.

(d) **Physical security:** controlled access to the fulfillment center and server areas, visitor logs, and CCTV monitoring.

(e) **Data minimization:** printing of Consumer data on packing slips and shipping labels limited to what is needed for delivery, and secure shredding of misprinted labels and paper records.

(f) **Logging and monitoring:** logging of access to order systems, with logs retained for at least twelve (12) months.

(g) **Business continuity:** regular backups, tested at least annually, and a documented incident response plan.
