# PARENT GUARANTY

This Parent Guaranty (this "**Guaranty**") is made as of March 1, 2024 by **Fosais Holdings Corp.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 2215 East Camelback Road, Suite 150, Phoenix, Arizona 85016 ("**Guarantor**"), in favor of **Dreitoul Software, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4410 Meridian Parkway, Suite 300, Wilmington, Delaware 19803 ("**Licensor**").

## RECITALS

A. Licensor and Fosais Solar Installations, LLC, an Arizona limited liability company ("**Licensee**"), are entering into a Software License Agreement dated as of March 1, 2024 (as amended, supplemented or otherwise modified from time to time, the "**Agreement**"). Under the Agreement, Licensor licenses its HelioDraft solar design and permitting platform to Licensee.

B. Guarantor owns, directly or indirectly, all of the issued and outstanding membership interests of Licensee. Guarantor will derive substantial direct and indirect benefit from the Agreement.

C. It is a condition to Licensor's entry into the Agreement that Guarantor execute and deliver this Guaranty.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of Licensor's entry into the Agreement and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, Guarantor agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Guaranty have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

1.2 **Guaranteed Obligations.** "**Guaranteed Obligations**" means all obligations, liabilities and indebtedness of Licensee to Licensor under or in connection with the Agreement and each Order Form, whether now existing or arising later, and whether absolute or contingent, liquidated or unliquidated. This includes:

(a) payment of all Fees;

(b) payment of interest, taxes and other amounts payable under the Agreement;

(c) all of Licensee's defense, indemnification and hold-harmless obligations; and

(d) the performance of all of Licensee's covenants and obligations under the Agreement.

## 2. GUARANTY

2.1 **Guaranty of Payment and Performance.** Guarantor absolutely, unconditionally and irrevocably guarantees to Licensor, as primary obligor and not merely as surety, the full and punctual payment and performance of all of Licensee's obligations under the Agreement, including all Guaranteed Obligations, when due in accordance with the Agreement.

2.2 **Payment on Demand.** If Licensee fails to pay or perform any Guaranteed Obligation when due, Guarantor shall pay or perform it within ten (10) business days after Licensor's written demand. The demand must describe the Guaranteed Obligation in reasonable detail.

2.3 **Guaranty of Payment, Not Collection.** This Guaranty is a guaranty of payment and performance and not of collection. Licensor is not required to take any of the following steps before making demand on, or proceeding against, Guarantor:

(a) proceed against Licensee or any other person;

(b) exhaust any remedy against Licensee; or

(c) pursue any other security or remedy.

2.4 **Defenses of Licensee.** Guarantor may assert any defense, set-off or counterclaim that Licensee may have under the Agreement, except defenses arising from the insolvency, bankruptcy or lack of capacity of Licensee.

## 3. NATURE OF GUARANTY

3.1 **Continuing Guaranty.** This Guaranty is a continuing guaranty. It remains in full force and effect until all Guaranteed Obligations have been paid and performed in full, including Guaranteed Obligations that survive expiration or termination of the Agreement under Section 14.4 of the Agreement.

3.2 **Obligations Not Affected.** The obligations of Guarantor under this Guaranty are not released, discharged or otherwise affected by any of the following:

(a) any amendment, modification, renewal, extension or supplement of the Agreement or any Order Form, including any renewal of the Agreement for a Renewal Term;

(b) any waiver, consent or indulgence granted to Licensee;

(c) any change in the ownership, structure or existence of Licensee; or

(d) any bankruptcy, insolvency, reorganization or similar proceeding involving Licensee.

3.3 **Reinstatement.** This Guaranty is reinstated if any payment of a Guaranted Obligation is rescinded or must be returned by Licensor upon the insolvency, bankruptcy or reorganization of Licensee, as though the payment had not been made.

## 4. WAIVERS

4.1 **Waivers.** To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, Guarantor waives:

(a) notice of acceptance of this Guaranty;

(b) presentment, protest and notice of dishonor;

(c) notice of any default by Licensee, other than the demand required by Section 2.2; and

(d) any right to require Licensor to marshal assets.

4.2 **Subrogation.** Until all Guaranteed Obligations then due have been paid in full, Guarantor shall not exercise any right of subrogation, reimbursement, contribution or indemnity against Licensee arising from payments made under this Guaranty.

## 5. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

Guarantor represents and warrants to Licensor as of the date of this Guaranty that:

(a) Guarantor is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware;

(b) Guarantor has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Guaranty, and its execution, delivery and performance have been duly authorized by all necessary corporate action;

(c) this Guaranty constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Guarantor, enforceable in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws affecting creditors' rights generally and to general principles of equity; and

(d) the execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty do not violate Guarantor's organizational documents, any law applicable to Guarantor, or any material agreement by which Guarantor is bound.

## 6. COSTS OF ENFORCEMENT

Guarantor shall reimburse Licensor for reasonable attorneys' fees and costs incurred in successfully enforcing this Guaranty against Guarantor.

7.1 **Governing Law.** This Guaranty is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws rules.

7.2 **Forum.** Any proceeding arising out of or relating to this Guaranty shall be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware. Guarantor submits to the personal jurisdiction of those courts.
