## 1. SCOPE OF THIS SIDE LETTER

1.1 This Side Letter is a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both parties for purposes of Section 16.5 of the Agreement.

1.2 This Side Letter does not amend, modify or waive any provision of the Agreement or any Order Form except for the fee terms set out in Sections 2 and 3 of this Side Letter. Except as expressly set out in Sections 2 and 3, the Agreement and each Order Form remain unchanged and in full force and effect.

## 2. ADJUSTED SEAT FEES

2.1 **Seat Fee.** With effect from December 1, 2026 (the "**Adjustment Date**"), the annual Seat fee for each Seat licensed by Licensee under the initial Order Form is reduced from $2,400 per Seat to $2,160 per Seat.

2.2 **Additional Seats.** Licensee may license up to fifteen (15) Seats in addition to the Seats licensed under the initial Order Form at an annual Seat fee of $2,160 per Seat, by written request to Licensor. Licensor shall provision each additional Seat within five (5) business days after receipt of Licensee's request.

2.3 **Minimum Seat Count.** The adjusted Seat fee in Section 2.1 is conditioned on Licensee maintaining not fewer than forty (40) active Seats from the Adjustment Date. If the number of active Seats falls below forty (40), the Seat fee in effect under the initial Order Form immediately before the Adjustment Date applies from the first day of the following calendar month.

## 3. INVOICING

3.1 **Credit.** Licensor shall issue Licensee a credit equal to the difference between the Seat fees prepaid by Licensee for the period from the Adjustment Date to the end of Licensee's current annual billing period and the Seat fees payable for that period under Section 2.1. The credit will be applied against the next invoice issued under the Agreement.

3.2 **Additional Seat Invoicing.** Fees for additional Seats licensed under Section 2.2 will be invoiced on a prorated basis from the date the Seat is provisioned to the end of Licensee's current annual billing period, and thereafter annually in advance with Licensee's other Seats.

3.3 **Payment Terms.** Section 5 of the Agreement applies to all amounts invoiced under this Side Letter.

## 4. NON-RENEWAL NOTICE

4.1 **Delivery.** Licensee's written notice of non-renewal of the Agreement under Section 14.1 of the Agreement, addressed to Licensor's General Counsel, was delivered on November 2, 2026 by email to legal@dreitoul.com, with a copy by nationally recognized overnight courier (the "**Non-Renewal Notice**").

4.2 **Licensor's Position.** Licensor's position is that the Non-Renewal Notice was not delivered within the period required by Section 14.1 of the Agreement, is untimely and is of no effect, and that the Agreement will accordingly renew for a Renewal Term in accordance with Section 14.1 of the Agreement.

4.3 **Licensee's Position.** Licensee disputes Licensor's position as stated in Section 4.2 and reserves all of its rights and remedies under the Agreement, at law and in equity with respect to the Non-Renewal Notice, the expiration of the Initial Term and any Renewal Term.

4.4 **No Waiver.** Neither party's execution of this Side Letter, nor the adjustment of Seat fees under Sections 2 and 3, constitutes an admission by either party, or a waiver of any right, position or remedy of either party, with respect to the matters described in this Section 4.
