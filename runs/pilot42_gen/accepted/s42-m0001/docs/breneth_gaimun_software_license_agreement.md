# SOFTWARE LICENSE AND SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

**Breneth Systems, Inc. – Gaimun Brewing Company, LLC**

This Software License and Subscription Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of November 2, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**Breneth Systems, Inc.**, a Colorado corporation with its principal place of business in Denver, Colorado ("**Breneth**"); and

**Gaimun Brewing Company, LLC**, a New York limited liability company with its principal place of business in Kingston, New York ("**Customer**").

Breneth and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 **Definitions.** Capitalized terms used in this Agreement have the following meanings:

(a) "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity or the power to direct its management and policies.

(b) "**Authorized User**" means an employee or individual contractor of Customer whom Customer authorizes to access and use the Platform under Customer's account.

(c) "**Change of Control**" means, with respect to Customer: (i) any merger, consolidation or other business combination in which Customer is not the surviving entity, or in which Customer becomes a subsidiary of another entity; (ii) the acquisition by any person or group of beneficial ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the equity or voting interests in Customer; or (iii) the sale or transfer of all or substantially all of Customer's assets.

(d) "**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 9.1.

(e) "**Customer Data**" means all data, information and materials that Customer or its Authorized Users submit to, upload to, or generate through the Platform, including recipes and formulations, batch and brew records, production volumes, inventory records, excise and tax reporting data, and distributor, customer and pricing information.

(f) "**Documentation**" means the user guides, technical specifications and help materials for the Platform that Breneth makes available to Customer, as updated from time to time.

(g) "**Fees**" means the Subscription Fees and any other fees payable under an Order Form.

(h) "**Initial Subscription Term**" means the initial period of the subscription stated in the Order Form.

(i) "**Order Form**" means each ordering document executed by both Parties that references this Agreement, including Order Form No. 001 attached as Schedule A.

(j) "**Platform**" means Breneth's hosted brewery-management software platform known as "BrewOps," including the batch and recipe tracking, TTB excise reporting, inventory management and distributor ordering modules, together with all updates, enhancements and Documentation made available under this Agreement.

(k) "**Renewal Term**" has the meaning given in Section 3.2.

(l) "**Service Credits**" has the meaning given in the Service Level Schedule.

(m) "**Service Level Schedule**" means the service level schedule attached as Schedule B.

(n) "**Side Letter**" means any side letter executed by both Parties with reference to this Agreement.

(o) "**Subscription Fees**" means the fees for access to the Platform stated in the Order Form.

(p) "**Subscription Start Date**" means the date stated as such in the Order Form.

(q) "**Subscription Term**" means the Initial Subscription Term together with all Renewal Terms.

(r) "**TTB**" means the Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau of the United States Department of the Treasury.

1.2 **Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections and Schedules are to sections of and schedules to this Agreement unless stated otherwise.

1.3 **Agreement Documents.** This Agreement consists of this body of terms, the Order Form, the Service Level Schedule and any Side Letter (together, the "**Agreement Documents**").

1.4 **Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict or inconsistency among the Agreement Documents, the following order of precedence applies: the Side Letter, then the Order Form, then the Service Level Schedule, then the body of this Agreement.

## 2. LICENSE AND ACCESS

2.1 **Subscription Grant.** Subject to Customer's compliance with this Agreement and payment of the Fees, Breneth grants Customer, during the Subscription Term, a non-exclusive, non-transferable (except as permitted in Section 14), non-sublicensable right for Customer and its Authorized Users to access and use the Platform and the Documentation for Customer's internal business operations.

2.2 **Authorized Users.** Customer may permit the number of Authorized Users stated in the Order Form. Customer is responsible for each Authorized User's compliance with this Agreement and for maintaining the confidentiality of account credentials.

2.3 **Restrictions.** Customer shall not, and shall not permit any person to: (a) copy, modify or create derivative works of the Platform; (b) reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble the Platform, except to the extent applicable law expressly permits despite this restriction; (c) sell, resell, rent, lease or otherwise make the Platform available to any third party other than Authorized Users; (d) use the Platform to build a competing product; or (e) interfere with the integrity or performance of the Platform.

2.4 **Regulatory Reporting.** The TTB excise reporting module generates reports from Customer Data. Customer remains responsible for reviewing, approving and submitting its returns and reports to the TTB and any state authority. Breneth shall maintain the reporting module so that its report formats conform to the TTB forms current as of the date of each update.

2.5 **Reservation of Rights.** Breneth and its licensors retain all right, title and interest in and to the Platform. No rights are granted except as expressly set out in this Agreement.

## 3. TERM AND RENEWAL

3.1 **Term of Agreement.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until the expiry or termination of the Subscription Term, unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 13.

3.2 **Renewal.** Upon expiry of the Initial Subscription Term, the subscription shall automatically renew for successive renewal periods, each equal in length to the Initial Subscription Term (each, a "**Renewal Term**"), unless either Party delivers written notice of non-renewal to the other Party no earlier than 60 days and no later than 45 days before the end of the then-current Initial Subscription Term or Renewal Term. A notice of non-renewal delivered outside that period shall not be effective. Each Renewal Term shall be on the terms of this Agreement and the then-current Order Form.

3.3 **Renewal Pricing.** Subscription Fees for any Renewal Term shall be the Subscription Fees in effect during the final year of the immediately preceding term, unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Fees.** Customer shall pay the Fees stated in the Order Form. Except as expressly provided in this Agreement, Fees are non-cancellable and non-refundable.

4.2 **Invoicing and Payment.** Breneth shall invoice the Fees as set out in the Order Form. Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of the invoice date, in U.S. dollars, by ACH or wire transfer to the account designated by Breneth.

4.3 **Disputed Invoices.** Customer may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith by notifying Breneth in writing, with reasonable supporting detail, before the payment due date. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days.

4.4 **Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due accrue interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law. If any undisputed amount remains unpaid for more than thirty (30) days after Breneth gives written notice of non-payment, Breneth may suspend access to the Platform until payment is made.

4.5 **Taxes.** Fees exclude all sales, use, value-added and similar taxes. Customer shall pay all such taxes, other than taxes on Breneth's net income, property or employees.

## 7. CUSTOMER DATA

7.1 **Ownership.** As between the Parties, Customer owns all right, title and interest in and to the Customer Data.

7.2 **License to Breneth.** Customer grants Breneth a non-exclusive, royalty-free license during the Subscription Term to host, process and use Customer Data solely to provide the Platform and support to Customer and to perform its obligations under this Agreement.

7.3 **Aggregated Data.** Breneth may generate de-identified, aggregated usage statistics regarding the operation of the Platform, provided that such statistics do not identify Customer or disclose any recipe, formulation, pricing, distributor or production information of Customer.

7.4 **Return and Deletion.** Upon expiry or termination of this Agreement and on Customer's written request made within thirty (30) days thereafter, Breneth shall make Customer Data available for export in a standard, machine-readable format. Breneth shall then delete Customer Data from its systems within sixty (60) days, except for copies retained in routine backups, which shall remain subject to Section 8 until overwritten.

## 8. DATA SECURITY

8.1 **Security Program.** Breneth shall implement and maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards designed to protect the security, confidentiality and integrity of Customer Data against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction, consistent with recognized industry standards for cloud-hosted business software.

8.2 **Specific Measures.** Without limiting Section 8.1, Breneth shall: (a) encrypt Customer Data in transit and at rest; (b) restrict access to Customer Data to personnel who need it to perform Breneth's obligations; (c) maintain daily backups of Customer Data with a retention period of not less than thirty (30) days; and (d) maintain a SOC 2 Type II report or equivalent independent audit, a summary of which Breneth shall provide to Customer on request no more than once per year.

8.3 **Security Incidents.** Breneth shall notify Customer in writing without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to, or loss, corruption or disclosure of, Customer Data (a "**Security Incident**"). Breneth shall promptly investigate, take reasonable steps to contain and remediate the Security Incident, restore affected Customer Data from backups where possible, and provide Customer with information reasonably required to meet its legal obligations.

8.4 **Use Restrictions.** Breneth shall not sell, disclose or make Customer Data available to any third party, except to subcontractors bound by written obligations no less protective than this Section 8 and Section 9, or as required by law.

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY

9.1 **Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party (the "**Discloser**") to the other (the "**Recipient**") that is designated as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including the terms of this Agreement. Customer Data is Customer's Confidential Information.

9.2 **Obligations.** The Recipient shall: (a) use the Discloser's Confidential Information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement; (b) protect it with at least the degree of care it uses for its own confidential information of a similar nature, and no less than reasonable care; and (c) disclose it only to its and its Affiliates' employees, advisors and contractors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Section 9.

9.3 **Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can show: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Recipient; (b) was lawfully known to the Recipient without restriction before disclosure; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction; or (d) is independently developed without use of the Discloser's Confidential Information.

9.4 **Compelled Disclosure.** The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, provided it gives the Discloser prompt notice (where legally permitted) and reasonable assistance in seeking a protective order.

13.3 **Effect of Termination.** On expiry or termination: (a) all rights granted to Customer cease, subject to Section 7.4; (b) Customer shall pay all Fees accrued through the effective date of termination; and (c) if Customer terminates under Section 13.1 or 13.2, Breneth shall refund any prepaid Fees for the period after the effective date of termination.
