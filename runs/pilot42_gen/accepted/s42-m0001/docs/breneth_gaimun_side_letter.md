**Re: BrewOps Software License and Subscription Agreement: Pricing Terms**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter agreement (this "**Side Letter**") is entered into by Breneth Systems, Inc. ("**Breneth**") and Gaimun Brewing Company, LLC ("**Customer**") with reference to the Software License and Subscription Agreement dated November 2, 2026 between Breneth and Customer (together with the Order Form and the Service Level Schedule, the "**Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given in the Agreement.

---

## RECITALS

A. Breneth and Customer have entered into the Agreement, under which Breneth provides Customer with access to the BrewOps Platform, including the batch and recipe tracking, TTB excise reporting, inventory management and distributor ordering modules, under Order Form No. 001.

B. Customer has informed Breneth that on September 30, 2026 Customer entered into a merger agreement under which Customer will become a wholly owned subsidiary of Ostrander Beverage Holdings LLC, with closing expected in the first quarter of 2027. Customer anticipates that, following closing, its production volumes and the number of personnel using the Platform may increase.

C. The Parties wish to record certain pricing terms for the Initial Subscription Term and for any expansion of Customer's use of the Platform.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

---

## 1. SUBSCRIPTION FEE PRICE HOLD

1.1 **Fixed Annual Fee.** The Annual Subscription Fee of $186,000 per year, invoiced annually in advance, as stated in the Order Form, shall remain fixed for each year of the Initial Subscription Term. Breneth shall not increase the Subscription Fees for the Platform modules and Authorized User count set out in Order Form No. 001 during the Initial Subscription Term.

1.2 **First Invoice.** Breneth shall issue the first annual invoice for Subscription Fees on or after the Subscription Start Date, and that invoice shall be payable in accordance with Section 4.2 of the Agreement.

---

## 2. IMPLEMENTATION AND ONBOARDING

2.1 **Waiver of Implementation Fees.** Breneth waives any implementation, onboarding, data migration or initial configuration fee that would otherwise apply to Customer's initial deployment of the Platform under Order Form No. 001.

2.2 **Onboarding Services.** The waiver in Section 2.1 covers Breneth's standard onboarding services, including import of Customer's existing recipe, batch and inventory records, configuration of the TTB excise reporting module, and up to twenty (20) hours of remote training for Authorized Users, to be completed before the Subscription Start Date.

---

## 3. EXPANSION PRICING

3.1 **Additional Authorized Users.** If Customer requires Authorized Users beyond the number stated in Order Form No. 001 during the Initial Subscription Term, Breneth shall provide them at a price per additional Authorized User no higher than the per-user rate implied by the Subscription Fees in Order Form No. 001, prorated for the remainder of the then-current subscription year.

3.2 **Additional Production Facilities.** If Customer adds a brewing or packaging facility that it owns or operates to its use of the Platform during the Initial Subscription Term, Breneth shall offer the Platform for that facility at a discount of fifteen percent (15%) from Breneth's then-current list price for a facility of comparable size.

3.3 **Ordering.** Any additional Authorized Users or facilities under this Section 3 shall be added by a further Order Form executed by both Parties.

---
