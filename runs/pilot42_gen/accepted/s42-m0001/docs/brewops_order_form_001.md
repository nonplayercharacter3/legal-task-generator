## 2. SUBSCRIPTION TERM

2.1 **Subscription Start Date.** The Subscription Start Date is January 1, 2027.

2.2 **Initial Subscription Term.** The Initial Subscription Term is 60 months, starting on the Subscription Start Date.

2.3 **Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Subscription Term, the subscription renews in accordance with Section 3.2 of the Agreement. Subscription Fees for any Renewal Term are determined under Section 3.3 of the Agreement.

---

## 3. FEES

3.1 **Annual Subscription Fee.** The Annual Subscription Fee is $186,000 per year, invoiced annually in advance.

3.2 **Invoicing.** Breneth shall issue the invoice for each subscription year on or after the first day of that subscription year. The first subscription year begins on the Subscription Start Date. Each subsequent subscription year begins on the anniversary of the Subscription Start Date.

3.3 **Payment Terms.** Invoices are payable in accordance with Section 4.2 of the Agreement. Section 4.3 of the Agreement governs disputed amounts.

3.4 **Implementation and Onboarding.** Implementation and onboarding services for the initial deployment of the Platform are as set out in the Side Letter between the Parties dated November 2, 2026.

3.5 **Taxes.** Fees are exclusive of taxes. Section 4.5 of the Agreement governs taxes.

---

## 4. SUBSCRIBED PLATFORM MODULES

Customer subscribes to the following BrewOps Platform modules. Each module is included in the Annual Subscription Fee:

| **Module** | **Description** |
|---|---|
| Batch and Recipe Tracking | Recipe and formulation management, brew logs, batch records, fermentation and packaging tracking |
| TTB Excise Reporting | Generation of excise and operational reports from Customer Data in TTB report formats, for Customer's review and submission |
| Inventory Management | Raw materials, packaging and finished-goods inventory, including tank and keg tracking |
| Distributor Ordering | Distributor order intake, pricing tables, order status and fulfillment records |

## 5. AUTHORIZED USERS AND SITES

5.1 **Authorized Users.** Customer may permit up to twenty-five (25) Authorized Users.

5.3 **Additional Users or Sites.** Any additional Authorized Users or facilities shall be added by a further Order Form executed by both Parties.
