# KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT

This KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of September 21, 2026, by and between **Kiveth Interactive, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), and **Odalys Brennt**, an individual ("**Executive**"). The Company and Executive are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. The Company and Zerex Forge Studios, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Zerex**"), have entered into an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of September 21, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, the Company will acquire the assets of Zerex relating to the Ashfall Requiem franchise.

B. Executive is the studio creative director of Zerex. She has led the creative direction of the Ashfall Requiem Titles since their inception and has substantial knowledge of the Ashfall Requiem franchise, its development pipeline and its creative personnel.

C. The Company wishes to employ Executive, and Executive wishes to be employed by the Company, effective as of the Closing, on the terms of this Agreement. This Agreement is the "Brennt Employment Agreement" referred to in the Purchase Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

### 1.1 Defined Terms.

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. In addition, the following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Base Salary**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.1.

"**Board**" means the board of directors of the Company.

"**Cause**" has the meaning set forth in Section 6.3.

"**Claims**" means any and all claims, demands, actions, suits, arbitrations, investigations and proceedings, whether civil, criminal, administrative or investigative, and all losses, damages, liabilities, judgments, settlements, fines, penalties, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising from or relating to them.

"**Code**" means the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning set forth in Section 9.1.

"**Effective Date**" means the Closing Date under the Purchase Agreement.

"**Good Reason**" has the meaning set forth in Section 6.4.

"**Release**" has the meaning set forth in Section 7.3.

"**Restricted Period**" means the period of Executive's employment with the Company and the twelve (12) months following the termination of that employment for any reason.

"**Titles**" means the Ashfall Requiem Titles, as defined in the Purchase Agreement, and each other video game title in the Ashfall Requiem franchise developed, published or commercialized by the Company or any of its Affiliates during the Term.

"**Work Product**" has the meaning set forth in Section 10.1.

### 1.2 Interpretation.

In this Agreement: (a) "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation"; (b) references to Sections are to Sections of this Agreement; (c) the singular includes the plural and vice versa; and (d) references to "$" are to United States dollars. The headings are for convenience of reference only and do not affect the interpretation of this Agreement.

## 2. EMPLOYMENT; TERM

### 2.1 Effectiveness.

This Agreement becomes effective on the Effective Date. If the Purchase Agreement is terminated before the Closing occurs, this Agreement will automatically terminate and be void, and neither Party will have any liability or obligation under it. Until the Effective Date, Executive remains an employee of Zerex, and nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship between the Company and Executive before the Effective Date.

### 2.2 Employment.

Effective as of the Effective Date, the Company shall employ Executive, and Executive accepts employment with the Company, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

### 2.3 Term.

The term of Executive's employment under this Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until the third (3rd) anniversary of the Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 6. After the Initial Term, the term will automatically extend for successive one-year periods unless either Party gives the other written notice of non-extension at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term. The Initial Term and any extensions are the "**Term**." A notice of non-extension by the Company is a termination without Cause effective at the end of the then-current Term.

## 3. POSITION AND DUTIES

### 3.1 Position.

During the Term, Executive shall serve as Franchise Creative Director, Ashfall Requiem, of the Company. Executive shall report to the Company's Chief Creative Officer.

### 3.2 Duties.

Executive shall have the duties, authority and responsibilities customary for a franchise creative director of a video game publisher of similar size and scope. These include: (a) leading the creative vision, narrative direction and art direction of the Titles; (b) directing the creative leads of the studio team responsible for the Titles; (c) collaborating with the Company's production, engineering and marketing functions on development milestones and release plans; (d) serving as the principal creative liaison with Vantrell Comics Group, LLC in connection with approvals under the Character License Agreement, to the extent the Character License Agreement has been assigned to the Company; and (e) such other duties consistent with her position as the Chief Creative Officer may reasonably assign from time to time.

### 3.3 Exclusive Service.

During the Term, Executive shall devote substantially all of her business time, attention and skill to the business of the Company and its Affiliates. Executive may (a) serve on the boards of civic, charitable and professional organizations, (b) speak at industry conferences and teach, and (c) manage her personal investments, in each case so long as those activities do not materially interfere with her duties under this Agreement or breach Sections 9 through 11. Executive may not serve on the board of any for-profit entity without the prior written consent of the Chief Creative Officer.

### 3.4 Place of Performance.

Executive's principal place of employment will be the Company's studio in Austin, Texas, or such other location within the Austin metropolitan area as the Company may designate. Executive acknowledges that her duties will require reasonable business travel, including to the Company's headquarters in San Mateo, California.

### 3.5 Company Policies.

Executive shall comply with the Company's written policies and codes of conduct, as in effect from time to time and made available to her, to the extent they do not conflict with this Agreement.

## 4. COMPENSATION

### 4.1 Base Salary.

The Company shall pay Executive an annual base salary of $410,000 (the "**Base Salary**"), payable in accordance with the Company's customary payroll practices, less applicable withholdings and deductions. The Compensation Committee of the Board shall review the Base Salary at least annually and may increase it, but not decrease it, during the Term.

### 4.2 Annual Bonus.

For each fiscal year of the Company ending during the Term, Executive will be eligible to receive an annual cash bonus with a target amount equal to fifty percent (50%) of the Base Salary in effect at the end of that fiscal year (the "**Target Bonus**"). The actual bonus will be determined by the Compensation Committee based on the achievement of individual and franchise performance objectives that the Company shall establish after consultation with Executive within ninety (90) days after the start of each fiscal year. For the fiscal year in which the Effective Date occurs, the bonus will be prorated based on the number of days of employment during that fiscal year. Any annual bonus will be paid no later than March 15 of the year following the fiscal year to which it relates, and, except as provided in Section 7, Executive must be employed on the payment date to receive it.

### 4.3 Retention Bonus.

The Company shall pay Executive a retention bonus of $1,200,000 (the "**Retention Bonus**") in three equal installments of $400,000. Each installment is payable within thirty (30) days after each of the first, second and third anniversaries of the Effective Date, provided Executive remains employed by the Company on the applicable anniversary. If the Company terminates Executive's employment without Cause, or Executive resigns for Good Reason, all unpaid installments of the Retention Bonus will be paid in a lump sum within sixty (60) days after the termination date, subject to Section 7.3.

### 4.4 Equity Award.

Subject to approval by the Compensation Committee of the board of directors of Pellucid Systems Co., Executive will be granted, within ninety (90) days after the Effective Date, restricted stock units with a grant-date value of $1,500,000 under the applicable equity incentive plan of Pellucid Systems Co. (the "**RSU Award**"). The RSU Award will vest in four equal annual installments beginning on the first anniversary of the Effective Date, subject to Executive's continued employment. It will be governed by the terms of the applicable plan and award agreement, except that Section 7.2(d) of this Agreement applies to the RSU Award.

### 4.5 Franchise Performance Award.

Executive will be eligible for a franchise performance award equal to one-half of one percent (0.5%) of the Net Franchise Revenue of *Ashfall Requiem II* in the first twenty-four (24) months after its commercial release, payable within ninety (90) days after the end of that period, provided Executive is employed by the Company on the commercial release date. "**Net Franchise Revenue**" means gross revenue recognized by the Company from sales of *Ashfall Requiem II* and its downloadable content, net of platform holder and storefront fees, refunds, chargebacks, sales taxes and royalties payable to third-party licensors, all as determined in accordance with the Company's accounting policies consistently applied.

## 5. BENEFITS AND EXPENSES

### 5.1 Benefit Plans.

During the Term, Executive is eligible to participate in the employee benefit plans, practices and programs maintained by the Company for its senior employees generally, in accordance with their terms as in effect from time to time. The Company may amend or terminate any such plan in accordance with its terms.

### 5.2 Service Credit.

For purposes of eligibility, vesting and paid time off accrual under the Company's benefit plans and policies (but not for benefit accrual under any defined benefit pension plan), Executive will be credited with her service with Zerex beginning on May 14, 2018, to the extent permitted by the applicable plan.

### 5.3 Paid Time Off.

Executive is entitled to paid time off in accordance with the Company's policy for senior employees, but not less than twenty-five (25) days per calendar year, prorated for partial years.

### 5.4 Business Expenses.

The Company shall reimburse Executive for all reasonable and documented business expenses incurred in the performance of her duties, in accordance with the Company's expense reimbursement policy as in effect from time to time.

## 6. TERMINATION OF EMPLOYMENT

### 6.1 General.

Executive's employment may be terminated during the Term by either Party at any time, subject to the notice requirements and payment obligations of this Section 6 and Section 7. On termination, Executive will be deemed to have resigned from all offices and directorships held with the Company and its Affiliates.

### 6.2 Death or Disability.

Executive's employment terminates automatically on her death. The Company may terminate Executive's employment on written notice if Executive has been unable, because of physical or mental illness or incapacity, to perform the essential functions of her position, with or without reasonable accommodation, for ninety (90) consecutive days or one hundred twenty (120) days in any twelve-month period ("**Disability**").

### 6.3 Termination by the Company for Cause.

The Company may terminate Executive's employment for Cause. "**Cause**" means Executive's: (a) conviction of, or plea of guilty or nolo contendere to, a felony or any crime involving fraud or dishonesty; (b) willful misconduct or gross negligence in the performance of her duties that causes material harm to the Company; (c) material breach of Section 9, 10 or 11; (d) willful and continued failure to perform her material duties, other than due to Disability, after written notice from the Company specifying the failure; or (e) material violation of the Company's written policies on harassment, discrimination or workplace conduct. For Cause under clause (c), (d) or (e), the Company must first give Executive written notice describing the conduct in reasonable detail, and, if the conduct is curable, Executive must have failed to cure it within thirty (30) days after receiving the notice.

### 6.4 Resignation by Executive for Good Reason.

Executive may resign for Good Reason. "**Good Reason**" means the occurrence of any of the following without Executive's written consent: (a) a material reduction in the Base Salary or Target Bonus; (b) a material diminution in Executive's title, authority, duties or responsibilities, including her removal from creative leadership of the Titles; (c) a relocation of Executive's principal place of employment by more than fifty (50) miles from Austin, Texas; or (d) a material breach of this Agreement by the Company. Good Reason exists only if Executive gives the Company written notice within sixty (60) days after the initial occurrence of the condition, the Company fails to cure the condition within thirty (30) days after receiving that notice, and Executive resigns within thirty (30) days after the end of the cure period.

### 6.5 Termination Without Cause; Resignation Without Good Reason.

The Company may terminate Executive's employment without Cause on thirty (30) days' written notice, or pay in lieu of notice. Executive may resign without Good Reason on sixty (60) days' written notice. The Company may waive all or part of that notice period, in which case the employment will end on the date the Company designates, and the Company shall pay Base Salary for the waived period.

## 7. PAYMENTS ON TERMINATION

### 7.1 Accrued Obligations.

On any termination of employment, the Company shall pay Executive: (a) earned but unpaid Base Salary through the termination date; (b) any annual bonus earned for a completed fiscal year but not yet paid; (c) accrued but unused paid time off, to the extent payable under Company policy; (d) unreimbursed business expenses properly incurred; and (e) vested benefits under the Company's benefit plans in accordance with their terms (together, the "**Accrued Obligations**").

### 7.2 Termination Without Cause or for Good Reason.

If the Company terminates Executive's employment without Cause, or Executive resigns for Good Reason, then in addition to the Accrued Obligations, and subject to Section 7.3, the Company shall:

(a) pay Executive an amount equal to twelve (12) months of the Base Salary then in effect, payable in substantially equal installments under the Company's payroll practices over the twelve (12) months following the termination date;

(b) pay Executive a prorated Target Bonus for the fiscal year of termination, payable when annual bonuses are paid to the Company's other senior employees;

(c) pay the unpaid installments of the Retention Bonus in accordance with Section 4.3;

(d) accelerate the vesting of the portion of the RSU Award that would have vested in the twelve (12) months following the termination date; and

(e) if Executive timely elects COBRA continuation coverage, pay or reimburse the employer portion of the premiums for that coverage for up to twelve (12) months following the termination date, or until Executive becomes eligible for group health coverage from a subsequent employer, if earlier.

### 7.3 Release.

The payments and benefits in Section 7.2 are conditioned on Executive executing, and not revoking, a general release of claims in favor of the Company and its Affiliates in a form reasonably acceptable to the Company (the "**Release**"), within sixty (60) days after the termination date. The Release will not release Executive's rights to the Accrued Obligations, her rights under Section 8, her rights as a holder of vested equity, or her rights under any directors' and officers' liability insurance policy.

### 7.4 Other Terminations.

On a termination for Cause, a resignation without Good Reason, or a termination due to death or Disability, the Company shall have no obligation to Executive other than the Accrued Obligations. On a termination due to death or Disability, the Company shall also pay the prorated Target Bonus described in Section 7.2(b).

### 7.5 No Mitigation.

Executive is not required to mitigate the amount of any payment under this Section 7 by seeking other employment, and no payment will be reduced by any compensation earned by Executive from subsequent employment.

## 8. INDEMNIFICATION AND INSURANCE

### 8.1 Indemnification as an Officer.

To the fullest extent permitted by the Company's certificate of incorporation, bylaws and applicable Law, the Company shall indemnify and hold harmless Executive in connection with any Claim to which Executive is made a party, or is threatened to be made a party, by reason of the fact that she is or was an officer or employee of the Company or any of its Affiliates, or is or was serving at the Company's request as a director, officer, employee or agent of another Person. The Company shall advance expenses to Executive in connection with any such Claim on the same basis as it advances expenses to its other officers.

### 8.2 Development of the Titles.

The Company shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Executive from and against any and all Claims arising out of or relating to the development of the Titles, including any Claim by a third party alleging that any Title, or any code, art asset, audio asset, narrative material, character, tool or other element of any Title, infringes, misappropriates or otherwise violates the Intellectual Property of that third party, in each case whether arising before or after the Effective Date and without limitation as to amount. The Company shall advance to Executive the reasonable costs of defending any such Claim as they are incurred, within thirty (30) days after receipt of reasonable supporting documentation.

### 8.3 Procedures.

Executive shall give the Company prompt written notice of any Claim for which she seeks indemnification under this Section 8, although any delay in giving notice will relieve the Company of its obligations only to the extent the Company is materially prejudiced by the delay. The Company may assume the defense of the Claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to Executive. Executive may engage separate counsel at the Company's expense if counsel for the Company advises that a conflict of interest exists between the Company and Executive. The Company shall not settle any Claim in a manner that imposes any obligation on Executive or admits any wrongdoing by her without her prior written consent. Executive shall cooperate reasonably with the Company in the defense of any Claim.

### 8.4 Insurance.

During the Term and for six (6) years thereafter, the Company shall cause Executive to be covered under the directors' and officers' liability insurance policies maintained by the Company or its Affiliates, on terms no less favorable than those provided to the Company's other officers.

### 8.5 Survival.

This Section 8 survives the termination of Executive's employment and of this Agreement.

## 9. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

### 9.1 Definition.

"**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information concerning the business of the Company and its Affiliates, including the Titles, unreleased content, source code, design documents, development schedules, engine and tools technology, player data, business plans, financial information, licensing terms and personnel information. Confidential Information includes non-public information relating to the Business acquired by the Company under the Purchase Agreement. It does not include information that is or becomes generally available to the public other than through Executive's breach of this Agreement.

### 9.2 Nondisclosure.

During and after the Term, Executive shall not use or disclose any Confidential Information, except as required in the performance of her duties for the Company or as required by Law. If Executive is required by Law to disclose Confidential Information, she shall, to the extent legally permitted, give the Company prompt notice so that the Company may seek a protective order.

### 9.3 Protected Activity.

Nothing in this Agreement prohibits Executive from reporting possible violations of Law to any Governmental Authority, making disclosures protected under whistleblower provisions of applicable Law, or discussing wages, hours or other terms and conditions of employment to the extent protected by applicable Law. Under the Defend Trade Secrets Act of 2016, Executive will not be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret Law for the disclosure of a trade secret made in confidence to a government official or an attorney solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of Law, or made in a complaint or other document filed under seal in a lawsuit or other proceeding.

### 9.4 Return of Property.

On termination of employment, or at any earlier time at the Company's request, Executive shall return to the Company all property of the Company and its Affiliates in her possession, including devkits, laptops, storage devices, documents and files, and shall permanently delete Confidential Information from any personal devices and accounts after providing copies to the Company.

## 10. WORK PRODUCT AND INVENTIONS

### 10.1 Assignment.

Executive agrees that all inventions, works of authorship, designs, characters, storylines, art, code, tools, know-how and other work product that she creates, conceives or reduces to practice, alone or with others, during the Term and within the scope of her employment or using the Company's resources (the "**Work Product**") are the sole property of the Company. To the extent any Work Product is not a work made for hire, Executive hereby assigns to the Company all of her right, title and interest in it, including all Intellectual Property in it.

### 10.2 Moral Rights.

To the extent permitted by Law, Executive waives, and agrees not to assert, any moral rights or rights of attribution or integrity in any Work Product. The Company shall credit Executive in the credits of each Title on which she serves as creative director, consistent with industry practice and the Company's crediting policy.

### 10.3 Excluded Inventions.

This Section 10 does not apply to any invention that Executive develops entirely on her own time, without using the Company's equipment, supplies, facilities or Confidential Information, and that does not relate to the Company's business or its actual or demonstrably anticipated research or development, and does not result from any work performed by Executive for the Company. Executive has listed on Exhibit A any personal projects she wishes to exclude from this Section 10.

### 10.4 Further Assurances.

During and after the Term, Executive shall execute such documents and take such actions as the Company reasonably requests, at the Company's expense, to perfect, register or enforce the Company's rights in the Work Product.

## 11. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

### 11.1 Non-Solicitation of Employees.

During the Restricted Period, Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, recruit or induce any employee of the Company or its Affiliates who worked on the Titles during the twelve (12) months before the solicitation to leave employment with the Company. General advertisements not targeted at those employees do not violate this Section 11.1.

### 11.2 Non-Interference.

During the Restricted Period, Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, induce or attempt to induce any licensor, platform holder, contractor or vendor of the Company or its Affiliates relating to the Titles to terminate or adversely modify its relationship with the Company.

### 11.3 Franchise Non-Competition.

During the Restricted Period, Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, develop, direct or provide creative services for any video game that uses the characters, settings, storylines or trademarks of the Ashfall Requiem franchise, other than for the Company or its Affiliates.

### 11.4 Non-Disparagement.

During and after the Term, Executive shall not make any public statement that disparages the Company, its Affiliates or the Titles, and the Company shall instruct its senior officers not to make any public statement that disparages Executive. This Section 11.4 does not restrict truthful testimony, protected activity under Section 9.3, or ordinary competitive statements.

### 11.5 Reasonableness; Reformation.

Executive acknowledges that the restrictions in this Section 11 are reasonable in scope and duration and are necessary to protect the Company's goodwill and Confidential Information, including the goodwill acquired under the Purchase Agreement. If any court determines that any restriction is unenforceable, the court may reform it to the maximum extent enforceable.

### 11.6 Remedies.

Executive agrees that a breach of Section 9, 10 or 11 would cause the Company irreparable harm, and that the Company is entitled to seek injunctive relief to enforce those Sections, in addition to any other remedy available to it.

## 12. MISCELLANEOUS

All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and will be deemed given when delivered by hand, one Business Day after being sent by a nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission during normal business hours. Notices to the Company shall be sent to Kiveth Interactive, Inc., 875 Mariner Point Drive, San Mateo, California 94404, Attention: General Counsel, Email: legalnotices@kiveth.com. Notices to Executive shall be sent to the most recent home address and personal email address on file with the Company.

### 12.2 Withholding.

### 12.3 Section 409A.

The Parties intend that the payments and benefits under this Agreement be exempt from, or comply with, Section 409A of the Code, and this Agreement shall be interpreted accordingly. Each installment payment is a separate payment for purposes of Section 409A. If Executive is a "specified employee" on her separation from service, any payment of deferred compensation that would otherwise be paid within six (6) months after that separation will instead be paid on the first business day after that six-month period, to the extent required to avoid additional tax under Section 409A. Any reimbursement under this Agreement will be paid no later than the end of the calendar year following the year in which the expense was incurred.

### 12.4 Section 280G.

If any payment or benefit to Executive would be subject to the excise tax under Section 4999 of the Code, it will be either paid in full or reduced to the extent necessary to avoid that excise tax, whichever results in the greater after-tax amount to Executive.

### 12.8 Jurisdiction.

### 12.9 Severability.
