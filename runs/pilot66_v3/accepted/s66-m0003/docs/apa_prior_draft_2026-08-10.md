# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**DRAFT: August 10, 2026**
*Confidential. For discussion purposes only. Not an offer capable of acceptance. This draft remains subject to Buyer's continuing due diligence and to review by all parties and their counsel. No agreement exists until this Agreement is executed and delivered by all parties.*

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026 (the "**Agreement Date**"), by and between **Kiveth Interactive, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and **Zerex Forge Studios, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller is an independent video game development studio. It owns, develops and exploits the interactive entertainment franchise known as "Ashfall Requiem," including the related source code, object code, development tools, art assets, trademarks and the contracts used in that business (the "**Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell, assign and transfer to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase and assume from Seller, the Purchased Assets and the Assumed Liabilities, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

C. At the Closing, Buyer and Odalys Brennt, Seller's studio creative director, will enter into a key employee agreement under which Ms. Brennt will become employed by Buyer effective as of the Closing (the "**Brennt Employment Agreement**").

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, representations, warranties and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

### 2.1 Purchase and Sale of Assets.

On the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement, at the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under all assets, properties and rights of every kind used or held for use in the Business, other than the Excluded Assets, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than Permitted Encumbrances (collectively, the "**Purchased Assets**"), including:

(a) the Ashfall Requiem Titles, including all builds, versions, master files and gold masters;

(b) all Seller Intellectual Property, including all source code, object code, art and audio assets, design documents, narrative materials and the Seller Tools, and all registrations and applications listed on Schedule 3.9(a) of the Disclosure Schedule;

(c) all Contracts listed on Schedule 2.1(c) of the Disclosure Schedule, including the Lumen Engine License and the Character License Agreement (the "**Assigned Contracts**");

(d) all development hardware, devkits, workstations, servers and other tangible personal property listed on Schedule 2.1(d) of the Disclosure Schedule;

(e) all accounts on digital storefronts, platform developer portals and online services relating to the Ashfall Requiem Titles, to the extent transferable;

(f) all books, records, bug databases, version control repositories and production schedules relating to the Business;

(g) all claims and causes of action of Seller against third parties relating to the Purchased Assets, including claims for past, present and future infringement of Seller Intellectual Property; and

(h) all goodwill associated with the Business and the Purchased Assets.

### 2.2 Excluded Assets.

The Purchased Assets do not include (collectively, the "**Excluded Assets**"): (a) cash and cash equivalents; (b) Contracts that are not Assigned Contracts; (c) Seller's organizational documents, minute books and Tax records; (d) Seller's insurance policies and rights under them; (e) Seller's employee benefit plans and assets attributable to them; (f) rights to Tax refunds for periods before the Closing; (g) the assets listed on Schedule 2.2 of the Disclosure Schedule; and (h) Seller's rights under this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements.

### 2.3 Assumed Liabilities.

At the Closing, Buyer shall assume only (a) the liabilities and obligations arising under the Assigned Contracts, to the extent required to be performed after the Closing, incurred in the ordinary course, and not relating to any breach or default by Seller on or before the Closing, and (b) the liabilities specifically listed on Schedule 2.3 of the Disclosure Schedule (collectively, the "**Assumed Liabilities**").

### 2.4 Excluded Liabilities.

Buyer shall not assume, and shall not be responsible for, any liabilities or obligations of Seller or its Affiliates of any kind, whether known or unknown, contingent or otherwise, other than the Assumed Liabilities (collectively, the "**Excluded Liabilities**"). The Excluded Liabilities include all liabilities arising out of or relating to: (a) the development, production, publication or sale of the Ashfall Requiem Titles or the operation of the Business before the Closing, including any claim that any Purchased Asset, as it existed or was exploited before the Closing, infringes or misappropriates the Intellectual Property of any Person; (b) Taxes of Seller or its members, and Taxes relating to the Business for any period ending on or before the Closing Date; (c) the employment or engagement of any current or former employee or contractor of Seller, to the extent arising on or before the Closing; (d) any Excluded Asset; (e) any action or proceeding pending or threatened as of the Closing, or arising from facts existing before the Closing; (f) any indebtedness of Seller; and (g) any breach or default by Seller under any Assigned Contract before the Closing.

### 2.5 Purchase Price.

The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $48,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities. At the Closing, Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price, less the Escrow Amount, to Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated by Seller in writing at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date.

### 2.6 Escrow.

At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit $4,850,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent, to be held and disbursed in accordance with the Escrow Agreement and this Agreement as security for the indemnification obligations of Seller under Article IX. Buyer and Seller shall each bear one-half of the fees and expenses of the Escrow Agent.

### 2.7 Non-Assignable Assets.

(a) This Agreement does not constitute an agreement to assign any Purchased Asset if an attempted assignment without a third party's consent would breach it, be ineffective or adversely affect Buyer's rights under it. If any such consent is not obtained before the Closing, the Closing shall nevertheless proceed, subject to Section 7.2(c), and Seller shall continue to hold that Purchased Asset.

(b) Following the Closing, Seller shall continue to use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain any such consent. Until it is obtained, Seller shall cooperate with Buyer in any lawful and commercially reasonable arrangement to provide Buyer the benefits of that Purchased Asset, and Buyer shall perform the related post-Closing obligations to the extent it receives those benefits. Once the consent is obtained, Seller shall assign the Purchased Asset to Buyer for no additional consideration.

### 2.8 Allocation of Purchase Price.

Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a schedule allocating the Purchase Price and the Assumed Liabilities among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended. If Seller does not object in writing within thirty (30) days after delivery, the schedule will be final. Any timely objection that the Parties cannot resolve within thirty (30) days will be resolved by an independent accounting firm mutually acceptable to the Parties, whose fees the Parties shall bear equally. The Parties shall file all Tax returns consistently with the final allocation.

### 2.9 Withholding.

Buyer and the Escrow Agent may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any amounts required to be withheld under applicable Tax Law. Amounts so withheld and paid over to the appropriate Governmental Authority are treated as having been paid to the Person in respect of which the withholding was made.

From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller, its Affiliates and their respective representatives from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to: (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement; (b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement; or (c) any Assumed Liability.

### 9.4 Indemnification Procedures.

(a) An indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party reasonably prompt written notice of any third-party claim, describing it in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice relieves the indemnifying party of its obligations only to the extent it is prejudiced.

(b) The indemnifying party may assume the defense of a third-party claim with counsel reasonably satisfactory to the indemnified party, except a claim seeking injunctive relief, a criminal proceeding, or a claim of Intellectual Property infringement that, if adversely determined, would reasonably be expected to impair the development or commercialization of any Ashfall Requiem Title.

(c) Neither party shall settle a third-party claim without the other's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, unless the settlement provides solely for money damages paid in full by the indemnifying party and includes a complete release of the indemnified party.

(d) Direct claims shall be asserted by written notice. The indemnifying party has thirty (30) days to respond, failing which it is deemed to have rejected the claim.

### 9.5 Limitations.

(a) *Cap.* The aggregate amount of all Losses for which Seller is liable under Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed $4,850,000, an amount equal to the Escrow Amount. The aggregate amount of all Losses for which Buyer is liable under Section 9.3(a) shall not exceed the same amount.

(b) *Exclusions from Cap.* The limitation in Section 9.5(a) does not apply to Losses arising out of (i) Fraud, (ii) any inaccuracy in or breach of any Fundamental Representation, or (iii) any Excluded Liability. Seller's aggregate liability for Losses described in clause (ii) shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(c) *Deductible.* Seller is not liable under Section 9.2(a) until the aggregate Losses under that Section exceed $[●], and then only for Losses in excess of that amount. This deductible does not apply to Fraud or to breaches of Fundamental Representations.

(d) *Other Recoveries; Mitigation.* Indemnifiable Losses are reduced by insurance proceeds actually received, net of costs of recovery. No indemnified party may recover more than once for the same Loss. Each indemnified party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate Losses.

### 9.6 Manner of Payment.

Losses payable to a Buyer Indemnified Party shall be satisfied first from the Escrow Fund in accordance with the Escrow Agreement. To the extent the Escrow Fund is insufficient or has been released, or the Losses are not subject to Section 9.5(a), Seller shall pay them directly to Buyer by wire transfer within ten (10) Business Days after their final determination.

### 9.7 Exclusive Remedies.

From and after the Closing, this Article IX is the sole and exclusive remedy of the Parties for any breach of any representation, warranty or covenant in this Agreement, other than claims arising from Fraud and claims for specific performance or other equitable relief.

### 9.8 Tax Treatment.
