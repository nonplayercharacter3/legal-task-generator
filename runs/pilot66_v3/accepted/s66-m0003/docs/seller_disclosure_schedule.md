## 14. Schedule 3.10(a): Engine, Middleware, Tools and Technology Licenses

### Item 1: Lumen Engine License

**Agreement:** Engine License Agreement dated January 1, 2021, between Lumen Engine Technologies, Inc. ("**Lumen**"), as licensor, and Seller, as licensee, as amended by Amendment No. 1 dated February 14, 2023.

**Licensed Technology:** The Lumen game engine, including runtime libraries, editor, rendering, animation and networking modules, platform support packages, and updates and upgrades released by Lumen during the term (the "**Lumen Engine**").

**Scope of License (Section 2):** A non-exclusive, worldwide license to use, modify and compile the Lumen Engine to develop, publish and commercialize the Ashfall Requiem Titles and any other titles designated by Seller under Section 2.4. Licensee owns all modifications made by it to the Lumen Engine source code. Lumen receives a non-exclusive license back to engine-level bug fixes submitted by Licensee through Lumen's developer portal.

**Term and Renewal (Section 13.1):** The initial term began on January 1, 2021 and ended on December 31, 2023. The license renewed for a first renewal term beginning January 1, 2024. The current term ends December 31, 2026. At the end of the current term, the license renews automatically for successive three-year renewal terms unless either party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least 120 days prior to the expiration of the then-current term.

**Royalties (Section 6 and Amendment No. 1):**

(a) During the current term, Licensee pays a royalty of four and one-half percent (4.5%) of Net Receipts (as defined in the license) from each title developed using the Lumen Engine, above a per-title threshold of $1,000,000 of Net Receipts per calendar quarter. Royalties are payable quarterly within forty-five (45) days after the end of each calendar quarter.

(b) For each contract year of any renewal term commencing on or after January 1, 2027, Licensee shall pay a minimum annual royalty guarantee of $1,800,000. The guarantee is payable in four equal quarterly installments in advance and is creditable against royalties earned in the same contract year. Any unrecouped portion is not carried forward and is not refundable.

**Post-Termination Rights (Section 13.4):** On expiration or termination, Licensee may continue to sell and support titles released before the effective date of expiration or termination, through the end of their commercial life. Royalties on those titles remain payable at the rate in effect at expiration, without any minimum guarantee. Licensee may not use the Lumen Engine to develop new titles or new content after expiration or termination.

**Assignment (Section 16.1):** Licensee may not assign the license, whether by sale of assets, merger, operation of law or otherwise, without Lumen's prior written consent. Lumen may not unreasonably withhold that consent for an assignment to a successor to the titles developed using the Lumen Engine.

**Notices (Section 17.2):** Notices to Lumen are to be sent to Lumen Engine Technologies, Inc., Attention: Licensing and Legal, by courier and email to the addresses in Section 17.2.

**Audit (Section 7.3):** Lumen may audit Licensee's royalty records once per calendar year on thirty (30) days' notice. The most recent audit, for calendar year 2024, was closed on April 22, 2025, without adjustment.
