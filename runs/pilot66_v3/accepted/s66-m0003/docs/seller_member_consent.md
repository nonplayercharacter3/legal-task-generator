# ACTION BY UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF MANAGERS AND THE MEMBERS OF ZEREX FORGE STUDIOS, LLC

**(a Delaware limited liability company)**

**Effective as of September 19, 2026**

---

## 1. Introduction

1.1 The undersigned are all of the managers (the "**Managers**," and together the "**Board of Managers**") and all of the members (the "**Members**") of **Zerex Forge Studios, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (the "**Company**"). Acting under Sections 18-302(d) and 18-404(d) of the Delaware Limited Liability Company Act, 6 Del. C. § 18-101 *et seq.* (the "**Act**"), and Sections 6.4 and 7.6 of the Amended and Restated Limited Liability Company Agreement of the Company dated as of March 1, 2021, as amended (the "**LLC Agreement**"), they consent to the adoption of the resolutions in this written consent (this "**Consent**"). They waive any requirement of a meeting, prior notice of a meeting, or any other formality that the Act or the LLC Agreement might otherwise require.

1.2 The Managers and the Members intend that this Consent have the same force and effect as a unanimous vote of the Board of Managers at a duly called meeting at which a quorum was present. They also intend that it have the same force and effect as a unanimous vote of the Members at a duly called meeting of the Members holding all of the outstanding Units (as defined in the LLC Agreement).

1.3 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Consent have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement (as defined below).

## 2. Recitals

2.1 The Company is an independent video game development studio. It owns, develops and exploits the interactive entertainment franchise known as "Ashfall Requiem," including the related source code, object code, development tools, art assets, trademarks and the contracts used in that business (the "**Business**").

2.2 Kiveth Interactive, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), has proposed to acquire substantially all of the assets of the Company used or held for use in the Business, and to assume certain specified liabilities of the Company, under an Asset Purchase Agreement between Buyer and the Company, to be dated as of September 21, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**").

2.3 The Purchase Agreement provides, among other things, that:

(a) the Company will sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver the Purchased Assets to Buyer, and Buyer will assume the Assumed Liabilities, on the terms and subject to the conditions of the Purchase Agreement (the "**Asset Sale**");

(b) the aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $48,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities;

(c) at the Closing, Buyer will deposit $4,850,000 of the Purchase Price (the "**Escrow Amount**") with Calder Trust Company, N.A., as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**"), to be held and disbursed under an escrow agreement among Buyer, the Company and the Escrow Agent (the "**Escrow Agreement**"), as security for the indemnification obligations of the Company under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement; and

(d) Buyer will pay the balance of the Purchase Price, after deduction of the Escrow Amount, to the Company at the Closing.

2.4 The Purchase Agreement contemplates that, at the Closing, the Company will execute and deliver the following agreements and instruments, together with the Escrow Agreement (collectively, the "**Ancillary Agreements**"):

(a) the Bill of Sale;

(b) the Assignment and Assumption Agreement;

(c) the IP Assignment Agreement; and

(d) the Transition Services Agreement, under which the Company will provide certain administrative and infrastructure services to Buyer for a transitional period.

2.5 The Purchase Agreement also contemplates that Buyer and Odalys Brennt, the Company's studio creative director, will enter into the Brennt Employment Agreement, under which Ms. Brennt will become employed by Buyer effective as of the Closing. The Company is not a party to the Brennt Employment Agreement.

2.6 Section 7.6(b) of the LLC Agreement provides that the Company may not sell, lease, exchange or otherwise dispose of all or substantially all of its assets, or of the assets of any material line of business, without the approval of the Board of Managers and the affirmative written consent of Members holding at least seventy-five percent (75%) of the outstanding Units. The Asset Sale is such a transaction.

2.7 The Board of Managers has reviewed the following, each in substantially final form:

(a) the execution version of the Purchase Agreement;

(b) the Seller Disclosure Schedule to be delivered by the Company concurrently with the execution of the Purchase Agreement (the "**Disclosure Schedule**"); and

(c) the forms of the Escrow Agreement and the other Ancillary Agreements available as of the date of this Consent.

The Board of Managers has also reviewed the terms of the Asset Sale with the Company's legal and financial advisors. It has considered the strategic alternatives available to the Company, the financial terms of the Asset Sale, and the effect of the Asset Sale on the Company, its Members, its employees and its counterparties. After this review, the Board of Managers has determined that the Asset Sale, on the terms of the Purchase Agreement, is advisable and in the best interests of the Company and its Members.

2.8 Copies of the execution version of the Purchase Agreement, the Disclosure Schedule and the forms of the Ancillary Agreements have been made available to each Member. Each Member has had the opportunity to ask questions of the Managers and of the Company's advisors about the Asset Sale.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Managers and the Members adopt the following resolutions:

## 3. Approval of the Asset Sale and the Purchase Agreement

3.1 **RESOLVED**, that the Asset Sale, on the terms and subject to the conditions of the Purchase Agreement, is approved, adopted and authorized in all respects, including for purposes of Section 7.6(b) of the LLC Agreement.

3.2 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the form, terms and provisions of the Purchase Agreement, substantially in the form of the execution version reviewed by the Board of Managers and made available to the Members, are approved, adopted and authorized in all respects. This approval extends to all exhibits and schedules to the Purchase Agreement and to the transactions it contemplates, including:

(a) the sale, assignment, transfer, conveyance and delivery to Buyer of the Purchased Assets;

(b) the assignment to Buyer of the Assigned Contracts;

(c) the assumption by Buyer of the Assumed Liabilities;

(d) the retention by the Company of the Excluded Assets and the Excluded Liabilities; and

(e) the Company's representations, warranties, covenants, agreements and indemnification obligations under the Purchase Agreement.

3.3 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Purchase Price of $48,500,000, plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities, is fair consideration for the Purchased Assets and is approved. The deposit of the Escrow Amount of $4,850,000 with the Escrow Agent at the Closing, in accordance with Section 2.6 of the Purchase Agreement, is also approved.

3.4 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Disclosure Schedule, substantially in the form reviewed by the Board of Managers, is approved. Each Authorized Officer (as defined in Section 4.1) is authorized to make such additions, deletions and changes to it before its delivery as that Authorized Officer, after consultation with the Company's counsel, determines are necessary to make it complete and accurate as of the Agreement Date.

## 4. Authorized Officers; Execution and Delivery

4.1 **RESOLVED**, that each of the following persons is designated an "**Authorized Officer**" for purposes of this Consent:

(a) Tobias Renwick, Managing Member of the Company; and

(b) Ilsa Marchetti, Chief Operating Officer of the Company.

4.2 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that each Authorized Officer, acting alone, is authorized, empowered and directed, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to execute and deliver the following:

(a) the Purchase Agreement, dated as of September 21, 2026, with such changes as the Authorized Officer executing it approves, that approval to be conclusively evidenced by that execution; and

(b) the Disclosure Schedule, concurrently with the execution of the Purchase Agreement.

The Managers and the Members intend that any changes so approved not materially and adversely alter the Purchase Price, the Escrow Amount or the scope of the Purchased Assets without further approval under this Consent.

4.3 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the forms, terms and provisions of each of the Ancillary Agreements are approved. Each Authorized Officer, acting alone, is authorized, empowered and directed, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to execute and deliver each Ancillary Agreement at or before the Closing, in a form consistent with the Purchase Agreement and with such changes as the Authorized Officer executing it approves, that approval to be conclusively evidenced by that execution. This includes:

(a) the Escrow Agreement among Buyer, the Company and Calder Trust Company, N.A., as Escrow Agent;

(b) the Bill of Sale;

(c) the Assignment and Assumption Agreement;

(d) the IP Assignment Agreement; and

(e) the Transition Services Agreement.

4.4 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that each Authorized Officer, acting alone, is authorized, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to execute and deliver each other certificate, instrument, notice, assignment and document that the Purchase Agreement or any Ancillary Agreement requires or contemplates. This includes:

(a) the officer's certificate contemplated by Section 5.2(d) of the Purchase Agreement, certifying this Consent and the satisfaction of the conditions in Sections 7.2(a) and 7.2(b) of the Purchase Agreement;

(b) an IRS Form W-9 of the Company;

(c) releases of all Encumbrances on the Purchased Assets other than Permitted Encumbrances;

(d) recordable assignments of the trademark registrations and applications, copyright registrations and domain names listed on Schedule 3.9(a) of the Disclosure Schedule, for filing with the applicable registries and offices; and

(e) such other customary instruments of transfer as Buyer may reasonably request under Section 5.2(i) or Section 6.13 of the Purchase Agreement.

4.5 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Authorized Officers are designated as the Company's Authorized Representatives under the Escrow Agreement. Each of them is authorized to do the following in the name and on behalf of the Company:

(a) execute and deliver Joint Written Instructions;

(b) respond to and deliver objections to Claim Notices;

(c) confirm funds-transfer instructions under the call-back procedures of the Escrow Agreement; and

(d) take all other action required or permitted of the Company under the Escrow Agreement.

## 5. Performance of Obligations Before and at Closing

5.1 **RESOLVED**, that from the Agreement Date until the Closing, the Authorized Officers are directed to cause the Company to conduct the Business in accordance with Section 6.1 of the Purchase Agreement. They are further directed to cause the Company to perform and comply with each of the covenants and agreements that the Purchase Agreement requires the Company to perform or comply with before or at the Closing.

5.2 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized and directed, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to take the following actions:

(a) to give all notices to, and to use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain all consents from, the third parties described on Schedule 3.3 of the Disclosure Schedule, in accordance with Section 6.3 of the Purchase Agreement;

(b) to obtain each Required Consent listed on Schedule 7.2(c) of the Disclosure Schedule, in form and substance reasonably satisfactory to Buyer, and to deliver copies of each Required Consent to Buyer at the Closing in accordance with Section 5.2(c) of the Purchase Agreement; and

(c) to deliver to Buyer at the Closing full and complete copies of all source code, build environments, version control repositories and asset libraries for the Ashfall Requiem Titles and the Seller Tools, in accordance with Section 5.2(h) of the Purchase Agreement.

5.3 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Authorized Officers are authorized to cause the Company to provide Buyer and its Representatives with the access to personnel, properties, books and records, source code repositories, Contracts and other documents and data relating to the Business required by Section 6.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

5.4 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that, in accordance with Section 6.4 of the Purchase Agreement, the Company and its Representatives shall not, from the Agreement Date until the earlier of the Closing and the termination of the Purchase Agreement, solicit, discuss, negotiate or enter into any agreement regarding any proposal from any Person other than Buyer to acquire the Business or any material portion of the Purchased Assets. The Authorized Officers are directed to inform the Company's Representatives of this restriction and to notify Buyer promptly of any such proposal the Company receives.

5.5 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Authorized Officers are authorized to obtain a certificate of good standing of the Company from the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware, dated as of a recent date before the Closing, for delivery under Section 5.2(e) of the Purchase Agreement.

## 6. Employees

6.1 **RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are authorized and directed to cooperate with Buyer in accordance with Section 6.6 of the Purchase Agreement in connection with any offers of employment Buyer may make to the employees of the Company listed on Schedule 3.11 of the Disclosure Schedule.

6.2 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Company shall, effective as of the Closing, terminate the employment of each Transferred Employee. The Company shall pay to each Transferred Employee all wages, bonuses, accrued paid time off and other amounts owed through the Closing, as required by Section 6.6(a) of the Purchase Agreement and applicable Law. The Authorized Officers are authorized to take all actions necessary to effect those terminations and payments, including the issuance of final paychecks and any required notices under applicable state Law.

6.3 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that, effective as of the Closing and in accordance with Section 6.6(b) of the Purchase Agreement, the Company waives any non-competition, non-solicitation or confidentiality restriction that would prevent any Transferred Employee, including Odalys Brennt, from performing services for Buyer. Each Authorized Officer is authorized to confirm that waiver in writing to Buyer or to any Transferred Employee on request.

6.4 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Managers and the Members acknowledge that Section 7.2(e) of the Purchase Agreement makes the execution of the Brennt Employment Agreement by Ms. Brennt a condition to Buyer's obligation to close. The Company shall not take any action intended to discourage Ms. Brennt, or any other employee, from accepting employment with Buyer.

## 7. Post-Closing Matters

7.1 **RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are authorized and directed to cause the Company to perform each of its covenants and agreements under the Purchase Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements that are to be performed after the Closing. These include:

(a) the confidentiality obligations in Section 6.7 of the Purchase Agreement;

(b) the obligation to remit payments relating to the Purchased Assets or the Business under Section 6.11 of the Purchase Agreement;

(c) the non-competition and non-solicitation covenants in Section 6.12 of the Purchase Agreement;

(d) the further assurances obligations in Section 6.13 of the Purchase Agreement; and

(e) the provision of services under the Transition Services Agreement.

7.2 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that, in accordance with Section 2.7 of the Purchase Agreement, if any Purchased Asset cannot be assigned at the Closing because a required third-party consent has not been obtained, the Authorized Officers are authorized and directed to do the following:

(a) cause the Company to continue to use commercially reasonable efforts after the Closing to obtain that consent;

(b) cause the Company to cooperate with Buyer in any lawful and commercially reasonable arrangement to provide Buyer the benefits of that Purchased Asset;

(c) cause the Company to pay over to Buyer all payments it receives in respect of that Purchased Asset after the Closing; and

(d) assign and transfer that Purchased Asset to Buyer, for no additional consideration, once the consent is obtained.

7.3 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that, in accordance with Section 6.14 of the Purchase Agreement, the Company shall cease all use of the name and mark "Ashfall Requiem," and any confusingly similar name or mark, within thirty (30) days after the Closing Date, except as required to refer historically to the Business. The Authorized Officers are directed to cause the Company to remove that name and mark from its general corporate materials within that period.

7.4 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Authorized Officers are authorized to review, and on behalf of the Company to accept or object to, the Allocation Schedule delivered by Buyer under Section 2.8 of the Purchase Agreement. They are also authorized to cause the Company to file all Tax returns consistently with the final Allocation Schedule.

7.5 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Authorized Officers are authorized to administer the Company's rights and obligations under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement on behalf of the Company. This authority includes:

(a) receiving and responding to claims for indemnification;

(b) assuming or participating in the defense of any Third-Party Claim where the Company is permitted to do so; and

(c) negotiating and settling claims, subject to the limitations of Section 9.4(c) of the Purchase Agreement.

The Authorized Officers shall report to the Board of Managers at least quarterly on the status of any pending claim. The Authorized Officers shall not settle any claim involving an amount in excess of $250,000 without the prior approval of the Board of Managers.

## 8. Tax Matters and Bulk Sales

8.1 **RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are authorized to cause the Company to do the following:

(a) bear one-half of all Transfer Taxes and fees incurred in connection with the Purchase Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements, as provided in Section 10.1 of the Purchase Agreement;

(b) timely file any Tax return or other document the Company is required by Law to file with respect to those Taxes and fees; and

(c) cooperate with Buyer in connection with Tax matters as contemplated by Sections 10.2 and 10.3 of the Purchase Agreement.

8.2 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the waiver of compliance with any bulk sales, bulk transfer or similar Laws in Section 6.10 of the Purchase Agreement is approved.

8.3 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Company shall bear one-half of the fees and expenses of the Escrow Agent, as provided in Section 2.6 of the Purchase Agreement and Section 8.1 of the Escrow Agreement. The Authorized Officers are authorized to pay those fees and expenses when due.

## 9. Fees and Expenses

9.1 **RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are authorized and directed to pay, on behalf of the Company, all fees and expenses incurred by the Company in connection with the Asset Sale, including the fees and disbursements of the Company's counsel, financial advisors and accountants, as each Authorized Officer considers necessary or appropriate. In accordance with Section 11.1 of the Purchase Agreement, each party bears its own costs and expenses whether or not the Closing occurs.

## 10. Termination

10.1 **RESOLVED**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized, on behalf of the Company, to exercise, or to refrain from exercising, any right of the Company to terminate the Purchase Agreement under Section 8.1 of the Purchase Agreement. The Authorized Officers shall first consult with the Board of Managers before giving any notice of termination, other than a termination by mutual written consent approved in advance by the Board of Managers.

## 11. General Authority

11.1 **RESOLVED**, that each Authorized Officer, acting alone, is authorized, empowered and directed, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to take all such further actions, and to execute, deliver and file all such further agreements, certificates, instruments, notices and documents, as that Authorized Officer determines to be necessary, appropriate or advisable to carry out the purposes and intent of the foregoing resolutions and to consummate the Asset Sale. The taking of any such action, or the execution, delivery or filing of any such document, will be conclusive evidence of that determination and of the authority conferred by this Consent.

11.2 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that any specific resolution that may be required by any Person to evidence the authority of the Company or the Authorized Officers in connection with the Asset Sale is adopted. This includes any resolution required by the Escrow Agent under its account-opening and know-your-customer procedures. Each Authorized Officer is authorized to certify that resolution as having been adopted by this Consent, and to insert it into the minute book of the Company following this Consent.

11.3 **RESOLVED FURTHER**, that all actions previously taken by any Manager, Member, officer, employee or agent of the Company in connection with the matters contemplated by the foregoing resolutions are ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects as the acts and deeds of the Company. This includes the negotiation of the Purchase Agreement, the Disclosure Schedule and the Ancillary Agreements, and the provision of access to the Company's data room, personnel and records to Buyer and its Representatives.
