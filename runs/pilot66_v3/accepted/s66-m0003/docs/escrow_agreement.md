# ESCROW AGREEMENT

This ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of ____________, 2026, by and among **Kiveth Interactive, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), **Zerex Forge Studios, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and **Calder Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller have entered into an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of September 21, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Seller will sell to Buyer the Purchased Assets relating to the Ashfall Requiem franchise, and Buyer will assume the Assumed Liabilities.

B. Section 2.6 of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit $4,850,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. The Escrow Amount secures the indemnification obligations of Seller under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

C. The Escrow Agent is willing to act as escrow agent, and to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Fund, on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

### 1.1 Defined Terms.

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. In addition, the following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which commercial banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning set forth in Section 5.1.

"**Closing Date**" means the date on which the Closing occurs under the Purchase Agreement. The Parties shall confirm the Closing Date to the Escrow Agent in the Joint Written Instruction delivered under Section 3.1.

"**Disputed Amount**" has the meaning set forth in Section 5.3.

"**Escrow Account**" means the segregated account established by the Escrow Agent under Section 3.2.

"**Escrow Fund**" means the Escrow Amount deposited with the Escrow Agent, together with all interest, dividends and other earnings on it, as reduced from time to time by disbursements made in accordance with this Agreement.

"**Escrow Release Date**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.1.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order, judgment or decree of a court of competent jurisdiction. An order is final and non-appealable if the time for appeal has expired and no appeal has been perfected. The Party presenting a Final Order must deliver it to the Escrow Agent together with a certificate of that Party's counsel to that effect, on which the Escrow Agent may conclusively rely.

"**Joint Written Instruction**" means a written instruction executed by an Authorized Representative of Buyer and an Authorized Representative of Seller and delivered to the Escrow Agent in accordance with Section 12.1.

"**Pending Claim Amount**" means, as of any date, the aggregate amount of all claims set forth in Claim Notices delivered before that date that have not been paid or finally resolved in accordance with this Agreement.

"**Authorized Representative**" means, for each of Buyer and Seller, the individuals designated on Exhibit A, as that Party may update from time to time by written notice to the Escrow Agent.

### 1.2 Interpretation.

In this Agreement: (a) "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation"; (b) references to Sections and Exhibits are to Sections of, and Exhibits to, this Agreement; (c) the singular includes the plural and vice versa; and (d) references to "$" are to United States dollars. The headings are for convenience of reference only and do not affect the interpretation of this Agreement.

## 2. APPOINTMENT OF ESCROW AGENT

### 2.1 Appointment.

Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent to act as escrow agent under this Agreement. The Escrow Agent accepts that appointment and agrees to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Fund in accordance with this Agreement.

### 2.2 Purpose.

The Escrow Fund secures the indemnification obligations of Seller to the Buyer Indemnified Parties under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement. The Escrow Fund is not a limitation on the amount of the Purchase Price, and its deposit does not constitute payment to Seller of any portion of the Purchase Price until it is released to Seller under this Agreement.

## 3. DEPOSIT AND INVESTMENT OF ESCROW FUND

### 3.1 Deposit.

At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by the Escrow Agent in writing. Concurrently with that deposit, Buyer and Seller shall deliver to the Escrow Agent a Joint Written Instruction confirming the Closing Date. The Escrow Agent shall promptly confirm receipt of the Escrow Amount to Buyer and Seller in writing.

### 3.2 Escrow Account.

The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Fund in a segregated account in its name as escrow agent for the benefit of Buyer and Seller. The Escrow Agent shall not commingle the Escrow Fund with any other funds held by it. The Escrow Fund is held in trust and is not subject to any lien, attachment, trustee process or other judicial process of any creditor of Buyer, Seller or the Escrow Agent.

### 3.3 Investment.

(a) The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Fund in the investment option selected by Buyer and Seller on Exhibit B, or in such other investment as Buyer and Seller may direct by Joint Written Instruction. Absent a written direction, the Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Fund in a non-interest-bearing deposit account insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation to the applicable limits.

(b) The Escrow Agent is not liable for any loss resulting from any investment made in accordance with this Section 3.3, or from any liquidation of an investment required to make a disbursement under this Agreement. The Escrow Agent has no obligation to invest or reinvest the Escrow Fund on the day of deposit if the funds are received after 11:00 a.m., Eastern Time.

(c) Buyer and Seller acknowledge that the Escrow Agent is not providing investment supervision, recommendations or advice.

### 3.4 Earnings.

All interest, dividends and other earnings on the Escrow Fund become part of the Escrow Fund and will be disbursed together with the principal in accordance with this Agreement.

## 4. RELEASE OF ESCROW FUND

### 4.1 Escrow Release Date.

On the date that is 15 months after the Closing Date (the "**Escrow Release Date**"), or if that date is not a Business Day, on the next Business Day, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Seller the then-remaining balance of the Escrow Fund, less the Pending Claim Amount as of the Escrow Release Date. Buyer and Seller shall deliver to the Escrow Agent, not later than five (5) Business Days before the Escrow Release Date, a Joint Written Instruction stating the Pending Claim Amount and directing the release. If the Parties fail to deliver that Joint Written Instruction, the Escrow Agent shall calculate the Pending Claim Amount from the Claim Notices received by it and not resolved in accordance with Section 5.

### 4.2 Retained Amounts.

The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold any portion of the Escrow Fund retained under Section 4.1 for a pending claim until that claim is resolved under Section 5. On resolution, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the retained amount as follows: (a) to Buyer, the amount (if any) payable to the Buyer Indemnified Parties in respect of that claim; and (b) to Seller, the balance of the amount retained for that claim.

### 4.3 Termination of Escrow.

When the entire Escrow Fund has been disbursed in accordance with this Agreement, this Agreement will terminate, except for Sections 7, 8, 10 and 12, which survive its termination and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

## 5. CLAIMS AGAINST THE ESCROW FUND

### 5.1 Claim Notices.

At any time before the Escrow Release Date, Buyer may deliver to Seller and the Escrow Agent a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**") asserting a claim for indemnification of a Buyer Indemnified Party under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement. Each Claim Notice must (a) describe the claim in reasonable detail, (b) state the amount of the Losses claimed or, if not then determinable, Buyer's good-faith estimate of them, and (c) identify the provision of the Purchase Agreement under which indemnification is sought. Buyer may amend a Claim Notice to update the amount claimed as additional information becomes available.

### 5.2 Undisputed Claims.

If Seller does not deliver to Buyer and the Escrow Agent a written objection to a Claim Notice within thirty (30) days after its receipt of that Claim Notice (the "**Objection Period**"), Seller will be deemed to have accepted the claim. On the first Business Day after the Objection Period expires, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Buyer, from the Escrow Fund, the amount set forth in that Claim Notice. However, if the Claim Notice states only an estimate, the Escrow Agent shall hold that amount until the Parties deliver a Joint Written Instruction or a Final Order fixes the amount.

### 5.3 Disputed Claims.

If Seller delivers a written objection to all or any portion of a claim within the Objection Period, the objection must state in reasonable detail the basis for the objection and the amount disputed (the "**Disputed Amount**"). The Escrow Agent shall disburse to Buyer, promptly after receipt of the objection, any portion of the claim that is not a Disputed Amount. The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the Disputed Amount until it receives either (a) a Joint Written Instruction directing the disbursement of the Disputed Amount, or (b) a Final Order determining the disposition of the Disputed Amount. On receipt of either, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the Disputed Amount as directed, within three (3) Business Days.

### 5.4 Negotiation.

During the thirty (30) days after the delivery of an objection under Section 5.3, Buyer and Seller shall negotiate in good faith to resolve the Disputed Amount. If they do not resolve it within that period, either Party may submit the dispute to the courts described in Section 12.6.

### 5.5 Third-Party Claims.

The defense and settlement of any Third-Party Claim that is the subject of a Claim Notice will be conducted in accordance with Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement. The Escrow Agent has no duty to monitor, and no responsibility for, the conduct of any Third-Party Claim.

## 6. DISBURSEMENTS

### 6.1 Disbursement Instructions.

The Escrow Agent shall disburse funds from the Escrow Fund only (a) in accordance with a Joint Written Instruction, (b) in accordance with a Final Order, or (c) as expressly provided in Sections 4 and 5. Each disbursement shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by the recipient Party on Exhibit C, or to such other account as that Party may designate by written notice to the Escrow Agent, subject to the call-back procedures in Section 6.4.

### 6.2 Source of Recovery.

The Escrow Fund shall be the sole and exclusive source of recovery for all Losses of the Buyer Indemnified Parties under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement. Buyer shall deliver all claims of the Buyer Indemnified Parties under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement to the Escrow Agent and Seller by Claim Notice in accordance with Section 5.1, and the Escrow Agent shall satisfy those claims from the Escrow Fund in accordance with Sections 4 and 5.

### 6.3 Insufficient Funds.

If the Escrow Fund is insufficient to satisfy in full any disbursement directed under this Agreement, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the amount then available in the Escrow Fund and shall have no further obligation in respect of that disbursement. If more than one claim is then payable, the Escrow Agent shall satisfy them in the order in which the related Claim Notices were received.

### 6.4 Security Procedures.

If the Escrow Agent receives any funds-transfer instruction other than one establishing the account details set forth on Exhibit C at the time this Agreement is executed, the Escrow Agent shall confirm the instruction by telephone call-back to an Authorized Representative of the instructing Party at the telephone number set forth on Exhibit A. The Escrow Agent may rely on the confirmation of any person it reasonably believes to be an Authorized Representative. The Escrow Agent may delay a disbursement until it has completed the call-back procedure. Buyer and Seller acknowledge that these procedures are commercially reasonable.

### 6.5 Timing.

Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, the Escrow Agent shall make each disbursement within three (3) Business Days after receipt of the Joint Written Instruction or Final Order directing it, provided that the instruction or order is received before 11:00 a.m., Eastern Time, on a Business Day. Instructions received after that time will be deemed received on the next Business Day.

## 7. DUTIES OF THE ESCROW AGENT

### 7.1 Scope of Duties.

The Escrow Agent shall have only the duties expressly set forth in this Agreement, which are ministerial in nature. No implied duties or obligations shall be read into this Agreement against the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent is not a party to the Purchase Agreement and has no duty to know or inquire into its terms. The Escrow Agent shall not be required to determine whether any Party has complied with the Purchase Agreement.

### 7.2 Reliance.

The Escrow Agent may rely, and shall be protected in acting or refraining from acting, on any written notice, instruction, certificate, Final Order or other document that it in good faith believes to be genuine and signed or presented by the proper Person. The Escrow Agent has no duty to inquire into or investigate the validity, accuracy or content of any such document.

### 7.3 Limitation of Liability.

The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any action taken or omitted by it in good faith, except to the extent that a court of competent jurisdiction determines that the Escrow Agent's gross negligence or willful misconduct was the primary cause of any loss to Buyer or Seller. In no event shall the Escrow Agent be liable for indirect, incidental, special, punitive or consequential loss or damage of any kind, including lost profits, even if it has been advised of the likelihood of that loss or damage.

### 7.4 Counsel.

The Escrow Agent may consult with counsel of its choice. It shall be fully protected in any action taken or omitted in good faith in accordance with the advice of that counsel.

### 7.5 Conflicting Demands.

If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting instructions or claims with respect to the Escrow Fund, or is in good-faith doubt as to its duties, it may refrain from taking any action other than retaining the Escrow Fund until it receives a Joint Written Instruction or a Final Order resolving the conflict. Alternatively, the Escrow Agent may file an action in interpleader in the courts described in Section 12.6 and deposit the Escrow Fund with the clerk of that court, after which it will be relieved of all further obligations with respect to the amounts deposited.

### 7.6 Force Majeure.

The Escrow Agent shall not be responsible or liable for any failure or delay in performing its obligations under this Agreement caused by forces beyond its reasonable control. These include strikes, work stoppages, accidents, acts of war or terrorism, civil disturbances, nuclear or natural catastrophes, epidemics, interruptions or failures of utilities, communications or computer systems, and acts of any Governmental Authority. The Escrow Agent shall use commercially reasonable efforts consistent with accepted practices in the banking industry to resume performance as soon as practicable.

### 7.7 Statements.

The Escrow Agent shall provide Buyer and Seller with a monthly statement showing the balance of the Escrow Fund, all deposits, investments, earnings and disbursements during the period. Each statement will be deemed accepted by Buyer and Seller unless either of them objects in writing within ninety (90) days after its delivery.

## 8. FEES AND INDEMNIFICATION OF THE ESCROW AGENT

### 8.1 Fees and Expenses.

Buyer and Seller shall each pay one-half of the fees of the Escrow Agent set forth on Exhibit D, together with one-half of its reasonable and documented out-of-pocket expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, incurred in the performance of its duties under this Agreement. The Escrow Agent's fees and expenses shall not be paid from the Escrow Fund unless Buyer and Seller so direct by Joint Written Instruction. As between Buyer and Seller, any Party that pays more than its one-half share is entitled to reimbursement from the other Party.

### 8.2 Indemnification.

Buyer and Seller shall jointly and severally indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Escrow Agent and its directors, officers, employees and agents from and against any and all losses, claims, damages, liabilities and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the Escrow Agent's performance of its duties under it. This indemnity does not apply to the extent a court of competent jurisdiction determines that a loss was primarily caused by the Escrow Agent's gross negligence or willful misconduct. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear one-half of any amount paid to the Escrow Agent under this Section 8.2, except that a Party whose breach of this Agreement or the Purchase Agreement caused the indemnified loss shall bear the full amount.

### 8.3 Security Interest.

To secure the payment of its fees, expenses and indemnification rights under this Section 8, the Escrow Agent may look first to the Parties as provided above. The Escrow Agent shall not have, and waives, any right of set-off or lien against the Escrow Fund, except to the extent Buyer and Seller have failed to pay amounts due to the Escrow Agent within thirty (30) days after written demand.

## 9. RESIGNATION AND REMOVAL OF THE ESCROW AGENT

### 9.1 Resignation.

The Escrow Agent may resign at any time by giving at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice to Buyer and Seller. The resignation will be effective on the appointment of a successor escrow agent and the transfer of the Escrow Fund to it.

### 9.2 Removal.

Buyer and Seller may remove the Escrow Agent at any time by Joint Written Instruction delivered at least thirty (30) days before the effective date of removal.

### 9.3 Successor.

Buyer and Seller shall appoint a successor escrow agent by Joint Written Instruction within thirty (30) days after notice of resignation or removal. The successor must be a national banking association or trust company having capital and surplus of at least $500,000,000. If no successor is appointed within that period, the Escrow Agent may petition a court of competent jurisdiction to appoint a successor. On the successor's written acceptance of its appointment, the Escrow Agent shall deliver the Escrow Fund and all related records to the successor. The successor will then succeed to all of the rights and obligations of the Escrow Agent under this Agreement.

### 9.4 Merger or Consolidation.

Any entity into which the Escrow Agent may be merged or converted, or with which it may be consolidated, or to which it may transfer all or substantially all of its corporate trust business, will be the successor escrow agent under this Agreement without further act.

## 10. TAX MATTERS

### 10.1 Tax Reporting.

For United States federal and applicable state income tax purposes, Buyer will be treated as the owner of the Escrow Fund until it is disbursed, and all interest and earnings on the Escrow Fund will be reported as income of Buyer. This treatment applies to the extent consistent with Treasury Regulations Section 1.468B-8 and other applicable Law. The Escrow Agent shall report the income in accordance with this Section 10.1 on IRS Form 1099 or such other form as applicable Law requires.

### 10.2 Tax Distributions.

Within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar quarter, at the written request of Buyer, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Buyer from the earnings of the Escrow Fund an amount equal to forty percent (40%) of the earnings allocated to Buyer for that quarter, to fund Buyer's tax liability on those earnings.

### 10.3 Tax Forms.

Each of Buyer and Seller shall deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9 or W-8, as applicable, on or before the execution of this Agreement. The Escrow Agent may withhold from any disbursement any amount required to be withheld under applicable Law, and shall have no liability for any withholding made in good faith.

### 10.4 Know Your Customer.

Buyer and Seller acknowledge that the Escrow Agent is required by applicable Law, including the USA PATRIOT Act, to obtain, verify and record information that identifies each Person that opens an account. Each of Buyer and Seller shall provide such information as the Escrow Agent reasonably requests to comply with those requirements.

### 11.2 Representations of the Escrow Agent.
