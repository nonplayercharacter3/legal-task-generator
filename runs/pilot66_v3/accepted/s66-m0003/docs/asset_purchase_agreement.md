# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**EXECUTION VERSION**

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of September 21, 2026 (the "**Agreement Date**"), by and between **Kiveth Interactive, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and **Zerex Forge Studios, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller is an independent video game development studio. It owns, develops and exploits the interactive entertainment franchise known as "Ashfall Requiem," including the related source code, object code, development tools, art assets, trademarks and the contracts used in that business (the "**Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell, assign and transfer to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase and assume from Seller, the Purchased Assets and the Assumed Liabilities, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

C. At the Closing, and as a condition to Buyer's willingness to enter into this Agreement, Buyer and Odalys Brennt, Seller's studio creative director, will enter into a key employee agreement under which Ms. Brennt will become employed by Buyer effective as of the Closing (the "**Brennt Employment Agreement**").

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, representations, warranties and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

### 2.1 Purchase and Sale of Assets.

On the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement, at the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under all of the assets, properties and rights of every kind that are used or held for use in the Business, other than the Excluded Assets, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than Permitted Encumbrances (collectively, the "**Purchased Assets**"), including:

(a) the Ashfall Requiem Titles, including all builds, versions, master files and gold masters;

(b) all Seller Intellectual Property, including all source code, object code, art assets, audio assets, design documents, narrative materials, the Seller Tools, and all trademarks, registrations and applications listed on Schedule 3.9(a) of the Disclosure Schedule;

(c) all Contracts listed on Schedule 2.1(c) of the Disclosure Schedule, including the Lumen Engine License and the Character License Agreement (the "**Assigned Contracts**");

(d) all development hardware, devkits, workstations, servers, peripherals and other tangible personal property listed on Schedule 2.1(d) of the Disclosure Schedule;

(e) all accounts on digital storefronts, platform developer portals and online services relating to the Ashfall Requiem Titles, to the extent transferable;

(f) all player data, community accounts and social media handles relating to the Business, to the extent transferable under applicable Law and the applicable privacy policies;

(g) all books, records, files, bug databases, version control repositories and production schedules relating to the Business, other than records Seller is required by Law to retain, of which Seller shall provide Buyer copies;

(h) all claims, causes of action, rights of recovery and rights of set-off of Seller against third parties relating to the Purchased Assets, including claims for past, present and future infringement of Seller Intellectual Property; and

(i) all goodwill associated with the Business and the Purchased Assets.

### 2.2 Excluded Assets.

The Purchased Assets do not include the following (collectively, the "**Excluded Assets**"): (a) cash and cash equivalents; (b) Contracts that are not Assigned Contracts; (c) Seller's organizational documents, minute books and Tax records; (d) all insurance policies of Seller and all rights under them; (e) all employee benefit plans of Seller and assets attributable to them; (f) all rights to Tax refunds relating to periods before the Closing; (g) the assets listed on Schedule 2.2 of the Disclosure Schedule; and (h) all rights of Seller under this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements.

### 2.3 Assumed Liabilities.

At the Closing, Buyer shall assume and agree to pay, perform and discharge only the following liabilities of Seller (collectively, the "**Assumed Liabilities**"): (a) all liabilities and obligations arising under the Assigned Contracts, but only to the extent they are required to be performed after the Closing, were incurred in the ordinary course, and do not relate to any failure to perform, improper performance, breach, default or violation by Seller on or before the Closing; and (b) the liabilities specifically listed on Schedule 2.3 of the Disclosure Schedule.

### 2.4 Excluded Liabilities.

Buyer shall not assume, and shall not be responsible to pay, perform or discharge, any liabilities or obligations of Seller or any of its Affiliates of any kind, whether known or unknown, contingent or otherwise, other than the Assumed Liabilities (collectively, the "**Excluded Liabilities**"). The Excluded Liabilities include:

(a) all liabilities arising out of or relating to the development, production, publication, marketing or sale of the Ashfall Requiem Titles or the operation of the Business before the Closing, including any claim that the Titles, the Seller Tools or any other Purchased Asset, as they existed or were exploited before the Closing, infringe, misappropriate or otherwise violate the Intellectual Property of any Person;

(b) all liabilities for Taxes of Seller or its members, and all Taxes relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets for any period ending on or before the Closing Date;

(c) all liabilities relating to the employment or engagement of any current or former employee, contractor or consultant of Seller, including wages, bonuses, severance, crunch-time compensation, accrued paid time off, and benefits, in each case to the extent arising on or before the Closing;

(d) all liabilities arising under any Excluded Asset;

(e) all liabilities arising out of any action, suit, claim or proceeding pending or threatened as of the Closing, or arising from facts or circumstances existing before the Closing;

(f) all indebtedness of Seller; and

(g) all liabilities under any Assigned Contract arising from any breach or default by Seller before the Closing.

### 2.5 Purchase Price.

The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $48,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities. At the Closing, Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price, less the Escrow Amount, to Seller by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by Seller in writing at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date.

### 2.6 Escrow.

At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit $4,850,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent, to be held and disbursed in accordance with the Escrow Agreement and this Agreement as security for the indemnification obligations of Seller under Article IX. Buyer and Seller shall each pay one-half of the fees and expenses of the Escrow Agent.

### 2.7 Non-Assignable Assets.

(a) This Agreement does not constitute an agreement to assign or transfer any Purchased Asset, including any Assigned Contract, if an attempted assignment or transfer without the consent of a third party would constitute a breach of it, be ineffective, or adversely affect the rights of Buyer under it. If any such consent is not obtained before the Closing, the Closing shall nevertheless proceed. That Purchased Asset shall not be assigned or transferred at the Closing, and Seller shall continue to hold it, with no adjustment to the Purchase Price.

(b) Following the Closing, Seller shall continue to use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain any such consent. Until the consent is obtained, Seller shall cooperate with Buyer in any lawful and commercially reasonable arrangement to provide Buyer the benefits of the Purchased Asset, including subcontracting, sublicensing or subleasing. To the extent Buyer receives those benefits, Buyer shall perform Seller's obligations under that Purchased Asset that arise after the Closing. Seller shall promptly pay over to Buyer all payments received by it in respect of that Purchased Asset after the Closing. Once the consent is obtained, Seller shall assign and transfer the Purchased Asset to Buyer for no additional consideration.

### 2.8 Allocation of Purchase Price.

Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a schedule allocating the Purchase Price, the Assumed Liabilities and any other relevant items among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Code (the "**Allocation Schedule**"). If Seller does not object in writing within thirty (30) days after delivery, the Allocation Schedule will be final and binding. If Seller timely objects, the Parties shall negotiate in good faith to resolve the dispute. If they cannot resolve it within thirty (30) days, an independent accounting firm mutually acceptable to the Parties shall resolve it, and the Parties shall bear its fees equally. The Parties shall file all Tax returns consistently with the Allocation Schedule.

### 2.9 Withholding.

Buyer and the Escrow Agent may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any amounts required to be deducted and withheld under applicable Tax Law. Amounts so withheld and paid over to the appropriate Governmental Authority will be treated for all purposes of this Agreement as having been paid to the Person in respect of which the deduction and withholding was made.

(c) *Settlement.* The Indemnifying Party shall not settle any Third-Party Claim without the prior written consent of the Indemnified Party, unless the settlement (i) provides solely for the payment of money damages that are paid in full by the Indemnifying Party, (ii) includes a complete and unconditional release of the Indemnified Party, and (iii) involves no admission of wrongdoing by the Indemnified Party. The Indemnified Party shall not settle any Third-Party Claim for which it seeks indemnification without the prior written consent of the Indemnifying Party, not to be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed.

(d) *Direct Claims.* Any claim by an Indemnified Party on account of a Loss that does not result from a Third-Party Claim (a "**Direct Claim**") shall be asserted by the Indemnified Party giving the Indemnifying Party reasonably prompt written notice, describing the Direct Claim in reasonable detail and indicating the estimated amount, if reasonably practicable, of the Loss.
