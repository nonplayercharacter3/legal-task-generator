## 1. PURPOSE AND BASIS OF THIS FORECAST

**1.1 Purpose.** Distributor delivers this rolling forecast under Section 5.4 of the Distribution Agreement between Supplier and Distributor dated as of March 3, 2025, as amended by Amendment No. 1 dated September 15, 2026 (together, the "**Agreement**"). Section 5.4 requires a quarterly rolling forecast of Distributor's expected Product requirements for the following four calendar quarters, broken down by Product. This forecast is delivered within the period required by that Section for the calendar quarter beginning October 1, 2026.

**1.2 First Forecast After Amendment No. 1.** This is Distributor's first rolling forecast since the Amendment Effective Date. As Section 3.5 of Amendment No. 1 requires, it includes a separate line for the BX-900.

## 2. SUMMARY

**2.1 Headline Forecast.** For the four calendar quarters from January 1, 2027 to December 31, 2027, Distributor forecasts requirements for twenty-six (26) BWTS units in addition to the units under open Purchase Orders. The indicative value of these units at current prices is US$11,852,500.

**2.2 Main Drivers.** The main drivers of the forecast are:

(a) the launch of the BX-900 in the Territory under the joint launch program in Section 6.1 of Amendment No. 1, which Distributor expects to open demand from large tanker and bulk carrier operators that the BX-700 cannot serve because of its treated flow capacity;

(b) continued retrofit demand from vessels calling regularly at Houston and Corpus Christi that have dry-dockings scheduled in 2027 and must have a compliant ballast water management method under 33 C.F.R. Part 151; and

(c) replacement demand from operators whose existing third-party systems have shown performance problems during port state control inspections in the Territory.

**2.3 Comparison with Previous Forecast.** Distributor's previous rolling forecast, submitted July 14, 2026, covered Q4 2026 through Q3 2027 and included nineteen (19) BWTS units, none of them BX-900 units. Excluding the BX-900, the overlapping quarters of the two forecasts differ by one unit, which reflects a BX-700 opportunity in Mobile that the Customer deferred to 2028.

**5.2 Commentary by Port.**

(a) **Houston.** Houston remains Distributor's largest market. Four of the seven forecast BX-900 units are for tanker operators with Houston-based technical management. Distributor expects most Houston retrofits to be carried out at Houston Ship Channel yards during scheduled dry-dockings.

(b) **New Orleans.** Demand in New Orleans comes mainly from offshore supply vessels, river-to-sea bulk carriers and articulated tug-barge units, for which the BX-300 and BX-500 are the usual fit. One BX-900 opportunity relates to a grain carrier operator with vessels loading at Lower Mississippi River terminals.

(c) **Mobile.** Mobile demand is limited to two units in this forecast period, following the deferral noted in Section 2.3.

(d) **Corpus Christi.** Corpus Christi demand is driven by crude and LPG export carriers calling at the port's terminals. Distributor expects two BX-900 units for crude carriers in the second half of 2027, subject to the outcome of vessel surveys scheduled for December 2026.

## 11. ASSUMPTIONS AND RISKS

**11.1 Assumptions.** This forecast assumes that:

(a) the United States Coast Guard type approvals for the BX-300, BX-500, BX-700 and BX-900 remain in effect without limitation throughout the forecast period;

(b) Supplier meets the confirmed delivery dates for open Purchase Orders and the lead times in Section 6.1;

(c) vessel dry-docking schedules notified to Distributor by Customers do not change materially;

(d) Gulf Coast shipyard capacity remains available for the scheduled retrofits; and

(e) no Force Majeure Event affects the ports in the Territory during the forecast period.

**11.2 Weather.** Retrofit schedules at Gulf Coast yards are exposed to the Atlantic hurricane season from June to November. Distributor has allowed for some schedule slippage in Q3 2027 but has not reduced forecast quantities on that account.

**11.3 Customer Timing.** Retrofit timing depends on Customers' trading patterns and charter commitments. In Distributor's experience, between ten percent (10%) and fifteen percent (15%) of forecast units move by one quarter between successive forecasts. Distributor will reflect any such movement in its next forecast.

**11.4 Competitive Factors.** Several of the Grade C and D opportunities in Section 4.4 involve competitive tenders against other BWTS manufacturers. Distributor's success in those tenders may depend on BX-900 lead times and the results of the joint launch program.
