# STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 1

## INSTALLATION AND COMMISSIONING SERVICES

This **STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 1** (this "**SOW**") is entered into as of June 16, 2025 (the "**SOW Effective Date**") by and between:

**BUPAIV BALLAST TECHNOLOGIES LTD.**, a private limited company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 08814372, with its registered office at Unit 4, Quayside Business Park, Southampton SO14 3TG, United Kingdom ("**Supplier**"); and

**MIBAIX MARINE SYSTEMS LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company, with its principal place of business at 2250 Clinton Drive, Suite 310, Houston, Texas 77020, United States ("**Distributor**").

Supplier and Distributor are each referred to in this SOW as a "**Party**" and together as the "**Parties**."

## 1. BACKGROUND AND INCORPORATION

**1.1 The Agreement.** This SOW is entered into under Section 8.2 of the Distribution Agreement between Supplier and Distributor dated as of March 3, 2025 (the "**Agreement**"). This SOW is the first Statement of Work under the Agreement.

**1.2 Incorporation.** This SOW is governed by and incorporates the terms of the Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this SOW have the meanings given to them in the Agreement. The rules of interpretation in Section 1.2 of the Agreement apply to this SOW. References in this SOW to Sections are to sections of this SOW, and references to Annexes are to the annexes to this SOW, unless this SOW states otherwise.

**1.3 Purpose.** This SOW sets out the terms on which Distributor will perform Installation Services for Products fitted to vessels in the Territory where Supplier engages Distributor to do so. These include Installation Services for Reserved Accounts offered to Distributor under Section 3.2 of the Agreement, and warranty and recall work under Sections 12.4 and 13.5 of the Agreement. This SOW also sets out the service standards and acceptance procedures that apply to that work.

## 2. DEFINITIONS

**2.1 Defined Terms.** In this SOW:

"**Acceptance Certificate**" means a certificate substantially in the form of Annex 3, signed in accordance with Section 5.4.

"**Commissioning**" means the start-up, functional testing, calibration and performance verification of an installed BWTS unit in accordance with Supplier's commissioning procedures, ending with completion of Supplier's commissioning checklist.

"**Job**" means a discrete engagement of Distributor under this SOW for one vessel, as described in a Work Order.

"**Owner**" means the owner, operator or manager of the vessel on which a Job is performed, and includes a Reserved Account where the Reserved Account is the contracting party for the vessel.

"**Retrofit Installation**" means the installation of a BWTS unit on a vessel in service, including structural foundations, piping tie-ins to the ballast system, electrical supply and cabling, control system integration and associated hot work, as further described in Section 3.2.

"**Site**" means the vessel, berth, anchorage, shipyard or dry dock in the Territory at which a Job is performed.

"**Warranty Work**" means the removal, repair, replacement and reinstallation of a Product or component that fails to conform to the warranties in Section 12.1 of the Agreement, and recall, retrofit or withdrawal work under Section 13.5 of the Agreement.

"**Work Order**" means a written order for a Job issued by Supplier and accepted by Distributor under Section 4.

## 3. SCOPE OF SERVICES

**3.1 Services.** Distributor shall perform the following Installation Services under this SOW when engaged by a Work Order:

(a) Retrofit Installations;

(b) Commissioning of BWTS units, whether or not Distributor performed the Retrofit Installation;

(c) Warranty Work; and

(d) pre-installation vessel surveys, under Section 3.3.

**3.2 Retrofit Installation.** For each Retrofit Installation, unless the Work Order states otherwise, Distributor shall:

(a) receive the BWTS unit and its components at the Site, check them against the packing list and record any shortage or visible damage;

(b) fabricate and install the foundations and supports shown on the approved vessel integration drawings;

(c) install the filter unit, UV reactor(s), CIP unit, control cabinet, flow meter and sensor package in the locations shown on the approved drawings;

(d) make all piping tie-ins between the BWTS unit and the vessel's ballast system, including installation of bypass, sampling and isolation valves;

(e) install power supply and control cabling, terminate it at the BWTS control cabinet and the vessel's switchboard and ballast control console, and test insulation resistance;

(f) carry out hydrostatic pressure testing of new piping;

(g) remove and dispose of packaging, offcuts and waste materials generated by the work in accordance with Applicable Law and the Site rules; and

(h) prepare as-built drawings showing any deviation from the approved drawings.

**3.3 Vessel Surveys.** Where a Work Order includes a pre-installation survey, Distributor shall attend the vessel, inspect the proposed installation space, ballast pumps, piping and electrical supply, and deliver a written survey report to Supplier within ten (10) Business Days after the attendance. The report shall identify any feature of the vessel that may require changes to Supplier's generic integration drawings.

**3.4 Commissioning.** For each Commissioning, Distributor shall:

(a) verify that the installation conforms to the approved drawings and Supplier's installation manuals;

(b) energize the BWTS unit and carry out the start-up sequence in Supplier's commissioning procedures;

(c) calibrate the UV intensity sensors and flow meter using Supplier's sensor calibration kits;

(d) carry out a treatment cycle on ballast uptake and a discharge cycle, including CIP operation, in accordance with Supplier's commissioning procedures;

(e) instruct the vessel's crew in the operation and routine maintenance of the BWTS unit and record the names of the crew members instructed;

(f) complete Supplier's commissioning checklist and deliver it to Supplier in accordance with Section 8.4 of the Agreement; and

(g) support any attendance by the classification society surveyor required for the vessel's records.

**3.5 Warranty Work.** For Warranty Work, Distributor shall diagnose the reported fault, report its findings to Supplier, and, on Supplier's instruction, remove the non-conforming Product or component and install the repaired or replacement item supplied by Supplier. Distributor shall return removed items to Supplier when Supplier asks, at Supplier's cost.

**3.6 Exclusions.** Unless a Work Order expressly includes them, the Installation Services do not include:

(a) design of the vessel integration or preparation of the drawings submitted for classification society or flag state approval;

(b) modification of the vessel's ballast pumps, sea chests or ballast tanks;

(c) removal of asbestos or other hazardous materials existing on the vessel;

(d) gas-freeing of spaces, except for the hot work permits referred to in Section 9.3; or

(e) operation of the vessel's ballast system other than during Commissioning.

**3.7 Supplier Responsibilities.** For each Job, Supplier shall:

(a) deliver, or procure delivery of, the Products and any free-issue materials stated in the Work Order to the Site by the date stated in the Work Order;

(b) provide the approved vessel integration drawings, the classification society approval and any flag state approval for the installation before work begins;

(c) provide current installation manuals, commissioning procedures and checklists for the relevant BWTS model under Section 4.3(c) of the Agreement;

(d) obtain from the Owner, or procure that the Owner provides, access to the vessel, the berth or dock arrangements, shore power where required, and the vessel's crew and systems needed for Commissioning; and

(e) provide technical support under Section 4.3(b) of the Agreement, and field service engineer attendance under Section 8.5 of the Agreement where the Work Order or the classification society requires it.

## 4. WORK ORDERS

**4.1 Issue.** Supplier shall request each Job by issuing a Work Order to Distributor by email. Each Work Order shall state:

(a) the vessel name, IMO number, flag and classification society;

(b) the Owner's name and contact details;

(c) the BWTS model and serial number;

(d) the Site and the planned start and completion dates;

(e) the services required under Section 3.1;

(f) any free-issue materials and the delivery date for the Products; and

(g) the applicable fee basis under Annex 1.

**4.2 Acceptance.** Distributor shall accept or decline each Work Order in writing within five (5) Business Days after receipt. Where Supplier offers a Reserved Account Job under Section 3.2 of the Agreement, Distributor's decision to decline does not affect its rights under that Section in respect of other Jobs. A Work Order is binding only once Distributor has accepted it.

**4.3 Changes.** Either Party may propose a change to an accepted Work Order. If a change affects the scope, schedule or fees of a Job, the Parties shall record the change in a written change order signed by both Parties before Distributor performs the changed work. Where the Owner, the classification society or a vessel condition not disclosed in the Work Order requires additional work, Distributor may perform work reasonably necessary to protect the vessel, persons or property before the change order is signed. Supplier shall pay for that work at the time-and-materials rates in Annex 1.

**4.4 Scheduling.** Distributor shall mobilize to the Site on the planned start date stated in the Work Order. Supplier acknowledges that vessel retrofit schedules depend on the vessel's trading pattern and port calls. Distributor is not responsible for delays caused by the late arrival of the vessel, the late delivery of Products or free-issue materials, the unavailability of the vessel's crew or systems, or the Owner's instructions.

## 5. SERVICE STANDARDS AND ACCEPTANCE

**5.1 Standards.** Distributor shall perform the Installation Services in accordance with Section 8.3 of the Agreement. Distributor shall use only technicians who have completed Supplier's training under Section 4.3(a) of the Agreement. Any subcontractor shall meet the requirements of Section 3.3 of the Agreement.

**5.2 Lead Technician.** For each Job, Distributor shall appoint a lead technician who shall be present at the Site whenever work is being carried out and who shall be Supplier's and the Owner's point of contact for the Job.

**5.3 Progress Reports.** During any Retrofit Installation scheduled to last more than five (5) days, Distributor shall send Supplier a short written progress report at the end of each working week. The report shall identify work completed, work outstanding, any delays and any issues requiring Supplier's or the Owner's action.

**5.4 Acceptance.** When Commissioning of a BWTS unit is complete, Distributor shall present an Acceptance Certificate to the Owner's representative and to Supplier's field service engineer, if attending. The Job is accepted when the Acceptance Certificate is signed by the Owner's representative or, where Supplier engaged Distributor directly and no Owner signature is available, by Supplier. If neither the Owner nor Supplier signs the Acceptance Certificate or delivers a written list of material non-conformities within five (5) Business Days after presentation, the Job is deemed accepted.

**5.5 Non-Conformities.** If the Owner or Supplier delivers a written list of material non-conformities in the Installation Services within the period stated in Section 5.4, Distributor shall correct them at its own cost and present a revised Acceptance Certificate. Items that do not affect the safe operation of the BWTS unit or its compliance with its type approval may be recorded as punch-list items. Recording them does not delay acceptance.

**5.6 Warranty Work.** Warranty Work is complete when Distributor has notified Supplier in writing that the repaired or replacement item has been installed and tested and the BWTS unit has been returned to service.

**5.7 Records.** Distributor shall keep, for at least five (5) years after completion of each Job, its work records, test results, calibration records, hot work permits and as-built drawings for the Job. Distributor shall make copies available to Supplier on reasonable request.

## 6. INDEMNIFICATION

**6.1 Agreement Indemnities.** Section 10 of the Agreement applies to the Installation Services. Supplier's indemnities under Sections 10.1, 10.2 and 10.3 of the Agreement extend to Products installed or commissioned under this SOW.

**6.2 Installation Indemnity.** Subject to Section 11 of the Agreement, Distributor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Supplier and its officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all Losses arising from any Environmental Claim arising out of or relating to the Installation Services performed by Distributor or its subcontractors under this SOW.

**6.3 Procedure.** Section 10.5 of the Agreement applies to any claim for indemnification under this Section 6.

## 7. FEES AND PAYMENT

**7.1 Fees.** Supplier shall pay Distributor the fees for each Job determined under Annex 1. Fixed fees apply to Retrofit Installations and Commissioning for Reserved Accounts. Warranty Work is charged at the labor rates set out in Part 3 of Schedule B to the Agreement, together with the costs reimbursable under Section 12.4 of the Agreement. Changed and additional work is charged at the time-and-materials rates in Annex 1.

**7.2 Expenses.** Supplier shall reimburse Distributor for reasonable and documented travel and accommodation expenses for Jobs at a Site more than one hundred (100) miles from the nearest Distributor facility in the Territory. Supplier shall also reimburse launch, crane and vessel access charges incurred with Supplier's prior approval, at cost.

**7.3 Invoicing.** Distributor shall invoice Supplier:

(a) for fixed-fee Jobs, forty percent (40%) of the fixed fee on mobilization to the Site and sixty percent (60%) on acceptance under Section 5.4; and

(b) for Warranty Work, time-and-materials work and expenses, monthly in arrears, with supporting time sheets and receipts.

**7.4 Payment.** Supplier shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days after receipt, by wire transfer to the account Distributor designates in writing. Sections 6.5 and 6.6 of the Agreement apply to invoices under this SOW as if Supplier were the paying party and Distributor the invoicing party.

**7.5 Set-Off.** Distributor may set off any undisputed amount due from Supplier under this SOW against any amount Distributor owes Supplier under the Agreement, by written notice to Supplier identifying the amounts set off.

**7.6 Taxes.** Fees are exclusive of sales, use and similar taxes. Supplier shall pay any such taxes that Applicable Law requires on the Installation Services, other than taxes on Distributor's income.

**7.7 Minimum Purchase Credit.** For each Product sold to a Reserved Account on which Distributor performs the Installation Services under this SOW, Distributor receives credit toward the Minimum Annual Purchase in accordance with Section 3.2 of the Agreement. Supplier shall confirm the net invoice value of each such Product to Distributor in writing within thirty (30) days after acceptance of the Job.

## 8. WORKMANSHIP WARRANTY

**8.1 Warranty.** Distributor warrants to Supplier that the Installation Services will be performed in a good and workmanlike manner, in accordance with Section 5.1. Distributor further warrants that, for twelve (12) months after acceptance of each Job, the work will be free from defects in workmanship.

**8.2 Remedy.** If Supplier notifies Distributor in writing of a breach of the warranty in Section 8.1 within the warranty period, Distributor shall re-perform the defective work at its own cost, at a time agreed with the Owner. Where the vessel is outside the Territory and cannot reasonably return to it, Distributor may instead reimburse Supplier for the reasonable documented cost of having the defective work corrected by a qualified contractor, up to the fee Distributor received for the defective portion of the work.

**8.3 Exclusions.** The warranty in Section 8.1 does not apply to any defect to the extent it results from a Defect in a Product, an error in drawings or instructions provided by Supplier or the Owner, operation of the BWTS unit by the vessel's crew other than in accordance with Supplier's instructions, or work performed by any person other than Distributor or its subcontractors.

**8.4 Relationship to Product Warranty.** The Installation Services performed under this SOW satisfy the requirement in Section 12.6(a) of the Agreement that installation be performed in accordance with Supplier's installation manuals, except to the extent Supplier shows that a particular installation did not conform to them.

## 9. PERSONNEL, SAFETY AND SITE CONDITIONS

**9.1 Personnel.** Distributor is responsible for the supervision, direction and control of its personnel and subcontractors at the Site. Distributor shall ensure that each technician holds a valid Transportation Worker Identification Credential where required for access to the Site, and holds any welding or electrical qualifications required by the classification society for the work assigned to that technician.

**9.2 Safety.** Distributor shall comply with the safety management system of the vessel and the safety rules of the Site, and with applicable occupational safety and health regulations, including 29 C.F.R. Part 1915 where it applies. Distributor shall report any accident, injury or near miss at the Site to Supplier and the Owner within twenty-four (24) hours.

**9.3 Hot Work.** Distributor shall not carry out hot work on a vessel unless a valid hot work permit has been issued under the vessel's and the Site's permit procedures and, where required, a marine chemist's certificate has been obtained. Supplier shall procure, or procure that the Owner procures, any marine chemist's certificate required for a Job.

**9.4 Ballast Operations.** During Retrofit Installation and Commissioning, Distributor shall not operate the vessel's ballast pumps or valves except under the instruction and supervision of the vessel's chief engineer or other authorized officer. Distributor shall follow the vessel's ballast water management plan in carrying out treatment and discharge cycles during Commissioning.

**9.5 Site Conditions.** If Distributor encounters a vessel condition that was not disclosed in the Work Order or the survey report, and that prevents the Job from being performed as planned, Distributor shall notify Supplier promptly and the Parties shall proceed under Section 4.3.

**9.6 Owner Access.** Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to ensure that the Owner provides Distributor with safe access to the vessel and the work areas, adequate lighting and ventilation, and secure storage for Products and tools at the Site.

## 10. INSURANCE

**10.1 Coverage.** Distributor shall maintain the insurance required by Section 14.2 of the Agreement throughout the term of this SOW. The ship repairer's legal liability insurance required by Section 14.2(b) of the Agreement shall cover the Installation Services performed under this SOW.

**10.2 Certificates.** On request, Distributor shall provide Supplier or an Owner with a certificate of insurance evidencing the coverage required by Section 10.1 for a particular Job.

## 11. TERM AND TERMINATION

**11.1 Term.** This SOW begins on the SOW Effective Date and continues until the expiration or termination of the Agreement, unless terminated earlier under this Section 11.

**11.2 Termination for Convenience.** Either Party may terminate this SOW on ninety (90) days' written notice to the other. Termination under this Section 11.2 does not affect any Work Order accepted before the notice is given, which shall be completed in accordance with this SOW.

**11.3 Cancellation of a Work Order.** Supplier may cancel an accepted Work Order by written notice to Distributor. If Supplier cancels a Work Order after Distributor has mobilized to the Site, Supplier shall pay Distributor for the work performed up to the date of cancellation at the time-and-materials rates in Annex 1, together with demobilization costs and any non-cancellable commitments Distributor has made for the Job. If Supplier cancels a Work Order less than five (5) Business Days before the planned start date, Supplier shall also pay a cancellation charge equal to two (2) technician-days at the standard day rate in Annex 1 for each technician scheduled for the Job.

**11.4 Effect.** On expiration or termination of this SOW, Supplier shall pay Distributor all fees and expenses earned and incurred up to the effective date, and Section 7.4 applies to those amounts. Sections 5.7, 6, 7, 8 and 11.4 survive expiration or termination of this SOW.
