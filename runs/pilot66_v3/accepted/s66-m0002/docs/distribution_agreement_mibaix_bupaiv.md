# DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This **DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 3, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

**BUPAIV BALLAST TECHNOLOGIES LTD.**, a private limited company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 08814372, with its registered office at Unit 4, Quayside Business Park, Southampton SO14 3TG, United Kingdom ("**Supplier**"); and

**MIBAIX MARINE SYSTEMS LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company, with its principal place of business at 2250 Clinton Drive, Suite 310, Houston, Texas 77020, United States ("**Distributor**").

Supplier and Distributor are each referred to in this Agreement as a "**Party**" and together as the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier designs and manufactures ballast water treatment systems for commercial vessels, together with related spare parts and treatment consumables, and holds United States Coast Guard type approval for certain of its system models.

B. Distributor sells, installs, commissions and services marine equipment for vessel owners, operators and shipyards. Distributor currently operates an exhaust gas scrubber servicing business at Gulf and Caribbean ports, and it maintains installation crews and technical personnel at the ports of Houston, New Orleans, Mobile and Corpus Christi.

C. Supplier wishes to appoint Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory, and Distributor wishes to accept that appointment, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

D. The Parties also intend that Distributor will perform installation and commissioning services for Products fitted to vessels in connection with retrofits, under Statements of Work entered into under this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 2. TERM

**2.1 Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for five (5) years from the Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 17.

**2.2 Renewal.** After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive renewal periods of two (2) years each (each, a "**Renewal Term**"), unless either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred eighty (180) days before the end of the Initial Term or the then-current Renewal Term. The Initial Term and all Renewal Terms are together the "**Term**."

**2.3 Renewal Conditions.** Before each Renewal Term begins, the Parties shall negotiate in good faith the Minimum Annual Purchase for that Renewal Term. If the Parties do not agree on a revised Minimum Annual Purchase by the first day of the Renewal Term, the Minimum Annual Purchase for the final Contract Year of the preceding term applies.

## 3. APPOINTMENT

**3.1 Exclusive Appointment.** Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Supplier appoints Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory for the Term, and Distributor accepts that appointment. During the Term, Supplier shall not appoint any other distributor, agent or reseller of the Products in the Territory. Supplier shall not itself sell the Products for delivery or installation in the Territory, except as permitted by Section 3.2.

**3.2 Reserved Accounts.** Supplier may continue to supply Products directly to the shipyards and fleet operators listed as reserved accounts in Part 3 of Schedule A ("**Reserved Accounts**") under global fleet agreements in effect on the Effective Date. Where a Reserved Account takes delivery of a Product in the Territory and requires installation or commissioning in the Territory, Supplier shall first offer Distributor the opportunity to perform the Installation Services on the terms of a Statement of Work. Distributor shall receive credit toward the Minimum Annual Purchase for the net invoice value of any Product sold to a Reserved Account for installation in the Territory where Distributor performs the Installation Services.

**3.3 Sub-Distributors and Subcontractors.** Distributor shall not appoint sub-distributors of the Products without Supplier's prior written consent, which Supplier shall not unreasonably withhold. Distributor may engage subcontractors to perform parts of the Installation Services, provided that Distributor remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors as if they were its own and that each subcontractor is trained to Supplier's installation standards under Section 4.3.

**3.4 Non-Competition.** During the Term and for a period of twenty-four (24) months after its expiration or termination, Distributor shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates under its control not to, directly or indirectly manufacture, distribute, sell, market or service any ballast water treatment, exhaust gas scrubber or other marine environmental compliance equipment other than Products, anywhere in the world. Distributor shall not hold any interest in, or act as agent, consultant or representative for, any person engaged in any of these activities.

**3.5 Sales Outside the Territory.** Distributor shall not actively solicit Customers for the Products outside the Territory, and shall not establish any branch, warehouse or installation facility for the Products outside the Territory, without Supplier's prior written consent. Distributor may respond to unsolicited requests from Customers whose vessels will call at a port in the Territory, and may supply Products to and perform Installation Services for those vessels while they are in the Territory.

**3.6 Supplier Referrals.** Supplier shall promptly refer to Distributor all inquiries it receives from Customers for the supply, installation or servicing of Products in the Territory.

**3.7 Independent Contractor Status.** Distributor buys and resells the Products for its own account. Distributor may set its own resale prices for the Products, and nothing in this Agreement restricts Distributor's freedom to do so.

## 4. SUPPLIER OBLIGATIONS

**4.1 Supply.** Supplier shall manufacture and supply the Products ordered by Distributor in accordance with accepted Purchase Orders, the Specifications and Applicable Law.

**4.2 Type Approval and Certification.** Supplier shall maintain throughout the Term, at its own cost, the United States Coast Guard type approval for each BWTS model listed in Schedule A, together with any other certifications Applicable Law requires for the Products to be lawfully installed and operated on vessels calling at ports in the Territory. Supplier shall notify Distributor in writing within five (5) Business Days of any suspension, withdrawal, limitation or adverse modification of any type approval or certification.

**4.3 Technical Support and Training.** Supplier shall:

(a) provide initial installation and commissioning training to up to twelve (12) of Distributor's technicians within ninety (90) days after the Effective Date, and annual refresher training afterwards, at a location in the Territory or at Supplier's facility in Southampton;

(b) provide remote technical support by telephone and email between 7:00 a.m. and 7:00 p.m. U.S. Central Time on Business Days, and an emergency escalation line at all times;

(c) supply Distributor with installation manuals, wiring diagrams, commissioning checklists, operation and maintenance manuals and other technical documentation for the Products, in English; and

(d) make a field service engineer available to attend commissioning in the Territory on Distributor's request at the day rates set out in Schedule B, plus reasonable travel expenses.

**4.4 Marketing Support.** Supplier shall supply Distributor with reasonable quantities of sales literature, technical brochures and product samples free of charge, and shall list Distributor as its exclusive distributor for the Territory on Supplier's website.

**4.5 Product Changes.** Supplier shall give Distributor at least one hundred twenty (120) days' prior written notice of any discontinuation of a Product or any material change to the form, fit or function of a Product. For at least seven (7) years after the last sale of a BWTS model to Distributor, Supplier shall continue to make available spare parts for that model, or functionally equivalent replacements.

**4.6 Regulatory Information.** Supplier shall promptly provide Distributor with all information in its possession that Distributor reasonably requests in order to respond to inquiries from the United States Coast Guard, the Environmental Protection Agency, any port state control authority or any classification society about a Product.

## 5. MINIMUM PURCHASE AND FORECASTS

**5.1 Minimum Annual Purchase.** In each Contract Year, Distributor shall purchase from Supplier Products with an aggregate net invoice value of not less than US$4,800,000 (the "**Minimum Annual Purchase**"). The Minimum Annual Purchase for the first Contract Year shall be prorated to reflect any period, beginning on the Effective Date, during which Supplier was unable to accept Purchase Orders because the training under Section 4.3(a) had not been completed.

**5.2 Credit Toward Minimum.** All Products invoiced by Supplier to Distributor in a Contract Year, and Products credited under Section 3.2, count toward the Minimum Annual Purchase for that Contract Year. Products that Supplier fails to deliver within thirty (30) days after the confirmed delivery date also count toward the Minimum Annual Purchase, whether or not Supplier ultimately delivers them. The same applies to Products ordered but not delivered because of a Force Majeure Event affecting Supplier.

**5.3 Shortfall.** If Distributor fails to meet the Minimum Annual Purchase in any Contract Year, Supplier shall give written notice of the shortfall within sixty (60) days after the end of that Contract Year. Distributor may cure the shortfall by issuing Purchase Orders for the shortfall amount within ninety (90) days after receiving the notice. If Distributor does not do so, Supplier may, as its sole and exclusive remedy, convert the appointment under Section 3.1 to a non-exclusive appointment by giving thirty (30) days' written notice. A failure to meet the Minimum Annual Purchase is not otherwise a breach of this Agreement.

**5.4 Forecasts.** By the fifteenth (15th) day of each calendar quarter, Distributor shall provide Supplier with a non-binding rolling forecast of its expected Product requirements for the following four (4) calendar quarters, broken down by Product. Forecasts are for planning purposes only and do not obligate Distributor to purchase, or Supplier to supply, any quantity of Products, except to the extent covered by an accepted Purchase Order.

**5.5 Capacity.** Supplier shall maintain sufficient manufacturing capacity and inventory to supply at least one hundred twenty percent (120%) of the quantities in Distributor's most recent forecast.

## 6. ORDERING, PRICING AND PAYMENT

**6.1 Purchase Orders.** Distributor shall order Products by issuing Purchase Orders to Supplier by email or through any electronic ordering system the Parties agree. Each Purchase Order shall state the Products and quantities ordered, the requested delivery date, the delivery location and the vessel name and IMO number, if known.

**6.2 Acceptance.** Supplier shall accept or reject each Purchase Order in writing within five (5) Business Days after receipt. Supplier shall not reject any Purchase Order that is consistent with this Agreement and Distributor's most recent forecast. A Purchase Order not rejected within that period is deemed accepted. Each accepted Purchase Order is governed exclusively by this Agreement. Any additional or conflicting terms in a Purchase Order, order acknowledgment, invoice or other business form of either Party have no effect.

**6.3 Prices.** The prices for the Products are set out in the Price List in Schedule B and are stated in United States dollars, Ex Works (Incoterms® 2020) Supplier's facility in Southampton, England. Supplier may adjust the Price List once per Contract Year, effective on the anniversary of the Effective Date, by giving Distributor at least ninety (90) days' prior written notice. No single annual increase shall exceed the lesser of (a) five percent (5%) and (b) the percentage change in the UK Producer Price Index for manufactured products over the preceding twelve (12) months. Price changes do not apply to Purchase Orders accepted before the effective date of the change.

**6.4 Most Favoured Pricing.** The prices charged to Distributor for any Product shall be no higher than the prices Supplier charges any other distributor in North America for the same Product in comparable quantities and on comparable terms.

**6.5 Invoicing and Payment.** Supplier shall invoice Distributor on shipment of each Product. Distributor shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days after receipt, by wire transfer to the account Supplier designates in writing. If Distributor disputes any invoice in good faith, it shall notify Supplier in writing of the disputed amount and the reasons for the dispute within fifteen (15) days after receipt and shall pay the undisputed portion when due. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days.

**6.6 Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due accrue interest from the due date until paid at the rate of one percent (1%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by Applicable Law, whichever is lower.

**6.7 Taxes.** Prices are exclusive of sales, use, value added, excise and similar taxes and customs duties. Distributor is responsible for United States import duties and for any sales and use taxes on its resale of the Products. Each Party is responsible for taxes on its own income.

**6.8 Credit Support.** Supplier may extend credit to Distributor up to a revolving credit limit of US$1,500,000, or any higher amount Supplier approves in writing. Supplier may require reasonable credit support, including a parent company guaranty, as a condition of extending credit above that limit.

## 7. DELIVERY, TITLE AND RISK

**7.1 Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver the Products Ex Works (Incoterms® 2020) Supplier's facility in Southampton, England, packed for ocean transport in accordance with Supplier's standard export packing specifications. Where Distributor requests, Supplier shall arrange ocean freight and marine cargo insurance to the port of delivery in the Territory as Distributor's agent and at Distributor's cost, invoiced at cost plus documented handling charges.

**7.2 Delivery Dates.** Supplier shall make each Product available for collection by the confirmed delivery date stated in its acceptance of the applicable Purchase Order. Supplier shall notify Distributor immediately if it expects any delay. If Supplier fails to make a BWTS unit available within fourteen (14) days after the confirmed delivery date for a reason other than a Force Majeure Event, Supplier shall pay Distributor liquidated damages equal to one-half of one percent (0.5%) of the price of the delayed BWTS unit for each full week of delay, up to a maximum of ten percent (10%) of that price. The Parties agree that these liquidated damages are a reasonable pre-estimate of Distributor's loss and are not a penalty. If the delay exceeds sixty (60) days, Distributor may cancel the affected Purchase Order without liability.

**7.3 Title and Risk.** Title to and risk of loss of each Product pass to Distributor on delivery under Section 7.1.

**7.4 Inspection and Acceptance.** Distributor shall inspect the Products within fifteen (15) Business Days after arrival at the port of delivery in the Territory. Distributor shall notify Supplier in writing of any shortage, visible damage or apparent non-conformity within that period. Supplier shall replace any missing, damaged or non-conforming Products at its own cost and as promptly as possible. Acceptance does not waive Distributor's rights under Section 12 for latent Defects.

**7.5 Documentation.** Supplier shall supply with each shipment a commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, certificate of conformity and a copy of the applicable type approval certificate. It shall also supply any other documents reasonably required for customs clearance in the United States.

**8.1 Scope.** Distributor may perform Installation Services for Products sold in the Territory, whether to Distributor's own Customers or, under Section 3.2, to Reserved Accounts.

**8.2 Statements of Work.** The Parties may enter into one or more Statements of Work describing the Installation Services Distributor will perform, the applicable service standards and acceptance procedures, the fees payable by Supplier to Distributor (where Supplier engages Distributor to perform services for a Reserved Account or under Supplier's warranty), and any other terms specific to the Installation Services. Each Statement of Work shall be numbered consecutively, shall refer to this Agreement and shall be signed by authorized representatives of both Parties. Each Statement of Work is governed by and incorporates the terms of this Agreement.

**8.3 Installation Standards.** Distributor shall perform all Installation Services in a good and workmanlike manner, in accordance with Supplier's installation manuals and commissioning procedures then in effect, the requirements of the applicable classification society and Applicable Law. Distributor shall use only technicians who have completed Supplier's training under Section 4.3(a).

**8.4 Commissioning Records.** For each Product it installs, Distributor shall complete Supplier's commissioning checklist and deliver a copy to Supplier within ten (10) Business Days after commissioning, together with the vessel name, IMO number, flag, Product serial number and date of commissioning. Supplier shall use the information to register the warranty under Section 12 and for no other purpose except as permitted by Section 15.

**8.5 Supplier Attendance.** Where a Statement of Work or a classification society requires attendance by Supplier's field service engineer at commissioning, Supplier shall provide that attendance in accordance with Section 4.3(d).
