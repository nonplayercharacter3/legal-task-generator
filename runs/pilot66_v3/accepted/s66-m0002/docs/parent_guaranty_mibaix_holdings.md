# PARENT COMPANY GUARANTY

This **PARENT COMPANY GUARANTY** (this "**Guaranty**") is given as of September 15, 2026 by:

**MIBAIX HOLDINGS CORP.**, a Delaware corporation, with its principal place of business at 2250 Clinton Drive, Suite 400, Houston, Texas 77020, United States ("**Guarantor**"),

in favor of:

**BUPAIV BALLAST TECHNOLOGIES LTD.**, a private limited company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 08814372, with its registered office at Unit 4, Quayside Business Park, Southampton SO14 3TG, United Kingdom ("**Supplier**").

## RECITALS

A. Supplier and Mibaix Marine Systems LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Distributor**"), are parties to a Distribution Agreement dated as of March 3, 2025. Under that agreement, Supplier appointed Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory. On the date of this Guaranty, Supplier and Distributor entered into Amendment No. 1 to that agreement.

B. Guarantor owns all of the issued and outstanding limited liability company interests in Distributor. Guarantor has entered into a share purchase agreement dated August 28, 2026 with Nordhaven Maritime Group plc. Under that agreement, Nordhaven Maritime Group plc will acquire all of the issued and outstanding shares of Guarantor, and closing is expected in the first quarter of 2027.

C. Section 5.2 of Amendment No. 1 requires Distributor to procure that Guarantor executes and delivers to Supplier a guaranty of Distributor's payment obligations under the Distribution Agreement. The increase in Distributor's revolving credit limit under Section 6.8 of the Distribution Agreement from US$1,500,000 to US$2,500,000 takes effect when Supplier receives this executed Guaranty.

D. Guarantor will derive substantial direct and indirect benefit from the continued supply of Products to Distributor on credit and from the increase in Distributor's credit limit. Guarantor is willing to give this Guaranty to induce Supplier to extend that credit.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the premises, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, Guarantor agrees as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Guaranty:

"**Agreement**" means the Distribution Agreement dated as of March 3, 2025 between Supplier and Distributor, as amended by Amendment No. 1 dated September 15, 2026, and as further amended, supplemented, extended or renewed from time to time in accordance with its terms.

"**Business Day**" has the meaning given in the Agreement.

"**Demand**" means a written demand for payment made by Supplier under Section 3.1.

"**Guaranteed Obligations**" means all present and future obligations of Distributor to pay money to Supplier under the Agreement, whether actual or contingent, and whether owed alone or jointly with any other person. These include:

(a) the price of all Products ordered under Purchase Orders accepted by Supplier and delivered in accordance with the Agreement;

(b) all freight, insurance, handling and other charges that Distributor must reimburse to Supplier under Section 7.1 of the Agreement;

(c) all fees payable to Supplier for field service engineer attendance under Section 4.3(d) of the Agreement;

(d) all interest accruing on late payments under Section 6.6 of the Agreement, including interest accruing after the commencement of any Insolvency Proceeding involving Distributor, whether or not allowed as a claim in that proceeding; and

(e) all costs and expenses payable by Guarantor under Section 11.4.

"**Insolvency Proceeding**" means any voluntary or involuntary proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency, reorganization, receivership, liquidation or similar law of any jurisdiction. It includes any case under Title 11 of the United States Code and any assignment for the benefit of creditors.

**1.2 Agreement Definitions.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Guaranty have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

**1.3 Interpretation.** In this Guaranty: (a) headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation; (b) "including" and similar words mean "including without limitation"; (c) references to Sections and Recitals are to sections of and recitals to this Guaranty unless stated otherwise; (d) the singular includes the plural and vice versa; and (e) references to any person include its successors and permitted assigns.

## 2. GUARANTY

**2.1 Guaranty of Payment.** Guarantor irrevocably and unconditionally guarantees to Supplier, as primary obligor and not merely as surety, the full and punctual payment when due of all Guaranteed Obligations. If Distributor fails to pay any Guaranteed Obligation when due, Guarantor shall pay that Guaranteed Obligation to Supplier on Demand.

**2.2 Payment, Not Collection.** This Guaranty is a guaranty of payment and not of collection. Guarantor's liability under this Guaranty is direct and independent of Distributor's obligations under the Agreement.

**2.3 Indemnity.** As a separate and independent obligation, Guarantor shall indemnify Supplier against any loss, cost or expense Supplier incurs because any Guaranteed Obligation is or becomes unenforceable, invalid or illegal against Distributor for any reason. The amount payable under this indemnity is the amount Supplier would otherwise have been entitled to recover from Distributor. This indemnity does not extend Guarantor's liability beyond the amounts Distributor would have owed under the Agreement if the Guaranteed Obligation had been enforceable.

**2.4 Defenses of Distributor.** Guarantor may assert against any Demand any defense, set-off or counterclaim that Distributor could assert against Supplier under the Agreement with respect to the relevant Guaranteed Obligation. This includes a good-faith invoice dispute raised under Section 6.5 of the Agreement. Guarantor may not, however, assert any defense arising from the incapacity, insolvency, dissolution or lack of authority of Distributor, or from any Insolvency Proceeding involving Distributor.

## 3. DEMAND AND PAYMENT

**3.1 Demand.** Supplier may make a Demand at any time after a Guaranteed Obligation has become due and remains unpaid for ten (10) Business Days after Supplier has given Distributor written notice of non-payment. Each Demand shall be in writing and signed by an authorized officer of Supplier. It shall identify the Guaranteed Obligations to which it relates, including the invoice numbers, invoice dates, due dates and the amounts outstanding. It shall also include a copy of the notice of non-payment given to Distributor.

**3.2 Payment Period.** Guarantor shall pay the amount stated in a valid Demand within ten (10) Business Days after receiving it.

**3.3 Multiple Demands.** Supplier may make more than one Demand under this Guaranty. A Demand for part of the Guaranteed Obligations does not prevent a later Demand for any other part.

**3.4 Currency.** Guarantor shall make all payments under this Guaranty in United States dollars, in immediately available funds, by wire transfer to the account Supplier designates in the Demand. If Supplier receives any amount in a currency other than United States dollars, whether under a judgment or otherwise, Guarantor's obligation is discharged only to the extent of the United States dollar amount Supplier is able to purchase with that amount on the Business Day after receipt, in accordance with normal banking procedures. Guarantor shall pay any shortfall as a separate obligation.

**3.5 Application of Payments.** Supplier shall apply each payment received from Guarantor to the Guaranteed Obligations identified in the relevant Demand, first to interest and then to principal, unless Guarantor and Supplier agree otherwise in writing.

## 4. NATURE OF OBLIGATIONS

**4.1 Continuing Guaranty.** This Guaranty is a continuing guaranty. It extends to the ultimate balance of the Guaranteed Obligations, regardless of any intermediate payment or discharge in whole or in part, and remains in effect until released in accordance with Section 12.

**4.2 Additional Security.** This Guaranty is in addition to, and not in substitution for, any other guaranty, security or right Supplier may hold at any time for the Guaranteed Obligations. Supplier need not enforce any other guaranty, security or right before making a Demand under this Guaranty.

**4.3 No Requirement to Pursue Distributor.** Supplier need not first take any action, bring any proceeding or obtain any judgment against Distributor, or file any claim in any Insolvency Proceeding involving Distributor, before making a Demand or enforcing this Guaranty, other than giving the notice of non-payment required by Section 3.1.

## 5. OBLIGATIONS NOT AFFECTED

**5.1 Matters Not Affecting Liability.** Guarantor's obligations under this Guaranty are not reduced, released or otherwise affected by any of the following, whether or not Guarantor knows of it or consents to it:

(a) any amendment, supplement, extension, renewal or replacement of the Agreement or any Statement of Work, including any change to the Products, the Price List, the credit limit or the payment terms, provided that Guarantor's liability for any increase in Distributor's revolving credit limit above US$2,500,000 requires Guarantor's written consent under Section 5.2;

(b) any time, waiver, indulgence or forbearance Supplier grants to Distributor;

(c) any release, compromise or settlement of any obligation of Distributor, other than a release or settlement in which Supplier expressly releases Guarantor in writing;

(d) any change in the name, constitution, legal form or ownership of Distributor or Guarantor;

(e) any merger, consolidation or amalgamation of Distributor or Guarantor with any other person;

(f) any Insolvency Proceeding involving Distributor, or any stay, discharge, disallowance or impairment of any Guaranteed Obligation in that proceeding;

(g) any invalidity, illegality or unenforceability of any Guaranteed Obligation, or any lack of capacity or authority of Distributor; or

(h) the expiration or termination of the Agreement, as to Guaranteed Obligations that arose before the expiration or termination or that arise afterwards under the provisions of the Agreement that survive it.

**5.2 Credit Limit.** Supplier may approve a revolving credit limit for Distributor higher than US$2,500,000 under Section 6.8 of the Agreement. If it does, Guarantor's liability for amounts outstanding in excess of US$2,500,000 at any time arises only if Guarantor has consented in writing to the higher limit before Supplier extends credit above US$2,500,000. Until Guarantor gives that consent, Guarantor's liability for the price of Products under clause (a) of the definition of Guaranteed Obligations is limited to US$2,500,000 at any one time, together with interest, costs and expenses relating to that amount.

## 6. WAIVERS

**6.1 Waiver of Notices.** To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, Guarantor waives: (a) notice of acceptance of this Guaranty; (b) notice of the creation, renewal, extension or accrual of any Guaranteed Obligation; (c) presentment, protest and notice of dishonor; and (d) any other notice or formality to which Guarantor might otherwise be entitled. This waiver does not apply to the Demand required by Section 3.1 and the copy of the notice of non-payment that must accompany it.

**6.2 Waiver of Suretyship Defenses.** To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, Guarantor waives any right to require Supplier to proceed against Distributor or any other person, to proceed against or exhaust any security, or to pursue any other remedy before proceeding against Guarantor. Guarantor also waives any defense based on any election of remedies by Supplier, subject to Section 2.4.

## 7. REINSTATEMENT

**7.1 Avoided Payments.** If any payment Supplier receives in respect of the Guaranteed Obligations, whether from Distributor, Guarantor or any other person, is avoided, rescinded or must otherwise be returned in any Insolvency Proceeding or for any other reason, this Guaranty is reinstated as to that payment. Guarantor's liability continues as if the payment had not been made.

**7.2 Survival of Reinstatement.** Section 7.1 applies even if this Guaranty has been released under Section 12 before the payment is avoided or returned, provided that Supplier notifies Guarantor in writing of the avoidance or return within two (2) years after the release.

## 8. SUBROGATION AND SUBORDINATION

**8.1 Subrogation.** Until all Guaranteed Obligations then due have been paid in full, Guarantor shall not exercise any right of subrogation, contribution, reimbursement or indemnity against Distributor that it acquires by making any payment under this Guaranty. After all Guaranteed Obligations then due have been paid in full, Guarantor is subrogated to Supplier's rights against Distributor to the extent of Guarantor's payments.

**8.2 Amounts Held in Trust.** If Guarantor receives any amount in exercise of a right referred to in Section 8.1 while any Guaranteed Obligation then due remains unpaid, Guarantor shall hold that amount on trust for Supplier and shall promptly pay it to Supplier for application to the Guaranteed Obligations.

**8.3 No Claims in Competition.** While any Guaranteed Obligation then due remains unpaid, Guarantor shall not prove in competition with Supplier in any Insolvency Proceeding involving Distributor for any amount Distributor owes to Guarantor in respect of payments under this Guaranty.

(c) the execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty have been duly authorized by all necessary corporate action on its part, including any required approval of its board of directors;

(d) this Guaranty has been duly executed and delivered by it and constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws affecting creditors' rights generally and to general principles of equity;

(e) the execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty do not violate its certificate of incorporation or bylaws or any law, regulation, judgment or order binding on it;

(f) no consent, approval or authorization of, or filing with, any governmental authority is required for it to execute, deliver and perform this Guaranty;

(g) no litigation, arbitration or administrative proceeding is pending or, to its knowledge, threatened against it that would reasonably be expected to have a material adverse effect on its ability to perform its obligations under this Guaranty; and

(h) it has received a copy of the Agreement and Amendment No. 1, and it has made its own independent assessment of the financial condition of Distributor without reliance on any information provided by Supplier.

**9.2 Solvency.** Guarantor represents and warrants that, after giving effect to this Guaranty, it is solvent, is able to pay its debts as they become due and has capital sufficient to carry on its business.

## 10. COVENANTS

**10.1 Financial Statements.** For as long as this Guaranty remains in effect, Guarantor shall deliver to Supplier:

(a) within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each of its fiscal years, a copy of its consolidated balance sheet as of the end of that year and its consolidated statements of income and cash flows for that year, audited by an independent certified public accounting firm; and

(b) within sixty (60) days after the end of each of the first three fiscal quarters of each fiscal year, a copy of its unaudited consolidated balance sheet and statement of income for that quarter, certified by its chief financial officer as fairly presenting its financial condition in all material respects.

**10.2 Confidentiality of Financial Information.** Supplier shall treat all financial statements and other financial information delivered under Section 10.1 as Confidential Information of Distributor for the purposes of Section 15 of the Agreement, and shall use that information only to monitor the credit extended to Distributor and to administer this Guaranty.

**10.3 Notice of Material Adverse Change.** Guarantor shall notify Supplier in writing within ten (10) Business Days after becoming aware of any event that would reasonably be expected to have a material adverse effect on its ability to perform its obligations under this Guaranty.

**10.4 Change of Name or Address.** Guarantor shall notify Supplier in writing at least ten (10) Business Days before any change in its legal name or its address for notices under Section 13.

## 11. PAYMENTS, TAXES AND EXPENSES

**11.1 No Set-Off by Guarantor.** Except as permitted by Section 2.4, Guarantor shall make all payments under this Guaranty without set-off or counterclaim.

**11.2 Taxes.** Guarantor shall make all payments under this Guaranty free and clear of, and without deduction for, any present or future taxes, levies, duties or withholdings, unless the deduction is required by law. If any deduction is required by law, Guarantor shall pay Supplier any additional amount necessary so that Supplier receives the full amount it would have received if no deduction had been required. Guarantor shall deliver to Supplier evidence of payment of the deducted amount to the relevant authority within thirty (30) days after the payment.

**11.3 Default Interest.** If Guarantor fails to pay any amount within the period stated in Section 3.2, the unpaid amount accrues interest from the end of that period until paid, at the rate stated in Section 6.6 of the Agreement. Interest accruing under this Section 11.3 does not duplicate interest that has accrued on the same amount under Section 6.6 of the Agreement.

**11.4 Enforcement Costs.** Guarantor shall pay Supplier, on demand, all reasonable and documented out-of-pocket costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, that Supplier incurs in successfully enforcing this Guaranty against Guarantor.

## 12. TERM AND RELEASE

**12.1 Term.** This Guaranty takes effect on its delivery to Supplier and remains in effect until the later of: (a) the expiration or termination of the Agreement; and (b) the date on which all Guaranteed Obligations that have arisen on or before that expiration or termination have been paid in full.

**12.2 Release.** Within ten (10) Business Days after the later date referred to in Section 12.1, Supplier shall deliver to Guarantor a written release of this Guaranty, subject to Section 7.

**12.3 Replacement Guaranty.** If Distributor provides Supplier with a replacement guaranty, letter of credit or other credit support reasonably acceptable to Supplier in a form and amount at least equivalent to this Guaranty, Supplier shall release Guarantor from this Guaranty, subject to Section 7, within ten (10) Business Days after receiving that replacement credit support. Supplier shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay its acceptance of replacement credit support offered under this Section 12.3.
