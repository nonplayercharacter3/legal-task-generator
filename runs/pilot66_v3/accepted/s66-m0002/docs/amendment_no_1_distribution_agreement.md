# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This **AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT** (this "**Amendment**") is entered into as of September 15, 2026 (the "**Amendment Effective Date**") by and between:

**BUPAIV BALLAST TECHNOLOGIES LTD.**, a private limited company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 08814372, with its registered office at Unit 4, Quayside Business Park, Southampton SO14 3TG, United Kingdom ("**Supplier**"); and

**MIBAIX MARINE SYSTEMS LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company, with its principal place of business at 2250 Clinton Drive, Suite 310, Houston, Texas 77020, United States ("**Distributor**").

Supplier and Distributor are each referred to in this Amendment as a "**Party**" and together as the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Supplier and Distributor are parties to a Distribution Agreement dated as of March 3, 2025 (the "**Agreement**"), under which Supplier appointed Distributor as its exclusive distributor of the Products in the Territory.

B. Supplier has developed the BX-900, a high-capacity filtration and UV ballast water treatment system for large tankers, bulk carriers and container vessels. The United States Coast Guard issued type approval for the BX-900 on July 22, 2026.

C. The Parties wish to add the BX-900 to the product range supplied under the Agreement, to set the price of the BX-900, to raise the Minimum Annual Purchase to reflect the expanded product range, to increase Distributor's revolving credit limit, and to make certain related changes to the Agreement.

D. Section 21.2 of the Agreement provides that the Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument that refers to the Agreement and is signed by authorized representatives of both Parties. This Amendment is such an instrument.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Amendment and in the Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The rules of interpretation in Section 1.2 of the Agreement apply to this Amendment as if set out in full. References in this Amendment to Sections and Schedules are to sections of and schedules to the Agreement, unless this Amendment states otherwise. References to Annexes are to the annexes to this Amendment.

**1.3 References to the Agreement.** From the Amendment Effective Date, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement," and each reference in any Statement of Work to "the Agreement," means the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

## 2. PRODUCTS

**2.1 Definition of Products.** The definition of "**Products**" in Section 1.1 of the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> "**Products**" means the ballast water treatment system models listed in Part 1 of Schedule A, as that Part is replaced by Amendment No. 1 to this Agreement and as further amended from time to time under Section 21.2.

**2.2 Schedule A, Part 1.** Part 1 (BWTS Units) of Schedule A to the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with Part 1 of Schedule A set out in Annex 1 to this Amendment.

**2.3 BX-900 Type Approval.** Supplier represents and warrants that, as of the Amendment Effective Date, the BX-900 holds United States Coast Guard type approval under certificate number 162.060/94/0, and that the certificate is in full force and effect and is not subject to any condition, limitation or pending review that would restrict the installation or operation of the BX-900 on vessels calling at ports in the Territory. Supplier shall deliver a copy of the type approval certificate and the accompanying approved operation, maintenance and safety manual to Distributor within five (5) Business Days after the Amendment Effective Date. The obligations of Supplier under Section 4.2 of the Agreement apply to the BX-900 from the Amendment Effective Date.

**2.4 BX-900 Specifications.** Supplier shall deliver to Distributor the Specifications for the BX-900, including its installation footprint, power requirements, piping interfaces, control system integration requirements and hazardous area classification, within ten (10) Business Days after the Amendment Effective Date. Supplier shall also deliver a set of generic vessel integration drawings for the BX-900 within that period, for use by Distributor in preparing retrofit proposals.

**2.5 BX-900 Training.** In addition to the training required by Section 4.3(a) of the Agreement, Supplier shall provide, at no charge to Distributor, BX-900 installation and commissioning training for up to eight (8) of Distributor's technicians. Supplier shall complete that training at Distributor's facility in Houston, Texas, or at another location in the Territory agreed by the Parties, by no later than November 30, 2026. Supplier shall bear the travel and accommodation costs of its own trainers. Each Party shall bear the costs of its own personnel attending the training. Technicians who complete the BX-900 training are trained to Supplier's installation standards for the purposes of Sections 3.3 and 8.3 of the Agreement with respect to the BX-900.

**2.6 First Commissionings.** For each of the first three (3) BX-900 units that Distributor commissions in the Territory, Supplier shall make a field service engineer available to attend commissioning, on Distributor's request, without charge for the engineer's day rate or travel expenses. Supplier's attendance does not reduce Distributor's responsibilities under Section 8.3 of the Agreement for the Installation Services it performs.

**2.7 Lead Times.** For Purchase Orders for the BX-900 accepted during the period from the Amendment Effective Date until March 2, 2027, the confirmed delivery date stated in Supplier's acceptance under Section 6.2 of the Agreement shall be no later than twenty (20) weeks after the date of the Purchase Order. For Purchase Orders for the BX-900 accepted after that period, the confirmed delivery date shall be no later than sixteen (16) weeks after the date of the Purchase Order. Section 7.2 of the Agreement applies to the BX-900 from the Amendment Effective Date.

## 3. MINIMUM ANNUAL PURCHASE

**3.1 Revised Minimum Annual Purchase.** The first sentence of Section 5.1 of the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> In each Contract Year, Distributor shall purchase from Supplier US$6,200,000 of Products per contract year, measured by aggregate net invoice value (the "**Minimum Annual Purchase**").

**3.2 Application.** The Minimum Annual Purchase stated in Section 3.1 of this Amendment applies to the Contract Year that begins on March 3, 2027 and to each Contract Year after that. For the Contract Year that began on March 3, 2026 and ends on March 2, 2027, the Minimum Annual Purchase remains US$4,800,000, as stated in Section 5.1 of the Agreement before this Amendment.

**3.3 Credit for BX-900 Purchase Orders.** For the purposes of Section 5.2 of the Agreement, BX-900 units ordered under a Purchase Order accepted before March 3, 2027 count toward the Minimum Annual Purchase for the Contract Year in which they are invoiced, even if they are invoiced in the Contract Year that begins on March 3, 2027.

**3.4 Renewal.** Section 2.3 of the Agreement continues to apply. For the purposes of that Section, the Minimum Annual Purchase for the final Contract Year of the Initial Term is the amount stated in Section 3.1 of this Amendment.

**3.5 Forecasts.** Distributor's first rolling forecast under Section 5.4 of the Agreement after the Amendment Effective Date shall include a separate line for the BX-900. Section 5.5 of the Agreement applies to the BX-900 from that forecast onward, except that during the period from the Amendment Effective Date until March 2, 2027, Supplier shall maintain sufficient manufacturing capacity to supply at least one hundred percent (100%) of the BX-900 quantities in Distributor's most recent forecast.

## 4. PRICING

**4.1 Schedule B, Part 1.** Part 1 (BWTS Units) of Schedule B to the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with Part 1 of Schedule B set out in Annex 2 to this Amendment.

**4.2 Price Adjustments.** The price of the BX-900 stated in Annex 2 is subject to adjustment under Section 6.3 of the Agreement. The first adjustment to the BX-900 price may take effect no earlier than March 3, 2028. The prices of the BX-300, BX-500 and BX-700 stated in Annex 2 reflect the adjustment notified by Supplier under Section 6.3 of the Agreement and effective on March 3, 2026, and this Amendment does not change those prices.

**4.3 Introductory Allowance.** For each BX-900 unit ordered under a Purchase Order accepted on or before March 2, 2027, Supplier shall issue Distributor a credit note equal to four percent (4%) of the unit price stated in Annex 2. Supplier shall issue the credit note at the same time as the invoice for the unit. Distributor may apply the credit note against any invoice issued by Supplier under the Agreement. The Minimum Annual Purchase credit for each such unit is measured by its net invoice value after applying the credit note.

**4.4 Most Favoured Pricing.** Section 6.4 of the Agreement applies to the BX-900 from the Amendment Effective Date.

## 5. CREDIT LIMIT

**5.1 Increase in Credit Limit.** In Section 6.8 of the Agreement, the reference to "US$1,500,000" is deleted and replaced with "US$2,500,000."

**5.2 Credit Support.** Distributor shall procure that Mibaix Holdings Corp. executes and delivers to Supplier, on or about the Amendment Effective Date, a guaranty of Distributor's payment obligations under the Agreement in favor of Supplier. The increase in the credit limit under Section 5.1 of this Amendment takes effect on the date Supplier receives that executed guaranty. Supplier confirms that, once the guaranty is delivered, it will not require any further credit support under Section 6.8 of the Agreement as a condition of extending credit up to the revised credit limit.

## 6. LAUNCH AND MARKETING

**6.1 Launch Program.** The Parties shall cooperate in a joint launch program for the BX-900 in the Territory during the period from the Amendment Effective Date until June 30, 2027. As part of that program:

(a) Supplier shall provide Distributor with BX-900 sales literature, technical brochures and a product video, in English and Spanish, free of charge;

(b) Supplier shall exhibit a BX-900 demonstration skid at one maritime trade event in the Territory selected by Distributor, and shall bear the costs of transporting the skid to and from that event; and

(c) Distributor shall present the BX-900 to the vessel owners, operators and ship managers with which it has a current commercial relationship in the Territory.

**6.2 Trade Events.** Attendance at the trade event referred to in Section 6.1(b) of this Amendment counts toward Distributor's obligation under Section 9.3 of the Agreement for the Contract Year in which the event takes place.

**6.3 Trademarks.** Schedule C to the Agreement is amended by adding the following row at the end of the table:

| Mark | Registration |
|---|---|
| BX-900 (word mark) | U.S. Application Serial No. 98/447,215 (pending) |

The license in Section 9.1 of the Agreement extends to that mark from the Amendment Effective Date.

**6.4 Website Listing.** Within fifteen (15) Business Days after the Amendment Effective Date, Supplier shall update the distributor listing on its website under Section 4.4 of the Agreement so that it identifies Distributor as Supplier's exclusive distributor of the BX-900 in the Territory.

## 7. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

**7.1 Mutual Representations.** Each Party represents and warrants to the other that:

(a) it is duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the jurisdiction of its organization;

(b) it has full corporate or limited liability company power and authority to execute and deliver this Amendment and to perform its obligations under the Agreement as amended by this Amendment;

(c) the execution, delivery and performance of this Amendment have been duly authorized by all necessary corporate or limited liability company action on its part;

(d) this Amendment has been duly executed and delivered by it and constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws affecting creditors' rights generally and to general principles of equity; and

(e) the execution, delivery and performance of this Amendment do not violate its organizational documents, any Applicable Law or any material agreement to which it is a party.

**7.2 Supplier Product Representation.** Supplier represents and warrants that the BX-900, when manufactured in accordance with the Specifications and installed and operated in accordance with Supplier's instructions, will meet the discharge standards of 33 C.F.R. § 151.2030 and the requirements of its type approval. The warranties in Section 12.1 of the Agreement apply to each BX-900 unit supplied under the Agreement.

**7.3 No Default.** Each Party confirms that, as of the Amendment Effective Date, it has not given the other Party any notice of breach under Section 17.1 of the Agreement that remains outstanding.

## 8. GENERAL

**8.1 Effect of Amendment.** Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect. This Amendment forms part of the Agreement, and the Agreement and this Amendment are to be read and construed together as one instrument.

**8.2 Statements of Work.** Each Statement of Work in effect on the Amendment Effective Date remains in full force and effect. The Parties may enter into Statements of Work for Installation Services for the BX-900 under Section 8.2 of the Agreement.

**8.3 Governing Law and Forum.** This Amendment is governed by Section 19 of the Agreement, which applies to this Amendment as if set out in full.

**8.4 Notices.** Notices under this Amendment shall be given in accordance with Section 21.1 of the Agreement.

**8.5 Costs.** Each Party bears its own costs and expenses in connection with the negotiation, preparation and execution of this Amendment.

**8.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Amendment is held invalid, illegal or unenforceable in any jurisdiction, Section 21.4 of the Agreement applies to that provision.

**8.7 Headings.** Headings in this Amendment are for convenience only and do not affect its interpretation.

**8.8 Counterparts and Electronic Signature.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, each of which is an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by PDF or through an electronic signature service are as effective as original signatures.
