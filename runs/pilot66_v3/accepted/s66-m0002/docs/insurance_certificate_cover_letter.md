## 4. Summary of Coverage

4.1 **Commercial general liability.** Mibaix's commercial general liability policy is written on an occurrence basis on ISO form CG 00 01 04 13, with manuscript endorsements. The limits are:

(a) US$5,000,000 each occurrence;

(b) US$5,000,000 general aggregate, applying per project and per location;

(c) US$5,000,000 products-completed operations aggregate;

(d) US$5,000,000 personal and advertising injury; and

(e) US$100,000 damage to premises rented to the insured.

These limits are written as primary limits under a single policy. They are not built up with an umbrella or excess layer. Products-completed operations coverage is included and is not excluded for marine or vessel work. The policy contains the Watercraft Exclusion amendment described in Attachment B. That amendment restores coverage for work Mibaix performs on vessels it does not own or operate.

4.2 **Ship repairer's legal liability.** Mibaix's ship repairer's legal liability policy is written on the American Institute Ship Repairer's Liability Clauses form, with Mibaix's scope endorsement. The limit is US$5,000,000 each occurrence. The policy covers Mibaix's legal liability for physical loss of or damage to vessels in its care, custody or control while Mibaix or its subcontractors perform repair, installation, conversion or commissioning work on them. This includes work performed afloat at berth or anchorage, and in shipyards and dry docks within the navigation limits of the policy. The navigation limits include all ports and waters of the United States Gulf of Mexico coast from Brownsville, Texas to Key West, Florida.

4.3 **Workers' compensation and employer's liability.** Mibaix's workers' compensation policy provides statutory coverage in Texas, Louisiana and Alabama, with other-states coverage under Part Three. The policy carries the United States Longshore and Harbor Workers' Compensation Act endorsement (WC 00 01 06 A) on an "if any" basis for all covered states. The employer's liability limits are US$1,000,000 each accident, US$1,000,000 disease each employee and US$1,000,000 disease policy limit.

4.4 **Maritime employer's liability.** Mibaix also carries maritime employer's liability coverage, scheduled on the marine liability certificate, with a limit of US$1,000,000 each accident. This coverage responds to claims by employees who may be found to be seamen under the Jones Act while working aboard vessels during installation and commissioning. The coverage is not required by the Agreement. We have shown it because Mibaix's technicians work aboard vessels, and your field service team may find it useful to know the coverage is in place.

4.5 **Automobile liability.** Mibaix's business automobile policy covers any auto (Symbol 1) and has a combined single limit of US$1,000,000 each accident for bodily injury and property damage. Hired and non-owned auto liability is included.

## 8. Coverage for Installation Services under Statement of Work No. 1

8.1 Section 10.1 of the SOW requires Mibaix to maintain the insurance required by Section 14.2 of the Agreement throughout the term of the SOW. It also requires the ship repairer's legal liability insurance to cover the Installation Services performed under the SOW. We have confirmed with Atlantic Mutual Marine Underwriters, Inc. that the scope of operations described in the policy includes:

> Installation, integration, retrofit, commissioning, start-up testing, calibration, repair and warranty service of ballast water treatment systems and related equipment aboard commercial vessels, including fabrication and installation of foundations and supports, piping tie-ins, electrical and control cabling, hydrostatic testing and associated hot work, whether performed by the insured or its subcontractors.

8.2 That description matches the services described in Sections 3.2 through 3.5 of the SOW. Hot work is covered, provided that the insured complies with the hot work permit and marine chemist certificate procedures of the vessel and the site. Section 9.3 of the SOW already requires Mibaix to comply with those procedures. The policy has no exclusion for work performed for Reserved Accounts or for warranty and recall work.

8.3 Work performed by subcontractors is covered under the ship repairer's legal liability policy and the commercial general liability policy, provided that the subcontractor is engaged under a written subcontract. Mibaix's standard subcontract requires each subcontractor to carry its own commercial general liability insurance of at least US$1,000,000 each occurrence and to name Mibaix as an additional insured. These subcontractor requirements are in addition to, and do not replace, Mibaix's own coverage.

8.4 Section 10.2 of the SOW requires Mibaix, on request, to provide Supplier or an Owner with a certificate for a particular Job. We can issue job-specific certificates naming a vessel Owner, shipyard or dock operator as certificate holder, usually within one (1) Business Day of a request. Requests should be sent to the email address in Section 12.1 and should state the vessel name, IMO number, Site and Work Order number.
