# OFFICE LEASE

**Dispatch Center Premises: 1700 Spring Garden Street, Suite 300, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania**

This Office Lease (this "**Lease**") is made as of July 14, 2023 (the "**Lease Date**") between:

**Spring Garden Commerce Partners, L.P.**, a Pennsylvania limited partnership with its principal office at 1700 Spring Garden Street, Suite 1200, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania 19130 ("**Landlord**"); and

**Rekaind Vertical Services, Inc.**, a Pennsylvania corporation with its principal place of business at 2401 Walnut Street, Suite 600, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania 19103 ("**Tenant**").

Landlord and Tenant are each a "**party**" and together the "**parties**".

## 2. PREMISES AND COMMON AREAS

2.1 **Lease of Premises.** Landlord leases the Premises to Tenant, and Tenant leases the Premises from Landlord, for the Term and on the terms of this Lease.

2.2 **Common Areas.** Tenant and its employees, contractors and invitees may use, in common with others, the Building lobby, elevators, stairways, corridors, restrooms, loading dock, garage and other areas that Landlord designates for the general use of tenants (the "**Common Areas**"). Landlord may change the Common Areas from time to time, provided that no change materially impairs access to, or use of, the Premises for the Permitted Use.

2.3 **Measurement.** The rentable areas stated in Section 1.1 have been measured in accordance with the BOMA Office Standard (ANSI/BOMA Z65.1-2017) and are not subject to remeasurement during the Term, except to reflect a change in the physical size of the Premises or the Building.

2.4 **Condition.** Subject to Landlord's obligations under Section 3 and Section 9, Tenant accepts the Premises in their "as is" condition on the Lease Date. Landlord represents that, on the Commencement Date, the Building structure, roof, and base building mechanical, electrical, plumbing, fire-life-safety and vertical transportation systems serving the Premises will be in good working order and in compliance with applicable codes.

## 3. TENANT IMPROVEMENTS

3.1 **Tenant Improvement Allowance.** Landlord shall provide Tenant an allowance of $55.00 per rentable square foot of the Premises, totaling $463,100.00 (the "**Allowance**"), toward the cost of designing and constructing Tenant's initial improvements (the "**Tenant Improvements**"), as described in the work letter attached as Exhibit B (the "**Work Letter**").

3.2 **Use of Allowance.** The Allowance may be applied to hard construction costs, architectural and engineering fees, permit fees, project management fees, and cabling, security and audio-visual installations. Up to $10.00 per rentable square foot of the Allowance may be applied to furniture, fixtures and equipment, including dispatch consoles.

3.3 **Disbursement.** Landlord shall disburse the Allowance in progress payments, no more often than monthly, within thirty (30) days after receiving Tenant's draw request together with invoices, lien waivers and an architect's certification of the work completed. Any portion of the Allowance not requested by the end of Lease Year 1 is forfeited.

3.4 **Approvals.** Tenant shall submit its plans and specifications for the Tenant Improvements to Landlord for approval. Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay its approval, and shall respond within ten (10) business days after each submission.

3.5 **Dispatch Center Infrastructure.** Landlord consents to the following Tenant Improvements, subject to approval of plans under Section 3.4:
(a) a dedicated server and telecommunications room with supplemental cooling;
(b) a raised-floor dispatch area;
(c) redundant telecommunications entrances to the Premises from two separate risers; and
(d) the generator connection described in Section 9.4.

## 4. TERM

4.1 **Term.** The Term begins on the Commencement Date and ends on the Expiration Date, unless terminated earlier or extended in accordance with this Lease.

4.2 **Early Access.** Tenant may enter the Premises from the Lease Date to construct the Tenant Improvements and install its cabling, equipment and furniture. Tenant's early access is subject to all provisions of this Lease other than the obligation to pay Base Rent and Additional Rent.

4.3 **Commencement Date Memorandum.** Within thirty (30) days after the Commencement Date, the parties shall sign a memorandum confirming the Commencement Date and Expiration Date in the form of Exhibit C.

4.4 **Renewal Option.** Tenant has one (1) option to extend the Term for a single period of five (5) years (the "**Renewal Term**"). To exercise the option, Tenant must give Landlord written notice not less than twelve (12) months and not more than eighteen (18) months before the Expiration Date. Tenant must not be in default beyond any applicable cure period on the date of its notice or on the first day of the Renewal Term. Base Rent for the first year of the Renewal Term shall equal ninety-five percent (95%) of the Fair Market Rent determined under Section 4.5, and shall increase by two and one-half percent (2.5%) on each anniversary thereafter. All other terms of this Lease apply during the Renewal Term, except that Tenant has no further renewal option and no tenant improvement allowance is payable unless the parties agree otherwise.

4.5 **Fair Market Rent.** "**Fair Market Rent**" means the annual rental rate per rentable square foot at which a willing tenant would lease comparable space in comparable Class A and Class B office buildings in the Callowhill, Spring Garden and Center City North submarkets of Philadelphia for a comparable term, taking into account all relevant concessions. If the parties have not agreed on Fair Market Rent within thirty (30) days after Tenant's exercise notice, each party shall appoint a licensed commercial real estate broker with at least ten (10) years' experience in the Philadelphia office market. If the two brokers' determinations differ by not more than five percent (5%), Fair Market Rent is their average. Otherwise, the two brokers shall appoint a third broker, who shall select whichever of the two determinations is closer to Fair Market Rent. Each party bears the cost of its own broker and half the cost of the third broker.

## 5. RENT AND ADDITIONAL RENT

5.1 **Payment of Base Rent.** Tenant shall pay Base Rent in equal monthly installments, in advance, on the first day of each calendar month, without demand and without set-off or deduction except as this Lease expressly permits. Rent for any partial month is prorated on a daily basis.

5.2 **Operating Expenses.** Beginning on the first anniversary of the Commencement Date, Tenant shall pay, as additional rent ("**Additional Rent**"), Tenant's Share of the amount by which Operating Expenses for each calendar year exceed Operating Expenses for the Base Year. "**Operating Expenses**" means the reasonable and customary costs Landlord incurs to operate, maintain, repair, insure and manage the Property, determined in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles consistently applied.

5.3 **Exclusions.** Operating Expenses exclude:
(a) capital expenditures, except the annual amortized portion of capital improvements that reduce Operating Expenses (to the extent of the savings) or are required by laws first enacted after the Lease Date;
(b) costs of leasing, including brokerage commissions, legal fees and tenant improvement costs for other tenants;
(c) principal, interest and other financing costs;
(d) depreciation;
(e) costs reimbursed by insurance, warranties or other tenants;
(f) management fees exceeding three percent (3%) of the gross revenues of the Property;
(g) costs arising from Landlord's negligence or breach of law or of any lease;
(h) charitable and political contributions; and
(i) costs of remediating hazardous substances present at the Property before the Commencement Date.

5.4 **Taxes.** Beginning on the first anniversary of the Commencement Date, Tenant shall pay, as Additional Rent, Tenant's Share of the amount by which real estate taxes and assessments on the Property for each calendar year exceed those for the Base Year. Taxes exclude Landlord's income, franchise, transfer, estate and inheritance taxes, and any penalties for late payment.

5.5 **Gross-Up.** If the Building is less than ninety-five percent (95%) occupied during any calendar year, including the Base Year, Operating Expenses that vary with occupancy shall be adjusted to reflect ninety-five percent (95%) occupancy.

5.6 **Controllable Expense Cap.** Tenant's Share of Controllable Operating Expenses shall not increase by more than five percent (5%) per calendar year on a cumulative, compounding basis over the Base Year. "**Controllable Operating Expenses**" means all Operating Expenses other than insurance premiums, utilities, snow removal, and costs of complying with laws first enacted after the Lease Date.

5.7 **Estimates and Reconciliation.** Landlord shall give Tenant a reasonable estimate of Additional Rent before each calendar year, and Tenant shall pay one-twelfth (1/12) of the estimate monthly with Base Rent. Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each calendar year, Landlord shall deliver a statement of actual Operating Expenses and taxes. Any overpayment shall be credited against the next amounts due or, after the end of the Term, refunded within thirty (30) days. Tenant shall pay any underpayment within thirty (30) days after receipt of the statement.

5.8 **Audit.** Tenant may, at its own cost and on thirty (30) days' notice given within one hundred eighty (180) days after receipt of an annual statement, inspect Landlord's records for that year at Landlord's management office. If the audit shows that Landlord overstated Tenant's Share of Operating Expenses by more than four percent (4%), Landlord shall reimburse Tenant's reasonable audit costs in addition to refunding the overpayment.

5.9 **Late Charge.** If any installment of rent is not received within five (5) days after its due date, Tenant shall pay a late charge of four percent (4%) of the overdue amount. Landlord shall waive the first late charge in any twelve (12) month period if Tenant pays within five (5) days after written notice.

5.10 **Place of Payment.** Tenant shall pay rent by ACH or wire transfer to the account Landlord designates in writing, or by check to Landlord's management office at the address in Section 23.

## 6. SECURITY DEPOSIT

6.1 **Deposit.** On signing this Lease, Tenant shall deposit the Security Deposit with Landlord as security for the performance of Tenant's obligations. The Security Deposit equals one month of Base Rent at the Lease Year 7 rate plus an additional amount equal to one half of one month of Base Rent at the Lease Year 1 rate, rounded to the nearest cent, and is not a limit on Tenant's liability.

6.2 **Application.** If Tenant defaults beyond any applicable cure period, Landlord may apply all or part of the Security Deposit to cure the default. Tenant shall restore the Security Deposit to its full amount within ten (10) days after written demand.

6.3 **Return.** Landlord shall return the unapplied balance of the Security Deposit to Tenant within thirty (30) days after the end of the Term and Tenant's surrender of the Premises in accordance with Section 20. Landlord shall hold the Security Deposit in accordance with the Pennsylvania Landlord and Tenant Act of 1951, to the extent applicable.

6.4 **Transfer.** If Landlord transfers its interest in the Property, Landlord shall transfer the Security Deposit to the transferee, and Landlord is then released from liability for its return upon the transferee's written assumption of that liability.

## 7. USE

7.1 **Permitted Use.** Tenant shall use the Premises only for the Permitted Use and for no other purpose without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

7.2 **Twenty-Four Hour Operation.** Landlord acknowledges that Tenant operates the Premises as a dispatch center on a twenty-four (24) hour, seven (7) day, year-round basis. Tenant and its employees and contractors shall have access to the Premises, the Building garage and the elevators serving the third floor at all times, subject only to Landlord's reasonable security procedures and to emergencies. Landlord shall issue access cards to Tenant's personnel on request at no charge for the first sixty (60) cards.

7.3 **Compliance with Laws.** Tenant shall comply with all laws applicable to Tenant's particular use of the Premises. Landlord shall comply with all laws applicable to the Building structure, the Common Areas and the base building systems, including the Americans with Disabilities Act and the City of Philadelphia building and fire codes, the cost of which shall be included in Operating Expenses only to the extent Section 5.3 permits.

7.4 **Rules and Regulations.** Tenant shall comply with the Building rules and regulations attached as Exhibit D, as Landlord may reasonably amend them on written notice. Landlord shall enforce the rules in a non-discriminatory manner. If a rule conflicts with this Lease, this Lease governs.

7.5 **Hazardous Materials.** Tenant shall not bring hazardous substances onto the Property, other than customary office and cleaning supplies and batteries for uninterruptible power supply equipment, each stored and used in compliance with law.

## 8. SERVICES AND UTILITIES

8.1 **Base Building Services.** Landlord shall provide the following services, the cost of which is included in Operating Expenses:
(a) heating, ventilation and air conditioning to the Premises from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. on weekdays and 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. on Saturdays, excluding Building holidays ("**Building Hours**");
(b) passenger elevator service at all times and freight elevator service during Building Hours, with after-hours freight service on reasonable advance request;
(c) janitorial service five (5) nights per week in accordance with the specifications in Exhibit E;
(d) hot and cold water for restrooms and kitchenettes; and
(e) lobby security staffing at all times.

8.2 **After-Hours HVAC.** Landlord shall provide HVAC outside Building Hours on Tenant's request at Landlord's actual cost, without markup, which on the Lease Date is $65.00 per hour per floor zone.

8.3 **Supplemental Cooling.** Tenant may install, as part of the Tenant Improvements, a supplemental cooling unit for its server and telecommunications room, connected to the Building condenser water loop. Landlord shall provide condenser water to that unit at all times. Tenant shall pay a condenser water charge of $750.00 per ton per year.

8.4 **Electricity.** Electricity for the Premises shall be measured by a separate submeter installed as part of the Tenant Improvements. Tenant shall pay Landlord for electricity consumed at Landlord's actual cost, without markup, within thirty (30) days after each monthly invoice.

8.5 **Telecommunications.** Tenant may contract directly with telecommunications and internet providers of its choice. Landlord shall provide reasonable access to the Building risers, telecommunications rooms and roof for Tenant's providers, subject to Landlord's standard access agreement.

8.6 **Interruption of Services.** Landlord is not liable for any interruption of services caused by events beyond its reasonable control, except as follows. If any interruption of a service Landlord is required to provide, not caused by Tenant, renders all or a material part of the Premises untenantable for more than three (3) consecutive business days, rent shall abate in proportion to the area affected from the fourth business day until the service is restored.

## 9. REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE; EMERGENCY POWER

9.1 **Landlord's Repairs.** Landlord shall maintain and repair, in good condition and in compliance with law, the Building structure, roof, exterior walls, foundation, Common Areas, and base building mechanical, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, fire-life-safety and vertical transportation systems up to their point of connection to the Premises.

9.2 **Tenant's Repairs.** Tenant shall maintain the interior of the Premises, the Tenant Improvements, its supplemental cooling unit and its own equipment in good condition, ordinary wear and tear and casualty excepted.

9.3 **Access by Landlord.** Landlord may enter the Premises on not less than twenty-four (24) hours' notice, and without notice in an emergency, to inspect, make repairs, or show the Premises to prospective purchasers, lenders and, during the last twelve (12) months of the Term, prospective tenants. Landlord shall minimize interference with Tenant's operations and shall not enter the dispatch floor or the server room except accompanied by Tenant's representative, other than in an emergency.

9.4 **Emergency Generator.** Landlord grants Tenant the right, during the Term, to connect to the Building's emergency generator for up to one hundred fifty (150) kilowatts of standby power for the Premises, through an automatic transfer switch installed as part of the Tenant Improvements. Tenant shall pay a generator charge of $450.00 per month and its share of the cost of fuel consumed during actual outages and testing, prorated by connected load. Landlord shall maintain the generator in good working order, test it at least monthly, and keep at least forty-eight (48) hours of fuel on site at all times. Landlord shall notify Tenant at least forty-eight (48) hours before any scheduled load test or generator shutdown.

9.5 **Uninterruptible Power Supply.** Tenant may install uninterruptible power supply equipment in its server and telecommunications room, at its own cost, subject to Landlord's approval of the location and floor loading, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

## 10. ALTERATIONS

10.1 **Consent.** After completion of the Tenant Improvements, Tenant shall not make alterations to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed. Landlord's consent is not required for cosmetic alterations, such as painting, carpeting and installation of furniture and equipment, that cost less than $40,000.00 in any single project and do not affect the Building structure or systems.

10.2 **Performance.** Tenant shall perform all alterations in a good and workmanlike manner, using licensed and insured contractors, and in compliance with law and the Building rules.

10.3 **Liens.** Tenant shall keep the Property free of mechanics' liens arising from work performed for Tenant. Tenant shall cause any such lien to be discharged or bonded within thirty (30) days after notice of its filing.

10.4 **Removal.** At the time Landlord approves an alteration, Landlord shall state whether Tenant must remove it at the end of the Term. Tenant is not required to remove any part of the Tenant Improvements other than its raised-floor dispatch area, its supplemental cooling unit and its above-standard cabling.

## 11. SIGNAGE

11.1 **Signage.** Landlord shall list Tenant in the Building lobby directory and provide Building-standard suite-entry signage at Landlord's cost. Tenant may install its name and logo on the suite-entry door, subject to Landlord's reasonable approval of the design.

## 12. INSURANCE

12.1 **Tenant's Insurance.** During the Term, Tenant shall maintain, with insurers authorized to do business in Pennsylvania and rated A- VIII or better by A.M. Best:
(a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate, which may be satisfied by a combination of primary and umbrella coverage;
(b) property insurance on the Tenant Improvements and Tenant's personal property for their full replacement cost;
(c) business interruption insurance sufficient to cover at least twelve (12) months of rent; and
(d) workers' compensation insurance at statutory limits and employer's liability insurance with limits of not less than $500,000.

12.2 **Additional Insureds.** Tenant shall name Landlord, its property manager and any mortgagee of which Tenant is notified as additional insureds under its commercial general liability policy. Tenant shall deliver certificates evidencing the required coverage before first entering the Premises and on each policy renewal.

12.3 **Landlord's Insurance.** Landlord shall maintain all-risk property insurance on the Building for its full replacement cost, excluding the Tenant Improvements, and commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $5,000,000 per occurrence. The cost of Landlord's insurance is included in Operating Expenses.

12.4 **Waiver of Subrogation.** Each party waives, and shall cause its property insurers to waive, all rights of recovery against the other party for loss or damage to property to the extent that loss is covered, or required by this Lease to be covered, by property insurance.

## 13. INDEMNIFICATION

13.1 **By Tenant.** Subject to Section 12.4, Tenant shall indemnify and hold Landlord harmless from third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage occurring in the Premises, or arising from the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its employees or contractors on the Property, except to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord or its agents.

13.2 **By Landlord.** Subject to Section 12.4, Landlord shall indemnify and hold Tenant harmless from third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage occurring in the Common Areas, or arising from the negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord or its agents, employees or contractors, except to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its employees or contractors.

13.3 **Survival.** This Section 13 survives the expiration or termination of this Lease as to events occurring before it.

## 14. CASUALTY

14.1 **Restoration.** If the Premises or the Building are damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord shall, within sixty (60) days, give Tenant a contractor's estimate of the time required to restore them. If the estimate exceeds two hundred seventy (270) days from the casualty, either party may terminate this Lease by written notice within thirty (30) days after receiving the estimate. Otherwise, Landlord shall restore the base building and the Premises (excluding Tenant's personal property) with reasonable diligence.

14.2 **Abatement.** Rent abates in proportion to the area of the Premises rendered untenantable by the casualty, from the date of the casualty until the Premises are restored, unless the casualty was caused by Tenant's negligence.

14.3 **End-of-Term Casualty.** If a casualty materially damages the Premises during the last twelve (12) months of the Term, either party may terminate this Lease by written notice within thirty (30) days after the casualty.

## 15. CONDEMNATION

15.1 **Taking.** If all of the Premises, or so much of the Premises or of access to them that Tenant cannot reasonably continue the Permitted Use, is taken by eminent domain, this Lease terminates on the date of the taking. On any partial taking that does not terminate this Lease, rent shall be reduced in proportion to the area taken.

15.2 **Award.** Landlord is entitled to the award for the taking, except that Tenant may claim separately from the condemning authority for its relocation costs and the unamortized value of the Tenant Improvements paid for by Tenant, provided that claim does not reduce Landlord's award.

## 16. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

16.1 **Consent Required.** Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet all or part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed. Landlord shall respond to Tenant's request within fifteen (15) business days after receiving the information it reasonably requires.

16.2 **Permitted Transfers.** Tenant may, without Landlord's consent but on at least ten (10) days' prior written notice, assign this Lease or sublet the Premises to an entity that controls, is controlled by or is under common control with Tenant, or to a successor to Tenant by merger, consolidation or acquisition of all or substantially all of Tenant's assets, provided the successor's net worth is not less than Tenant's net worth on the Lease Date.

16.3 **Excess Rent.** Tenant shall pay Landlord fifty percent (50%) of any rent received from a subtenant or assignee in excess of the rent payable under this Lease, after deducting Tenant's reasonable costs of the transaction, including brokerage commissions, legal fees and improvement costs. This Section 16.3 does not apply to permitted transfers under Section 16.2.

16.4 **No Release.** No assignment or sublease releases Tenant from its obligations under this Lease, unless Landlord agrees otherwise in writing.

## 17. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

17.1 **Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":
(a) Tenant fails to pay rent when due and does not cure the failure within ten (10) days after written notice from Landlord;
(b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation under this Lease and does not cure the failure within thirty (30) days after written notice from Landlord, or, if the failure cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days, Tenant does not begin the cure within that period and pursue it diligently to completion; or
(c) Tenant becomes the subject of a bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days, or makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors.

17.2 **Remedies.** Upon an Event of Default, Landlord may, by written notice, terminate this Lease or terminate Tenant's right of possession without terminating this Lease, and may recover possession of the Premises by legal proceedings. Landlord may recover rent due through the date of termination and damages for the balance of the Term, discounted to present value at the rate of five percent (5%) per year, less the fair rental value of the Premises for that period. Landlord shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate its damages.

17.3 **No Confession of Judgment.** Landlord has no right to confess judgment against Tenant, whether for rent, possession or otherwise, and no provision of this Lease authorizes any confession of judgment.

17.4 **Landlord Default.** If Landlord fails to perform any obligation under this Lease within thirty (30) days after written notice from Tenant, or such longer period as is reasonably necessary provided Landlord begins the cure within that period and pursues it diligently, Tenant may pursue any remedy available at law. If the failure concerns a repair within the Premises that materially interferes with Tenant's operation of its dispatch center, and Landlord fails to begin the repair within five (5) business days after a second notice, Tenant may perform the repair and Landlord shall reimburse Tenant's reasonable costs within thirty (30) days after invoice.

## 18. SUBORDINATION AND ESTOPPEL

18.1 **Subordination.** This Lease is subordinate to any mortgage now or later encumbering the Property, provided the mortgagee delivers to Tenant a commercially reasonable subordination, non-disturbance and attornment agreement under which Tenant's possession will not be disturbed so long as no Event of Default exists. Landlord shall deliver such an agreement from its current mortgagee within sixty (60) days after the Lease Date.

18.2 **Estoppel Certificates.** Each party shall, within fifteen (15) business days after the other's request, deliver a certificate confirming whether this Lease is in full force and effect, the dates to which rent has been paid, and whether, to its knowledge, any default exists.

## 19. QUIET ENJOYMENT

19.1 So long as no Event of Default exists, Tenant shall peaceably and quietly enjoy the Premises for the Term without interference by Landlord or anyone claiming through Landlord.

## 20. SURRENDER AND HOLDING OVER

20.1 **Surrender.** At the end of the Term, Tenant shall surrender the Premises broom clean and in good condition, ordinary wear and tear, casualty and alterations Tenant is not required to remove excepted. Tenant shall remove its personal property and any alterations it is required to remove under Section 10.4, and shall repair any damage caused by the removal.

20.2 **Holding Over.** If Tenant remains in possession after the end of the Term without Landlord's written consent, Tenant is a tenant at sufferance. Tenant shall pay rent for each month of holdover at one hundred twenty-five percent (125%) of the Base Rent in effect at the end of the Term for the first sixty (60) days, and one hundred fifty percent (150%) thereafter, together with Additional Rent. Tenant is liable for consequential damages caused by holding over only if the holdover continues for more than sixty (60) days after Landlord's written notice that it has entered into a lease with a successor tenant for the Premises.

## 21. PARKING

21.1 Tenant may use the number of parking spaces stated in Section 1.1 in the Building garage throughout the Term, at Landlord's prevailing monthly rate for unreserved spaces, which on the Lease Date is $210.00 per space per month. The garage shall be accessible to Tenant's permit holders at all times.

Landlord shall pay the Broker's commission under a separate agreement. Each party shall indemnify the other against claims by any other broker claiming through the indemnifying party.
