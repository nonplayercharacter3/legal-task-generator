## 2. Chronic Availability Failure

2.1 Section 2.1 of the Service Level Schedule commits Velert to make the Core Functions available with a Platform Availability of at least 99.5% in each Measurement Period. Section 4.1 of the Service Level Schedule provides that a Chronic Availability Failure occurs under an Order Form where there is Platform Availability below 99.5% in three consecutive calendar months. Each month's Platform Availability is calculated under Section 2.2 and reported, or required to be reported, in the Service Report for the applicable Measurement Period.

2.2 Velert's own Service Reports for the Order Form record Platform Availability below 99.5% in each of June, July and August 2026, as follows:

| Measurement Period | Platform Availability (as reported by Velert) | Service Report Delivered |
|---|---|---|
| June 2026 | 98.87% | July 7, 2026 |
| July 2026 | 99.12% | August 6, 2026 |
| August 2026 | 98.41% | September 4, 2026 |

2.3 Velert's Service Report for the August 2026 Measurement Period was delivered to us on September 4, 2026. It was sent by email to Rekaind's operational contacts at liftsense-ops@rekaind.com and posted in the support portal. That report confirmed that Platform Availability for the third consecutive calendar month was below the 99.5% commitment. June, July and August 2026 are three consecutive calendar months in which Platform Availability under the Order Form was below 99.5%. A Chronic Availability Failure has therefore occurred under the Order Form.

2.4 The Service Reports also show that the failures in those months were not isolated or marginal, and that they affected the functions of the Platform on which Rekaind most depends. In summary:

(a) **June 2026.** The June Service Report records three Severity 1 Incidents. One was a regional data ingestion node failure on June 16, 2026. During that failure, Equipment Data from Licensed Units connected through the affected node was not received or processed for approximately four hours and forty minutes. Entrapment and out-of-service alerts for those units were delayed beyond the delivery time stated in the Documentation.

(b) **July 2026.** The July Service Report records two Severity 1 Incidents and four Severity 2 Incidents. These included a failure of the LiftSense Technician mobile application on July 22 and 23, 2026, which prevented Authorized Users from closing work orders. They also included a delay in alert delivery for Licensed Units running a particular LS-G4 Gateway firmware version.

(c) **August 2026.** The August Service Report records the lowest Platform Availability of the period. It also records two separate occasions, on August 11 and August 27, 2026, on which the alert-delivery Core Function described in Section 1.3(b) of the Service Level Schedule suffered Downtime of more than thirty (30) consecutive minutes affecting five percent (5%) or more of the Licensed Units. On August 27, 2026, Rekaind's dispatch center was required to place monitored units in the Philadelphia Metro Region and the South Jersey Region on manual monitoring for most of an evening shift.

2.5 Section 4.5 of the Service Level Schedule required a remediation plan after the second consecutive failing Measurement Period. Velert delivered that plan on August 19, 2026, and the Parties' senior technical representatives met to review it on August 25, 2026. Rekaind's participation in that review does not affect its rights, as Section 4.5 of the Service Level Schedule expressly provides. The plan did not prevent a third consecutive failing month.

## 3. Termination of the Order Form

3.1 On the basis of the Chronic Availability Failure described in Section 2, Rekaind hereby terminates Order Form No. 001 under Section 12.6 of the Agreement and Section 4.2 of the Service Level Schedule.

3.2 As Section 4.2 of the Service Level Schedule requires, we identify the following:

(a) **Order Form being terminated:** Order Form No. 001, Order Effective Date March 3, 2025, Licensor Reference VS-REK-2025-001. This covers the Licensed Units described in Section 1.1 of the Order Form, being 1,140 elevator and escalator units in Pennsylvania, New Jersey and Delaware, as reflected in the unit register from time to time.

(b) **Measurement Periods relied on:** the June 2026, July 2026 and August 2026 Measurement Periods, as reported in the Service Reports identified in Section 2.2 of this letter.

3.3 Order Form No. 001 is the only Order Form in effect under the Agreement. Section 12.1 of the Agreement provides that the Agreement continues until the expiration or termination of the last Order Form then in effect. Accordingly, Rekaind's position is that the Term of the Agreement ends on the effective date of termination of the Order Form, subject to the provisions that survive under Section 12.9 of the Agreement.

## 8. Continuity of Critical Alerts Through the Termination Date

8.1 The Licensed Units are passenger elevators and escalators in occupied buildings. Until the Termination Date, Velert must continue to provide the Platform in full in accordance with the Agreement and the Service Level Schedule. This includes the alert-delivery Core Function, the secondary alert-routing path described in Section 5.3 of the Service Level Schedule, and the Severity 1 procedures in Section 6.3 of the Service Level Schedule.

8.2 Rekaind will migrate monitoring of the Licensed Units to its replacement arrangements in phases, by service region, during the period before the Termination Date. Rekaind will notify Velert's primary operational contact at least two (2) Business Days before each regional cutover. Until Rekaind confirms in writing that the cutover for a region is complete, Velert must not deactivate, reroute or reduce alerting for any Licensed Unit in that region. Velert must also continue to notify Rekaind's dispatch center at (215) 555-0110 of any Incident affecting alert delivery in accordance with Section 6.3(c) of the Service Level Schedule.

8.3 Velert must not carry out any Scheduled Maintenance or major release affecting the dispatch or work-order applications between the date of this letter and the Termination Date without Rekaind's prior written agreement. Emergency maintenance under Section 5.2 of the Service Level Schedule is not subject to this request.

8.4 Under Section 12.7(d) of the Agreement, Velert shall deactivate the Platform services for the Licensed Units after the Termination Date. Please coordinate the timing of that deactivation with Rekaind's Director of Dispatch Operations so that no Licensed Unit is left without monitoring.
