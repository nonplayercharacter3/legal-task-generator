## 1. LICENSED UNITS

1.1 **Licensed Units.** The Licensed Units under this Order Form are 1,140 elevator and escalator units in Pennsylvania, New Jersey and Delaware. Each unit is serviced by Licensee under a maintenance contract between Licensee and the owner or manager of the building in which the unit is installed.

1.2 **Unit Allocation by State.** As of the Order Effective Date, the Licensed Units are allocated as follows:

| State | Traction Elevators | Hydraulic Elevators | Escalators and Moving Walks | Total Units |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pennsylvania | 318 | 286 | 108 | 712 |
| New Jersey | 131 | 127 | 38 | 296 |
| Delaware | 54 | 63 | 15 | 132 |
| **Total** | **503** | **476** | **161** | **1,140** |

1.3 **Unit Register.** Within fifteen (15) days after the Order Effective Date, Licensee shall deliver to Licensor a unit register in the electronic format specified in the Documentation. The register will identify each Licensed Unit by:
(a) Licensee's internal unit identifier;
(b) the state equipment registration or certificate number, where one exists;
(c) the building name and street address;
(d) the unit type, manufacturer, controller make and model, and year of installation or last modernization; and
(e) the dispatch contacts to whom alerts for that unit are to be routed.

The unit register forms part of this Order Form once it is delivered. Licensee shall update the unit register through the Platform's administration console whenever a unit is added or removed under Section 2.4 of the Agreement.

1.4 **Adding Units.** Units added to this Order Form under Section 2.4(a) of the Agreement must be located in Pennsylvania, New Jersey or Delaware. Added units are charged at the per-unit rate set out in Section 3.1 of this Order Form, prorated for the remainder of the then-current annual billing period. Licensee may add units located in any other state of the United States by a separate Order Form, or by a written amendment to this Order Form signed by both Parties.

1.5 **Minimum Unit Commitment.** For purposes of Section 2.4(b) of the Agreement, the minimum unit commitment under this Order Form is 1,000 Licensed Units. Licensee may remove units under Section 2.4(b) of the Agreement only to the extent that the number of Licensed Units after the removal is not less than the minimum unit commitment.

1.6 **Replacement Units.** Where a Licensed Unit is replaced or fully modernized, and the replacement or modernized unit remains under a maintenance contract with Licensee, Licensee may transfer the Licensed Unit designation to that unit without additional subscription fee. Licensee shall reflect the transfer in the unit register. Any new Connected Device required for the replacement or modernized unit is charged at the prices in Section 4.2.

## 3. FEES

3.1 **Annual Subscription Fee.** The Annual Subscription Fee under this Order Form is $410,400 ($360 per Licensed Unit per year). The Annual Subscription Fee is calculated on the number of Licensed Units stated in Section 1.1, as adjusted from time to time under Section 2.4 of the Agreement and Sections 1.4 and 1.5 of this Order Form.

3.2 **Inclusions.** The Annual Subscription Fee includes:
(a) access to and use of the Platform for the Licensed Units by an unlimited number of Authorized Users;
(b) the LiftSense web dispatch console, the LiftSense Technician mobile application for iOS and Android, and the LiftSense customer portal described in Section 3.3 of the Agreement;
(c) the predictive-maintenance analytics module, including component-wear forecasting for doors, drives, hydraulic systems and escalator step chains;
(d) entrapment, passenger-alarm, out-of-service and safety-related fault alerting, including the secondary alert-routing path described in Section 5.3 of the Service Level Schedule;
(e) standard application programming interface access for integration with Licensee's accounting and customer relationship management systems, subject to the rate limits stated in the Documentation;
(f) Support Services and all Updates; and
(g) cellular connectivity for Connected Devices, as described in Section 4.4.

3.3 **One-Time Implementation Fee.** Licensee shall pay a one-time implementation fee of $68,500. The fee covers the following:
(a) project management for the implementation plan described in Section 4.1 of the Agreement;
(b) configuration of the Platform for Licensee's dispatch center, service regions and escalation rules;
(c) data migration of Licensee's existing maintenance histories, callback records and equipment records under Section 4.5 of the Agreement, for up to seven (7) years of historical records;
(d) the initial training described in Section 5; and
(e) integration of the Platform with Licensee's existing accounting system through the standard application programming interface.

The implementation fee is invoiced in two installments:
- fifty percent (50%) on the Order Effective Date; and
- fifty percent (50%) on the date on which the Platform first goes live for at least five hundred (500) Licensed Units.

3.4 **Invoicing.** Licensor shall invoice the Annual Subscription Fee annually in advance on the first day of each annual billing period, in accordance with Section 7.1 of the Agreement. For Year 1, the Annual Subscription Fee is invoiced on the Order Effective Date. Fees for Licensed Units that are not yet connected to the Platform on the Order Effective Date are nonetheless payable from the Order Effective Date. However, Licensor shall issue a credit equal to fifty percent (50%) of the prorated per-unit subscription fee for each Licensed Unit for each full month in Year 1 during which that unit is not connected to the Platform because of a delay in delivery of Connected Devices attributable to Licensor.

3.5 **Payment.** Payment terms are as set out in Section 7.2 of the Agreement. Payments shall be made by ACH or wire transfer to the account Licensor designates in writing on its letterhead, signed by an authorized officer of Licensor. Licensee may verify any change in payment instructions by telephone with Licensor's finance contact before making payment, and Licensee is not in breach for any reasonable delay required for that verification.

3.6 **Purchase Order.** Licensee may issue a purchase order for the Fees for its internal administrative purposes. Any terms in or attached to a purchase order are of no effect. Licensor shall include Licensee's purchase order number on its invoices where Licensee provides one.

3.7 **Taxes.** Fees are stated exclusive of taxes. Taxes are payable in accordance with Section 7.6 of the Agreement. Licensee shall provide Licensor with any applicable resale or exemption certificates within thirty (30) days after the Order Effective Date.

## 4. CONNECTED DEVICES AND CONNECTIVITY

4.1 **Supply of Connected Devices.** Licensor shall supply Connected Devices for the Licensed Units in accordance with Section 4.2 of the Agreement. Licensor shall deliver Connected Devices to Licensee's warehouse at 4100 Robbins Avenue, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania 19135, or to another address in Pennsylvania, New Jersey or Delaware that Licensee designates, in the quantities and on the dates stated in the implementation plan.

4.2 **Connected Device Pricing.** The prices for Connected Devices are as follows:

| Device | Description | Unit Price |
|---|---|---|
| LS-G4 Gateway | Monitoring gateway with integrated cellular modem, for traction and hydraulic elevators | $485 |
| LS-CIM Controller Interface Module | Controller interface for supported microprocessor-based controllers | $210 |
| LS-RLY Relay Interface Kit | Monitoring-only relay interface for relay-logic and legacy controllers | $340 |
| LS-ESC Escalator Sensor Package | Sensor package for escalators and moving walks, including step, handrail and comb-plate monitoring | $615 |
| LS-DOOR Door Operator Sensor | Optional door-cycle and door-force sensor | $95 |
| LS-ANT External Antenna | External cellular antenna for hoistways with limited signal | $45 |

Licensor shall not increase these prices during the Initial Order Term. Connected Device charges are invoiced on delivery in accordance with Section 7.1 of the Agreement.

4.3 **Estimated Device Quantities.** Based on the unit allocation in Section 1.2, the Parties estimate that the initial deployment will require approximately:
- 979 LS-G4 Gateways;
- 640 LS-CIM Controller Interface Modules;
- 339 LS-RLY Relay Interface Kits; and
- 161 LS-ESC Escalator Sensor Packages.

These estimates are for planning purposes only. Licensee is charged only for Connected Devices actually ordered and delivered.

4.4 **Connectivity.** For purposes of Section 6.2 of the Agreement, Licensor shall supply cellular connectivity for all LS-G4 Gateways and LS-ESC Escalator Sensor Packages installed on Licensed Units, through its carrier agreements. The cost of that connectivity is included in the Annual Subscription Fee. Licensee remains responsible for electrical power at each Licensed Unit and for obtaining building-owner consents for installation. Where cellular signal is insufficient at a Licensed Unit after installation of an LS-ANT External Antenna, the Parties shall agree on an alternative connection method in writing.

4.5 **Warranty Labor Rate.** For purposes of Section 4.3 of the Agreement, Licensor shall reimburse Licensee's reasonable labor costs for removal and reinstallation of a defective Connected Device at the rate of $145 per technician hour, up to a maximum of three (3) technician hours per device. Licensee shall submit reimbursement claims monthly, with reasonable supporting documentation, and Licensor shall pay or credit approved claims within forty-five (45) days.

4.6 **Spare Devices.** Licensor shall supply to Licensee, at no charge, a stock of spare Connected Devices equal to two percent (2%) of the number of each device type installed, rounded up to the nearest whole number. Licensee may hold the spares at its warehouse for immediate replacement of failed devices. Licensor shall replenish the spare stock within five (5) Business Days after Licensee uses a spare device to replace a device covered by the warranty in Section 4.3 of the Agreement.

## 5. IMPLEMENTATION AND TRAINING

5.1 **Implementation Plan.** The Parties shall agree on the implementation plan under Section 4.1 of the Agreement within thirty (30) days after the Order Effective Date. The Parties' current target deployment sequence is:

| Phase | Region | Approximate Units | Target Go-Live |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 | Philadelphia, Bucks, Montgomery and Delaware Counties, Pennsylvania | 420 | June 30, 2025 |
| Phase 2 | Chester, Lancaster and Berks Counties, Pennsylvania, and New Castle County, Delaware | 380 | September 30, 2025 |
| Phase 3 | Southern and central New Jersey, and Kent and Sussex Counties, Delaware | 340 | December 31, 2025 |

Target go-live dates may be adjusted in the implementation plan by agreement of the Parties' project managers.

5.2 **Project Managers.** Each Party shall appoint a project manager to coordinate the implementation. The project managers shall meet at least weekly during the implementation by video conference. Either Party may replace its project manager on written notice to the other.

5.3 **Initial Training.** For purposes of Section 4.6 of the Agreement, Licensor shall provide the following initial training:
(a) two (2) days of on-site training at Licensee's dispatch center in Philadelphia for up to twenty (20) dispatchers and supervisors;
(b) six (6) half-day technician training sessions, held at locations in Licensee's service regions designated by Licensee, for up to twenty-five (25) technicians per session, covering installation of Connected Devices and use of the LiftSense Technician mobile application;
(c) one (1) day of administrator training for up to six (6) Licensee system administrators; and
(d) recorded training modules made available through the support portal for onboarding of new Authorized Users.

Licensor shall bear its own travel and accommodation expenses for the training in Section 5.3(a) and (b).

5.4 **Additional Training.** Licensee may request additional on-site training at the rate of $1,800 per trainer per day, plus Licensor's reasonable and documented travel expenses incurred in accordance with Licensee's travel policy.

5.5 **Acceptance of Go-Live.** The Platform is treated as live for a phase when both of the following apply:
(a) Connected Devices have been installed and are transmitting Equipment Data for at least ninety-five percent (95%) of the Licensed Units in that phase; and
(b) Licensee has confirmed in writing, acting reasonably, that alert routing to its designated dispatch contacts has been tested successfully for those units.

Licensee shall give or reasonably withhold that confirmation within five (5) Business Days after Licensor's request.
