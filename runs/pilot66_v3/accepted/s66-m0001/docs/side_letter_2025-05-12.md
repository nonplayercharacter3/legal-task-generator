## 1. BACKGROUND AND DEFINITIONS

1.1 **The Agreement.** The Parties entered into a Software License Agreement made effective as of March 3, 2025 (the "**Agreement**"). The Agreement includes the Service Level Schedule attached to it as Exhibit A and Order Form No. 001 with an Order Effective Date of March 3, 2025 (the "**Order Form**"). Under the Order Form, Licensee subscribes to the Platform for 1,140 elevator and escalator units in Pennsylvania, New Jersey and Delaware.

1.2 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given in the Agreement. References in this Side Letter to Sections are to Sections of the Agreement unless stated otherwise.

1.3 **Status.** This Side Letter is a "Side Letter" as defined in Section 1.22 of the Agreement. It is also a written instrument amending the Agreement in accordance with Section 15.9 of the Agreement.

1.4 **Side Letter Effective Date.** This Side Letter takes effect on the date on which it has been countersigned by Licensee (the "**Side Letter Effective Date**"). Except where expressly stated otherwise, each change made by this Side Letter applies to Third-Party Claims first asserted against a Licensee Indemnitee on or after the Side Letter Effective Date and to matters arising on or after that date.

## 2. RESTRUCTURING OF LICENSOR INDEMNIFICATION

2.1 **Replacement of Section 10.1.** Section 10.1 of the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with new Section 10.1 and new Section 10.2 set out in Sections 2.2 and 2.3 of this Side Letter.

> **10.1 Intellectual Property Indemnification by Licensor.** Licensor shall defend Licensee, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents (the "**Licensee Indemnitees**") against any claim, suit or proceeding brought by a third party (a "**Third-Party Claim**"). Licensor shall also indemnify and hold the Licensee Indemnitees harmless from all damages, settlements, fines, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees and expert witness fees) arising from any such Third-Party Claim, to the extent the Third-Party Claim alleges any of the following:
>
> (a) that the Platform, any Connected Device, or Licensee's or its Authorized Users' use of either in accordance with this Agreement, infringes or misappropriates any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret or other Intellectual Property Rights of a third party in the United States, Canada, the European Union, the United Kingdom or any jurisdiction in which any component of the Platform is hosted or any Connected Device is manufactured;
>
> (b) that any open-source software incorporated by Licensor into the Platform or the firmware of any Connected Device has been used, distributed or made available in breach of the license terms applicable to that open-source software;
>
> (c) that any Platform report, dashboard or output made available by Licensee to its customers, building owners or authorities having jurisdiction under Section 3.1(d) or Section 3.3, in the form generated by the Platform, infringes or misappropriates the Intellectual Property Rights of a third party; or
>
> (d) that Licensor's use of Equipment Data to compile Aggregated Data under Section 8.4 infringes or misappropriates the rights of a third party, where the Third-Party Claim is brought against a Licensee Indemnitee.

2.3 **New Section 10.2.** The following is inserted as new Section 10.2 of the Agreement:

> **10.2 Bodily Injury and Property Damage Indemnification by Licensor.** Licensor shall defend the Licensee Indemnitees against any Third-Party Claim, and shall indemnify and hold the Licensee Indemnitees harmless from all damages, settlements, fines, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from any Third-Party Claim, to the extent the Third-Party Claim arises from bodily injury, death or damage to tangible property caused by any defect, error or failure of the Platform or any Connected Device. This includes any failure of the Platform to generate, transmit or deliver an entrapment, passenger-alarm or safety-related fault alert in accordance with the Documentation, and any failure of the secondary alert-routing path described in Section 5.3 of the Service Level Schedule.

2.4 **Renumbering.** Existing Sections 10.2, 10.3, 10.4 and 10.5 of the Agreement are renumbered as Sections 10.3, 10.4, 10.5 and 10.6, respectively. The text of those Sections is unchanged except as provided in Section 2.5 of this Side Letter.

## 3. INFORMATION SECURITY REPORTING

3.1 **Penetration Testing.** In addition to its obligations under Section 8.3 of the Agreement, Licensor shall engage an independent, qualified third-party security firm at least once in each calendar year to perform penetration testing of the Platform. This testing shall cover the cloud-hosted services, the web and mobile applications, the application programming interfaces and the Connected Device communication channels. Licensor shall provide Licensee with an executive summary of the results within thirty (30) days after receiving the final test report. The summary shall identify the number and severity of findings and the status of remediation for each finding rated critical or high.

3.2 **Remediation of Findings.** Licensor shall remediate any penetration testing finding rated critical within thirty (30) days after receiving the final test report, and any finding rated high within ninety (90) days. Licensor shall report the status of remediation of those findings in the next Service Report delivered after each deadline.

3.3 **SOC 2 Bridge Letters.** Where the period covered by Licensor's most recent SOC 2 Type II report ended more than three (3) months before Licensee's request under Section 8.3 of the Agreement, Licensor shall also provide a bridge letter. The bridge letter shall be signed by an officer of Licensor and shall confirm whether any material change has occurred in Licensor's control environment since the end of that reporting period.

3.4 **Security Questionnaire.** Once in each calendar year, Licensor shall complete Licensee's reasonable information security questionnaire relating to the Platform within thirty (30) days after Licensee's request. The questionnaire shall be no broader in scope than the industry-standard questionnaires generally used by maintenance contractors in the vertical transportation industry.

3.5 **Connected Device Firmware.** Licensor shall digitally sign all firmware updates for Connected Devices. Licensor shall not deploy any firmware update to Connected Devices installed on Licensed Units unless the update has first been validated in Licensor's test environment against each controller make and model identified in the unit register. Licensor shall give Licensee's operational contacts at least five (5) Business Days' advance notice of each scheduled firmware deployment, except for emergency security patches, for which notice shall be given as soon as reasonably practicable.

3.6 **Security Contacts.** Each Party shall designate a security contact for the purposes of Section 8.5 of the Agreement and this Section 3. Licensor's initial security contact is its Chief Information Security Officer, reachable at security@velertsystems.com. Licensee's initial security contact is its Director of Information Technology, reachable at it-security@rekaind.com. Either Party may change its security contact by email notice to the other Party's security contact.

## 4. ACCOUNT GOVERNANCE

4.1 **Customer Success Manager.** Licensor shall assign a dedicated Customer Success Manager for the Mid-Atlantic Region to Licensee's account. This person shall be the primary Licensor operational contact identified in Section 6.2 of the Order Form. Licensor shall give Licensee at least thirty (30) days' notice before replacing the Customer Success Manager, except where the replacement results from resignation, termination of employment, illness or leave. Licensor shall consult Licensee in good faith on the selection of a replacement.

4.2 **Executive Sponsors.** Each Party shall appoint an executive sponsor for the relationship. Licensor's executive sponsor is its Chief Executive Officer. Licensee's executive sponsor is its Chief Operating Officer. The executive sponsors shall meet at least twice in each calendar year, in person or by video conference, to review the following:
(a) the overall relationship;
(b) the Platform roadmap, including planned features relevant to Licensee's maintenance operations; and
(c) any matter escalated to them by the Parties' operational contacts.

4.3 **Roadmap Briefings.** Licensor shall brief Licensee's designated representatives on the Platform roadmap at least once in each calendar quarter. This may be done as part of the quarterly senior review meeting described in Section 7.3 of the Service Level Schedule. Roadmap information disclosed under this Section 4.3 is Licensor's Confidential Information. Licensor makes no commitment to deliver any feature described in a roadmap briefing except as stated in the Agreement or an Order Form.

4.4 **User Advisory Group.** Licensor shall invite Licensee to participate in any customer advisory group or user council that Licensor establishes for the Platform. Licensee may nominate up to two (2) representatives. Participation is at Licensee's discretion and at each Party's own cost. Feedback Licensee provides in that forum is subject to Section 3.7 of the Agreement.

## 5. IMPLEMENTATION REPORTING

5.1 **Implementation Status Reports.** Until the Platform has gone live for all three phases described in Section 5.1 of the Order Form, Licensor's project manager shall deliver a written implementation status report to Licensee's project manager every two (2) weeks. Each report shall identify:
(a) the number of Connected Devices delivered, installed and transmitting Equipment Data, by phase and by service region;
(b) the status of data migration under Section 4.5 of the Agreement;
(c) the status of integration with Licensee's accounting system;
(d) any risks to the target go-live dates and the steps proposed to address them; and
(e) any open actions assigned to either Party.

5.2 **Delivery Forecasts.** Licensor shall provide Licensee with a rolling sixty (60) day forecast of Connected Device deliveries by device type with each implementation status report. Where Licensor becomes aware that a delivery will be delayed by more than ten (10) Business Days beyond the date in the implementation plan, Licensor shall notify Licensee's project manager within two (2) Business Days. The notice shall state the cause of the delay and a revised delivery date.

5.3 **Installation Support.** During each implementation phase, Licensor shall make a field application engineer available, by telephone and video, from 7:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Eastern Time on each Business Day. The engineer shall support Licensee's technicians with the installation and commissioning of Connected Devices. At Licensee's reasonable request, Licensor shall also make a field application engineer available on site at Licensed Units within Licensee's service regions for up to ten (10) days in each implementation phase. This on-site support is at no additional charge.

5.4 **Post-Implementation Review.** Within thirty (30) days after the Platform has gone live for the final implementation phase, the Parties' project managers shall hold a post-implementation review. Licensor's project manager shall then deliver a written summary of the following within ten (10) Business Days after the review:
(a) lessons learned;
(b) outstanding defects; and
(c) any items to be carried into ordinary Support Services.

## 6. INTEGRATION SUPPORT

6.1 **Sandbox Environment.** During each Subscription Term, Licensor shall make available to Licensee a non-production sandbox environment of the Platform at no additional charge. Licensee shall use the sandbox only for developing, testing and validating integrations between the Platform and Licensee's accounting and customer relationship management systems. The sandbox shall run the then-current production release of the Platform, or the release scheduled to be deployed to production within the next thirty (30) days. Licensee shall not load into the sandbox personal information of building occupants other than test data. The sandbox is excluded from the service levels in the Service Level Schedule.

6.2 **API Change Notice.** Licensor shall give Licensee at least ninety (90) days' advance notice before deprecating or making any backward-incompatible change to any application programming interface used by the integration delivered under Section 3.3(e) of the Order Form. Licensor shall continue to support the deprecated version of the interface for at least six (6) months after the replacement version becomes generally available.

6.3 **Integration Documentation.** Licensor shall maintain current documentation for the integration delivered under Section 3.3(e) of the Order Form. This includes data mappings, field definitions, error codes and retry logic. Licensor shall make that documentation available to Licensee's system administrators through the support portal.

6.4 **Customer Relationship Management Integration.** At Licensee's request, Licensor shall provide up to forty (40) hours of integration engineering support during the Initial Order Term, at no additional charge. This support is to assist Licensee in integrating the Platform with its customer relationship management system through the standard application programming interface. Additional integration engineering support is available at the rate of $1,800 per engineer per day, consistent with the rate in Section 5.4 of the Order Form.

Licensor shall also upload them to Licensee's supplier invoicing portal where Licensee provides access to that portal.
