## 1. NATURE OF THIS CERTIFICATE

1.1 **Information Only.** This certificate is issued as a matter of information only. It confers no rights upon the Certificate Holder other than those provided in the policies and endorsements described below. It does not affirmatively or negatively amend, extend or alter the coverage afforded by those policies. It does not create a contract of insurance between the issuing insurers, their authorized representative or producer, and the Certificate Holder.

1.2 **Endorsements Govern.** Where the Certificate Holder is an additional insured, the policies must carry, or be endorsed to provide, additional insured status. Where waiver of subrogation is indicated, the terms and conditions of the policy apply, and certain policies may require an endorsement. The endorsements listed in Section 17 have been issued and are in force under the policies identified. Copies are available to the Certificate Holder on request to the Producer.

1.3 **Policy Terms.** The insurance afforded by the policies described in this certificate is subject to all the terms, exclusions and conditions of those policies. The limits shown may have been reduced by paid claims. As of the Date Issued, the Producer has not been notified of any paid claim that has reduced any aggregate limit shown below.

## 6. COMMERCIAL GENERAL LIABILITY

6.1 **Policy Form.** The commercial general liability policy is written on ISO form CG 00 01 (04 13), occurrence basis, as modified by the endorsements listed in Section 17.

6.2 **Limits of Insurance.**

| Coverage | Limit |
|---|---|
| Each Occurrence | $2,000,000 |
| Damage to Rented Premises (each occurrence) | $300,000 |
| Medical Expense (any one person) | $10,000 |
| Personal and Advertising Injury | $2,000,000 |
| General Aggregate | $4,000,000 |
| Products-Completed Operations Aggregate | $4,000,000 |

6.3 **Aggregate Application.** The general aggregate limit applies per project and per location under endorsement CG 25 03 and CG 25 04.

6.4 **Coverages Included.** The policy includes:
(a) products-completed operations coverage, including coverage for bodily injury and property damage arising out of the Named Insured's products, which include the LiftSense monitoring gateways, controller interface modules, relay interface kits, escalator sensor packages and related hardware and embedded firmware;
(b) contractual liability coverage for liability assumed by the Named Insured under an "insured contract", including the tort liability of another assumed in a written contract, without modification by any contractual liability limitation endorsement;
(c) premises and operations coverage;
(d) personal and advertising injury coverage; and
(e) broad form property damage.

6.5 **Deductible.** No deductible or self-insured retention applies to the commercial general liability policy.

6.6 **Exclusions of Note.** The policy contains the standard ISO electronic data exclusion and the access or disclosure of confidential or personal information exclusion. Liability arising from those exposures is addressed under the policies described in Sections 10 and 11. The policy does not contain a professional services exclusion applicable to bodily injury or property damage arising out of the Named Insured's products.

## 10. TECHNOLOGY ERRORS AND OMISSIONS

10.1 **Policy Form.** The policy is written on Insurer D's Technology Professional Liability form MSI-TPL-500 (07 23), on a claims-made and reported basis.

10.2 **Limits and Retention.**

| Coverage | Limit |
|---|---|
| Each Claim | $5,000,000 |
| Policy Aggregate | $5,000,000 |
| Retention (each claim) | $100,000 |
| Retroactive Date | January 1, 2019 (full prior acts to that date) |

10.3 **Scope of Coverage.** The policy covers claims arising from any actual or alleged act, error, omission, negligence or breach of duty in the rendering of, or failure to render, technology services and technology products by or on behalf of the Named Insured. This includes:
(a) the design, development, hosting, operation, maintenance and support of the LiftSense remote-monitoring and predictive-maintenance software platform;
(b) the failure of technology products, including software and embedded firmware, to perform the function or serve the purpose intended;
(c) breach of contract claims to the extent they arise from such acts, errors or omissions; and
(d) implementation, data migration, integration and training services.

10.4 **Defense Costs.** Defense costs are payable in addition to the limits of liability up to an additional $1,000,000, and within the limits thereafter.

10.5 **Extended Reporting Period.** The policy provides an optional extended reporting period of up to thirty-six (36) months on non-renewal or cancellation by either the insurer or the Named Insured.
