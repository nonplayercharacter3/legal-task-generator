# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 16, 2026, by and between **Topar Medical Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Pritor Healthcare Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Celestra Imaging Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

B. The Company designs, manufactures, sells and services computed tomography ("**CT**") and magnetic resonance imaging ("**MRI**") systems.

C. Before the Closing, Seller will contribute the Assigned Contracts to the Company under the Bill of Sale.

D. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, the parties agree as follows:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Buyer Cap**" has the meaning given in Section 9.5(d).

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE; PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Purchase and Sale; Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign and transfer to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all liens, for an aggregate purchase price of $186,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), payable as set out in Sections 2.2 through 2.4.

**2.2 Closing Payment.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay to Seller, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated by Seller in writing at least two Business Days before the Closing Date, an amount equal to the Purchase Price minus the Escrow Amount and minus the Deferred Payment, being $155,400,000.

**2.3 Escrow Deposit.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent, to be held and disbursed under the Escrow Agreement. The Escrow Funds are available to satisfy claims for indemnification by Buyer under Article IX. Buyer and Seller shall each promptly deliver joint written instructions to the Escrow Agent to release Escrow Funds when required by this Agreement or the Escrow Agreement. Any Escrow Funds not subject to a pending claim at the end of the escrow period stated in the Escrow Agreement shall be released to Seller.

**2.4 Deferred Payment.** Buyer shall pay to Seller $12,000,000, payable 12 months after the Closing Date (the "**Deferred Payment**"), by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by Seller under Section 2.2. The Deferred Payment is part of the Purchase Price. It is not subject to setoff for any claim under Article IX unless Seller has agreed to that claim in writing or it has been finally determined by a court of competent jurisdiction. Any amount of the Deferred Payment not paid when due shall bear interest at the prime rate published in *The Wall Street Journal* on the due date plus 2% per annum until paid.

**2.5 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement any Taxes required by applicable law to be withheld. Any amount so withheld and paid over to the proper Governmental Authority shall be treated as paid to Seller.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on the third Business Day after the conditions in Article VII are satisfied or waived (other than conditions to be satisfied at the Closing), or on another date the parties agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) stock certificates representing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers duly executed in blank;

(b) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(c) a copy of the Bill of Sale, duly executed by Seller and the Company;

(d) written resignations, effective as of the Closing, of the directors and officers of the Company listed by Buyer at least five Business Days before the Closing Date;

(e) a certificate of the Secretary of Seller certifying the resolutions of Seller's board of directors authorizing this Agreement and the transactions under it;

(f) a good standing certificate for the Company issued by the Secretary of State of Delaware dated within ten Business Days before the Closing Date;

(g) the certificate required by Section 7.2(c); and

(h) a properly completed IRS Form W-9 of Seller.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver:

(a) to Seller, the payment required by Section 2.2;

(b) to the Escrow Agent, the Escrow Amount required by Section 2.3;

(c) to Seller, the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Buyer and the Escrow Agent;

(d) to Seller, a certificate of an authorized officer of Buyer certifying the resolutions of Buyer's managing member authorizing this Agreement and the transactions under it; and

(e) to Seller, the certificate required by Section 7.3(c).

The Company is duly qualified to do business in each jurisdiction where qualification is required, except where the failure to qualify would not be material to the Company.

This Agreement has been duly executed and delivered by Seller and constitutes its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms, subject to bankruptcy and equitable principles.

**4.4 No Conflicts; Consents.** The execution, delivery and performance by Seller of this Agreement and the Bill of Sale, and the consummation of the transactions under them, do not (a) violate the certificate of incorporation or bylaws of Seller or the Company, (b) violate any law applicable to Seller or the Company, or (c) require any consent of, or notice to, any Person under any contract to which Seller or the Company is a party, including the Assigned Contracts, or result in a breach of, default under or right of termination of any such contract, except as set out in Schedule 4.4 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**4.9 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker engaged by Seller or the Company is entitled to any fee in connection with the transactions under this Agreement for which Buyer or the Company would be liable.

**4.10 No Other Representations.** Except for the representations and warranties in this Article IV, neither Seller nor any other Person makes any representation or warranty, express or implied, about Seller, the Company or the Shares.

Buyer has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Escrow Agreement. ## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to operate its business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice and to use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve its business organization and relationships with customers, suppliers and regulators. Without Buyer's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, Seller shall cause the Company not to (a) issue any equity interest, (b) declare any dividend other than cash dividends paid before the Closing, (c) incur indebtedness for borrowed money over $1,000,000, or (d) enter into or terminate any contract required to be listed on Schedule 4.6.

**6.2 Pre-Closing Contribution.** Before the Closing, Seller shall execute and deliver, and shall cause the Company to execute and deliver, the Bill of Sale, assigning to the Company all of Seller's right, title and interest in and to the Assigned Contracts, effective March 30, 2026.

**6.3 Access to Information.** Until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access, during normal business hours and on reasonable notice, to the Company's properties, books and records.

**6.4 Regulatory Filings.** Each party shall make any filing required of it under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976 within ten Business Days after the date of this Agreement and shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain early termination of the waiting period.

**6.5 Confidentiality.** After the Closing, Seller shall keep confidential all non-public information about the Company, except as required by law.

**6.6 Further Assurances.** Each party shall execute and deliver any further documents and take any further actions reasonably requested by the other party to carry out the transactions under this Agreement.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Each Party's Obligations.** The obligations of each party to close are subject to (a) the expiration or termination of any waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976, and (b) the absence of any order of a Governmental Authority restraining the Closing.

**7.2 Conditions to Buyer's Obligations.** The obligations of Buyer to close are further subject to:

(a) the Fundamental Representations of Seller being true and correct in all respects, and the other representations and warranties of Seller being true and correct in all material respects, in each case as of the Closing Date;

(b) Seller having performed in all material respects its covenants required to be performed before the Closing; and

(c) Buyer having received a certificate of an officer of Seller confirming the matters in Sections 7.2(a) and 7.2(b), together with the other deliverables in Section 3.2.

**7.3 Conditions to Seller's Obligations.** The obligations of Seller to close are further subject to:

(a) the representations and warranties of Buyer being true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;

(b) Buyer having performed in all material respects its covenants required to be performed before the Closing; and

(c) Seller having received a certificate of an officer of Buyer confirming the matters in Sections 7.3(a) and 7.3(b), together with the deliverables in Section 3.3.

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing (a) by mutual written consent of the parties; (b) by either party if the Closing has not occurred by July 31, 2026, unless the failure results from that party's breach; or (c) by either party if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after written notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated under Section 8.1, it shall become void without liability of any party, except for liability for willful breach before termination and except that this Section 8.2 and Article XI survive.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement survive the Closing until the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date, except that the Fundamental Representations survive until 60 days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants survive until fully performed. Any claim made in writing with reasonable detail before the end of the applicable survival period survives until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer, the Company and their Affiliates and representatives from and against all Losses arising out of:

(a) any breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in Article IV;

(b) any breach of any covenant of Seller in this Agreement; and

(c) any Specified Matters.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates and representatives from and against all Losses arising out of:

(a) any breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in Article V; and

(b) any breach of any covenant of Buyer in this Agreement, including its obligations under Sections 2.3 and 2.4.

**9.4 Claims Procedures.** A party seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall give the indemnifying party (the "**Indemnifying Party**") prompt written notice of any claim, describing it in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice does not relieve the Indemnifying Party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced. For any third-party claim, the Indemnifying Party may assume the defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party by written notice within 20 days after receiving notice of the claim. The Indemnified Party may participate at its own expense. The Indemnifying Party shall not settle any third-party claim without the Indemnified Party's consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, unless the settlement involves only the payment of money by the Indemnifying Party and includes a full release of the Indemnified Party.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) *Seller Cap.* The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed $18,600,000, except that Seller's aggregate liability for breaches of Fundamental Representations, together with all other amounts paid by Seller under this Article IX, shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(b) *Deductible.* Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a), other than for breaches of Fundamental Representations, unless and until the aggregate Losses under Section 9.2(a) exceed $1,860,000, and then only for Losses in excess of that amount.

(c) *Exceptions to Seller Limitations.* The cap in Section 9.5(a) and the deductible in Section 9.5(b) shall not apply to Losses under Section 9.2(c), or to Losses arising from fraud by Seller.

(d) *Buyer Cap.* The aggregate liability of Buyer under Section 9.3 shall not exceed $5,000,000 (the "**Buyer Cap**").

(e) *Exceptions to Buyer Cap.* The Buyer Cap shall not apply to (i) Losses arising from fraud by Buyer, (ii) Buyer's obligation to pay the Purchase Price, including the Deferred Payment, or (iii) Buyer's obligations to deliver joint written instructions to the Escrow Agent for the release of Escrow Funds under Section 2.3 and the Escrow Agreement.

(f) *Materiality Scrape.* For purposes of calculating Losses only, materiality qualifiers in the representations and warranties shall be disregarded.

(g) *Insurance.* Losses shall be calculated net of insurance proceeds actually received by the Indemnified Party for the same Losses, less costs of recovery and resulting premium increases.

**9.6 Source of Recovery.** Any amount owed by Seller under Section 9.2 shall be satisfied first from the Escrow Funds, by joint written instructions of Buyer and Seller to the Escrow Agent, and then, to the extent the Escrow Funds are insufficient and subject to Section 9.5, directly by Seller.

**9.7 Mitigation.** Each Indemnified Party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate Losses after becoming aware of the event giving rise to them.

**9.8 Exclusive Remedy.** After the Closing, the indemnification provided in this Article IX is the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties for any breach of the representations, warranties and covenants in this Agreement, except for claims of fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, and claims for payment of the Deferred Payment.

**9.9 Tax Treatment.** The parties shall treat any indemnification payment under this Article IX as an adjustment to the Purchase Price for tax purposes, unless otherwise required by law.

## ARTICLE X. TAX MATTERS

**10.1 Pre-Closing Taxes.** Seller shall prepare and file, at its expense, all income Tax returns of the Company for periods ending on or before the Closing Date, and shall pay all Taxes of the Company for those periods.

**10.2 Transfer Taxes.** Buyer and Seller shall each bear one half of any transfer, documentary, sales, use, stamp or similar Taxes arising from the sale of the Shares.

**10.3 Cooperation.** Buyer and Seller shall cooperate reasonably in preparing and filing Tax returns and in any audit or proceeding relating to Taxes of the Company.
