# ESCROW AGREEMENT

This ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Escrow Agreement**") is entered into as of [__________], 2026 by and among **Pritor Healthcare Partners, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), **Topar Medical Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Ashcombe Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller have entered into a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 16, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Seller will sell to Buyer all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of **Celestra Imaging Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

B. Section 2.3 of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. The Escrow Funds are to be available to satisfy claims for indemnification by Buyer under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

C. The Escrow Agent is willing to hold and disburse the Escrow Funds on the terms of this Escrow Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Escrow Agreement, the Parties and the Escrow Agent agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **Purchase Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Escrow Agreement have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement. These terms include "Buyer Cap," "Closing," "Closing Date," "Business Day" and "Losses." The Escrow Agent is not charged with knowledge of those definitions except as expressly set out in this Escrow Agreement.

1.2 **Defined Terms.** In this Escrow Agreement:

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 3.1.

"**Disputed Amount**" has the meaning given in Section 3.3.

"**Escrow Account**" means the segregated account established by the Escrow Agent under Section 2.2.

"**Escrow Amount**" means $18,600,000.

"**Escrow Funds**" means the Escrow Amount as deposited with the Escrow Agent, together with any earnings on it, less any amounts disbursed under this Escrow Agreement.

"**Escrow Period**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction under Section 10.5. A Final Order must be accompanied by a certificate of counsel to the presenting Party stating that the order or judgment is final and non-appealable.

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means written instructions signed by an authorized representative of each of Buyer and Seller and delivered to the Escrow Agent under Section 10.1.

"**Pending Claim Amount**" means, at any time, the aggregate amount claimed in all Claim Notices delivered before that time. It excludes any amount that has been paid from the Escrow Funds, released by Joint Written Instructions or resolved by a Final Order.

## 2. Appointment; Deposit; Investment

2.1 **Appointment and Deposit.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent as escrow agent under this Escrow Agreement, and the Escrow Agent accepts the appointment. At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit with the Escrow Agent $18,600,000 (the Escrow Amount) by wire transfer of immediately available funds. The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt of the Escrow Amount to Buyer and Seller in writing on the day it is received.

2.2 **Escrow Account.** The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds in a segregated account in the name of the Escrow Agent for the benefit of Buyer and Seller. The Escrow Funds shall not be commingled with any other funds. They are not subject to any lien, attachment or other judicial process of any creditor of any Party, and they may be disbursed only as provided in this Escrow Agreement.

2.3 **Investment.** Unless Joint Written Instructions direct otherwise, the Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds in a U.S. Treasury money market fund rated AAA by at least one nationally recognized statistical rating organization. The Escrow Agent may also hold the Escrow Funds in an interest-bearing deposit account of the Escrow Agent insured to the extent available by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation. Earnings on the Escrow Funds become part of the Escrow Funds. The Escrow Agent is not liable for any investment loss incurred in accordance with this Section 2.3.

2.4 **Ownership for Tax Purposes.** For income tax purposes, Buyer shall be treated as the owner of the Escrow Funds and shall report all earnings on them, until the Escrow Funds are distributed. Buyer and Seller shall each deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9 or W-8, as applicable, at or before the Closing. The Escrow Agent shall issue IRS Forms 1099 or other required tax reports for earnings on the Escrow Funds.

## 3. Claims Against the Escrow Funds

3.1 **Claim Notices.** At any time before the end of the Escrow Period, Buyer may deliver to Seller and the Escrow Agent a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**") of a claim for indemnification under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement. Each Claim Notice shall describe the claim in reasonable detail and state the amount of Losses claimed, or a good-faith estimate of them, and the basis for indemnification.

3.2 **Response.** Within 30 days after receiving a Claim Notice, Seller may deliver to Buyer and the Escrow Agent a written objection to all or part of the claimed amount (an "**Objection Notice**"). If the Escrow Agent receives no Objection Notice within that 30-day period, Buyer and Seller shall, within five Business Days after the period ends, deliver Joint Written Instructions to release the claimed amount to Buyer.

3.3 **Disputed Claims.** If Seller timely delivers an Objection Notice, the amount objected to (the "**Disputed Amount**") shall remain in the Escrow Account. Buyer and Seller shall negotiate in good faith for 30 days to resolve the dispute. The Escrow Agent shall disburse the Disputed Amount only on receipt of Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. Any undisputed portion of the claim shall be released to Buyer under Section 3.2.

3.4 **Delivery of Joint Written Instructions.** Each of Buyer and Seller shall promptly deliver Joint Written Instructions whenever this Escrow Agreement or the Purchase Agreement requires the release of Escrow Funds, including on the resolution of any claim and at the end of the Escrow Period.

## 4. Escrow Period; Final Release

4.1 **Escrow Period.** The escrow under this Escrow Agreement shall continue for 18 months after the Closing Date (the "**Escrow Period**"). If the last day of the Escrow Period is not a Business Day, the Escrow Period ends on the next Business Day.

4.2 **Final Release.** Within five Business Days after the end of the Escrow Period, Buyer and Seller shall deliver Joint Written Instructions directing the Escrow Agent to release to Seller all remaining Escrow Funds, less the Pending Claim Amount at that time. The Escrow Agent shall release those Escrow Funds to Seller within two Business Days after receiving the Joint Written Instructions.

4.3 **Release of Retained Amounts.** The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold any amount retained for a Pending Claim Amount after the end of the Escrow Period. When each claim is resolved, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the retained amount for that claim under Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. Any balance retained for that claim and not payable to Buyer shall be released to Seller.

## 5. Disbursements

5.1 **Method.** The Escrow Agent shall make all disbursements by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in the applicable Joint Written Instructions or Final Order. If no account is designated, the Escrow Agent shall pay to the account listed for the recipient on Schedule 1.

5.2 **Order of Disbursement.** If the Escrow Funds are not enough to pay all amounts directed to be disbursed on the same day, the Escrow Agent shall disburse them in the order in which it received the applicable Joint Written Instructions or Final Orders.

5.3 **Security Procedures.** Before disbursing any funds, the Escrow Agent shall confirm the disbursement by telephone call-back to an authorized representative listed on Schedule 2, other than the representative who signed the instruction. The Escrow Agent may rely on that confirmation.

## 6. Duties of the Escrow Agent

6.1 **Limited Duties.** The Escrow Agent's duties are limited to those expressly set out in this Escrow Agreement. They are ministerial in nature, and no implied duties or obligations shall be read into this Escrow Agreement against the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent is not a party to the Purchase Agreement and is not bound by it.

6.2 **Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any written notice, instruction, certificate or signature that it believes in good faith to be genuine and authorized. It has no duty to inquire into the validity or accuracy of any such document.

6.3 **Counsel.** The Escrow Agent may consult counsel of its choice. It is not liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith in accordance with the advice of that counsel.

6.4 **Conflicting Instructions.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting instructions or is uncertain about its duties, it may refrain from acting until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. It may also file an interpleader action in a court of competent jurisdiction under Section 10.5 and deposit the Escrow Funds with that court.

6.5 **Statements.** The Escrow Agent shall provide Buyer and Seller with monthly statements showing all deposits, earnings and disbursements in the Escrow Account.

## 7. Fees and Expenses

7.1 **Fees.** The Escrow Agent's fees are set out in Schedule 3. Buyer and Seller shall each pay one half of the Escrow Agent's fees and of its reasonable out-of-pocket expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, incurred in performing its duties.

7.2 **No Setoff Against Escrow Funds.** The Escrow Agent shall not pay its fees or expenses from the Escrow Funds, and it has no lien on or right of setoff against the Escrow Funds.

## 8. Limitations of Liability; Indemnification

8.1 **Escrow Agent Liability.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any action taken or omitted under this Escrow Agreement, except to the extent of its gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud, as finally determined by a court of competent jurisdiction. In no event is the Escrow Agent liable for special, indirect, consequential or punitive damages.

8.2 **Indemnification of Escrow Agent.** Buyer and Seller shall, jointly and severally, indemnify and hold harmless the Escrow Agent and its directors, officers, employees and agents from and against all losses, claims, damages, liabilities and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of this Escrow Agreement. This indemnity does not cover losses arising from the Escrow Agent's gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear one half of any amount paid under this Section 8.2, except that a Party whose breach of this Escrow Agreement caused the loss shall bear all of it.

8.3 **Liability of Buyer.** The aggregate liability of Buyer to Seller arising under or in connection with this Escrow Agreement or Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, including with respect to release of Escrow Funds, shall not exceed the Buyer Cap, except for fraud.

8.4 **Survival.** This Section 8 survives the termination of this Escrow Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

## 9. Resignation and Removal of Escrow Agent

9.1 **Resignation.** The Escrow Agent may resign at any time by giving at least 30 days' written notice to Buyer and Seller.

9.2 **Removal.** Buyer and Seller may remove the Escrow Agent by Joint Written Instructions delivered at least 30 days before the effective date of removal.

9.3 **Successor.** Buyer and Seller shall appoint a successor escrow agent by Joint Written Instructions. The successor must be a bank or trust company with combined capital and surplus of at least $500,000,000. If no successor has been appointed within 30 days after notice of resignation or removal, the Escrow Agent may petition a court of competent jurisdiction under Section 10.5 to appoint one. The resigning or removed Escrow Agent shall deliver the Escrow Funds to the successor. Its duties end when that delivery is made.

## 10. Miscellaneous

10.1 **Notices.** All notices and instructions under this Escrow Agreement shall be in writing and are effective when received. They may be delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier or by email in PDF format, to the addresses below or to another address a party designates by notice.

If to Buyer: Pritor Healthcare Partners, LLC, Attention: Managing Partner, at the address set out on Buyer's signature page.

If to Seller: Topar Medical Systems, Inc., Attention: General Counsel, at the address set out on Seller's signature page.

If to the Escrow Agent: Ashcombe Trust Company, N.A., Attention: Corporate Trust and Escrow Services, at the address set out on the Escrow Agent's signature page.

10.2 **Authorized Representatives.** Each of Buyer and Seller has designated its authorized representatives on Schedule 2. Each may change those designations by written notice to the Escrow Agent signed by an existing authorized representative.

10.3 **Amendment; Waiver.** This Escrow Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the waiving party.

10.4 **Assignment.** No party may assign this Escrow Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties. However, any entity into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or to which it transfers substantially all of its corporate trust business, becomes the successor Escrow Agent without further act.

10.5 **Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Escrow Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware, or, if that court lacks jurisdiction, the federal courts located in Delaware, for any action arising out of this Escrow Agreement. EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

10.6 **USA PATRIOT Act.** Buyer and Seller acknowledge that federal law requires the Escrow Agent to obtain, verify and record information that identifies each person opening an account. Each of them shall provide the Escrow Agent with the information it reasonably requests for that purpose.

10.7 **Termination.** This Escrow Agreement terminates when all Escrow Funds have been disbursed under its terms. Sections 7 and 8 survive termination.

10.8 **Severability.** If any provision of this Escrow Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

10.9 **Counterparts.** This Escrow Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
