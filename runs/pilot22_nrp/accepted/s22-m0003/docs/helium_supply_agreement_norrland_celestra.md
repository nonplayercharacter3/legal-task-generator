# LIQUID HELIUM SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This LIQUID HELIUM SUPPLY AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 1, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Norrland Cryogenics, Inc.**, a Minnesota corporation ("**Supplier**"), and **Celestra Imaging Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"). Supplier and Customer are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer designs, manufactures, sells and services magnetic resonance imaging ("**MRI**") systems. These systems use superconducting magnets cooled with liquid helium.

B. Supplier produces, purifies and distributes liquid helium and related cryogenic products and services.

C. Customer wishes to buy liquid helium from Supplier for magnet energization and cool-down at its manufacturing facility and for fills and refills at customer sites. Supplier wishes to supply that helium on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" means, as to any person, any other person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that person.

"**Delivery Point**" means the Facility or the Field Site named in the applicable Purchase Order.

"**Dewar**" means a portable, vacuum-insulated cryogenic container owned by Supplier and used to deliver Product.

"**Facility**" means Customer's manufacturing and magnet test facility identified in Schedule A, and any other Customer facility the Parties add to Schedule A in writing.

"**Field Site**" means a hospital, imaging center or other customer location in the continental United States where a Customer MRI system is installed or being installed.

"**Force Majeure Event**" has the meaning given in Section 12.1.

"**Forecast**" has the meaning given in Section 3.1.

"**Product**" means liquid helium meeting the Specifications.

"**Purchase Order**" means a written or electronic order for Product issued by Customer under Section 3.2.

"**Specifications**" means the purity and quality specifications for Product set out in Schedule B.

"**Term**" has the meaning given in Section 13.1.

## 2. Supply

2.1 **Supply Commitment.** During the Term, Supplier shall sell and deliver to Customer, and Customer shall buy from Supplier, the quantities of Product ordered by Customer under Purchase Orders accepted in accordance with Section 3.3.

2.2 **Minimum Annual Volume.** Customer shall buy at least 60,000 liquid liters of Product in each contract year of the Term (the "**Minimum Annual Volume**"). If Customer buys less than the Minimum Annual Volume in a contract year, Customer shall pay Supplier, within 45 days after receiving Supplier's invoice, an amount equal to 25% of the then-current price per liter multiplied by the shortfall. Any shortfall caused by Supplier's failure to deliver, by an allocation under Section 4.3 or by a Force Majeure Event is excluded when calculating the shortfall.

2.3 **Non-Exclusivity.** Customer may buy liquid helium from other suppliers for any quantity that exceeds the Minimum Annual Volume, or for any quantity that Supplier does not deliver when required by an accepted Purchase Order.

## 3. Forecasts and Orders

3.1 **Forecasts.** By the 15th day of each calendar month, Customer shall deliver to Supplier a non-binding rolling forecast of its expected Product requirements by Delivery Point for the following six months (each a "**Forecast**").

3.2 **Purchase Orders.** Customer shall order Product by Purchase Order. Each Purchase Order shall state the quantity, Delivery Point, requested delivery date and any special handling instructions. Purchase Orders for delivery to the Facility shall be issued at least five business days before the requested delivery date. Purchase Orders for delivery to a Field Site shall be issued at least ten business days before the requested delivery date.

3.3 **Acceptance.** Supplier shall accept each Purchase Order that is consistent with this Agreement and that does not exceed 120% of the quantity in the most recent Forecast for the relevant month. Supplier shall confirm acceptance or rejection within two business days after receipt. Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to fill quantities above that threshold.

3.4 **Conflicting Terms.** Any pre-printed or additional terms in a Purchase Order, acknowledgment or invoice are of no effect. Only the terms of this Agreement apply to the purchase and sale of Product.

## 4. Delivery; Title and Risk of Loss

4.1 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Product in Dewars or bulk transport containers to the Delivery Point, DAP (Incoterms 2020). At Field Sites, Supplier's trained technician shall transfer Product into the magnet cryostat when Customer's field engineer requests it, following Customer's written fill procedure.

4.2 **Title and Risk of Loss.** Title to and risk of loss of Product pass to Customer when Product is delivered at the Delivery Point. Where Supplier transfers Product into a magnet cryostat, title and risk pass when the transfer is complete. Title to Dewars stays with Supplier at all times.

4.3 **Allocation.** If Supplier cannot meet all of its customers' requirements for liquid helium because of a supply shortage, Supplier may allocate its available supply among its customers on a fair and non-discriminatory basis. Customer's share shall be no less favorable than the proportion of Supplier's contracted volumes that Customer represented in the 12 months before the shortage. Supplier shall give Customer prompt written notice of any allocation and its expected duration.

4.4 **Dewars.** Customer shall return each Dewar, empty and in good condition except for normal wear, within 30 days after delivery. Supplier may charge the Dewar rental fee in Schedule C for each day after that period. Customer shall pay the replacement cost in Schedule C for any Dewar lost or damaged beyond normal wear while in Customer's possession.

## 5. Price and Payment

5.1 **Price.** The prices for Product, delivery and fill services are set out in Schedule C.

5.2 **Price Adjustment.** Supplier may adjust the per-liter price once in each contract year, on at least 60 days' prior written notice. The adjustment may not exceed the percentage change in Supplier's documented cost of crude and refined helium over the preceding 12 months, plus 3%. If an increase exceeds 10%, Customer may terminate this Agreement by written notice given within 30 days after receiving Supplier's notice. Termination takes effect on the date the increase would have taken effect.

5.3 **Invoicing and Payment.** Supplier shall invoice Customer monthly in arrears for Product delivered and services performed in the preceding month. Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 45 days after receipt. Late payments bear interest at 1% per month, or the highest rate permitted by law if lower.

5.4 **Disputed Invoices.** Customer may withhold payment of any amount it disputes in good faith by giving Supplier written notice with reasonable detail before the payment due date. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within 30 days.

5.5 **Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use and similar taxes. Customer shall pay those taxes, unless it provides a valid exemption certificate. Each Party is responsible for its own income taxes.

## 6. Quality; Warranty

6.1 **Specifications.** Supplier warrants that all Product will conform to the Specifications on delivery and will be free of contamination that could impair the operation of a superconducting magnet.

6.2 **Certificates of Analysis.** Supplier shall provide a certificate of analysis for each lot of Product delivered.

6.3 **Non-Conforming Product.** Customer shall notify Supplier of any non-conforming Product within 30 days after delivery, or promptly after discovering a latent defect. At Customer's option, Supplier shall replace the non-conforming Product at no charge or credit Customer for its price and associated delivery charges. Supplier shall also reimburse Customer's reasonable, documented costs to purge and re-fill any magnet cryostat contaminated by non-conforming Product, subject to Section 10.

6.4 **Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS STATED IN THIS SECTION 6, SUPPLIER MAKES NO OTHER WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 7. Safety and Compliance

7.1 **Compliance with Law.** Each Party shall comply with all laws applicable to its performance under this Agreement. This includes U.S. Department of Transportation regulations on the transport of hazardous materials and Occupational Safety and Health Administration requirements.

7.2 **Safety.** Supplier shall provide safety data sheets for Product. Supplier's personnel at the Facility or any Field Site shall follow the site's posted safety rules and Customer's reasonable written safety instructions.

7.3 **Personnel.** Supplier is responsible for the training, supervision and conduct of its personnel. Supplier's fill technicians shall be trained in cryogenic handling and in safe work practices near energized MRI magnets.

## 8. Insurance

8.1 During the Term and for one year afterward, Supplier shall maintain, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best: (a) commercial general liability insurance, including products and completed operations, of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence; (b) automobile liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per accident; (c) workers' compensation insurance as required by law; and (d) employer's liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per accident.

8.2 Supplier shall name Customer as an additional insured on the policies in Sections 8.1(a) and 8.1(b), and shall provide certificates of insurance on request.

## 9. Indemnification

9.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Customer and its Affiliates, and their directors, officers and employees, from and against all third-party claims, and the resulting losses, damages, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, to the extent arising from (a) bodily injury, death or property damage caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Supplier or its personnel, (b) non-conforming Product, or (c) Supplier's breach of Section 7.1.

9.2 **By Customer.** Customer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Supplier and its Affiliates, and their directors, officers and employees, from and against all third-party claims, and the resulting losses, damages, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, to the extent arising from (a) bodily injury, death or property damage caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Customer or its personnel, or (b) Customer's breach of Section 7.1.

9.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of the claim. It shall allow the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement, and shall provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnifying Party may not settle any claim in a way that imposes an obligation or admission on the indemnified Party without that Party's written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

## 10. Limitation of Liability

10.1 EXCEPT FOR A PARTY'S INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTION 9, A PARTY'S BREACH OF SECTION 11, OR A PARTY'S GROSS NEGLIGENCE OR WILLFUL MISCONDUCT, NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR FOR LOST PROFITS, ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.

10.2 EXCEPT FOR THE OBLIGATIONS AND CONDUCT EXCLUDED IN SECTION 10.1 AND CUSTOMER'S PAYMENT OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTION 5, EACH PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT IN ANY CONTRACT YEAR SHALL NOT EXCEED THE AMOUNTS PAID AND PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER UNDER THIS AGREEMENT IN THE 12 MONTHS BEFORE THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

## 11. Confidentiality

11.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other Party under this Agreement, including pricing, Forecasts, customer site lists and technical information. Each Party shall use that information only to perform this Agreement.

11.2 Section 11.1 does not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, that the receiving Party already lawfully knew, that it independently develops, or that it lawfully receives from a third party without a duty of confidentiality. A Party may disclose confidential information when required by law, after giving the other Party prompt notice where lawful. A Party may also disclose it in confidence to its professional advisers and to actual or prospective acquirers or financing sources.

11.3 This Section 11 survives for three years after the end of the Term.

## 12. Force Majeure

12.1 Neither Party is liable for any failure or delay in performance, other than payment obligations, caused by events beyond its reasonable control. These events include acts of God, fire, flood, war, terrorism, epidemic, government action, the failure of a third-party helium source or refinery outside Supplier's control, and labor disputes not involving the affected Party's own employees (each a "**Force Majeure Event**").

12.2 The affected Party shall give prompt written notice of the Force Majeure Event and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a Force Majeure Event prevents Supplier from delivering at least 50% of Customer's ordered quantities for more than 90 consecutive days, either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice.

## 13. Term and Termination

13.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for three years (the "**Initial Term**"). It then renews automatically for successive one-year periods unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 120 days before the end of the then-current period (the Initial Term and all renewals together, the "**Term**").

13.2 **Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after receiving written notice describing it.

13.3 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes subject to any bankruptcy or receivership proceeding that is not dismissed within 60 days.

13.4 **Effect of Termination.** Termination does not affect any rights or obligations accrued before termination. Customer shall pay for all Product delivered before termination and shall return all Dewars within 30 days. Sections 4.4, 5, 6.3, 6.4, 9, 10, 11 and 14 survive termination.

## 14. General

14.1 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld. Either Party may, however, assign this Agreement without consent, on written notice to the other Party, to an Affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of the business to which this Agreement relates. A change in the ownership or control of either Party is not an assignment under this Section 14.1 and does not require the consent of the other Party.

14.2 **Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and are effective when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier or by email with confirmation of receipt, to the address set out on the receiving Party's signature page or another address it designates by notice.

14.3 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency relationship.

14.4 **Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods does not apply.

14.5 **Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the waiving Party.

14.6 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

14.7 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
