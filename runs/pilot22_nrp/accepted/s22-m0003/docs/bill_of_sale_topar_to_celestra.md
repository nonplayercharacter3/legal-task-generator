# BILL OF SALE AND ASSIGNMENT AND ASSUMPTION AGREEMENT

This BILL OF SALE AND ASSIGNMENT AND ASSUMPTION AGREEMENT (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made effective as of March 30, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Topar Medical Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Assignor**"), and **Celestra Imaging Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation and a wholly owned subsidiary of Assignor ("**Assignee**").

## RECITALS

A. Assignor is the sole stockholder of Assignee. Assignee designs, manufactures, sells and services computed tomography ("**CT**") and magnetic resonance imaging ("**MRI**") systems.

B. Assignor is party to the imaging-software contracts listed in Annex A (the "**Assigned Contracts**"). The software and services under the Assigned Contracts are used in the design, operation and servicing of Assignee's CT and MRI systems.

C. Assignor and Pritor Healthcare Partners, LLC ("**Buyer**") have entered into a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 16, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Assignor will sell to Buyer all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Assignee.

D. Section 6.2 of the Purchase Agreement requires Assignor to assign the Assigned Contracts to Assignee before the Closing. Assignor wishes to make that assignment, and Assignee wishes to accept it and assume the Assumed Liabilities, on the terms of this Bill of Sale.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, Assignor and Assignee agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given in the Purchase Agreement.

1.2 In this Bill of Sale:

"**Assumed Liabilities**" means all liabilities and obligations of Assignor under the Assigned Contracts to the extent they arise and are to be performed on or after the Effective Date. Assumed Liabilities do not include any liability arising out of any breach or default by Assignor under any Assigned Contract occurring before the Effective Date.

"**Contract Rights**" means all of Assignor's rights, title and interest in, to and under the Assigned Contracts. This includes all licenses granted to Assignor under them, all rights to use, install and receive updates to the software licensed under them, all rights to receive support, maintenance and other services, all warranties and indemnities given by the counterparties, and all prepaid fees and deposits under them.

"**Excluded Liabilities**" means all liabilities and obligations of Assignor other than the Assumed Liabilities.

## 2. Assignment

2.1 **Assignment of Assigned Contracts.** Effective as of the Effective Date, Assignor sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to Assignee, and Assignee accepts, all of the Contract Rights. This includes Assignor's rights under the Reconstruction Software License Agreement with Okavi Algorithms GmbH described in Item 1 of Annex A.

2.2 **Delivery of Software and Materials.** On or promptly after the Effective Date, Assignor shall deliver to Assignee, or confirm that Assignee already holds, all of the following that are in Assignor's possession and relate to the Assigned Contracts: copies of each Assigned Contract and its amendments, all license keys and access credentials, all software media and documentation, and all support records.

2.3 **No Other Assets.** This Bill of Sale conveys only the Contract Rights. It does not convey any other asset of Assignor.

## 3. Assumption

3.1 **Assumption of Assumed Liabilities.** Effective as of the Effective Date, Assignee assumes and agrees to pay, perform and discharge the Assumed Liabilities when due.

3.2 **Excluded Liabilities.** Assignee does not assume, and Assignor retains and shall pay, perform and discharge when due, all Excluded Liabilities.

## 4. Fees and Proration

4.1 License, subscription, support and maintenance fees under the Assigned Contracts shall be prorated between Assignor and Assignee as of the Effective Date. Assignor bears fees for periods before the Effective Date. Assignee bears fees for periods on and after the Effective Date.

4.2 If either party pays an amount that is the responsibility of the other under Section 4.1, the other party shall reimburse it within 30 days after receiving a written request with reasonable supporting documentation.

## 5. Notices to Counterparties

5.1 Within ten Business Days after the Effective Date, Assignor and Assignee shall jointly notify each counterparty to an Assigned Contract of the assignment under this Bill of Sale. Each notice shall give the counterparty Assignee's contact and invoicing details.

5.2 After the Effective Date, any payment, notice, update or other communication that Assignor receives under an Assigned Contract shall be promptly forwarded to Assignee.

## 6. Representations of Assignor

Assignor represents to Assignee that:

(a) Assignor has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Bill of Sale;

(b) Assignor is the contracting party under each Assigned Contract, and each Assigned Contract is in full force and effect; and

(c) Assignor has not previously assigned, pledged or encumbered any of the Contract Rights.

## 7. Further Assurances

At the request of either party, the other party shall execute and deliver any further instruments of transfer and assumption and take any further actions reasonably necessary to carry out the assignment and assumption under this Bill of Sale.

## 8. Relationship to Purchase Agreement

This Bill of Sale is delivered under Section 6.2 of the Purchase Agreement. Nothing in this Bill of Sale expands, limits or otherwise modifies the representations, warranties, covenants or indemnities in the Purchase Agreement.

## 9. Miscellaneous

9.1 **Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale binds and benefits Assignor and Assignee and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

9.2 **Governing Law.** This Bill of Sale is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

9.3 **Amendment.** This Bill of Sale may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties.

9.4 **Severability.** If any provision of this Bill of Sale is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

9.5 **Counterparts.** This Bill of Sale may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.

## ANNEX A — ASSIGNED CONTRACTS

**Item 1. Reconstruction Software License Agreement**

- **Counterparty:** Okavi Algorithms GmbH ("**Licensor**")
- **Licensee:** Topar Medical Systems, Inc. ("**Licensee**")
- **Date:** June 1, 2022, as amended by Amendment No. 1 dated September 12, 2024
- **Subject matter:** A non-exclusive, worldwide license to embed and distribute Licensor's iterative image reconstruction software in CT and MRI systems. The license covers Licensor's low-dose CT reconstruction and accelerated MRI reconstruction modules. It also includes annual support and version updates.
- **Term:** Through May 31, 2029, with automatic renewal for successive two-year terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal.
- **Fees:** Annual platform fee plus a per-unit royalty for each system shipped.
- **Section 14.2 (Assignment):** "Licensee shall not assign, delegate or otherwise transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations hereunder, whether voluntarily, by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior written consent of Licensor. Any change of control of Licensee, including any transfer of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities of Licensee, shall be deemed a transfer requiring the prior written consent of Licensor. Any purported assignment or transfer in violation of this Section 14.2, including upon any change of control of Licensee, shall be null and void, and Licensor may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice."

**Item 2. Imaging Workflow Software Subscription Agreement**

- **Counterparty:** Meridell Clinical Software, Inc.
- **Licensee:** Topar Medical Systems, Inc.
- **Date:** January 15, 2024
- **Subject matter:** A subscription to scan-protocol management and DICOM workflow software used by service engineers at installed sites for CT and MRI systems.
- **Term:** Through January 14, 2027.
- **Fees:** Annual subscription fee, payable in advance.
- **Assignment:** Either party may assign the agreement to an Affiliate or to a successor to the business to which it relates, on written notice to the other party.

**Item 3. Service Diagnostics Software License Agreement**

- **Counterparty:** Halvren Technologies LLC
- **Licensee:** Topar Medical Systems, Inc.
- **Date:** August 3, 2023
- **Subject matter:** A license to remote diagnostics and log-analysis software used to service installed CT and MRI systems.
- **Term:** Perpetual, with annual maintenance renewable at Licensee's option.
- **Fees:** One-time license fee (paid). Annual maintenance fee.
- **Assignment:** Licensee may assign the agreement without consent, on written notice to the licensor.
