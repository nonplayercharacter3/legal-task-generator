# EXHIBIT B

# FORM OF BILL OF SALE

This BILL OF SALE (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made and delivered as of ____________, 20__ (the "**Closing Date**"), by **Tenein Energy Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), to and for the benefit of **Gleidesk Renewables Acquisition Corp.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and, if Buyer so directs under Section 1.3 of the Purchase Agreement, **Tenein Rooftop Solar, Inc.**, a California corporation (the "**Company**" and, together with Buyer, as applicable, the "**Transferee**").

## 1. Background; Defined Terms

1.1 This Bill of Sale is delivered under Sections 1.3 and 3.2(a)(ii) of the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of October 20, 2026, by and among Buyer, Seller and the Company (the "**Purchase Agreement**").

1.2 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. These terms include "Cap," "Closing," "Disclosure Schedule," "Encumbrance," "Permitted Encumbrances" and "Transferred Assets."

## 2. Conveyance

2.1 For good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which Seller acknowledges, including the payment of the Purchase Price under the Purchase Agreement, Seller hereby sells, conveys, assigns, transfers and delivers to the Transferee all of Seller's right, title and interest in and to the Transferred Assets, free and clear of all Encumbrances. The Transferee shall have and hold the Transferred Assets as its own forever.

2.2 The Transferred Assets consist of the following assets owned by Seller and used in the Business, each as more fully listed on Schedule 1.3 of the Disclosure Schedule:

(a) the installation vehicles, including trucks, vans and trailers, identified by year, make, model and vehicle identification number (the "**Vehicles**");

(b) the inverter and racking inventory, including inverters, optimizers, mounting rails, flashings, clamps and related balance-of-system components, wherever located; and

(c) the warehouse equipment, including forklifts, pallet racking, lifts, hand tools, power tools and shop equipment.

2.3 Concurrently with this Bill of Sale, Seller is delivering the certificate of title for each Vehicle, duly endorsed for transfer to the Transferee. Seller is also delivering any other document reasonably required by the California Department of Motor Vehicles to register the Vehicles in the name of the Transferee.

## 3. No Assumption of Liabilities

The Transferee does not assume, and this Bill of Sale does not transfer to the Transferee, any liability or obligation of Seller relating to the Transferred Assets that arises before the Closing. This includes any indebtedness secured by any Transferred Asset, any unpaid Tax, registration fee or penalty relating to any Vehicle for any period before the Closing Date, and any claim arising from the ownership or operation of any Transferred Asset before the Closing. Seller retains all of those liabilities and obligations and shall pay, perform and discharge them when due.

## 4. Title Warranty

4.1 Seller warrants to the Transferee that:

(a) Seller has good and valid title to each of the Transferred Assets, free and clear of all Encumbrances;

(b) Seller has full power and authority to sell, convey, assign, transfer and deliver the Transferred Assets to the Transferee; and

(c) on delivery of this Bill of Sale, good and valid title to the Transferred Assets is vested in the Transferee, free and clear of all Encumbrances.

4.2 Seller shall warrant and defend the title of the Transferee to the Transferred Assets against the lawful claims of all persons claiming by, through or under Seller.

4.3 Seller's liability for any breach of the warranties in this Section 4 is subject to the Cap set forth in Section 9.4(a) of the Purchase Agreement. Claims under this Section 4 shall be made in accordance with the procedures in Section 9.5 of the Purchase Agreement.

4.4 Except as set forth in this Section 4 and in the Purchase Agreement, Seller makes no other representation or warranty with respect to the Transferred Assets.

## 5. Further Assurances; Power of Attorney

5.1 From time to time after the Closing, at the Transferee's request and without further consideration, Seller shall execute and deliver such further instruments of sale, conveyance and transfer, and take such other actions, as the Transferee may reasonably request. These may be needed to vest title to any Transferred Asset more effectively in the Transferee, to complete the registration of any Vehicle, or to put the Transferee in actual possession and operating control of the Transferred Assets.

5.2 Seller hereby irrevocably appoints the Transferee as its attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, to demand and receive any Transferred Asset and to endorse, in the name of Seller, any certificate of title, check or other instrument relating to any Transferred Asset. This power is coupled with an interest.

5.3 If Seller or any of its affiliates receives any payment or other property that relates to a Transferred Asset after the Closing, Seller shall hold it in trust for the Transferee and shall promptly deliver it to the Transferee.

## 6. Relationship to Purchase Agreement

This Bill of Sale is executed and delivered under the Purchase Agreement. It is subject to the terms of the Purchase Agreement as provided in Section 11.12 of the Purchase Agreement. Nothing in this Bill of Sale expands the obligations that Buyer or the Company has under the Purchase Agreement.

## 7. Governing Law; Jurisdiction

7.1 This Bill of Sale, and all claims arising out of or relating to it or to the sale, conveyance and transfer of the Transferred Assets, are governed by the laws of the State of California, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction.

7.2 Each of Seller and the Transferee irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state courts located in Fresno County, California, for any action arising out of or relating to this Bill of Sale or the Transferred Assets. Each of them waives any objection to venue in those courts.

## 8. Successors and Assigns

This Bill of Sale binds Seller and its successors and assigns. It benefits Buyer, the Company and their respective successors and assigns.
