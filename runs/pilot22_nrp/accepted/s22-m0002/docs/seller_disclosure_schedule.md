## 9. Schedule 4.12 – Installation Warranties

9.1 **Forms of Warranty.** The Company provides the following warranties to customers under its standard residential and commercial installation agreements:

(a) *Workmanship warranty.* Ten (10) years for residential installations and five (5) years for commercial installations. Both run from the date of permission to operate.

(b) *Roof-penetration warranty.* Ten (10) years against leaks at roof penetrations made by the Company.

(c) *Production warranty.* This applies to residential systems sold on the Company's "Performance Plus" program only. It guarantees ninety percent (90%) of estimated first-year production, declining by one-half percent (0.5%) per year, for ten (10) years.

Manufacturer warranties on modules, inverters and racking pass through to customers. They are not obligations of the Company.

9.2 **Assumed Warranty Obligations.** "**Assumed Warranty Obligations**" means all obligations of the Company under the workmanship, roof-penetration and production warranties described in Section 9.1 of this Schedule 4.12, for installations whether completed before or after the Closing Date. This includes all repair, replacement, roof-remediation and production-shortfall payment obligations owed to customers under those warranties.

9.3 **Installed Base.** As of September 30, 2026, the Company had completed approximately 6,120 residential installations and 214 commercial installations that remain within the applicable workmanship warranty period. Of the residential installations, 1,480 are enrolled in the Performance Plus program.
