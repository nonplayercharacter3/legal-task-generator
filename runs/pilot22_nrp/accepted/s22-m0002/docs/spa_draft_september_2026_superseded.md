# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

*Buyer Draft – September 24, 2026*
*Privileged & Confidential – Subject to FRE 408 and Further Review by All Parties*

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026, by and among **Gleidesk Renewables Acquisition Corp.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), **Tenein Energy Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and **Tenein Rooftop Solar, Inc.**, a California corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of common stock of the Company (the "**Shares**").

B. The Company designs, sells and installs residential and commercial rooftop solar energy systems in the State of California (the "**Business**").

C. Seller owns certain installation vehicles, inverter and racking inventory and warehouse equipment that are used in the Business but are not owned by the Company.

D. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, the Shares and those assets, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS; PURCHASE AND SALE

**1.1 Definitions.** Capitalized terms used in this Agreement have the following meanings:

(a) "**Ancillary Documents**" means the Bill of Sale and each other certificate, instrument and document delivered under this Agreement.

(b) "**Bill of Sale**" means the bill of sale in the form attached as Exhibit B, to be executed and delivered by Seller at the Closing.

(c) "**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given in Section 3.1.

(d) "**Disclosure Schedule**" means the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer on the date of this Agreement.

(e) "**Encumbrance**" means any lien, pledge, mortgage, security interest, charge, claim, lease, conditional sale arrangement or other restriction on title, other than Permitted Encumbrances.

(f) "**Fraud**" means actual and intentional fraud under Delaware common law in the making of a representation or warranty in this Agreement.

(g) "**Fundamental Representations**" has the meaning given in Section 9.4(b).

(h) "**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

(i) "**Material Adverse Effect**" means any event, change or effect that is, or would reasonably be expected to be, materially adverse to the business, results of operations or financial condition of the Company, taken as a whole. General economic or industry conditions, changes in law, and changes in net metering or tariff policy of general application are excluded, except to the extent they affect the Company disproportionately.

(j) "**Permitted Encumbrances**" means liens for Taxes not yet due and payable, and statutory liens of landlords, carriers and mechanics that arose in the ordinary course for amounts not yet delinquent.

(k) "**Transferred Assets**" means the installation vehicles, inverter and racking inventory and warehouse equipment owned by Seller and used in the Business, as listed on Schedule 1.3 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**1.2 Purchase and Sale of Shares.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver the Shares to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase them from Seller, free and clear of all Encumbrances.

**1.3 Transfer of Transferred Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, convey, assign and transfer the Transferred Assets to Buyer, or to the Company at Buyer's direction, free and clear of all Encumbrances, by delivering the Bill of Sale. Buyer does not assume, and the Bill of Sale shall not transfer, any liability of Seller relating to the Transferred Assets that arises before the Closing.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Shares and the Transferred Assets is $48,500,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Section 2.3.

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price, as adjusted by the Estimated Closing Adjustment, by wire transfer of immediately available funds as follows:

(a) first, to the holders of Closing Indebtedness, the amounts specified in the payoff letters delivered under Section 3.2(a)(iv); and

(b) second, the balance to Seller, to the account Seller designates in writing at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date.

**2.3 Working Capital Adjustment.**

(a) At least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a good-faith estimate of Closing Working Capital and of Closing Indebtedness. The Purchase Price paid at Closing shall be increased or decreased, as the case may be, by the amount by which estimated Closing Working Capital exceeds or falls short of $3,200,000 (the "**Target Working Capital**"), and shall be reduced by estimated Closing Indebtedness (together, the "**Estimated Closing Adjustment**").

(b) Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a statement of actual Closing Working Capital and Closing Indebtedness. Seller may object within thirty (30) days after delivery. Any dispute that the parties do not resolve within thirty (30) days after an objection shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm of national standing that both parties approve. That firm shall act as an expert and not as an arbitrator, and its determination shall be final and binding.

(c) Within five (5) Business Days after the final determination, Buyer or Seller, as applicable, shall pay the net adjustment amount to the other party.

**2.4 Allocation.** The Purchase Price shall be allocated between the Shares and the Transferred Assets in accordance with Schedule 2.4 of the Disclosure Schedule. The parties shall file all Tax returns consistently with that allocation.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely, by electronic exchange of documents and signatures. It shall occur on the third (3rd) Business Day after the last of the conditions in Article VII is satisfied or waived, other than conditions that by their nature are to be satisfied at the Closing, or on such other date as Buyer and Seller agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Closing Deliverables.**

(a) At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(i) stock certificates evidencing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers duly executed in blank;

(ii) the Bill of Sale, duly executed by Seller, together with certificates of title for each titled vehicle included in the Transferred Assets, duly endorsed for transfer;

(iii) written resignations of the directors and officers of the Company that Buyer designates;

(iv) payoff letters for all Closing Indebtedness and evidence that all related Encumbrances have been released;

(v) the certificate required by Section 7.2(c); and

(vi) a properly completed IRS Form W-9 from Seller.

(b) At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver the Purchase Price in accordance with Section 2.2 and the certificate required by Section 7.3(c).

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer, as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date, as follows:

**4.1 Organization; Capitalization; Title to Shares.** Seller is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware. The Company is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of California. The Shares constitute all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of the Company, have been duly authorized and validly issued, and are fully paid and non-assessable. Seller is the record and beneficial owner of the Shares, free and clear of all Encumbrances. There are no outstanding options, warrants, rights or other commitments relating to the capital stock of the Company.

**4.2 Authority; Enforceability.** Seller and the Company each has full power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and the Ancillary Documents to which it is a party and to perform its obligations under them. This Agreement has been duly executed and delivered by Seller and the Company and constitutes their legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable bankruptcy and equitable principles.

**4.3 No Conflicts; Consents.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller and the Company do not (a) violate their organizational documents, (b) violate any applicable law, or (c) require any consent under, or result in a breach of or default under, any Material Contract, except as set forth in Schedule 4.3 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**4.4 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the audited balance sheet of the Company as of December 31, 2025, the related statements of income and cash flows for the year then ended, and the unaudited balance sheet as of June 30, 2026 (the "**Financial Statements**"). The Financial Statements were prepared in accordance with GAAP consistently applied and fairly present, in all material respects, the financial position and results of operations of the Company as of their respective dates.

**4.5 Absence of Changes.** Since June 30, 2026, the Company has conducted the Business in the ordinary course, and there has not been any Material Adverse Effect.

**4.6 Title to Transferred Assets.** Seller has good and valid title to each of the Transferred Assets, free and clear of all Encumbrances. At the Closing, delivery of the Bill of Sale will vest good and valid title to the Transferred Assets in Buyer or its designee, free and clear of all Encumbrances. The Transferred Assets are in good operating condition, ordinary wear and tear excepted.

**4.7 Material Contracts.** Schedule 4.7 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each contract to which the Company is a party that involves annual payments in excess of $250,000, or that is a supply, dealer, financing or power purchase agreement material to the Business (the "**Material Contracts**"). Each Material Contract is valid and binding on the Company and in full force and effect. Neither the Company nor, to Seller's Knowledge, any other party is in material breach of any Material Contract.

**4.8 Compliance with Laws; Permits.** The Company is, and since January 1, 2023 has been, in compliance in all material respects with applicable laws, including California contractor licensing requirements and the interconnection rules of each applicable utility. The Company holds all permits and licenses required to conduct the Business as currently conducted, each of which is listed on Schedule 4.8 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**4.9 Litigation.** Except as set forth in Schedule 4.9 of the Disclosure Schedule, there is no action, suit or proceeding pending or, to Seller's Knowledge, threatened against the Company or affecting the Shares or the Transferred Assets.

**4.10 Real Property.**

(a) The Company does not own any real property. Schedule 4.10(a) of the Disclosure Schedule lists each lease under which the Company leases real property (the "**Leases**"), including the lease of the Company's warehouse facility in Fresno, California with Pellucid Logistics Park LLC (the "**Fresno Warehouse Lease**"). For each Lease, the schedule states its term, expiration date and any renewal rights.

(b) Each Lease is valid, binding and in full force and effect. The Company is not in material default under any Lease.

**4.11 Taxes.** The Company has timely filed all income and other material Tax returns required to be filed by it and has paid all Taxes shown as due on those returns. There are no Encumbrances for Taxes on the assets of the Company or on the Transferred Assets, other than Permitted Encumbrances.

**4.12 Installation Warranties.** Schedule 4.12 of the Disclosure Schedule describes the forms of workmanship, roof-penetration and production warranties that the Company has given to customers and states the warranty claims history of the Company since January 1, 2023. Each installation completed by the Company was performed in a good and workmanlike manner and in material compliance with applicable building codes and manufacturer installation requirements.

**4.13 Employees; Benefit Plans.** Schedule 4.13 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each employee benefit plan maintained by the Company. Each plan has been operated in material compliance with its terms and applicable law. The Company is not a party to any collective bargaining agreement.

**4.14 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Seller or the Company.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**5.1 Organization and Authority.** Buyer is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware. It has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement.

**5.2 Enforceability.** This Agreement constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Buyer, enforceable in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable bankruptcy and equitable principles.

**5.3 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer will have at the Closing sufficient immediately available funds to pay the Purchase Price.

**5.4 Investment Intent.** Buyer is acquiring the Shares for its own account and not with a view to their distribution in violation of applicable securities laws.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to conduct the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice. Without Buyer's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, Seller shall not cause or permit the Company to:

(a) amend its organizational documents;

(b) issue or sell any equity securities;

(c) incur indebtedness for borrowed money outside the ordinary course;

(d) enter into, materially amend or terminate any Material Contract or Lease; or

(e) sell, transfer or encumber any Transferred Asset or any material asset of the Company.

**6.2 Access to Information.** Before the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access, during normal business hours and on reasonable advance notice, to the properties, books and records, contracts and personnel of the Company.

**6.3 Third-Party Consents.** Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain, before the Closing, each consent listed on Schedule 4.3 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**6.4 Leases.** Before the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to perform its obligations under each Lease and to timely exercise any renewal option under the Fresno Warehouse Lease in accordance with its terms. Seller shall deliver to Buyer a copy of any notice given or received by the Company under any Lease promptly after it is given or received.

**6.5 Exclusivity.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing or the earlier termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not, and shall cause the Company not to, solicit, initiate or engage in discussions concerning any sale of the Shares, the Business or any material portion of the Company's assets with any person other than Buyer.

**6.6 Notification.** Before the Closing, Seller shall promptly notify Buyer in writing of any fact or event that causes, or would reasonably be expected to cause, any condition in Section 7.2 not to be satisfied. No notice under this Section 6.6 shall cure any breach or affect any remedy available to Buyer.

**6.7 Confidentiality.** After the Closing, Seller shall keep confidential all non-public information concerning the Company and the Business, except as required by law.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of All Parties.** The obligations of each party to consummate the Closing are subject to the following conditions: no governmental order shall be in effect that restrains or prohibits the transactions contemplated by this Agreement, and any required waiting period under applicable antitrust law shall have expired or been terminated.

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** The obligation of Buyer to consummate the Closing is subject to the satisfaction or waiver by Buyer of the following conditions:

(a) the Fundamental Representations shall be true and correct in all respects, and all other representations and warranties of Seller shall be true and correct in all material respects, in each case as of the Closing Date;

(b) Seller and the Company shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by them before the Closing;

(c) Buyer shall have received a certificate of an authorized officer of Seller certifying that the conditions in Sections 7.2(a) and 7.2(b) have been satisfied;

(d) no Material Adverse Effect shall have occurred; and

(e) Seller shall have delivered each item listed in Section 3.2(a).

**7.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** The obligation of Seller to consummate the Closing is subject to the satisfaction or waiver by Seller of the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Buyer shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;

(b) Buyer shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before the Closing; and

(c) Seller shall have received a certificate of an authorized officer of Buyer to that effect.

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing:

(a) by mutual written consent of Buyer and Seller;

(b) by Buyer or Seller, if the Closing has not occurred on or before January 31, 2027, unless the failure to close results from a breach by the terminating party;

(c) by Buyer, if Seller has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.2 would not be satisfied, and the breach is not cured within twenty (20) days after written notice; or

(d) by Seller, if Buyer has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.3 would not be satisfied, and the breach is not cured within twenty (20) days after written notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated, it shall become void and no party shall have any further liability, except under Section 6.7, this Section 8.2 and Article XI, and except for liability for willful breach or Fraud.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement survive the Closing until 18 months after the Closing Date. The Fundamental Representations and the representations in Section 4.11 (Taxes) survive until sixty (60) days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants survive until fully performed. Any claim asserted in writing with reasonable specificity before the end of the applicable survival period survives until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify Buyer, the Company and their respective affiliates, officers, directors and employees (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") against, and hold them harmless from, all Losses incurred by them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement or in any Ancillary Document;

(b) any breach of any covenant of Seller or, before the Closing, the Company in this Agreement;

(c) any Closing Indebtedness or any Tax of the Company for a period ending on or before the Closing Date, to the extent not taken into account in the final determination under Section 2.3; and

(d) any workmanship, roof-penetration or production warranty claim, or any related property damage claim, arising out of an installation completed by the Company before the Closing Date.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify Seller and its affiliates, officers, managers and employees (the "**Seller Indemnitees**") against, and hold them harmless from, all Losses incurred by them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement; and

(b) any breach of any covenant of Buyer in this Agreement.

**9.4 Limitations.**

(a) *Cap.* The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 9.2(a) and Section 9.2(d) shall not exceed 10% of the Purchase Price ($4,850,000) (the "**Cap**").

(b) *Exclusions from the Cap.* The Cap does not apply to Losses arising out of any inaccuracy in or breach of the Fundamental Representations (Sections 4.1, 4.2 and 4.6 Title to Transferred Assets) or arising out of Fraud. Seller's aggregate liability for those Losses shall not exceed the Purchase Price, except in the case of Fraud. The representations in Sections 4.1, 4.2 and 4.6 are the "**Fundamental Representations**".

(c) *Deductible.* Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) until the aggregate Losses under that Section exceed $485,000 (the "**Deductible**"). Seller shall then be liable only for Losses in excess of the Deductible. The Deductible does not apply to Losses arising out of any inaccuracy in or breach of the Fundamental Representations or arising out of Fraud.

(d) *Buyer Limitations.* The aggregate liability of Buyer under Section 9.3(a) shall not exceed the Cap.

(e) *Materiality Scrape.* For purposes of calculating Losses under this Article IX, any materiality or Material Adverse Effect qualifier in a representation or warranty shall be disregarded.

**9.5 Indemnification Procedures.** An indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any third-party claim. A delay in notice relieves the indemnifying party of its obligations only to the extent it is materially prejudiced. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of a third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party. It shall not settle a claim without the indemnified party's consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money and includes a full release of the indemnified party.

**9.6 Exclusive Remedy.** After the Closing, this Article IX is the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties for any claim arising out of this Agreement. This Section 9.6 does not limit claims for Fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, or the procedures in Section 2.3.

**9.7 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments under this Article IX shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes, unless otherwise required by law.

## ARTICLE X. TAX MATTERS

**10.1 Pre-Closing Returns.** Seller shall prepare, at its expense, all Tax returns of the Company for periods ending on or before the Closing Date that are due after the Closing Date. Seller shall submit each such return to Buyer for review at least twenty (20) days before filing.

**10.2 Transfer Taxes.** All sales, use, vehicle registration and similar transfer Taxes arising from the transfer of the Shares and the Transferred Assets shall be borne one-half by Buyer and one-half by Seller.

**10.3 Cooperation.** Buyer and Seller shall cooperate reasonably in the preparation of Tax returns and the conduct of any Tax audit or proceeding relating to the Company.

## ARTICLE XI. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, each party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with this Agreement.

**11.2 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing. They are deemed given when delivered personally, one Business Day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth on the signature pages or to such other address as a party designates by notice.

**11.3 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**11.4 Successors and Assigns.** This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns. No party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties. Buyer may, however, assign its rights to any affiliate or, after the Closing, to any lender as collateral security.

**11.5 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the Buyer Indemnitees and Seller Indemnitees under Article IX, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the parties.

**11.6 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by each party. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**11.7 Headings; Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. The word "including" means "including without limitation."

**11.8 Governing Law.** This Agreement, and all claims arising out of or relating to it, are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction.

**11.9 Jurisdiction; Venue.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the transactions contemplated by it. If that court lacks subject-matter jurisdiction, the action shall be brought in the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware. Each party waives any objection to venue in those courts.

**11.10 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**11.11 Specific Performance.** The parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if any provision of this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its terms. Each party is entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief, in addition to any other remedy.

**11.12 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
