# ESCROW AGREEMENT

**AGREED FORM: EXHIBIT A TO STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

**THIS ESCROW AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026 by and among the following parties (each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**"):

- **Develstrand Medical Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**");
- **Proubeirt Imaging Group, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"); and
- **Ashcombe Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**").

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of July 14, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer will purchase from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Kestrova Imaging Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

B. Section 2.2(a) of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. The Escrow Amount secures Seller's indemnification obligations under Article 9 of the Purchase Agreement.

C. The Escrow Agent is willing to act as escrow agent on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings set forth below. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. The Escrow Agent is not required to refer to the Purchase Agreement to perform its duties under this Agreement.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a day on which commercial banks in New York City are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.1.

"**Closing Date**" means the date on which the Closing occurs under the Purchase Agreement, as confirmed to the Escrow Agent in the Joint Written Instruction delivered under Section 2.2.

"**Escrow Funds**" means, at any time, the Escrow Amount then held by the Escrow Agent, together with all interest and other income earned on it, less all amounts disbursed under this Agreement.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction. An order is final and non-appealable when the time for appeal has expired and no appeal has been filed.

"**Joint Written Instruction**" means a written instruction signed by an authorized representative of each of Buyer and Seller and delivered to the Escrow Agent.

"**Objection Notice**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.2.

"**Pending Claim Amount**" means, at any time, the total amount of all claims stated in Claim Notices delivered before that time that remain unresolved and unpaid. It includes any portion of a claim that is subject to an Objection Notice that has not been resolved under Section 4.3.

"**Release Date**" means the date that is eighteen (18) months after the Closing Date.

## 2. APPOINTMENT; DEPOSIT

**2.1 Escrow Amount.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit with the Escrow Agent $14,000,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in Schedule 1.

**2.2 Appointment.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent to act as escrow agent under this Agreement, and the Escrow Agent accepts that appointment. When it receives the Escrow Amount, the Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt in writing to Buyer and Seller. Buyer and Seller shall deliver to the Escrow Agent, on the Closing Date, a Joint Written Instruction confirming the Closing Date.

**2.3 Segregated Account.** The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds in a segregated account in the name of the Escrow Agent, for the benefit of Buyer and Seller. It shall not commingle the Escrow Funds with any other funds. The Escrow Funds are not subject to any lien, attachment, set-off or other judicial process by any creditor of any Party.

## 3. INVESTMENT OF ESCROW FUNDS

**3.1 Permitted Investment.** The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds in an interest-bearing, FDIC-insured deposit account or a United States government money market fund, as Buyer and Seller jointly direct in Schedule 2. If the Escrow Agent receives no investment direction, it shall hold the Escrow Funds uninvested in a non-interest-bearing deposit account.

**3.2 Liquidation.** The Escrow Agent may liquidate any investment as needed to make a disbursement under this Agreement. It is not liable for any loss resulting from an investment made under Section 3.1 or from a liquidation made under this Section 3.2.

**3.3 Income.** All interest and other income earned on the Escrow Funds forms part of the Escrow Funds. It shall be disbursed with the Escrow Funds under this Agreement.

## 4. CLAIMS AGAINST THE ESCROW FUNDS

**4.1 Claim Notices.** At any time before the Release Date, Buyer may deliver to Seller and the Escrow Agent a written notice of a claim for indemnification under Article 9 of the Purchase Agreement (a "**Claim Notice**"). Each Claim Notice must describe the claim in reasonable detail and state the amount claimed, which may be a good-faith estimate.

**4.2 Objection.** Seller may object to all or part of a claim by delivering a written notice of objection to Buyer and the Escrow Agent (an "**Objection Notice**") within thirty (30) days after the Escrow Agent receives the Claim Notice. Each Objection Notice must state the amount objected to and the basis for the objection in reasonable detail.

**4.3 Disbursement on Claims.** The Escrow Agent shall disburse amounts on a claim as follows:

(a) If the Escrow Agent receives no Objection Notice within the thirty (30) day period in Section 4.2, it shall pay Buyer the amount stated in the Claim Notice from the Escrow Funds within five (5) Business Days after that period ends.

(b) If the Escrow Agent receives an Objection Notice for only part of a claim, it shall pay Buyer the undisputed portion within five (5) Business Days after receiving the Objection Notice.

(c) The Escrow Agent shall hold the disputed portion of any claim until it receives either a Joint Written Instruction or a copy of a Final Order. It shall then disburse that portion as directed in the instruction or order, within five (5) Business Days after receipt.

**4.4 Insufficient Funds.** If the Escrow Funds are insufficient to pay a claim in full, the Escrow Agent shall pay the remaining Escrow Funds to Buyer. The Escrow Agent has no further obligation for that claim. Any shortfall is recoverable directly from Seller under Section 9.6 of the Purchase Agreement and does not involve the Escrow Agent.

## 5. RELEASE OF ESCROW FUNDS

**5.1 Release on the Release Date.** Within five (5) Business Days after the Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall pay Seller the Escrow Funds then remaining, less the Pending Claim Amount.

**5.2 Retained Amounts.** The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold any amount retained under Section 5.1 for a pending claim. It shall disburse that amount under Section 4.3 when the claim is resolved. If any part of the retained amount is not paid to Buyer on resolution of the claim, the Escrow Agent shall pay that part to Seller within five (5) Business Days after resolution.

**5.3 Joint Written Instructions.** Despite the other provisions of this Section 5 and of Section 4, the Escrow Agent shall disburse Escrow Funds at any time in accordance with a Joint Written Instruction.

**5.4 Method of Payment.** The Escrow Agent shall make all payments by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the accounts designated in Schedule 1, or to another account a Party designates in writing. The Escrow Agent shall confirm any change in payment instructions by telephone call-back under Section 9.

## 6. DUTIES OF THE ESCROW AGENT

**6.1 Limited Duties.** The Escrow Agent's duties are only those expressly set forth in this Agreement. They are purely ministerial. No implied duties or obligations apply to the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent is not a party to the Purchase Agreement and is not bound by it.

**6.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any written notice, instruction or signature that it believes in good faith to be genuine and to have been signed or delivered by the proper Person. It has no duty to verify the accuracy of any statement in a notice or instruction.

**6.3 Liability.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any action taken or omitted under this Agreement, except to the extent caused by its own gross negligence, willful misconduct or bad faith. In no event is the Escrow Agent liable for indirect, special, consequential or punitive damages.

**6.4 Counsel.** The Escrow Agent may consult legal counsel of its choice. It is fully protected in acting or not acting in good faith on the advice of that counsel.

**6.5 Conflicting Demands.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting demands or instructions about the Escrow Funds, it may withhold disbursement of the affected funds until it receives a Joint Written Instruction or a Final Order. It may also deposit the affected funds with a court described in Section 10.2 and seek an order in interpleader.

**6.6 Compliance with Orders.** The Escrow Agent may comply with any order, judgment or decree of a court of competent jurisdiction that affects the Escrow Funds. It is not liable to any Party for doing so, even if the order is later reversed or vacated.

## 7. FEES; INDEMNIFICATION OF THE ESCROW AGENT

**7.1 Fees and Expenses.** The Escrow Agent is entitled to the fees set forth in Schedule 3, together with its reasonable out-of-pocket expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees. Buyer and Seller shall each pay one-half of those fees and expenses.

**7.2 Indemnification.** Buyer and Seller shall jointly and severally indemnify the Escrow Agent and hold it harmless from all losses, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of its performance of this Agreement. This indemnity does not apply to the extent of the Escrow Agent's gross negligence, willful misconduct or bad faith. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear one-half of any amount paid under this Section 7.2, except that a Party whose breach of this Agreement or the Purchase Agreement caused the loss shall bear all of it.

**7.3 No Set-Off Against Escrow Funds.** The Escrow Agent shall not deduct its fees, expenses or indemnity claims from the Escrow Funds.

**7.4 Survival.** This Section 7 survives the termination of this Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

## 8. TAX MATTERS

**8.1 Tax Owner.** For U.S. federal and applicable state income tax purposes, Buyer is treated as the owner of the Escrow Funds until they are disbursed. Buyer shall report all income earned on the Escrow Funds, whether or not distributed. Each disbursement to Seller is treated as a payment of Purchase Price, with imputed interest as required by Section 483 or Section 1274 of the Internal Revenue Code.

**8.2 Tax Forms.** Before the Closing Date, each of Buyer and Seller shall deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9.

**8.3 Reporting and Withholding.** The Escrow Agent shall file all information returns required by law with respect to income earned on the Escrow Funds. It may withhold from any disbursement any taxes it is required by law to withhold.

## 9. SECURITY PROCEDURES

**9.1 Call-Back.** Before acting on any funds-transfer instruction that is not a standing instruction in Schedule 1, or on any change to the account information in Schedule 1, the Escrow Agent shall confirm the instruction by telephone call-back to a person listed in Schedule 4 for the relevant Party. That person must be someone other than the person who signed the instruction.

**9.2 Authorized Persons.** Each of Buyer and Seller may update its list of authorized persons in Schedule 4 by written notice to the Escrow Agent. The update becomes effective when the Escrow Agent acknowledges receipt.

## 10. MISCELLANEOUS

**10.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**10.2 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement. EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**10.3 Resignation and Removal.** The Escrow Agent may resign by giving Buyer and Seller at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice. Buyer and Seller may jointly remove the Escrow Agent by giving it at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice. In either case, Buyer and Seller shall jointly appoint a successor escrow agent. The resigning or removed Escrow Agent shall transfer the Escrow Funds to the successor when the successor signs a joinder to this Agreement. If no successor is appointed within thirty (30) days after the notice, the Escrow Agent may petition a court described in Section 10.2 to appoint one.

**10.4 Termination.** This Agreement terminates when the Escrow Agent has disbursed all Escrow Funds under this Agreement.

**10.5 Notices.** Notices must be in writing. They are deemed given when delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the following addresses (or another address a Party designates by notice):

- **Buyer:** Develstrand Medical Holdings, Inc., Attention: General Counsel.
- **Seller:** Proubeirt Imaging Group, LLC, Attention: Chief Legal Officer.
- **Escrow Agent:** Ashcombe Trust Company, N.A., Attention: Corporate Trust and Escrow Services.

Notices to the Escrow Agent are effective only when the Escrow Agent actually receives them.

**10.6 Assignment.** No Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Parties, except that Buyer may assign its rights under this Agreement to any Person to which it assigns its rights under the Purchase Agreement in accordance with Section 10.4 of the Purchase Agreement, on written notice to Seller and the Escrow Agent. Any successor to the Escrow Agent by merger, conversion or consolidation, or by transfer of all or substantially all of its corporate trust business, becomes the Escrow Agent under this Agreement without further action.

**10.7 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by all Parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the Party against whom it is asserted.

**10.8 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**10.9 USA PATRIOT Act.** Buyer and Seller acknowledge that the Escrow Agent must obtain, verify and record information that identifies each Person that opens an account. Each of Buyer and Seller shall provide the information the Escrow Agent reasonably requests for that purpose.

**10.10 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and together they constitute one instrument.
