**Re: Stock Purchase Agreement dated July 14, 2026: Side Letter**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter (this "**Side Letter**") refers to the Stock Purchase Agreement, dated as of July 14, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"), between Develstrand Medical Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and Proubeirt Imaging Group, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**"). The Purchase Agreement relates to the purchase by Buyer of all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Kestrova Imaging Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

## RECITALS

A. Under Section 5.6 of the Purchase Agreement, Seller delivered to Buyer on September 8, 2026 a Schedule Supplement (the "**September Supplement**"). The September Supplement discloses the inspectional observations issued to the Company on FDA Form 483 following the FDA's inspection of the Company's principal manufacturing facility (the "**Form 483 Matter**").

B. Buyer responded to the September Supplement by letter dated October 20, 2026 (the "**Buyer Response Letter**"). In that letter Buyer disputed the September Supplement and the adequacy of Seller's disclosure of the Form 483 Matter. Buyer has not delivered a notice of termination of the Purchase Agreement. Seller disagrees with the positions stated in the Buyer Response Letter.

C. Buyer and Seller wish to confirm certain arrangements relating to the Closing and to amend the Purchase Agreement as set forth in this Side Letter.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Defined Terms; Interpretation

**1.1** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

**1.2** Section 1.2 (Interpretation) of the Purchase Agreement applies to this Side Letter as if set out in full, with references to "this Agreement" read as references to this Side Letter.

## 2. Closing Arrangements

**2.1 Closing Date.** The parties intend that the Closing will take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents on the Closing Date determined under Section 2.3 of the Purchase Agreement.

**2.2 Payment Instructions.** For purposes of Section 2.2(c) of the Purchase Agreement, Seller will deliver its written wire instructions to Buyer at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date. Buyer may confirm those instructions by telephone with Seller's Chief Financial Officer before releasing funds.

**2.3 Escrow.** At the Closing, Buyer will pay the Escrow Amount of $14,000,000 to Ashcombe Trust Company, N.A., as Escrow Agent, under Section 2.2(a) of the Purchase Agreement. The Escrow Amount will be held and disbursed under the Escrow Agreement and Article 9 of the Purchase Agreement.

**2.4 Officer and Director Resignations.** Buyer will deliver its designation of the directors and officers of the Company whose resignations are to be delivered under Section 2.3(b)(iii) of the Purchase Agreement at least five (5) Business Days before the Closing Date. Seller will cause each designated individual to deliver a written resignation, effective as of the Closing.

## 3. Amendment of Section 1.1 of the Purchase Agreement

**3.1** Section 1.1 of the Purchase Agreement is amended by deleting the definition of "Fundamental Representations" in its entirety and replacing it with the following:

> "**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties set forth in Sections 4.1 and 4.2.

**3.2** Each reference in the Purchase Agreement to "Fundamental Representations" will be read as a reference to that term as amended by Section 3.1 of this Side Letter.

## 4. Reservation of Positions

**4.1** Neither the execution of this Side Letter nor the consummation of the Closing constitutes an admission by Buyer or Seller about the September Supplement, the Form 483 Matter or the positions stated in the Buyer Response Letter.

**4.2** Each of Buyer and Seller reserves its positions about the September Supplement and the Buyer Response Letter.

## 5. Effectiveness; Effect on Purchase Agreement

**5.1 Effectiveness.** This Side Letter becomes effective on the Closing, upon execution by Buyer and Seller. If the Purchase Agreement is terminated before the Closing, this Side Letter will be of no force or effect.

**5.2 Amendment.** This Side Letter is a written instrument signed by Buyer and Seller for purposes of Section 10.2 of the Purchase Agreement. It expressly amends the Purchase Agreement to the extent set forth in Section 3.

**5.3 Purchase Agreement Otherwise Unchanged.** Except as expressly amended by this Side Letter, the Purchase Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect.

## 6. Miscellaneous

**6.1 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Side Letter is governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction. Section 10.8 of the Purchase Agreement applies to this Side Letter.

**6.2 Notices.** Notices under this Side Letter will be given in accordance with Section 10.3 of the Purchase Agreement.

**6.3 Amendment.** This Side Letter may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer and Seller.

**6.4 Counterparts.** This Side Letter may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
