# KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT

**AGREED FORM: EXHIBIT C TO STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT**

**THIS KEY EMPLOYEE AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026, by and between **Kestrova Imaging Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), and **Dr. Linnea Okafor-Brandt**, an individual (the "**Executive**"). It becomes effective as of the Effective Date (as defined below).

## RECITALS

A. The Executive is employed by the Company as Vice President, Engineering.

B. Proubeirt Imaging Group, LLC ("**Seller**") and Develstrand Medical Holdings, Inc. ("**Parent**") are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated July 14, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Parent will acquire all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of the Company (the "**Transaction**").

C. The Executive will receive substantial benefits from the Transaction and from continued employment with the Company after the Transaction. The Executive has access to the Company's trade secrets, product designs, customer relationships and regulatory submissions. The Company and Parent are willing to proceed with the Transaction and to continue the Executive's employment only if the Executive enters into this Agreement, including the covenants in Section 7 and Section 8.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises in this Agreement, the Executive's continued employment and the Retention Bonus described in Section 4.4, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Affiliate**" means any Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with the Company, including, from and after the Effective Date, Parent and its subsidiaries.

"**Board**" means the board of directors of the Company.

"**Business**" means the design and manufacture of CT detector modules.

"**Cause**" has the meaning set forth in Section 6.2.

"**Closing Date**" means the date on which the closing of the Transaction occurs under the Purchase Agreement.

"**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information of or relating to the Company or its Affiliates, in any form. It includes product designs, schematics, firmware, source code, test data, manufacturing processes, supplier terms, pricing, customer lists, business plans, and all regulatory submissions, correspondence and inspection records relating to the FDA or any other Governmental Authority. Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach of this Agreement by the Executive.

"**Effective Date**" means the Closing Date.

"**Good Reason**" has the meaning set forth in Section 6.3.

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government, or any agency, court, tribunal or other regulatory or administrative body thereof, including the United States Food and Drug Administration.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Restricted Period**" means the period beginning on the Effective Date and ending twenty-four (24) months after the Closing Date.

"**Work Product**" has the meaning set forth in Section 7.3.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation."

## 2. EMPLOYMENT; TERM

**2.1 Effectiveness.** This Agreement becomes effective on the Effective Date. If the Purchase Agreement is terminated before the Closing Date, this Agreement is void *ab initio*. In that case the Executive's employment continues on the terms in effect immediately before the date of this Agreement.

**2.2 Term.** The Company shall employ the Executive under this Agreement for an initial term beginning on the Effective Date and ending on the third (3rd) anniversary of the Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"). After the Initial Term, the term renews automatically for successive one-year periods (each, a "**Renewal Term**"), unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term. The Initial Term and any Renewal Terms together are the "**Term**." The Term ends earlier on any termination of employment under Section 6.

## 3. POSITION AND DUTIES

**3.1 Position.** The Executive shall serve as Vice President, Engineering of the Company and shall report to the Company's Chief Executive Officer.

**3.2 Duties.** The Executive shall have the duties, authority and responsibilities customary for that position. These include leadership of product engineering, design controls, design history files, verification and validation, and engineering support for regulatory submissions. The Executive shall also perform other duties consistent with the position that the Chief Executive Officer reasonably assigns.

**3.3 Full Time and Attention.** The Executive shall devote her full business time and efforts to the Company. She may (a) serve on civic, charitable or professional boards, (b) publish and lecture in her field, and (c) manage personal investments. Each of these activities is permitted only if it does not materially interfere with her duties and does not violate Section 7 or Section 8.

**3.4 Place of Performance.** The Executive's principal place of employment is the Company's headquarters. She will travel for business as reasonably required.

## 4. COMPENSATION

**4.1 Base Salary.** The Company shall pay the Executive an annual base salary of $385,000 ("**Base Salary**"), in accordance with its regular payroll practices. The Base Salary will be reviewed annually and may be increased, but not decreased, at the Board's discretion.

**4.2 Annual Bonus.** For each fiscal year during the Term, the Executive is eligible for an annual cash bonus with a target of forty percent (40%) of Base Salary. Payment is based on achievement of performance goals set by the Board after consultation with the Executive. Any annual bonus will be paid no later than March 15 of the year following the year to which it relates. Except as provided in Section 6.5, the Executive must be employed on the payment date to receive it.

**4.3 Equity Awards.** The Executive is eligible for equity incentive awards under Parent's equity incentive plan. Within ninety (90) days after the Effective Date, Parent's compensation committee intends to grant the Executive restricted stock units with a grant-date value of $900,000. The units will vest in equal annual installments over four (4) years, subject to the terms of the plan and the applicable award agreement.

**4.4 Retention Bonus.** The Company shall pay the Executive a retention bonus of $600,000 (the "**Retention Bonus**") in two installments:

(a) $300,000 within thirty (30) days after the first anniversary of the Effective Date; and

(b) $300,000 within thirty (30) days after the second anniversary of the Effective Date.

Each installment is payable only if the Executive remains employed on the applicable anniversary. If the Company terminates her employment without Cause, or she resigns for Good Reason, before an installment is paid, any unpaid installments will be paid under Section 6.5.

## 5. BENEFITS AND EXPENSES

**5.1 Benefit Plans.** The Executive may participate in the employee benefit plans maintained by the Company or Parent for similarly situated senior employees, subject to the terms of those plans.

**5.2 Paid Time Off.** The Executive is entitled to twenty-five (25) days of paid time off per year, in accordance with Company policy.

**5.3 Expenses.** The Company shall reimburse the Executive for reasonable business expenses she incurs in performing her duties, in accordance with Company policy.

**5.4 Indemnification; Insurance.** The Company shall indemnify the Executive to the fullest extent permitted by its organizational documents and applicable law. It shall also cover her under its directors' and officers' liability insurance on the same basis as other senior officers.

## 6. TERMINATION OF EMPLOYMENT

**6.1 General.** Either party may terminate the Executive's employment at any time, subject to the terms of this Section 6.

**6.2 Termination by the Company for Cause.** The Company may terminate the Executive's employment for Cause. "**Cause**" means any of the following by the Executive:

(a) conviction of, or plea of guilty or *nolo contendere* to, a felony;

(b) willful misconduct or gross negligence that causes material harm to the Company;

(c) material breach of Section 7 or Section 8;

(d) knowing falsification of any design, test or regulatory record; or

(e) continued willful failure to perform material duties after written notice and a thirty (30) day cure period.

**6.3 Resignation for Good Reason.** The Executive may resign for Good Reason. "**Good Reason**" means any of the following, without her written consent:

(a) a material reduction in Base Salary or target bonus;

(b) a material diminution in title, authority or duties;

(c) relocation of her principal place of employment by more than fifty (50) miles; or

(d) a material breach of this Agreement by the Company.

To resign for Good Reason, the Executive must give written notice within sixty (60) days after the initial occurrence of the condition. The Company then has thirty (30) days to cure it. If the condition is not cured, she must resign within thirty (30) days after the cure period ends.

**6.4 Death or Disability.** The Executive's employment terminates automatically on her death. The Company may terminate her employment if she is unable to perform her essential duties, with or without reasonable accommodation, for one hundred twenty (120) consecutive days, or one hundred eighty (180) days in any twelve-month period, because of physical or mental incapacity.

**6.5 Severance.** If the Company terminates the Executive's employment without Cause, or she resigns for Good Reason, the Company shall provide the following, subject to Section 6.6:

(a) continued payment of Base Salary for twelve (12) months;

(b) a pro-rated annual bonus for the year of termination, based on actual performance;

(c) payment of any unpaid installments of the Retention Bonus, within sixty (60) days after termination; and

(d) payment of COBRA premiums for up to twelve (12) months.

**6.6 Release.** The payments and benefits in Section 6.5 depend on two conditions:

(a) the Executive must sign, and not revoke, a general release of claims in a form reasonably acceptable to the Company, within sixty (60) days after termination; and

(b) she must continue to comply with Section 7 and Section 8.

**6.7 Accrued Obligations.** On any termination, the Company shall pay the Executive:

(a) earned but unpaid Base Salary;

(b) accrued but unused paid time off, to the extent required by Company policy;

(c) unreimbursed business expenses; and

(d) vested benefits under the Company's benefit plans.

## 7. CONFIDENTIALITY AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**7.1 Confidentiality.** During and after her employment, the Executive shall hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence. She shall not use or disclose it except in performing her duties for the Company.

**7.2 Permitted Disclosures.** Nothing in this Agreement prohibits the Executive from:

(a) reporting possible violations of law to any Governmental Authority;

(b) participating in any investigation by a Governmental Authority; or

(c) making disclosures protected under whistleblower laws.

Under the Defend Trade Secrets Act of 2016, the Executive will not be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for disclosing a trade secret in either of the following ways:

- in confidence to a government official or an attorney, solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law; or
- in a complaint or other document filed under seal in a lawsuit or other proceeding.

**7.3 Work Product.** All inventions, designs, improvements, software, documentation and other work product that the Executive conceives or develops during her employment and that relate to the actual or anticipated business of the Company (collectively, "**Work Product**") are the sole property of the Company. The Executive hereby assigns to the Company all of her right, title and interest in all Work Product. She shall sign all documents reasonably requested to perfect the Company's rights. This Section 7.3 does not apply to any invention that qualifies for exclusion under applicable state law.

**7.4 Return of Property.** On termination of employment, the Executive shall promptly return all Company property, documents and Confidential Information in her possession.

## 8. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

**8.1 Non-Competition.** During her employment and for a period of 24 months after the Closing Date, the Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, anywhere in the world, engage in the Business. This restriction applies whether she acts as an owner, partner, employee, consultant, officer, director, advisor or otherwise. It also covers owning, managing, operating, controlling or providing services to any Person that engages in the Business.

The following do not violate this Section 8.1:

(a) passive ownership of less than two percent (2%) of a publicly traded company; and

(b) employment with a diversified company that has a division engaged in the Business, as long as the Executive provides no services to, and has no responsibility for, that division.

**8.2 Non-Solicitation of Employees.** During the Restricted Period, the Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, recruit or hire any employee of the Company or its Affiliates, or induce any such employee to leave employment. General advertisements not targeted at those employees do not violate this Section 8.2.

**8.3 Non-Solicitation of Customers and Suppliers.** During the Restricted Period, the Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit any customer or supplier of the Company with whom she had material contact during the last two (2) years of her employment. Solicitation is prohibited if its purpose is either of the following:

(a) diverting business from the Company; or

(b) inducing the customer or supplier to terminate or adversely modify its relationship with the Company.

**8.4 Non-Disparagement.** During and after her employment, the Executive shall not make any statement that disparages the Company, Parent or their products, officers or directors. This Section 8.4 does not restrict truthful statements made under Section 7.2 or required by law.

**8.5 Reasonableness; Consideration.** The Executive acknowledges the following:

(a) the covenants in this Section 8 are reasonable in duration, geographic scope and subject matter;

(b) they are necessary to protect the goodwill acquired by Parent in the Transaction and the Company's Confidential Information; and

(c) she has received adequate consideration for them, including the Retention Bonus and her continued employment.

**8.6 Reformation.** If a court of competent jurisdiction finds any covenant in this Section 8 unenforceable because of its duration or scope, the court may reduce the duration or scope to the extent necessary to make it enforceable. The covenant will then be enforced as so reduced.

**8.7 Tolling.** If the Executive violates Section 8.2 or Section 8.3, the Restricted Period for that covenant is extended by the period of the violation.

## 9. REMEDIES

**9.1 Injunctive Relief.** The Executive acknowledges that a breach of Section 7 or Section 8 would cause the Company irreparable harm for which money damages would be inadequate. The Company is entitled to injunctive relief and specific performance, without posting a bond, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.

**9.2 Forfeiture.** If the Executive materially breaches Section 7 or Section 8, the Company's obligation to make any further payment under Section 6.5 ends.

## 10. MISCELLANEOUS

**10.1 Assignment.** This Agreement is personal to the Executive and she may not assign it. The Company may assign this Agreement to Parent, to any Affiliate, or to any successor to all or substantially all of its business or assets.

**10.2 Third-Party Beneficiary.** Parent is an express third-party beneficiary of Section 7 and Section 8 and may enforce them.

**10.3 Notices.** Notices must be in writing. They are deemed given when delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the following addresses:

- **Company:** Kestrova Imaging Systems, Inc., Attention: Chief Executive Officer, with a copy to Develstrand Medical Holdings, Inc., Attention: General Counsel.
- **Executive:** the most recent address in the Company's records.

**10.4 Section 409A.** This Agreement is intended to comply with, or be exempt from, Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code and will be interpreted accordingly. Each installment payment is a separate payment. Any payment conditioned on a termination of employment will be made only on a "separation from service."

**10.5 Withholding.** The Company may withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement all taxes required to be withheld by law.

**10.6 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by the Executive and an authorized officer of the Company. No waiver is effective unless in writing.

**10.7 Severability.** Subject to Section 8.6, if any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**10.8 Survival.** Sections 6.5 through 6.7, 7, 8, 9 and 10 survive the termination of the Executive's employment and of this Agreement.

**10.9 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

**10.10 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and together they constitute one instrument.
