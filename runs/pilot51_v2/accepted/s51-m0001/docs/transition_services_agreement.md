# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

**THIS TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 1, 2023 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Proubeirt Imaging Group, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (the "**Service Provider**"), and **Kestrova Imaging Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Service Recipient**"). The Service Provider and the Service Recipient are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. The Service Recipient is a wholly owned subsidiary of the Service Provider. It designs, manufactures, sells and services MRI receive coils, CT detector modules and ultrasound transducer arrays.

B. The Service Provider maintains centralized enterprise resource planning systems, a regulatory affairs function and a field service dispatch organization for its group companies.

C. The Service Recipient wishes to receive certain of these services from the Service Provider, and the Service Provider is willing to provide them, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** In this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with such Person. For this purpose, "control" means the possession, directly or indirectly, of the power to direct the management and policies of a Person, whether through ownership of voting securities, by contract or otherwise.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a day on which commercial banks in New York City are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Change of Control**" means, with respect to the Service Recipient, any transaction or series of related transactions that results in any of the following:

(a) any Person or group of Persons, other than the Service Provider or its Affiliates, acquiring beneficial ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the outstanding voting securities of the Service Recipient;

(b) a merger, consolidation or similar transaction involving the Service Recipient, after which the Service Provider and its Affiliates cease to hold more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting power of the surviving entity; or

(c) the sale of all or substantially all of the assets of the Service Recipient to any Person other than the Service Provider or its Affiliates.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning set forth in Section 8.1.

"**FDA**" means the United States Food and Drug Administration.

"**Fees**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.1.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, governmental authority or other entity.

"**Recipient Data**" means all data and information of the Service Recipient that is entered into, stored in, processed by or generated through the Services. It includes customer records, pricing, bills of materials, inventory and supply-chain data, service histories, complaint files and regulatory submissions.

"**Service Level**" means, for each Service, the performance standard set forth for that Service in Schedule A.

"**Services**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.1.

"**Term**" has the meaning set forth in Section 9.1.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to Sections and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

## 2. SERVICES

**2.1 Provision of Services.** During the Term, the Service Provider shall provide, or cause one or more of its Affiliates to provide, the following services to the Service Recipient, each as further described in Schedule A (collectively, the "**Services**"):

(a) **ERP Hosting.** Hosting, operation, maintenance and support of the enterprise resource planning environment used by the Service Recipient. This covers order management, procurement, inventory, production planning, finance and the device history record modules.

(b) **Regulatory Affairs Support.** Preparation, review and submission of premarket notifications, establishment registrations and device listings, and medical device reports. It also includes correction and removal reports and responses to FDA correspondence and inspectional observations, together with maintenance of the related regulatory files.

(c) **Field Service Dispatch.** Operation of the call center, ticketing system and technician scheduling platform used to receive, log, triage and dispatch customer service requests for installed products of the Service Recipient.

**2.2 Standard of Performance.** The Service Provider shall perform the Services:

(a) with the same degree of care, skill and priority with which it performs similar services for itself and its other Affiliates;

(b) in accordance with the applicable Service Levels; and

(c) in compliance with all applicable laws, including 21 C.F.R. Parts 803, 806, 807 and 820 to the extent they apply to the Services.

**2.3 Personnel.** The Service Provider shall assign suitably qualified personnel to perform the Services. The Service Provider may determine which of its personnel perform the Services, but it shall designate a service manager for each Service as a point of contact for the Service Recipient.

**2.4 Additional Services.** The Service Recipient may request services not described in Schedule A. If the Parties agree in writing to provide any such service, it will become a Service and its fees and Service Levels will be set out in an amendment to Schedule A.

**2.5 Service Recipient Cooperation.** The Service Recipient shall provide the Service Provider with the information, access and assistance reasonably required to perform the Services. It shall promptly notify the Service Provider of any change in its operations that may affect the Services.

## 3. SYSTEMS AND DATA

**3.1 Access to Systems.** The Service Provider shall grant the Service Recipient's authorized users access to the ERP environment and the field service dispatch platform, subject to the Service Provider's reasonable security policies as provided to the Service Recipient in writing.

**3.2 Ownership of Recipient Data.** As between the Parties, the Service Recipient owns all Recipient Data. The Service Provider shall use Recipient Data solely to perform the Services.

**3.3 Data Security.** The Service Provider shall maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards designed to protect Recipient Data against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration or destruction. These safeguards shall be no less protective than those the Service Provider applies to its own data of a similar nature. The Service Provider shall notify the Service Recipient within forty-eight (48) hours after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to Recipient Data.

**3.4 Records.** The Service Provider shall maintain complete and accurate records relating to the Services, including all regulatory filings made on behalf of the Service Recipient. It shall retain them for the periods required by applicable law, and in any event for at least the design and expected life of the relevant device, but no less than two (2) years from the date of release for commercial distribution.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

**4.1 Fees.** The Service Recipient shall pay the Service Provider the fees for each Service set forth in Schedule B (the "**Fees**"). Fees are calculated on the basis of the Service Provider's fully loaded cost of providing the relevant Service plus a margin of five percent (5%).

**4.2 Invoicing.** The Service Provider shall invoice the Service Recipient monthly in arrears. Each invoice must itemize the Fees by Service and include reasonable supporting detail.

**4.3 Payment.** The Service Recipient shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt. If it disputes any amount in good faith, it shall notify the Service Provider in writing within that period and pay the undisputed portion. The Parties shall work together in good faith to resolve the dispute.

**4.4 Fee Adjustments.** The Service Provider may adjust the Fees once in each calendar year, on at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice, to reflect changes in its actual cost of providing the Services. No adjustment may increase the Fees for any Service by more than five percent (5%) over the Fees in effect for the preceding twelve months.

**4.5 Taxes.** Fees are exclusive of sales, use, value-added and similar taxes. The Service Recipient shall pay those taxes, other than taxes on the Service Provider's net income.

## 5. GOVERNANCE

**5.1 Relationship Managers.** Each Party shall appoint a relationship manager to oversee performance of this Agreement. The relationship managers shall meet at least quarterly to review performance against the Service Levels and any open issues.

**5.2 Service Level Failures.** If the Service Provider fails to meet a Service Level in any calendar month, it shall do the following within ten (10) Business Days:

(a) investigate the root cause of the failure;

(b) deliver a written corrective action plan to the Service Recipient; and

(c) implement that plan promptly.

**5.3 Escalation.** Any dispute that the relationship managers cannot resolve within fifteen (15) Business Days shall be escalated to the chief operating officer of each Party.

## 6. REGULATORY MATTERS

**6.1 Responsibility for Compliance.** The Service Recipient remains the manufacturer of record for its devices. It retains ultimate responsibility for its compliance with applicable FDA requirements. The Service Provider shall perform the regulatory affairs support Services under the direction of the Service Recipient's designated management representative.

**6.2 Inspections.** The Service Provider shall cooperate with the Service Recipient in connection with any FDA inspection of the Service Recipient. It shall make available its personnel and the records it maintains under Section 3.4.

**6.3 Notification.** The Service Provider shall notify the Service Recipient within two (2) Business Days after receiving any correspondence from the FDA relating to the Service Recipient or its devices.

## 7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**7.1 Service Provider Property.** The Service Provider retains all right, title and interest in its systems, software, tools, methods and know-how used to provide the Services.

**7.2 Recipient Property.** The Service Recipient retains all right, title and interest in its intellectual property and in the Recipient Data. Any deliverable created specifically for the Service Recipient under this Agreement, including regulatory submissions and design documentation, is the property of the Service Recipient.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY

**8.1 Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement, including the Recipient Data. It excludes information that:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach of this Agreement;

(b) was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure; or

(c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction.

**8.2 Obligations.** Each Party shall hold the other Party's Confidential Information in confidence. It shall use that information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement. It shall not disclose that information except to its employees, Affiliates and advisors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 8.

**8.3 Compelled Disclosure.** A Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law or by a governmental authority. Where legally permitted, it shall first give the other Party prompt notice and a reasonable opportunity to seek a protective order.

## 9. TERM AND TERMINATION

**9.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until June 30, 2027, unless terminated earlier under this Section 9 (the "**Term**").

**9.2 Termination of Individual Services.** The Service Recipient may terminate any individual Service on at least ninety (90) days' prior written notice to the Service Provider. On termination of a Service, the Fees will be reduced accordingly.

**9.3 Change of Control.** This Agreement shall terminate automatically upon a Change of Control of the Service Recipient, unless the Change of Control has received the prior written consent of the Service Provider.

**9.4 Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement if the other Party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing the breach in reasonable detail. For a failure to pay undisputed Fees, the cure period is fifteen (15) days.

**9.5 Termination for Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

**9.6 Effect of Termination.** On expiration or termination of this Agreement or of any Service:

(a) the Service Recipient shall pay all Fees accrued through the effective date of termination;

(b) the Service Provider shall return all Recipient Data to the Service Recipient within thirty (30) days, in a commonly used, machine-readable format, and then securely delete all copies, except as required to be retained by law; and

(c) Sections 3.2, 3.4, 4, 7, 8, 9.6, 10, 11 and 12 survive.

**9.7 Migration Assistance.** For up to ninety (90) days after expiration or termination of this Agreement, the Service Provider shall provide reasonable assistance to migrate the Services and the Recipient Data to the Service Recipient or its designee. This assistance is chargeable at the Service Provider's then-current cost plus a margin of five percent (5%).

## 10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**10.1 Exclusion of Indirect Damages.** Neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of or relating to this Agreement. This exclusion does not apply to breaches of Section 3.3 or Section 8, or to a Party's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

**10.2 Cap.** The aggregate liability of each Party under this Agreement shall not exceed the total Fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. This cap does not apply to breaches of Section 3.3 or Section 8, a Party's payment obligations, or a Party's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

## 11. INDEMNIFICATION

**11.1 By the Service Provider.** The Service Provider shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Service Recipient and its officers, directors and employees from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from any third-party claim to the extent caused by the Service Provider's gross negligence, willful misconduct or breach of Section 3.3 or Section 8.

**11.2 By the Service Recipient.** The Service Recipient shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Service Provider and its officers, directors and employees from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising from any third-party claim to the extent caused by the Service Recipient's gross negligence, willful misconduct or breach of Section 8.

**11.3 Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of any claim and reasonable cooperation in its defense. The indemnifying Party may control the defense and settlement of the claim, but may not settle it in a way that imposes any obligation on the indemnified Party without its prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

## 12. MISCELLANEOUS

**12.1 Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship between them.

**12.2 Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for any delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, epidemics, war, terrorism, labor disputes (other than those of the affected Party's own workforce) or failures of public utilities. The affected Party shall give prompt notice and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. Payment obligations are not excused by this Section 12.2.

**12.3 Notices.** Notices must be in writing. They are deemed given when delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the following addresses (or another address a Party designates by notice):

- **Service Provider:** Proubeirt Imaging Group, LLC, Attention: Chief Legal Officer.
- **Service Recipient:** Kestrova Imaging Systems, Inc., Attention: Chief Executive Officer.

**12.4 Assignment.** The Service Provider may assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, to any Person without the consent of the Service Recipient, on written notice to the Service Recipient. The Service Recipient may not assign this Agreement, by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior written consent of the Service Provider. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 12.4 is void. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

**12.5 Subcontracting.** The Service Provider may subcontract any portion of the Services to its Affiliates or to third-party vendors. It remains responsible for the performance of its subcontractors.

**12.6 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the Party against whom it is asserted.

**12.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**12.8 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

**12.9 Jurisdiction.** Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

**12.10 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and together they constitute one instrument.
