# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**THIS STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT** (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of July 14, 2026, by and between **Develstrand Medical Holdings, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and **Proubeirt Imaging Group, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Seller**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller is the record and beneficial owner of all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Kestrova Imaging Systems, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**").

B. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE 2. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchase and Sale; Purchase Price.** On the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement, at the Closing Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all Liens, for an aggregate purchase price of $186,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment in accordance with Section 2.4.

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay:

(a) to the Escrow Agent, the Escrow Amount, to be held and disbursed in accordance with the Escrow Agreement and Article 9;

(b) on behalf of the Company, the amounts set forth in the payoff letters delivered under Section 2.3(b)(vi); and

(c) to Seller, the Purchase Price, less the Escrow Amount, less the amounts paid under Section 2.2(b), plus or minus the Estimated Working Capital Adjustment, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by Seller at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date.

**2.3 Closing; Closing Deliverables.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents on the third (3rd) Business Day after the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions in Article 6 (other than conditions to be satisfied at the Closing), or on such other date as the parties agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

(a) *Buyer Deliverables.* At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver:

(i) the payments described in Section 2.2;

(ii) a counterpart of the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Buyer;

(iii) the certificate of an officer of Buyer described in Section 6.3(c); and

(iv) a certificate of the secretary of Buyer certifying the resolutions of Buyer's board of directors authorizing this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby.

(b) *Seller Deliverables.* At the Closing, Seller shall deliver, or cause to be delivered, to Buyer:

(i) certificates representing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers duly executed in blank;

(ii) a counterpart of the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(iii) written resignations, effective as of the Closing, of each director and officer of the Company designated by Buyer at least five (5) Business Days before the Closing;

(iv) the certificate of an officer of Seller described in Section 6.2(c);

(v) a certificate of the secretary of Seller certifying the resolutions of Seller's board of managers authorizing this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby;

(vi) customary payoff letters for all indebtedness for borrowed money of the Company, together with evidence of release of all related Liens;

(vii) evidence reasonably satisfactory to Buyer of the termination, effective as of the Closing and without liability to the Company, of each Affiliate Contract other than the Transition Services Agreement;

(viii) certificates of good standing of the Company and Seller issued by the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware, each dated within ten (10) days before the Closing Date; and

(ix) a properly completed IRS Form W-9 of Seller.

**2.4 Working Capital Adjustment.** Not later than three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a good-faith estimate of the Company's Closing Working Capital and the resulting adjustment against a target of $21,500,000 (the "**Estimated Working Capital Adjustment**"). Within ninety (90) days after the Closing, Buyer shall deliver its calculation of Closing Working Capital. Disputes not resolved within thirty (30) days shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm of national standing mutually selected by the parties, whose determination shall be final and binding. Any resulting payment shall be made within five (5) Business Days after final determination.

**2.5 Withholding.** Buyer and the Escrow Agent may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement such amounts as are required to be deducted and withheld under applicable Tax Law.

## ARTICLE 3. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as of the date hereof and as of the Closing Date that:

**3.1 Organization.** Buyer is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware.

**3.2 Authority.** Buyer has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party. This Agreement constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Buyer, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**3.3 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer will have at the Closing sufficient immediately available funds to pay the Purchase Price and all other amounts payable by Buyer hereunder.

**3.4 Investment Intent.** Buyer is acquiring the Shares for its own account for investment and not with a view to distribution in violation of applicable securities Laws.

**3.5 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission from Seller in connection with the transactions contemplated hereby based on arrangements made by Buyer.

## ARTICLE 4. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date hereof and as of the Closing Date that:

**4.1 Organization and Good Standing.** Each of Seller and the Company is duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware and has all requisite power and authority to own its assets and conduct its business as currently conducted. The Company is duly qualified to do business in each jurisdiction where such qualification is required.

**4.2 Authority; Enforceability.** Seller has full limited liability company power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party. The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement have been duly authorized by all necessary action of Seller. This Agreement constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 Capitalization.** The authorized capital stock of the Company consists of 10,000,000 shares of common stock, par value $0.001 per share, of which 4,250,000 shares are issued and outstanding, all of which constitute the Shares and are owned of record and beneficially by Seller free and clear of all Liens. All Shares are duly authorized, validly issued, fully paid and non-assessable. There are no outstanding options, warrants, convertible securities, phantom equity or other rights obligating the Company to issue, sell or repurchase any equity interest.

**4.4 No Conflicts; Consents.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not (a) violate the organizational documents of Seller or the Company, (b) violate any Law applicable to Seller or the Company, or (c) except as set forth in Section 4.4 of the Disclosure Schedule, require consent under, conflict with or result in a breach of or default under any material Contract to which the Company is a party.

**4.5 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the audited balance sheets of the Company as of December 31, 2024 and December 31, 2025 and the related statements of income and cash flows for the years then ended, and the unaudited balance sheet as of May 31, 2026 and the related statements for the five months then ended (collectively, the "**Financial Statements**"). The Financial Statements were prepared in accordance with GAAP consistently applied and fairly present in all material respects the financial position and results of operations of the Company as of their respective dates.

**4.6 Absence of Certain Changes.** Since December 31, 2025, the Company has conducted the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, and there has not been any Material Adverse Effect.

**4.7 Material Contracts.** Section 4.7 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each Contract of the Company involving annual payments in excess of $500,000, each Affiliate Contract, each Contract containing exclusivity or non-competition obligations binding on the Company, and each Contract with a Governmental Authority. Each such Contract is valid, binding and in full force and effect, and neither the Company nor, to the Knowledge of Seller, any counterparty is in material breach thereof.

**4.8 Intellectual Property.** The Company owns or has a valid right to use all intellectual property used in or necessary for the conduct of the Business. To the Knowledge of Seller, the conduct of the Business does not infringe, misappropriate or otherwise violate the intellectual property rights of any Person.

**4.9 Litigation.** There is no action, suit, proceeding or investigation pending or, to the Knowledge of Seller, threatened against the Company or affecting the Business.

**4.10 Employees and Benefit Plans.** Section 4.10 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each employee benefit plan of the Company. Each such plan has been maintained in material compliance with its terms and applicable Law. The Company is not party to any collective bargaining agreement.

**4.11 Taxes.** All Tax returns required to be filed by the Company have been timely filed and are true, correct and complete in all material respects, and all Taxes due and owing by the Company have been paid.

**4.12 Regulatory Compliance.** (a) The Company is, and since January 1, 2021 has been, in compliance in all material respects with all Healthcare Laws. (b) Each product of the Company that is a medical device has been cleared or approved by the FDA, or is exempt from premarket notification, and is designed, manufactured, labeled, stored and distributed in compliance with its clearance or approval and with 21 C.F.R. Part 820. (c) Since January 1, 2021, the Company has not received any FDA Form 483, warning letter, untitled letter, or notice of inspectional observations, and has not initiated or been required to initiate any recall, field correction or market withdrawal. (d) All reports, registrations, listings, submissions and filings required to be made by the Company with the FDA have been timely made and were complete and accurate in all material respects when made.

**4.13 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission from Buyer or the Company in connection with the transactions contemplated hereby based on arrangements made by Seller or the Company.

## ARTICLE 5. COVENANTS

**5.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date hereof until the Closing, except as consented to in writing by Buyer (such consent not to be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed), Seller shall cause the Company to (a) conduct the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, (b) use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve its business organization, regulatory clearances and relationships with customers, suppliers and employees, and (c) not amend its organizational documents, issue equity, incur indebtedness for borrowed money outside the ordinary course, or enter into, amend or terminate any material Contract.

**5.2 Access to Information.** From the date hereof until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to afford Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the properties, books, records, Contracts and personnel of the Company.

**5.3 Efforts; Regulatory Filings.** Each party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to take all actions necessary to consummate the transactions contemplated hereby, including making any filings required under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976 within ten (10) Business Days after the date hereof.

**5.4 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, hold in confidence all confidential information concerning the Company and the Business.

**5.5 Non-Competition; Non-Solicitation by Seller.** For a period of five (5) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly engage in the Business anywhere in the world, or solicit for employment any employee of the Company, except through general solicitations not targeted at such employees.

**5.6 Schedule Supplements.**

(a) From time to time before the Closing, Seller may supplement or amend the Disclosure Schedule with respect to any matter first arising after the date hereof, or of which Seller first obtains knowledge after the date hereof, that would cause any representation or warranty in Article 4 to be inaccurate (each, a "**Schedule Supplement**").

(b) Each Schedule Supplement shall be delivered to Buyer in writing in accordance with Section 10.3 and shall describe the relevant matter in reasonable detail.

(c) If any matter disclosed in a Schedule Supplement would cause the condition in Section 6.2(a) not to be satisfied, Buyer may terminate this Agreement by delivering written notice of termination to Seller within fifteen (15) Business Days after Buyer's receipt of such Schedule Supplement. If Buyer does not deliver such notice within such period, Buyer shall be deemed to have irrevocably waived its right to terminate this Agreement with respect to such matter, and such Schedule Supplement shall be deemed to have cured any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty arising from such matter for all purposes of this Agreement, including for purposes of Section 6.2(a) and Article 9.

**5.7 Transition Services.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall provide, or cause to be provided, to the Company the ERP hosting, regulatory affairs support and field service dispatch services that Seller provides to the Company as of the date hereof, which services shall be provided under the Transition Services Agreement in accordance with its terms through June 30, 2027.

**5.8 Exclusivity.** From the date hereof until the earlier of the Closing and the termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not, and shall cause the Company and their respective representatives not to, solicit, initiate or engage in discussions with any Person other than Buyer regarding any sale of the Shares or any material portion of the assets of the Company.

**5.9 Notification.** From the date hereof until the Closing, Seller shall promptly notify Buyer in writing of any FDA inspection of any facility of the Company and of any correspondence received from the FDA.

**5.10 Public Announcements.** Neither party shall issue any press release or public announcement relating to this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except as required by Law, and except for the joint press release to be issued on the date hereof in the form agreed by the parties.

## ARTICLE 6. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**6.1 Conditions to Obligations of Each Party.** The obligations of each party to consummate the Closing are subject to (a) the expiration or termination of any applicable waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976, and (b) the absence of any Law or order of a Governmental Authority restraining or prohibiting the transactions contemplated hereby.

**6.2 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** The obligations of Buyer to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction or Buyer's waiver of the following:

(a) the Fundamental Representations shall be true and correct in all respects, and all other representations and warranties of Seller in Article 4 shall be true and correct in all respects (disregarding materiality qualifiers) except where the failure to be so true and correct would not have a Material Adverse Effect, in each case as of the Closing Date as though made on that date;

(b) Seller shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at the Closing;

(c) Buyer shall have received a certificate of an officer of Seller certifying satisfaction of the conditions in Sections 6.2(a), (b) and (d);

(d) since the date hereof, there shall not have occurred any Material Adverse Effect;

(e) Buyer shall have received the Key Employee Agreement, in the agreed form attached as Exhibit C, duly executed by the Company and the Key Employee, which includes the Key Employee's covenant not to compete with the Business for a period of 24 months after the Closing Date, and such agreement shall be in full force and effect; and

(f) Seller shall have delivered each of the items listed in Section 2.3(b).

**6.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** The obligations of Seller to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction or Seller's waiver of the following:

(a) the representations and warranties of Buyer in Article 3 shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;

(b) Buyer shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before or at the Closing; and

(c) Seller shall have received a certificate of an officer of Buyer certifying satisfaction of the conditions in Sections 6.3(a) and (b), and Buyer shall have delivered each of the items listed in Section 2.3(a).

## ARTICLE 7. TERMINATION

**7.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing:

(a) by mutual written consent of Buyer and Seller;

(b) by Buyer, in accordance with Section 5.6(c);

(c) by Buyer, if Seller has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 6.2 would not be satisfied, and such breach is not cured within twenty (20) days after written notice, provided that this Section 7.1(c) shall not apply to any matter disclosed in a Schedule Supplement;

(d) by Seller, if Buyer has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 6.3 would not be satisfied, and such breach is not cured within twenty (20) days after written notice; or

(e) by either party, if the Closing has not occurred on or before January 31, 2027, provided that the right to terminate under this Section 7.1(e) shall not be available to a party whose breach caused the failure of the Closing to occur by such date.

**7.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated under Section 7.1, it shall become void and of no further effect without liability of any party, except that Sections 5.10, this Section 7.2 and Article 10 shall survive, and no termination shall relieve any party from liability for Fraud or willful breach before termination.

## ARTICLE 8. TAX MATTERS

**8.1 Pre-Closing Taxes.** Seller shall be responsible for and shall indemnify Buyer against all Taxes of the Company attributable to any taxable period, or portion thereof, ending on or before the Closing Date.

**8.2 Transfer Taxes.** All transfer, documentary, sales, use, stamp and registration Taxes incurred in connection with this Agreement shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**8.3 Cooperation.** Buyer and Seller shall cooperate fully, as reasonably requested, in connection with the filing of Tax returns and any audit or proceeding with respect to Taxes of the Company.

## ARTICLE 9. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement shall survive the Closing until the date that is eighteen (18) months after the Closing Date, except that the Fundamental Representations shall survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants shall survive in accordance with their terms. No claim may be brought after the applicable survival period, except that claims asserted in writing with reasonable specificity before expiration shall survive until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer, the Company and their respective Affiliates, officers, directors and employees (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses arising out of or resulting from:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in Article 4 or in any certificate delivered by Seller hereunder;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement; and

(c) any indebtedness of the Company or transaction expenses not paid at or before the Closing.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify Seller and its Affiliates against all Losses arising out of any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in Article 3 or any breach of any covenant of Buyer in this Agreement.

**9.4 Limitations.**

(a) Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) unless and until the aggregate amount of all Losses thereunder exceeds $1,860,000 (the "**Deductible**"), in which event Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of the Deductible. The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed $18,600,000 (10% of the Purchase Price) (the "**Indemnity Cap**").

(b) The Deductible and the Indemnity Cap shall not apply to Losses arising out of or resulting from Fraud or any breach of a Fundamental Representation, and the aggregate liability of Seller for such Losses shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(c) For purposes of calculating Losses under this Article 9 (but not for determining whether a breach has occurred), all materiality and Material Adverse Effect qualifiers shall be disregarded.

(d) Losses shall be calculated net of any insurance proceeds actually received by the Buyer Indemnitees in respect thereof.

**9.5 Claims Procedures.** A party seeking indemnification shall give prompt written notice to the indemnifying party describing the claim in reasonable detail, provided that failure to give such notice shall not relieve the indemnifying party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced thereby. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party, provided that Seller may not assume the defense of any claim brought by a Governmental Authority or involving criminal liability. No party shall settle a third-party claim without the consent of the other, not to be unreasonably withheld.

**9.6 Escrow; Order of Recovery.** Losses payable to the Buyer Indemnitees under Section 9.2 shall be satisfied first from the Escrow Amount in accordance with the Escrow Agreement, and thereafter directly by Seller. On the date that is eighteen (18) months after the Closing Date, the Escrow Agent shall release to Seller the remaining Escrow Amount, less the aggregate amount of all then-pending claims, in accordance with the Escrow Agreement.

**9.7 Exclusive Remedy.** From and after the Closing, the indemnification provisions of this Article 9 and Article 8 shall be the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties with respect to this Agreement, except for claims of Fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, and claims under Section 2.4.

**9.8 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes, unless otherwise required by Law.

**10.5 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the Buyer Indemnitees under Article 9, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the parties and their permitted assigns.

**10.10 Specific Performance.** The parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if any provision of this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its terms, and that each party shall be entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief, without proof of actual damages or posting of a bond, in addition to any other remedy.

**10.11 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together shall constitute one instrument.
