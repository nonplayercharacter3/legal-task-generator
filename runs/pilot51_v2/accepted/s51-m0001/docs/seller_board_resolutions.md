# PROUBEIRT IMAGING GROUP, LLC

## ACTION BY UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF MANAGERS

**July 13, 2026**

The undersigned, being all of the members of the Board of Managers (the "**Board**") of Proubeirt Imaging Group, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (the "**Company**"), take the following actions by unanimous written consent without a meeting. They do so under Section 18-404(d) of the Delaware Limited Liability Company Act and the Company's Amended and Restated Limited Liability Company Agreement (the "**LLC Agreement**"). The undersigned waive all notice requirements in connection with these actions and adopt the following resolutions with the same force and effect as if adopted at a duly called meeting of the Board at which a quorum was present and acting throughout.

## 1. Background

**1.1** The Company is the record and beneficial owner of all 4,250,000 issued and outstanding shares of common stock, par value $0.001 per share (the "**Shares**"), of Kestrova Imaging Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Kestrova**").

**1.2** The Board has reviewed a proposed Stock Purchase Agreement (the "**Purchase Agreement**") between the Company, as seller, and Develstrand Medical Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, the Company would sell all of the Shares to Buyer for an aggregate purchase price of $186,000,000, subject to adjustment as provided in the Purchase Agreement (the "**Transaction**").

**1.3** The Board has also reviewed the following:

(a) the form of Escrow Agreement to be entered into at the Closing among Buyer, the Company and Ashcombe Trust Company, N.A., as escrow agent, under which $14,000,000 of the purchase price will be deposited at the Closing; and

(b) the form of joint press release to be issued by the Company and Buyer on the date of signing of the Purchase Agreement.

**1.4** The Board has received presentations from the Company's management and its legal and financial advisors on the terms of the Transaction. It has considered the Transaction in light of the Company's strategic plan.

**1.5** Capitalized terms used but not defined in these resolutions have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. Approval of the Purchase Agreement and the Transaction

**RESOLVED**, that the Board has determined that the Purchase Agreement and the Transaction are advisable and in the best interests of the Company and its members;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the form, terms and provisions of the Purchase Agreement, substantially in the form presented to the Board, are approved, including the following:

(a) the sale of the Shares to Buyer for a purchase price of $186,000,000, subject to adjustment as provided in the Purchase Agreement;

(b) the representations, warranties, covenants and indemnification obligations of the Company in the Purchase Agreement; and

(c) the governing law of the Purchase Agreement, which is the law of the State of New York;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Chief Executive Officer, the Chief Financial Officer and the Chief Legal Officer of the Company (each, an "**Authorized Officer**") are each authorized to execute and deliver the Purchase Agreement on behalf of the Company. Each may approve changes to the Purchase Agreement that he or she determines to be necessary or appropriate and not materially adverse to the Company. His or her execution of the Purchase Agreement is conclusive evidence of that determination; and

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Company is authorized to perform its obligations under the Purchase Agreement and to consummate the Transaction on its terms and subject to its conditions.

## 3. Approval of the Escrow Agreement

**RESOLVED**, that the form, terms and provisions of the Escrow Agreement, substantially in the form presented to the Board, are approved;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to execute and deliver the Escrow Agreement on behalf of the Company at the Closing, with any changes that the Authorized Officer determines to be necessary or appropriate and not materially adverse to the Company; and

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Company is authorized to perform its obligations under the Escrow Agreement.

## 4. Public Announcement

**RESOLVED**, that the issuance of the joint press release announcing the Transaction, substantially in the form presented to the Board, is approved. Each Authorized Officer is authorized to approve its final form and to cause it to be issued on the date of signing of the Purchase Agreement, in accordance with Section 5.10 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 5. Regulatory Filings

**RESOLVED**, that the Authorized Officers are each authorized to prepare, execute and file, on behalf of the Company, any notification and report form required under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976 in connection with the Transaction. They are further authorized to pay any related filing fees, to respond to any request for additional information from a Governmental Authority, and to make any other filing that the Authorized Officer determines to be required in connection with the Transaction.

## 6. Closing Deliverables

**RESOLVED**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to execute and deliver, on behalf of the Company, the following:

(a) the stock certificates representing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers duly executed in blank;

(b) the officer's certificate described in Section 6.2(c) of the Purchase Agreement;

(c) an IRS Form W-9 of the Company; and

(d) each other certificate, instrument and document that the Company is required to deliver at the Closing under Section 2.3(b) of the Purchase Agreement;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Secretary of the Company is authorized to certify these resolutions to Buyer at the Closing, as contemplated by Section 2.3(b)(v) of the Purchase Agreement, and to certify the incumbency and signatures of the Authorized Officers.

## 7. General Authority

**RESOLVED**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to take all further actions, and to execute and deliver all further agreements, certificates, instruments and documents, in the name and on behalf of the Company, that the Authorized Officer determines to be necessary or appropriate to carry out the purposes of these resolutions. These actions include the payment of fees and expenses. The taking of any such action, or the execution and delivery of any such document, is conclusive evidence of that determination; and

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that all actions previously taken by any manager, officer or authorized representative of the Company in connection with the Transaction and consistent with these resolutions are ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects.

## 8. Effectiveness; Counterparts

**8.1** This written consent is effective as of the date first written above upon execution by all of the members of the Board. It shall be filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Board.

**8.2** This written consent may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and together they constitute one instrument.
