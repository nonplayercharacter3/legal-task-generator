# Develstrand Medical Holdings to Acquire Kestrova Imaging Systems from Proubeirt Imaging Group for $186 Million

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**July 14, 2026**

*Joint press release issued by Develstrand Medical Holdings, Inc. and Proubeirt Imaging Group, LLC under Section 5.10 of the Stock Purchase Agreement*

## 1. Announcement

Develstrand Medical Holdings, Inc. ("**Develstrand**") and Proubeirt Imaging Group, LLC ("**Proubeirt**") today announced that they have entered into a definitive Stock Purchase Agreement. Under the agreement, Develstrand will acquire all of the issued and outstanding shares of Kestrova Imaging Systems, Inc. ("**Kestrova**"), a wholly owned subsidiary of Proubeirt, for a purchase price of $186,000,000 in cash, subject to customary adjustments.

The Stock Purchase Agreement was signed on July 14, 2026.

## 2. About the Transaction

Kestrova designs, manufactures, sells and services MRI receive coils, CT detector modules and ultrasound transducer arrays for medical imaging equipment manufacturers and healthcare providers.

The acquisition will add Kestrova's component engineering and manufacturing capabilities to Develstrand's medical technology portfolio. After closing, Kestrova will operate as a wholly owned subsidiary of Develstrand.

The purchase price will be paid in cash at closing. A portion of the purchase price will be deposited with an escrow agent at closing, as is customary for transactions of this kind.

## 3. Management and Employees

Kestrova's engineering organization will continue to be led by Dr. Linnea Okafor-Brandt, Vice President, Engineering, who has entered into arrangements to remain with Kestrova after closing. Develstrand expects to retain Kestrova's employees and its existing manufacturing operations.

## 4. Continuity for Customers

Develstrand and Proubeirt are committed to an orderly transition for Kestrova's customers, suppliers and employees. Kestrova will continue to fulfill existing customer orders and service commitments in the ordinary course before and after closing.

## 5. Statements from the Parties

"Kestrova has built a strong position in imaging components, with deep expertise across MRI, CT and ultrasound technologies," said a spokesperson for Develstrand. "We look forward to welcoming the Kestrova team and supporting the continued growth of the business."

"This transaction allows Proubeirt to sharpen its strategic focus while placing Kestrova with an owner well positioned to invest in its products and people," said a spokesperson for Proubeirt.

## 6. Timing and Approvals

The transaction is expected to close after the satisfaction of customary closing conditions, including expiration or termination of the applicable waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976. The parties currently expect the transaction to close in the fourth quarter of 2026.

The terms of the transaction are set out in the Stock Purchase Agreement. This press release does not modify or supplement those terms.

## 7. About Develstrand Medical Holdings, Inc.

Develstrand Medical Holdings, Inc. is a Delaware corporation that develops, acquires and operates medical technology businesses serving healthcare providers and device manufacturers.

## 8. About Proubeirt Imaging Group, LLC

Proubeirt Imaging Group, LLC is a Delaware limited liability company that owns and operates a group of medical imaging businesses.

## 9. About Kestrova Imaging Systems, Inc.

Kestrova Imaging Systems, Inc. is a Delaware corporation that designs, manufactures, sells and services MRI receive coils, CT detector modules and ultrasound transducer arrays.
