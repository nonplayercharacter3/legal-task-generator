# DOURETH WATER SOLUTIONS, INC.

## ACTION BY UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS

**Effective as of August 3, 2026**

The undersigned, being all of the members of the Board of Directors (the "**Board**") of Doureth Water Solutions, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), act by unanimous written consent without a meeting under Section 141(f) of the General Corporation Law of the State of Delaware (the "**DGCL**") and the Company's Bylaws. They waive all notice and adopt the following recitals and resolutions with the same force and effect as if adopted at a meeting of the Board duly called and held at which a quorum was present and acting throughout.

## 1. Background

**1.1 The Transaction.** Management has presented to the Board a proposal for the Company to acquire the municipal and industrial water treatment chemicals business (the "**Business**") of Routand Chemical Company, a Delaware corporation ("**Routand**"). The Business includes the coagulant, sodium hypochlorite and corrosion-inhibitor blending plant in Lake Charles, Louisiana (the "**Lake Charles Facility**"). Routand will retain its industrial cleaning chemicals division, which is not part of the Business.

**1.2 Purchase Agreement.** The acquisition is to be made under an Asset Purchase Agreement between Routand, as Seller, and the Company, as Buyer, to be dated as of August 3, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). A substantially final form of the Purchase Agreement, with its exhibits, has been provided to each director. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this consent have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

**1.3 Consideration.** Under Section 2.5 of the Purchase Agreement, the Purchase Price for the Purchased Assets is $86,400,000, plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities, subject to adjustment under Section 2.8 of the Purchase Agreement. The Purchase Price is payable at the Closing as follows:

(a) a Closing Cash Payment of $65,760,000, as adjusted by the Estimated Working Capital Adjustment, by wire transfer to Routand;

(b) the Company's issuance to Routand of the Seller Note in the principal amount of $12,000,000; and

(c) the deposit of the Escrow Amount of $8,640,000 with Pellwick Trust Company, N.A., as Escrow Agent, under Section 2.6 of the Purchase Agreement and the Escrow Agreement.

**1.4 Ancillary Agreements.** At the Closing, the Company is to execute and deliver the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party. These include:

(a) the Seller Note, a subordinated promissory note bearing interest at a fixed rate of six and one-half percent (6.50%) per annum and maturing on October 1, 2030 (the "**Seller Note**");

(b) the Indemnity Escrow Agreement among the Company, Routand and the Escrow Agent (the "**Escrow Agreement**");

(c) the Assignment and Assumption Agreement between the Company and Routand;

(d) acceptance of the Bill of Sale delivered by Routand; and

(e) the Transitional Supply Side Letter between Routand, as supplier, and the Company, as purchaser, for the transitional supply of caustic soda feedstock to the Lake Charles Facility (the "**Supply Side Letter**").

**1.5 Key Employee.** Section 7.2(e) of the Purchase Agreement makes it a condition to the Company's obligation to close that Annika Solberg-Reyes have entered into an employment agreement with the Company, effective as of the Closing, as Plant Director of the Lake Charles Facility (the "**Plant Director Employment Agreement**").

**1.6 Board Review.** The Board has reviewed the terms of the Purchase Agreement and the forms of the Ancillary Agreements. It has also reviewed management's presentation on the Business, the financial, environmental and operational due diligence on the Lake Charles Facility, and the proposed financing of the Closing Cash Payment and the Escrow Amount. The Board has had the opportunity to ask questions of management and the Company's advisors.

## 2. Approval of the Purchase Agreement

**RESOLVED**, that the Board determines that the Purchase Agreement and the transactions contemplated by it (the "**Transaction**") are advisable, fair to and in the best interests of the Company and its stockholders;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the form, terms and provisions of the Purchase Agreement, substantially in the form provided to the Board, are approved and adopted in all respects; and

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Chief Executive Officer, the Chief Financial Officer, the General Counsel and any other officer of the Company designated by any of them (each, an "**Authorized Officer**") is authorized, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to execute and deliver the Purchase Agreement, with such changes as the Authorized Officer executing it approves. Execution by an Authorized Officer is conclusive evidence of that approval.

## 3. Approval of the Ancillary Agreements

**RESOLVED**, that the form, terms and provisions of each Ancillary Agreement to which the Company is a party, substantially in the forms provided to the Board, are approved;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Company is authorized to issue the Seller Note to Routand at the Closing in the principal amount of $12,000,000, and to perform its obligations under the Seller Note, including its payment obligations;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Company is authorized to deposit the Escrow Amount of $8,640,000 with Pellwick Trust Company, N.A., as Escrow Agent, at the Closing, and to enter into and perform the Escrow Agreement;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Company is authorized to enter into and perform the Supply Side Letter, including its forecast, minimum purchase and payment obligations; and

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to execute and deliver each Ancillary Agreement to which the Company is a party, with such changes as the Authorized Officer executing it approves. Execution by an Authorized Officer is conclusive evidence of that approval.

## 4. Payment of the Purchase Price

**RESOLVED**, that the Company is authorized to pay the Closing Cash Payment of $65,760,000, as adjusted by the Estimated Working Capital Adjustment, to Routand at the Closing, and to make any payment required under Section 2.8 of the Purchase Agreement following final determination of Closing Working Capital; and

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to draw on the Company's cash on hand and on available committed financing to fund the Closing Cash Payment and the Escrow Amount, and to execute and deliver any borrowing notices, funds flow instructions and wire authorizations required for that purpose.

## 5. Plant Director Employment Agreement

**RESOLVED**, that the Company is authorized to enter into the Plant Director Employment Agreement with Annika Solberg-Reyes, effective as of the Closing, on terms consistent with those presented to the Board; and

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to execute and deliver the Plant Director Employment Agreement and any related offer letter, with such changes as the Authorized Officer executing it approves.

## 6. Transferred Employees

**RESOLVED**, that the Company is authorized to offer employment, effective as of the Closing, to the employees of the Business listed in Schedule 4.13 to the Purchase Agreement, in accordance with Section 6.6 of the Purchase Agreement, and to enroll those employees who accept in the Company's benefit plans in accordance with their terms.

## 7. Regulatory Filings and Permits

**RESOLVED**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized to prepare, execute and file, in the name and on behalf of the Company:

(a) any notification and report form under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976, and to pay the related filing fee;

(b) any application, notice or other filing with the Louisiana Department of Environmental Quality or any other Governmental Authority for the transfer or reissuance of the permits for the Lake Charles Facility listed in Schedule 4.11 to the Purchase Agreement; and

(c) any qualification to do business in the State of Louisiana, or any other filing, that is necessary or advisable in connection with the Transaction.

## 8. Secretary's Certificate

**RESOLVED**, that the Secretary or any Assistant Secretary of the Company is authorized to certify these resolutions and the incumbency of the Authorized Officers, and to deliver that certificate to Routand at the Closing as required by Section 3.3(e) of the Purchase Agreement.

## 9. General Authority

**RESOLVED**, that each Authorized Officer is authorized, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to take all further actions, and to execute and deliver all further agreements, instruments, certificates and documents, including the closing deliveries required by Section 3.3 of the Purchase Agreement, as the Authorized Officer considers necessary or advisable to consummate the Transaction and carry out the purposes of these resolutions, and to pay all related fees and expenses;

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that all actions previously taken by any director, officer or agent of the Company in connection with the Transaction and consistent with these resolutions are ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects; and

**RESOLVED FURTHER**, that the Closing is expected to occur on October 1, 2026, or on such other date as the parties to the Purchase Agreement agree in writing under Section 3.1 of the Purchase Agreement, and the authority granted by these resolutions continues until the Closing occurs or the Purchase Agreement is terminated under Article VIII of the Purchase Agreement.

## 10. Effectiveness; Counterparts

This consent may be executed in counterparts and delivered by electronic transmission, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument. It is effective as of the date first written above when signed by all directors. It shall be filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Board.
