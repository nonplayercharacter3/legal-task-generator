**Re: Transitional Supply of Caustic Soda Feedstock**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter agreement (this "**letter agreement**") is the Transitional Supply Side Letter referred to in the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of August 3, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). The parties to the Purchase Agreement are Routand Chemical Company, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and Doureth Water Solutions, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"). It is delivered under Sections 3.2(d) and 3.3(d) of the Purchase Agreement on the Closing Date. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this letter agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

Seller and Buyer agree as follows:

## 1. Supply Commitment

**1.1 Product.** During the Term, Seller shall sell and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, membrane-grade liquid caustic soda (sodium hydroxide) at 50% concentration (the "**Product**"). The Product must meet the specifications in Annex A (the "**Specifications**"). Buyer shall use the Product as feedstock for the manufacture of sodium hypochlorite and other products of the Business at the Lake Charles Facility.

**1.2 Term.** This letter agreement begins on the Closing Date and continues for twenty-four (24) months after it (the "**Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 6. The parties may extend the Term by written agreement for up to two (2) successive periods of six (6) months each.

**1.3 Supply Priority.** Seller shall give Buyer's firm orders priority at least equal to the priority Seller gives its own internal requirements, including those of the Retained Business.

## 2. Forecasts and Orders

**2.1 Monthly Forecasts.** Before the fifteenth (15th) day of each calendar month, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a written rolling forecast of its Product requirements for each of the following six (6) months. Each forecast must state volumes by delivery month and delivery point. Buyer shall include with each forecast the demand data supporting it in reasonable detail. That data includes anticipated sodium hypochlorite production volumes and the municipal and industrial customer requirements driving those volumes (collectively, "**Buyer Forecast Information**").

**2.2 Binding Portion.** The first month of each forecast is a firm order. Buyer shall purchase, and Seller shall supply, the volume forecast for that month, subject to a tolerance of plus or minus ten percent (10%). The second and third months of each forecast are binding within a tolerance of plus or minus twenty percent (20%). The remaining months are non-binding estimates for planning purposes only.

**2.3 Minimum and Maximum Volumes.** Buyer shall order at least 1,800 dry short tons of Product in each calendar quarter during the Term. Seller is not required to supply more than 3,000 dry short tons in any calendar month unless it agrees in writing.

**2.4 Shortfall.** If Buyer's purchases in a calendar quarter fall below the minimum in Section 2.3, Buyer shall pay Seller, within thirty (30) days after invoice, an amount equal to fifteen percent (15%) of the then-current Price multiplied by the shortfall volume. That amount is not a penalty but a reasonable estimate of Seller's damages. It is Seller's sole remedy for the shortfall.

## 3. Price and Payment

**3.1 Price.** The price for the Product (the "**Price**") for each calendar month is the average of the U.S. Gulf Coast liquid caustic soda contract price published by the pricing service named in Annex B for the prior month, less a discount of four percent (4%), plus the freight charges set out in Annex B. Annex B also contains the pricing formula and its inputs (the "**Pricing Terms**").

**3.2 Invoicing.** Seller shall invoice Buyer upon each delivery. Buyer shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days after its receipt, by wire transfer to the account Seller designates in writing.

**3.3 Disputed Invoices.** Buyer may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith if it gives Seller written notice of the dispute before the due date, describing it in reasonable detail. The parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days.

**3.4 Taxes.** The Price excludes sales, use and similar Taxes. Buyer shall pay those Taxes, unless Buyer provides a valid exemption certificate.

## 4. Delivery, Quality and Title

**4.1 Delivery.** Seller shall deliver the Product by rail tank car or tank truck to the Lake Charles Facility, using the rail spur and loading racks included in the Purchased Assets, or to another delivery point Buyer reasonably designates within the State of Louisiana. Delivery terms are FCA Seller's production facility (Incoterms 2020), with Seller arranging transport at Buyer's cost in accordance with Annex B.

**4.2 Title and Risk.** Title to and risk of loss of the Product pass to Buyer when the Product is delivered to the carrier at Seller's production facility.

**4.3 Quality.** Seller warrants that the Product delivered under this letter agreement (a) conforms to the Specifications, (b) is free of liens and encumbrances, and (c) has been produced, handled and shipped in compliance with all applicable Laws. Each shipment must be accompanied by a certificate of analysis.

**4.4 Nonconforming Product.** Buyer may reject any shipment that does not conform to the Specifications if it gives written notice to Seller within ten (10) Business Days after delivery. At Buyer's election, Seller shall replace the nonconforming Product promptly at Seller's cost or credit Buyer for its Price. Seller shall bear the costs of returning or disposing of the nonconforming Product.

**4.5 Safety.** Each party shall comply with all applicable Laws relating to the handling, transport and storage of the Product, including Environmental Laws and hazardous materials transportation regulations. Seller shall provide current safety data sheets for the Product.

## 5. Force Majeure

Neither party is liable for any failure or delay in performance, other than a payment obligation, to the extent caused by events beyond its reasonable control. Such events include hurricanes, floods, fires, explosions, acts of Governmental Authorities, rail embargoes and unplanned outages at Seller's chlor-alkali production units (each, a "**Force Majeure Event**"). The affected party shall promptly notify the other party and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. During a Force Majeure Event affecting Seller's supply, Seller shall allocate its available Product among Buyer and its other customers, including the Retained Business, on a pro rata basis by reference to volumes over the preceding three (3) months. Buyer may purchase replacement Product from third parties during the Force Majeure Event without breaching this letter agreement. Those purchases count toward Buyer's minimum volume under Section 2.3.

## 6. Termination

**6.1 Termination for Breach.** Either party may terminate this letter agreement by written notice if the other party materially breaches it and does not cure the breach within thirty (30) days after written notice describing it.

**6.2 Termination for Insolvency.** Either party may terminate this letter agreement immediately by written notice if the other party becomes subject to any proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar Law, or makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors.

**6.3 Extended Force Majeure.** Buyer may terminate this letter agreement on written notice if a Force Majeure Event prevents Seller from supplying at least fifty percent (50%) of Buyer's firm orders for more than sixty (60) consecutive days.

**6.4 Effect of Termination.** Termination does not affect obligations accrued before termination. Sections 3, 4.3, 4.4, 8, 9 and 10 survive termination or expiration.

## 7. Assignment

Seller may assign this letter agreement, or any of its rights or obligations under it, to any Person without the consent of Buyer. Seller shall give Buyer written notice of the assignment within ten (10) Business Days after it becomes effective. Buyer may not assign this letter agreement, or any of its rights or obligations under it, without the prior written consent of Seller. Any purported assignment in violation of this Section 7 is void. This letter agreement binds and benefits the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

## 8. Confidentiality

Each party shall hold in confidence, and use only for the purposes of this letter agreement, all non-public information that the other party discloses to it under this letter agreement. That information includes the Pricing Terms and, in the case of Seller, Buyer Forecast Information. This obligation does not apply to information that (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through breach of this Section 8, (b) is lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality, or (c) must be disclosed by Law or by order of a Governmental Authority, provided that the disclosing party gives prompt notice to the other party where lawful. Each party may disclose confidential information to its directors, officers, employees, Affiliates and professional advisors who need to know it and are bound by duties of confidentiality. This Section 8 survives for three (3) years after expiration or termination of this letter agreement.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

Except for breaches of Section 8, a party's indemnification obligations for third-party claims, and liability for gross negligence or willful misconduct, neither party is liable to the other for consequential, incidental, indirect, special or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of this letter agreement.

## 10. General

**10.1 Relationship to Purchase Agreement.** This letter agreement is an Ancillary Agreement.

**10.2 Notices.** Notices under this letter agreement shall be in writing and given in the manner, and to the addresses, provided in Section 12.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

**10.3 Amendment; Waiver.** This letter agreement may be amended, and any provision of it waived, only by a written instrument signed by both parties.

**10.4 Governing Law.** This letter agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule.

**10.5 Jurisdiction.** Any legal suit, action or proceeding arising out of or based upon this letter agreement shall be instituted exclusively in the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware. Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of that court in any such suit, action or proceeding.

**10.6 Severability.** If any provision of this letter agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**10.7 Counterparts.** This letter agreement may be executed in counterparts. Signatures delivered by electronic transmission are deemed original signatures.
