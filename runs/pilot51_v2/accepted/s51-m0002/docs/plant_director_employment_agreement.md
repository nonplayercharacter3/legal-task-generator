# EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of August 3, 2026, by and between **Doureth Water Solutions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), and **Annika Solberg-Reyes** (the "**Executive**"). It becomes effective on the Effective Date defined in Section 1.1.

## RECITALS

A. The Company and Routand Chemical Company, a Delaware corporation ("**Routand**"), are parties to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of August 3, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, the Company is acquiring Routand's municipal and industrial water treatment chemicals business (the "**Business**"). The Business includes the coagulant, sodium hypochlorite and corrosion-inhibitor blending plant in Lake Charles, Louisiana (the "**Lake Charles Facility**").

B. The Executive is employed by Routand as Plant Director of the Lake Charles Facility. Section 7.2(e) of the Purchase Agreement makes it a condition to the Company's obligation to close that the Executive have entered into an employment agreement with the Company, effective as of the Closing, as Plant Director of the Lake Charles Facility.

C. The Company wishes to employ the Executive, and the Executive wishes to accept that employment, on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises in this Agreement, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. Effectiveness; Term

**1.1 Effective Date.** This Agreement becomes effective on, and only on, the date on which the Closing under the Purchase Agreement occurs. The Closing is expected on October 1, 2026 (the date on which the Closing actually occurs, the "**Effective Date**"). If the Purchase Agreement is terminated before the Closing under Article VIII of the Purchase Agreement, this Agreement is void and of no effect. In that case neither party has any obligation under it.

**1.2 Term.** The Executive's employment under this Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated under Section 5 (the "**Employment Period**"). The Executive's employment is not for any fixed term.

**1.3 Transition from Routand.** The Executive acknowledges that her employment with Routand ends at the Closing. Routand remains responsible for all compensation, benefits and other liabilities relating to her employment with Routand before the Closing, as provided in the Purchase Agreement. The Company shall recognize the Executive's service with Routand for purposes of eligibility and vesting under the Company's benefit plans, and for vacation accrual, to the extent permitted by those plans and applicable Law.

## 2. Position and Duties

**2.1 Position.** During the Employment Period, the Executive shall serve as Plant Director, Lake Charles Facility. She shall report to the Company's Senior Vice President, Manufacturing Operations, or another officer the Chief Executive Officer designates.

**2.2 Duties.** The Executive shall have overall responsibility for the safe, compliant and efficient operation of the Lake Charles Facility. Her responsibilities include:

(a) production planning and the operation of the blending, packaging, storage and loading operations at the Lake Charles Facility;

(b) process safety management, occupational health and safety, and the Lake Charles Facility's compliance with Environmental Laws and the permits for the Lake Charles Facility;

(c) management of plant personnel, including hiring, supervision and discipline in accordance with Company policy;

(d) coordination of feedstock receipt and inventory, including the receipt of caustic soda feedstock under the Company's supply arrangements;

(e) preparation and management of the annual operating and capital budgets for the Lake Charles Facility; and

(f) the integration of the Lake Charles Facility into the Company's operations after the Closing.

**2.3 Exclusive Service.** The Executive shall devote her full business time and attention to the Company. She may serve on civic, charitable or professional association boards, and manage her personal investments, if those activities do not materially interfere with her duties or conflict with Section 7 or Section 8.

**2.4 Location.** The Executive's principal place of employment is the Lake Charles Facility. She shall travel as reasonably required for her duties.

## 3. Compensation

**3.1 Base Salary.** The Company shall pay the Executive an annual base salary of $248,000 (the "**Base Salary**"). The Base Salary is payable in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices. The Compensation Committee of the Company's Board of Directors shall review the Base Salary annually and may increase, but not decrease, it.

**3.2 Annual Bonus.** For each fiscal year during the Employment Period, the Executive is eligible for an annual cash bonus. Her target bonus is forty percent (40%) of Base Salary. The actual bonus depends on achievement of plant safety, environmental compliance, production and cost objectives that the Company sets after consulting the Executive. For fiscal year 2026, the bonus is prorated for the portion of the year after the Effective Date. Any bonus is paid no later than March 15 of the year after the year to which it relates. Except as provided in Section 6, the Executive must be employed on the payment date to receive it.

**3.3 Retention Bonus.** The Company shall pay the Executive a retention bonus of $150,000 (the "**Retention Bonus**") in two equal installments. The first installment is payable within thirty (30) days after the first anniversary of the Effective Date. The second installment is payable within thirty (30) days after the second anniversary of the Effective Date. To receive an installment, the Executive must remain employed through the applicable anniversary. Section 6.2 sets out an exception.

**3.4 Equity Award.** Subject to approval by the Compensation Committee, the Company shall grant the Executive, within ninety (90) days after the Effective Date, restricted stock units under the Company's equity incentive plan with a grant-date value of $200,000. The award vests in equal annual installments over four (4) years, subject to the plan and the applicable award agreement.

**3.5 Withholding.** All payments under this Agreement are subject to applicable tax withholding.

## 4. Benefits and Expenses

**4.1 Benefits.** The Executive may participate in the employee benefit plans that the Company maintains for its senior plant management, subject to their terms. These include medical, dental, vision, life and disability insurance and the Company's 401(k) plan. The Company may amend or terminate any plan in accordance with its terms.

**4.2 Paid Time Off.** The Executive is entitled to twenty-five (25) days of paid time off per year, in addition to Company holidays, under Company policy.

**4.3 Expenses.** The Company shall reimburse the Executive for reasonable business expenses incurred in performing her duties, in accordance with Company policy.

**4.4 Vehicle and Communications.** The Company shall provide the Executive with a vehicle allowance of $900 per month. It shall also provide a mobile device suitable for after-hours operational and emergency response.

## 5. Termination

**5.1 Termination Events.** The Executive's employment may be terminated:

(a) by the Company for Cause;

(b) by the Company without Cause, on thirty (30) days' written notice or pay in lieu of notice;

(c) by the Executive for Good Reason, under Section 5.3;

(d) by the Executive without Good Reason, on sixty (60) days' written notice. The Company may waive all or part of that notice period and pay Base Salary in lieu of it; or

(e) automatically on the Executive's death, or by the Company on her Disability.

**5.2 Cause.** "**Cause**" means:

(a) the Executive's conviction of, or plea of guilty or no contest to, a felony or a crime involving fraud or dishonesty;

(b) willful misconduct or gross negligence in performing her duties, including willful violation of process safety or Environmental Law requirements, that causes or is reasonably likely to cause material harm to the Company;

(c) material breach of this Agreement or of a material written Company policy, if the breach is not cured within twenty (20) days after written notice, to the extent it can be cured; or

(d) willful failure to perform her material duties, other than because of illness or Disability, if not cured within twenty (20) days after written notice.

**5.3 Good Reason.** "**Good Reason**" means any of the following without the Executive's written consent:

(a) a material reduction in Base Salary or target bonus;

(b) a material diminution in her title, authority or duties, including removal as the senior manager responsible for the Lake Charles Facility; or

(c) relocation of her principal place of employment by more than fifty (50) miles from the Lake Charles Facility.

To resign for Good Reason, the Executive must give written notice within sixty (60) days after the condition first occurs. The Company then has thirty (30) days to cure. If the Company does not cure, the Executive must resign within thirty (30) days after the cure period ends.

**5.4 Disability.** "**Disability**" means the Executive's inability to perform the essential functions of her position, with or without reasonable accommodation, for ninety (90) consecutive days or one hundred twenty (120) days in any twelve-month period, as determined in accordance with applicable Law.

## 6. Payments on Termination

**6.1 Accrued Obligations.** On any termination, the Company shall pay the Executive:

(a) earned but unpaid Base Salary;

(b) accrued but unused paid time off, to the extent required by Company policy or Louisiana law;

(c) unreimbursed business expenses; and

(d) vested benefits under the Company's plans in accordance with their terms

(collectively, the "**Accrued Obligations**").

**6.2 Severance.** If the Company terminates the Executive's employment without Cause, or she resigns for Good Reason, she shall receive, in addition to the Accrued Obligations:

(a) continued Base Salary for twelve (12) months after the termination date;

(b) a prorated annual bonus for the year of termination, based on actual performance, paid when bonuses are paid to other participants;

(c) any unpaid installment of the Retention Bonus, paid within sixty (60) days after the termination date; and

(d) if she timely elects COBRA continuation coverage, payment of the employer portion of premiums for up to twelve (12) months.

**6.3 Release.** The Executive's receipt of the payments in Section 6.2 is conditioned on two things. First, she must sign, and not revoke, a general release of claims in a form reasonably acceptable to the Company within sixty (60) days after the termination date. Second, she must continue to comply with Sections 7 and 8.

**6.4 Section 409A.** This Agreement is intended to comply with, or be exempt from, Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code, and shall be interpreted accordingly. Each installment payment is a separate payment. If the release period spans two calendar years, payments conditioned on the release begin in the second year. References to termination of employment mean a "separation from service."

## 7. Confidential Information; Work Product

**7.1 Confidentiality.** The Executive shall not use or disclose, during or after the Employment Period, any confidential or proprietary information of the Company or its Affiliates, except as required to perform her duties or by Law. That information includes formulations, product specifications, process data, customer lists and requirements, pricing, supplier terms, demand forecasts and environmental data. This obligation also applies to the confidential information of the Business that the Company acquires under the Purchase Agreement.

**7.2 Protected Activity.** Nothing in this Agreement prohibits the Executive from reporting possible violations of Law to any Governmental Authority, including the Louisiana Department of Environmental Quality or the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency. She does not need to notify the Company of any such report. Under the Defend Trade Secrets Act, the Executive has immunity for certain confidential disclosures of trade secrets to government officials or attorneys, or in sealed court filings.

**7.3 Work Product.** All inventions, process improvements, formulations and other work product that the Executive creates in the course of her employment are the sole property of the Company. She shall execute any documents reasonably required to confirm the Company's ownership.

**7.4 Return of Property.** On termination, the Executive shall return all Company property and documents in her possession.

## 8. Restrictive Covenants

**8.1 Non-Competition.** During the Employment Period and for twenty-four (24) months after it ends, the Executive shall not, in any of the Parishes of Calcasieu, Cameron, Jefferson Davis, Beauregard, Allen, Acadia, Vermilion, Lafayette, East Baton Rouge, Ascension, Iberville and St. Charles, Louisiana (the "**Restricted Area**"), carry on or engage in a business similar to the Company's business of manufacturing, blending or selling coagulants, sodium hypochlorite or corrosion inhibitors for municipal or industrial water treatment. She is also prohibited from being employed by any Person that carries on such a business in the Restricted Area in a role similar to her role with the Company. This Section 8.1 is intended to comply with La. R.S. 23:921(C).

**8.2 Non-Solicitation.** During the Employment Period and for twenty-four (24) months after it ends, the Executive shall not, within the Restricted Area:

(a) solicit any customer of the Company to purchase water treatment chemicals from a competing business; or

(b) solicit or hire any employee of the Company who works at the Lake Charles Facility to leave the Company's employment.

**8.3 Remedies.** The Executive acknowledges that a breach of Section 7 or this Section 8 would cause the Company irreparable harm. In addition to any other remedy, the Company is entitled to injunctive relief to enforce those Sections, without posting bond to the extent permitted by Law.

## 9. Representations of the Executive

The Executive represents to the Company that she is not bound by any agreement that would prevent her from performing her duties under this Agreement. She has disclosed to the Company any restrictive covenants owed to Routand. She acknowledges that Routand's employment of her, and any related agreement with Routand, end at the Closing. She will not bring to the Company, or use in her work, any confidential information of Routand that relates to the Retained Business.

## 10. Miscellaneous

**10.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement, including "Closing," "Environmental Laws," "Governmental Authority," "Law," "Person," "Affiliate" and "Retained Business," have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

**10.2 Notices.** Notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission. Notices to the Company go to its principal executive office, attention: General Counsel. Notices to the Executive go to the most recent address in the Company's records.

**10.3 Assignment.** This Agreement is personal to the Executive, and she may not assign it. The Company may assign it to any Affiliate, or to a successor to all or substantially all of the business or assets of the Company or of the Lake Charles Facility, if the assignee assumes the Company's obligations in writing.

**10.4 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended or waived only by a written instrument signed by the Executive and an authorized officer of the Company.

**10.5 Severability; Reformation.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect. If a court finds any restriction in Section 8 overbroad, it may reform the restriction to the maximum extent enforceable under Louisiana law.

**10.6 Governing Law; Venue.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Louisiana, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule. Any action arising out of this Agreement shall be brought in the Fourteenth Judicial District Court for the Parish of Calcasieu or the United States District Court for the Western District of Louisiana.

**10.7 Prior Offers.** This Agreement replaces any offer letter or term sheet regarding the Executive's employment with the Company that was exchanged before the date of this Agreement.

**10.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts. Signatures delivered by electronic transmission are deemed original signatures.
