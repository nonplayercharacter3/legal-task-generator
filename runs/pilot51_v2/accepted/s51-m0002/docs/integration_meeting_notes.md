Transfer applications were filed with the Louisiana Department of Environmental Quality (LDEQ) on August 21, 2026. LDEQ has acknowledged receipt and has not raised any deficiency to date.

The Director, Procurement and Supply Chain will confirm that the contract is on the Assigned Contracts list or arrange a replacement.

## 4. Caustic Soda Feedstock Supply

4.1 The Director, Procurement and Supply Chain summarized the operating mechanics of the Transitional Supply Side Letter to be signed at the Closing:
- the supply term is twenty-four (24) months after the Closing Date;
- rolling six-month forecasts are due before the 15th day of each month, beginning in October 2026;
- the first forecast month is a firm order, and the second and third months are binding within tolerance; and
- Doureth must order a minimum of 1,800 dry short tons per calendar quarter.

4.2 Annika Solberg-Reyes confirmed that historical consumption at the Lake Charles Facility runs comfortably above the quarterly minimum. She will provide twelve months of feedstock receipt data, through the clean-team protocol, so that procurement can build the first forecast.

4.3 Deliveries will continue to arrive by rail tank car on the plant's rail spur, with tank-truck backup. Routand's controller confirmed that no change to the railcar fleet or unloading procedure is expected at the Closing.

4.4 The working group agreed that Doureth procurement will own forecast preparation after the Closing, with input from the Plant Director on production scheduling. Distribution of forecast workpapers will be limited to procurement, plant management and finance.
