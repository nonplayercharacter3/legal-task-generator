# SUBORDINATED PROMISSORY NOTE

**$12,000,000**
**Dated: October 1, 2026**

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **Doureth Water Solutions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **Routand Chemical Company**, a Delaware corporation (together with its successors and permitted assigns, the "**Holder**"), the principal amount of **TWELVE MILLION DOLLARS ($12,000,000)**, together with interest on the unpaid principal balance, in the manner and on the dates provided in this Subordinated Promissory Note (this "**Note**").

This Note is the Seller Note referred to in, and is issued under Section 2.5(b) of, the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of August 3, 2026, between the Holder, as Seller, and the Maker, as Buyer (the "**Purchase Agreement**").

## 1. Definitions

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Business Day**" has the meaning given in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Closing Date**" means October 1, 2026.

"**Event of Default**" has the meaning given in Section 7.1.

"**Maturity Date**" means October 1, 2030.

"**Payment Date**" means each of the first Business Day of January, April, July and October, beginning January 4, 2027.

"**Senior Credit Agreement**" means the credit agreement between the Maker, as borrower, and the Senior Lender, as amended, restated, refinanced or replaced from time to time.

"**Senior Indebtedness**" means all obligations of the Maker under the Senior Credit Agreement and the related loan documents, including principal, interest (including post-petition interest, whether or not allowed), fees, expenses and indemnities.

"**Senior Lender**" means the administrative agent and lenders under the Senior Credit Agreement, and their successors and assigns.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Section 1.2 of the Purchase Agreement applies to this Note, with references to "this Agreement" read as references to this Note.

## 2. Interest

**2.1 Rate.** Interest accrues on the unpaid principal balance of this Note from the Closing Date until paid in full, at a fixed rate of six and one-half percent (6.50%) per annum.

**2.2 Computation.** Interest is computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

**2.3 Default Rate.** While an Event of Default is continuing, interest accrues on the unpaid principal balance at a rate equal to two percent (2.00%) per annum above the rate otherwise applicable.

**2.4 Maximum Lawful Rate.** No interest in excess of the maximum rate permitted by applicable Law shall be payable. Any amount received in excess of that rate shall be applied to reduce principal, or returned to the Maker if principal has been paid in full.

## 3. Payments

**3.1 Interest Payments.** Accrued and unpaid interest is payable quarterly in arrears on each Payment Date.

**3.2 Principal Payments.** The principal of this Note is payable as follows:

(a) $3,000,000 on October 1, 2028;

(b) $3,000,000 on October 1, 2029; and

(c) the remaining unpaid principal balance, together with all accrued and unpaid interest, on the Maturity Date.

**3.3 Manner of Payment.** All payments shall be made in lawful money of the United States by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account the Holder designates by written notice to the Maker at least five (5) Business Days before the applicable payment date. If a payment falls due on a day that is not a Business Day, it is due on the next Business Day, and interest continues to accrue in the meantime.

**3.4 Application.** Payments are applied first to costs of collection payable under Section 7.3, then to accrued and unpaid interest, and then to principal.

## 4. Prepayment

The Maker may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time, without premium or penalty, upon at least five (5) Business Days' written notice to the Holder. Each prepayment shall be accompanied by accrued and unpaid interest on the principal amount prepaid. Partial prepayments are applied to the principal installments in inverse order of maturity.

## 5. Right of Set-Off

**5.1 Set-Off.** The Maker may set off against any amount of principal or interest payable under this Note any amount that has been finally determined to be owed to a Buyer Party under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement and that is recoverable other than from the Escrow Fund under Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement.

**5.2 Final Determination.** An amount is "finally determined" for purposes of Section 5.1 when (a) the Maker and the Holder have agreed it in writing, or (b) it has been determined by a final, non-appealable order of a court of competent jurisdiction.

**5.3 Notice of Set-Off.** The Maker shall give the Holder written notice of any set-off at least ten (10) Business Days before the payment date against which it is applied. The notice shall state the amount set off and identify the claim and the final determination on which it is based.

**5.4 Effect.** Any amount set off under this Section 5 is treated as a payment under this Note on the date of the set-off and is applied under Section 3.4. A set-off made in accordance with this Section 5 is not a failure to pay and does not constitute an Event of Default.

## 6. Subordination

**6.1 Subordination.** The indebtedness evidenced by this Note is subordinate and junior in right of payment to the prior payment in full in cash of all Senior Indebtedness, on the terms of this Section 6 and of any subordination agreement among the Holder, the Maker and the Senior Lender.

**6.2 Permitted Payments.** Unless a payment default or an event of default under the Senior Credit Agreement has occurred and is continuing and the Senior Lender has given written notice of it to the Holder and the Maker (a "**Blockage Notice**"), the Maker may make, and the Holder may receive and retain, scheduled payments of principal and interest under this Note.

**6.3 Payment Blockage.** After a Blockage Notice is delivered, the Maker shall make no payment under this Note until the earliest of (a) cure or waiver of the default, (b) withdrawal of the Blockage Notice by the Senior Lender, and (c) 179 days after delivery of the Blockage Notice. No more than one Blockage Notice may be delivered in any 360-day period. Payments not made during a blockage period accrue and become due on its expiration.

**6.4 Insolvency.** On any distribution of the Maker's assets in any liquidation, dissolution, bankruptcy, reorganization or similar proceeding, the Senior Indebtedness shall first be paid in full in cash before the Holder is entitled to receive any payment on this Note.

**6.5 Turnover.** Any payment received by the Holder in violation of this Section 6 shall be held in trust for the Senior Lender and promptly paid over to it.

**6.6 Set-Off Not Restricted.** Nothing in this Section 6 limits the Maker's rights under Section 5.

## 7. Events of Default; Remedies

**7.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) the Maker fails to pay any principal or interest under this Note within ten (10) Business Days after it is due, other than by reason of a set-off permitted by Section 5 or a payment blocked under Section 6;

(b) the Maker commences a voluntary case under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar Law, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or consents to the appointment of a receiver, trustee or custodian for all or substantially all of its assets;

(c) an involuntary case is commenced against the Maker under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar Law and is not dismissed within sixty (60) days; or

(d) the Senior Indebtedness is accelerated following an event of default under the Senior Credit Agreement.

**7.2 Remedies.** On the occurrence of an Event of Default under Section 7.1(b) or (c), the entire unpaid principal of this Note and all accrued interest become immediately due and payable without notice or any other action. While any other Event of Default is continuing, the Holder may, subject to Section 6, declare by written notice to the Maker the entire unpaid principal and all accrued interest immediately due and payable.

**7.3 Costs of Collection.** The Maker shall pay all reasonable costs of collection, including reasonable attorneys' fees, incurred by the Holder in enforcing this Note after an Event of Default.

## 8. Transfer

**8.1 Assignment by Holder.** The Holder may not sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer this Note or any interest in it without the prior written consent of the Maker, except to an Affiliate of the Holder that agrees in writing to be bound by this Note and by any subordination agreement with the Senior Lender. Any transferee takes this Note subject to the Maker's rights under Section 5.

**8.2 Assignment by Maker.** The Maker may not assign its obligations under this Note without the prior written consent of the Holder, except to an assignee of the Maker's rights under the Purchase Agreement permitted by Section 12.6 of the Purchase Agreement, provided that the Maker remains liable for its obligations under this Note.

**8.3 Lost Note.** On receipt of evidence reasonably satisfactory to the Maker of the loss, theft, destruction or mutilation of this Note, and of an indemnity reasonably satisfactory to the Maker, the Maker shall issue a replacement note of like tenor.

## 9. Governing Law; Jurisdiction

**9.1 Governing Law.** This Note and all claims arising out of or relating to it shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

**9.2 Jurisdiction.** Any legal suit, action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Note, including the payment, enforcement or set-off of any amount under it, shall be instituted exclusively in the state courts of the State of New York sitting in the Borough of Manhattan or the United States District Court for the Southern District of New York. The Maker and the Holder each irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of those courts in any such suit, action or proceeding, and waive any objection to venue in, and any claim of inconvenient forum regarding, those courts.

**9.3 Service of Process.** Each of the Maker and the Holder agrees that service of process in any such suit, action or proceeding may be made by delivery to its address for notices under Section 10.1.

**9.4 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH OF THE MAKER AND THE HOLDER IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION OR PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS NOTE.

## 10. Miscellaneous

**10.1 Notices.** All notices under this Note shall be in writing and given in the manner, and to the addresses, provided in Section 12.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

**10.2 Waivers by Maker.** Except as expressly provided in this Note, the Maker waives presentment, demand for payment, notice of dishonor, protest and notice of protest.

**10.3 Amendment; Waiver.** This Note may be amended, and any provision of it waived, only by a written instrument signed by the Maker and the Holder. No failure or delay by the Holder in exercising any right under this Note operates as a waiver of that right.

**10.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Note is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**10.5 Successors.** This Note binds the Maker and its successors and permitted assigns and benefits the Holder and its successors and permitted assigns.

**10.6 Headings.** Headings in this Note are for convenience only and do not affect its interpretation.
