# BILL OF SALE

This BILL OF SALE (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made and delivered as of October 1, 2026 (the "**Closing Date**"), by **Routand Chemical Company**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), to and in favor of **Doureth Water Solutions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller and Buyer are parties to the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of August 3, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Seller has agreed to sell, convey, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer the Purchased Assets, and Buyer has agreed to purchase and acquire them, on the terms and subject to the conditions of the Purchase Agreement.

B. Section 3.2(a) of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to deliver this Bill of Sale to Buyer at the Closing.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, including the Purchase Price paid under Section 2.5 of the Purchase Agreement, the receipt and sufficiency of which Seller acknowledges, Seller agrees as follows:

## 1. Definitions

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. Conveyance of Purchased Assets

**2.1 Transfer.** Effective as of the Closing, Seller sells, conveys, assigns, transfers and delivers to Buyer and its successors and assigns, and Buyer accepts, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the Purchased Assets, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than Permitted Encumbrances. The Purchased Assets include:

(a) all tangible personal property located at or used primarily in the operation of the Lake Charles Facility, including tanks, blending and packaging lines, loading racks, pumps, piping, laboratory equipment, vehicles, tools and other machinery and equipment, as described in Section 2.1(a) of the Purchase Agreement;

(b) all inventory of finished products, raw materials, work in process, packaging and supplies of the Business, as described in Section 2.1(b) of the Purchase Agreement;

(c) all of Seller's rights under the Assigned Contracts listed in Schedule 2.1(c) to the Purchase Agreement;

(d) all permits and environmental authorizations held for the Business, to the extent transferable, as described in Section 2.1(d) of the Purchase Agreement;

(e) all formulations, product specifications, trade secrets, trademarks and other intellectual property used primarily in the Business, as described in Section 2.1(e) of the Purchase Agreement;

(f) all accounts receivable of the Business, as described in Section 2.1(f) of the Purchase Agreement; and

(g) all books, records, customer lists and supplier lists of the Business, as described in Section 2.1(g) of the Purchase Agreement.

**2.2 Real Property.** The owned real property at the Lake Charles Facility described in Schedule 2.1(a) to the Purchase Agreement is conveyed to Buyer by the special warranty deeds delivered under Section 3.2(c) of the Purchase Agreement, and not by this Bill of Sale.

**2.3 Excluded Assets.** Nothing in this Bill of Sale sells, conveys, assigns, transfers or delivers to Buyer, or is deemed to do so, any right, title or interest in, to or under any Excluded Asset, including any asset of the Retained Business. Seller retains all of its right, title and interest in the Excluded Assets.

## 3. No Assumption of Liabilities

This Bill of Sale conveys assets only. Buyer's assumption of the Assumed Liabilities is made by the Assignment and Assumption Agreement delivered under Section 3.2(b) of the Purchase Agreement. Buyer does not assume, and nothing in this Bill of Sale makes Buyer liable for, any Excluded Liability.

## 4. Terms of the Purchase Agreement

This Bill of Sale is delivered under, and is subject to the terms and conditions of, the Purchase Agreement. The representations, warranties, covenants, agreements and indemnities in the Purchase Agreement are not merged into this Bill of Sale and continue in accordance with the terms of the Purchase Agreement. Nothing in this Bill of Sale expands or limits the rights or obligations of Seller or Buyer under the Purchase Agreement.

## 5. Further Assurances

Consistent with Section 6.8 of the Purchase Agreement, Seller shall, at Buyer's reasonable request and without further consideration, execute and deliver such further deeds of conveyance, assignments, consents and other instruments, and take such further actions, as Buyer may reasonably require to vest in Buyer good and valid title to the Purchased Assets and to put Buyer in actual possession and operating control of them.

## 6. Power of Attorney

Seller irrevocably appoints Buyer and its successors and assigns as Seller's attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, in Seller's name but on Buyer's behalf and for Buyer's benefit, solely to (a) demand and receive any of the Purchased Assets, (b) endorse checks and other instruments received in payment of accounts receivable included in the Purchased Assets, and (c) institute and prosecute proceedings that Buyer reasonably considers necessary to collect, assert or enforce any claim, right or title of any kind in or to the Purchased Assets. This power is coupled with an interest and is irrevocable.

## 7. Miscellaneous

**7.1 Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale binds Seller and its successors and assigns and benefits Buyer and its successors and permitted assigns.

**7.2 Governing Law.** This Bill of Sale shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule.

**7.3 Jurisdiction.** Any legal suit, action or proceeding arising out of or based upon this Bill of Sale shall be instituted exclusively in the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware, in accordance with Section 12.9 of the Purchase Agreement.

**7.4 Headings.** Headings in this Bill of Sale are for convenience only and do not affect its interpretation.

**7.5 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Bill of Sale may be executed in counterparts. Signatures delivered by electronic transmission are deemed original signatures.
