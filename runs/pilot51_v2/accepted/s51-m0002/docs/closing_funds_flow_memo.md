## CLOSING FUNDS FLOW MEMORANDUM

**To:** Chief Executive Officer; General Counsel; Treasurer
**From:** Office of the Chief Financial Officer
**Date:** September 29, 2026
**Re:** Funds flow for the Closing under the Asset Purchase Agreement with Routand Chemical Company
**Closing Date:** October 1, 2026

---

## 1. Purpose

1.1 This memorandum sets out the sources and uses of funds, and the wire instructions, for the Closing of Doureth Water Solutions, Inc.'s ("**Doureth**" or "**Buyer**") acquisition of the Business from Routand Chemical Company ("**Routand**" or "**Seller**"). The acquisition is made under the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of August 3, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this memorandum have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

1.2 This memorandum is an internal operating document. It records the payments to be made at the Closing under Sections 2.5, 2.6 and 3.3 of the Purchase Agreement, as authorized by the Board's unanimous written consent effective as of August 3, 2026. It does not amend or interpret the Purchase Agreement or any Ancillary Agreement.

## 2. Purchase Price Components

2.1 Under Section 2.5 of the Purchase Agreement, the Purchase Price is $86,400,000, plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities, subject to adjustment under Section 2.8. It is paid at the Closing as follows:

| Component | Purchase Agreement reference | Amount | Form of payment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Closing Cash Payment (before Estimated Working Capital Adjustment) | Section 2.5(a) | $65,760,000 | Wire to Seller |

| Escrow Amount | Sections 2.5(c) and 2.6 | $8,640,000 | Wire to Escrow Agent |
| **Purchase Price** | **Section 2.5** | **$86,400,000** | |

2.2 **Estimated Working Capital Adjustment.** Routand delivered its good-faith estimate of Closing Working Capital on September 25, 2026, more than three (3) Business Days before the Closing, as Section 2.8 requires. Doureth finance has reviewed the estimate and has no objection for Closing purposes. The estimate produces an upward adjustment of $412,500 to the Closing Cash Payment. The final calculation of Closing Working Capital will be delivered by Buyer within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date under Section 2.8.

3.3 **Non-cash consideration.** The Seller Note in the principal amount of $12,000,000 is delivered to Routand at the Closing under Section 3.3(b) of the Purchase Agreement. It involves no cash movement on the Closing Date. Total consideration for the Purchased Assets at Closing is therefore $66,172,500 in cash to Routand, $8,640,000 deposited in escrow, and the $12,000,000 Seller Note, for a total of $86,812,500. That total equals the $86,400,000 Purchase Price plus the $412,500 Estimated Working Capital Adjustment.

3.4 **Seller-side costs.** Routand bears its own share of the Escrow Agent fee and transfer charges, and its own transaction expenses. It will pay those amounts directly, and none of them is deducted from the Closing Cash Payment.

## 5. Wire Verification and Release Sequence

5.1 **Call-back verification.** The Treasurer's office has verified each set of beneficiary instructions by telephone call-back to a known contact at the beneficiary, using a number not taken from the instruction letter itself. Instructions for Wires 2 and 3 were confirmed with Pellwick Trust Company, N.A. Corporate Trust and Escrow Services, in line with the authorized representative and call-back procedures in Section 9 of the Escrow Agreement. Any change to wire instructions received after September 29, 2026 will not be acted on without a new call-back and approval by the CFO.

5.2 **Release sequence on the Closing Date.**

(a) Confirm receipt of the term loan proceeds in Doureth's operating account.

(b) Confirm with Corporate Counsel that all signature pages are held in escrow and that the conditions in Article VII of the Purchase Agreement have been satisfied or waived. This includes receipt of the Seller officer's certificate under Section 7.2(c) and the Plant Director Employment Agreement under Section 7.2(e).

(c) Release Wire 2 (Escrow Amount). Obtain Escrow Agent's written acknowledgment of receipt under Section 2.1 of the Escrow Agreement.

(d) Release Wire 1 (Adjusted Closing Cash Payment).

(e) Release Wires 3, 4 and 5.

(f) Corporate Counsel releases signature pages, including the executed Seller Note, Escrow Agreement, Transitional Supply Side Letter, Bill of Sale, Assignment and Assumption Agreement and deeds, and confirms that the Closing has occurred.

5.3 **Fed reference numbers.** The Treasurer will circulate Fed reference numbers for each wire to the distribution list, Routand's chief financial officer and the Escrow Agent as soon as available.

## 6. Post-Closing Finance Items

6.1 **Working capital true-up.** Buyer's calculation of Closing Working Capital is due within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date under Section 2.8 of the Purchase Agreement. Any resulting payment will be made in cash within five (5) Business Days after final determination.

6.2 **Purchase price allocation.** Buyer will prepare the Section 1060 allocation schedule and deliver it to Seller within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date under Section 2.7 of the Purchase Agreement.

6.3 **Seller Note servicing.** The Seller Note bears interest at a fixed rate of six and one-half percent (6.50%) per annum, payable quarterly in arrears. The first Payment Date is January 4, 2027. Treasury will calendar the quarterly interest payments, the principal installments of $3,000,000 each on October 1, 2028 and October 1, 2029, and the Maturity Date of October 1, 2030.

6.4 **Tax reporting.** Doureth will deliver its IRS Form W-9 to the Escrow Agent on or before the Closing Date under Section 8.1 of the Escrow Agreement. Earnings on the Escrow Funds will be reported as income of Doureth until disbursement.

6.5 **Property tax proration.** Real and personal property taxes on the Purchased Assets for 2026 will be prorated as of the Closing Date under Section 10.2 of the Purchase Agreement, through the post-Closing settlement statement.
