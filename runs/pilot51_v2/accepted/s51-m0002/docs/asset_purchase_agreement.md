# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of August 3, 2026, by and between **Routand Chemical Company**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Doureth Water Solutions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller is engaged in the business of manufacturing, blending, packaging, marketing, selling and distributing coagulants, sodium hypochlorite, corrosion inhibitors and related water treatment chemicals for municipal and industrial customers (the "**Business**"). The Business includes the operations conducted at Seller's blending plant located in Lake Charles, Louisiana (the "**Lake Charles Facility**").

B. Seller also operates an industrial cleaning chemicals division (the "**Retained Business**"). The Retained Business is not part of the Business and is not being sold under this Agreement.

C. Seller wishes to sell and assign to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase and assume from Seller, substantially all of the assets and certain specified liabilities of the Business, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New Orleans, Louisiana or Wilmington, Delaware are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Closing Date**" has the meaning set forth in Section 3.1.

"**Escrow Agent**" means Pellwick Trust Company, N.A.

"**Escrow Agreement**" means the Indemnity Escrow Agreement to be entered into at Closing among Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent, substantially in the form of Exhibit C.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchased Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, convey, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase and acquire from Seller, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than Permitted Encumbrances, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under all assets, properties and rights used or held for use primarily in the Business (the "**Purchased Assets**"), including:

(a) the Lake Charles Facility, including the owned real property described in Schedule 2.1(a) and all buildings, tanks, blending and packaging lines, rail spurs, loading racks and other improvements and fixtures on it;

(b) all inventory of finished products, raw materials, work in process, packaging and supplies of the Business;

(c) the contracts listed in Schedule 2.1(c) (the "**Assigned Contracts**");

(d) all permits and environmental authorizations held for the Business, to the extent transferable;

(e) all formulations, product specifications, trade secrets, trademarks and other intellectual property used primarily in the Business;

(f) all accounts receivable of the Business; and

(g) all books, records, customer lists and supplier lists of the Business.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** The Purchased Assets do not include: (a) cash and cash equivalents; (b) all assets of the Retained Business; (c) Seller's corporate records, minute books and Tax Returns; (d) insurance policies of Seller and rights under them; (e) contracts not listed in Schedule 2.1(c); and (f) Seller's rights under this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements (collectively, the "**Excluded Assets**").

**2.3 Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume and agree to pay, perform and discharge only the following Liabilities of Seller (the "**Assumed Liabilities**"):

(a) all trade accounts payable of the Business incurred in the ordinary course and reflected in Closing Working Capital;

(b) all Liabilities under the Assigned Contracts to the extent arising and required to be performed after the Closing, excluding any Liability arising from a breach by Seller before the Closing; and

(c) all Liabilities arising under Environmental Laws relating to the Purchased Assets or the Lake Charles Facility, whether arising prior to, at or after the Closing.

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer shall not assume, and Seller shall retain and be responsible for, all Liabilities of Seller other than the Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"), including: (a) all Liabilities relating to the Retained Business or the Excluded Assets; (b) all Taxes of Seller and all Taxes relating to the Business for pre-Closing tax periods; (c) all Liabilities for indebtedness for borrowed money of Seller; (d) all Liabilities for product sold by the Business before the Closing; (e) all Liabilities to employees and former employees of Seller relating to periods before the Closing, including under Seller's benefit plans; and (f) all Liabilities arising out of litigation pending or threatened against Seller as of the Closing.

**2.5 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $86,400,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities. The Purchase Price is subject to adjustment under Section 2.8 and shall be paid at Closing as follows:

(a) $65,760,000, as adjusted by the Estimated Working Capital Adjustment, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated by Seller (the "**Closing Cash Payment**");

(b) by Buyer's issuance and delivery to Seller of the Seller Note in the principal amount of $12,000,000; and

(c) by deposit of the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent under Section 2.6.

**2.6 Escrow.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit $8,640,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent, to be held in the Escrow Fund and disbursed in accordance with this Agreement and the Escrow Agreement.

**2.7 Allocation.** The Purchase Price and Assumed Liabilities shall be allocated among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code and a schedule prepared by Buyer within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date and delivered to Seller for review. The parties shall file all Tax Returns consistently with that allocation.

**2.8 Working Capital Adjustment.** Not less than three (3) Business Days before the Closing, Seller shall deliver a good-faith estimate of Closing Working Capital. Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver its calculation of Closing Working Capital. Any dispute not resolved within thirty (30) days shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm mutually selected by the parties, whose determination shall be final and binding. Any resulting payment shall be made in cash within five (5) Business Days after final determination.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents on October 1, 2026, or on such other date as the parties may agree in writing, subject to satisfaction or waiver of the conditions in Article VII (the date on which the Closing occurs, the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliveries.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer: (a) the Bill of Sale, duly executed; (b) the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, duly executed; (c) special warranty deeds for the owned real property at the Lake Charles Facility; (d) the Escrow Agreement and the Transitional Supply Side Letter, each duly executed by Seller; (e) a certificate of the Secretary of Seller certifying board resolutions authorizing this Agreement; and (f) the officer's certificate required by Section 7.2(c).

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliveries.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver: (a) the Closing Cash Payment to Seller; (b) the Seller Note, duly executed, to Seller; (c) the Escrow Amount to the Escrow Agent; (d) the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, the Escrow Agreement and the Transitional Supply Side Letter, each duly executed by Buyer; and (e) a certificate of the Secretary of Buyer certifying resolutions of Buyer's board of directors authorizing this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements.

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Seller represents and warrants to Buyer that, except as set forth in the Disclosure Schedule, the statements in this Article IV are true and correct as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date.

**4.1 Organization.** Seller is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware, and is duly qualified to do business in the State of Louisiana.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full corporate power and authority to enter into this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party and to perform its obligations under them. This Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 Title to Purchased Assets.** Seller has good and valid title to, or a valid leasehold interest in, all of the Purchased Assets, free and clear of Encumbrances other than Permitted Encumbrances.

**4.4 No Conflicts; Consents.** The execution and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not violate Seller's organizational documents or any Law applicable to Seller, or require any consent under any Material Contract, except as listed in Schedule 4.4.

**4.5 Financial Statements.** The audited statements of operations and net assets of the Business for fiscal years 2024 and 2025, and the unaudited statements for the six months ended June 30, 2026, have been prepared in accordance with GAAP applied on a consistent basis and fairly present in all material respects the financial position and results of operations of the Business.

**4.6 Absence of Changes.** Since December 31, 2025, the Business has been conducted in the ordinary course and there has been no Material Adverse Effect.

**4.7 Material Contracts.** Schedule 4.7 lists each contract of the Business with annual payments exceeding $250,000, each municipal supply contract, and each contract containing exclusivity or most-favored-customer terms (the "**Material Contracts**"). Each Material Contract is valid and binding, and neither Seller nor, to Seller's Knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach.

**4.8 Condition of Assets.** The tangible Purchased Assets are in good operating condition and repair, ordinary wear and tear excepted, and are adequate for the uses to which they are being put.

**4.9 Real Property.** Schedule 4.9 describes all real property owned or leased in the Business. No condemnation proceeding is pending or, to Seller's Knowledge, threatened against the Lake Charles Facility.

**4.10 Legal Proceedings; Compliance with Laws.** There is no action pending or, to Seller's Knowledge, threatened against Seller relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets. Seller has complied in all material respects with all Laws applicable to the Business.

**4.11 Permits.** Schedule 4.11 lists all permits required for the conduct of the Business. All such permits are valid and in full force and effect.

**4.12 Environmental Matters.** (a) The Business and the Lake Charles Facility are, and for the past five (5) years have been, in compliance in all material respects with all Environmental Laws. (b) Seller holds and is in compliance with all permits required under Environmental Laws for the operation of the Business, including air and wastewater discharge permits for the Lake Charles Facility. (c) There has been no Release of Hazardous Materials at, on, under or from the Lake Charles Facility in violation of Environmental Laws or requiring investigation or remediation. (d) Seller has not received any notice, demand or request for information alleging a violation of or Liability under Environmental Laws relating to the Business. (e) Seller has made available to Buyer all environmental site assessments, audits and reports relating to the Lake Charles Facility in its possession.

**4.13 Employees.** Schedule 4.13 lists each employee of the Business, with title, base compensation and hire date. There is no pending or, to Seller's Knowledge, threatened labor dispute affecting the Business.

**4.14 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Seller.

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**5.1 Organization.** Buyer is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware.

**5.2 Authority.** Buyer has full corporate power and authority to enter into this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party, including the Seller Note, and to perform its obligations under them. This Agreement constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Buyer.

**5.3 No Conflicts.** The execution and performance of this Agreement by Buyer do not violate Buyer's organizational documents or any Law applicable to Buyer.

**5.4 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer has sufficient cash on hand or available under committed financing to pay the Closing Cash Payment and the Escrow Amount.

**5.5 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Buyer.

**5.6 Independent Investigation.** Buyer has conducted its own independent investigation of the Business and acknowledges that it has relied only on the representations and warranties in Article IV.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business Before Closing.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, Seller shall operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, maintain the Purchased Assets in good condition, and use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve its relationships with customers, suppliers and employees.

**6.2 Access to Information.** Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the Lake Charles Facility and to the books, records and personnel of the Business, including access to conduct Phase I environmental site assessments.

**6.3 Efforts; Consents.** Each party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to satisfy the conditions in Article VII and to obtain all third-party consents listed in Schedule 4.4.

**6.4 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, hold in confidence all confidential information concerning the Business.

**6.5 Non-Competition; Non-Solicitation.** For a period of five (5) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly, (a) engage in the manufacture, blending or sale of coagulants, sodium hypochlorite or corrosion inhibitors for municipal or industrial water treatment in the United States, or (b) solicit any customer of the Business or any employee of Buyer who was an employee of the Business. The conduct of the Retained Business as conducted on the date of this Agreement does not violate this Section 6.5.

**6.6 Employees.** Buyer shall offer employment, effective as of the Closing, to each employee of the Business listed in Schedule 4.13 on terms of base pay no less favorable than those in effect immediately before the Closing. Seller shall cooperate with Buyer to facilitate the transfer of those employees who accept Buyer's offer.

**6.7 Bulk Sales.** The parties waive compliance with any applicable bulk sales Laws.

**6.8 Further Assurances.** After the Closing, each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments and take such further actions as may be reasonably required to carry out the provisions of this Agreement.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of Both Parties.** The obligations of each party to close are subject to: (a) no Law or order of any Governmental Authority restraining the transactions contemplated by this Agreement; and (b) expiration or termination of any applicable waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976.

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** The obligations of Buyer to close are further subject to: (a) the representations and warranties of Seller being true and correct in all material respects (or, for Fundamental Representations, in all respects) as of the Closing Date; (b) Seller having performed in all material respects its covenants under this Agreement; (c) Buyer having received a certificate of an officer of Seller to the effect of clauses (a) and (b); (d) the transfer to Buyer of the environmental permits for the Lake Charles Facility listed in Schedule 4.11, or issuance of replacement permits; (e) Annika Solberg-Reyes having entered into an employment agreement with Buyer, effective as of the Closing, as Plant Director of the Lake Charles Facility; and (f) no Material Adverse Effect having occurred.

**7.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** The obligations of Seller to close are further subject to: (a) the representations and warranties of Buyer being true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date; (b) Buyer having performed in all material respects its covenants under this Agreement; and (c) Buyer having made the deliveries in Section 3.3.

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing: (a) by mutual written consent; (b) by either party if the Closing has not occurred by December 31, 2026, provided that the terminating party's breach is not the cause of the delay; (c) by Buyer if Seller has materially breached this Agreement and has not cured the breach within thirty (30) days after written notice; or (d) by Seller if Buyer has materially breached this Agreement and has not cured the breach within thirty (30) days after written notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated, it shall become void without Liability of any party, except for Liability for willful breach and except that Sections 6.4 and Article XII shall survive.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The General Representations shall survive the Closing until 18 months after the Closing Date. The Fundamental Representations shall survive until sixty (60) days after expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants shall survive in accordance with their terms. No claim may be brought for breach of a representation or warranty after its survival period expires, except a claim for which written notice was given before expiration.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify and hold harmless Buyer and its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors and employees (the "**Buyer Parties**"), from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement or any certificate delivered under it;

(b) any breach of any covenant of Seller in this Agreement; or

(c) any Excluded Liability.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates, and their respective officers, directors and employees (the "**Seller Parties**"), from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation, warranty or covenant of Buyer in this Agreement;

(b) any Assumed Liability; or

(c) the operation of the Business by Buyer after the Closing.

**9.4 Source of Recovery.**

(a) Claims under Section 9.2 shall first be satisfied from the Escrow Fund, to the extent funds are then available.

(b) The Escrow Fund shall be the sole and exclusive source of recovery for the Buyer Parties for any Losses under Section 9.2(a), other than Losses arising from a breach of any Fundamental Representation or from fraud.

(c) Losses under Section 9.2(a) arising from a breach of a Fundamental Representation or from fraud, and Losses under Sections 9.2(b) and 9.2(c), to the extent not satisfied from the Escrow Fund, may be recovered directly from Seller or by set-off under Section 9.7.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) until the aggregate Losses under it exceed $864,000 (the "**Basket**"), after which Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of the Basket. Seller's aggregate liability under Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed the Escrow Amount (the "**Cap**"). Buyer's aggregate liability under Section 9.3(a) shall not exceed the Escrow Amount.

(b) Aggregate Losses recoverable for breaches of Fundamental Representations shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

(c) The Basket and the Cap shall not apply to claims for fraud, to claims under Sections 9.2(b) or 9.2(c), and shall not apply to claims under Section 9.3(b).

(d) Losses shall be calculated net of insurance proceeds actually received. No party shall be liable for punitive damages except to the extent paid to a third party.

**9.6 Claims Procedure.** An indemnified party shall give the indemnifying party prompt written notice of any claim, describing it in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice shall not relieve the indemnifying party of its obligations except to the extent it is actually prejudiced. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party, and shall not settle any such claim without the indemnified party's consent unless the settlement provides solely for money damages paid by the indemnifying party and a full release of the indemnified party.

**9.7 Set-Off.** Buyer may set off against amounts payable under the Seller Note any amount finally determined to be owed to a Buyer Party under this Article IX that is recoverable other than from the Escrow Fund under Section 9.4, in accordance with the terms of the Seller Note.

**9.8 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims of fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, and claims under Section 2.8, this Article IX is the exclusive remedy of the parties for any claim arising out of this Agreement.

**9.9 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes, unless otherwise required by Law.

## ARTICLE X. TAX MATTERS

**10.1 Transfer Taxes.** All transfer, documentary, sales, use, stamp and registration Taxes incurred in connection with this Agreement shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**10.2 Proration.** Real and personal property Taxes on the Purchased Assets for any period that includes the Closing Date shall be prorated between Seller and Buyer as of the Closing Date.

## ARTICLE XI. EMPLOYEE MATTERS

**11.1 Seller Plans.** Seller shall retain all Liabilities under its employee benefit plans. Transferred employees shall cease participation in those plans as of the Closing.

**11.2 WARN Act.** Seller shall be responsible for any Liability under the Worker Adjustment and Retraining Notification Act arising from actions taken by Seller before the Closing.

## ARTICLE XII. MISCELLANEOUS

**12.1 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, each party shall bear its own expenses in connection with this Agreement. Escrow Agent fees shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**12.2 Notices.** All notices shall be in writing and deemed given when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth in Schedule 12.2, or such other address as a party may designate by notice.

**12.3 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**12.4 Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict or inconsistency between this Agreement and any Ancillary Agreement, this Agreement shall prevail; provided, however, that the Seller Note shall control with respect to its payment, set-off and enforcement.

**12.5 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by each party. No waiver shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party.

**12.6 Successors and Assigns.** This Agreement shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns. Neither party may assign its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except that Buyer may assign its rights to any Affiliate or to any lender as collateral, provided that Buyer remains liable for its obligations.

**12.7 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the Buyer Parties and the Seller Parties under Article IX, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the parties and their successors and permitted assigns.

**12.8 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule.

**12.9 Submission to Jurisdiction.** Any legal suit, action or proceeding arising out of or based upon this Agreement or the transactions contemplated by it shall be instituted exclusively in the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware, and each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of that court in any such suit, action or proceeding.

**12.10 Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each party irrevocably waives any right to trial by jury in any action arising out of this Agreement.

**12.11 Specific Performance.** The parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if any provision of this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its terms, and that each party shall be entitled to specific performance in addition to any other remedy.

**12.12 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original. Signatures delivered by electronic transmission shall be deemed original signatures.
