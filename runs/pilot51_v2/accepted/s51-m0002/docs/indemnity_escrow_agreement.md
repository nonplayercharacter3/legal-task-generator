# INDEMNITY ESCROW AGREEMENT

This INDEMNITY ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of October 1, 2026 (the "**Closing Date**") by and among **Doureth Water Solutions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), **Routand Chemical Company**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Pellwick Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**").

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of August 3, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is acquiring from Seller the Purchased Assets of the Business, including the Lake Charles Facility.

B. Section 2.6 of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. The Escrow Amount is part of the Purchase Price and is to be held and disbursed under this Agreement.

C. The Escrow Agent is willing to act as escrow agent on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 3.1.

"**Claimed Amount**" means the amount of Losses claimed in good faith by a Buyer Party in a Claim Notice. Where the amount is not yet known, it means Buyer's reasonable good-faith estimate of the amount.

"**Escrow Funds**" means the Escrow Amount deposited with the Escrow Agent under Section 2.1, together with all interest, dividends and other earnings on it, as reduced from time to time by disbursements under this Agreement.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction. The party presenting it to the Escrow Agent shall deliver it together with a certificate of that party's counsel stating that it is final and non-appealable.

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means written instructions to the Escrow Agent signed by an Authorized Representative of each of Buyer and Seller.

"**Objection Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 3.2.

"**Release Date**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Section 1.2 of the Purchase Agreement applies to this Agreement. References in it to "this Agreement" are read as references to this Agreement.

## 2. Appointment; Deposit; Investment

**2.1 Appointment and Deposit.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent as escrow agent under this Agreement, and the Escrow Agent accepts the appointment. On the Closing Date, Buyer shall deposit with the Escrow Agent $8,640,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") by wire transfer of immediately available funds. The Escrow Agent shall acknowledge receipt in writing to Buyer and Seller. It shall hold the Escrow Funds in a segregated account (the "**Escrow Account**") and disburse them only as this Agreement provides.

**2.2 Investment.** The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds in the Pellwick Government Obligations Money Market Fund or a successor fund, unless Buyer and Seller jointly direct otherwise in writing. The Escrow Agent may liquidate any investment as needed to make a disbursement under this Agreement. It is not liable for any loss of principal or income resulting from an investment made in accordance with this Section 2.2.

**2.3 No Other Use.** The Escrow Funds shall not be subject to any lien, attachment, trustee process or other judicial process of any creditor of any party. They shall be used only for the purposes set out in this Agreement.

## 3. Claims Against the Escrow Funds

**3.1 Claim Notices.** If a Buyer Party asserts a claim for indemnification under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement that is to be satisfied from the Escrow Funds, Buyer may deliver to Seller and the Escrow Agent a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**"). A Claim Notice must:

(a) describe the claim in reasonable detail, to the extent then known;

(b) state the Claimed Amount; and

(c) identify the provision of the Purchase Agreement under which indemnification is sought.

**3.2 Objection.** Seller may dispute all or part of a Claimed Amount by delivering a written objection to Buyer and the Escrow Agent within thirty (30) days after its receipt of the Claim Notice (an "**Objection Notice**"). An Objection Notice must state the disputed amount and the basis for the dispute in reasonable detail.

**3.3 Undisputed Claims.** If the Escrow Agent does not receive an Objection Notice within the thirty (30) day period in Section 3.2, the Claimed Amount is deemed undisputed. The Escrow Agent shall then disburse the Claimed Amount to Buyer within five (5) Business Days after the end of that period. If an Objection Notice disputes only part of a Claimed Amount, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the undisputed part to Buyer within the same time.

**3.4 Disputed Claims.** The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold any disputed part of a Claimed Amount. It shall disburse that part only in accordance with (a) Joint Written Instructions or (b) a Final Order. Buyer and Seller shall negotiate in good faith for at least thirty (30) days after delivery of an Objection Notice to resolve the dispute. Once a dispute is resolved by agreement, they shall promptly deliver Joint Written Instructions reflecting the resolution.

**3.5 Insufficient Funds.** If the Escrow Funds are insufficient to pay a Claimed Amount in full, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the Escrow Funds then available. It has no further obligation with respect to that claim. Claims are paid from the Escrow Funds in the order in which the Escrow Agent receives the Claim Notices.

## 4. Release of Escrow Funds

**4.1 Release.** On the first Business Day after the date that is nine (9) months after the Closing Date (the "**Release Date**"), the Escrow Agent shall release to Seller all remaining Escrow Funds, less the aggregate of all Claimed Amounts under Claim Notices delivered before the Release Date that have not then been paid or finally resolved (the "**Retained Amount**"). Seller shall be entitled to that release without any further action by Buyer.

**4.2 Retained Amount.** The Escrow Agent shall hold the Retained Amount after the Release Date. Within five (5) Business Days after each pending claim is resolved by Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order, the Escrow Agent shall:

(a) disburse to Buyer the amount, if any, payable to Buyer for that claim; and

(b) release to Seller the part of the Retained Amount attributable to that claim that is not payable to Buyer.

**4.3 Joint Written Instructions.** Notwithstanding Sections 3 and 4.1, the Escrow Agent shall at any time disburse Escrow Funds in accordance with Joint Written Instructions. Buyer and Seller shall promptly deliver Joint Written Instructions whenever an amount becomes payable under this Agreement.

**4.4 Earnings.** All interest and other earnings on the Escrow Funds form part of the Escrow Funds. They are disbursed in the same proportion as the principal amounts to which they relate.

## 5. The Escrow Agent

**5.1 Duties.** The Escrow Agent's duties are only those expressly set out in this Agreement. They are ministerial in nature, and no implied duties or obligations apply to the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent is not a party to, and is not bound by or required to inquire into, the Purchase Agreement or any other agreement between Buyer and Seller.

**5.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any written notice, instruction, certificate or Final Order that it believes in good faith to be genuine and signed or presented by the proper person. It need not investigate the facts stated in any such document.

**5.3 Standard of Care.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith under this Agreement. This does not apply to its own gross negligence or willful misconduct. In no event is the Escrow Agent liable for special, indirect, consequential or punitive damages.

**5.4 Conflicting Demands.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting instructions, or is uncertain about its duties, it may refrain from acting until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. It may also deposit the disputed Escrow Funds with the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware in an interpleader action.

**5.5 Indemnification of Escrow Agent.** Buyer and Seller shall, jointly and severally, indemnify and hold harmless the Escrow Agent and its directors, officers, employees and agents from all losses, claims, damages, liabilities and expenses arising out of its performance of this Agreement, including reasonable attorneys' fees. This indemnity does not apply to losses resulting from the Escrow Agent's gross negligence or willful misconduct. As between Buyer and Seller, each bears one-half of any amount paid under this Section 5.5. This Section 5.5 survives termination of this Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

**5.6 Counsel.** The Escrow Agent may consult counsel of its choice. It is not liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith in accordance with the advice of that counsel.

## 6. Resignation and Removal

**6.1 Resignation.** The Escrow Agent may resign by giving thirty (30) days' written notice to Buyer and Seller.

**6.2 Removal.** Buyer and Seller may jointly remove the Escrow Agent by giving it thirty (30) days' written notice.

**6.3 Successor.** Buyer and Seller shall jointly appoint a successor escrow agent, which must be a bank or trust company with combined capital and surplus of at least $500,000,000. If no successor has been appointed within thirty (30) days after notice of resignation or removal, the Escrow Agent may petition the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware to appoint one. On a successor's written acceptance of appointment, the resigning or removed Escrow Agent shall deliver the Escrow Funds to the successor and is then discharged from further obligations under this Agreement.

## 7. Fees and Expenses

The Escrow Agent is entitled to the fees set out in Schedule A and to reimbursement of its reasonable out-of-pocket expenses. Consistent with Section 12.1 of the Purchase Agreement, Buyer and Seller shall each bear one-half of those fees and expenses. The Escrow Agent shall not pay its fees or expenses from the Escrow Funds.

## 8. Tax Matters

**8.1 Tax Owner.** For U.S. federal and applicable state income tax purposes, Buyer is treated as the owner of the Escrow Funds, and all earnings on them are reported as income of Buyer, until disbursement. Each of Buyer and Seller shall deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9 on or before the Closing Date.

**8.2 Reporting.** The Escrow Agent shall report earnings on the Escrow Funds as required by Law. It may withhold any Taxes required by Law from any disbursement.

**8.3 Treatment of Disbursements.** Disbursements of Escrow Funds to Buyer are treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price to the extent provided in Section 9.9 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 9. Authorized Representatives; Security Procedures

**9.1 Authorized Representatives.** Each of Buyer and Seller shall deliver to the Escrow Agent, on or before the Closing Date, a certificate in the form of Schedule B. The certificate shall name the individuals authorized to sign instructions on its behalf (each, an "**Authorized Representative**") and include their specimen signatures. A party may change its Authorized Representatives by written notice to the Escrow Agent.

**9.2 Call-Back Verification.** Before disbursing any funds on instructions other than Joint Written Instructions delivered in person, the Escrow Agent shall confirm the instructions by telephone call-back to an Authorized Representative of the instructing party, other than the individual who signed the instructions. The Escrow Agent may rely on that confirmation.

## 10. Termination

This Agreement terminates when all Escrow Funds have been disbursed in accordance with its terms. Sections 5.3, 5.5 and 7 survive termination.

However, any entity into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or that succeeds to substantially all of its corporate trust business, becomes the successor Escrow Agent without further action. A permitted assignee of Buyer's rights under Section 12.6 of the Purchase Agreement succeeds to Buyer's rights under this Agreement on written notice to Seller and the Escrow Agent.

**11.4 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule.

**11.5 Jurisdiction.** Any legal suit, action or proceeding arising out of or based upon this Agreement shall be instituted exclusively in the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware. Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of that court in any such suit, action or proceeding.

**11.6 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION OR PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**11.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**11.8 Patriot Act.** Buyer and Seller acknowledge that the Escrow Agent must obtain, verify and record information identifying each person that opens an account with it. Each of them shall provide the Escrow Agent with the information it reasonably requests for that purpose.

**11.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which is deemed an original. Signatures delivered by electronic transmission are deemed original signatures.
