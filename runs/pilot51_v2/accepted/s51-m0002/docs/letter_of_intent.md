**DOURETH WATER SOLUTIONS, INC.**

June 15, 2026

Routand Chemical Company
Attention: Chief Executive Officer

**Re: Letter of Intent – Proposed Acquisition of the Municipal and Industrial Water Treatment Chemicals Business of Routand Chemical Company**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter of intent (this "**Letter**") sets out the principal terms on which Doureth Water Solutions, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Doureth**"), proposes to acquire the municipal and industrial water treatment chemicals business of Routand Chemical Company, a Delaware corporation ("**Routand**"). Doureth and Routand are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

Paragraphs 1 through 9 are non-binding statements of the Parties' present intentions. Paragraphs 10 through 15 (the "**Binding Provisions**") are legally binding obligations of the Parties.

## 1. Transaction Structure

Doureth will acquire substantially all of the assets used or held for use primarily in Routand's business of manufacturing, blending, packaging, marketing, selling and distributing coagulants, sodium hypochlorite, corrosion inhibitors and related water treatment chemicals for municipal and industrial customers (the "**Business**"). The Business includes Routand's blending plant in Lake Charles, Louisiana (the "**Lake Charles Facility**"). The acquisition will be structured as an asset purchase (the "**Transaction**") under a definitive asset purchase agreement and related ancillary agreements (the "**Definitive Agreements**").

Routand will retain its industrial cleaning chemicals division (the "**Retained Business**"). The Retained Business is not part of the Business and will not be acquired by Doureth.

## 2. Purchased Assets and Excluded Assets

The assets to be acquired (the "**Purchased Assets**") will include:

(a) the Lake Charles Facility, including the owned real property and all buildings, tanks, blending and packaging lines, rail spurs, loading racks and other improvements and fixtures;

(b) all inventory of finished products, raw materials, work in process, packaging and supplies of the Business;

(c) customer, supply and other contracts of the Business to be scheduled in the Definitive Agreements;

(d) all permits and environmental authorizations held for the Business, to the extent transferable;

(e) all formulations, product specifications, trade secrets, trademarks and other intellectual property used primarily in the Business;

(f) all accounts receivable of the Business; and

(g) all books, records, customer lists and supplier lists of the Business.

The Purchased Assets will not include cash and cash equivalents, the assets of the Retained Business, Routand's corporate and tax records, or Routand's insurance policies.

## 3. Purchase Price

The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets will be $86,400,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to a customary working capital adjustment against a target to be agreed. The Purchase Price will be paid at closing (the "**Closing**") as follows:

(a) $65,760,000 in cash, as adjusted by the estimated working capital adjustment, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to Routand;

(b) $12,000,000 by Doureth's issuance to Routand of a subordinated promissory note (the "**Seller Note**"). The Seller Note will bear interest at a fixed rate to be agreed, will be subordinated to Doureth's senior acquisition financing, and will be subject to Doureth's right to set off indemnification amounts owed to Doureth under the Definitive Agreements; and

(c) $8,640,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") deposited with a mutually acceptable escrow agent. The Escrow Amount will be held as security for Routand's indemnification obligations under the Definitive Agreements and released on terms to be set out in the Definitive Agreements.

## 4. Liabilities

Doureth will assume only the following liabilities of the Business: (a) trade accounts payable incurred in the ordinary course and reflected in closing working capital; and (b) obligations under assigned contracts arising and to be performed after the Closing, other than liabilities arising from any breach by Routand before the Closing.

All pre-Closing environmental liabilities will be retained by Routand. These are all liabilities under environmental laws arising out of or relating to the ownership or operation of the Business, the Purchased Assets or the Lake Charles Facility before the Closing, including liabilities for any release of hazardous materials at, on, under or from the Lake Charles Facility occurring before the Closing and any related investigation or remediation, whether known or unknown at the Closing. Routand will indemnify Doureth for those liabilities.

Routand will also retain all other liabilities of the Business and of Routand not expressly assumed by Doureth. These include liabilities relating to the Retained Business, pre-Closing taxes, indebtedness for borrowed money, products sold before the Closing, pre-Closing employee and benefit plan liabilities, and pending or threatened litigation.

## 5. Representations, Warranties and Indemnification

The Definitive Agreements will contain representations, warranties, covenants and indemnities customary for a transaction of this type. These will include representations on title, financial statements, material contracts, permits, compliance with laws, and environmental matters at the Lake Charles Facility. Survival periods, baskets, caps and the role of the Escrow Amount and the Seller Note as sources of recovery will be negotiated in the Definitive Agreements.

## 6. Transitional Supply Arrangement

At the Closing, Routand and Doureth will enter into a transitional supply arrangement under which Routand will supply membrane-grade liquid caustic soda feedstock to the Lake Charles Facility for a term of approximately twenty-four (24) months after the Closing. Pricing will be based on a published U.S. Gulf Coast contract index, less an agreed discount, plus freight. Volumes will be set by a forecast and ordering mechanism to be agreed.

## 7. Employees

Doureth intends to offer employment, effective as of the Closing, to the employees of the Business on terms of base pay no less favorable than those in effect immediately before the Closing. It will be a condition to Doureth's obligation to close that Annika Solberg-Reyes, Plant Director of the Lake Charles Facility, have entered into an employment agreement with Doureth effective as of the Closing.

## 8. Restrictive Covenants

Routand will agree not to compete with the Business in the United States, and not to solicit customers or transferred employees of the Business, for five (5) years after the Closing. The covenant will not restrict the Retained Business as conducted on the date of the Definitive Agreements.

## 9. Conditions; Timing

The Closing will be subject to customary conditions, including:

(a) completion of Doureth's business, financial, legal and environmental due diligence, including Phase I environmental site assessments of the Lake Charles Facility, with results satisfactory to Doureth;

(b) negotiation and execution of the Definitive Agreements;

(c) expiration or termination of any applicable waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976;

(d) transfer or reissuance of the environmental permits for the Lake Charles Facility;

(e) approval of the Transaction by Doureth's board of directors; and

(f) the absence of any material adverse change in the Business.

The Parties intend to sign the Definitive Agreements in early August 2026 and to close on or about October 1, 2026.

## 10. Access and Due Diligence

Routand will give Doureth and its representatives reasonable access, during normal business hours, to the Lake Charles Facility and to the books, records, contracts and personnel of the Business. Doureth may conduct environmental site assessments, which will be limited to non-invasive Phase I work unless Routand agrees otherwise in writing.

## 11. Exclusivity

From the date of Routand's countersignature of this Letter until the earlier of (a) August 31, 2026 and (b) the date either Party notifies the other in writing that it is ending negotiations, Routand will not, and will cause its Affiliates and representatives not to, directly or indirectly solicit, encourage, negotiate or enter into any agreement with any person other than Doureth for the sale of the Business or any material part of the Purchased Assets. Routand will promptly notify Doureth of any unsolicited proposal of that kind it receives.

## 12. Confidentiality

The Mutual Confidentiality Agreement between the Parties dated April 2, 2026 remains in full force and effect and applies to this Letter and to all information exchanged in connection with the Transaction. Neither Party will make any public announcement about the Transaction without the other Party's prior written consent, except as required by law.

## 13. Expenses

Each Party will bear its own costs and expenses in connection with this Letter and the Transaction, whether or not the Transaction is completed.

## 14. Non-Binding Nature

Except for the Binding Provisions, this Letter is not a binding agreement and creates no legally enforceable obligation of either Party. No binding obligation to complete the Transaction will arise unless and until the Definitive Agreements are executed and delivered by both Parties. Either Party may end negotiations at any time, for any reason, by written notice to the other Party, without liability other than for breach of the Binding Provisions.

## 15. Governing Law; Counterparts

This Letter is governed by the internal laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule. Any proceeding to enforce the Binding Provisions shall be brought exclusively in the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware. This Letter may be executed in counterparts and delivered by electronic transmission, each of which is deemed an original.
