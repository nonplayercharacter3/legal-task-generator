# SUBORDINATED PROMISSORY NOTE

**$8,000,000.00**

**Issue Date:** the Closing Date (as defined in the Purchase Agreement)

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **TINAV BEVERAGE PARTNERS, INC.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Maker**"), hereby unconditionally promises to pay to the order of **BRAIKAL BEVERAGE GROUP, INC.**, a Delaware corporation (together with its successors and registered assigns, "**Holder**"), the principal sum of EIGHT MILLION AND NO/100 DOLLARS ($8,000,000.00), together with interest on its unpaid principal balance, in accordance with the terms of this Subordinated Promissory Note (this "**Note**").

This Note is the "Seller Note" referred to in the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of October 2, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"), between Maker, as buyer, and Holder, as seller, under which Maker is acquiring all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Copperwake Brewing Company, an Oregon corporation (the "**Company**"). This Note is issued and delivered at the Closing as part of the Purchase Price.

## 1. Principal; Definitions

1.1 **Principal Amount.** The principal amount of this Note is $8,000,000 (the "**Principal Amount**"). The outstanding principal balance of this Note at any time is the Principal Amount less all payments and prepayments of principal made under this Note before that time.

1.2 **Defined Terms.** In this Note:

"**Business Day**" means any day except Saturday, Sunday or any other day on which commercial banks located in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to be closed.

"**Default Rate**" has the meaning given in Section 2.3.

"**Event of Default**" has the meaning given in Section 6.1.

"**Interest Payment Date**" means the last Business Day of each March, June, September and December, beginning with the first such date to occur after the Issue Date, and the Maturity Date.

"**Issue Date**" means the date on which the Closing occurs.

"**Maturity Date**" means the fifth (5th) anniversary of the Issue Date or, if that date is not a Business Day, the next succeeding Business Day.

"**Obligations**" means all principal, interest (including interest accruing after the filing of any petition in bankruptcy, whether or not allowed), fees, costs and other amounts owing by Maker under this Note.

"**Person**" means an individual, corporation, partnership, joint venture, limited liability company, governmental authority, unincorporated organization, trust, association or other entity.

"**Senior Indebtedness**" has the meaning given in Section 9.1.

"**Subsidiary**" means any Person of which Maker owns, directly or indirectly, more than fifty percent (50%) of the outstanding voting equity, including, from and after the Closing, the Company.

1.3 **Purchase Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. Interest

2.1 **Rate.** Interest shall accrue on the outstanding principal balance of this Note from and including the Issue Date to but excluding the date of payment in full, at a fixed rate equal to six percent (6.00%) per annum.

2.2 **Payment of Interest.** Accrued and unpaid interest shall be payable in arrears on each Interest Payment Date. Interest accrued on any principal amount prepaid under Section 4 shall be payable on the date of that prepayment.

2.3 **Default Interest.** While any Event of Default under Section 6.1(a) or 6.1(d) is continuing, the outstanding principal balance of this Note and, to the extent permitted by applicable law, any overdue interest shall bear interest at a rate per annum equal to the rate otherwise applicable under Section 2.1 plus two percent (2.00%) (the "**Default Rate**"). Interest at the Default Rate shall be payable on demand.

2.4 **Computation.** All interest under this Note shall be computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

2.5 **Maximum Lawful Rate.** Nothing in this Note shall require Maker to pay interest at a rate exceeding the maximum rate permitted by applicable law. If any amount paid as interest would exceed that maximum, the excess shall be applied to reduce the outstanding principal balance of this Note or, if principal has been paid in full, refunded to Maker.

## 3. Payments

3.1 **Principal Installments.** Maker shall repay the principal of this Note as follows:

(a) on the Interest Payment Date occurring in the last calendar quarter in which the second (2nd) anniversary of the Issue Date falls, and on each Interest Payment Date after that date (other than the Maturity Date), an installment of principal in the amount of $500,000; and

(b) on the Maturity Date, the entire then outstanding principal balance of this Note, together with all accrued and unpaid interest and all other Obligations then outstanding.

3.2 **Manner of Payment.** All payments under this Note shall be made in lawful money of the United States of America, in immediately available funds, by wire transfer to the account designated by Holder in a written notice delivered to Maker at least five (5) Business Days before the relevant payment date. Until Maker receives such a notice, payments shall be made to the account into which the cash portion of the Purchase Price was paid at the Closing.

3.3 **Payment Dates.** If any payment under this Note falls due on a day that is not a Business Day, it shall be made on the next succeeding Business Day, and interest shall continue to accrue for the period of the extension.

3.4 **Application of Payments.** Each payment received by Holder shall be applied first to costs and expenses then due under Section 7.3, second to interest at the Default Rate then due, third to accrued and unpaid interest then due, and fourth to the outstanding principal balance of this Note, in the inverse order of the installments due under Section 3.1.

3.5 **Note Register.** Maker shall maintain at its principal office a register in which it records the name and address of each registered holder of this Note and each transfer of this Note made in accordance with Section 8. Maker may treat the Person in whose name this Note is registered as the owner of this Note for all purposes, and payment to that Person shall discharge Maker's obligation to the extent of the payment.

## 4. Prepayment

4.1 **Voluntary Prepayment.** Maker may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time and from time to time without premium or penalty, upon not less than five (5) Business Days' prior written notice to Holder specifying the prepayment date and the principal amount to be prepaid. Each partial prepayment shall be in a minimum principal amount of $250,000 or, if less, the outstanding principal balance of this Note.

4.2 **Mandatory Prepayment on Sale of the Company.** If Maker sells, transfers or otherwise disposes of (a) a majority of the outstanding equity interests of the Company, or (b) all or substantially all of the assets of the Company, in each case to a Person that is not a Subsidiary or Affiliate of Maker, Maker shall prepay the entire outstanding principal balance of this Note, together with all accrued and unpaid interest, on the date of consummation of that transaction, subject to Section 9.

4.3 **Effect of Prepayment.** Any prepayment of principal shall be accompanied by accrued and unpaid interest on the amount prepaid to the date of prepayment. Amounts prepaid may not be reborrowed.

## 5. Reporting Covenants

So long as any Obligations remain outstanding, Maker shall deliver, or cause the Company to deliver, to Holder:

5.1 **Monthly Financial Statements.** Within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar month, the unaudited balance sheet of the Company as at the end of that month and the related statements of income and cash flow for that month and for the fiscal year to date, setting forth in comparative form the figures for the corresponding periods of the preceding fiscal year, certified by the chief financial officer or controller of Maker as fairly presenting in all material respects the financial condition and results of operations of the Company, subject to normal year-end adjustments and the absence of notes.

5.2 **Depletion Reports.** Within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar month, the distributor depletion reports received by the Company for that month, showing, by distributor and by brand and package type, the volume of the Company's products sold through by each distributor to retail accounts, together with the Company's shipments to each distributor for that month.

5.3 **Annual Financial Statements.** Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year of Maker, the consolidated balance sheet of Maker and its Subsidiaries as at the end of that fiscal year and the related consolidated statements of income, stockholders' equity and cash flow for that fiscal year, audited by an independent certified public accounting firm of recognized standing.

5.4 **Compliance Certificate.** Together with each delivery under Section 5.3, a certificate of the chief financial officer of Maker stating that, to the officer's knowledge after reasonable inquiry, no Default or Event of Default has occurred and is continuing or, if one has occurred and is continuing, describing it and the action Maker proposes to take with respect to it.

5.5 **Notice of Default.** Promptly, and in any event within five (5) Business Days after any executive officer of Maker obtains knowledge of it, written notice of the occurrence of any Event of Default, or any event that with notice or lapse of time or both would constitute an Event of Default (a "**Default**").

5.6 **Other Information.** Promptly after Holder's reasonable written request, such other information regarding the financial condition or operations of Maker and the Company as Holder may reasonably request, to the extent such information is reasonably available to Maker.

## 6. Events of Default

6.1 **Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) *Non-payment.* Maker fails to pay (i) any principal of this Note when due, whether at stated maturity, by required prepayment or otherwise, or (ii) any interest or other amount payable under this Note within five (5) Business Days after it becomes due;

(b) *Reporting.* Maker fails to observe or perform any covenant in Section 5, and the failure continues for thirty (30) days after Maker's receipt of written notice of it from Holder;

(c) *Other Covenants.* Maker fails to observe or perform any other covenant or agreement contained in this Note, and the failure continues for thirty (30) days after Maker's receipt of written notice of it from Holder;

(d) *Voluntary Insolvency.* Maker or the Company (i) commences a voluntary case or proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency, reorganization, receivership or similar law, (ii) consents to the entry of an order for relief against it in an involuntary case, (iii) consents to the appointment of a custodian, receiver, trustee, assignee or liquidator of it or of all or substantially all of its property, (iv) makes a general assignment for the benefit of its creditors, or (v) admits in writing its inability to pay its debts generally as they become due;

(e) *Involuntary Insolvency.* An involuntary case or proceeding is commenced against Maker or the Company under any bankruptcy, insolvency, reorganization, receivership or similar law, and the case or proceeding is not dismissed or stayed within sixty (60) days after its commencement, or an order for relief is entered in it;

(f) *Acceleration of Senior Indebtedness.* Any Senior Indebtedness in an aggregate principal amount exceeding $5,000,000 is accelerated and declared due and payable before its stated maturity, and the acceleration is not rescinded within thirty (30) days; or

(g) *Judgments.* One or more final, non-appealable judgments for the payment of money in an aggregate amount exceeding $2,500,000 (to the extent not covered by insurance as to which the insurer has not denied coverage) is entered against Maker or the Company and remains unsatisfied, unvacated and unstayed for sixty (60) days.

6.2 **Cure.** An Event of Default is "continuing" until it has been cured or waived in writing by Holder.

## 7. Remedies

7.1 **Acceleration.** Upon the occurrence and during the continuance of any Event of Default (other than an Event of Default under Section 6.1(d) or 6.1(e)), Holder may, by written notice to Maker, declare the entire outstanding principal balance of this Note, together with all accrued and unpaid interest and all other Obligations, to be immediately due and payable, whereupon those amounts shall become immediately due and payable without presentment, demand, protest or other notice of any kind, all of which Maker waives.

7.2 **Automatic Acceleration.** Upon the occurrence of any Event of Default under Section 6.1(d) or 6.1(e), the entire outstanding principal balance of this Note, together with all accrued and unpaid interest and all other Obligations, shall automatically become immediately due and payable without any declaration, notice or other action by Holder.

7.3 **Costs of Collection.** Maker shall pay on demand all reasonable costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees and disbursements, incurred by Holder in collecting or enforcing payment of this Note after the occurrence of an Event of Default, whether or not suit is brought.

7.4 **Remedies Cumulative.** Subject to Section 9, the rights and remedies of Holder under this Note are cumulative and in addition to any other rights and remedies available to Holder at law or in equity. No failure or delay by Holder in exercising any right or remedy shall operate as a waiver of it, and no single or partial exercise of any right or remedy shall preclude any other or further exercise of it or of any other right or remedy.

## 8. Assignment

8.1 **Assignment by Holder.** Holder may assign, transfer, negotiate or pledge this Note and its rights under this Note, in whole or in part, to any Person, without the consent of Maker. Each assignee or transferee shall succeed to all of the rights of Holder under this Note to the extent of the interest assigned or transferred, including the rights under Sections 5 and 7.

8.2 **Registration of Transfer.** Holder shall give Maker written notice of any assignment or transfer of this Note, specifying the name and address of the assignee or transferee and the principal amount assigned or transferred, within ten (10) Business Days after the assignment or transfer. Upon surrender of this Note to Maker together with that notice, Maker shall record the transfer in the register maintained under Section 3.5 and, at Holder's request, execute and deliver to the assignee or transferee a new note in substantially the form of this Note, dated the date to which interest has been paid, in a principal amount equal to the principal amount so assigned or transferred, and, if less than all of this Note is assigned or transferred, a new note to Holder for the balance. Until such notice is received, Maker may continue to make payments to the registered Holder.

8.3 **Assignment by Maker.** Maker may not assign or delegate any of its obligations under this Note without the prior written consent of Holder, and any purported assignment or delegation without that consent shall be void; provided that no consent shall be required for the assumption of this Note by a successor to Maker by merger or consolidation that assumes all of the Obligations by operation of law or by written instrument delivered to Holder.

8.4 **Securities Laws.** This Note has not been registered under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or any state securities laws. Holder shall not transfer this Note except in compliance with those laws.

8.5 **Lost or Destroyed Note.** Upon receipt of evidence reasonably satisfactory to Maker of the loss, theft, destruction or mutilation of this Note and, in the case of loss, theft or destruction, an indemnity agreement reasonably satisfactory to Maker (without the requirement of a bond), or, in the case of mutilation, upon surrender and cancellation of this Note, Maker shall execute and deliver a new note of like tenor and principal amount.

## 9. Subordination

9.1 **Agreement to Subordinate.** Maker, for itself and its successors, and Holder, by its acceptance of this Note, for itself and its successors and assigns, agree that the payment of the Obligations is subordinated, to the extent and in the manner provided in this Section 9, to the prior payment in full in cash of all Senior Indebtedness. "**Senior Indebtedness**" means all obligations of Maker and its Subsidiaries for borrowed money owing to any bank, insurance company, commercial finance company or other institutional lender under any credit agreement, loan agreement, note purchase agreement or similar instrument, whether outstanding on the Issue Date or incurred after it, together with all interest, fees, expenses, indemnities and other amounts owing in respect of them (including interest accruing after the commencement of any insolvency proceeding, whether or not allowed), and any refinancing, replacement or extension of any of them; provided that Senior Indebtedness shall not include any indebtedness owing to any Affiliate of Maker.

9.2 **Payment Blockage.** If any payment default on Senior Indebtedness has occurred and is continuing, or if any other event of default on Senior Indebtedness has occurred and is continuing that permits the holders of that Senior Indebtedness to accelerate its maturity and Maker and Holder have received written notice of it from the holders or their agent (a "**Blockage Notice**"), Maker shall not make, and Holder shall not accept, any payment of principal or interest on this Note until (a) in the case of a payment default, the default is cured or waived or the Senior Indebtedness is paid in full, and (b) in the case of any other event of default, the earliest of (i) the cure or waiver of that event of default, (ii) the payment in full of the Senior Indebtedness, and (iii) the date that is one hundred seventy-nine (179) days after delivery of the Blockage Notice. No more than one Blockage Notice under clause (b) may be delivered in any period of three hundred sixty (360) consecutive days.

9.3 **Insolvency.** In any distribution of assets of Maker upon any dissolution, winding up, liquidation or reorganization of Maker, whether in bankruptcy, insolvency or receivership proceedings or upon an assignment for the benefit of creditors, the holders of Senior Indebtedness shall first be entitled to receive payment in full in cash of all Senior Indebtedness before Holder is entitled to receive any payment on account of this Note.

9.4 **Turnover.** If Holder receives any payment on this Note that it is not entitled to receive under this Section 9, Holder shall hold the payment in trust for the benefit of the holders of Senior Indebtedness and shall promptly pay it over to them or their agent.

9.5 **Accrual; Resumption.** Interest shall continue to accrue during any period in which payment is blocked under this Section 9, and any payment not made because of this Section 9 shall be made promptly upon the termination of the blockage period, together with interest at the rate provided in Section 2.1. The failure to make any payment by reason of this Section 9 shall not prevent the occurrence of an Event of Default; provided that Holder shall not exercise any remedy under Section 7 during any blockage period other than the giving of notice.

9.6 **Subordination Agreements.** Holder shall, at Maker's request, execute and deliver to any holder of Senior Indebtedness a subordination agreement in customary form consistent with this Section 9.

9.7 **Obligations Absolute.** The provisions of this Section 9 define the relative rights of Holder and the holders of Senior Indebtedness. Nothing in this Section 9 shall impair, as between Maker and Holder, the obligation of Maker, which is absolute and unconditional, to pay the Obligations in accordance with their terms.

## 10. Governing Law; Jurisdiction

10.1 **Governing Law.** This Note, and all claims or causes of action (whether in contract, tort or statute) that may be based upon, arise out of or relate to this Note, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.

10.2 **Jurisdiction.** Any suit, action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Note shall be brought exclusively in the courts of the State of New York sitting in the County of New York or the United States District Court for the Southern District of New York, and Maker irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of those courts in any such suit, action or proceeding. Maker irrevocably waives any objection to the laying of venue in those courts and any claim that any such suit, action or proceeding has been brought in an inconvenient forum. Service of process by mail to Maker's address for notices under Section 11 shall be effective service of process in any such suit, action or proceeding.

10.3 **Nature of Instrument.** Maker acknowledges that this Note is an instrument for the payment of money only.

10.4 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** MAKER AND HOLDER EACH IRREVOCABLY AND UNCONDITIONALLY WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY APPLICABLE LAW, ANY RIGHT IT MAY HAVE TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUIT, ACTION OR PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS NOTE.

## 11. Notices

All notices, requests, demands and other communications under this Note shall be in writing and shall be deemed given (a) when delivered by hand (with written confirmation of receipt); (b) when received by the addressee if sent by a nationally recognized overnight courier (receipt requested); (c) on the date sent by e-mail of a PDF document (with confirmation of transmission and without notice of non-delivery) if sent during normal business hours of the recipient, and on the next Business Day if sent after normal business hours; or (d) on the third day after the date mailed, by certified or registered mail, return receipt requested, postage prepaid. Notices shall be sent to Maker and to the initial Holder at their respective addresses for notices under the Purchase Agreement, and to any subsequent registered Holder at the address recorded for it in the register maintained under Section 3.5, or in each case at such other address as a party may specify by notice given in accordance with this Section 11.

## 12. Waivers

12.1 **Presentment.** Maker waives presentment for payment, demand, notice of dishonor, protest and notice of protest of this Note, and all other notices in connection with the delivery, acceptance, performance, default or enforcement of this Note, except notices expressly required by this Note.

12.2 **Extensions.** Holder may extend the time of payment of any amount due under this Note, accept partial payments or release any Person liable on this Note, in each case without affecting the liability of Maker.

## 13. Miscellaneous

13.1 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Note may be amended, modified or supplemented only by a written instrument signed by Maker and Holder. No waiver of any provision of this Note shall be effective unless set forth in a writing signed by the party against whom the waiver is to be enforced, and any such waiver shall be effective only in the specific instance and for the specific purpose for which it is given.

13.2 **Successors and Assigns.** This Note shall be binding upon Maker and its successors and permitted assigns and shall inure to the benefit of Holder and its successors and assigns.

13.3 **Severability.** If any provision of this Note is held invalid, illegal or unenforceable in any jurisdiction, that provision shall be ineffective only to the extent of the invalidity, illegality or unenforceability, without affecting the remaining provisions of this Note or the validity of that provision in any other jurisdiction.

13.5 **Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" shall be deemed to be followed by the words "without limitation." The word "or" is not exclusive. References to Sections are to Sections of this Note unless otherwise stated.

13.6 **Cancellation.** After all Obligations have been paid in full, Holder shall surrender this Note to Maker for cancellation, and this Note shall not be reissued.
