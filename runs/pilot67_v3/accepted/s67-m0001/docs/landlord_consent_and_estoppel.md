## 1. Background

1.1 **The Lease.** Furnace Street Holdings, LLC ("**Landlord**"), as landlord, and Tenant, as tenant, are parties to the Industrial Lease dated June 1, 2017, as amended by the First Amendment dated August 15, 2021 (as so amended, the "**Lease**"). The Lease covers approximately 46,500 rentable square feet of industrial space, together with the attached taproom and the adjacent paved yard, at 1180 Furnace Street, Marlow Bend, Oregon 97389 (the "**Premises**").

1.2 **The Transaction.** Landlord understands the following. Braikal Beverage Group, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Tenant. Buyer and Seller have entered into a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of October 2, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under it, Buyer will acquire from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Tenant (the "**Transaction**"). The consummation of the Transaction is the "**Closing**," and the date on which it occurs is the "**Closing Date**."

1.3 **Consent Requirement.** Section 18.2 of the Lease deems a transfer of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting stock of the tenant to be an assignment requiring Landlord's prior written consent. Tenant has asked Landlord to give that consent to the Transaction. Tenant has also asked Landlord to certify certain matters about the Lease for the benefit of Tenant, Buyer and the other Relying Parties.

1.4 **Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this certificate have the meanings given to them in the Lease.

## 2. Consent

2.1 **Consent to the Transaction.** Landlord consents to the Transaction, including the transfer of all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Tenant from Seller to Buyer. Landlord confirms that this consent satisfies Section 18.2 of the Lease with respect to the Transaction.

2.2 **No Assignment of the Lease.** Landlord acknowledges the following:

(a) the Transaction does not involve any assignment of the Lease by Tenant or any sublease of any part of the Premises;

(b) Tenant will remain the tenant under the Lease after the Closing; and

(c) the Lease will continue in full force and effect after the Closing in accordance with its terms, without modification by reason of the Transaction.

2.3 **No Consent Fee; No Conditions.** Landlord's consent is unconditional except as stated in Section 2.4. Landlord does not require any of the following in connection with its consent:

(a) any consent fee, transfer premium, profit-sharing payment, increase in rent, or reimbursement of Landlord's costs;

(b) any additional or replacement security deposit or letter of credit; or

(c) any guaranty of the Lease by Buyer or any other Person.

2.4 **Effective Time of Consent.** This consent is effective as of the date of this certificate. It applies to the Transaction if the Closing occurs on or before April 30, 2027. If the Closing has not occurred by that date, this consent shall lapse, and Tenant may request a new consent under Section 18.2 of the Lease.

2.5 **Future Transfers.** This consent applies only to the Transaction. It does not waive Section 18.2 of the Lease, or Landlord's other rights under the Lease, with respect to any future assignment, sublease or transfer of stock or other interests. The following, however, shall not require any further consent of Landlord in connection with the Transaction:

(a) any change in the directors or officers of Tenant at or after the Closing;

(b) any pledge by Buyer of the shares of Tenant, or any grant by Tenant of a security interest in its personal property at the Premises, to any lender providing financing to Buyer or Tenant at the Closing; and

(c) any change in Tenant's name to another name that includes the word "Copperwake."

## 3. Estoppel Certifications

Landlord certifies to Tenant, Buyer and each other Relying Party that, as of the date of this certificate:

3.1 **Lease Documents.** The Lease is in full force and effect. The documents listed in **Schedule A** are true, correct and complete copies of all of the documents comprising the Lease, and they constitute the entire agreement between Landlord and Tenant concerning the Premises. The Lease has not been modified, supplemented or amended in any way except as listed in Schedule A. There are no side letters, oral agreements or other understandings between Landlord and Tenant concerning the Premises or the Lease.

3.2 **Term.** The current term of the Lease commenced on June 1, 2017. The initial term expires on May 31, 2032. Tenant has two (2) options to extend the term of the Lease, each for a period of five (5) years, on the terms set out in the Lease. Neither option has been exercised or has lapsed. No event has occurred that would cause either option to become void or unexercisable.

3.3 **Rent.**

(a) The current monthly base rent payable under the Lease is $55,800.00. Base rent increases by three percent (3%) on each June 1 during the term, as provided in the Lease. The next increase is scheduled for June 1, 2027.

(b) Tenant pays, as additional rent, its share of real property taxes, insurance and common area operating expenses in accordance with the Lease. Tenant's current estimated monthly payment of that additional rent is $11,160.00.

(c) Base rent and estimated additional rent have been paid through November 30, 2026. No rent has been paid more than one (1) month in advance.

(d) The reconciliation of additional rent for calendar year 2025 has been completed and settled. No amount is owing by either party in respect of it. Landlord is not aware of any fact that would give rise to a material adjustment of additional rent for calendar year 2026, other than ordinary year-end reconciliation.

3.4 **Security Deposit.** Tenant has deposited with Landlord a cash security deposit of $111,600.00. Landlord holds the full amount of that deposit. Landlord has not applied any part of it to any obligation of Tenant. No letter of credit has been delivered under the Lease.

3.5 **No Defaults.** To Landlord's knowledge:

(a) Tenant is not in default under the Lease;

(b) no event has occurred which, with the giving of notice or the passage of time or both, would constitute a default by Tenant under the Lease; and

(c) Landlord has not delivered to Tenant any notice of default under the Lease that remains uncured.

Landlord is not in default under the Lease. Landlord has not received from Tenant any notice of default by Landlord that remains uncured.

3.6 **No Claims or Offsets.** To Landlord's knowledge, Tenant has no defense, offset, counterclaim or credit against the rent or other amounts payable under the Lease. Landlord has no claim against Tenant under the Lease for any amount, other than rent and additional rent not yet due and payable.

3.7 **Allowances and Landlord Work.** All construction work and improvements that Landlord was required to perform under the Lease have been completed. All tenant improvement allowances, refurbishment allowances or other payments that Landlord was required to make to Tenant under the Lease have been paid in full. There are no free rent periods, rent abatements or rent concessions remaining under the Lease.

3.8 **Alterations.** Landlord has approved, or the Lease permits without Landlord's approval, all alterations, additions and improvements made by Tenant to the Premises before the date of this certificate. These include the installation of the brewhouse, fermentation and conditioning vessels, glycol and refrigeration systems, boiler, packaging equipment and canning line, and the build-out of the taproom. Landlord does not require Tenant to remove any of them at the end of the term except as provided in the Lease.

3.9 **Permitted Use.** The Premises may be used under the Lease for the following purposes:

(a) the brewing, fermentation, packaging, storage and wholesale distribution of beer, hard seltzer and other malt beverages;

(b) contract brewing, co-packing and alternating proprietorship operations for third parties;

(c) the operation of a taproom, including the retail sale of beverages for on-premises and off-premises consumption, food service, merchandise sales, tours and private events; and

(d) related office and ancillary uses.

Landlord has not given Tenant any notice that Tenant's current use or operation of the Premises violates the Lease.

3.10 **Third-Party Occupancy.** Landlord acknowledges that the following arrangements are in effect at the Premises, as Tenant has disclosed to Landlord:

(a) the Alternating Proprietorship Agreement dated August 1, 2023 between Tenant and Ochre Owl Brewing, LLC, under which Ochre Owl operates a separately bonded alternating premises at the brewery under its own Brewer's Notice; and

(b) Tenant's contract brewing and co-packing arrangements with third-party customers.

Landlord agrees that none of those arrangements is a sublease, assignment or other transfer requiring Landlord's consent under the Lease. Landlord also agrees that none of them gives rise to any default by Tenant under the Lease.

3.11 **Options and Rights of Others.** Landlord has not granted to any Person other than Tenant any option to purchase, right of first refusal, right of first offer or other right to purchase or lease all or any part of the Premises. Landlord has not granted any right to occupy any part of the Premises that is in effect during the term of the Lease.

3.12 **Tenant's Rights.** Tenant has a right of first offer to lease approximately 8,000 square feet of adjacent yard space if Landlord elects to make it available, as provided in the First Amendment. That right remains in full force and effect. Landlord has not made that space available to any other Person.

3.13 **Mortgage.** The Premises are encumbered by a deed of trust in favor of Willamette Trust Bank, N.A. (the "**Mortgagee**"). That deed of trust secures a loan to Landlord. The Mortgagee's consent to the Transaction is not required under its loan documents or the Lease. Any subordination, non-disturbance and attornment agreement entered into with Tenant remains in effect in accordance with its terms.

3.14 **Condemnation and Sale.** Landlord has not received any notice of any pending or threatened condemnation or eminent domain proceeding affecting the Premises. Landlord has not entered into any contract to sell, transfer or ground lease the Premises.

3.15 **Guaranty.** No guaranty of Tenant's obligations under the Lease is in effect.

3.16 **Brokers.** No leasing commission or broker's fee is payable by Tenant to any Person in connection with the Lease or this certificate.

## 4. Landlord Waiver of Interest in Tenant's Personal Property

4.1 **Personal Property.** Landlord acknowledges the following:

(a) the brewing, cellar, refrigeration and packaging equipment, the canning line and its components and spare parts, the taproom furniture, fixtures and equipment, inventory, raw materials, finished goods, kegs and other trade fixtures and personal property located at the Premises (the "**Tenant Property**") are the property of Tenant, or of the third parties from whom Tenant leases or uses them; and

(b) the Tenant Property does not become part of the real property or the property of Landlord by reason of being affixed to the Premises.

4.2 **No Lien.** Landlord does not claim, and waives, any lien, security interest, right of distraint or levy or other interest in the Tenant Property, whether arising under the Lease, by statute or otherwise. This waiver does not limit Landlord's rights against Tenant under the Lease, including its right to require removal of Tenant Property and restoration of the Premises at the end of the term as provided in the Lease.

4.3 **Transfers of Personal Property.** Landlord acknowledges that the canning line installed at the Premises may be transferred to Tenant at or before the Closing. Landlord agrees that this transfer requires no consent of Landlord, and that no notice to Landlord is required. Landlord also agrees that the transfer does not affect the status of the canning line as Tenant Property under Section 4.1.

4.4 **Lender Access.** If a lender to Buyer or Tenant holding a security interest in the Tenant Property (a "**Secured Lender**") gives Landlord written notice of its security interest, Landlord shall give that Secured Lender a copy of any notice of default it gives Tenant under the Lease. The Secured Lender may enter the Premises, on reasonable prior notice to Landlord, during the period of ninety (90) days after any termination of the Lease or Tenant's right of possession. The purpose of entry is solely to remove the Tenant Property in which it holds a security interest. The Secured Lender shall:

(a) repair any damage to the Premises caused by the removal;

(b) pay Landlord the per diem base rent and additional rent under the Lease for each day it occupies the Premises for that purpose after the termination; and

(c) maintain commercial general liability insurance naming Landlord as additional insured while on the Premises.

The Secured Lender shall have no obligation to cure any default of Tenant or to perform Tenant's other obligations under the Lease.

## 5. Notices After the Closing

5.1 **Tenant Notice Address.** From and after the Closing Date, Landlord shall send all notices to Tenant under the Lease to Tenant at the Premises, Attention: General Manager. Landlord shall send a copy of each notice of default to Buyer at the address for Buyer's notices under the Purchase Agreement, Attention: General Counsel. Tenant may change these addresses by notice to Landlord in accordance with the Lease.

5.2 **Landlord Notice Address.** Notices to Landlord under the Lease shall continue to be sent to the address for Landlord set out in the Lease, until Landlord designates another address by notice to Tenant.

5.3 **Closing Notice.** Tenant shall notify Landlord in writing of the Closing Date within ten (10) Business Days after the Closing. In this certificate, "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday in the State of Oregon.

5.4 **Insurance Certificates.** Within thirty (30) days after the Closing Date, Tenant shall deliver to Landlord updated certificates of the insurance that the Lease requires Tenant to carry, naming Landlord, and the Mortgagee if the Lease so requires, as additional insureds.

Landlord represents to Tenant, Buyer and each other Relying Party that:

(a) Landlord is the fee owner of the Premises and the landlord under the Lease;

(b) Landlord has not assigned, transferred or pledged its interest as landlord under the Lease, except for the deed of trust and related assignment of leases and rents in favor of the Mortgagee;

(c) Landlord has full power and authority to execute and deliver this certificate and to give the consent in Section 2, and no consent of any other Person is required for it to do so; and

(d) the individual signing this certificate on behalf of Landlord is duly authorized to do so.

## 8. Effect of Certificate

8.1 **Binding Effect.** This certificate is binding on Landlord and its successors and assigns, including any successor owner of the Premises and any purchaser at foreclosure or by deed in lieu of foreclosure.

8.2 **Estoppel.** Landlord shall be estopped from asserting against any Relying Party any claim, default or fact inconsistent with the certifications in Section 3, to the extent that the Relying Party relied on those certifications in good faith.

8.3 **No Amendment.** Except for the consent given in Section 2 and the waiver and agreements in Section 4, this certificate does not amend, modify or waive any provision of the Lease. It does not relieve Tenant of any obligation under the Lease. If the certifications in Section 3 differ from the Lease, Landlord is bound by them as provided in Section 8.2 as against the Relying Parties. As between Landlord and Tenant, the Lease continues in accordance with its terms.

8.4 **Knowledge.** Where a certification in this certificate is made "to Landlord's knowledge," it refers to the actual knowledge of Landlord's managing member and its property manager for the Premises, after review of Landlord's lease files for the Premises. It does not imply any duty to make any physical inspection of the Premises.

## 9. Reliance

9.1 **Relying Parties.** Landlord acknowledges that each of the following (each, a "**Relying Party**") will rely on this certificate in connection with the Transaction and any related financing, and may do so:

(a) Tenant;

(b) Buyer and its Affiliates;

(c) any lender providing financing to Buyer or Tenant in connection with the Transaction, and any agent for those lenders; and

(d) the successors and permitted assigns of each of them.

In this certificate, "**Affiliate**" of a Person means any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Person.

9.2 **Seller.** Seller is not a Relying Party for purposes of this certificate.

10.1 **Governing Law.** This certificate is governed by the laws of the State of Oregon, which govern the Lease.

10.2 **Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This certificate may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together shall constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by e-mail in PDF format or by an electronic signature service complying with the U.S. federal ESIGN Act of 2000 and the Oregon Uniform Electronic Transactions Act shall be effective as delivery of an original signature.

10.3 **Headings.** Headings in this certificate are for convenience of reference only and do not affect its interpretation.

10.4 **Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" shall be deemed to be followed by the words "without limitation." The word "or" is not exclusive.

10.5 **Severability.** If any provision of this certificate is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.
