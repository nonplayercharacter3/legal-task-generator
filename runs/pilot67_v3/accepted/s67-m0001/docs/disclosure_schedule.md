## SECTION 9.2(d)
### SPECIFIED MATTERS

**Specified Matter 1 — Marlow Bend Wastewater Notice of Violation**

(a) *Description.* Notice of Violation No. NOV-2026-031 dated March 18, 2026, issued by the City Wastewater Division to the Company under the Discharge Permit and Chapter 13.20 of the Marlow Bend Municipal Code. The notice alleges that the Company's brewery wastewater discharge to the City's publicly owned treatment works exceeded the permitted BOD local limit of 1,200 mg/L on eleven sampling dates between August 2025 and February 2026. Measured concentrations ranged up to 3,850 mg/L. The notice also includes all related surcharges, administrative penalties, enforcement actions and compliance obligations, whether arising before or after the Closing, to the extent they relate to discharges occurring on or before the Closing Date or to the compliance schedule described in paragraph (b).

(b) *Compliance Schedule.* By letter dated May 6, 2026, the City Wastewater Division proposed a compliance schedule. Under it, the Company would design, permit, install and commission an on-site wastewater pretreatment system capable of consistently meeting the BOD local limit by June 30, 2027, and pay interim high-strength surcharges until commissioning. The Company submitted a response and preliminary design narrative on June 19, 2026. The compliance schedule has not yet been finalized [DR 6.2.3 to 6.2.6].

(c) *Estimated Cost.* The Company's engineering consultant, Larkspur Water Engineering, PLLC, estimated in its report dated July 28, 2026 that the cost to design, permit, construct and commission the pretreatment system is $3,100,000. That figure excludes interim surcharges and any administrative penalty [DR 6.2.7].

(d) *Recovery.* Losses arising out of Specified Matter 1 shall be recoverable by the Buyer Indemnitees solely from the Indemnity Escrow Fund.
