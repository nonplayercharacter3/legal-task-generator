# BILL OF SALE

**(Canning Line)**

This BILL OF SALE (this "**Bill of Sale**") is made, executed and delivered as of the Closing Date (as defined in the Purchase Agreement referred to below) by **Braikal Beverage Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), to and in favor of **Copperwake Brewing Company**, an Oregon corporation (the "**Company**"). **Tinav Beverage Partners, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), acknowledges it for the purposes stated in Section 12.

## RECITALS

A. Seller and Buyer have entered into a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of October 2, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under it, Buyer is acquiring from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company.

B. Seller owns the canning line installed at the Company's brewery at 1180 Furnace Street, Marlow Bend, Oregon 97389 (the "**Brewery**"). Since its installation in June 2021, the Company has used the canning line in the Business under the unwritten equipment use arrangement described in item 3 of Section 4.19 of the Disclosure Schedule (the "**Equipment Use Arrangement**").

C. Section 6.10 of the Purchase Agreement requires Seller to execute and deliver this Bill of Sale at or before the Closing. Seller must also take all other actions reasonably necessary to vest in the Company all right, title and interest in the canning line, free and clear of all Encumbrances.

D. This Bill of Sale is the "Bill of Sale" referred to in the Purchase Agreement and is an Ancillary Agreement under it.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Bill of Sale and in the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, Seller agrees as follows:

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 **Purchase Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Bill of Sale have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. These include "Action," "Affiliate," "Business," "Business Day," "Closing," "Closing Date," "Contracts," "Disclosure Schedule," "Encumbrance," "Governmental Authority," "Law," "Person" and "Tax."

1.2 **Defined Terms.** In this Bill of Sale:

"**Canning Line**" means the integrated aluminum can packaging line installed at the Brewery and described in Part A of Schedule 1. It includes each of the following, wherever located and whether or not affixed to the Brewery premises:

(a) each machine, module and component listed in Part A of Schedule 1;

(b) all conveyors, guide rails, transfer tables, accumulation tables, change parts, format parts, seamer chucks, rolls and tooling, filling valves, CO2 and nitrogen dosing equipment, and lid and end feeding equipment used with or held for use with the line;

(c) all programmable logic controllers, human-machine interfaces, servo drives, sensors, vision and fill-level inspection systems, electrical cabinets, wiring and control hardware forming part of the line;

(d) all spare parts, consumable wear parts and maintenance kits for the line, including those listed in Part B of Schedule 1, wherever stored;

(e) all accessions, attachments, additions, replacements and improvements to any of the foregoing; and

(f) the Line Documentation and the Line Software, to the extent provided in Section 2.1.

"**Effective Time**" means the time at which the Closing is deemed effective under Section 3.1 of the Purchase Agreement.

"**Line Contracts**" means the Contracts listed in Part C of Schedule 1, and any other warranty, service, support or parts supply arrangement with the manufacturer, integrator or any supplier of the Canning Line or of any of its components, to the extent the arrangement relates to the Canning Line.

"**Line Documentation**" means all operating manuals, maintenance manuals, electrical and pneumatic schematics, parts lists, drawings, installation and commissioning records, factory and site acceptance test reports, calibration records, seam inspection records, maintenance and repair logs, safety and lockout/tagout procedures, risk assessments, CE or UL declarations, and other technical documentation relating to the Canning Line, in any form or medium. This includes documentation held by Seller, its Affiliates or any third party on Seller's behalf.

"**Line Software**" means all firmware, PLC and HMI programs, recipe and format parameter files, source and object code to the extent in Seller's possession or control, backups, configuration files, licenses and license keys used to operate, control or monitor the Canning Line.

1.3 **Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" shall be deemed to be followed by the words "without limitation." The word "or" is not exclusive. References to Sections and Schedules are to Sections of and Schedules to this Bill of Sale unless otherwise stated. Headings are for reference only and do not affect interpretation.

## 2. Conveyance

2.1 **Transfer of the Canning Line.** Effective as of the Effective Time, Seller hereby irrevocably and unconditionally sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to the Company all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under the following (together, the "**Transferred Assets**"), free and clear of all Encumbrances. The Company hereby accepts them.

(a) the Canning Line;

(b) the Line Documentation;

(c) the Line Software, including all of Seller's rights under any license for it, to the extent those rights are transferable;

(d) the Line Contracts, to the extent assignable, subject to Section 5;

(e) all rights under any express or implied warranty, representation, indemnity or guarantee given by any manufacturer, integrator, vendor, installer or service provider with respect to the Canning Line or any part of it, to the extent assignable; and

(f) all claims, causes of action, rights of recovery and rights of set-off of Seller against any third party relating to the Canning Line, including in respect of any defect, damage or loss, and all insurance proceeds received or receivable by Seller in respect of any damage to or loss of the Canning Line occurring before the Effective Time and not repaired or replaced before the Effective Time.

2.2 **Consideration.** The transfer under Section 2.1 is made as part of the transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement and is supported by the consideration paid under it. Neither the Company nor Buyer shall pay any additional consideration for the Transferred Assets.

2.3 **No Assumption of Liabilities.** The Company does not assume, and shall not be responsible for, any liability or obligation of Seller or any of its Affiliates of any kind, whether known or unknown, accrued or contingent. This includes any liability or obligation:

(a) arising out of Seller's acquisition, financing, ownership or installation of the Canning Line;

(b) under any purchase agreement, financing arrangement, lease or security agreement relating to the Canning Line;

(c) for any amount payable under any Line Contract for any period before the Effective Time; or

(d) for any Tax of Seller or its Affiliates, including any personal property Tax assessed against Seller in respect of the Canning Line for any period ending before the Effective Time.

Seller shall pay, perform and discharge all such liabilities and obligations when due.

2.4 **Title and Risk.** Title to and risk of loss of the Transferred Assets pass to the Company at the Effective Time.

## 3. Delivery and Possession

3.1 **Delivery in Place.** The parties acknowledge that the Canning Line is installed at the Brewery and is in the possession of the Company. Delivery of the Canning Line shall be effected in place at the Brewery at the Effective Time. No physical removal, relocation or reinstallation is required. Seller shall not enter the Brewery to remove, disconnect, alter or inspect the Canning Line after the Effective Time.

3.2 **Spare Parts Held Elsewhere.** If any spare parts, change parts, tooling or other items forming part of the Canning Line are held at any location other than the Brewery or the warehouse premises at 77 Kiln Road, Unit C, Marlow Bend, Oregon 97389, Seller shall, at its own expense, deliver them to the Brewery within ten (10) Business Days after the Closing Date. Seller bears the risk of loss of those items until delivery.

3.3 **Line Documentation.** At or before the Effective Time, Seller shall deliver to the Company all Line Documentation in Seller's or its Affiliates' possession or control that is not already located at the Brewery. Delivery shall be made in native electronic format where available, and otherwise in printed or PDF form. Seller may retain one archival copy of the Line Documentation solely to the extent required by applicable Law or by Seller's bona fide record retention policies. Any retained copy shall be used only for those purposes.

3.4 **Line Software.** At or before the Effective Time, Seller shall deliver to the Company the following:

(a) current backups of all PLC and HMI programs, recipe and format files and configuration files for the Canning Line;

(b) all passwords, administrator credentials and access codes needed to operate, program and maintain the Canning Line; and

(c) all license keys and license certificates for the Line Software.

Seller shall remove all remote-access credentials held by Seller or its Affiliates' personnel, and shall disable any remote-access connection to the Canning Line's control system that runs to any network of Seller or its Affiliates.

## 4. Condition of the Canning Line

4.1 **Ordinary Operation.** Seller acknowledges that the Company has operated and maintained the Canning Line in the ordinary course since its installation under the Equipment Use Arrangement. Seller has made the Canning Line available to Buyer for inspection under Section 6.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

4.2 **Purchase Agreement Representations.** The Canning Line is a tangible asset of the Company for purposes of Section 4.9(b) of the Purchase Agreement from and after the Effective Time. The representations and warranties of Seller in the Purchase Agreement, including Sections 4.9(b), 4.9(c) and 4.19, apply to the Canning Line in accordance with their terms. Nothing in this Bill of Sale limits those representations and warranties or the rights of the Buyer Indemnitees under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

## 5. Line Contracts; Non-Assignable Rights

5.1 **Assignment of Line Contracts.** To the extent any Line Contract or warranty right can be assigned without the consent of a third party, it is assigned to the Company under Section 2.1 as of the Effective Time. Seller shall give each counterparty to a Line Contract written notice of the assignment within five (5) Business Days after the Closing Date, in a form reasonably acceptable to the Company. The notice shall instruct the counterparty to deal with the Company directly from and after the Effective Time.

5.2 **Consents.** If any Line Contract, warranty right or license for any Line Software cannot be assigned without a third party's consent, and that consent has not been obtained by the Effective Time, then:

(a) this Bill of Sale does not constitute an assignment of that item if an attempted assignment would breach it or be ineffective;

(b) Seller shall continue to use commercially reasonable efforts, at its own expense, to obtain the consent as promptly as practicable after the Closing Date;

(c) until the consent is obtained, Seller shall hold the item for the exclusive benefit of the Company and shall enforce it at the Company's direction and for the Company's account. Seller shall pass through to the Company all benefits under it, including services, parts, warranty repairs, software updates and amounts recovered; and

(d) when the consent is obtained, the item shall be automatically assigned to the Company under Section 2.1 without further action.

5.3 **Costs.** Seller shall bear any consent fee, transfer fee, relicensing fee or similar charge imposed by any manufacturer, integrator, licensor or service provider in connection with the assignment of any Line Contract or Line Software license to the Company.

## 6. Termination of Equipment Use Arrangement

6.1 **Termination.** Effective as of the Effective Time, the Equipment Use Arrangement is terminated. This is consistent with item 3 of Section 4.19 of the Disclosure Schedule and Section 6.11 of the Purchase Agreement.

6.2 **Release.** Seller, for itself and its Affiliates, releases the Company from all claims, rent, usage fees, reimbursements, maintenance charges and other amounts arising under or in connection with the Equipment Use Arrangement or the Company's use of the Canning Line before the Effective Time. Seller also releases the Company from any claim relating to the Canning Line's condition, wear, modification or maintenance during that period. Any amount owed by the Company to Seller for the use of the Canning Line and recorded in the intercompany payable described in item 4 of Section 4.19 of the Disclosure Schedule shall be settled or capitalized as provided in that item. It shall not otherwise give rise to any claim against the Company.

## 7. Encumbrances and Filings

7.1 **Releases.** At or before the Effective Time, Seller shall deliver to the Company evidence reasonably satisfactory to Buyer of the release of any Encumbrance on any of the Transferred Assets. This includes UCC-3 termination statements for any financing statement naming Seller or any of its Affiliates as debtor that covers any of the Transferred Assets, and any lien release required by any lender of Seller or its Affiliates.

7.2 **Authorization to File.** Seller authorizes the Company and Buyer, and any of their counsel, to file in any filing office any UCC-3 termination or amendment statement needed to reflect the release of any Encumbrance on the Transferred Assets that Seller has failed to file within ten (10) Business Days after the Closing Date.

7.3 **Fixture Matters.** To the extent any part of the Canning Line is or may be considered a fixture at the Brewery, Seller confirms that it has not granted any Person a fixture filing or other interest in the Canning Line. Seller also confirms that, as between Seller and the Company, the Canning Line is personal property owned by the Company from and after the Effective Time.

## 8. Representations and Warranties of Seller

Seller represents and warrants to the Company and Buyer that:

(a) Seller has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Bill of Sale. This Bill of Sale has been duly authorized, executed and delivered by Seller and constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller enforceable in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable bankruptcy, insolvency and similar Laws and general principles of equity;

(b) Seller has good and marketable title to the Transferred Assets, free and clear of all Encumbrances. No Person other than Seller and, under the Equipment Use Arrangement, the Company has any right, title or interest in or to any of the Transferred Assets;

(c) upon delivery of this Bill of Sale, the Company will have good and marketable title to the Transferred Assets, free and clear of all Encumbrances;

(d) no Affiliate of Seller owns or holds any interest in any equipment, part, tooling, software or documentation used in the operation of the Canning Line;

(e) Schedule 1 is a true and complete description of the principal machines and components of the Canning Line, the spare parts inventory for it as of September 30, 2026, and the Line Contracts; and

(f) the execution, delivery and performance of this Bill of Sale by Seller do not conflict with or result in a breach of Seller's certificate of incorporation or bylaws, any Contract to which Seller is a party, or any Law applicable to Seller.

These representations and warranties are made in addition to, and not in substitution for, the representations and warranties of Seller in the Purchase Agreement.

## 9. Further Assurances

9.1 **General.** At and after the Effective Time, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, without further consideration, execute and deliver such additional bills of sale, assignments, releases, certificates and other instruments, and take such further actions, as the Company or Buyer may reasonably request. The purpose is to vest in the Company, perfect and confirm its title to the Transferred Assets, and enable the Company to enjoy their full use and benefit.

9.2 **Assets Received.** If Seller or any of its Affiliates receives any Transferred Asset, or any payment, refund, credit, part or service relating to a Transferred Asset, after the Effective Time, Seller shall hold it in trust for the Company. Seller shall deliver it to the Company within ten (10) Business Days after receipt.

9.3 **Correspondence.** Seller shall forward to the Company promptly, and in any event within five (5) Business Days after receipt, any notice, invoice, service bulletin, safety notice, recall notice or other correspondence relating to the Canning Line that it or any of its Affiliates receives after the Effective Time.

9.4 **Power of Attorney.** Seller irrevocably appoints the Company, and any officer of the Company, as Seller's attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, for one limited purpose. That purpose is to execute and deliver, in Seller's name, any instrument described in Section 9.1 that Seller fails to execute and deliver within ten (10) Business Days after the Company's written request, and to demand and receive any Transferred Asset. This power of attorney is coupled with an interest and survives the dissolution, liquidation or bankruptcy of Seller.

## 10. Taxes

10.1 **Transfer Taxes.** Any transfer, documentary, sales, use or similar Taxes arising from the transfer of the Transferred Assets under this Bill of Sale shall be borne and paid by Seller as provided in Section 8.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

10.2 **Property Taxes.** Seller shall file all personal property Tax returns and pay all personal property Taxes assessed against Seller in respect of the Canning Line for any assessment period ending before the Effective Time. For any period that begins before and ends after the Effective Time, Seller shall bear the portion of those Taxes attributable to the period before the Effective Time. That portion is determined pro rata on a daily basis. Seller and the Company shall cooperate in notifying the Lane County Assessor of the change in ownership of the Canning Line.

## 11. Effectiveness

11.1 **Effectiveness.** This Bill of Sale is executed and delivered at the Closing and becomes effective at the Effective Time.

11.2 **Termination of Purchase Agreement.** If this Bill of Sale is executed in escrow before the Closing and the Purchase Agreement is terminated in accordance with its terms before the Closing, this Bill of Sale shall be void and of no further force or effect. In that case any document delivered in escrow under it shall be returned to Seller, and the Equipment Use Arrangement shall continue as in effect immediately before the date of this Bill of Sale.

## 12. Acceptance by the Company; Buyer Acknowledgment

12.1 **Company.** The Company executes this Bill of Sale to evidence its acceptance of the Transferred Assets and its agreement to Sections 2.3, 6 and 10.2.

12.2 **Buyer.** Buyer executes this Bill of Sale solely to acknowledge it and to take the benefit of Sections 7, 8, 9 and 13.6. Buyer is an intended third-party beneficiary of those Sections and may enforce them directly against Seller. Buyer assumes no obligation under this Bill of Sale.

## 13. Miscellaneous

13.1 **Governing Law.** This Bill of Sale, and all claims or causes of action (whether in contract, tort or statute) that may be based upon, arise out of or relate to it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal Laws of the State of Delaware. No choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction shall apply. Matters of perfection, priority and filing of any Encumbrance on the Transferred Assets are governed by the Law of the applicable jurisdiction.

13.2 **Jurisdiction.** Any legal suit, Action or proceeding arising out of or based upon this Bill of Sale shall be instituted exclusively in the Delaware Court of Chancery. Solely if the Delaware Court of Chancery declines to accept or lacks subject matter jurisdiction, it shall be instituted in the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware. Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of those courts in any such suit, Action or proceeding. This Section 13.2 does not prevent the Company from making any filing with any filing office or Governmental Authority described in Section 7 or 10.2.

13.3 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY AND UNCONDITIONALLY WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY APPLICABLE LAW, ANY RIGHT IT MAY HAVE TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUIT, ACTION OR PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS BILL OF SALE.

13.4 **Notices.** All notices under this Bill of Sale shall be given in the manner provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement. Notices to Seller shall be sent to the address for Seller under the Purchase Agreement. Notices to the Company and to Buyer shall be sent to the address for Buyer under the Purchase Agreement. Notices to the Company shall be marked for the attention of the General Manager of the Company.

13.5 **Successors and Assigns.** This Bill of Sale is binding upon and inures to the benefit of Seller, the Company and Buyer and their respective successors and assigns. The Company may assign its rights under this Bill of Sale, together with any of the Transferred Assets, without the consent of Seller. Permitted assignees are Buyer, any Affiliate of Buyer, any lender as collateral, and any acquirer of all or any part of the Business. Seller may not assign this Bill of Sale or delegate any of its obligations under it without the prior written consent of the Company.

13.6 **Equitable Relief.** Seller acknowledges that the Canning Line is integral to the Company's packaging operations, including its co-packing services described in Section 4.7(b)(3) of the Disclosure Schedule. A breach or threatened breach of Sections 3, 5, 7 or 9 would cause the Company irreparable harm for which monetary damages would not be an adequate remedy. If such a breach or threatened breach occurs, the Company and Buyer shall be entitled to equitable relief, including specific performance and injunctive relief, without any requirement to post bond. This relief is in addition to any other remedy available to them.

13.7 **Cumulative Remedies.** The rights and remedies of the Company and Buyer under this Bill of Sale are cumulative with their rights and remedies under the Purchase Agreement and at law or in equity.

13.8 **Amendment; Waiver.** This Bill of Sale may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Seller and the Company and acknowledged by Buyer. No waiver shall be effective unless set forth in a writing signed by the party against whom it is to be enforced.

13.9 **Severability.** If any provision of this Bill of Sale is held invalid, illegal or unenforceable in any jurisdiction, that provision shall be ineffective only to that extent. The remaining provisions of this Bill of Sale, and the validity of that provision in any other jurisdiction, are not affected.

13.10 **Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Bill of Sale may be executed in counterparts. Each counterpart shall be deemed an original, and all of them together shall constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by e-mail in PDF format or by an electronic signature service complying with the U.S. federal ESIGN Act of 2000 shall be effective as delivery of an original signature.
