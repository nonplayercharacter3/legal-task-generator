## 4. Commercial Property

4.1 **Blanket Limit.** The Commercial Property policy provides a single blanket limit of $22,000,000 for building improvements and betterments, business personal property, stock and inventory (including finished goods valued at selling price), and business income and extra expense at both insured locations. The blanket limit is based on the Statement of Values (Enclosure 5). That statement shows total insurable values of approximately $21,400,000. It is subject to a 90% coinsurance clause, which is waived by an agreed value endorsement that is effective through the expiration of the current policy period.

4.2 **Equipment Breakdown.** Equipment breakdown coverage is included within the blanket limit. It covers the brewhouse, fermentation and conditioning vessels, glycol chillers, boiler, compressors, packaging equipment and electrical distribution equipment at the brewery. Spoilage of product in process caused by mechanical breakdown or loss of refrigeration is covered subject to a sublimit of $500,000 per occurrence.

4.3 **Business Income.** Business income and extra expense coverage applies on an actual loss sustained basis for up to twelve months. It includes a 60-day extended period of indemnity. Contingent business income coverage for dependent properties, including key suppliers, applies subject to a sublimit of $250,000.

4.4 **Property of Others.** The Statement of Values includes, as property of others in the Company's care, custody or control, the canning line installed at the brewery. Our records show that this canning line is currently owned by Braikal and used by the Company. The policy's property-of-others provision covers it at replacement cost, and it is included within the blanket limit. We understand from the Company that the canning line is to be transferred to the Company at the closing under a bill of sale. Once you confirm that the transfer is complete, we will ask Granite Plains to re-classify the canning line as business personal property of the named insured. We do not expect a change in premium, because the line is already in the declared values.

4.5 **Notable Sublimits and Exclusions.** The policy includes the following sublimits, among others:

(a) flood: $1,000,000 annual aggregate (the insured locations are outside the FEMA Special Flood Hazard Area);

(b) earth movement: $2,500,000 annual aggregate, subject to a deductible of 5% of total insurable values at the affected location, with a minimum of $100,000;

(c) accounts receivable and valuable papers: $250,000 each; and

(d) electronic data: $100,000.

The policy contains customary exclusions for wear and tear, gradual deterioration, contamination (other than spoilage as described in Section 4.2) and governmental action.

4.6 **Mortgagee and Loss Payee.** The Company's secured lender is endorsed on the Commercial Property policy as lender loss payee. We understand that the Company's credit facility is to be repaid and its security interests released at the closing. Once you send us the payoff letter or release, we will ask Granite Plains to delete the lender loss payee endorsement. We will also add any new lender loss payee that Tinav designates.

| Limit | Amount |

5.2 **Additional Insureds.** The policy contains blanket additional insured endorsements for the following categories of Persons:

(a) landlords of premises leased to the named insured. This currently includes Furnace Street Holdings, LLC for the brewery and taproom and Ridgeline Logistics Park, LLC for the warehouse;

(b) vendors of the named insured's products, on the ISO CG 20 15 broad form vendors endorsement. This includes the Company's wholesale distributors;

(c) Persons with whom the named insured has agreed in a written contract to provide additional insured status, including contract brewing and co-packing customers; and

(d) the named insured's parent organization, as noted in Section 2.1.

Additional insured coverage under clauses (a) through (c) is primary and non-contributory where the underlying written contract requires it. Each of these endorsements includes a waiver of subrogation where required by written contract.

5.3 **Liquor Liability.** The Liquor Liability policy covers the Company's taproom and on-premises retail operations, including tastings and special events at the insured locations. It also covers off-premises events for which the Company holds a temporary or special event permit. The policy is written on an occurrence basis. It includes assault and battery coverage at the full policy limits.

5.4 **Open Claims.** The loss run for the General Liability policy (Enclosure 6) shows one open claim. It is the premises liability claim captioned *Harlan v. Copperwake Brewing Company*, Lane County Circuit Court Case No. 26CV19044, arising from a slip-and-fall in the taproom on January 11, 2026. Granite Plains has accepted the tender of that claim under a reservation of rights and has appointed defense counsel. The claim is reserved within policy limits. There are no other open claims under the General Liability or Liquor Liability policies.

## 6. Product Contamination and Recall

6.1 **Coverage Scope.** The Product Contamination and Recall policy issued by Lindmark Specialty Insurance Company covers the following, each arising from accidental contamination, malicious product tampering or governmental recall of the Company's products:

(a) recall expenses, including notification, shipping, disposal and replacement costs;

(b) the insured's own lost gross profit for up to twelve months;

(c) rehabilitation expenses; and

(d) third-party recall costs incurred by distributors and retailers.

The limit is $2,000,000 in the aggregate, subject to a $50,000 deductible per insured event.

6.2 **Scope of Products.** The policy covers all products manufactured, packaged or distributed by the named insured. These include beer, hard seltzer and flavored malt beverages sold under the COPPERWAKE brands. The policy also covers products manufactured or packaged by the named insured for third parties under contract brewing, co-packing and alternating proprietorship arrangements, but only to the extent the named insured is legally liable or contractually responsible for the recall costs. Products of a tenant brewer that are produced under that tenant brewer's own Brewer's Notice and quality control are not covered unless the contamination results from the named insured's own equipment or operations.

6.3 **Crisis Consultant.** The policy includes access, without erosion of the limit, to the insurer's designated crisis management consultant for the first 72 hours after notice of an insured event.

## 7. Commercial Auto and Workers' Compensation

7.1 **Commercial Auto.** The Commercial Auto policy covers the Company's scheduled vehicles. These are two refrigerated box trucks used for self-distribution and event delivery, one cargo van and one pickup. Liability coverage applies to symbol 1 (any auto). Hired and non-owned auto liability is included. Uninsured and underinsured motorist coverage and personal injury protection are included at the minimum limits required by Oregon law. The Company's freight to out-of-state distributors is carried by a third-party carrier, and that carrier's own coverage applies to those shipments.

7.2 **Workers' Compensation.** SAIF Corporation provides statutory workers' compensation coverage for all of the Company's Oregon employees, together with employer's liability coverage of $1,000,000 each accident, $1,000,000 disease (policy limit) and $1,000,000 disease (each employee). The policy is written on an Oregon-only basis. The Company has no employees regularly based outside Oregon. Employees traveling temporarily to Washington and Idaho are covered under the policy's other-states provisions to the extent permitted by those states' laws.

7.3 **Renewal of Workers' Compensation.** The current SAIF policy expires January 1, 2027. SAIF has issued a renewal offer on substantially the same terms, with an experience modification factor of 0.91 for the 2027 policy year (compared with 0.94 for 2026). We will bind the renewal on the Company's instructions before expiration and will send you an updated certificate promptly after it is issued. Because SAIF bills on a policy-year basis with a deposit premium, binding the renewal before the closing will not affect any coverage after the closing. Any change in the Company's ownership will be reported to SAIF as described in Section 10.

## 8. Umbrella

8.1 **Limits and Underlying Schedule.** The Umbrella policy provides a $10,000,000 limit each occurrence and in the aggregate. It sits in excess of the Commercial General Liability, Liquor Liability, Commercial Auto and Employer's Liability coverages scheduled as underlying insurance. It drops down to replace exhausted underlying aggregates. Where a claim is not covered by underlying insurance but is covered by the Umbrella, the policy is subject to a self-insured retention of $10,000.

8.2 **Follow-Form.** The Umbrella follows form to the underlying General Liability policy for products/completed operations and to the Liquor Liability policy for liquor liability exposures. Its additional insured provisions extend to any Person that is an additional insured under the underlying policies, to the extent the underlying written contract requires it.

10.1 **Policy Provisions.** We have reviewed each policy listed in Section 3 for provisions triggered by a change in ownership or control of the named insured. Each policy continues in force for its stated term after the closing, subject to its terms. The Company remains the first named insured.

With the Company's and Braikal's consent, we have given each insurer advance confidential notice that a change in ownership is expected. We will give formal notice promptly after you confirm that the closing has occurred.
