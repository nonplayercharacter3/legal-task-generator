# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

This INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT (this "**Assignment**") is entered into as of the Closing Date (as defined in the Purchase Agreement referred to below) by and between **Braikal Beverage Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Assignor**"), and **Copperwake Brewing Company**, an Oregon corporation ("**Assignee**"). It is acknowledged by **Tinav Beverage Partners, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), for the purposes stated in Section 12.

## RECITALS

A. Assignor and Buyer have entered into a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of October 2, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is acquiring from Assignor all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of Assignee.

B. Assignor is the record and beneficial owner of the COPPERWAKE trademarks and related registrations and applications, domain names and social media accounts. Assignor is also the owner of the recipes, formulations, process specifications, yeast strains and related know-how used in the Business, which are recorded in Assignor's name.

C. Section 6.10 of the Purchase Agreement requires Assignor to execute and deliver this Assignment at or before the Closing. Section 6.10 also requires Assignor to take all other actions reasonably necessary to vest in Assignee all right, title and interest in the Intellectual Property conveyed by this Assignment, free and clear of all Encumbrances.

D. This Assignment is the "IP Assignment" referred to in the Purchase Agreement. It is an Ancillary Agreement under the Purchase Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Assignment and in the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **Purchase Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Assignment have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement, including "Business," "Closing," "Closing Date," "Encumbrance," "Intellectual Property," "Person" and "Disclosure Schedule."

1.2 **Defined Terms.** In this Assignment:

"**Assigned Domain Names**" means the internet domain names listed in Part C of Schedule 1, together with all registrations of them, all rights to renew them, and all content, DNS records and email routing associated with them.

"**Assigned IP**" means, collectively, the Assigned Marks, the Assigned Domain Names, the Assigned Social Media Accounts, the Assigned Recipes and Know-How, the Assigned Copyrights and the related rights described in Section 2.1(g).

"**Assigned Copyrights**" means all copyrights and works of authorship, registered or unregistered, owned by Assignor and used or held for use in the Business. These include label artwork, packaging designs, can wraps, tap handle designs, point-of-sale materials, website content, photographs, videos and other marketing materials relating to the Company's products, and all registrations and applications for any of them.

"**Assigned Marks**" means (a) the trademarks, service marks, registrations and applications listed in Parts A and B of Schedule 1; (b) all other trademarks, service marks, trade names, brand names, product names, logos, slogans, trade dress and other source identifiers owned by Assignor that consist of or contain the word COPPERWAKE, or that are used or held for use in connection with any product of the Business, whether registered or unregistered; (c) all common-law rights in any of the foregoing; and (d) all goodwill of the Business connected with the use of, and symbolized by, any of the foregoing.

"**Assigned Recipes and Know-How**" means all recipes, formulations, ingredient specifications, hop and malt bills, water chemistry profiles, mash, boil, fermentation, conditioning and carbonation schedules, packaging specifications, quality specifications, sensory standards, batch records and other process documentation owned by Assignor and used or held for use in the Business. This includes those for the Company's year-round, seasonal and limited-release beers and for its hard seltzer and other flavored malt beverage products. It also includes the house yeast strain designated "CW-1" and all other yeast strains, cultures, cryogenic and slant stocks and propagation records owned by Assignor and used or held for use in the Business, and all trade secrets and confidential information embodied in any of the foregoing.

"**Assigned Social Media Accounts**" means the social media accounts listed in Part D of Schedule 1, together with all followers, content, posts, messages, administrative credentials and associated data.

"**Effective Time**" means the time at which the Closing is deemed effective under Section 3.1 of the Purchase Agreement.

"**PLM System**" means the Braikal product lifecycle management system in which the Assigned Recipes and Know-How are recorded.

"**Trademark License**" means the Trademark License Agreement dated March 4, 2019 between Assignor, as licensor, and Assignee, as licensee, described in Section 4.10(c)(i) of the Disclosure Schedule.

1.3 **Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" shall be deemed to be followed by the words "without limitation." The word "or" is not exclusive. References to Sections and Schedules are to Sections of and Schedules to this Assignment unless otherwise stated. Headings are for reference only and do not affect interpretation.

## 2. Assignment

2.1 **Assignment of Assigned IP.** Effective as of the Effective Time, Assignor hereby irrevocably and unconditionally sells, assigns, transfers, conveys and delivers to Assignee, and Assignee hereby accepts, all of Assignor's right, title and interest throughout the world in, to and under the Assigned IP, free and clear of all Encumbrances, including:

(a) the Assigned Marks, together with the goodwill of the Business connected with the use of and symbolized by them;

(b) all registrations, applications, renewals and extensions of the Assigned Marks, and all rights to file applications for registration of any of them in any jurisdiction;

(c) the Assigned Domain Names and the Assigned Social Media Accounts;

(d) the Assigned Recipes and Know-How, including all physical and electronic embodiments of them and all biological material comprising any yeast strain included in them;

(e) the Assigned Copyrights, including all registrations and applications for them and all rights to register them;

(f) all rights to collect royalties, license fees and other payments under or with respect to any of the Assigned IP accruing after the Effective Time; and

(g) all rights to sue for and recover damages, profits and any other remedy for any past, present or future infringement, dilution, misappropriation or other violation of any of the Assigned IP, all rights of priority and protection of interests in it under the laws of any jurisdiction, and all rights corresponding to any of the foregoing throughout the world.

2.2 **Goodwill.** Assignor acknowledges that the Assigned Marks are being assigned together with the goodwill of the Business connected with their use and symbolized by them. Assignee is the successor to that portion of the Business to which the Assigned Marks pertain. Assignor agrees that, from and after the Effective Time, all use of the Assigned Marks and all goodwill associated with them inure solely to the benefit of Assignee.

2.3 **Class 33 Application.** Assignor confirms that U.S. Application Serial No. 98/441,306 for the mark COPPERWAKE in Class 33 was filed under Section 1(a) of the Lanham Act on the basis of use in commerce by Assignee as licensee of Assignor under the Trademark License, and that it is assigned under this Assignment together with the goodwill of the Business to which it pertains.

2.4 **No Retained Rights.** Assignor does not retain, and shall not assert, any right, title, interest or license in or to any of the Assigned IP after the Effective Time. Assignor shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, use, register or attempt to register any of the Assigned Marks, or any mark, name, domain name or social media handle confusingly similar to any of them, in any jurisdiction.

2.5 **Consideration.** The assignment under this Section 2 is made as part of the transactions contemplated by the Purchase Agreement and is supported by the consideration paid under it. No additional consideration is payable by Assignee or Buyer for the Assigned IP.

## 3. Delivery of Assigned IP

3.1 **Recipes and Know-How.** At or before the Effective Time, Assignor shall deliver to Assignee complete and accurate copies of all Assigned Recipes and Know-How in Assignor's possession or control. Delivery shall be made in both native electronic format and a printed or PDF format reasonably acceptable to Assignee. Assignor shall export from the PLM System all records relating to the Company's products, including all version histories, specification sheets, batch records, lab results and change logs. Assignor shall certify in writing to Assignee, within thirty (30) days after the Closing Date, that the export is complete.

3.2 **Yeast Strains.** At or before the Effective Time, Assignor shall deliver to Assignee, at the brewery, all cryogenic stocks, slants, plates and other samples of the CW-1 yeast strain and any other yeast strain included in the Assigned Recipes and Know-How that are held by Assignor or its Affiliates or by any third-party yeast bank on Assignor's behalf. Assignor shall also deliver all related propagation, viability and genetic characterization records. Assignor shall instruct each such yeast bank in writing to treat Assignee as the owner of the relevant strains and to act only on Assignee's instructions.

3.3 **Domain Names.** Within five (5) Business Days after the Closing Date, Assignor shall initiate and complete the transfer of each Assigned Domain Name from Assignor's account with its registrar to an account designated by Assignee. Assignor shall provide all authorization codes and shall take all steps the registrar requires. Until the transfer is complete, Assignor shall maintain each Assigned Domain Name in good standing, shall not alter its DNS records or content except at Assignee's direction, and shall hold it in trust for Assignee.

3.4 **Social Media Accounts.** At the Effective Time, Assignor shall deliver to Assignee all login credentials, recovery email addresses, two-factor authentication devices or codes and other information necessary for Assignee to exercise exclusive administrative control of each Assigned Social Media Account. Assignor shall cooperate with Assignee and each platform operator to transfer ownership of each account to Assignee and to remove all Assignor personnel as administrators.

3.5 **Copyright Materials.** At or before the Effective Time, Assignor shall deliver to Assignee all native design files, source files, artwork, photographs and other materials embodying the Assigned Copyrights that are in Assignor's possession or control.

3.6 **Prosecution Files.** Within ten (10) Business Days after the Closing Date, Assignor shall deliver, or shall instruct its trademark counsel to deliver, to Assignee or its designated counsel the complete prosecution and maintenance files for each registration and application included in the Assigned Marks. These files include all specimens, declarations, office actions, responses, docketing reports and correspondence with any trademark office.

## 4. Destruction of Retained Copies; Confidentiality

4.1 **Deletion.** Within thirty (30) days after Assignor completes the export required by Section 3.1, Assignor shall permanently delete from the PLM System and from all other systems, devices and storage media under its or its Affiliates' control all copies of the Assigned Recipes and Know-How. Assignor shall destroy all physical copies in its or its Affiliates' possession. Assignor shall deliver to Assignee a certificate of an officer of Assignor confirming that it has done so. Assignor may retain copies only to the extent required by applicable Law or by bona fide document retention policies relating to automatic electronic back-up systems. Any copies so retained shall not be accessed or used for any purpose other than compliance with Law and shall be held subject to Section 4.2.

4.2 **Confidentiality.** The Assigned Recipes and Know-How are confidential information concerning the Company and the Business for purposes of Section 6.5 of the Purchase Agreement. Assignor shall hold them in confidence in accordance with that Section.

## 5. Termination of Trademark License

5.1 **Termination.** Effective as of the Effective Time, the Trademark License is terminated. No provision of the Trademark License survives its termination, including any provision that by its terms would otherwise survive.

5.2 **Mutual Release.** Assignor, for itself and its Affiliates, releases Assignee from all royalties, fees, reimbursements and other amounts payable under the Trademark License and from all claims under it, whether accrued before or after the Effective Time. Assignee releases Assignor from all obligations under the Trademark License accruing after the Effective Time.

5.3 **Prior Use.** All use of the Assigned Marks by Assignee before the Effective Time under the Trademark License shall inure to the benefit of Assignee as the successor owner of the Assigned Marks.

## 6. Recordation

6.1 **Short-Form Assignments.** Concurrently with the execution of this Assignment, Assignor shall execute and deliver to Assignee:

(a) a short-form trademark assignment in the form of **Exhibit A** for recordation with the United States Patent and Trademark Office (the "**USPTO**"); and

(b) such assignment documents as the Oregon Secretary of State requires to record the transfer of Oregon Registration No. 41887.

The short-form assignments are intended for recordation purposes only. They do not limit or expand the rights and obligations of the parties under this Assignment.

6.2 **Authorization.** Assignor authorizes Assignee and its counsel to record this Assignment, or the short-form assignments described in Section 6.1, with the USPTO, the Oregon Secretary of State and any other Governmental Authority or registrar in any jurisdiction. Assignor also authorizes the relevant authorities and registrars to record Assignee as the owner of the Assigned IP.

6.3 **Costs.** Assignee shall bear the government filing fees for recording the assignments described in Section 6.1. Any transfer, documentary, sales, use or similar Taxes arising from the transfers under this Assignment shall be borne and paid by Assignor as provided in Section 8.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 7. Maintenance Before Recordation

7.1 **Interim Maintenance.** If any deadline for filing a declaration of use, renewal application, response to an office action or other maintenance or prosecution document for any registration or application included in the Assigned Marks falls before the assignment of that registration or application has been recorded, Assignor shall, at Assignee's written request and expense and in accordance with Assignee's reasonable instructions, make that filing or take that action. Alternatively, Assignor shall cooperate with Assignee so that Assignee may make that filing in its own name.

7.2 **Notices Received.** If Assignor or any of its Affiliates receives, after the Effective Time, any office action, notice of opposition, cancellation petition, cease-and-desist letter, infringement notice or other correspondence relating to any of the Assigned IP, Assignor shall forward it to Assignee promptly, and in any event within five (5) Business Days after receipt.

7.3 **Payments Received.** If Assignor or any of its Affiliates receives, after the Effective Time, any payment to which Assignee is entitled under Section 2.1(f), Assignor shall hold the payment in trust for Assignee and shall remit it to Assignee within ten (10) Business Days after receipt.

## 8. Further Assurances

8.1 **General.** At and after the Effective Time, Assignor shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, without further consideration, execute and deliver such additional assignments, declarations, powers of attorney and other instruments, and take such further actions, as Assignee or Buyer may reasonably request to vest, perfect, record, maintain, enforce or defend Assignee's rights in the Assigned IP in any jurisdiction.

8.2 **Power of Attorney.** Assignor irrevocably appoints Assignee, and any officer of Assignee, as Assignor's attorney-in-fact, with full power of substitution, solely to execute, deliver and file any document described in Section 8.1 that Assignor fails to execute and deliver within ten (10) Business Days after Assignee's written request. This power of attorney is coupled with an interest and survives the dissolution, liquidation or bankruptcy of Assignor.

8.3 **Enforcement Cooperation.** At Assignee's request and expense (other than the cost of Assignor's own personnel time), Assignor shall cooperate reasonably with Assignee in any Action concerning any of the Assigned IP. Cooperation includes making available relevant documents and personnel with knowledge of the creation, use, registration or enforcement of the Assigned IP, and joining as a party where required by Law for standing purposes.

8.4 **Inventors and Authors.** To the extent any Assigned Recipes and Know-How or Assigned Copyrights were created by an employee or contractor of Assignor or any of its Affiliates (other than an employee of Assignee), Assignor confirms that it has obtained, or shall obtain at its own expense, a written assignment to Assignor of all of that Person's rights in them. Upon Assignee's request, Assignor shall deliver copies of those assignments to Assignee.

## 9. Representations and Warranties

9.1 **Representations of Assignor.** Assignor represents and warrants to Assignee and Buyer that:

(a) Assignor has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Assignment, and this Assignment has been duly authorized, executed and delivered by Assignor and constitutes a legal, valid and binding obligation of Assignor enforceable in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable bankruptcy, insolvency and similar Laws and general principles of equity;

(b) Assignor is the sole and exclusive owner of all right, title and interest in and to the Assigned IP, free and clear of all Encumbrances, and has not previously assigned, transferred, licensed or granted any interest in any of the Assigned IP to any Person other than Assignee under the Trademark License;

(c) no Affiliate of Assignor (other than Assignee) owns or claims any right, title or interest in any Intellectual Property used in or necessary for the conduct of the Business; and

(d) Schedule 1 lists all registrations and applications for Intellectual Property, all domain names and all social media accounts owned by Assignor or any of its Affiliates and used in the Business, and it corresponds to Section 4.10(a) of the Disclosure Schedule.

9.2 **Representations of Assignee.** Assignee represents and warrants to Assignor that it has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Assignment, and that this Assignment has been duly authorized, executed and delivered by Assignee.

9.3 **Relationship to Purchase Agreement.** The representations and warranties in Section 9.1 are made in addition to, and not in substitution for, the representations and warranties of Seller in the Purchase Agreement, including Sections 4.9(c), 4.10 and 4.19 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 10. Remedies

10.1 **Equitable Relief.** Assignor acknowledges that the Assigned IP, and particularly the Assigned Recipes and Know-How and the CW-1 yeast strain, are unique. A breach or threatened breach of Sections 2.4, 3, 4 or 8 would cause Assignee irreparable harm for which monetary damages would not be an adequate remedy. In the event of such a breach or threatened breach, Assignee and Buyer shall be entitled to equitable relief, including a temporary restraining order, an injunction and specific performance, without any requirement to post bond, in addition to any other remedy available to them.

10.2 **Cumulative Remedies.** The rights and remedies of Assignee and Buyer under this Assignment are cumulative with their rights and remedies under the Purchase Agreement and at law or in equity.

## 11. Effectiveness

11.1 **Conditional Effectiveness.** This Assignment is executed and delivered at the Closing and becomes effective at the Effective Time.

11.2 **Termination of Purchase Agreement.** If this Assignment is executed in escrow before the Closing and the Purchase Agreement is terminated in accordance with its terms before the Closing, this Assignment shall be void and of no further force or effect. In that case any document delivered in escrow under it shall be returned to Assignor.

## 12. Buyer Acknowledgment; Third-Party Beneficiary

Buyer executes this Assignment solely to acknowledge it and to take the benefit of Sections 8, 9 and 10. Buyer is an intended third-party beneficiary of those Sections and may enforce them directly against Assignor. Buyer assumes no obligation under this Assignment.

## 13. Miscellaneous

13.1 **Governing Law.** This Assignment, and all claims or causes of action (whether in contract, tort or statute) that may be based upon, arise out of or relate to this Assignment, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal Laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law provision or rule that would cause the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction. Matters of the registrability, validity and recordation of any Assigned IP in any jurisdiction are governed by the Laws of that jurisdiction.

13.2 **Jurisdiction.** Any legal suit, Action or proceeding arising out of or based upon this Assignment shall be instituted exclusively in the Delaware Court of Chancery (or, solely if the Delaware Court of Chancery declines to accept or lacks subject matter jurisdiction, the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware), and each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of those courts in any such suit, Action or proceeding. This Section 13.2 does not prevent Assignee from making any filing with, or taking any action before, the USPTO, the Oregon Secretary of State or any other trademark office, domain name registrar or dispute resolution provider in respect of the Assigned IP.

13.3 **Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY AND UNCONDITIONALLY WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY APPLICABLE LAW, ANY RIGHT IT MAY HAVE TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUIT, ACTION OR PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS ASSIGNMENT.

13.4 **Notices.** All notices under this Assignment shall be given in the manner provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement. Notices to Assignor shall be sent to the address for Seller under the Purchase Agreement. Notices to Assignee and to Buyer shall be sent to the address for Buyer under the Purchase Agreement, with notices to Assignee marked for the attention of the General Manager of Assignee.

13.5 **Successors and Assigns.** This Assignment is binding upon and inures to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and assigns. Assignee may assign this Assignment, and any of its rights under it, together with any of the Assigned IP, to Buyer, to any Affiliate of Buyer, to any lender as collateral, or to any acquirer of all or any part of the Business, without the consent of Assignor. Assignor may not assign this Assignment or delegate any of its obligations under it without the prior written consent of Assignee.

13.6 **Amendment; Waiver.** This Assignment may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Assignor and Assignee and acknowledged by Buyer. No waiver shall be effective unless set forth in a writing signed by the party against whom it is to be enforced.

13.7 **Severability.** If any provision of this Assignment is held invalid, illegal or unenforceable in any jurisdiction, that provision shall be ineffective only to that extent, without affecting the remaining provisions of this Assignment or the validity of that provision in any other jurisdiction.

13.8 **Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Assignment may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together shall constitute one instrument. Signatures delivered by e-mail in PDF format or by electronic signature service complying with the U.S. federal ESIGN Act of 2000 shall be effective as delivery of an original signature. Assignor shall deliver an original wet-ink signature on Exhibit A if required by any trademark office.
