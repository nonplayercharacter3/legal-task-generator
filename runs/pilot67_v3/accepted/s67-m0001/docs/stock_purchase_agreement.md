# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of October 2, 2026, by and between **Tinav Beverage Partners, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), and **Braikal Beverage Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller is the record and beneficial owner of all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of **Copperwake Brewing Company**, an Oregon corporation (the "**Company**").

B. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

C. At the Closing, Buyer will deliver part of the Purchase Price by issuing the Seller Note to Seller, and part of the cash portion of the Purchase Price will be deposited with the Escrow Agent as security for Seller's indemnification obligations under this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants, representations, warranties and agreements in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchase and Sale; Purchase Price.** At the Closing, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of Seller's right, title and interest in and to the Shares, free and clear of all Encumbrances. The aggregate purchase price for the Shares is $48,000,000, being $40,000,000 cash plus the $8,000,000 Seller Note (the "**Purchase Price**").

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall:

(a) pay to Seller, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in writing by Seller at least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, an amount equal to $40,000,000 less the Indemnity Escrow Amount;

(b) deposit the Indemnity Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent in accordance with Section 2.4; and

(c) execute and deliver the Seller Note to Seller.

**2.3 Indebtedness and Transaction Expenses.** Seller shall cause all Indebtedness of the Company and all unpaid Transaction Expenses to be repaid or paid in full at or before the Closing, and shall deliver to Buyer customary payoff letters and releases of Encumbrances for all Indebtedness for borrowed money, in each case in form reasonably satisfactory to Buyer.

**2.4 Indemnity Escrow.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit $2,400,000 of the cash portion of the Purchase Price (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent, to be held in a segregated account and disbursed by the Escrow Agent in accordance with Article IX and the Escrow Agreement. The Indemnity Escrow Fund shall serve as a source of payment for Seller's obligations under Article IX. Buyer and Seller shall each pay one-half of the fees and expenses of the Escrow Agent.

**2.5 Withholding.** Buyer shall be entitled to deduct and withhold from the Purchase Price all Taxes that Buyer is required to deduct and withhold under any provision of Tax Law. All amounts so withheld and paid over to the appropriate Governmental Authority shall be treated for all purposes of this Agreement as having been delivered to Seller. Buyer shall give Seller reasonable advance notice of any intended withholding and shall cooperate with Seller to reduce or eliminate it. Seller shall deliver to Buyer at Closing a properly completed IRS Form W-9.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, the consummation of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures at 10:00 a.m. Pacific Time on the third (3rd) Business Day after the last of the conditions to Closing set forth in Article VII has been satisfied or waived (other than conditions which, by their nature, are to be satisfied on the Closing Date), or at such other time, date or place as Seller and Buyer may mutually agree in writing.
