# EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This EXECUTIVE EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of October 2, 2026, by and between **Copperwake Brewing Company**, an Oregon corporation (the "**Company**"), and **Imelda Sorrentino**, an individual residing in the State of Oregon ("**Executive**"). The Company and Executive are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Executive is currently employed by the Company as its head brewer.

B. Tinav Beverage Partners, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Parent**"), and Braikal Beverage Group, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Braikal**"), have entered into a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of October 2, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Parent will acquire all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company. The consummation of that acquisition is the "**Closing**," and the date on which it occurs is the "**Closing Date**," each as defined in the Purchase Agreement.

C. Parent has required, as a condition to its obligation to consummate the Closing, that Executive enter into this Agreement. Executive has had the opportunity to review this Agreement with counsel of her choosing.

D. The Company wishes to continue to employ Executive following the Closing, and Executive wishes to continue that employment, on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises in this Agreement and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. Effectiveness

1.1 **Effective Date.** This Agreement becomes effective automatically as of the Closing Date (the "**Effective Date**"), without further action by either Party.

1.2 **Failure of Closing.** If the Purchase Agreement is terminated in accordance with its terms before the Closing, this Agreement shall be void *ab initio* and of no further force or effect. In that case Executive's employment shall continue on the terms in effect immediately before the date of this Agreement, and neither Party shall have any obligation under this Agreement.

1.3 **Prior Arrangements.** From the Effective Date, this Agreement supersedes any offer letter, employment agreement, bonus or incentive letter, or other arrangement between Executive and the Company, or between Executive and Braikal or any of its affiliates, relating to Executive's employment with the Company. Nothing in this Agreement affects (a) Executive's accrued but unpaid base salary, expense reimbursements or vested benefits for any period before the Effective Date, or (b) any obligation Executive owes under any prior confidentiality or invention assignment agreement with respect to the period before the Effective Date.

1.4 **Continuous Service.** Executive's service with the Company before the Effective Date shall be recognized for purposes of vacation accrual, eligibility and vesting under the Company's benefit plans to the extent permitted by those plans and applicable law.

## 2. Employment; Position and Duties

2.1 **Position.** The Company shall employ Executive, and Executive shall serve the Company, as **Head Brewer and Director of Brewing Operations**. Executive shall report to the General Manager of the Company or such other officer of the Company or Parent as the Company may designate (the "**Supervisor**").

2.2 **Duties.** Executive shall have the duties, authority and responsibilities customary for a head brewer of a craft brewery of comparable size, and such other duties consistent with her position as the Supervisor may reasonably assign. These duties include:

(a) overseeing recipe development, brewing, fermentation, conditioning and packaging of all of the Company's products;

(b) maintaining the Company's yeast library, recipe records, batch records and process documentation;

(c) supervising quality assurance and quality control, including sensory panels, laboratory testing and shelf-life programs;

(d) supervising brewing and cellar staff, including hiring and performance management, within budgets approved by the Company;

(e) supporting the Company's compliance with Alcohol Laws (as defined in the Purchase Agreement), including the accuracy of production data used in brewer's reports of operations and excise tax returns, and the submission of formulas and labels for regulatory approval;

(f) supporting the Company's compliance with its industrial wastewater discharge and other environmental permits as they relate to brewing operations; and

(g) supporting the Company's contract brewing and co-packing customers on technical and production matters.

2.3 **Full Time and Attention.** Executive shall devote her full business time, attention and best efforts to the business of the Company. Executive may (a) serve on the boards or committees of civic, charitable, educational or brewing trade organizations (including judging at recognized beer competitions and participating in brewers' guild activities), and (b) manage her personal investments, in each case so long as those activities do not materially interfere with her duties under this Agreement or breach Sections 8 through 11.

2.4 **Place of Performance.** Executive's principal place of employment shall be the Company's brewery in Marlow Bend, Oregon, subject to reasonable business travel. The Company shall not require Executive to relocate her principal place of employment more than fifty (50) miles from that location without her written consent.

2.5 **Policies.** Executive shall comply with the Company's written policies and codes of conduct as in effect from time to time, to the extent they are consistent with this Agreement, including policies on workplace safety, alcohol consumption on premises and responsible service.

## 3. Term

3.1 **Initial Term.** The term of Executive's employment under this Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until the third (3rd) anniversary of the Effective Date (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 6.

3.2 **Renewal.** After the Initial Term, the term shall renew automatically for successive one-year periods (each, a "**Renewal Term**") unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the Initial Term or the then-current Renewal Term. The Initial Term and any Renewal Terms are the "**Term**." A notice of non-renewal by the Company shall be treated as a termination without Cause under Section 6.5, effective at the end of the Term then in effect.

## 4. Compensation

4.1 **Base Salary.** The Company shall pay Executive a base salary at the annual rate of $185,000 (as adjusted from time to time, the "**Base Salary**"), payable in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices, but no less often than monthly. The Base Salary shall be reviewed at least annually and may be increased, but not decreased, at the Company's discretion.

4.2 **Annual Bonus.** For each fiscal year of the Company that ends during the Term, beginning with the fiscal year ending December 31, 2027, Executive shall be eligible to earn an annual cash bonus (the "**Annual Bonus**") with a target amount equal to twenty-five percent (25%) of the Base Salary paid for that year. The Annual Bonus shall be based on the achievement of performance objectives set by the Company after consultation with Executive within sixty (60) days after the start of the relevant fiscal year. These objectives may include production volume, quality metrics, cost of goods sold, product development milestones and safety. Any Annual Bonus earned shall be paid no later than March 15 of the year after the fiscal year to which it relates. Except as provided in Section 7, Executive must be employed on the payment date to receive the Annual Bonus. For the portion of fiscal year 2026 following the Effective Date, the Company shall pay Executive a bonus under the Company's existing bonus practices for brewing staff, pro-rated for the portion of that year following the Effective Date.

4.3 **Long-Term Incentive.** Executive shall be eligible to participate in any long-term incentive plan that Parent or the Company adopts for senior employees of the Company. The amount and terms of any award shall be determined by the administrator of that plan.

4.4 **Withholding.** All payments under this Agreement are subject to withholding of applicable federal, state and local taxes and other deductions required by law or authorized by Executive.

## 5. Benefits; Expenses; Leave

5.1 **Benefit Plans.** Executive shall be eligible to participate in the employee benefit plans, programs and arrangements that the Company or Parent makes available to similarly situated employees of the Company, subject to their terms. These plans currently include medical, dental, vision, life and disability insurance and a 401(k) plan. The Company may amend or terminate any plan in accordance with its terms and applicable law.

5.2 **Paid Time Off.** Executive shall be entitled to twenty (20) days of paid time off per calendar year, accrued and used in accordance with Company policy, in addition to Company holidays. Executive shall also be entitled to paid sick time to the extent required by Oregon law and to any leave required by the Oregon Family Leave Act, Paid Leave Oregon or the federal Family and Medical Leave Act.

5.3 **Business Expenses.** The Company shall reimburse Executive for reasonable, documented business expenses incurred in performing her duties, in accordance with Company policy. These include travel to industry conferences, beer competitions, supplier sites and contract brewing customers. Executive shall be entitled each calendar year to attend at least two industry conferences or professional education programs at the Company's expense, subject to reasonable budget approval.

5.4 **Professional Development.** The Company shall pay Executive's dues for membership in professional brewing associations reasonably related to her position, and the reasonable costs of continuing education or certification programs approved in advance by the Supervisor.

## 6. Termination of Employment

6.1 **General.** Executive's employment may be terminated during the Term as provided in this Section 6. On termination for any reason, Executive shall be deemed to have resigned from all officer, director and other positions she then holds with the Company, Parent or any of their affiliates.

6.2 **Death or Disability.** Executive's employment shall terminate automatically on her death. The Company may terminate Executive's employment on written notice if Executive has a Disability. "**Disability**" means Executive's inability, due to a physical or mental impairment, to perform the essential functions of her position, with or without reasonable accommodation, for ninety (90) consecutive days or one hundred twenty (120) days in any twelve-month period. This definition shall be interpreted and applied consistently with the Americans with Disabilities Act, ORS Chapter 659A and other applicable law.

6.3 **By the Company for Cause.** The Company may terminate Executive's employment for Cause. "**Cause**" means Executive's:

(a) conviction of, or plea of guilty or no contest to, a felony or any crime involving fraud, dishonesty or moral turpitude;

(b) willful misconduct or gross negligence in performing her duties that causes material harm to the Company;

(c) willful and continued failure to perform her material duties (other than a failure resulting from Disability) after written notice from the Company specifying the failure in reasonable detail;

(d) material breach of this Agreement, including Sections 8 through 11;

(e) knowing violation of Alcohol Laws, food safety laws or environmental laws that results in, or is reasonably likely to result in, the suspension or revocation of any Permit (as defined in the Purchase Agreement) of the Company; or

(f) material violation of a written Company policy prohibiting harassment, discrimination or retaliation.

Before terminating Executive for Cause under clause (b), (c), (d) or (f), the Company shall give Executive written notice describing the conduct in reasonable detail. If the conduct is reasonably capable of cure, Executive shall have thirty (30) days after receipt of the notice to cure it.

6.4 **By Executive for Good Reason.** Executive may terminate her employment for Good Reason. "**Good Reason**" means the occurrence of any of the following without Executive's written consent:

(a) a material reduction in the Base Salary or target Annual Bonus opportunity;

(b) a material diminution in Executive's title, authority, duties or responsibilities, including the removal of her responsibility for recipe development and brewing quality;

(c) a relocation of Executive's principal place of employment in breach of Section 2.4; or

(d) a material breach of this Agreement by the Company.

Executive must give the Company written notice of the condition within sixty (60) days after its initial occurrence. The Company shall then have thirty (30) days to cure the condition. If the Company does not cure it, Executive must resign within thirty (30) days after the cure period ends.

6.5 **By the Company Without Cause.** The Company may terminate Executive's employment without Cause on thirty (30) days' written notice. The Company may pay Base Salary in lieu of all or part of that notice period.

6.6 **By Executive Without Good Reason.** Executive may resign without Good Reason on sixty (60) days' written notice to the Company. The Company may waive all or part of that notice period, in which case it shall pay Executive her Base Salary for the waived portion.

6.7 **Termination Date.** The "**Termination Date**" is the date on which Executive's employment terminates under this Section 6.

## 7. Payments on Termination

7.1 **Accrued Obligations.** On any termination, the Company shall pay or provide to Executive (or her estate) the following (the "**Accrued Obligations**"):

(a) earned but unpaid Base Salary through the Termination Date, paid within the time required by ORS 652.140;

(b) accrued but unused paid time off, to the extent payable under Company policy or applicable law;

(c) any Annual Bonus earned for a completed fiscal year but not yet paid;

(d) unreimbursed business expenses properly incurred and submitted under Section 5.3; and

(e) vested benefits under the Company's benefit plans, in accordance with their terms.

7.2 **Severance.** If the Company terminates Executive's employment without Cause, or Executive resigns for Good Reason, in addition to the Accrued Obligations, and subject to Section 7.3, the Company shall:

(a) continue to pay Executive her Base Salary, at the rate in effect on the Termination Date, for twelve (12) months after the Termination Date (the "**Severance Period**"), in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices;

(b) pay Executive a pro-rated Annual Bonus for the fiscal year in which the Termination Date occurs, based on actual performance for that year and the number of days Executive was employed during it, payable when annual bonuses are paid to other employees of the Company, and in any event no later than March 15 of the following year; and

(c) if Executive timely elects COBRA continuation coverage, pay or reimburse the employer portion of the premium for that coverage for Executive and her covered dependents during the Severance Period, or until Executive becomes eligible for group health coverage from a subsequent employer, if earlier.

7.3 **Release.** The payments and benefits under Section 7.2 (the "**Severance Benefits**") are conditioned on Executive executing a general release of claims in favor of the Company, Parent and their affiliates, in a form reasonably acceptable to the Company, and that release becoming effective and irrevocable within sixty (60) days after the Termination Date. Severance Benefits shall begin on the first payroll date after the release becomes irrevocable, and the first installment shall include all amounts that would otherwise have been paid since the Termination Date. If the sixty-day period spans two calendar years, payments shall begin in the second year. The Severance Benefits are also conditioned on Executive's continued compliance with Sections 8 through 11.

7.4 **Other Terminations.** On termination due to death or Disability, for Cause, or by Executive without Good Reason, Executive shall be entitled only to the Accrued Obligations. On termination due to death or Disability, Executive (or her estate) shall also receive the pro-rated Annual Bonus described in Section 7.2(b).

7.5 **No Mitigation.** Executive is not required to mitigate the amount of any payment under this Section 7 by seeking other employment. Except as provided in Section 7.2(c), no payment shall be reduced by compensation Executive earns from other employment.

## 8. Confidential Information

8.1 **Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information concerning the business of the Company, Parent or their affiliates that Executive obtains or develops during her employment, whether before or after the Effective Date. It includes:

(a) recipes, formulations, ingredient specifications, hop and malt selections, water chemistry profiles, mash, fermentation and conditioning schedules, and other brewing processes;

(b) yeast strains, cultures and propagation methods;

(c) product development plans and unreleased products;

(d) customer, distributor and contract brewing customer information, pricing, volumes and terms;

(e) supplier terms and costs;

(f) financial information, business plans and forecasts; and

(g) personnel information other than information about Executive herself.

Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach by Executive, or general brewing skill, knowledge and experience that Executive possessed before her employment or that is generally known in the brewing industry.

8.2 **Obligations.** During her employment and at all times afterwards, Executive shall hold Confidential Information in confidence and shall not use or disclose it except as required to perform her duties for the Company or as authorized in writing by the Company. Executive shall take reasonable precautions to protect Confidential Information from unauthorized use or disclosure.

8.3 **Permitted Disclosures.** Nothing in this Agreement prohibits Executive from:

(a) reporting possible violations of law to any governmental agency, or making other disclosures protected under whistleblower laws;

(b) disclosing information about unlawful employment practices, including discrimination, harassment or sexual assault, to the extent protected by ORS 659A.370 or other applicable law;

(c) discussing her own wages and terms of employment; or

(d) complying with a subpoena or court order, provided that, to the extent legally permitted, she gives the Company prompt notice so that it may seek a protective order.

Under the Defend Trade Secrets Act of 2016 (18 U.S.C. § 1833(b)), Executive shall not be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for disclosing a trade secret (a) in confidence to a government official or an attorney solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law, or (b) in a complaint or other document filed under seal in a lawsuit or other proceeding.

## 9. Work Product and Inventions

9.1 **Assignment.** Executive agrees that all recipes, formulations, processes, yeast strains, inventions, improvements, designs, labels, artwork, writings, names, trade dress and other work product, together with all Intellectual Property (as defined in the Purchase Agreement) in them, that she conceives, develops or reduces to practice, alone or with others, during her employment (whether before or after the Effective Date) and that relate to the business of the Company, or that result from work performed for the Company or with the use of its equipment, facilities, ingredients or Confidential Information (collectively, "**Work Product**"), are the sole and exclusive property of the Company. Executive hereby assigns to the Company all of her right, title and interest in and to all Work Product. To the extent any Work Product is a work of authorship, it is a "work made for hire" for the Company to the extent permitted by law.

9.2 **Excluded Inventions.** Section 9.1 does not apply to any invention that Executive develops entirely on her own time without using the Company's equipment, supplies, facilities or Confidential Information and that neither relates to the Company's business or actual or demonstrably anticipated research or development nor results from any work Executive performs for the Company. Executive has listed on **Exhibit A** any inventions or recipes she developed before her employment with the Company that she wishes to exclude from this Agreement. If Exhibit A is blank, Executive represents that there are none.

9.3 **Cooperation.** During and after her employment, Executive shall execute such documents and take such actions as the Company reasonably requests, at the Company's expense, to perfect, register or enforce its rights in Work Product. These include assignments, declarations and trademark or copyright filings. Executive shall be compensated at a reasonable hourly rate for any such assistance requested after the Termination Date.

9.4 **Records.** Executive shall maintain complete and current recipe, batch and process records for all of the Company's products in the Company's designated systems. Those records are the Company's property.

9.5 **Moral Rights; Attribution.** To the extent permitted by law, Executive waives any moral rights in Work Product. The Company may, but is not required to, credit Executive as brewer or developer of any product in marketing materials.

## 10. Restrictive Covenants

10.1 **Acknowledgments.** Executive acknowledges that:

(a) her position gives her access to the Company's trade secrets, including its recipes, yeast strains and brewing processes;

(b) she is engaged in administrative, executive or professional work, performs predominantly intellectual, managerial or creative tasks, exercises discretion and independent judgment, and is paid on a salary basis;

(c) her annual gross salary and commissions at the time of termination are expected to exceed the threshold amount in effect under ORS 653.295(1)(e); and

(d) she received a copy of this Agreement, including this Section 10, at least two weeks before the Effective Date, and this Agreement is entered into in connection with a bona fide advancement of Executive by the Company.

10.2 **Non-Competition.** During her employment and for twelve (12) months after the Termination Date (the "**Restricted Period**"), Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, within the States of Oregon and Washington, serve as head brewer, brewmaster, director of brewing, production manager or in any other brewing, recipe development or quality leadership role for any Person engaged in the commercial production of beer, hard seltzer or other malt beverages (a "**Competing Business**"). This Section 10.2 does not restrict Executive from (a) owning, as a passive investment, less than two percent (2%) of the publicly traded securities of any Person, (b) teaching or engaging in academic brewing programs, or (c) home brewing for personal, non-commercial use.

10.3 **Enforceability Conditions.** This Section 10.2 shall apply after the Termination Date only if (a) the Company terminates Executive's employment for Cause, (b) Executive resigns without Good Reason, or (c) the Company otherwise elects, by written notice to Executive within ten (10) days after the Termination Date, to pay Executive during the Restricted Period, for the period of restriction, the greater of fifty percent (50%) of her annual gross base salary and commissions at the time of termination and fifty percent (50%) of the threshold amount then in effect under ORS 653.295, as permitted by ORS 653.295(1)(e). Within thirty (30) days after the Termination Date, the Company shall provide Executive with a signed, written copy of this Agreement as required by ORS 653.295(4).

10.4 **Non-Solicitation of Business Relationships.** During the Restricted Period, Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit or induce any distributor, contract brewing or co-packing customer, or supplier of the Company with whom Executive had material contact during the last two years of her employment to terminate or adversely modify its relationship with the Company.

10.5 **Non-Solicitation of Employees.** During the Restricted Period, Executive shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit or induce any employee of the Company to leave the Company's employ, or hire any person who was an employee of the Company during the six months before the hiring. General advertisements not targeted at Company employees do not breach this Section 10.5.

10.6 **Non-Disparagement.** During and after her employment, Executive shall not make any public statement that is intended to disparage the Company, its products or its officers. The Company shall instruct its officers and directors not to make any public statement that is intended to disparage Executive. This Section 10.6 is subject to Section 8.3.

10.7 **Reformation.** If a court of competent jurisdiction determines that any restriction in this Section 10 is unenforceable as written, the court may modify that restriction to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, and shall enforce it as modified.

## 11. Return of Property

On the Termination Date, or earlier on the Company's request, Executive shall return to the Company all property of the Company in her possession or control. This property includes recipe books, batch records, laboratory notebooks, yeast samples, keys, access cards, computers, phones and documents containing Confidential Information. Executive shall permanently delete Confidential Information from any personal devices or accounts after first returning a copy to the Company, and shall confirm in writing that she has done so. Executive may retain copies of this Agreement and documents relating to her own compensation and benefits.

## 12. Remedies

12.1 **Equitable Relief.** Executive acknowledges that a breach of Sections 8 through 11 would cause the Company irreparable harm for which money damages would not be an adequate remedy. In that event, the Company shall be entitled to seek temporary, preliminary and permanent injunctive relief, in addition to any other remedy available to it.

12.2 **Tolling.** The Restricted Period shall be extended by any period during which Executive is in breach of Section 10, to the extent permitted by Oregon law, provided that no such extension shall cause the post-employment non-competition period under Section 10.2 to exceed twelve (12) months.

## 13. Section 409A

13.1 **Intent.** The Parties intend that payments under this Agreement be exempt from, or comply with, Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 and the regulations under it ("**Section 409A**"). This Agreement shall be interpreted accordingly. Each installment payment is a separate payment for purposes of Section 409A.

13.2 **Separation from Service.** Any payment under this Agreement that is subject to Section 409A and payable on termination of employment shall be paid only on Executive's "separation from service" within the meaning of Section 409A.

13.3 **Reimbursements.** Reimbursements under this Agreement shall be paid no later than the end of the calendar year after the year in which the expense is incurred. The amount eligible for reimbursement in one year shall not affect the amount eligible in any other year, and the right to reimbursement is not subject to liquidation or exchange for another benefit.

13.4 **Specified Employee.** If Executive is a "specified employee" under Section 409A on the date of her separation from service, any payment subject to Section 409A that would otherwise be made within six months after that date shall instead be paid in a lump sum on the first business day after the end of that six-month period, or on her death, if earlier.

## 14. Indemnification; Insurance

The Company shall indemnify Executive, and advance her expenses, to the fullest extent permitted by the Company's articles of incorporation, bylaws and the Oregon Business Corporation Act with respect to claims arising from her service as an officer or employee of the Company. The Company shall maintain directors' and officers' liability insurance covering Executive on the same basis as other officers of the Company.

## 15. Executive Representations

Executive represents that:

(a) she is not bound by any agreement with any Person other than the Company, Braikal or its affiliates that would prevent her from entering into or performing this Agreement; and

(b) she will not use or disclose in her work for the Company any confidential information or trade secrets belonging to any former employer or other third party.

## 16. Notices

All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given (a) when delivered by hand; (b) when received if sent by nationally recognized overnight courier; or (c) on the date sent by e-mail, if sent during normal business hours of the recipient, and otherwise on the next business day. Notices to the Company shall be sent to the attention of the General Manager at the Company's principal office, with a copy to the General Counsel of Parent. Notices to Executive shall be sent to her most recent home address and personal e-mail address on file with the Company. Either Party may change its address for notices by notice to the other.

## 17. Governing Law; Venue

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Oregon, without giving effect to any choice or conflict of law rule that would cause the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction. Any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be brought in the state or federal courts located in the State of Oregon, and each Party submits to the jurisdiction of those courts. EACH PARTY WAIVES, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, ANY RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

## 18. General Provisions

18.1 **Assignment.** This Agreement is personal to Executive and may not be assigned by her. The Company may assign this Agreement to Parent or to any successor to all or substantially all of the business or assets of the Company, and the Company shall require any such successor to assume this Agreement expressly.

18.2 **Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Executive and an authorized officer of the Company. No waiver shall be effective unless in writing. A waiver of any breach shall not be a waiver of any other breach.

18.3 **Severability.** Subject to Section 10.7, if any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

18.4 **Survival.** Sections 7 through 17 and this Section 18 survive the termination of Executive's employment and of this Agreement in accordance with their terms.

18.5 **Third-Party Beneficiary.** Parent is an intended third-party beneficiary of Sections 8 through 12 and may enforce them directly.

18.6 **Headings; Construction.** Headings are for convenience only. The word "including" means "including without limitation." This Agreement shall not be construed against either Party as drafter.

18.7 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which shall be an original and all of which together shall constitute one instrument.
