# UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF PEINOL PET NUTRITION, INC.

**A Delaware Corporation**

**Effective as of March 3, 2026**

The undersigned, being all of the members of the Board of Directors (the "**Board**") of Peinol Pet Nutrition, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "**Corporation**"), consent to and adopt the following resolutions. They do so in accordance with Section 141(f) of the General Corporation Law of the State of Delaware (the "**DGCL**") and the Bylaws of the Corporation. The resolutions take effect as if adopted at a duly called and held meeting of the Board at which a quorum was present and acting throughout.

## 1. Background

**1.1 Proposed Acquisition.** The Corporation has negotiated to acquire all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of Ostbrook Kibble Company, a Wisconsin corporation (the "**Company**"). The seller is Laraisk Family Holdings, LLC, a Wisconsin limited liability company (the "**Seller**"). The Company manufactures, packages and distributes dry and wet pet food products. The acquisition is the "**Acquisition**."

**1.2 Letter of Intent.** The Corporation and the Seller entered into a non-binding letter of intent dated December 12, 2025, setting out the principal proposed terms of the Acquisition.

**1.3 Materials Reviewed.** Before executing this consent, the Board received and reviewed the following:

(a) the substantially final form of the Stock Purchase Agreement among the Corporation, as buyer, the Seller and the Company (the "**Purchase Agreement**"), including the Disclosure Schedule delivered under it;

(b) the forms of the Ancillary Agreements described in Section 2.2; and

(c) presentations from management and the Corporation's legal and financial advisors concerning the Acquisition. Those presentations covered the business, financial, legal, regulatory and food safety due diligence on the Company, and the proposed senior acquisition financing.

**1.4 Determination.** The Board has considered the terms of the Acquisition and of the Transaction Documents defined in Section 2.2. It has determined that the Acquisition is advisable and in the best interests of the Corporation and its stockholders.

## 2. Approval of the Acquisition and Transaction Documents

**2.1 Purchase Agreement.** RESOLVED, that the Acquisition and the Purchase Agreement are approved and adopted in all respects. The Purchase Agreement provides for the Corporation to purchase the Shares for aggregate consideration of $84,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment as provided in the Purchase Agreement. The Purchase Price is payable at the closing of the Acquisition (the "**Closing**") as follows:

(a) a cash payment to the Seller of $67,800,000, as adjusted under the Purchase Agreement;

(b) delivery to the Seller of the Seller Note described in Section 2.2(a); and

(c) deposit of $4,200,000 (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**") with Cobalt Harbor Trust Company, N.A., as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**").

**2.2 Ancillary Agreements.** RESOLVED, that each of the following agreements and instruments, in the forms presented to the Board, is approved. Together with the Purchase Agreement, they are the "**Transaction Documents**."

(a) the Subordinated Promissory Note to be issued by the Corporation to the Seller in the original principal amount of $12,000,000 (the "**Seller Note**"). The Seller Note is subordinated to the Corporation's senior acquisition financing on the terms set forth in it;

(b) the Indemnity Escrow Agreement among the Corporation, the Seller and the Escrow Agent (the "**Escrow Agreement**"), under which the Escrow Agent will hold and disburse the Indemnity Escrow Amount;

(c) the letter agreement between the Corporation and the Seller concerning personal guaranties given by members of the Seller of certain obligations of the Company; and

(d) each other agreement, instrument, certificate and document contemplated by the Purchase Agreement to be executed and delivered by the Corporation at or before the Closing.

**2.3 Issuance of the Seller Note.** RESOLVED, that the Corporation is authorized to issue and deliver the Seller Note to the Seller at the Closing as part of the Purchase Price. The Corporation is also authorized to perform its obligations under the Seller Note in accordance with its terms.

**2.4 Escrow Deposit.** RESOLVED, that the Corporation is authorized to deposit the Indemnity Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent at the Closing in accordance with the Purchase Agreement and the Escrow Agreement.

## 3. Senior Acquisition Financing

**3.1 Approval.** RESOLVED, that the Corporation is authorized to enter into a senior credit agreement with an administrative agent and the lenders party to it, on or about the Closing Date. The agreement will provide senior acquisition financing to fund the cash portion of the Purchase Price and related fees and expenses. Its terms must be substantially consistent with the commitment and term sheet presented to the Board, with such changes as an Authorized Officer approves.

**3.2 Related Documents.** RESOLVED, that the Corporation is further authorized to execute, deliver and perform the security, guaranty and other loan documents customary for such financing. This includes any subordination agreement to be entered into with the Seller under the Seller Note.

## 4. Authorized Officers

**4.1 Designation.** RESOLVED, that each of the Chief Executive Officer, the Chief Financial Officer and the General Counsel of the Corporation is an "**Authorized Officer**." Each Authorized Officer, acting alone, is authorized and directed, in the name and on behalf of the Corporation, to take the actions listed in Section 4.2.

**4.2 Authorized Actions.** Each Authorized Officer may:

(a) execute and deliver the Transaction Documents and the financing documents described in Section 3, with such changes as the Authorized Officer approves as necessary or advisable and not materially adverse to the Corporation. The Authorized Officer's execution conclusively evidences that approval;

(b) cause the Corporation to pay the Purchase Price and all fees and expenses incurred in connection with the Acquisition;

(c) execute and deliver all certificates required of the Corporation under the Purchase Agreement, including the officer's certificate required for the Closing; and

(d) take all other actions, and execute all other documents, that the Authorized Officer considers necessary or advisable to consummate the Acquisition and carry out the purposes of these resolutions.

## 5. General Resolutions

**5.1 Ratification.** RESOLVED, that all actions previously taken by any director, officer, employee or agent of the Corporation in connection with the matters contemplated by these resolutions are ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects. This includes the execution of the letter of intent and the Confidentiality Agreement between the Corporation and the Company dated October 6, 2025.

**5.2 Secretary's Certificate.** RESOLVED, that the Secretary or any Assistant Secretary of the Corporation is authorized to certify these resolutions. That officer may also certify the incumbency and specimen signatures of the Authorized Officers to the Seller, the Escrow Agent, the senior lenders and any other Person who requests it.

## 6. Counterparts; Filing

This consent may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, and each counterpart is deemed an original. Together, the counterparts constitute one consent. This consent shall be filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Board.
