## 1. Introduction

**1.1 Delivery.** Laraisk Family Holdings, LLC, a Wisconsin limited liability company ("**Seller**"), delivers this disclosure schedule (this "**Disclosure Schedule**") under Article IV of the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 4, 2026 (the "**Agreement**"). The parties to the Agreement are Peinol Pet Nutrition, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), Seller, and Ostbrook Kibble Company, a Wisconsin corporation (the "**Company**"). Seller delivers this Disclosure Schedule concurrently with the execution of the Agreement.

**1.2 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Disclosure Schedule have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

**1.3 Numbering.** The section numbers in this Disclosure Schedule correspond to the section numbers of the Agreement. Each schedule is referred to in the Agreement as "Schedule" followed by the number of the corresponding section of the Agreement.

**1.4 Headings and Summaries.** Headings are inserted for convenience of reference only. Where this Disclosure Schedule summarizes or describes a contract or other document, the summary does not purport to be complete. It is qualified in its entirety by the full text of that document, a true and complete copy of which has been made available to Buyer in the electronic data room maintained for the Transactions (the "**Data Room**").

**1.5 No Admission.** No disclosure in this Disclosure Schedule constitutes an admission by Seller or the Company to any third party of any liability or obligation, or of any breach or violation of any contract or Law.

## SCHEDULE 4.8 — MATERIAL CONTRACTS

**Part A. Supply, Co-Manufacturing and Customer Contracts (Section 4.8(a))**

1. **Co-Manufacturing and Supply Agreement**, dated as of September 1, 2022, between the Company and Pawford Retail Group, Inc. ("**Pawford**"), as amended by Amendment No. 1 dated as of January 15, 2024 (the "**Pawford Agreement**").

   (a) *Subject matter:* The Company manufactures, packages and supplies dry kibble and wet canned pet food products sold under Pawford's private-label brands. The Company also supplies certain co-branded "Ostbrook Farmhouse" products.

   (b) *Term:* Initial term through August 31, 2027. The agreement then renews automatically for successive two-year terms unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least 180 days before the end of the then-current term.

   (c) *Volume:* Annual purchases by Pawford were approximately $31,600,000 in fiscal year 2025. Pawford is the Company's largest customer by revenue.

   (d) *Termination (Section 14.3):* Pawford may terminate the Pawford Agreement on 60 days' written notice if any transfer of more than 50% of the voting equity of the Company occurs, whether directly or indirectly and whether in one transaction or a series of related transactions, without Pawford's prior written consent.

   (e) *Other:* Product specifications and quality agreement (Exhibit C). Annual pricing review. Customer chargeback and recall cost-allocation provisions (Section 11).

2. **Manufacturing Services Agreement**, dated as of March 10, 2023, between the Company and Brindlewood Animal Health Corp. The Company manufactures prescription-diet dry formulas. Annual purchases were approximately $6,250,000 in fiscal year 2025. The term runs through March 9, 2027.

3. **Supply Agreement**, dated as of June 1, 2021, between the Company and Northvale Farm & Feed Cooperative, as amended. The Company supplies branded dry pet food for distribution through the cooperative's member retail stores. Annual purchases were approximately $4,900,000 in fiscal year 2025. The agreement has an evergreen term and may be terminated by either party on 120 days' notice.

4. **Distribution Agreement**, dated as of February 1, 2024, between the Company and Tallgrass Pet Distributors, LLC. The agreement grants non-exclusive regional distribution rights for the Company's branded wet and dry products in the Upper Midwest. Annual purchases were approximately $3,400,000 in fiscal year 2025. The term runs through January 31, 2027.

5. **Co-Packing Agreement**, dated as of October 1, 2024, between the Company and Harrowgate Treat Co. The Company co-packs baked dog biscuits and treats. Annual purchases were approximately $1,150,000 in fiscal year 2025. The term runs through September 30, 2026.
