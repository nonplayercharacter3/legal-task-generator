**Re: Personal Guaranties of Ostbrook Kibble Company Obligations**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter agreement (this "**Side Letter**") is delivered in connection with the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 4, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"), by and among Peinol Pet Nutrition, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), Laraisk Family Holdings, LLC, a Wisconsin limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and Ostbrook Kibble Company, a Wisconsin corporation (the "**Company**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is purchasing all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company from Seller.

Seller has informed Buyer that certain members of Seller have personally guaranteed obligations of the Company to third parties. Seller has asked Buyer to address those guaranties after the Closing. This Side Letter sets out Buyer's agreement on that subject. It is an Ancillary Agreement for purposes of the Purchase Agreement.

## 1. Definitions

**1.1 Purchase Agreement Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement, including "Closing," "Closing Date," "Losses," "Business Day" and "Ancillary Agreements."

**1.2 Additional Terms.** In this Side Letter:

"**Guarantor Members**" means the members of Seller listed on Annex A, each in that member's individual capacity.

"**Guaranties**" means the personal guaranties, suretyship agreements and similar credit support instruments described on Annex A, each as in effect on the date of this Side Letter.

"**Guaranteed Contracts**" means:

(a) the equipment lease under which the Company leases the twin-screw extruder lines and related drying and coating equipment used at its manufacturing facility; and

(b) the ingredient supply contracts under which the Company purchases protein meals, grains, fats and vitamin and mineral premixes,

in each case as described on Annex A.

"**Guaranteed Obligations**" means all obligations and liabilities of the Company under the Guaranteed Contracts, whether arising before, on or after the Closing Date, for which any Guarantor Member is or may become liable under any Guaranty.

## 2. Indemnification of Seller and Guarantor Members

**2.1 Indemnity.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and each Guarantor Member, and their respective heirs, estates, successors and permitted assigns, from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to the Guaranteed Obligations or any Guaranty. This includes any demand made on any Guarantor Member under a Guaranty, and any payment made or performance rendered by any Guarantor Member in satisfaction of the Guaranteed Obligations.

**2.2 Reimbursement.** Buyer shall pay to Seller or the applicable Guarantor Member the amount of any Losses covered by Section 2.1 within ten Business Days after receiving a written demand. The demand shall be accompanied by reasonable supporting documentation, such as a copy of the demand received under the Guaranty and evidence of payment.

**2.3 Designation.** Buyer's obligations under this Section 2 are deemed a Buyer Fundamental Obligation.

**2.4 Procedures.** Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement applies to any claim under this Section 2, as if Seller or the applicable Guarantor Member were the Indemnified Party and Buyer were the Indemnifying Party.

## 3. Release Efforts

**3.1 Efforts to Obtain Releases.** Before and after the Closing, Buyer shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain from each counterparty to a Guaranteed Contract a written release of each Guarantor Member from the applicable Guaranty. Those efforts may include offering a substitute guaranty or other credit support from Buyer or one of its Affiliates. Buyer is not required to pay any consent fee or other consideration to any counterparty to obtain a release, other than customary substitute credit support.

**3.2 Cooperation.** Seller shall, and shall use commercially reasonable efforts to cause each Guarantor Member to, cooperate reasonably with Buyer in seeking those releases. That cooperation includes executing customary release documentation reasonably requested by a counterparty.

**3.3 No Amendments Increasing Exposure.** After the Closing, Buyer shall not permit the Company to amend, extend or renew any Guaranteed Contract in a manner that would increase the liability of any Guarantor Member under a Guaranty, unless that Guarantor Member has first been released from the Guaranty or consents in writing.

## 4. Guarantor Members as Beneficiaries

Each Guarantor Member is an express third-party beneficiary of Section 2 and Section 3.3 and may enforce them directly against Buyer. No other Person, other than the parties to this Side Letter, has any rights under it.

## 5. Miscellaneous

**5.1 Notices.** Notices under this Side Letter shall be given in the manner provided in Section 11.1 of the Purchase Agreement. Notices to a Guarantor Member shall be sent in care of Seller.

**5.2 Amendment and Waiver.** This Side Letter may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer and Seller. No amendment that adversely affects the rights of a Guarantor Member under Section 2 is effective against that Guarantor Member without that member's written consent. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the party against whom it is asserted.

**5.3 Assignment.** No party may assign this Side Letter without the prior written consent of the other party. However, Buyer may collaterally assign its rights under this Side Letter to its senior lenders without relieving Buyer of its obligations.

**5.4 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Side Letter shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to any conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction. Sections 11.8 and 11.10 of the Purchase Agreement apply to this Side Letter as if set out in full in it.

**5.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Side Letter is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**5.6 Counterparts.** This Side Letter may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
