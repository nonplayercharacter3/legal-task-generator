IT MAY NOT BE SOLD, ASSIGNED, PLEDGED OR OTHERWISE TRANSFERRED EXCEPT AS PERMITTED IN SECTION 10.**

## 1. Promise to Pay; Defined Terms

**1.1 Promise to Pay.** For value received, **Peinol Pet Nutrition, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Maker**"), promises to pay to the order of **Laraisk Family Holdings, LLC**, a Wisconsin limited liability company ("**Holder**"), the principal amount of $12,000,000 (the "**Principal Amount**"), together with interest on the unpaid principal balance as provided in Section 2. All payments are subject to the terms of this subordinated promissory note (this "**Note**").

**1.2 Purchase Agreement.** Maker issues this Note under Section 2.2(b) of the Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 4, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"), by and among Maker, as Buyer, Holder, as Seller, and Ostbrook Kibble Company, a Wisconsin corporation (the "**Company**"). This Note is the "Seller Note" referred to in the Purchase Agreement. It is delivered as part of the Purchase Price for the Shares.

**1.3 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required to close.

"**Default Rate**" means the Interest Rate plus two percent (2.0%) per annum.

"**Interest Payment Date**" means the last Business Day of each March, June, September and December, beginning with the first such date to occur after the Issue Date.

"**Interest Rate**" means seven percent (7.0%) per annum.

"**Issue Date**" means the Closing Date.

"**Maturity Date**" means the fifth anniversary of the Issue Date.

"**Senior Agent**" means the administrative agent or collateral agent under the Senior Credit Agreement, and any successor in that capacity.

"**Senior Credit Agreement**" means the credit agreement entered into by Maker on or about the Issue Date with the Senior Agent and the lenders party to it, providing senior acquisition financing for the Transactions, as amended, restated, supplemented, refinanced or replaced from time to time.

"**Senior Indebtedness**" has the meaning given in Section 6.1.

## 2. Interest

**2.1 Accrual.** Interest shall accrue on the unpaid principal balance of this Note from the Issue Date until paid in full, at the Interest Rate. Interest is computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

**2.2 Payment of Interest.** Accrued interest is payable in arrears on each Interest Payment Date and on the Maturity Date. If a payment of cash interest on an Interest Payment Date is prohibited under Section 6, the unpaid interest shall be added to the principal balance of this Note on that Interest Payment Date. Interest added to principal in that way bears interest under Section 2.1 from that date.

**2.3 Default Interest.** While an Event of Default under Section 7.1(a) or 7.1(c) is continuing, the unpaid principal balance and any overdue interest shall bear interest at the Default Rate.

**2.4 Usury Savings.** In no event shall the interest payable under this Note exceed the maximum rate permitted by applicable Law. Any amount received in excess of that rate shall be applied to reduce the principal balance, or, if principal has been paid in full, refunded to Maker.

## 3. Payment of Principal; Manner of Payment

**3.1 Maturity.** The entire unpaid principal balance of this Note, together with all accrued and unpaid interest, is due and payable on the Maturity Date.

**3.2 Manner of Payment.** All payments under this Note shall be made in lawful money of the United States, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by Holder in writing at least five Business Days before the payment date. If any payment falls due on a day that is not a Business Day, it shall be made on the next Business Day, and interest shall continue to accrue for the additional days.

**3.3 Application of Payments.** Each payment shall be applied first to accrued and unpaid interest, and then to unpaid principal.

**3.4 Withholding.** Maker may deduct and withhold from any payment under this Note the amounts required to be deducted and withheld under applicable Tax Law. Any amount so withheld and paid to the appropriate Governmental Authority is treated as paid to Holder.

## 4. Prepayment

**4.1 Voluntary Prepayment.** Maker may prepay this Note in whole or in part at any time, without premium or penalty, upon at least five Business Days' written notice to Holder. Each prepayment shall include accrued and unpaid interest on the amount prepaid. Any prepayment is subject to Section 6.

**4.2 Mandatory Prepayment on Change of Control.** If Maker consummates a sale of all or substantially all of its assets, or a merger or other transaction in which the stockholders of Maker immediately before the transaction cease to hold a majority of the voting power of the surviving entity, Maker shall prepay this Note in full on consummation of that transaction. The prepayment is subject to Section 6.

## 5. Set-Off

**5.1 Right of Set-Off.** Maker may set off against any amount of principal or interest otherwise payable under this Note the amount of any Losses for which any Buyer Indemnitee is entitled to indemnification from Seller under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, in accordance with Section 9.6 of the Purchase Agreement.

**5.2 Losses.** For purposes of this Note, "**Losses**" means out-of-pocket damages, liabilities, judgments, fines, penalties and reasonable attorneys' fees actually paid by a Buyer Indemnitee to a third party, excluding costs of any product recall, market withdrawal or stock recovery, and excluding any diminution in value, lost profits, or multiple-of-earnings or similar valuation-based damages.

**5.3 Determination of Set-Off Amounts.** Maker may set off an amount under Section 5.1 only to the extent that the amount:

(a) has been agreed in writing by Holder;

(b) is deemed accepted by Holder under the claims procedures of Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement; or

(c) has been finally determined by a final, non-appealable judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction under Section 12.2.

**5.4 Pending Claims.** If Maker has delivered a good-faith written notice of a claim for Losses that has not been resolved under Section 5.3 when a payment falls due, Maker may withhold from that payment an amount equal to the claimed amount, not exceeding the payment then due. Maker shall pay the withheld amount, with interest at the Interest Rate from its original due date, within five Business Days after the claim is resolved, to the extent the claim is resolved in Holder's favor.

**5.5 Notice; Effect.** Maker shall give Holder written notice of each set-off or withholding, stating the amount and the related claim. Any amount set off under this Section 5 reduces the amounts owed under this Note and is treated as paid to Holder on the date of the set-off. The proper exercise of the right of set-off or withholding under this Section 5 is not an Event of Default.

## 6. Subordination

**6.1 Senior Indebtedness.** "**Senior Indebtedness**" means all obligations of Maker and its Subsidiaries, now existing or later arising, under the Senior Credit Agreement and the related loan documents. This includes principal, interest (including interest accruing after the commencement of any insolvency proceeding, whether or not allowed), fees, expenses, indemnities, and obligations under hedging and cash-management arrangements secured under those loan documents, together with any refinancing or replacement of any of them.

**6.2 Subordination.** The payment of all amounts under this Note is subordinated in right of payment to the prior payment in full in cash of all Senior Indebtedness. "Payment in full" means payment of all Senior Indebtedness and termination of all commitments under the Senior Credit Agreement.

**6.3 Permitted Payments.** Until the Senior Indebtedness is paid in full, Maker may make, and Holder may receive, regularly scheduled payments of cash interest under Section 2.2, and payments of principal permitted under the Senior Credit Agreement, only if no payment default or other event of default under the Senior Credit Agreement has occurred and is continuing or would result from that payment.

**6.4 Payment Blockage.** If a payment default under the Senior Credit Agreement occurs, no payment shall be made on this Note until that default is cured or waived. If any other event of default under the Senior Credit Agreement occurs, and the Senior Agent delivers a written blockage notice to Maker and Holder, no payment shall be made on this Note for up to 179 days after Holder receives that notice, unless the default is earlier cured or waived or the Senior Agent consents. No more than one blockage notice may be delivered in any period of 360 consecutive days.

**6.5 Insolvency.** In any liquidation, dissolution, bankruptcy, reorganization or similar proceeding of Maker, the holders of Senior Indebtedness are entitled to receive payment in full before Holder receives any payment on this Note. Any distribution that would otherwise be payable to Holder shall be paid directly to the Senior Agent until the Senior Indebtedness is paid in full.

**6.6 Turnover.** If Holder receives any payment on this Note that is prohibited by this Section 6, Holder shall hold it in trust for the holders of Senior Indebtedness and promptly pay it over to the Senior Agent.

**6.7 Standstill.** Until the Senior Indebtedness is paid in full, Holder shall not accelerate this Note or commence any enforcement action against Maker until the earliest of (a) 180 days after Holder gives written notice of an Event of Default to the Senior Agent, (b) acceleration of the Senior Indebtedness, and (c) commencement of an insolvency proceeding of Maker.

**6.8 Subordination Agreement.** At the request of the Senior Agent, Holder shall execute a subordination agreement with the Senior Agent on terms consistent with this Section 6 and otherwise customary for subordinated seller financing.

**6.9 Set-Off Not a Payment.** A set-off or withholding by Maker under Section 5 is not a payment to Holder for purposes of this Section 6.

## 7. Events of Default

**7.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Maker fails to pay any principal of this Note when due, or fails to pay any interest within ten Business Days after it is due, in each case to the extent the payment is not prohibited by Section 6 and is not the subject of a set-off or withholding under Section 5;

(b) Maker fails to perform any other covenant in this Note, and the failure continues for 30 days after Holder gives written notice of it to Maker;

(c) Maker commences a voluntary case or proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar Law, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or has an involuntary case commenced against it that is not dismissed within 60 days; or

(d) the Senior Indebtedness is accelerated as a result of an event of default under the Senior Credit Agreement, and the acceleration is not rescinded.

**7.2 Remedies.** Subject to Section 6, while an Event of Default is continuing, Holder may declare the unpaid principal balance and all accrued interest immediately due and payable by written notice to Maker. On an Event of Default under Section 7.1(c), those amounts become due and payable automatically, without notice or other action.

**7.3 Costs of Collection.** Subject to Section 6, Maker shall pay Holder's reasonable costs of collection, including reasonable attorneys' fees, incurred in enforcing this Note after an Event of Default.

## 8. Waivers

Maker waives presentment, demand, protest, notice of dishonor and all other notices in connection with this Note, except notices expressly required by it. No delay or omission by Holder in exercising any right is a waiver of that right, and no single or partial exercise of any right precludes any further exercise of it.

## 9. Amendment

This Note may be amended, and any provision of it waived, only by a written instrument signed by Maker and Holder. No amendment of Section 6 is effective without the written consent of the Senior Agent while any Senior Indebtedness is outstanding.

## 10. Transfer

Holder may not sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer this Note or any interest in it without the prior written consent of Maker and, while any Senior Indebtedness is outstanding, the Senior Agent. Any permitted transferee takes this Note subject to Sections 5 and 6. Maker may collaterally assign its rights under this Note to the Senior Agent.

## 11. Notices

All notices under this Note shall be in writing and given in the manner provided in Section 11.1 of the Purchase Agreement, to the addresses of Buyer and Seller provided under that Section, or to such other address as a party designates by notice.

## 12. Governing Law; Jurisdiction; Jury Waiver

**12.1 Governing Law.** This Note, and all claims and causes of action arising out of or relating to it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to any conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction.

**12.2 Jurisdiction.** Maker and Holder each irrevocably submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts sitting in New York County, New York, for any action arising out of or relating to this Note. Each waives any objection to venue in those courts and any claim of inconvenient forum.

**12.3 Waiver of Jury Trial.** MAKER AND HOLDER EACH IRREVOCABLY WAIVE ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS NOTE.

## 13. Miscellaneous

**13.1 Successors and Assigns.** This Note binds Maker and its successors and permitted assigns, and benefits Holder and its successors and permitted assigns.

**13.2 Severability.** If any provision of this Note is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**13.3 Lost Note.** On receipt of an affidavit of loss and a customary indemnity from Holder, Maker shall issue a replacement note on the same terms in place of any lost, stolen or destroyed Note.
