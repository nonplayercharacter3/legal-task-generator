# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of March 4, 2026, by and among **Peinol Pet Nutrition, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), **Laraisk Family Holdings, LLC**, a Wisconsin limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and **Ostbrook Kibble Company**, a Wisconsin corporation (the "**Company**"). Buyer, Seller and the Company are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Seller is the record and beneficial owner of all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company (the "**Shares**").

B. The Company manufactures, packages and distributes dry and wet pet food products.

C. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. PURCHASE AND SALE

**1.1 Purchase and Sale of the Shares.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase and acquire from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all Liens, constituting all of the issued and outstanding stock of the Company.

**1.2 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by the electronic exchange of documents and signatures. It shall occur on the third Business Day after the conditions set forth in Article VII have been satisfied or waived, other than conditions that by their nature are to be satisfied at the Closing, or on such other date as Buyer and Seller agree in writing. The date on which the Closing occurs is the "**Closing Date**."

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Purchase Price.** The aggregate consideration for the Shares is $84,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment as provided in Section 2.5.

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay and deliver the Purchase Price as follows:

(a) to Seller, by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by Seller in writing at least two Business Days before the Closing Date, an amount equal to $67,800,000, plus or minus the Estimated Adjustment Amount determined under Section 2.5(a) (the "**Closing Cash Payment**");

(b) to Seller, the Seller Note in the original principal amount of $12,000,000, duly executed by Buyer; and

(c) to Cobalt Harbor Trust Company, N.A., as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**"), by wire transfer of immediately available funds, $4,200,000 (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**"). The Escrow Agent shall hold this amount in accordance with the Escrow Agreement.

**2.3 Seller Note.** The Seller Note shall be a subordinated promissory note issued by Buyer to Seller in the form attached as Exhibit A. It shall bear interest and be payable on the terms set forth in it, and it shall be subordinated to Buyer's Senior Indebtedness as provided in it.

**2.4 Indemnity Escrow.** The Indemnity Escrow Amount shall secure, in part, Seller's indemnification obligations under Article IX. It shall be held and disbursed by the Escrow Agent in accordance with the Escrow Agreement, which Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent shall enter into at the Closing in the form attached as Exhibit B.

**2.5 Working Capital Adjustment.**

(a) *Estimated Adjustment.* At least three Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a good-faith estimate of Closing Working Capital, with reasonable supporting detail. If estimated Closing Working Capital exceeds the Target Working Capital, the Closing Cash Payment shall be increased by the excess. If estimated Closing Working Capital is less than the Target Working Capital, the Closing Cash Payment shall be decreased by the shortfall. The amount of any such increase or decrease is the "**Estimated Adjustment Amount**."

(b) *Closing Statement.* Within 90 days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a statement setting forth Buyer's calculation of Closing Working Capital (the "**Closing Statement**").

(c) *Disputes.* Seller may dispute the Closing Statement by written notice delivered within 45 days after receiving it. Any dispute not resolved by the Parties within 30 days after that notice shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm mutually selected by Buyer and Seller. That firm's determination shall be final and binding absent manifest error.

(d) *Final Payment.* Within five Business Days after Closing Working Capital is finally determined, Buyer or Seller, as applicable, shall pay the difference between the final adjustment amount and the Estimated Adjustment Amount by wire transfer of immediately available funds.

**2.6 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement the amounts required to be deducted and withheld under applicable Tax Law. Any amount so withheld and paid to the appropriate Governmental Authority shall be treated as paid to the Person in respect of which the withholding was made.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING DELIVERIES

**3.1 Seller Deliveries.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver or cause to be delivered to Buyer:

(a) stock certificates evidencing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or accompanied by stock powers duly executed in blank;

(b) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Seller;

(c) written resignations, effective as of the Closing, of the directors and officers of the Company designated by Buyer;

(d) the certificate required by Section 7.2(d);

(e) a properly completed IRS Form W-9 of Seller; and

(f) the third-party consents listed on Schedule 4.6.

**3.2 Buyer Deliveries.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver or cause to be delivered:

(a) the Closing Cash Payment, to Seller;

(b) the Seller Note, duly executed by Buyer, to Seller;

(c) the Indemnity Escrow Amount, to the Escrow Agent;

(d) the Escrow Agreement, duly executed by Buyer, to Seller; and

(e) the certificate required by Section 7.3(c), to Seller.

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer concurrently with the execution of this Agreement (the "**Disclosure Schedule**"), Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization and Good Standing.** Seller is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Wisconsin. The Company is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Wisconsin. The Company is duly qualified to do business in each jurisdiction where such qualification is required, except where the failure to be so qualified would not have a Material Adverse Effect.

**4.2 Authority; Enforceability.** Seller and the Company each have full power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and each Ancillary Agreement to which it is a party, and to perform its obligations under them. This Agreement constitutes, and each such Ancillary Agreement will constitute when executed, the legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller and the Company, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 Capitalization.** The authorized capital stock of the Company consists of 10,000 shares of common stock, of which 1,000 shares are issued and outstanding and constitute the Shares. All of the Shares are duly authorized, validly issued, fully paid and non-assessable. There are no outstanding options, warrants, rights, convertible securities or other agreements relating to the issuance, sale or transfer of any equity interest in the Company.

**4.4 Title to Shares.** Seller is the sole record and beneficial owner of the Shares, free and clear of all Liens. At the Closing, Buyer will acquire good and valid title to the Shares, free and clear of all Liens.

**4.5 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance by Seller and the Company of this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements do not and will not:

(a) violate the organizational documents of Seller or the Company;

(b) violate any Law applicable to Seller or the Company; or

(c) except as set forth on Schedule 4.6, conflict with, result in a breach of, constitute a default under, or give rise to any right of termination, acceleration or modification under, any Material Contract.

**4.6 Consents.** Except as set forth on Schedule 4.6, no consent, approval, waiver or authorization of, or notice to or filing with, any Governmental Authority or other Person is required in connection with the execution, delivery and performance by Seller and the Company of this Agreement or the consummation of the Transactions.

**4.7 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the audited balance sheets of the Company as of December 31, 2024 and December 31, 2025, together with the related statements of income and cash flows for the years then ended (the "**Financial Statements**"). The Financial Statements were prepared in accordance with GAAP consistently applied. They fairly present in all material respects the financial position and results of operations of the Company as of their dates and for the periods covered.

**4.8 Material Contracts.** Schedule 4.8 lists each of the following contracts to which the Company is a party (the "**Material Contracts**"):

(a) each supply, co-manufacturing or customer contract involving annual payments in excess of $500,000;

(b) each equipment lease and each ingredient or raw-material supply contract involving annual payments in excess of $250,000; and

(c) each contract for indebtedness, guaranty or lien.

Each Material Contract is valid, binding and in full force and effect. Neither the Company nor, to Seller's Knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach of or default under any Material Contract.

**4.9 Absence of Changes.** Since December 31, 2025, the Company has conducted its business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice, and there has not been any Material Adverse Effect.

**4.10 Litigation.** Except as set forth on Schedule 4.10, there is no Action pending or, to Seller's Knowledge, threatened against the Company or affecting the Shares.

**4.11 Compliance with Laws; Permits.** The Company is, and since January 1, 2023 has been, in compliance in all material respects with all applicable Laws. The Company holds all Permits material to the conduct of its business, including all registrations and licenses required by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration, the U.S. Department of Agriculture and state feed control officials.

**4.12 Taxes.** The Company has timely filed all income and other material Tax Returns required to be filed by it, and has paid all Taxes shown as due on them. There are no Liens for Taxes on the assets of the Company other than Permitted Liens. No audit or other proceeding with respect to Taxes of the Company is pending or threatened in writing.

**4.13 Environmental Matters.** The Company is in compliance in all material respects with all Environmental Laws. The Company has not received any written notice of any violation of, or liability under, any Environmental Law that remains unresolved.

**4.14 Product Safety and Recalls.** Each product manufactured, packaged or distributed by the Company since January 1, 2021 has been:

(a) manufactured in compliance in all material respects with the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act, the Food Safety Modernization Act and applicable state feed laws;

(b) free from adulteration and misbranding within the meaning of those Laws;

(c) free from contamination by Salmonella, Listeria, aflatoxin and other pathogens or toxins in excess of permitted levels; and

(d) formulated to meet the nutrient profiles stated on its labeling.

Since January 1, 2021, the Company has not conducted or been requested by any Governmental Authority or customer to conduct any product recall, market withdrawal or stock recovery, and to Seller's Knowledge no facts exist that would reasonably be expected to give rise to one.

**4.15 Intellectual Property.** The Company owns or has a valid right to use all Intellectual Property used in its business. To Seller's Knowledge, the conduct of the business does not infringe the Intellectual Property of any Person.

**4.16 Employees and Benefit Plans.** Schedule 4.16 lists each material Benefit Plan. Each Benefit Plan has been maintained in compliance in all material respects with its terms and applicable Law. The Company is not party to any collective bargaining agreement.

**4.17 Real Property.** Schedule 4.17 lists all real property owned or leased by the Company. The Company has good title to, or a valid leasehold interest in, each such property, free and clear of all Liens other than Permitted Liens.

**4.18 Insurance.** Schedule 4.18 lists all insurance policies maintained by the Company, including product liability and product contamination coverage. All such policies are in full force and effect.

**4.19 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the Transactions based on arrangements made by Seller or the Company.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as of the date of this Agreement and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**5.1 Organization.** Buyer is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware.

**5.2 Authority; Enforceability.** Buyer has full corporate power and authority to execute and deliver this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party, including the Seller Note, and to perform its obligations under them. That execution, delivery and performance has been duly authorized by all necessary corporate action of Buyer. This Agreement constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Buyer, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**5.3 No Conflicts; Consents.** The execution, delivery and performance by Buyer of this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements do not violate Buyer's organizational documents or any Law applicable to Buyer, and require no consent of any Governmental Authority.

**5.4 Sufficiency of Funds.** At the Closing, Buyer will have sufficient immediately available funds to pay the Closing Cash Payment and the Indemnity Escrow Amount.

**5.5 Investment Intent.** Buyer is acquiring the Shares for its own account for investment and not with a view to distribution in violation of applicable securities Laws.

**5.6 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the Transactions based on arrangements made by Buyer.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date of this Agreement until the Closing, except as consented to in writing by Buyer, Seller shall cause the Company to:

(a) operate in the ordinary course of business consistent with past practice;

(b) maintain its assets, Permits and insurance;

(c) maintain its food safety, quality assurance and supplier verification programs; and

(d) not declare dividends, issue equity, incur indebtedness outside the ordinary course, or amend any Material Contract.

**6.2 Access.** Until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to afford Buyer and its representatives reasonable access, during normal business hours, to the Company's properties, books, records, contracts and personnel, including its manufacturing facilities and quality assurance records.

**6.3 Efforts; Consents.** Each Party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to cause the conditions in Article VII to be satisfied. Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain, before the Closing, each consent listed on Schedule 4.6.

**6.4 Notification.** Seller shall promptly notify Buyer in writing of any fact or event that would reasonably be expected to cause any condition in Section 7.2 not to be satisfied, including any product complaint, regulatory inspection observation or recall inquiry.

**6.5 Exclusivity.** Until the Closing or the earlier termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not, and shall cause the Company not to, solicit, negotiate or enter into any agreement with any Person other than Buyer regarding any sale of the Shares or of all or substantially all of the Company's assets.

**6.6 Confidentiality.** After the Closing, Seller shall keep confidential all non-public information concerning the Company, except as required by Law.

**6.7 Non-Competition.** For five years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, directly or indirectly, engage in the manufacture or distribution of pet food products in the United States or Canada.

## ARTICLE VII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**7.1 Conditions to Obligations of Each Party.** The obligations of each Party to consummate the Closing are subject to the absence of any Order or Law restraining or prohibiting the Transactions.

**7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** The obligations of Buyer to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction, or waiver by Buyer, of the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Seller in Article IV shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date, except that the Fundamental Representations shall be true and correct in all respects;

(b) Seller and the Company shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by them before the Closing;

(c) each of the consents listed on Schedule 4.6 shall have been obtained and shall be in full force and effect;

(d) Buyer shall have received a certificate of an authorized officer of Seller certifying satisfaction of the conditions in Sections 7.2(a) and 7.2(b);

(e) no Material Adverse Effect shall have occurred; and

(f) Seller shall have made the deliveries required by Section 3.1.

**7.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** The obligations of Seller to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction, or waiver by Seller, of the following conditions:

(a) the representations and warranties of Buyer in Article V shall be true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;

(b) Buyer shall have performed in all material respects all covenants required to be performed by it before the Closing; and

(c) Seller shall have received a certificate of an authorized officer of Buyer certifying satisfaction of the conditions in Sections 7.3(a) and 7.3(b), together with the deliveries required by Section 3.2.

## ARTICLE VIII. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing:

(a) by mutual written consent of Buyer and Seller;

(b) by either Buyer or Seller, if the Closing has not occurred by June 30, 2026, provided that the terminating Party's breach is not the principal cause of the failure to close;

(c) by Buyer, if Seller or the Company has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.2 would not be satisfied, and the breach is not cured within 20 days after written notice; or

(d) by Seller, if Buyer has breached any representation, warranty or covenant such that a condition in Section 7.3 would not be satisfied, and the breach is not cured within 20 days after written notice.

**8.2 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination under Section 8.1, this Agreement shall become void and of no further effect. No Party shall have any liability, except for liability for any willful breach of this Agreement before termination. Sections 6.6, this Section 8.2 and Article XI shall survive termination.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The Fundamental Representations shall survive the Closing until 60 days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. All other representations and warranties of Seller and Buyer contained in this Agreement shall survive until the Escrow Release Date. The covenants and agreements of the Parties, and the Buyer Fundamental Obligations, shall survive the Closing until fully performed in accordance with their terms. Any claim asserted in writing with reasonable specificity before the expiration of the applicable survival period shall survive until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer, its Affiliates (including, after the Closing, the Company) and their respective directors, officers, employees and agents (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in Article IV;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller or, before the Closing, the Company in this Agreement; and

(c) any Pre-Closing Taxes or Indebtedness of the Company not reflected in Closing Working Capital.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller, its members and their respective Affiliates, managers, officers and agents (the "**Seller Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in Article V;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement; and

(c) any breach or non-performance of any Buyer Fundamental Obligation.

**9.4 Claims Procedures.**

(a) *Notice.* A Person seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall give prompt written notice of the claim to the Party from whom indemnification is sought (the "**Indemnifying Party**"). The notice shall describe the claim in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice shall not relieve the Indemnifying Party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced by the failure.

(b) *Third-Party Claims.* The Indemnifying Party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the Indemnified Party, by written notice delivered within 20 days after receiving notice of the claim. The Indemnifying Party may not settle any such claim without the Indemnified Party's consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money and includes a full release of the Indemnified Party. Seller may not assume the defense of any claim relating to a product recall or market withdrawal, or any claim brought by a Governmental Authority or a Material Contract counterparty.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) *Cap.* The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 9.2(a), and the aggregate liability of Buyer under Section 9.3(a), shall not exceed $8,400,000.

(b) *Basket.* Neither Seller under Section 9.2(a) nor Buyer under Section 9.3(a) shall be liable until the aggregate Losses for which it would otherwise be liable exceed $420,000. Once that threshold is exceeded, the Indemnifying Party shall be liable for all such Losses from the first dollar.

(c) *Materiality Scrape.* For purposes of calculating Losses under this Article IX, any materiality or Material Adverse Effect qualification in any representation or warranty shall be disregarded.

(d) *Exclusions.* The limitations in Sections 9.5(a) and 9.5(b) shall not apply to Losses arising from the Fundamental Representations or Fraud. Those limitations shall not apply to Buyer Fundamental Obligations.

(e) *Insurance.* Losses shall be calculated net of insurance proceeds actually received by the Indemnified Party in respect of the matter, less the costs of recovery and any resulting premium increase.

**9.6 Source of Recovery; Set-Off.** Any amount owed by Seller to a Buyer Indemnitee under this Article IX shall be satisfied in the following order:

(a) first, from the Indemnity Escrow Amount, in accordance with the Escrow Agreement;

(b) second, at Buyer's election, by set-off against principal and accrued interest otherwise payable under the Seller Note, in accordance with the Seller Note; and

(c) third, directly from Seller.

**9.7 Exclusive Remedy.** From and after the Closing, the indemnification provided in this Article IX shall be the sole and exclusive remedy of the Parties for any claim arising out of this Agreement. This Section 9.7 does not limit claims for Fraud, claims under Section 2.5, or claims for specific performance or injunctive relief.

**9.8 Treatment of Payments.** All indemnification payments under this Article IX shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes, unless otherwise required by Law.
