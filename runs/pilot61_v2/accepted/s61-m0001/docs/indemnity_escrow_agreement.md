# INDEMNITY ESCROW AGREEMENT

This INDEMNITY ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of ____________, 2026, by and among **Peinol Pet Nutrition, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), **Laraisk Family Holdings, LLC**, a Wisconsin limited liability company ("**Seller**"), and **Cobalt Harbor Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Principal**" and together the "**Principals**."

## RECITALS

A. Buyer, Seller and Ostbrook Kibble Company, a Wisconsin corporation (the "**Company**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of March 4, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is purchasing from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock of the Company.

B. Section 2.2(c) of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit a portion of the Purchase Price with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. That deposit secures, in part, Seller's indemnification obligations under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

C. The Principals wish to appoint the Escrow Agent to hold and disburse that deposit, and the Escrow Agent is willing to act, on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND APPOINTMENT

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required to close.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 5.1.

"**Escrow Funds**" means the Indemnity Escrow Amount deposited under Section 2.1, together with all interest, dividends and other income earned on it, less any amounts disbursed under this Agreement.

"**Escrow Release Date**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction determined in accordance with Section 12. An order or judgment is non-appealable when the time for appeal has expired without an appeal being filed, or when all appeals have been finally decided.

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means written instructions executed by authorized representatives of both Buyer and Seller and delivered to the Escrow Agent.

"**Pending Claim Amount**" means, as of any date, the aggregate amount of all claims set forth in Claim Notices delivered before that date that have not been resolved and paid under Section 5 or Section 6.

**1.2 Appointment.** The Principals appoint the Escrow Agent as escrow agent under this Agreement. The Escrow Agent accepts that appointment and agrees to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Funds in accordance with this Agreement.

## 2. ESCROW DEPOSIT

**2.1 Deposit.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit with the Escrow Agent, by wire transfer of immediately available funds, $4,200,000 (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**"). The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt of the Indemnity Escrow Amount to the Principals in writing on the day it is received.

**2.2 Escrow Account.** The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Funds in a segregated account established in the name of the Escrow Agent for the benefit of the Principals (the "**Escrow Account**"). The Escrow Funds shall not be commingled with any other funds held by the Escrow Agent.

**2.3 No Security Interest.** The Escrow Funds are not subject to any lien, right of set-off or other claim of the Escrow Agent or any creditor of the Escrow Agent. No Principal may pledge, assign or encumber its interest in the Escrow Funds, except as provided in this Agreement.

## 3. INVESTMENT OF ESCROW FUNDS

**3.1 Permitted Investments.** Unless otherwise directed by Joint Written Instructions, the Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds in a U.S. government money market fund registered under the Investment Company Act of 1940 that invests solely in direct obligations of the United States and repurchase agreements backed by those obligations.

**3.2 Liquidity.** The Escrow Agent shall have the right to liquidate any investment as needed to make any disbursement required under this Agreement. The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any loss resulting from an investment made in accordance with this Section 3, or from any liquidation of an investment before its maturity.

**3.3 Income.** All interest and other income earned on the Escrow Funds shall become part of the Escrow Funds and shall be disbursed in the same manner as the Indemnity Escrow Amount.

## 4. RELEASE OF ESCROW FUNDS

**4.1 Escrow Release Date.** The "**Escrow Release Date**" is the date that is six (6) months after the Closing Date. If that date is not a Business Day, the Escrow Release Date is the next Business Day. Within two Business Days after the Closing, the Principals shall deliver a Joint Written Instruction to the Escrow Agent confirming the Closing Date and the resulting Escrow Release Date.

**4.2 Release on the Escrow Release Date.** Within three Business Days after the Escrow Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Seller the Escrow Funds then remaining, less the Pending Claim Amount as of the Escrow Release Date. The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the Pending Claim Amount until each related claim is resolved under Section 5 or Section 6.

**4.3 Release on Resolution of Pending Claims.** Upon the resolution of any claim included in the Pending Claim Amount, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the amount retained for that claim:

(a) to Buyer, to the extent the claim is resolved in Buyer's favor; and

(b) after the Escrow Release Date, to Seller, to the extent the claim is resolved in Seller's favor or the retained amount exceeds the amount payable to Buyer.

**4.4 Release by Joint Written Instructions.** The Escrow Agent shall disburse Escrow Funds at any time in accordance with Joint Written Instructions.

**4.5 Method of Disbursement.** All disbursements shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by the receiving Principal in writing. The Escrow Agent may confirm any wire instructions by telephone call-back to a person listed on Schedule 1, as described in Section 7.6.

## 5. CLAIMS PROCEDURE

**5.1 Claim Notice.** At any time before the Escrow Release Date, Buyer may deliver to Seller and the Escrow Agent a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**") of any claim for indemnification under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement. Each Claim Notice shall state:

(a) the amount claimed, or a good-faith estimate of that amount if it cannot then be determined; and

(b) a description in reasonable detail of the facts giving rise to the claim.

**5.2 Objection.** Seller may object to all or any part of a claim by delivering a written notice of objection (an "**Objection Notice**") to Buyer and the Escrow Agent. The Objection Notice must be delivered within 30 days after Seller receives the Claim Notice and shall state the basis of the objection in reasonable detail.

**5.3 Uncontested Claims.** If the Escrow Agent does not receive an Objection Notice within the 30-day period in Section 5.2, Seller is deemed to have accepted the claim. On the fifth Business Day after that period expires, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Buyer the amount claimed in the Claim Notice, to the extent of the Escrow Funds then available. If Seller objects to only part of a claim, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the uncontested part to Buyer in the same manner.

**5.4 Contested Claims.** If Seller timely delivers an Objection Notice, the Escrow Agent shall retain the contested amount. It shall disburse that amount only upon receipt of:

(a) Joint Written Instructions; or

(b) a Final Order, accompanied by a certificate of the presenting Principal's counsel that the order is final and non-appealable.

**5.5 Estimated Claims.** If a Claim Notice states an estimated amount, Buyer may deliver an updated Claim Notice as the amount becomes determined. Any updated Claim Notice relating to a claim first noticed before the Escrow Release Date is treated as delivered before the Escrow Release Date.

## 6. DISBURSEMENTS UNDER FINAL ORDERS

**6.1 Final Orders.** The Escrow Agent shall disburse Escrow Funds in accordance with any Final Order delivered to it under Section 5.4(b). It may rely conclusively on that Final Order and the accompanying certificate of counsel without further inquiry.

**6.2 Conflicting Demands.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting instructions or demands concerning the Escrow Funds, it may refrain from acting until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. Alternatively, it may deposit the disputed Escrow Funds with the court specified in Section 12.2 and file an action in interpleader. Upon that deposit, the Escrow Agent is released from all further obligations with respect to the deposited funds.

## 7. DUTIES OF THE ESCROW AGENT

**7.1 Limited Duties.** The Escrow Agent shall perform only the duties expressly set forth in this Agreement, which are purely ministerial in nature. No implied duties or obligations shall be read into this Agreement against the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent is not a party to the Purchase Agreement and has no duty to review, interpret or enforce it.

**7.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any written notice, instruction, certificate or other document it believes in good faith to be genuine and signed by the proper party. It has no duty to verify the accuracy of any statement in any such document.

**7.3 Standard of Care.** The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence or willful misconduct as determined by a Final Order. In no event shall the Escrow Agent be liable for special, indirect, consequential or punitive damages.

**7.4 Counsel.** The Escrow Agent may consult with counsel of its choice. It shall not be liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith in accordance with the advice of that counsel.

**7.5 Statements.** The Escrow Agent shall deliver to each Principal a monthly statement of the Escrow Account, showing the balance, investments, income and disbursements for the period.

**7.6 Security Procedures.** Each Principal shall deliver to the Escrow Agent a list of its authorized representatives and call-back contacts in the form of Schedule 1. Before executing any funds transfer instruction, other than an instruction delivered under Section 4.2, the Escrow Agent shall confirm it by telephone with an authorized representative of the delivering Principal who did not sign the instruction.

**7.7 Force Majeure.** The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any failure or delay in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, war, terrorism, pandemic, labor disputes, or failure of communications or computer systems.

## 8. FEES AND EXPENSES

**8.1 Fees.** The Escrow Agent shall be entitled to the fees set forth on Schedule 2, together with reimbursement of its reasonable out-of-pocket expenses, including reasonable fees of counsel.

**8.2 Allocation.** As between the Principals, all fees and expenses of the Escrow Agent shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller. Each Principal is responsible to the Escrow Agent for its share. If a Principal fails to pay its share within 30 days after invoice, the other Principal may pay it and recover the amount from the non-paying Principal.

**8.3 No Deduction from Escrow Funds.** The Escrow Agent shall not deduct its fees or expenses from the Escrow Funds without Joint Written Instructions.

## 9. INDEMNIFICATION OF THE ESCROW AGENT

**9.1 Indemnity.** The Principals, jointly and severally, shall indemnify and hold harmless the Escrow Agent and its directors, officers, employees and agents from and against all losses, claims, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or in connection with this Agreement. This indemnity does not apply to any such amounts caused by the gross negligence or willful misconduct of the Escrow Agent, as determined by a Final Order.

**9.2 Allocation Between Principals.** As between Buyer and Seller, any amount paid under Section 9.1 shall be borne equally. However, a Principal whose breach of this Agreement or the Purchase Agreement gave rise to the indemnified amount shall bear the entire amount.

**9.3 Survival.** This Section 9 survives the termination of this Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

## 10. RESIGNATION AND REMOVAL OF THE ESCROW AGENT

**10.1 Resignation.** The Escrow Agent may resign at any time by giving at least 30 days' written notice to the Principals.

**10.2 Removal.** The Principals may remove the Escrow Agent at any time by Joint Written Instructions delivered at least 30 days before the effective date of removal.

**10.3 Successor.** The Principals shall jointly appoint a successor escrow agent, which must be a bank or trust company with capital and surplus of at least $500,000,000. If no successor has been appointed within 30 days after notice of resignation or removal, the Escrow Agent may petition the court specified in Section 12.2 to appoint one. The resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent takes effect only when the successor accepts its appointment in writing and the Escrow Agent delivers the Escrow Funds to that successor.

## 11. TAX MATTERS

**11.1 Tax Owner.** For U.S. federal and applicable state income tax purposes, Buyer is treated as the owner of the Escrow Funds until they are disbursed. All income earned on the Escrow Funds shall be reported as income of Buyer.

**11.2 Tax Reporting.** The Escrow Agent shall prepare and file all required IRS Forms 1099 with respect to income earned on the Escrow Funds. Each Principal shall deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9 at the Closing.

**11.3 Tax Distributions.** Within 10 Business Days after Buyer's written request, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Buyer, from the income earned on the Escrow Funds, an amount equal to 25% of that income for the preceding calendar quarter, to cover Buyer's tax liability on it.

## 12. GOVERNING LAW; JURISDICTION

**12.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement, and all claims and causes of action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, the Escrow Account or the Escrow Funds, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware. This applies without regard to any conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of the laws of another jurisdiction.

**12.2 Jurisdiction.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware for any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement, including any dispute concerning a Claim Notice, an Objection Notice or the release of any Escrow Funds. If that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware. Each party waives any objection to venue in those courts and any claim of inconvenient forum.

**12.3 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION OR PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**13.3 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the party against whom it is asserted.

**13.4 Assignment.** No party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties. However, Buyer may collaterally assign its rights under this Agreement to its senior lenders. Any entity into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or to which it transfers substantially all of its corporate trust business, becomes the successor Escrow Agent without further act.
