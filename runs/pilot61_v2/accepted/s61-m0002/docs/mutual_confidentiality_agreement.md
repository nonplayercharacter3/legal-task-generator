# MUTUAL CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

This MUTUAL CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 12, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Vogert Live Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Vogert**"), and **Dretusk Presents, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Dretusk**"). Vogert and Dretusk are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to evaluate and negotiate a possible transaction in which Vogert would acquire all or part of the regional concert promotion business conducted by Dretusk (the "**Proposed Transaction**"). In doing so, each Party expects to disclose Confidential Information to the other. This Agreement sets out the terms on which each Party will receive, use and protect the other Party's Confidential Information. The Party disclosing information is the "**Disclosing Party**," and the Party receiving it is the "**Receiving Party**."

## 2. Definitions

2.1 "**Affiliate**" means, with respect to a Party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Party.

2.2 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information, in any form or medium, disclosed by or on behalf of the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party or its Representatives on or after the Effective Date in connection with the Proposed Transaction. It includes:

(a) financial statements, budgets, forecasts, tax information and accounting records;

(b) artist promoter agreements, venue booking agreements, sponsorship agreements and ticketing services agreements, and their terms, including artist guarantees, deal splits, settlement figures and radius clauses;

(c) ticket sales data, advance sales reports, refund histories, pricing strategies and customer, purchaser and subscriber lists and data;

(d) unannounced event dates, talent routing, festival line-ups and plans for the "Ironfern Fest" music festival;

(e) employee and contractor information; and

(f) all notes, analyses, compilations, forecasts and other materials prepared by the Receiving Party or its Representatives to the extent they contain or reflect any of the foregoing (the "**Derivative Materials**").

The fact that the Parties are discussing the Proposed Transaction, and the status and terms of those discussions, are also Confidential Information of each Party (the "**Transaction Information**").

2.3 "**Representatives**" means a Party's Affiliates and its and their respective managers, directors, officers, employees, members, prospective financing sources, attorneys, accountants and financial advisors.

## 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party or its Representatives;

(b) was lawfully in the possession of the Receiving Party or its Representatives, without an obligation of confidentiality, before its disclosure by the Disclosing Party;

(c) is or becomes available to the Receiving Party or its Representatives on a non-confidential basis from a third party that, to the Receiving Party's knowledge, is not bound by an obligation of confidentiality to the Disclosing Party; or

(d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party or its Representatives without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

## 4. Obligations of the Receiving Party

4.1 **Use.** The Receiving Party shall use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely to evaluate, negotiate and, if applicable, complete the Proposed Transaction (the "**Permitted Purpose**"), and for no other purpose.

4.2 **Non-Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall keep the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information strictly confidential and shall not disclose it to any person other than its Representatives who need to know it for the Permitted Purpose. Before disclosure, the Receiving Party shall inform each such Representative of the confidential nature of the information and direct the Representative to treat it in accordance with this Agreement. The Receiving Party is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives.

4.3 **Standard of Care.** The Receiving Party shall protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care.

4.4 **Personal Data.** To the extent Confidential Information includes personal information about ticket purchasers, subscribers, employees or other individuals, the Disclosing Party may provide it in aggregated, anonymized or sample form until the Parties agree otherwise in writing. The Receiving Party shall handle any personal information it receives in compliance with applicable privacy and data security laws, shall not use it to contact any individual, and shall notify the Disclosing Party promptly upon becoming aware of any unauthorized access to it.

4.5 **Notice of Unauthorized Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party promptly in writing upon becoming aware of any unauthorized use or disclosure of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information, and shall cooperate reasonably with the Disclosing Party to limit its effect.

## 5. Compelled Disclosure

If the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is requested or required by law, regulation, subpoena, court order or other legal process to disclose any Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall, to the extent legally permitted, give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice so that the Disclosing Party may seek a protective order or other remedy at its own expense. The Receiving Party shall cooperate reasonably with those efforts. If no protective order or other remedy is obtained, the Receiving Party or its Representative may disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information that its counsel advises is legally required to be disclosed, and shall use reasonable efforts to obtain assurance that the disclosed information will be treated confidentially.

## 6. Contact; Non-Solicitation

6.1 **Contact.** Without the prior written consent of Dretusk, Vogert and its Representatives shall not, in connection with the Proposed Transaction, initiate contact with any artist, artist manager, booking agent, venue, sponsor, ticketing provider or employee of Dretusk regarding the Proposed Transaction or Dretusk's Confidential Information. Contacts made in the ordinary course of Vogert's business that do not involve the Proposed Transaction or use Confidential Information are not restricted.

6.2 **Employees.** For twelve (12) months after the Effective Date, neither Party shall, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment any employee of the other Party with whom it first had contact, or of whom it first became aware, through the evaluation of the Proposed Transaction. This Section 6.2 does not restrict general solicitations not targeted at the other Party's employees, or the hiring of any person who responds to them or who approaches the hiring Party on his or her own initiative.

## 7. Return and Destruction

Upon the written request of the Disclosing Party, or upon either Party's notice that it has terminated discussions regarding the Proposed Transaction, the Receiving Party shall, within ten (10) business days, return or destroy all Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party in its or its Representatives' possession and destroy all Derivative Materials. Upon request, an officer of the Receiving Party shall certify in writing that it has done so. The Receiving Party and its Representatives may retain copies to the extent required by law, regulation or bona fide internal document retention policies, and copies stored in automatic electronic backup systems that are not readily accessible. Any retained information remains subject to this Agreement for so long as it is retained.

## 8. No Representations; No Obligation

8.1 **No Representations.** Each Disclosing Party provides its Confidential Information "as is." Neither Party makes any representation or warranty as to the accuracy or completeness of its Confidential Information, except as may be set forth in a definitive written agreement for the Proposed Transaction, if and when signed.

8.2 **No Obligation to Proceed.** This Agreement does not obligate either Party to enter into the Proposed Transaction or to continue discussions. No agreement regarding the Proposed Transaction will exist unless and until a definitive written agreement has been signed and delivered by both Parties. Either Party may end discussions at any time by written notice to the other.

8.3 **No License.** All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any license or other right in any trademark, copyright, trade secret or other intellectual property of the Disclosing Party, including the "Ironfern Fest" marks.

## 9. Term

This Agreement continues in effect for two (2) years after the Effective Date. The Receiving Party's obligations with respect to Confidential Information that constitutes a trade secret under applicable law continue for so long as that information remains a trade secret.

## 10. Remedies

Each Party acknowledges that unauthorized use or disclosure of the other Party's Confidential Information may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would not be an adequate remedy. Each Party is therefore entitled to seek specific performance and injunctive relief, without proof of actual damages and without posting a bond, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.

## 11. General

11.1 **Notices.** Notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set out on the signature page, or to any other address a Party designates by notice.

11.2 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that either Party may assign it to a successor to all or substantially all of its business. This Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

11.3 **Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of that right.

11.4 **Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware for any action arising out of this Agreement.

11.5 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

11.6 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature and PDF transmission. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
