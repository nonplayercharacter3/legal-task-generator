# SUBORDINATED PROMISSORY NOTE

THIS NOTE AND THE OBLIGATIONS EVIDENCED HEREBY ARE SUBORDINATED IN RIGHT OF PAYMENT TO SENIOR INDEBTEDNESS TO THE EXTENT AND IN THE MANNER SET FORTH IN SECTION 8. THIS NOTE HAS NOT BEEN REGISTERED UNDER THE SECURITIES ACT OF 1933, AS AMENDED, OR ANY STATE SECURITIES LAWS.

**$4,000,000** | **Issue Date: June 30, 2026**

FOR VALUE RECEIVED, **Vogert Live Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Maker**"), hereby promises to pay to the order of **Dretusk Presents, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (together with its successors and permitted assigns, "**Holder**"), the principal amount stated in Section 1, together with interest on the unpaid principal balance as provided in this Note.

This Note is the "Seller Note" issued under Section 2.5(b) of the Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of May 15, 2026, between Maker, as Buyer, and Dretusk Presents, Inc., as Seller (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Note have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

## 1. Principal Amount

The original principal amount of this Note is $4,000,000 (the "**Principal Amount**"). The Principal Amount represents $4,000,000 of the Purchase Price payable to Holder under the Purchase Agreement. The outstanding principal balance of this Note at any time equals the Principal Amount, reduced by all payments of principal made by Maker and all amounts set off under Section 4.

## 2. Maturity; Payments

(a) *Maturity Date.* The entire unpaid principal balance of this Note, together with all accrued and unpaid interest, shall be due and payable in full on June 30, 2028 (the "**Maturity Date**"), unless accelerated earlier under Section 7.

(b) *Manner of Payment.* All payments under this Note shall be made in lawful money of the United States by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated by Holder in writing. If any payment falls due on a day that is not a Business Day, it shall be due on the next Business Day, and interest shall continue to accrue in the meantime.

(c) *Application.* Each payment shall be applied first to accrued and unpaid interest and then to principal.

## 3. Interest

(a) *Rate.* The unpaid principal balance of this Note shall bear simple interest at a fixed rate of six percent (6.0%) per annum from the Issue Date until paid in full. Interest shall be computed on the basis of a 365-day year and the actual number of days elapsed.

(b) *Payment of Interest.* Accrued interest shall be payable quarterly in arrears on the last Business Day of each March, June, September and December, beginning September 30, 2026, and on the Maturity Date.

(c) *Default Interest.* While an Event of Default continues, the unpaid principal balance shall bear interest at the rate in Section 3(a) plus two percent (2.0%) per annum.

(d) *Usury.* Interest under this Note shall not exceed the maximum rate permitted by applicable Law. Any amount paid in excess of that rate shall be applied to principal or refunded to Maker.

## 4. Set-off

(a) *Right of Set-off.* Maker may set off against any principal or interest payable under this Note the amount of any Indemnifiable Losses. "**Indemnifiable Losses**" means Losses to which any Buyer Indemnitee is entitled under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, together with any amount payable by Seller to Maker under Section 2.7(e) of the Purchase Agreement that remains unpaid when due.

(b) *Procedure.* To exercise its right of set-off, Maker shall deliver to Holder a written notice stating the amount to be set off and describing in reasonable detail the claim on which the set-off is based. Amounts subject to a claim that has been finally determined, or agreed in writing by Holder, shall be permanently deducted from the outstanding principal balance of this Note. Maker may withhold payment of amounts subject to a pending claim until the claim is resolved. If a pending claim is resolved in Holder's favor, Maker shall pay the withheld amount, with interest at the rate in Section 3(a), within ten (10) Business Days after resolution.

(c) *Set-off Expiration Date.* Maker's right of set-off under this Section 4 applies only to claims for which notice is delivered to Holder on or before the 180th day after the Issue Date (the "**Set-off Expiration Date**"). Any claim noticed on or before the Set-off Expiration Date may continue to be set off until it is finally resolved.

(d) *No Event of Default.* Any set-off or withholding made in good faith under this Section 4 shall not constitute a failure to pay or an Event of Default.

## 5. Limited Recourse

Holder's aggregate liability for all Indemnifiable Losses, whether recovered by set-off against this Note or otherwise, shall in no event exceed the Indemnity Cap. Recovery by set-off under Section 4 shall be credited against the Indemnity Cap dollar for dollar.

## 6. Information Rights

Until this Note is paid in full, Maker shall deliver to Holder:

(a) within twenty (20) days after the end of each calendar month, monthly ticket sales and artist settlement reports for the Business. These reports shall show, for each event promoted by Maker, gross ticket receipts, tickets sold and refunded, advance ticket sales for future events, artist guarantees paid and owed, and final settlement amounts with artists and venues;

(b) within forty-five (45) days after the end of each fiscal quarter, the unaudited balance sheet and statement of income of Maker for that quarter;

(c) within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each fiscal year, the annual financial statements of Maker, reviewed or audited by an independent accounting firm;

(d) promptly, and in any event within five (5) Business Days after an executive officer of Maker becomes aware of it, written notice of any Event of Default or of any event that, with notice or lapse of time, would become an Event of Default; and

(e) such other information about the financial condition and operations of Maker as Holder may reasonably request.

## 7. Events of Default; Remedies

(a) *Events of Default.* Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(i) Maker fails to pay any principal of this Note when due, or fails to pay any interest within ten (10) Business Days after it is due, in each case other than as permitted under Section 4;

(ii) Maker fails to perform any other covenant in this Note and does not cure the failure within thirty (30) days after written notice from Holder;

(iii) Maker becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or commences any voluntary case or proceeding under any bankruptcy, insolvency or similar Law, or any involuntary case is commenced against Maker and is not dismissed within sixty (60) days;

(iv) the maturity of any Senior Indebtedness in excess of $1,000,000 is accelerated; or

(v) a Change of Control occurs without the prior written consent of Holder.

(b) *Change of Control.* "**Change of Control**" means (i) any Person or group of Persons (within the meaning of Section 13(d) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934) acquiring, directly or indirectly, by merger, consolidation, purchase of equity or otherwise, beneficial ownership of more than fifty percent of the voting equity of Maker or of any Person that directly or indirectly controls Maker; or (ii) the sale, lease or other transfer of all or substantially all of the assets of Maker in one transaction or a series of related transactions.

(c) *Remedies.* Upon an Event of Default described in Section 7(a)(iii), the entire unpaid principal balance of this Note and all accrued interest shall become immediately due and payable without any action by Holder. Upon any other Event of Default, Holder may, by written notice to Maker, declare the entire unpaid principal balance of this Note and all accrued interest immediately due and payable. Subject to Section 8, Holder may then exercise all rights and remedies available under this Note and applicable Law.

(d) *Costs of Collection.* Maker shall pay all reasonable costs of collection incurred by Holder after an Event of Default, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

## 8. Subordination

(a) *Subordination.* The payment of principal and interest on this Note is subordinate in right of payment to the prior payment in full of all Senior Indebtedness. "**Senior Indebtedness**" means all obligations of Maker for borrowed money owed to banks or other institutional lenders under any credit facility, together with interest, fees and other amounts owed on those obligations.

(b) *Payment Blockage.* While any payment default or acceleration of Senior Indebtedness continues, Maker shall not make, and Holder shall not accept, any payment on this Note, other than set-off under Section 4. Regularly scheduled payments of interest may otherwise be made and received.

(c) *Turnover.* Any payment received by Holder in violation of this Section 8 shall be held in trust for the holders of Senior Indebtedness and promptly paid over to them.

(d) *Subordination Agreement.* Holder shall execute any subordination agreement reasonably requested by the holders of Senior Indebtedness that is consistent with this Section 8.

## 9. Assignment

Holder may assign, transfer or pledge this Note or any interest in it to any Person without the consent of Maker. Any assignee shall succeed to all of Holder's rights under this Note. Holder shall give Maker written notice of any assignment, stating the name and address of the assignee, within ten (10) Business Days after it occurs. Maker may not assign or delegate any of its obligations under this Note without the prior written consent of Holder. Any purported assignment by Maker in violation of this Section 9 is void.

## 10. Prepayment

Maker may prepay this Note at any time, in whole or in part, without premium or penalty. Each prepayment shall be accompanied by accrued and unpaid interest on the principal amount prepaid.

## 11. Miscellaneous

(a) *Notices.* All notices under this Note shall be given in writing in the manner set forth in Section 12.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

(b) *Amendment; Waiver.* This Note may be amended, and any provision waived, only by a written instrument signed by Maker and Holder. No delay or failure by Holder in exercising any right shall operate as a waiver of that right.

(c) *Waiver of Presentment.* Maker waives presentment, demand, protest and notice of dishonor.

(d) *Lost Note.* If this Note is lost, stolen, destroyed or mutilated, Maker shall issue a replacement note on the same terms upon receipt of an affidavit of loss and customary indemnity from Holder.

(e) *Governing Law; Jurisdiction.* This Note is governed by the Laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Maker and Holder submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware for any action arising out of this Note. EACH OF MAKER AND HOLDER WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

(f) *Severability.* If any provision of this Note is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

(g) *Successors.* This Note binds Maker and its successors and benefits Holder and its successors and assigns.
