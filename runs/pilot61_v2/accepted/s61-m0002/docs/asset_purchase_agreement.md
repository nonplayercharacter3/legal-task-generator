# ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This ASSET PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of May 15, 2026, by and between **Dretusk Presents, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), and **Vogert Live Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller operates a regional concert promotion business. The business promotes live music events under artist promoter agreements and venue booking agreements, owns and operates the "Ironfern Fest" music festival, and maintains related ticketing operations and customer data (as conducted by Seller as of the date hereof, the "**Business**").

B. Seller wishes to sell, and Buyer wishes to purchase, substantially all of the assets of Seller used in the Business, and Buyer is willing to assume certain liabilities of Seller, in each case on the terms and subject to the conditions of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with that Person.

"**Ancillary Agreements**" means the Bill of Sale, the Assignment and Assumption Agreement, the Intellectual Property Assignment, the Seller Note and the Transition Services Agreement, together with each other agreement, certificate or instrument delivered under this Agreement.

"**Assigned Contracts**" means the Contracts listed on Schedule 2.1(a). These include all artist promoter agreements, venue booking agreements, sponsorship agreements and ticketing services agreements of the Business.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Closing Deferred Revenue**" means the aggregate amount of cash received by Seller before the Effective Time from advance ticket sales for events scheduled to occur after the Effective Time, net of refunds paid before the Effective Time, determined in accordance with the Accounting Principles.

"**Accounting Principles**" means GAAP, applied on a basis consistent with the preparation of the Financial Statements, as modified by the principles set forth on Schedule 1.1.

"**Contract**" means any contract, agreement, lease, license, commitment, purchase order or other legally binding arrangement, whether written or oral.

"**Effective Time**" means 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on the Closing Date.

"**Fraud**" means actual and intentional fraud by a party with respect to the making of the representations and warranties in Article IV or Article V, as applicable.

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties of Seller in Section 4.1 (Organization), Section 4.2 (Authority), Section 4.5 (Title to Purchased Assets) and Section 4.14 (Brokers).

"**GAAP**" means United States generally accepted accounting principles, consistently applied.

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local or foreign government or political subdivision, or any agency, court or tribunal thereof.

"**Intellectual Property**" means all trademarks, service marks, trade names, domain names, social media accounts, copyrights, trade secrets, software, databases and other intellectual property rights.

"**Law**" means any statute, law, ordinance, regulation, rule, code, order or judgment of any Governmental Authority.

"**Liabilities**" means any liabilities, obligations or commitments of any nature, whether asserted or unasserted, known or unknown, absolute or contingent, accrued or unaccrued, matured or unmatured.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, Liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, interest, awards, penalties, fines, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

"**Person**" means an individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Seller Note**" means the subordinated promissory note in the original principal amount of $4,000,000, issued by Buyer to Seller at the Closing substantially in the form of Exhibit A.

"**Ticketing Data**" means all customer lists, purchaser records, email and SMS subscriber lists, ticket sales histories and related data of the Business, in whatever form maintained.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." Headings are for convenience only. References to Articles, Sections, Schedules and Exhibits are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchased Assets.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer, convey and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than Permitted Encumbrances, all of Seller's right, title and interest in, to and under all assets, properties and rights used or held for use in the Business (the "**Purchased Assets**"), including:

(a) the Assigned Contracts;

(b) the "Ironfern Fest" name, marks and logos and all related goodwill, together with all other Intellectual Property used in the Business, as listed on Schedule 2.1(b);

(c) the Ticketing Data;

(d) all deposits and prepaid amounts paid by Seller to venues, artists and vendors for events scheduled after the Closing;

(e) all staging, production, office and other tangible personal property listed on Schedule 2.1(e);

(f) all permits and licenses held for the Business, to the extent transferable; and

(g) all books, records, files and goodwill of the Business.

**2.2 Excluded Assets.** The Purchased Assets exclude: (a) cash and cash equivalents of Seller, other than deposits described in Section 2.1(d); (b) Seller's corporate records, minute books and tax returns; (c) Contracts that are not Assigned Contracts; (d) Seller's employee benefit plans; (e) Seller's insurance policies; and (f) the rights of Seller under this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements (collectively, the "**Excluded Assets**").

**2.3 Assumed Liabilities.** At the Closing, Buyer shall assume and agree to pay, perform and discharge when due only the following Liabilities of Seller (the "**Assumed Liabilities**"):

(a) all Liabilities arising under the Assigned Contracts, whether arising before, on or after the Closing Date;

(b) all obligations to ticket holders for events scheduled to occur after the Closing Date, to the extent of the Closing Deferred Revenue included in the calculation of the Purchase Price; and

(c) the Liabilities specifically listed on Schedule 2.3(c).

**2.4 Excluded Liabilities.** Buyer shall not assume, and Seller shall retain and be responsible for, all Liabilities of Seller other than the Assumed Liabilities (the "**Excluded Liabilities**"). The Excluded Liabilities include: (a) Liabilities for Taxes of Seller or relating to the Business for any pre-Closing tax period; (b) Liabilities relating to the Excluded Assets; (c) Liabilities to Seller's employees arising before the Closing, including accrued wages, bonuses and benefits; (d) indebtedness for borrowed money of Seller; and (e) Liabilities arising from any action pending against Seller as of the Closing.

**2.5 Purchase Price.** The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets is $24,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment under Sections 2.6 and 2.7, plus the assumption of the Assumed Liabilities. Buyer shall pay the Purchase Price at the Closing as follows:

(a) Buyer shall pay to Seller, by wire transfer of immediately available funds, $20,000,000, *minus* the Estimated Closing Deferred Revenue, *plus* the Estimated Closing Deposits (the "**Closing Cash Payment**"); and

(b) Buyer shall issue and deliver the Seller Note to Seller, representing $4,000,000 of the Purchase Price.

**2.6 Estimated Closing Statement.** At least three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a written statement setting forth Seller's good-faith estimate of (a) the Closing Deferred Revenue (the "**Estimated Closing Deferred Revenue**") and (b) the aggregate deposits described in Section 2.1(d) (the "**Estimated Closing Deposits**"), together with reasonable supporting detail.

**2.7 Post-Closing Adjustment.**

(a) *Closing Statement.* Within forty-five (45) days after the Closing Date, Seller shall prepare and deliver to Buyer a statement (the "**Closing Statement**") setting forth Seller's calculation of the final Closing Deferred Revenue and the final amount of the deposits described in Section 2.1(d), each prepared in accordance with the Accounting Principles, together with reasonable supporting documentation.

(b) *Objection.* If Buyer disagrees with any item in the Closing Statement, Buyer may, within thirty (30) days after delivery of the Closing Statement (the "**Review Period**"), deliver to Seller a written notice of objection (an "**Objection Notice**") specifying in reasonable detail each disputed item, the amount in dispute and the basis therefor. During the Review Period, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access to the books, records and working papers used in preparing the Closing Statement.

(c) *Effect of Failure to Object.* If Buyer does not deliver an Objection Notice before the expiration of the Review Period, the Closing Statement shall be final and binding on the parties. If Buyer timely delivers an Objection Notice, any items not disputed in it shall be final and binding on the parties.

(d) *Resolution of Disputes.* If Buyer timely delivers an Objection Notice, Buyer and Seller shall negotiate in good faith to resolve the disputed items for thirty (30) days. Any items still unresolved at the end of that period shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm of national standing mutually agreed by the parties (the "**Independent Accountant**"). The Independent Accountant shall act as an expert and not as an arbitrator. It shall resolve only the disputed items, within the range of values asserted by the parties, and its determination shall be final and binding on the parties absent manifest error. The fees of the Independent Accountant shall be allocated between Buyer and Seller in inverse proportion to the degree to which each prevails.

(e) *Payment.* Within five (5) Business Days after the Closing Statement becomes final, (i) if the final Closing Deferred Revenue exceeds the Estimated Closing Deferred Revenue, or the final deposits are less than the Estimated Closing Deposits, Seller shall pay the net shortfall to Buyer, and (ii) if the net adjustment is in Seller's favor, Buyer shall pay the net amount to Seller, in each case by wire transfer of immediately available funds.

**2.8 Allocation.** The Purchase Price and the Assumed Liabilities shall be allocated among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code and the methodology set forth on Schedule 2.8. The parties shall file all tax returns consistently with that allocation.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on June 30, 2026, or on such other date as the parties may agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**"). The Closing shall be deemed effective as of the Effective Time.

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer: (a) a bill of sale for the Purchased Assets (the "**Bill of Sale**"); (b) an assignment and assumption agreement for the Assigned Contracts and Assumed Liabilities (the "**Assignment and Assumption Agreement**"); (c) an assignment of the Intellectual Property included in the Purchased Assets, including the "Ironfern Fest" marks (the "**Intellectual Property Assignment**"); (d) a transition services agreement (the "**Transition Services Agreement**"); (e) the third-party consents listed on Schedule 4.4; (f) releases of all Encumbrances on the Purchased Assets other than Permitted Encumbrances; and (g) a certificate of Seller's secretary certifying board and stockholder approval of this Agreement.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver to Seller: (a) the Closing Cash Payment; (b) the Seller Note, duly executed by Buyer; (c) the Assignment and Assumption Agreement and the Transition Services Agreement, each duly executed by Buyer; and (d) a certificate of an officer of Buyer certifying the resolutions of Buyer's Board of Managers approving this Agreement.

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the disclosure schedules delivered by Seller (the "**Disclosure Schedule**"), Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date hereof and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization.** Seller is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware and has full corporate power to own the Purchased Assets and to operate the Business.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full corporate power and authority to enter into this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party and to perform its obligations under them. This Agreement constitutes Seller's legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable against Seller in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not (a) violate Seller's organizational documents, (b) violate any Law applicable to Seller, or (c) except as set forth on Schedule 4.4, require consent under, or result in a breach of, any Assigned Contract.

**4.4 Consents.** Schedule 4.4 lists each Assigned Contract that requires the consent of a counterparty to its assignment to Buyer.

**4.5 Title to Purchased Assets.** Seller has good and valid title to all Purchased Assets, free and clear of all Encumbrances other than Permitted Encumbrances.

**4.6 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the audited balance sheet and statements of income and cash flows of the Business for the fiscal years ended December 31, 2024 and 2025 and the unaudited balance sheet and statement of income as of March 31, 2026 (the "**Financial Statements**"). The Financial Statements have been prepared in accordance with GAAP and fairly present in all material respects the financial position and results of operations of the Business as of their respective dates.

**4.7 Material Contracts.** Schedule 2.1(a) lists every artist promoter agreement, venue booking agreement, sponsorship agreement and ticketing services agreement of the Business. Each Assigned Contract is valid and binding on Seller and in full force and effect. Neither Seller nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach of any Assigned Contract. Schedule 4.7 lists all artist guarantees, unpaid settlements and outstanding deposit obligations under the Assigned Contracts as of the date hereof.

**4.8 Ticketing and Deferred Revenue.** Schedule 4.8 sets forth, for each event scheduled after the date hereof, the number of tickets sold, gross receipts collected and refunds paid or owed as of the date hereof. All ticket sales of the Business have been recorded accurately in Seller's books and records.

**4.9 Intellectual Property; Data.** Seller owns or has the valid right to use all Intellectual Property used in the Business, including the "Ironfern Fest" marks. Seller has collected, stored and used the Ticketing Data in compliance in all material respects with applicable privacy Laws and Seller's published privacy policies. To Seller's knowledge, no data security breach has occurred involving the Ticketing Data.

**4.10 Permits.** Schedule 4.10 lists all permits held for the Business, including festival, assembly, alcohol and fire permits for Ironfern Fest. Each is in full force and effect.

**4.11 Compliance with Laws; Litigation.** Seller has operated the Business in compliance in all material respects with applicable Laws. There is no action pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against Seller relating to the Business or the Purchased Assets.

**4.12 Employees.** Schedule 4.12 lists each employee of the Business and his or her title, compensation and date of hire.

**4.13 Taxes.** Seller has timely filed all tax returns required to be filed relating to the Business and has paid all Taxes shown as due on those returns.

**4.14 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Seller.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as of the date hereof and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**5.1 Organization.** Buyer is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware.

**5.2 Authority.** Buyer has full limited liability company power and authority to enter into this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party and to perform its obligations under them. This Agreement constitutes Buyer's legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable against Buyer in accordance with its terms.

**5.3 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Buyer do not violate Buyer's organizational documents or any Law applicable to Buyer.

**5.4 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by Buyer.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date hereof until the Closing, Seller shall operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice. Seller shall not, without Buyer's prior written consent, enter into, amend or terminate any Assigned Contract, grant any artist guarantee in excess of $250,000, or sell or encumber any Purchased Asset.

**6.2 Access.** Before the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access to the books, records, Contracts and personnel of the Business.

**6.3 Consents.** Seller shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain before the Closing the consents listed on Schedule 4.4.

**6.4 Non-Competition; Non-Solicitation.** For three (3) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, directly or indirectly, (a) engage in the promotion of live music events or music festivals within the territory described on Schedule 6.4, or (b) solicit any artist, venue or sponsor that is a counterparty to an Assigned Contract to terminate or adversely modify its relationship with the Business.

**6.5 Confidentiality.** After the Closing, Seller shall keep confidential all information relating to the Business, the Purchased Assets and the Ticketing Data, except as required by Law.

**6.6 Name Change.** Within thirty (30) days after the Closing Date, Seller shall cease all use of the "Ironfern Fest" marks and any confusingly similar names.

**6.7 Post-Closing Receipts.** Any payment received by Seller after the Closing relating to a Purchased Asset shall be held in trust for Buyer and remitted to Buyer within five (5) Business Days.

**6.8 Further Assurances.** Each party shall execute and deliver such further instruments, and take such further actions, as may be reasonably required to carry out this Agreement.

## ARTICLE VII. EMPLOYEES

**7.1 Offers of Employment.** Buyer may offer employment, effective as of the Closing, to the employees of the Business that Buyer selects. Seller shall terminate the employment of each employee who accepts such an offer (each, a "**Transferred Employee**") effective as of the Closing.

**7.2 Seller Obligations.** Seller shall pay each Transferred Employee all wages, accrued vacation, bonuses and other compensation earned through the Closing Date.

## ARTICLE VIII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**8.1 Conditions to Buyer's Obligations.** Buyer's obligation to close is subject to the following: (a) Seller's representations and warranties are true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date; (b) Seller has performed its covenants in all material respects; (c) the consents marked with an asterisk on Schedule 4.4 have been obtained; and (d) Seller has delivered the items in Section 3.2.

**8.2 Conditions to Seller's Obligations.** Seller's obligation to close is subject to the following: (a) Buyer's representations and warranties are true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date; (b) Buyer has performed its covenants in all material respects; and (c) Buyer has delivered the items in Section 3.3.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties of Seller in this Agreement, other than the Fundamental Representations, shall survive the Closing until the Set-off Expiration Date (as defined in the Seller Note). The Fundamental Representations and the representations in Section 4.13 (Taxes) shall survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. The representations and warranties of Buyer shall survive the Closing for the same period as Seller's representations, other than the Fundamental Representations. The covenants and agreements of the parties shall survive until fully performed. No claim may be made for breach of a representation or warranty after its survival period ends. However, any claim asserted in writing with reasonable specificity before the end of the survival period shall survive until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer and its Affiliates and their respective managers, officers, employees and agents (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in this Agreement;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement; or

(c) any Excluded Liability.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Seller and its Affiliates and their respective directors, officers, employees and agents (the "**Seller Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any of them arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in this Agreement;

(b) any Assumed Liability; or

(c) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement.

**9.4 Deductible.** Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) until the aggregate Losses under that Section exceed $240,000 (the "**Deductible**"). Thereafter, Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of the Deductible.

**9.5 Cap; Exceptions.**

(a) The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed $2,400,000 (the "**Indemnity Cap**"). The aggregate liability of Buyer under Section 9.3(a) shall not exceed the Indemnity Cap.

(b) The Deductible and the Indemnity Cap shall not apply to Losses arising out of Fraud, any inaccuracy in or breach of a Fundamental Representation or the representations in Section 4.13 (Taxes), any Excluded Liability, or claims under Section 9.3(b).

**9.6 Indemnification Procedures.**

(a) *Direct Claims.* A party seeking indemnification (the "**Indemnified Party**") shall give the party from which indemnification is sought (the "**Indemnifying Party**") prompt written notice of the claim, describing it in reasonable detail. Failure to give prompt notice shall not relieve the Indemnifying Party of its obligations except to the extent it is actually prejudiced.

(b) *Third-Party Claims.* The Indemnifying Party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably satisfactory to the Indemnified Party by written notice within fifteen (15) days after receiving notice of the claim. The Indemnifying Party shall not settle any third-party claim without the Indemnified Party's consent unless the settlement involves only the payment of money and includes a full release of the Indemnified Party. Such consent shall not be unreasonably withheld.

**9.7 Set-off.** Buyer may set off any amount to which a Buyer Indemnitee is entitled under this Article IX against amounts otherwise payable under the Seller Note, in accordance with the terms of the Seller Note.

**9.8 Exclusive Remedy.** Except in the case of Fraud and claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, the indemnification provisions of this Article IX, together with Section 2.7, are the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties for any claim arising out of this Agreement.

**9.9 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments under this Article IX shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for tax purposes, unless otherwise required by Law.

## ARTICLE X. TERMINATION

**10.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing (a) by mutual written consent of the parties; (b) by either party if the Closing has not occurred by July 31, 2026, unless the terminating party's breach caused the delay; or (c) by either party upon a material breach by the other party that is not cured within fifteen (15) days after written notice.

**10.2 Effect of Termination.** If this Agreement is terminated, it shall become void and of no further effect, except for this Section 10.2 and Article XII. No party shall be relieved of liability for any willful breach before termination.

## ARTICLE XI. TAX MATTERS

**11.1 Transfer Taxes.** Buyer and Seller shall each bear fifty percent (50%) of all transfer, sales, use and similar Taxes arising from the transfer of the Purchased Assets.

**11.2 Straddle Periods.** Personal property and similar periodic Taxes on the Purchased Assets for any period that includes the Closing Date shall be prorated between Seller and Buyer on a per diem basis.

## ARTICLE XII. MISCELLANEOUS

**12.1 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided herein, each party shall bear its own costs and expenses in connection with this Agreement.

**12.2 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing. Notice is deemed given when delivered personally, one (1) Business Day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, or when sent by email with confirmation of transmission, in each case to the address of the party set forth on the signature page (or such other address as a party designates by notice).

**12.3 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing and signed by the party granting it.

**12.4 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party. However, Buyer may assign its rights hereunder to one or more of its Affiliates or, for collateral purposes, to its lenders. This Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

**12.5 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the Buyer Indemnitees and Seller Indemnitees under Article IX, this Agreement is for the sole benefit of the parties and their permitted successors and assigns.

**12.6 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the Laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**12.7 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware for any action arising out of this Agreement. EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**12.8 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**12.9 Ancillary Agreements.** In the event of any conflict between the terms of this Agreement and the terms of any Ancillary Agreement, the terms of this Agreement shall prevail; provided, however, that the Seller Note shall control as to payment, set-off and recourse.

**12.10 Specific Performance.** The parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if any provision of this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its terms. Each party is entitled to specific performance and injunctive relief, in addition to any other remedy.

**12.11 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
