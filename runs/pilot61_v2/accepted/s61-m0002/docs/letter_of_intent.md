# LETTER OF INTENT

**VOGERT LIVE HOLDINGS, LLC**

March 2, 2026

**PRIVATE AND CONFIDENTIAL**

Dretusk Presents, Inc.
Attention: Chief Executive Officer

**Re: Proposed Acquisition of the Concert Promotion Business of Dretusk Presents, Inc.**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter of intent (this "**Letter**") sets out the principal terms on which **Vogert Live Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), proposes to acquire substantially all of the assets of **Dretusk Presents, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Seller**"), used in Seller's regional concert promotion business (the "**Business**"). The proposed acquisition is referred to in this Letter as the "**Transaction**." Except as provided in Section 12, this Letter is a non-binding expression of the parties' present intentions.

## 1. Structure of the Transaction

Buyer, a wholly owned subsidiary of Vogert Entertainment Group, Inc., will purchase the Purchased Assets (as described in Section 2) and assume the Assumed Liabilities (as described in Section 3) under a definitive asset purchase agreement to be negotiated by the parties (the "**Definitive Agreement**"). The closing of the Transaction (the "**Closing**") is targeted to occur on or about June 30, 2026, subject to the conditions described in Section 8.

## 2. Purchased Assets

The assets to be acquired (the "**Purchased Assets**") will include all of Seller's right, title and interest in the assets used or held for use in the Business, free and clear of all liens other than customary permitted liens, including:

(a) the artist promoter agreements, venue booking agreements, sponsorship agreements and ticketing services agreements of the Business that Buyer elects to assume (the "**Assigned Contracts**");

(b) the "Ironfern Fest" name, marks, logos, domain names and social media accounts, and all related goodwill and other intellectual property used in the Business;

(c) all customer lists, purchaser records, subscriber lists, ticket sales histories and related ticketing data of the Business;

(d) all deposits and prepaid amounts paid by Seller to venues, artists and vendors for events scheduled after the Closing;

(e) the staging, production and office equipment of the Business;

(f) transferable permits and licenses held for the Business; and

(g) the books, records and goodwill of the Business.

Seller's cash (other than the deposits described in clause (d)), corporate records, tax returns, benefit plans, insurance policies and contracts that are not Assigned Contracts will be excluded.

## 3. Assumed Liabilities

At the Closing, Buyer will assume only obligations under Assigned Contracts arising after the Closing, together with obligations to ticket holders for events scheduled after the Closing to the extent of the deferred revenue credited to Buyer under Section 4(b) (together, the "**Assumed Liabilities**"). Seller will retain all other liabilities of Seller and the Business, including all liabilities under the Assigned Contracts arising before the Closing (such as artist guarantees, unpaid venue settlements and refunds for events cancelled or rescheduled before the Closing), pre-Closing taxes, employee liabilities arising before the Closing, indebtedness and pending litigation.

## 4. Purchase Price

(a) *Amount.* The aggregate purchase price for the Purchased Assets will be $24,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"). It will be payable at the Closing as follows:

(i) $20,000,000 in cash, by wire transfer of immediately available funds, subject to the adjustments described in Section 4(b); and

(ii) $4,000,000 by delivery of a subordinated promissory note issued by Buyer to Seller (the "**Seller Note**"), on the terms described in Section 5.

(b) *Adjustments.* The cash portion of the Purchase Price will be reduced by the amount of cash received by Seller before the Closing from advance ticket sales for events scheduled after the Closing, net of refunds paid before the Closing, and increased by the amount of the deposits described in Section 2(d). Seller will deliver an estimate of these amounts before the Closing. A final calculation, with a customary review and dispute resolution process involving an independent accounting firm, will be provided for in the Definitive Agreement.

## 5. Seller Note

The Seller Note will have a principal amount of $4,000,000 and a term of two years from the Closing, with a maturity date of June 30, 2028. It will bear interest at a fixed rate to be agreed and will be subordinated to Buyer's senior credit facilities on terms reasonably acceptable to Buyer's senior lenders. Buyer will have the right to set off against amounts payable under the Seller Note any amounts to which Buyer is entitled under the indemnification provisions of the Definitive Agreement. Buyer may prepay the Seller Note at any time without premium or penalty. The other terms of the Seller Note will be negotiated as part of the Definitive Agreement.

## 6. Representations, Survival and Indemnification

(a) *Representations.* The Definitive Agreement will contain representations and warranties of Seller customary for a transaction of this type. These will include representations as to organization, authority, title to the Purchased Assets, financial statements, material contracts (including artist guarantees and unpaid settlements), ticket sales and deferred revenue, intellectual property and data privacy, permits (including Ironfern Fest festival, assembly, alcohol and fire permits), compliance with laws, litigation, employees, taxes and brokers.

(b) *Survival.* Seller's general representations and warranties will survive for 18 months after Closing. Representations as to organization, authority, title to the Purchased Assets and brokers (the "**Fundamental Representations**") and the tax representations will survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. Covenants will survive until fully performed.

(c) *Indemnification.* Seller will indemnify Buyer for breaches of Seller's representations, warranties and covenants and for all liabilities retained by Seller. Buyer will indemnify Seller for breaches of Buyer's representations, warranties and covenants and for the Assumed Liabilities.

(d) *Limitations.* Seller's indemnity for breaches of general representations will be subject to a deductible equal to one percent (1%) of the Purchase Price and a cap equal to ten percent (10%) of the Purchase Price ($2,400,000). The deductible and cap will not apply to fraud, breaches of the Fundamental Representations or the tax representations, or liabilities retained by Seller. Buyer's indemnity for breaches of its representations will be subject to the same cap.

## 7. Due Diligence

From the date of this Letter until the Closing or the earlier termination of negotiations, Seller will give Buyer and its advisors reasonable access to the books, records, contracts, financial information, ticketing systems and personnel of the Business. Buyer's due diligence will include a review of the artist and venue contracts, advance ticket sales and refund history, the Ironfern Fest permits and site arrangements, and the Business's data privacy practices.

## 8. Definitive Agreement; Conditions

The parties will negotiate in good faith with the aim of signing the Definitive Agreement by May 15, 2026. The Closing will be subject to customary conditions, including:

(a) satisfactory completion of Buyer's due diligence;

(b) execution of the Definitive Agreement and related ancillary agreements, including a bill of sale, an assignment and assumption agreement, an intellectual property assignment, the Seller Note and a transition services agreement;

(c) receipt of required third-party consents to the assignment of material Assigned Contracts;

(d) approval of the Transaction by Buyer's Board of Managers and by Seller's board of directors and stockholders; and

(e) the absence of any material adverse change in the Business.

## 9. Restrictive Covenants

The Definitive Agreement will include a covenant by Seller not to engage in the promotion of live music events or music festivals in an agreed territory, and not to solicit artists, venues or sponsors of the Business, in each case for three (3) years after the Closing. Seller will cease use of the "Ironfern Fest" marks after a short transition period.

## 10. Exclusivity

For a period of sixty (60) days after the date of this Letter (the "**Exclusivity Period**"), Seller will not, and will cause its officers, directors, stockholders, employees and advisors not to, directly or indirectly solicit, encourage, negotiate or enter into any agreement with any person other than Buyer regarding the sale of the Business, any material portion of its assets or any equity interest in Seller. Seller will promptly notify Buyer of any unsolicited proposal of this kind. The Exclusivity Period will be extended automatically for an additional thirty (30) days if the parties are negotiating the Definitive Agreement in good faith at the end of the initial period.

## 11. Confidentiality; Announcements

The existence and terms of this Letter, and all information exchanged in connection with the Transaction, are subject to the Mutual Confidentiality Agreement previously entered into between Buyer and Seller. Neither party will make any public announcement regarding the Transaction without the prior written consent of the other party, except as required by law.

## 12. Binding and Non-Binding Provisions

Only Sections 10, 11, 12, 13 and 14 of this Letter (the "**Binding Provisions**") are legally binding on the parties. All other provisions of this Letter are non-binding statements of intent. No legally binding obligation to complete the Transaction will arise unless and until the Definitive Agreement is executed and delivered by both parties. Either party may terminate negotiations at any time by written notice to the other. Termination will not affect the Binding Provisions, which will survive for twelve (12) months after the date of this Letter.

## 13. Expenses

Each party will bear its own legal, accounting, financial advisory and other expenses incurred in connection with this Letter and the Transaction, whether or not the Transaction is completed.

## 14. Governing Law; Counterparts

This Letter is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. This Letter may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature and PDF transmission, each of which will be deemed an original.
