# WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF MANAGERS OF VOGERT LIVE HOLDINGS, LLC

**Dated as of September 8, 2026**

The undersigned, being all of the members of the Board of Managers (the "**Board**") of **Vogert Live Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (the "**Company**"), act under Section 18-404(d) of the Delaware Limited Liability Company Act and Section 6.7 of the Amended and Restated Limited Liability Company Agreement of the Company (the "**LLC Agreement**"). They hereby waive all requirements of notice and adopt the following recitals and resolutions by unanimous written consent without a meeting. These resolutions have the same force and effect as if adopted at a duly called meeting of the Board at which a quorum was present and acting throughout.

## RECITALS

A. The Company is a party to an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of May 15, 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**") with **Dretusk Presents, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Seller**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, the Company acquired the regional concert promotion business of the Seller, including the Assigned Contracts, the "Ironfern Fest" festival brand and the Ticketing Data. The Closing occurred on June 30, 2026. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Consent have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

B. Under Section 2.7(a) of the Purchase Agreement, the Seller is required to prepare and deliver to the Company the Closing Statement, setting out the Seller's calculation of the final Closing Deferred Revenue and the final amount of the deposits described in Section 2.1(d) of the Purchase Agreement.

C. The Seller delivered the Closing Statement to the Company by email to the Company's Chief Financial Officer on August 3, 2026.

D. The Company's finance team has reviewed the Closing Statement against the Company's ticketing platform records, the box office reconciliations transferred at the Closing and Schedule 4.8 to the Purchase Agreement. Management has reported to the Board that the Closing Statement understates the Closing Deferred Revenue attributable to advance ticket sales for events scheduled after the Effective Time, including the 2027 Ironfern Fest early-bird and VIP tiers. Management currently estimates the understatement at approximately $1,150,000. Based on its review, the finance team believes that (i) refunds that were issued after the Effective Time for shows rescheduled by the Seller before the Closing were netted against receipts, and (ii) receipts collected through the Company's third-party ticketing agent in the final week of June 2026 were omitted. The Company disputes the Closing Deferred Revenue figure set out in the Closing Statement.

E. The Board has reviewed the Company's operating plan for the 2027 festival season, including the proposed production, talent and marketing budget for the 2027 Ironfern Fest presented by management (the "**2027 Festival Budget**").

F. **Vogert Entertainment Group, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation and the sole member of the Company (the "**Parent**"), entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated as of August 28, 2026 (the "**Merger Agreement**") with Sablecrest Capital Partners and certain of its affiliates. Under the Merger Agreement, a subsidiary formed by Sablecrest Capital Partners will merge with and into the Parent, and the Parent will survive as a wholly owned subsidiary of an entity controlled by funds managed by Sablecrest Capital Partners (the "**Merger**"). The Merger is expected to close in the first quarter of 2027, subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions in the Merger Agreement. The Parent has asked the Company, as a wholly owned subsidiary, to take certain actions in support of the Merger. These include providing financial and operating information, cooperating in the preparation of disclosure schedules and regulatory filings, and delivering customary closing certificates.

G. The Board has determined that the actions described in the resolutions below are advisable and in the best interests of the Company.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT:

## 1. CLOSING STATEMENT; DEFERRED REVENUE DISPUTE

**RESOLVED,** that the Board hereby determines that the Company does not accept the Closing Deferred Revenue figure set out in the Closing Statement delivered by the Seller on August 3, 2026, insofar as that figure relates to advance ticket sales for events scheduled after the Effective Time;

**RESOLVED FURTHER,** that the President and the Chief Financial Officer of the Company (the "**Authorized Officers**") are each hereby authorized and directed, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to prepare, execute and deliver to the Seller an Objection Notice under Section 2.7(b) of the Purchase Agreement. The Objection Notice shall specify in reasonable detail each disputed item, the amount in dispute and the basis for it, including the matters described in Recital D;

**RESOLVED FURTHER,** that the Authorized Officers are each hereby authorized to request from the Seller access to the books, records and working papers used in preparing the Closing Statement, and to engage outside accountants to assist in the review; and

**RESOLVED FURTHER,** that the Authorized Officers are each hereby authorized to negotiate with the Seller to resolve the disputed items. If those items are not resolved, the Authorized Officers are authorized to submit them to an Independent Accountant under Section 2.7(d) of the Purchase Agreement, and to execute any engagement letter with that Independent Accountant on terms they deem appropriate. Any settlement of the disputed items that would reduce the Company's claim by more than $250,000 shall require further approval of the Board.

## 2. 2027 FESTIVAL BUDGET

**RESOLVED,** that the 2027 Festival Budget, in the form presented to the Board, is hereby approved;

**RESOLVED FURTHER,** that the Authorized Officers are each hereby authorized to enter into artist promoter agreements, venue booking agreements, production and vendor agreements and sponsorship agreements for the 2027 Ironfern Fest consistent with the 2027 Festival Budget. Any single artist guarantee in excess of $500,000, and any single agreement providing for aggregate payments by the Company in excess of $1,000,000, shall require prior approval of the Board.

## 3. PARTICIPATION IN THE PARENT MERGER

**RESOLVED,** that the Board acknowledges the Parent's entry into the Merger Agreement with Sablecrest Capital Partners. The Company's participation in the transactions contemplated by the Agreement and Plan of Merger, to the extent required of the Company as a subsidiary of the Parent, is hereby authorized and approved;

**RESOLVED FURTHER,** that the Authorized Officers are each hereby authorized, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to take the following actions in connection with the Merger as the Parent may reasonably request:

(a) furnish financial statements, operating data, contract lists and other information concerning the Company and its business;

(b) cooperate in the preparation of disclosure schedules, regulatory filings and other submissions;

(c) operate the business of the Company in compliance with the interim operating covenants of the Merger Agreement applicable to subsidiaries of the Parent; and

(d) execute and deliver officer's certificates, joinders and other customary closing documents; and

**RESOLVED FURTHER,** that the Authorized Officers are each hereby authorized to coordinate with the officers of the Parent and with counsel to the Parent regarding the timing of the closing of the Merger and any related integration planning.

## 4. GENERAL AUTHORITY

**RESOLVED,** that the Authorized Officers are each hereby authorized and empowered, in the name and on behalf of the Company, to take all further actions that they deem necessary or appropriate to carry out the purposes of the foregoing resolutions. This authority includes executing and delivering documents, instruments and certificates and paying fees and expenses. The taking of any such action or the execution of any such document shall be conclusive evidence of that authority; and

**RESOLVED FURTHER,** that all actions previously taken by any manager or officer of the Company in connection with the matters contemplated by the foregoing resolutions, to the extent consistent with them, are hereby ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects.

## 5. EFFECTIVENESS; COUNTERPARTS

This Consent shall be effective as of the date first written above and shall be filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Board. It may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature and PDF transmission. Each counterpart shall be deemed an original, and all of them together shall constitute one instrument.
