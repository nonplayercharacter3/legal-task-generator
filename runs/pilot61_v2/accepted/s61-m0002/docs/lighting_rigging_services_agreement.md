# LIGHTING AND RIGGING SERVICES AGREEMENT

This LIGHTING AND RIGGING SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of July 20, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Vogert Live Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Client**"), and **Lumenhoist Production Services, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Vendor**"). Client and Vendor are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## 1. Background and Scope

1.1 **Background.** Client promotes live music events, including concerts at third-party venues and the "Ironfern Fest" music festival. Vendor provides stage lighting, rigging, truss, motor and related production services and equipment for live events.

1.2 **Engagement.** Client engages Vendor, on a non-exclusive basis, to provide the lighting and rigging services described in one or more event orders signed by both Parties (each, an "**Event Order**"). Vendor accepts the engagement on the terms of this Agreement.

1.3 **Event Orders.** Each Event Order shall identify (a) the event, venue and event dates; (b) load-in, show and load-out schedules; (c) the lighting plot, rigging plot and equipment list; (d) the crew to be supplied by Vendor; and (e) the fees for that event. Each Event Order is governed by and incorporates this Agreement. An Event Order may modify a term of this Agreement for that event only if it identifies the Section being modified.

1.4 **No Minimum Commitment.** Client is not obligated to issue any minimum number of Event Orders. Vendor is not obligated to accept any proposed Event Order until it is signed by both Parties.

## 2. Services

2.1 **Services.** For each Event Order, Vendor shall (a) supply the lighting fixtures, consoles, dimming, power distribution, truss, chain motors, rigging hardware and cabling listed in the Event Order (the "**Equipment**"); (b) transport the Equipment to and from the venue; (c) install, focus, program and operate the lighting systems; (d) hang, rig and strike all truss and suspended loads; and (e) provide the qualified crew listed in the Event Order (together, the "**Services**").

2.2 **Standard of Performance.** Vendor shall perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner, in accordance with generally accepted industry standards for live event production. Vendor shall comply with all applicable Laws, venue rules and the reasonable instructions of Client's production manager.

2.3 **Rigging Safety.** Vendor shall:

(a) perform all rigging under the direct supervision of a rigger certified by the Entertainment Technician Certification Program (ETCP) or an equivalent recognized program;

(b) prepare a rigging plot showing load points, point loads and total suspended weight for each event, and deliver it to Client and the venue at least ten (10) days before load-in;

(c) not exceed the rated capacity of any venue rigging point, roof structure, ground-support system or Equipment component;

(d) inspect all motors, chain, slings, shackles and truss before each event and remove from service any item that is damaged or overdue for inspection; and

(e) maintain inspection and maintenance records for the Equipment and provide them to Client on request.

2.4 **Outdoor Events.** For outdoor events, including Ironfern Fest, Vendor shall comply with the wind and weather action plan agreed with Client for the event. Vendor's crew chief may halt work or lower suspended loads when, in his or her reasonable judgment, weather conditions create a safety risk. Vendor shall not be in breach of this Agreement for any delay or cancellation caused by such a safety decision made in good faith.

2.5 **Personnel.** Vendor shall supply crew members who are qualified, trained and, where required by Law or venue rules, licensed for their assigned tasks. Vendor is solely responsible for the hiring, supervision, compensation, benefits and tax withholding of its personnel. Client may require Vendor to remove from an event any crew member who acts unsafely or unprofessionally. Vendor shall promptly provide a qualified replacement.

2.6 **Labor Requirements.** Where a venue requires the use of local stagehand labor or union personnel, Client and Vendor shall identify those requirements in the Event Order. Vendor shall coordinate its crew with that labor as directed by the venue.

## 3. Client Responsibilities

3.1 Client shall (a) provide Vendor with access to the venue at the scheduled load-in and load-out times; (b) furnish venue rigging specifications, structural load ratings and power availability that are in Client's possession; (c) arrange adequate power service at the venue as specified in the Event Order; and (d) designate a production manager with authority to give instructions to Vendor on Client's behalf.

3.2 Client shall arrange reasonable secured storage for the Equipment at the venue during the event, where the venue makes such storage available.

## 4. Fees and Payment

4.1 **Fees.** Client shall pay Vendor the fees set out in each Event Order. Unless the Event Order provides otherwise, fees include Equipment rental, crew labor for the scheduled hours, programming time and transportation.

4.2 **Deposit.** For each Event Order, Client shall pay a deposit of thirty percent (30%) of the estimated fees within ten (10) days after the Event Order is signed.

4.3 **Overtime and Additional Services.** Crew time in excess of the scheduled hours, and any Equipment or services added at Client's written request after the Event Order is signed, shall be charged at the rates in Schedule 1. Vendor shall obtain approval from Client's production manager, which may be given by email or text message, before incurring overtime or adding Equipment, except where necessary to address an immediate safety risk.

4.4 **Invoicing.** Vendor shall invoice the balance of the fees, together with any approved overtime and additional charges, within fifteen (15) days after load-out. Invoices shall include reasonable supporting detail, including crew time sheets.

4.5 **Payment Terms.** Client shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt. If Client disputes any portion of an invoice in good faith, it shall notify Vendor in writing within fifteen (15) days after receipt, describing the disputed amount and the reason for the dispute. Client shall pay the undisputed portion when due. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute promptly.

4.6 **Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due shall bear interest at one percent (1%) per month, or the maximum rate permitted by Law, if lower.

4.7 **Taxes.** Fees are exclusive of sales, use and similar taxes, which Client shall pay where applicable. Each Party is responsible for taxes on its own income.

## 5. Cancellation and Rescheduling

5.1 **Cancellation by Client.** Client may cancel any Event Order by written notice to Vendor. Client shall pay the following cancellation fee, as a percentage of the estimated fees for the event:

(a) notice given more than sixty (60) days before load-in: no fee, and Vendor shall refund the deposit;

(b) notice given between thirty (30) and sixty (60) days before load-in: the deposit only;

(c) notice given fewer than thirty (30) days before load-in: fifty percent (50%); and

(d) notice given fewer than seven (7) days before load-in: seventy-five percent (75%), plus any non-refundable third-party costs incurred by Vendor with Client's approval.

5.2 **Rescheduling.** If Client reschedules an event to a date within twelve (12) months after the original date and Vendor is available on the new date, the deposit shall be applied to the rescheduled event and no cancellation fee shall apply.

5.3 **Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for failure or delay in performance caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including severe weather, fire, flood, epidemic, act of government, civil disturbance, labor strike (other than by the affected Party's own personnel) or the cancellation of an event by a venue or governmental authority (a "**Force Majeure Event**"). If an event is cancelled because of a Force Majeure Event, Client shall pay Vendor for Services actually performed and non-refundable third-party costs actually incurred, and Vendor shall refund any remaining deposit.

## 6. Equipment

6.1 **Ownership.** Vendor retains title to all Equipment. Client shall not permit any lien to attach to the Equipment arising from Client's acts.

6.2 **Risk of Loss.** Vendor bears the risk of loss of or damage to the Equipment, except to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Client or its employees, contractors or invitees (other than Vendor and its personnel).

6.3 **Condition.** Vendor shall deliver Equipment that is in good working order, properly maintained and compliant with applicable safety standards. Vendor shall supply replacement Equipment at its own cost if any item fails during an event.

## 7. Insurance

7.1 Throughout the Term and for any event performed under an Event Order, Vendor shall maintain, with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best:

(a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate;

(b) umbrella or excess liability insurance with limits of not less than $5,000,000;

(c) commercial automobile liability insurance with a combined single limit of not less than $1,000,000;

(d) workers' compensation insurance as required by Law and employer's liability insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000; and

(e) equipment floater or inland marine insurance covering the full replacement value of the Equipment.

7.2 The policies in Sections 7.1(a) and 7.1(b) shall name Client, its Affiliates and each venue identified in an Event Order as additional insureds, and shall be primary and non-contributory. Vendor shall deliver certificates of insurance to Client before each event and shall give Client at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of cancellation or material reduction of coverage.

## 8. Indemnification

8.1 **By Vendor.** Vendor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Client, its Affiliates and their respective managers, officers, employees and agents from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees ("**Losses**"), arising out of third-party claims to the extent caused by (a) the negligence or willful misconduct of Vendor or its personnel; (b) the failure of any Equipment or rigging supplied or installed by Vendor; or (c) Vendor's breach of this Agreement or violation of applicable Law.

8.2 **By Client.** Client shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Vendor and its members, officers, employees and agents from and against all Losses arising out of third-party claims to the extent caused by (a) the negligence or willful misconduct of Client or its employees; or (b) Client's breach of this Agreement or violation of applicable Law.

8.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of any claim. Failure to give prompt notice relieves the indemnifying Party of its obligations only to the extent it is actually prejudiced. The indemnifying Party shall control the defense with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified Party. It shall not settle any claim that imposes obligations on, or admits fault by, the indemnified Party without that Party's written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

9.1 Neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special or punitive damages, or for lost profits or lost ticket revenue, arising out of this Agreement, even if advised of their possibility.

9.2 Except for obligations under Section 8, a Party's gross negligence or willful misconduct, and Client's payment obligations, each Party's aggregate liability under any Event Order shall not exceed the total fees payable under that Event Order.

## 10. Term and Termination

10.1 **Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until December 31, 2027 (the "**Term**"). It renews automatically for successive one-year periods unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current period.

10.2 **Termination for Convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement on ninety (90) days' written notice. Any Event Order signed before the notice is given shall continue to be performed under this Agreement unless cancelled under Section 5.1.

10.3 **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any Event Order by written notice if the other Party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within fifteen (15) days after written notice. Client may terminate an Event Order immediately if Vendor commits a material safety violation that is not promptly corrected.

10.4 **Effect.** On termination, Client shall pay Vendor for Services performed through the termination date. Sections 4, 6.1, 8, 9, 11 and 12 survive termination.

## 11. Confidentiality; Publicity

11.1 Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public information of the other Party disclosed in connection with this Agreement, including artist riders, show designs, event budgets, unannounced event dates and venue arrangements. A Party may disclose such information only to its personnel and advisors who need to know it for this Agreement and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective, or as required by Law.

11.2 Lighting designs and programming created by Vendor for an event may be used by Vendor in its portfolio after the event has been publicly performed, provided that Vendor does not disclose any artist's confidential information. Vendor shall not use Client's names or marks, including "Ironfern Fest," in marketing materials without Client's prior written consent.

## 12. General

12.1 **Independent Contractor.** Vendor is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship.

12.2 **Notices.** Notices shall be in writing and delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set out on the signature page or to any other address designated by notice.

12.3 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. However, either Party may assign this Agreement to an Affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business to which this Agreement relates, on written notice to the other Party. Vendor may not subcontract rigging services without Client's prior written consent.

12.4 **Affiliate.** "**Affiliate**" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with a Party.

12.5 **Law.** "**Law**" means any statute, ordinance, regulation, code, permit condition or order of any governmental authority.

12.6 **Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only in a writing signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing.

12.7 **Governing Law; Venue.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware.

12.8 **Severability.** If any provision is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

12.9 **Counterparts.** This Agreement and any Event Order may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which is deemed an original.
