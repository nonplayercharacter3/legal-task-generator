# MUTUAL CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT

**by and between**

**VOTOX GLAZING HOLDINGS, INC.**

and

**STOUNAISK INDUSTRIES, INC.**

Dated as of August 17, 2026

---

This MUTUAL CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of August 17, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between Votox Glazing Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Votox**"), and Stounaisk Industries, Inc., an Ohio corporation ("**Stounaisk**"). Votox and Stounaisk are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Stounaisk owns all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of Lumacrest Facade Systems, Inc., a Pennsylvania corporation (the "**Company**").

B. The Parties wish to evaluate and negotiate a possible transaction in which Votox would acquire the Company (the "**Proposed Transaction**"). In connection with that evaluation, each Party expects to disclose to the other certain non-public information.

C. The Parties wish to set out the terms on which that information will be disclosed, used and protected.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Confidential Information.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information, in any form, that a Party or its Representatives (the "**Disclosing Party**") furnishes to the other Party or its Representatives (the "**Receiving Party**") on or after the Effective Date in connection with the Proposed Transaction. Confidential Information includes:

(a) financial statements, forecasts, budgets, tax returns and tax workpapers;

(b) customer, supplier, project, bid and pricing information;

(c) product specifications, coating and fabrication process parameters, quality procedures and test data;

(d) information about real property, permits and environmental conditions, including environmental site assessment reports;

(e) employee and benefits information; and

(f) all notes, analyses, compilations and other materials prepared by the Receiving Party or its Representatives to the extent they contain or reflect any of the foregoing (collectively, "**Derivative Materials**").

Information about the Company that Stounaisk or the Company furnishes is Confidential Information of Stounaisk.

**1.2 Exclusions.** Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can show:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a disclosure by the Receiving Party or its Representatives in breach of this Agreement;

(b) was lawfully in the Receiving Party's possession on a non-confidential basis before its disclosure under this Agreement;

(c) becomes available to the Receiving Party on a non-confidential basis from a source, other than the Disclosing Party or its Representatives, that is not known by the Receiving Party to be bound by a confidentiality obligation to the Disclosing Party; or

(d) was independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

**1.3 Representatives.** "**Representatives**" means a Party's Affiliates and its and their directors, officers, employees, agents, attorneys, accountants, financial advisors, environmental and other consultants and, in the case of Votox, its actual or prospective debt financing sources. "**Affiliate**" means any person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with a Party.

**1.4 Transaction Information.** "**Transaction Information**" means the fact that the Parties are evaluating the Proposed Transaction, the fact that Confidential Information has been made available, and the status and terms of any discussions between the Parties.

## 2. OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECEIVING PARTY

**2.1 Use.** The Receiving Party shall use the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information solely to evaluate, negotiate and, if applicable, consummate the Proposed Transaction.

**2.2 Non-Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall keep the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information and the Transaction Information confidential. It shall not disclose them to any person other than its Representatives who need to know the information for the purpose described in Section 2.1. Before disclosure, the Receiving Party shall inform those Representatives of the confidential nature of the information and direct them to treat it in accordance with this Agreement. The Receiving Party is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives.

**2.3 Standard of Care.** The Receiving Party shall protect the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information using at least the degree of care it uses to protect its own information of a similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care.

**2.4 Notice of Unauthorized Disclosure.** The Receiving Party shall notify the Disclosing Party promptly after it becomes aware of any unauthorized use or disclosure of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information. It shall cooperate reasonably with the Disclosing Party to limit the effect of that use or disclosure.

## 3. COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

If the Receiving Party or any of its Representatives is requested or required by law, regulation, subpoena, court order or the rules of any securities exchange to disclose any Confidential Information or Transaction Information, the Receiving Party shall, to the extent legally permitted:

(a) give the Disclosing Party prompt written notice so that the Disclosing Party may seek, at its own expense, a protective order or other appropriate remedy; and

(b) cooperate reasonably with the Disclosing Party's efforts to obtain that remedy.

If no such remedy is obtained, the Receiving Party or its Representative may disclose only the portion of the information that its counsel advises is legally required to be disclosed, and shall use reasonable efforts to obtain assurance that confidential treatment will be accorded to it.

## 4. SITE VISITS AND CONTACT

**4.1 Site Visits.** Any visit by Votox or its Representatives to the Company's plants in Erie, Pennsylvania and Rochester, New York, or to any facility of Stounaisk, shall be scheduled in advance through Stounaisk's designated contact. Visitors shall comply with the host's reasonable safety and security rules. No sampling of soil, groundwater, building materials or other media shall be performed without Stounaisk's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld for a sampling plan recommended in a Phase I environmental site assessment.

**4.2 No Contact.** Without Stounaisk's prior consent, Votox shall not, and shall direct its Representatives not to, initiate contact regarding the Proposed Transaction or the Confidential Information with any officer, employee, customer, supplier or landlord of the Company, other than persons Stounaisk designates for that purpose. This Section 4.2 does not restrict contacts in the ordinary course of business unrelated to the Proposed Transaction.

## 5. NON-SOLICITATION

For a period of twelve (12) months after the Effective Date, Votox shall not, and shall cause its controlled Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly solicit for employment any officer or salaried employee of the Company with whom Votox first had contact, or of whom it first became aware, in connection with the Proposed Transaction. This Section 5 does not prohibit general solicitations through advertisements or search firms not directed at those persons, or the hiring of any person who responds to them. This Section 5 ceases to apply upon the closing of the Proposed Transaction.

## 6. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION

Upon the Disclosing Party's written request, the Receiving Party shall promptly, and in any event within fifteen (15) Business Days, return or destroy all of the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information in its or its Representatives' possession, and destroy all Derivative Materials. On request, an authorized officer of the Receiving Party shall certify that destruction in writing. The Receiving Party and its Representatives may, however, retain copies (a) as required by law, regulation or bona fide internal compliance policy, or (b) stored in automatic electronic backup systems that are not readily accessible. Any retained copies remain subject to this Agreement for so long as they are retained, notwithstanding the expiration of the term in Section 9. "**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

## 7. NO REPRESENTATIONS; NO OBLIGATION

**7.1 No Representations.** Neither Party nor any of its Representatives makes any representation or warranty, express or implied, as to the accuracy or completeness of its Confidential Information. Neither Party shall have any liability to the other resulting from the use of Confidential Information, except as may be provided in a definitive agreement for the Proposed Transaction.

**7.2 No Obligation to Proceed.** Neither Party is obligated to pursue or consummate the Proposed Transaction. No agreement providing for the Proposed Transaction shall exist unless and until a definitive written agreement has been executed and delivered. Either Party may terminate discussions at any time for any reason.

**7.3 No License.** Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any license or other right in the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information or intellectual property, except the limited right of use in Section 2.1.

## 8. REMEDIES

Each Party acknowledges that money damages may not be a sufficient remedy for a breach of this Agreement and that the Disclosing Party may suffer irreparable harm from such a breach. The Disclosing Party is therefore entitled to seek specific performance and injunctive relief, without proof of actual damages and without posting a bond, in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity.

## 9. TERM

This Agreement shall remain in effect for two (2) years after the Effective Date. Expiration does not relieve either Party of liability for any breach occurring before expiration.

## 10. GENERAL

**10.1 Notices.** All notices shall be in writing and deemed given when delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the following addresses, or another address a Party designates by notice:

If to Votox: Votox Glazing Holdings, Inc., [address], Attention: General Counsel.

If to Stounaisk: Stounaisk Industries, Inc., 2200 Sandusky Pike, Fremont, Ohio, Attention: Chief Executive Officer.

**10.2 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction. Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in New York County, New York in any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

**10.3 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION OR PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**10.4 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that Votox may assign its rights under this Agreement to any Affiliate that acquires the Company.

**10.5 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No failure or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement operates as a waiver of that right. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the Party against whom it is asserted.

**10.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**10.7 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all counterparts together constitute one instrument.
