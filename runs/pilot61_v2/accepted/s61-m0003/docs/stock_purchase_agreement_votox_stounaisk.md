This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026, by and between Stounaisk Industries, Inc., an Ohio corporation ("**Seller**"), and Votox Glazing Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of capital stock (the "**Shares**") of Lumacrest Facade Systems, Inc., a Pennsylvania corporation (the "**Company**").

B. The Company fabricates tempered, laminated and insulated glass units and curtain-wall glazing systems at manufacturing plants located in Erie, Pennsylvania and Rochester, New York (the "**Business**").

C. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE

**2.1 Purchase and Sale; Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, free and clear of all Liens, for an aggregate purchase price of $63,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment as provided in Section 2.3.

**2.2 Closing Payments.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay or cause to be paid, by wire transfer of immediately available funds:

(a) to the Escrow Agent, the Indemnity Escrow Amount and the Adjustment Escrow Amount, in accordance with Section 2.4;

(b) on behalf of the Company, the Indebtedness and Transaction Expenses set forth in the Estimated Closing Statement, to the payees thereof; and

(c) to Seller, the Purchase Price, plus or minus the estimated adjustments under Section 2.3(a), less the amounts paid under Sections 2.2(a) and 2.2(b).

**2.3 Purchase Price Adjustment.**

(a) Not later than three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a statement (the "**Estimated Closing Statement**") setting forth its good faith estimate of Closing Working Capital, Indebtedness and Transaction Expenses.

(b) Within ninety (90) days after the Closing Date, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a statement (the "**Closing Statement**") setting forth its calculation of Closing Working Capital, Indebtedness and Transaction Expenses. Seller shall have thirty (30) days to deliver a written notice of objection. Any dispute not resolved within thirty (30) days after delivery of an objection shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm mutually selected by Buyer and Seller, whose determination shall be final and binding absent manifest error.

(c) If the final Purchase Price exceeds the estimated Purchase Price paid at Closing, Buyer shall pay the excess to Seller. If the estimated Purchase Price exceeds the final Purchase Price, the shortfall shall be paid to Buyer first from the Adjustment Escrow Amount and, to the extent insufficient, by Seller directly. Buyer and Seller shall deliver joint written instructions to the Escrow Agent to release any remaining Adjustment Escrow Amount to Seller.

**2.4 Escrow.**

(a) At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit with the Escrow Agent (i) $6,300,000 (the "**Indemnity Escrow Amount**") and (ii) $750,000 (the "**Adjustment Escrow Amount**"), each to be held in a separate account and disbursed in accordance with this Agreement and the Escrow Agreement.

(b) The Indemnity Escrow Amount shall be available to satisfy claims of the Buyer Indemnitees under Article IX. The Adjustment Escrow Amount shall be available solely to satisfy amounts owed to Buyer under Section 2.3(c).

(c) Fees and expenses of the Escrow Agent shall be borne one-half by Buyer and one-half by Seller.

**2.5 Withholding.** Buyer shall be entitled to deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement such amounts as are required to be withheld under applicable Tax Law.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the purchase and sale of the Shares (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents on the third (3rd) Business Day after satisfaction or waiver of the conditions in Article VIII (other than conditions to be satisfied at Closing), or on such other date as the parties agree (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer: (a) stock certificates for the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or with stock powers attached; (b) the Escrow Agreement and the Transition Services Agreement, duly executed by Seller; (c) resignations of the directors and officers of the Company designated by Buyer; (d) payoff letters for all Indebtedness; (e) a properly completed IRS Form W-9 of Seller; and (f) the certificate described in Section 8.2(c).

**3.3 Buyer Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver the payments described in Section 2.2, the Escrow Agreement duly executed by Buyer, and the certificate described in Section 8.3(c).

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date hereof until Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to operate the Business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice and to use commercially reasonable efforts to preserve its assets, relationships and goodwill. Without Buyer's prior written consent, the Company shall not incur Indebtedness outside the ordinary course, sell material assets, amend any Material Contract, or declare any dividend other than cash dividends paid prior to the Closing.

**6.2 Access to Information.** Seller shall cause the Company to afford Buyer and its representatives reasonable access during normal business hours to the properties, books and records of the Company, including for the purpose of conducting environmental assessments of the Erie and Rochester plants, subject to the Confidentiality Agreement between the parties.

**6.3 Efforts; Consents.** Each party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to satisfy the conditions to Closing and to obtain all consents required in connection with the transactions contemplated hereby.

**6.4 Exclusivity.** Until the earlier of the Closing and the termination of this Agreement, Seller shall not, and shall cause the Company and its representatives not to, solicit, negotiate or enter into any agreement regarding an acquisition of the Company or any material portion of its assets by any Person other than Buyer.

**6.5 Transition Services.** At Closing, Seller and the Company shall enter into the Transition Services Agreement, under which Seller shall provide to the Company the coating, warehousing and other services described therein.

**6.6 Non-Competition; Non-Solicitation.** For a period of five (5) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, directly or indirectly engage in the fabrication of architectural glass products in North America in competition with the Business, or solicit for employment any employee of the Company, except through general solicitations not targeted at such employees.

**6.7 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, hold in confidence all confidential information concerning the Company and the Business.

## ARTICLE VII. TAX MATTERS

**7.1 Tax Returns.** Seller shall prepare, or cause to be prepared, at its expense all Tax returns of the Company for taxable periods ending on or before the Closing Date that are due after the Closing Date, in a manner consistent with past practice, and shall deliver each such return to Buyer for review and comment at least thirty (30) days before filing. Buyer shall prepare all other Tax returns of the Company.

**7.2 Straddle Periods.** For any taxable period beginning before and ending after the Closing Date, Taxes shall be allocated to the Pre-Closing Tax Period (a) for property and similar ad valorem Taxes, ratably on a daily basis, and (b) for all other Taxes, on a closing-of-the-books basis as of the end of the Closing Date.

**7.3 Cooperation; Tax Contests.** Buyer and Seller shall cooperate fully in connection with the filing of Tax returns and any audit or proceeding with respect to Taxes of the Company. Buyer shall control any Tax contest relating to a Pre-Closing Tax Period, provided that Seller may participate at its own expense and Buyer shall not settle any such contest without Seller's consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

**7.4 Transfer Taxes.** All transfer, documentary, stamp and similar Taxes arising from the transactions contemplated hereby shall be borne one-half by Buyer and one-half by Seller.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties contained in this Agreement shall survive the Closing until the Release Date (as defined in the Escrow Agreement); provided that the Fundamental Representations and the representations and warranties in Section 4.14 (Taxes) shall survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations. The covenants and agreements of the parties shall survive the Closing in accordance with their terms or, if no term is specified, until fully performed. No claim may be asserted in respect of any representation or warranty unless a Claim Notice is delivered prior to the expiration of the applicable survival period, and any claim so asserted shall survive until finally resolved.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer, the Company and their respective Affiliates, officers, directors and employees (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses incurred by any Buyer Indemnitee arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in Article IV or in any certificate delivered by Seller hereunder;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement;

(c) any Pre-Closing Taxes;

(d) any Pre-Closing Environmental Liabilities; and

(e) any Indebtedness or Transaction Expenses not paid at or prior to Closing or taken into account in the final Purchase Price.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify Seller and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to (a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in Article V, or (b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Buyer in this Agreement.

**9.4 Claims Procedures.**

(a) An indemnified party shall deliver a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**") to the indemnifying party describing in reasonable detail the nature and basis of the claim and, to the extent known, a good faith estimate of the Losses. Failure to give prompt notice shall not relieve the indemnifying party of its obligations except to the extent it is materially prejudiced thereby. Any Claim Notice delivered by a Buyer Indemnitee shall be delivered concurrently to the Escrow Agent.

(b) The indemnifying party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party by written notice within twenty (20) days after receipt of the Claim Notice, provided that Seller may not assume the defense of any claim relating to Taxes except as provided in Section 7.3, or any claim seeking injunctive relief against the Company. No party shall settle any third-party claim without the consent of the other, not to be unreasonably withheld.

(c) If the indemnifying party does not object in writing to a direct claim within thirty (30) days after receipt of the Claim Notice, the claim shall be conclusively deemed a liability of the indemnifying party.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) The aggregate liability of Seller for all Losses under this Article IX shall not exceed the Cap Amount.

(b) Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) unless and until the aggregate amount of all Losses thereunder exceeds $630,000 (the "**Deductible**"), in which event Seller shall be liable only for Losses in excess of the Deductible.

(c) The limitations in Sections 9.5(a) and 9.5(b) shall not apply to Losses arising out of or relating to (i) Fraud, (ii) any breach of any Fundamental Representation, or (iii) the matters described in Section 9.2(d), provided that Seller's aggregate liability under this Article IX shall in no event exceed the Purchase Price.

(d) For purposes of calculating Losses under this Article IX (but not for determining whether a breach has occurred), all qualifications as to materiality or Material Adverse Effect shall be disregarded.

(e) Losses shall be calculated net of insurance proceeds actually received by the indemnified party in respect thereof, less the costs of recovery.

**9.6 Manner of Payment.** Any Losses payable to a Buyer Indemnitee shall be satisfied first from the Indemnity Escrow Amount, to the extent then available, and thereafter directly by Seller. Buyer and Seller shall promptly deliver joint written instructions to the Escrow Agent to release funds in respect of any claim finally determined under this Agreement.

**9.7 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes unless otherwise required by Law.

**9.8 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims of Fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, and disputes under Section 2.3, the indemnification provisions of this Article IX shall be the sole and exclusive remedy of the parties with respect to this Agreement and the transactions contemplated hereby.
