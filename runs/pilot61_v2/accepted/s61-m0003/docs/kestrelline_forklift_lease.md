# MASTER EQUIPMENT LEASE AGREEMENT

**by and between**

**KESTRELLINE MATERIAL HANDLING LLC**, as Lessor,

and

**LUMACREST FACADE SYSTEMS, INC.**, as Lessee

Dated as of March 3, 2025

Lease No. KMH-2025-0417

---

This MASTER EQUIPMENT LEASE AGREEMENT (this "**Lease**") is entered into as of March 3, 2025 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between Kestrelline Material Handling LLC, a Pennsylvania limited liability company ("**Lessor**"), and Lumacrest Facade Systems, Inc., a Pennsylvania corporation ("**Lessee**"). Lessor and Lessee are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## 1. LEASE OF EQUIPMENT

**1.1 Lease.** Lessor leases to Lessee, and Lessee leases from Lessor, the material handling equipment described in each equipment schedule executed by the Parties under this Lease (each, a "**Schedule**"). The equipment includes all attachments, accessories, replacement parts, batteries and chargers listed in or furnished under a Schedule (collectively, the "**Equipment**"). Schedule No. 1 is attached to this Lease.

**1.2 Schedules.** Each Schedule incorporates the terms of this Lease and is a separate lease of the Equipment described in it. If a Schedule conflicts with this Lease, the Schedule controls only for the Equipment described in that Schedule. The Parties may add Equipment by executing additional Schedules.

**1.3 Delivery and Acceptance.** Lessor shall deliver the Equipment to the location stated in the applicable Schedule (the "**Equipment Location**") on or about the delivery date stated in it. Lessee shall inspect the Equipment within five (5) business days after delivery. Lessee shall then either sign a delivery and acceptance certificate or notify Lessor in writing of any nonconformity. If Lessee does neither within that period, the Equipment is deemed accepted. Lessor shall promptly repair or replace any nonconforming Equipment at its cost.

## 2. TERM

**2.1 Term.** The term for each unit of Equipment begins on the date of its acceptance (the "**Commencement Date**") and continues for the number of months stated in the applicable Schedule (the "**Initial Term**").

**2.2 Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term, the lease of each unit continues on a month-to-month basis at the same Monthly Rent. Either Party may end the month-to-month term by giving the other at least thirty (30) days' written notice.

**2.3 Return.** When the lease of any unit ends, Lessee shall make the unit available for pickup at the Equipment Location. The unit must be in the same condition as when delivered, ordinary wear and tear excepted. Lessor shall arrange and pay for pickup. Lessor shall inspect returned units within ten (10) business days after pickup. It shall give Lessee a written itemized statement of any damage beyond ordinary wear and tear, as defined in the Return Condition Standards attached as Exhibit A. Lessee shall pay the reasonable cost of repairing that damage within thirty (30) days after receiving Lessor's invoice.

## 3. RENT AND CHARGES

**3.1 Monthly Rent.** Lessee shall pay the monthly rent stated in the applicable Schedule for each unit (the "**Monthly Rent**"). Monthly Rent is payable in advance on the first day of each month. Rent for any partial month is prorated on a daily basis.

**3.2 Invoicing and Payment.** Lessor shall invoice Lessee monthly. Lessee shall pay each invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt, by ACH transfer to the account Lessor designates in writing. Lessor shall confirm any change to its payment instructions by telephone with Lessee's accounts payable contact before the change takes effect.

**3.3 Hour Meter Overage.** Each Schedule states the annual hour allowance for each unit. Within thirty (30) days after each anniversary of the Commencement Date, Lessor shall read the hour meter on each unit. Lessor may invoice Lessee for hours used above the allowance at the overage rate stated in the Schedule.

**3.4 Late Payment.** Any undisputed amount not paid when due bears interest from the due date at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by law.

**3.5 Disputed Invoices.** Lessee may withhold any invoiced amount it disputes in good faith. To do so, Lessee must give Lessor written notice of the dispute before the payment due date. The Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute within thirty (30) days after the notice. No interest accrues on a disputed amount that is resolved in Lessee's favor.

**3.6 Taxes.** Lessee shall pay all sales, use and similar taxes imposed on the rental of the Equipment. Lessor shall state those taxes separately on each invoice. Lessor shall pay all personal property taxes assessed on the Equipment and all taxes on its own income.

## 4. USE, MAINTENANCE AND OPERATION

**4.1 Permitted Use.** Lessee shall use the Equipment only at the Equipment Location and only for handling glass, racks, raw materials and finished goods in the ordinary course of its business. Lessee shall not exceed the rated load capacity of any unit. Lessee shall not move any unit to another location without Lessor's prior written consent, which Lessor shall not unreasonably withhold.

**4.2 Operators.** Only Lessee employees or contractors who have completed operator training and evaluation under 29 C.F.R. § 1910.178(l) may operate the Equipment. Lessee shall keep training records and make them available to Lessor on request.

**4.3 Planned Maintenance.** Lessor shall perform planned maintenance on each unit at the intervals stated in the applicable Schedule. Planned maintenance includes inspection, lubrication, adjustment, fluid and filter changes, and battery and charger servicing. Lessor bears the cost of planned maintenance, which is included in the Monthly Rent.

**4.4 Repairs.** Lessor shall make all repairs needed because of normal wear or mechanical failure, at its cost. Lessor shall respond to a service call within eight (8) business hours after Lessee reports it. If a unit is out of service for more than two (2) consecutive business days, Lessor shall provide a substitute unit of comparable capacity at no additional charge. Lessee shall pay the reasonable cost of repairs needed because of misuse, negligence, accident or operation by untrained personnel.

**4.5 Daily Inspections.** Lessee shall cause its operators to perform the daily pre-shift inspection described in the manufacturer's operator manual. Lessee shall remove from service, and promptly report to Lessor, any unit found to be unsafe.

**4.6 Alterations.** Lessee shall not alter any unit or add attachments without Lessor's prior written consent. Any approved attachment supplied by Lessor becomes part of the Equipment.

## 5. TITLE; RISK OF LOSS; INSURANCE

**5.1 Title.** The Equipment is and remains the property of Lessor. This Lease is a true lease and not a sale or financing arrangement. Lessee acquires no right, title or interest in the Equipment other than the right to use it under this Lease. Lessee shall keep the Equipment free of all liens arising by, through or under Lessee. Lessor may file a precautionary UCC financing statement describing the Equipment.

**5.2 Risk of Loss.** From delivery until Lessor picks up a unit, Lessee bears the risk of loss, theft, damage or destruction of that unit, except loss or damage caused by Lessor or its personnel. If a unit is lost, stolen or damaged beyond repair, Lessee shall notify Lessor promptly. Lessee shall then pay Lessor the stipulated loss value of the unit stated in the applicable Schedule. The lease of that unit ends on payment, and Monthly Rent for that unit stops accruing.

**5.3 Lessee Insurance.** During the term, Lessee shall maintain, at its expense:

(a) property insurance covering the Equipment for its full replacement value, naming Lessor as loss payee; and

(b) commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence, naming Lessor as an additional insured.

Lessee shall deliver certificates of insurance to Lessor on request. Each policy shall provide that the insurer will give Lessor at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of cancellation.

**5.4 Lessor Insurance.** Lessor shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and workers' compensation insurance as required by law. These policies shall cover Lessor's personnel while they are performing maintenance, repairs, delivery or pickup at the Equipment Location.

## 6. WARRANTIES

**6.1 Lessor Warranty.** Lessor warrants that, on delivery, each unit will be in good operating condition and will comply with the applicable ANSI/ITSDF B56.1 safety standard. Lessor also warrants that it has the right to lease the Equipment to Lessee. Lessor shall pass through to Lessee, to the extent assignable, the benefit of any manufacturer warranty on the Equipment.

**6.2 Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS SET FORTH IN SECTION 6.1, LESSOR MAKES NO REPRESENTATION OR WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, WITH RESPECT TO THE EQUIPMENT, INCLUDING ANY WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 7. INDEMNIFICATION; LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**7.1 Indemnification by Lessee.** Lessee shall indemnify and hold harmless Lessor and its members, officers and employees from third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage. This obligation applies to the extent the claims arise from the operation or use of the Equipment by Lessee or its personnel.

**7.2 Indemnification by Lessor.** Lessor shall indemnify and hold harmless Lessee and its directors, officers and employees from third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage. This obligation applies to the extent the claims arise from either of the following:

(a) the negligence or willful misconduct of Lessor or its personnel; or

(b) a defect in the Equipment existing at delivery or caused by Lessor's maintenance or repair.

**7.3 Limitation of Liability.** Neither Party is liable to the other for any lost profits or for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages arising out of this Lease. This Section 7.3 does not limit either Party's obligations under Sections 7.1 and 7.2 for amounts paid to third parties.

## 8. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

**8.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Lessee fails to pay any undisputed amount within fifteen (15) days after receiving written notice that the amount is past due;

(b) either Party fails to perform any other material obligation under this Lease, and the failure continues for thirty (30) days after it receives written notice describing the failure in reasonable detail; or

(c) either Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of a voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

**8.2 Remedies.** If an Event of Default by Lessee occurs, Lessor may take any of the following actions upon written notice to Lessee:

(a) terminate the lease of any or all affected units;

(b) enter the Equipment Location during normal business hours, upon reasonable advance notice, to recover those units; and

(c) recover all accrued and unpaid amounts, together with its reasonable costs of recovery.

If an Event of Default by Lessor occurs, Lessee may terminate the lease of any or all affected units upon written notice to Lessor. In that case Lessee is not liable for Monthly Rent for those units after the termination date. Each Party also retains any other remedies available to it at law.

**8.3 Mitigation.** Each Party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate its damages resulting from an Event of Default.

## 9. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**9.1 Assignment.** Lessee shall not assign this Lease or sublease any Equipment without Lessor's prior written consent, which Lessor shall not unreasonably withhold. Lessor may assign its right to receive payments under this Lease to a financing source upon written notice to Lessee. No assignment by Lessor relieves Lessor of its maintenance and repair obligations or impairs Lessee's right of quiet enjoyment of the Equipment.

**9.2 Quiet Enjoyment.** So long as no Event of Default by Lessee has occurred and is continuing, neither Lessor nor any person claiming through Lessor shall disturb Lessee's possession and use of the Equipment.

**9.3 Notices.** All notices shall be in writing. They are deemed given when delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses set forth on the signature page or another address a Party designates by notice.

**9.4 Governing Law; Venue.** This Lease is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, including Article 2A of the Pennsylvania Uniform Commercial Code, without regard to conflict of laws principles. Any action arising out of this Lease shall be brought in the state or federal courts located in Erie County, Pennsylvania. Each Party submits to the jurisdiction of those courts.

**9.5 Amendment and Waiver.** This Lease and any Schedule may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the Party against whom it is asserted.

**9.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Lease is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**9.7 Entire Agreement.** This Lease, together with its Schedules and Exhibit A, is the entire agreement of the Parties about the leasing of the Equipment. It supersedes all prior proposals and quotations about that subject.

**9.8 Counterparts.** This Lease and any Schedule may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all counterparts together constitute one instrument.

## EXHIBIT A – RETURN CONDITION STANDARDS

Ordinary wear and tear means deterioration that results from normal use of a unit within its rated capacity and hour allowance, and that is consistent with the planned maintenance Lessor performed. Ordinary wear and tear includes the following:

1. tire wear within the manufacturer's replacement limits;
2. minor scratches, scuffs and paint wear that do not expose bare metal over more than 10% of any panel;
3. normal battery capacity loss consistent with the battery's age and charge cycles; and
4. seat and upholstery wear without tears or exposed padding.

Damage beyond ordinary wear and tear includes the following:

1. bent or cracked forks, masts, carriages, overhead guards or glass rack boom attachments;
2. broken lights, gauges, alarms or safety devices;
3. punctured or chunked tires;
4. battery damage caused by improper watering or charging by Lessee; and
5. missing attachments or components listed in the applicable Schedule.
