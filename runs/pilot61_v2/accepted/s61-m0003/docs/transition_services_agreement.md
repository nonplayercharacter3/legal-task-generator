# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

**by and between**

**STOUNAISK INDUSTRIES, INC.**, as Provider,

and

**LUMACREST FACADE SYSTEMS, INC.**, as Recipient

Dated as of [●], 2026

*Form – Exhibit B to Stock Purchase Agreement*

---

This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026, by and between Stounaisk Industries, Inc., an Ohio corporation ("**Provider**"), and Lumacrest Facade Systems, Inc., a Pennsylvania corporation (the "**Company**"). Provider and the Company are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Provider and Votox Glazing Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of [●], 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is acquiring from Provider all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of the Company.

B. Before the Closing, the Company has relied on Provider's retained operations for the coating of certain glass products, the warehousing of raw materials and finished goods, and certain shared administrative functions.

C. The Purchase Agreement requires Provider and the Company to enter into this Agreement at the Closing. Under this Agreement, Provider will continue to provide those services to the Company for a transitional period after the Closing on the terms set forth below.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with such Person.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Closing**" and "**Closing Date**" have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Company Materials**" means all raw glass, interlayer, spacer, sealant, coating stock, work-in-process, finished goods, racks, packaging and other tangible property of the Company delivered to, stored at or processed at the Facility in connection with the Services.

"**Facility**" means the real property owned by Provider located at 2200 Sandusky Pike, Fremont, Ohio, including the coating lines, warehouse buildings, loading docks, rail spur and related improvements located on that property, from which Provider will perform the coating and warehousing Services under this Agreement.

"**Omitted Service**" has the meaning set forth in Section 2.3.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Service Coordinator**" has the meaning set forth in Section 3.4.

"**Service Fees**" has the meaning set forth in Section 4.1.

"**Services**" means the services described in Schedule A, as that schedule may be amended from time to time in accordance with this Agreement, together with any Omitted Service added under Section 2.3.

"**Term**" has the meaning set forth in Section 7.1.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to Sections and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for convenience only.

## 2. SERVICES

**2.1 Provision of Services.** During the Term, Provider shall provide, or cause one or more of its Affiliates to provide, the Services to the Company. Each Service shall be provided for the period stated for that Service in Schedule A, or, if no period is stated, for the Term.

**2.2 Scope.** Each Service shall be substantially similar in scope, volume, nature and priority to the corresponding service Provider furnished to the Company during the twelve (12) months before the Closing Date. Provider is not required to provide any Service in volumes materially greater than the volumes furnished during that period, unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing.

**2.3 Omitted Services.** The Company may notify Provider within sixty (60) days after the Closing Date of any service that meets both of the following conditions:

(a) Provider or its Affiliates furnished the service to the Company during the twelve (12) months before the Closing Date; and

(b) the service is reasonably necessary for the operation of the Business but is not listed in Schedule A (an "**Omitted Service**").

Provider shall then use commercially reasonable efforts to provide the Omitted Service on terms consistent with this Agreement. The Parties shall amend Schedule A to describe the Omitted Service and its Service Fee, which shall be determined on the basis set forth in Section 4.1.

**2.4 Third-Party Consents.** If the provision of any Service requires the consent of a third party, Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain that consent. The Company shall bear any reasonable out-of-pocket consent fees that Provider incurs with the Company's prior written approval. If a consent cannot be obtained, the Parties shall cooperate in good faith to implement a reasonable alternative arrangement.

**2.5 No Transfer of Title.** Title to all Company Materials remains with the Company at all times. Provider shall hold Company Materials at the Facility as bailee for the Company. Provider shall keep Company Materials segregated from, and identifiable apart from, Provider's own inventory. Provider shall not permit any Lien to attach to Company Materials arising by, through or under Provider.

## 3. STANDARD OF PERFORMANCE

**3.1 Standard of Care.** Provider shall perform the Services:

(a) in a good and workmanlike manner;

(b) with the same degree of care, skill and diligence it used in providing the corresponding services to the Company during the twelve (12) months before the Closing Date; and

(c) in compliance with all applicable Laws and permits.

**3.2 Coating Specifications.** Provider shall perform coating Services in accordance with the Company's product specifications and quality procedures in effect on the Closing Date, and any revisions to them that the Company delivers to Provider in writing and that do not materially increase Provider's cost of performance. Provider shall maintain lot traceability records for all coated glass and deliver copies to the Company on request.

**3.3 Personnel.** Provider shall determine which of its personnel perform the Services. Provider shall remain responsible for the acts and omissions of those personnel and of any Affiliate or subcontractor performing Services.

**3.4 Service Coordinators.** Each Party shall designate an individual to act as its primary contact for the administration of this Agreement (each, a "**Service Coordinator**"). The Service Coordinators shall meet, in person or by video conference, at least monthly during the Term to review performance, volumes and scheduling.

**3.5 Disclaimer.** EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET FORTH IN THIS AGREEMENT, PROVIDER MAKES NO REPRESENTATION OR WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, WITH RESPECT TO THE SERVICES, INCLUDING ANY WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

**4.1 Service Fees.** The Company shall pay Provider a fee for each Service (the "**Service Fees**") equal to Provider's actual cost of providing that Service. Actual cost consists of the following, in each case without mark-up:

(a) direct labor and benefits;

(b) materials and consumables;

(c) utilities consumed in providing the Service; and

(d) a reasonable allocation of indirect costs, determined on a basis consistent with Provider's allocation of those costs to the Company before the Closing.

Schedule A sets out the estimated monthly Service Fee for each Service.

**4.2 Invoicing and Payment.** Provider shall invoice the Company monthly in arrears. Each invoice shall show the Service Fees for each Service, together with reasonable supporting detail. The Company shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt.

**4.3 Disputed Amounts.** The Company may withhold any invoiced amount that it disputes in good faith, provided it delivers written notice of the dispute to Provider before the payment due date. The Service Coordinators shall attempt to resolve the dispute within fifteen (15) Business Days. Provider shall continue to provide the Services while any dispute is pending.

**4.4 Taxes.** The Company shall bear any sales, use or similar Taxes imposed on the provision of the Services. Each Party shall bear its own income Taxes.

**4.5 Records and Audit.** Provider shall maintain accurate books and records of the costs underlying the Service Fees. Upon reasonable notice, and not more than once per calendar quarter, the Company or its representatives may inspect those books and records during normal business hours.

## 5. ACCESS; COMPANY MATERIALS

**5.1 Access to the Facility.** During the Term, Provider shall afford the Company's employees, carriers and representatives reasonable access to the Facility during normal operating hours, upon reasonable advance notice, for three purposes:

(a) delivering, inspecting and retrieving Company Materials;

(b) conducting quality audits of the coating Services; and

(c) conducting physical inventory counts.

Persons granted access shall comply with Provider's reasonable site safety and security rules, as communicated to the Company in writing.

**5.2 Risk of Loss; Insurance.** Provider bears the risk of loss of, and damage to, Company Materials while they are in Provider's possession at the Facility or in transit on Provider's carriers. Provider shall maintain, at its expense, property insurance covering Company Materials at the Facility at replacement cost. Provider shall also maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $5,000,000 per occurrence, naming the Company as an additional insured. Provider shall furnish certificates of insurance to the Company on request.

**5.3 Inventory Reconciliation.** Provider shall deliver a monthly report of all Company Materials on hand at the Facility. Within fifteen (15) Business Days after the end of the Term, Provider shall return all remaining Company Materials to the Company's Erie, Pennsylvania or Rochester, New York plant, as the Company directs, at the Company's cost of freight.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY; DATA

**6.1 Confidentiality.** Each Party shall hold in confidence all non-public information of the other Party received in connection with this Agreement, including the Company's product specifications, pricing, customer and project information. Each Party shall use that information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement. This Section 6.1 does not apply to information that falls within any of the following categories:

(a) information that is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement;

(b) information that was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure; or

(c) information that is required to be disclosed by Law, provided the receiving Party gives prompt notice to the disclosing Party where lawful.

**6.2 Company Data.** All data and records relating to the Company or the Business that are generated, stored or processed by Provider in performing the Services are the property of the Company. Provider shall deliver all such data and records to the Company in a commercially reasonable electronic format within thirty (30) days after expiration or termination of the applicable Service. Following delivery, Provider shall delete that data from its systems, except for archival copies retained in accordance with Provider's standard backup procedures, which shall remain subject to Section 6.1.

## 7. TERM AND TERMINATION

**7.1 Term.** This Agreement shall commence on the Closing Date and shall continue until the date that is 9 months after the Closing Date (the "**Term**"), unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Article 7. The Company may extend the Term for any Service for up to two (2) additional periods of thirty (30) days each by written notice to Provider delivered at least thirty (30) days before the scheduled expiration.

**7.2 Termination of Individual Services.** The Company may terminate any Service, in whole or in part, for convenience upon thirty (30) days' written notice to Provider. The Service Fees shall be reduced accordingly. Provider may not terminate any Service for convenience.

**7.3 Termination for Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it in reasonable detail. Provider may terminate for the Company's non-payment only with respect to undisputed amounts that remain unpaid for thirty (30) days after the Company receives written notice of non-payment.

**7.4 Effect of Termination.** Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement or any Service, the Company shall pay all Service Fees accrued through the effective date of termination. The following provisions survive any expiration or termination: Sections 2.5, 4.3, 4.5, 5.3, 6.1, 6.2 and 7.4 and Articles 8 and 9.

**7.5 Transition Assistance.** Before the expiration or termination of each Service, Provider shall cooperate in good faith with the Company and Buyer to migrate that Service to the Company or its designee. This cooperation includes reasonable knowledge transfer regarding coating process parameters and warehouse management procedures used for the Company Materials.

## 8. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY; INDEMNIFICATION

**8.1 Limitation.** Except for a Party's gross negligence, willful misconduct, breach of Article 6 or obligations under Section 8.2, the aggregate liability of each Party under this Agreement shall not exceed the total Service Fees paid and payable under this Agreement. Neither Party shall be liable to the other under this Agreement for any punitive or exemplary damages, except to the extent paid to a third party.

**8.2 Indemnification.** Each Party shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the other Party and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of third-party claims to the extent caused by the indemnifying Party's breach of this Agreement, negligence or willful misconduct in connection with the Services.

**8.3 Relationship to Purchase Agreement.** This Agreement governs only the provision of the Services. It does not limit, expand or otherwise modify any right or obligation of Provider or Buyer under the Purchase Agreement.

## 9. MISCELLANEOUS

**9.1 Force Majeure.** Neither Party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performance, other than a payment obligation, to the extent caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including fire, flood, labor disputes not involving its own employees, or acts of Governmental Authority. The affected Party shall give prompt notice and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event prevents Provider from performing any coating or warehousing Service for more than fifteen (15) consecutive days, the Company may obtain the Service from a third party and terminate that Service without penalty.

**9.2 Independent Contractor.** Provider acts as an independent contractor in performing the Services. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency relationship between the Parties.

**9.3 Notices.** All notices shall be in writing and deemed given when delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses below or another address a Party designates by notice:

If to Provider: Stounaisk Industries, Inc., 2200 Sandusky Pike, Fremont, Ohio, Attention: Chief Executive Officer.

If to the Company: Lumacrest Facade Systems, Inc., c/o Votox Glazing Holdings, Inc., [address], Attention: General Counsel.

**9.4 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party. However, the Company may assign this Agreement without consent in either of the following cases:

(a) to Buyer or any of its Affiliates; or

(b) to any successor to all or substantially all of the Business.

**9.5 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement, including Schedule A, may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties. No waiver shall be effective unless it is in writing and signed by the Party against whom it is asserted.

**9.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**9.7 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the Laws of any other jurisdiction. Each Party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in New York County, New York in any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

**9.8 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION OR PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**9.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
