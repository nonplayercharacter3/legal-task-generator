# ESCROW AGREEMENT

**by and among**

**VOTOX GLAZING HOLDINGS, INC.**, as Buyer,

**STOUNAISK INDUSTRIES, INC.**, as Seller,

and

**PELLUCID TRUST COMPANY, N.A.**, as Escrow Agent

Dated as of [●], 2026

*Form: Exhibit A to Stock Purchase Agreement*

---

This ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [●], 2026, by and among Votox Glazing Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("**Buyer**"), Stounaisk Industries, Inc., an Ohio corporation ("**Seller**"), and Pellucid Trust Company, N.A., a national banking association (the "**Escrow Agent**"). Buyer and Seller are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of [●], 2026 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is acquiring from Seller all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of Lumacrest Facade Systems, Inc., a Pennsylvania corporation (the "**Company**").

B. Section 2.4 of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit the Indemnity Escrow Amount and the Adjustment Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent at the Closing. The Escrow Agent will hold each amount in a separate account and disburse it in accordance with this Agreement.

C. The Escrow Agent is willing to act as escrow agent on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth in this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** The following terms have the meanings set forth below. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Adjustment Escrow Account**" means the segregated account established by the Escrow Agent under Section 3.2(b) to hold the Adjustment Escrow Funds.

"**Adjustment Escrow Funds**" means the Adjustment Escrow Amount of $750,000 deposited with the Escrow Agent under Section 3.1, together with all interest and other earnings on that amount, less any disbursements made under this Agreement.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning given to it in Section 9.4(a) of the Purchase Agreement.

"**Closing Date**" has the meaning given to it in the Purchase Agreement.

"**Escrow Accounts**" means the Indemnity Escrow Account and the Adjustment Escrow Account.

"**Escrow Funds**" means the Indemnity Escrow Funds and the Adjustment Escrow Funds.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order, judgment or decree of a court specified in Section 11.2 that directs the disbursement of all or part of the Escrow Funds. An order is non-appealable when the time for appeal has expired without an appeal being filed, or when every appeal has been finally determined. The Party delivering the order must deliver with it a certificate of its counsel to that effect.

"**Indemnity Escrow Account**" means the segregated account established by the Escrow Agent under Section 3.2(a) to hold the Indemnity Escrow Funds.

"**Indemnity Escrow Funds**" means the Indemnity Escrow Amount of $6,300,000 deposited with the Escrow Agent under Section 3.1, together with all interest and other earnings on that amount, less any disbursements made under this Agreement.

"**Indemnity Release Date**" means the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date. If that date is not a Business Day, the Indemnity Release Date is the next Business Day.

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means written instructions executed by an authorized representative of each of Buyer and Seller, substantially in the form of Exhibit 1, directing the Escrow Agent to disburse all or part of the Escrow Funds.

"**Objection Notice**" has the meaning set forth in Section 5.2(b).

"**Pending Claim Amount**" means, as of any date, the aggregate amount of Losses claimed in all Claim Notices delivered to the Escrow Agent in accordance with Section 5.2(a) that, as of that date, have not been paid from the Indemnity Escrow Funds or released from the Indemnity Escrow Account under this Agreement.

"**Release Date**" means the date that is ninety (90) days after the Closing Date. If that date is not a Business Day, the Release Date is the next Business Day.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to Sections and Exhibits are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for convenience only.

## 2. APPOINTMENT OF ESCROW AGENT

**2.1 Appointment.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent as escrow agent under this Agreement. The Escrow Agent accepts the appointment and agrees to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Funds in accordance with this Agreement.

**2.2 Duties Limited.** The Escrow Agent's duties are limited to those expressly set forth in this Agreement. No implied duties or obligations shall be read into this Agreement against the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent is not a party to, and has no duties or obligations under, the Purchase Agreement or any other agreement between the Parties, whether or not it has knowledge of that agreement. A reference in this Agreement to the Purchase Agreement does not make the Escrow Agent responsible for its terms.

## 3. ESCROW DEPOSIT

**3.1 Deposit.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deposit with the Escrow Agent, by wire transfer of immediately available funds, the following amounts:

(a) $6,300,000, representing the Indemnity Escrow Amount; and

(b) $750,000, representing the Adjustment Escrow Amount.

**3.2 Separate Accounts.** Upon receipt, the Escrow Agent shall credit each amount to its own segregated account, as follows:

(a) the Indemnity Escrow Amount to the Indemnity Escrow Account; and

(b) the Adjustment Escrow Amount to the Adjustment Escrow Account.

The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt of each deposit to Buyer and Seller in writing on the day of receipt. Funds in one Escrow Account shall not be used to satisfy any disbursement payable from the other Escrow Account.

**3.3 No Ownership by Escrow Agent.** The Escrow Funds shall be held in trust for the benefit of Buyer and Seller. They shall not be subject to any lien, attachment, trustee process or other judicial process of any creditor of any party, except as provided by applicable law. The Escrow Agent has no interest in the Escrow Funds other than its rights under Articles 7 and 8.

## 4. INVESTMENT OF ESCROW FUNDS

**4.1 Permitted Investments.** The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Funds in the money market deposit account or fund identified on Schedule 1, unless Buyer and Seller jointly direct otherwise in writing. Any jointly directed investment must be in direct obligations of the United States government, or in money market funds that invest solely in such obligations.

**4.2 Earnings.** Interest and other earnings on each Escrow Account shall be credited to, and form part of, that Escrow Account.

**4.3 Liquidation.** The Escrow Agent may liquidate any investment as needed to make a disbursement required by this Agreement. The Escrow Agent is not liable for any loss resulting from an investment made in accordance with this Article 4, or from any liquidation made to fund a required disbursement.

## 5. DISBURSEMENTS

**5.1 Adjustment Escrow Funds.**

(a) The Escrow Agent shall disburse Adjustment Escrow Funds in accordance with Joint Written Instructions delivered under Section 2.3(c) of the Purchase Agreement.

(b) On the Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Seller all Adjustment Escrow Funds then remaining in the Adjustment Escrow Account, unless Buyer has delivered to the Escrow Agent and Seller, before the Release Date, a written notice stating that amounts remain to be determined under Section 2.3 of the Purchase Agreement. If Buyer delivers such a notice, the Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the remaining Adjustment Escrow Funds and disburse them only in accordance with Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order.

**5.2 Indemnity Claims.**

(a) *Delivery of Claim Notices.* A Buyer Indemnitee seeking payment from the Indemnity Escrow Funds shall deliver a copy of its Claim Notice to the Escrow Agent at the same time it delivers the Claim Notice to Seller. Each Claim Notice must state the amount of Losses claimed, or a good faith estimate of that amount.

(b) *Objection.* Seller may object to all or part of a claim by delivering a written notice to the Escrow Agent and Buyer within thirty (30) days after the Escrow Agent receives the Claim Notice (an "**Objection Notice**"). Each Objection Notice must identify the portion of the claimed amount to which Seller objects.

(c) *Payment of Unobjected Amounts.* The Escrow Agent shall pay to Buyer from the Indemnity Escrow Funds any of the following amounts, within five (5) Business Days after the relevant event:

(i) the full amount claimed in a Claim Notice, if the Escrow Agent has not received an Objection Notice by the end of the thirty (30) day period in Section 5.2(b); or

(ii) any portion of a claimed amount that is not disputed in a timely Objection Notice, after the Escrow Agent receives that Objection Notice.

**5.3 Disputed Amounts.** If Seller delivers a timely Objection Notice, the Escrow Agent shall continue to hold the disputed amount in the Indemnity Escrow Account. The Escrow Agent shall release a disputed amount only upon receipt of one of the following:

(a) Joint Written Instructions; or

(b) a Final Order of a court specified in Section 11.2.

The Escrow Agent shall disburse the disputed amount in accordance with the Joint Written Instructions or the Final Order within five (5) Business Days after receiving it. Buyer and Seller shall deliver Joint Written Instructions promptly after any claim is resolved by agreement.

**5.4 Release on Indemnity Release Date.** On the Indemnity Release Date, the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Seller the amount, if any, by which the Indemnity Escrow Funds then exceed the Pending Claim Amount. The Escrow Agent shall retain an amount equal to the Pending Claim Amount in the Indemnity Escrow Account. It shall disburse that retained amount, claim by claim, only in accordance with Sections 5.2 and 5.3. When any claim is resolved, the Escrow Agent shall release to Seller the portion of the retained amount attributable to that claim that is not paid to Buyer.

**5.5 Joint Written Instructions.** Buyer and Seller may at any time direct a disbursement of all or part of the Escrow Funds by Joint Written Instructions. The Escrow Agent shall act on those instructions within three (3) Business Days after receipt.

**5.6 Method of Payment.** All disbursements shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account designated in writing by the recipient Party. Before any first wire transfer to a new account, the Escrow Agent shall confirm the instructions by telephone call-back to an authorized representative listed on Schedule 2 who is not the person who delivered them. The Escrow Agent may delay a disbursement until it has completed that confirmation.

## 6. RIGHTS AND PROTECTIONS OF ESCROW AGENT

**6.1 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any written notice, instruction, certificate or order that it believes in good faith to be genuine and signed or presented by the proper person. The Escrow Agent has no duty to inquire into the validity, accuracy or content of any such document. It also has no duty to determine whether any claim under the Purchase Agreement is valid or properly asserted.

**6.2 Standard of Care.** The Escrow Agent is not liable for any action taken or omitted in good faith, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud, as finally determined by a court of competent jurisdiction. In no event is the Escrow Agent liable for any special, indirect, consequential or punitive damages.

**6.3 Counsel.** The Escrow Agent may consult counsel of its choice. It is fully protected in acting or refraining from acting in good faith on the advice of that counsel.

**6.4 Conflicting Demands.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting demands or instructions concerning the Escrow Funds, it may refrain from acting until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. Alternatively, it may file an action in interpleader in a court specified in Section 11.2 and deposit the disputed Escrow Funds with that court. Upon deposit, the Escrow Agent is released from all further obligations with respect to the deposited funds.

**6.5 Compliance with Orders.** The Escrow Agent may comply with any writ, order, judgment or decree of a court of competent jurisdiction affecting the Escrow Funds. It is not liable to any party for doing so, even if the order is later reversed, modified or vacated.

## 7. INDEMNIFICATION OF ESCROW AGENT

**7.1 Indemnity.** Buyer and Seller, jointly and severally, shall indemnify and hold harmless the Escrow Agent and its directors, officers, employees and agents from and against all losses, claims, damages, liabilities and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, arising out of or in connection with this Agreement. This indemnity does not extend to losses caused by the gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud of the Escrow Agent, as finally determined by a court of competent jurisdiction.

**7.2 Allocation Between Parties.** As between Buyer and Seller, any amount paid under Section 7.1 shall be borne one-half by Buyer and one-half by Seller. If the amount arises from the act or omission of one Party, that Party shall bear it in full.

**7.3 Survival.** This Article 7 survives the termination of this Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

## 8. FEES AND EXPENSES

**8.1 Fees.** The Escrow Agent shall be entitled to the fees set forth on Schedule 3, together with reimbursement of its reasonable out-of-pocket expenses.

**8.2 Payment.** Consistent with Section 2.4(c) of the Purchase Agreement, the fees and expenses of the Escrow Agent shall be borne one-half by Buyer and one-half by Seller. The Escrow Agent shall not deduct its fees or expenses from the Escrow Funds, except fees and expenses that remain unpaid for sixty (60) days after the Escrow Agent has invoiced the Parties for them.

## 9. RESIGNATION AND REMOVAL

**9.1 Resignation.** The Escrow Agent may resign at any time by giving Buyer and Seller at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice.

**9.2 Removal.** Buyer and Seller may jointly remove the Escrow Agent at any time by giving it at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice.

**9.3 Successor.** Buyer and Seller shall jointly appoint a successor escrow agent before a resignation or removal takes effect. The successor must be a bank or trust company with combined capital and surplus of at least $100,000,000. If no successor has been appointed within thirty (30) days after notice of resignation or removal, the Escrow Agent may petition a court specified in Section 11.2 to appoint one. The resignation or removal takes effect when the successor accepts its appointment in writing and the Escrow Agent delivers the Escrow Funds to the successor.

## 10. TAX MATTERS

**10.1 Tax Reporting.** Buyer is treated as the owner of the Escrow Funds for income tax purposes until they are disbursed. All interest and other earnings on the Escrow Funds shall be reported as income of Buyer. The Escrow Agent shall issue the required tax information returns accordingly.

**10.2 Tax Forms.** Each of Buyer and Seller shall deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9 on or before the Closing Date.

**10.3 Withholding.** The Escrow Agent may withhold from any disbursement any amount it is required to withhold under applicable tax law.

**10.4 Tax Distributions.** Within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar quarter, the Escrow Agent shall pay Buyer from the Escrow Funds an amount equal to forty percent (40%) of the earnings credited to the Escrow Accounts during that quarter.

## 11. MISCELLANEOUS

**11.1 Notices.** All notices and instructions shall be in writing. They are deemed given when delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email of a signed PDF with confirmation of transmission, to the following addresses, or another address a party designates by notice:

If to Buyer: Votox Glazing Holdings, Inc., [address], Attention: General Counsel.

If to Seller: Stounaisk Industries, Inc., 2200 Sandusky Pike, Fremont, Ohio, Attention: Chief Executive Officer.

If to the Escrow Agent: Pellucid Trust Company, N.A., [address], Attention: Corporate Trust and Escrow Services.

Notices to the Escrow Agent are effective only when actually received. Any notice received after 2:00 p.m. Eastern Time is deemed received on the next Business Day.

**11.2 Governing Law; Jurisdiction.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of laws principles that would require the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction. Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware in any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the Escrow Funds. If that court lacks subject matter jurisdiction, each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Superior Court of the State of Delaware or the United States District Court for the District of Delaware. Each party waives any objection to venue in those courts and any claim that those courts are an inconvenient forum.

**11.3 Waiver of Jury Trial.** EACH PARTY IRREVOCABLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY ACTION OR PROCEEDING ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.

**11.4 Termination.** This Agreement terminates when all Escrow Funds have been disbursed in accordance with its terms. Articles 6 and 7 and Section 11.2 survive termination.

**11.5 Assignment.** No party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties. However, any entity into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or to which it transfers all or substantially all of its corporate trust business, becomes the successor Escrow Agent without further action.

**11.6 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the party against whom it is asserted.

**11.7 Patriot Act.** Buyer and Seller acknowledge that the Escrow Agent must obtain, verify and record information that identifies each person establishing an account. Each of Buyer and Seller shall provide the information the Escrow Agent reasonably requests for that purpose.

**11.8 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**11.9 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all counterparts together constitute one instrument.
