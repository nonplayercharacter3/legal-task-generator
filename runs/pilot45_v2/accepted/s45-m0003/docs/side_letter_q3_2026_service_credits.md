**Re: Side Letter – Q3 2026 Platform Availability and Service Credits**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter agreement (this **"Side Letter"**) is a Side Letter under the Master Services Agreement dated as of April 1, 2026 (the **"MSA"**) between **Noubin Auto Exchange, LLC** (**"Noubin"**) and **Trupel Lanecast, Inc.** (**"Trupel"**). It relates to the Platform provided under Trupel Order Form No. 1, the Service Level Schedule and the Statement of Work. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given in the MSA or the Service Level Schedule, as applicable.

## 1. Purpose

**1.1** This Side Letter records the following matters:

(a) Platform availability at the Covered Sites for the Measurement Periods of July, August and September 2026;

(b) the service credits due for those Measurement Periods; and

(c) the status of the notice that Noubin delivered to Trupel about those Measurement Periods.

**1.2** The parties have discussed these matters at their quarterly business review under Section 9.2 of the Statement of Work and in subsequent correspondence between their project managers.

## 2. Q3 2026 Availability and Notice

**2.1 Monthly Availability.** The parties acknowledge that Monthly Availability fell below the Monthly Availability Service Level of 99.5% Platform availability per calendar month in each of the following Measurement Periods, as shown in Trupel's monthly service level reports delivered under Section 7.1 of the Service Level Schedule:

| Measurement Period | Monthly Availability | Principal Cause Reported by Trupel |
|---|---|---|
| July 2026 | 99.1% | Failover delay at primary data center during scheduled sale at New Castle Auction |
| August 2026 | 98.6% | Video streaming and bid transmission outages affecting Elkton Auction and Salisbury Auction |
| September 2026 | 98.9% | Software release defect affecting bid synchronization at all Covered Sites |

**2.2 Excluded Downtime.** Trupel has not claimed any Excluded Downtime for those Measurement Periods, apart from scheduled maintenance it notified under Section 4.1(a) of the Service Level Schedule. That scheduled maintenance has been excluded from the figures in Section 2.1.

**2.3 Noubin Notice.** Noubin delivered to Trupel a written notice of Chronic Failure dated October 19, 2026. The notice was delivered on that date by email, with a copy sent by overnight courier, in accordance with Section 13 of the MSA. In the notice, Noubin gives notice of termination of the Agreement under Section 6.2 of the Service Level Schedule, effective January 15, 2027.

**2.4 Trupel Position.** Trupel acknowledges that it received the notice on October 19, 2026. Trupel disputes the timeliness of the notice under Section 6.2 of the Service Level Schedule and does not accept that the notice is effective to terminate the Agreement.

**2.5 Noubin Position.** Noubin maintains that its notice of Chronic Failure is valid and effective, and that the Agreement will terminate on the date stated in the notice.

**2.6 Continued Performance.** Until the parties resolve the matters in Sections 2.4 and 2.5, or the Agreement terminates, each party shall continue to perform its obligations under the Agreement. Trupel shall continue to provide the Services at all Covered Sites in accordance with the Agreement.

## 3. Q3 2026 Service Credits

**3.1 Credits Due.** Trupel confirms that the following service credits are due under Section 5.1 of the Service Level Schedule, calculated as a percentage of the Monthly Platform Fees for each Measurement Period:

| Measurement Period | Monthly Availability | Service Credit (Section 5.1) |
|---|---|---|
| July 2026 | 99.1% | 5% |
| August 2026 | 98.6% | 10% |
| September 2026 | 98.9% | 10% |

**3.2 Sale-Day Credits.** Trupel confirms that, in addition, sale-day credits are due under Section 5.2 of the Service Level Schedule for the following periods of Unavailability, each lasting more than thirty (30) consecutive minutes during a scheduled sale:

(a) one (1) sale day at New Castle Auction in July 2026;

(b) one (1) sale day at each of Elkton Auction and Salisbury Auction in August 2026; and

(c) one (1) sale day at each Covered Site in September 2026.

**3.3 Maximum.** The total service credits for each Measurement Period are subject to the maximum in Section 5.3 of the Service Level Schedule.

**3.4 Application.** Trupel shall apply the credits described in Sections 3.1 and 3.2 in accordance with Section 4.5 of the MSA and Section 5.4 of the Service Level Schedule. To the extent any of those credits has not already been applied, Trupel shall apply it on its next invoice to Noubin. Each invoice on which a credit is applied shall identify the credit by Measurement Period and Covered Site.

## 4. Remediation

**4.1 Remediation Plan.** By November 13, 2026, Trupel shall deliver to Noubin a written remediation plan for the causes of Unavailability identified in Section 2.1. The plan shall address:

(a) the primary-site failover performance required by Section 8.2 of the Statement of Work;

(b) release testing and rollback procedures for Platform software releases; and

(c) video and bid-transmission monitoring at the Elkton Auction and Salisbury Auction.

**4.2 Root Cause Analyses.** Trupel confirms that it has delivered, or will deliver with the remediation plan, the root cause analyses required by Section 7.3 of the Service Level Schedule for each period of Unavailability longer than sixty (60) minutes in the Measurement Periods covered by this Side Letter.

**4.3 Monitoring Records.** On Noubin's request, Trupel shall give Noubin access to the monitoring records for July, August and September 2026 under Section 7.2 of the Service Level Schedule.

## 5. Reservation of Rights

**5.1** Nothing in this Side Letter waives, releases or limits any right or remedy of either party under the Agreement or applicable law. Each party reserves all of its rights and remedies, including in respect of the matters described in Section 2.

**5.2** Neither party's signature of this Side Letter, nor the application or acceptance of any service credit described in Section 3, is an admission by either party regarding the matters described in Sections 2.4 and 2.5.
