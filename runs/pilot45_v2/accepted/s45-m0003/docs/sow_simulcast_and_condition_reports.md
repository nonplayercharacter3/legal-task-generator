# STATEMENT OF WORK

## Simulcast Bidding Platform and Vehicle Condition Report Services

This **Statement of Work** is entered into as of April 1, 2026 under the Master Services Agreement dated as of April 1, 2026 (the **"MSA"**) between **Noubin Auto Exchange, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (**"Noubin"**), and **Trupel Lanecast, Inc.**, a Georgia corporation (**"Trupel"**).

## 1. Background and Structure

**1.1 Purpose.** This Statement of Work describes the Services Trupel will provide to Noubin at the Covered Sites. Those Services are:

(a) the implementation and operation of the Platform for online simulcast bidding; and

(b) inspector-prepared Condition Reports for vehicles offered at Noubin's auctions.

**1.2 Incorporation.** This Statement of Work is the "Statement of Work" referred to in the MSA. It forms part of the Agreement and is governed by it. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Statement of Work have the meanings given in the MSA or the Order Form.

**1.3 Related Documents.** The Fees for the Platform are set out in Trupel Order Form No. 1. Platform availability standards, service credits and related remedies are set out in the Service Level Schedule.

## 2. Definitions

In this Statement of Work:

- **"Arbitration Period"** means the post-sale arbitration period that applies to a vehicle under Noubin's published auction rules.
- **"Covered Sites"** has the meaning given in the Order Form. The Covered Sites are the New Castle Auction, the Elkton Auction and the Salisbury Auction.
- **"Inspection Standard"** means the vehicle inspection and grading standard described in Section 5.3.
- **"Lane Equipment"** means the cameras, microphones, auctioneer consoles, clerk terminals and related hardware that Trupel installs in the auction lanes at the Covered Sites.
- **"Platform Go-Live Date"** has the meaning given in Section 4.1.
- **"Trupel Inspectors"** means the vehicle inspectors whom Trupel or its subcontractors employ or engage to inspect vehicles and prepare Condition Reports under this Statement of Work.
- **"Vehicle Disclosures"** means all information about a vehicle that is displayed to Buyers on the Platform. This includes vehicle descriptions, photographs, announcements, odometer statements, title status and brand information, frame and structural damage disclosures, and Condition Reports.

## 3. Simulcast Platform Services

**3.1 Platform Functions.** Trupel shall make the Platform available to Noubin and its authorized users at each Covered Site. The Platform shall provide, at a minimum, the following functions:

(a) live video and audio streaming of each auction lane;

(b) real-time remote bidding by registered Buyers, synchronized with the auctioneer's in-lane bid calling;

(c) display of Vehicle Disclosures for each vehicle before and during its run through the lane;

(d) proxy and pre-sale bidding;

(e) Buyer registration, credentialing and credit-limit controls configured to Noubin's requirements; and

(f) post-sale reporting of sale results to Noubin's auction management system.

**3.2 Lane Equipment.** Trupel shall supply, install, maintain and replace all Lane Equipment needed to operate the Platform at the Covered Sites. Lane Equipment remains Trupel's property. Noubin shall provide reasonable space, electrical power and network connection points in each lane.

**3.3 Integration.** Trupel shall integrate the Platform with Noubin's auction management system so that the following information is exchanged automatically:

(a) run lists;

(b) Consignor floor and reserve prices; and

(c) sale results.

Noubin shall give Trupel reasonable access to the documentation and technical contacts for that system.

**3.4 Sale-Day Support.** Trupel shall provide one (1) on-site technician at each Covered Site during each scheduled weekly sale for the first eight (8) weeks after the Platform Go-Live Date. After that, Trupel shall provide remote sale-day support in accordance with the Service Level Schedule.

**3.5 Buyer Support.** Trupel shall operate a Buyer help line during all scheduled sale hours. The help line shall assist remote Buyers with registration, log-in and bidding issues.

## 4. Implementation and Go-Live

**4.1 Platform Go-Live Date.** The Platform Go-Live Date is **June 1, 2026**. On that date the Platform shall go live for simulcast bidding at all three Covered Sites.

**4.2 Implementation Milestones.** Trupel shall complete the following milestones by the dates shown:

| Milestone | Description | Target Date |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Project kickoff; site surveys at all Covered Sites | April 15, 2026 |
| 2 | Lane Equipment installed and network tested at all Covered Sites | May 8, 2026 |
| 3 | Integration with Noubin's auction management system completed and tested | May 15, 2026 |
| 4 | Buyer migration and registration campaign launched; Noubin staff trained | May 22, 2026 |
| 5 | Dry-run sale at each Covered Site completed to Noubin's reasonable satisfaction | May 28, 2026 |
| 6 | Platform Go-Live | June 1, 2026 |

**4.3 Acceptance.** Noubin shall notify Trupel within three (3) business days after each dry-run sale under Milestone 5 whether the dry run is satisfactory. If Noubin identifies material defects, Trupel shall correct them and repeat the dry run before go-live at the affected Covered Site.

**4.4 Delay.** Trupel shall notify Noubin promptly of any anticipated delay in meeting a milestone, with the cause and a recovery plan. Implementation is at no charge to Noubin, as provided in the Order Form.

**4.5 Training.** Before the Platform Go-Live Date, Trupel shall provide training sessions at each Covered Site for Noubin's auctioneers, clerks, arbitration staff and dealer-services personnel.

## 5. Condition Report Services

**5.1 Scope.** For each vehicle that Noubin or a Consignor designates for inspection, Trupel shall cause a Trupel Inspector to inspect the vehicle at the Covered Site and prepare a Condition Report.

**5.2 Turnaround.** Trupel shall complete and publish each Condition Report to the Platform no later than 6:00 p.m. local time on the business day before the sale in which the vehicle is scheduled to run. This applies if the vehicle is made available for inspection at least forty-eight (48) hours before that sale.

**5.3 Inspection Standard.** Each Condition Report shall include, at a minimum:

(a) the vehicle identification number, verified against the vehicle;

(b) the odometer reading as displayed;

(c) a numeric condition grade under Trupel's published grading scale;

(d) at least twenty-four (24) photographs, including the exterior, interior, odometer, tires and undercarriage;

(e) exterior and interior damage notations;

(f) the results of an on-board diagnostic scan;

(g) a tire-tread measurement for each tire; and

(h) observations of frame, structural or flood damage.

**5.4 Inspectors.** Trupel shall ensure that each Trupel Inspector:

(a) has completed Trupel's inspector training program; and

(b) is certified under Trupel's internal quality program.

Trupel shall audit at least two percent (2%) of Condition Reports each month for accuracy. On request, Trupel shall share the audit results with Noubin.

**5.5 Corrections.** If Noubin or a Buyer identifies an error in a Condition Report before the sale, Trupel shall correct and republish the Condition Report before the vehicle runs.

**5.6 Arbitration Support.** Trupel shall cooperate with Noubin's arbitration staff during the Arbitration Period for any vehicle for which Trupel prepared a Condition Report. That cooperation includes providing inspection photographs, scan data and the Trupel Inspector's notes within two (2) business days after Noubin requests them.

**5.7 Use of Condition Reports.** Noubin's rights to use Condition Reports are as set out in Section 6.4 of the MSA.

## 6. Vehicle Disclosures

**6.1 Display.** Trupel shall display Vehicle Disclosures on the Platform as Noubin, Consignors and Trupel Inspectors supply or prepare them. Trupel shall not alter any Vehicle Disclosure supplied by Noubin or a Consignor, except to format it for display.

**6.2 Consignor Content.** For purposes of Section 9.2 of the MSA, "Consignor Content" includes all Vehicle Disclosures displayed on the Platform, including condition reports prepared by Trupel Inspectors.

**6.3 Announcements.** Noubin's auctioneers and clerks may make lane announcements that supplement or amend Vehicle Disclosures. Trupel shall ensure that the Platform displays those announcements to remote Buyers in real time before bidding on the vehicle opens.

**6.4 Records.** Trupel shall retain each Vehicle Disclosure, in the form displayed at the time of sale, for at least the longer of:

(a) twenty-four (24) months after the sale; and

(b) the period any applicable law requires.

## 7. Condition Report Fees

**7.1 Fee.** Noubin shall pay Trupel **$38 per Condition Report** completed and published to the Platform in accordance with Section 5.

**7.2 No Fee.** No fee is payable in any of the following cases:

(a) a Condition Report that is not published within the turnaround time in Section 5.2;

(b) a Condition Report that must be corrected under Section 5.5 because of a Trupel Inspector's error; or

(c) a repeat inspection of the same vehicle for the same sale.

**7.3 Invoicing.** Trupel shall invoice Condition Report fees monthly in arrears with the Platform simulcast fees, in accordance with Section 4.2 of the MSA and the Order Form.

## 8. Hosting, Redundancy and Disaster Recovery

**8.1 Hosting.** Trupel shall host the Platform in at least two (2) geographically separate data centers located in the United States. Those data centers must be operated by Trupel or by hosting providers that hold current SOC 2 Type II reports.

**8.2 Redundancy.** Trupel shall maintain the Platform in an active-passive or active-active configuration across those data centers. The configuration shall allow failover of bidding, video and Vehicle Disclosure functions within fifteen (15) minutes after a primary-site failure.

**8.3 Disaster Recovery.** Trupel shall maintain a written disaster recovery plan for the Platform. The plan shall provide for a recovery point objective of no more than five (5) minutes and a recovery time objective of no more than one (1) hour. Trupel shall test the plan at least annually and shall give Noubin a summary of the results.

**8.4 Network Redundancy.** At each Covered Site, Trupel shall provide a backup cellular connection for the Lane Equipment. The backup connection shall fail over automatically if Noubin's primary internet connection is interrupted.

## 9. Governance

**9.1 Project Managers.** Each party shall appoint a project manager for this Statement of Work and notify the other party of that person's name. The project managers shall meet at least every week until the Platform Go-Live Date, and at least monthly after that.

**9.2 Quarterly Reviews.** The parties shall hold a quarterly business review covering the following matters:

(a) Platform performance;

(b) Condition Report accuracy and audit results;

(c) arbitration trends; and

(d) planned Platform releases.

**9.3 Personnel.** Trupel personnel performing Services at the Covered Sites are subject to Section 2.4 of the MSA.

**9.4 Escalation.** Disputes under this Statement of Work shall first be escalated between the project managers. If they cannot resolve a dispute, it shall be escalated under Section 14.3 of the MSA.
