# TRUPEL ORDER FORM NO. 1

**Order Form No. 1** under the Master Services Agreement dated as of April 1, 2026 between Noubin Auto Exchange, LLC and Trupel Lanecast, Inc. (the **"MSA"**)

## 1. Order Details

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Customer | Noubin Auto Exchange, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (**"Noubin"**) |
| Provider | Trupel Lanecast, Inc., a Georgia corporation (**"Trupel"**) |
| Order Form Number | 1 |
| Order Form Effective Date | April 1, 2026 |
| Customer Billing Contact | Accounts Payable, Noubin Auto Exchange, LLC, Wilmington, Delaware |
| Provider Billing Contact | Finance Department, Trupel Lanecast, Inc., Atlanta, Georgia |

1.1 This Order Form is entered into under, and forms part of, the MSA. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Order Form have the meanings given in the MSA.

1.2 This Order Form takes effect on the Order Form Effective Date of April 1, 2026.

## 2. Services Ordered

2.1 **Platform Subscription.** Trupel shall provide Noubin and its authorized users with access to the Platform for the conduct of online simulcast bidding at Noubin's auction lanes at the following locations (the **"Covered Sites"**):

| Site | Location |
|---|---|
| New Castle Auction | New Castle, Delaware |
| Elkton Auction | Elkton, Maryland |
| Salisbury Auction | Salisbury, Maryland |

2.2 **Condition Report Services.** Trupel shall provide inspector-prepared Condition Reports for vehicles offered at the Covered Sites, as described in the Statement of Work.

2.3 **Implementation.** Trupel shall implement the Platform at the Covered Sites in accordance with the implementation plan and milestones in the Statement of Work. The target Platform Go-Live Date is June 1, 2026.

2.4 **Service Levels.** The Platform is subject to the availability standards, service credits and related remedies in the Service Level Schedule.

## 3. Term

3.1 **Initial Term.** Sixty (60) months from the Order Form Effective Date.

3.2 The Initial Term under Section 12.1 of the MSA begins on April 1, 2026 and continues for sixty (60) months, unless the Agreement is terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.

## 4. Fees

4.1 **Platform Simulcast Fee.** $42 per vehicle sold through simulcast bidding.

4.2 **Fee Basis.** A vehicle is "sold through simulcast bidding" when the winning bid for that vehicle is placed through the Platform by a remote Buyer and the sale is recorded as final in Noubin's auction management system. No Platform simulcast fee is payable for any of the following vehicles:

(a) vehicles sold to a Buyer bidding in person in the lane;

(b) vehicles that do not sell or are sold "if" and not subsequently confirmed; or

(c) sales that are unwound, cancelled or reversed through Noubin's post-sale arbitration process within the applicable arbitration period. For these sales, Trupel shall credit any fee already invoiced on the next invoice.

4.3 **Condition Report Fees.** Fees for Condition Reports are as set out in the Statement of Work.

4.4 **Implementation Fees.** None. Trupel bears all costs of implementing the Platform at the Covered Sites through the Platform Go-Live Date.

4.5 **No Minimum Commitment.** This Order Form contains no minimum monthly or annual volume or fee commitment.

## 5. Invoicing and Payment

5.1 Trupel shall invoice Fees monthly in arrears in accordance with Section 4.2 of the MSA. Each invoice shall be broken down by Covered Site.

5.2 Noubin shall pay invoices in accordance with Section 4.3 of the MSA. Any service credits accrued under the Service Level Schedule shall be applied as provided in Section 4.5 of the MSA.

5.3 Invoices shall be submitted electronically to the Customer Billing Contact identified in Section 1.

## 6. Reporting

6.1 Within ten (10) business days after the end of each calendar month, Trupel shall provide Noubin with a usage report for that month. The report shall show, for each Covered Site:

(a) the number of vehicles offered through the Platform;

(b) the number of vehicles sold through simulcast bidding;

(c) the number of registered remote Buyers who placed at least one bid; and

(d) the number of Condition Reports completed.

6.2 Usage reports are Customer Data.

## 7. Additional Sites

7.1 Noubin may request that Trupel provide the Platform at an additional auction site. Any additional site becomes a Covered Site only under a subsequent order form, or a written amendment to this Order Form, signed by both parties.

## 8. General

8.1 This Order Form may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together form one instrument.
