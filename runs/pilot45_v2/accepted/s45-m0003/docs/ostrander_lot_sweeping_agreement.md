# LOT MAINTENANCE AND SWEEPING SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Lot Maintenance and Sweeping Services Agreement (this **"Agreement"**) is entered into as of January 5, 2026 (the **"Effective Date"**) by and between **Noubin Auto Exchange, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (**"Noubin"**), and **Ostrander Lot Sweeping Co.**, a Delaware corporation (**"Contractor"**). Noubin and Contractor are each a **"party"** and together the **"parties."**

## 1. Services

**1.1 Scope.** Contractor shall provide parking lot, vehicle staging area and drive-lane sweeping and related maintenance services (the **"Services"**) at the following Noubin auction sites (each, a **"Site"**):

| Site | Location |
|---|---|
| New Castle Auction | New Castle, Delaware |
| Elkton Auction | Elkton, Maryland |
| Salisbury Auction | Salisbury, Maryland |

**1.2 Included Services.** The Services consist of:

(a) mechanical sweeping of all paved parking, staging, inspection and lane-approach areas;

(b) removal and lawful disposal of litter, debris, glass and loose materials collected;

(c) emptying of exterior trash receptacles located on paved areas;

(d) spot cleaning of oil and fluid stains using absorbent material, and reporting of any larger spill to Noubin's site manager; and

(e) a visual inspection of paved surfaces during each visit, with a written note to Noubin of any potholes, damaged curbing or failed striping observed.

**1.3 Schedule.** Contractor shall perform the Services at each Site twice weekly. At least one visit shall fall on the day before Noubin's scheduled weekly sale at that Site. All visits shall be completed between 10:00 p.m. and 6:00 a.m. local time, unless Noubin's site manager agrees otherwise in writing.

**1.4 Additional Services.** On Noubin's written request, Contractor shall provide additional sweeping visits, pressure washing or snow-season cinder clean-up at the rates in Exhibit A. Contractor shall not perform any additional service without a written request from Noubin.

**1.5 Standard of Performance.** Contractor shall perform the Services in a good and workmanlike manner, using properly maintained equipment and trained personnel, and in compliance with all applicable laws, including environmental and waste-disposal laws.

## 2. Site Conduct

**2.1 Vehicles on Site.** Contractor acknowledges that consigned vehicles are parked and staged throughout each Site. Contractor shall operate its sweepers and other equipment with due care, keep a safe clearance from parked vehicles and shall not move, enter or touch any consigned vehicle.

**2.2 Site Rules.** Contractor's personnel shall comply with Noubin's posted site safety, security and access rules. Each person shall carry Contractor identification while on a Site.

**2.3 Damage Reporting.** Contractor shall report any damage to a vehicle, building, fixture or other property caused or observed during a visit to Noubin's site manager before leaving the Site.

## 3. Fees and Payment

**3.1 Fees.** Noubin shall pay Contractor the fees in Exhibit A.

**3.2 Invoicing.** Contractor shall invoice monthly in arrears. Each invoice shall list the visits completed at each Site during the month and any additional services performed.

**3.3 Payment.** Noubin shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receiving it. Noubin may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith, provided that it notifies Contractor of the dispute in reasonable detail before the payment due date.

**3.4 Missed Visits.** No fee is payable for a scheduled visit that Contractor does not complete, unless Noubin cancelled the visit.

**3.5 Price Adjustments.** Contractor may increase its fees once in each Renewal Term by written notice delivered at least sixty (60) days before the start of that Renewal Term. No increase may exceed four percent (4%) over the fees then in effect.

## 4. Term and Termination

**4.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for twelve (12) months (the **"Initial Term"**). It then renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month periods (each, a **"Renewal Term"**), unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the end of the then-current term.

**4.2 Termination for Convenience.** Either party may terminate this Agreement for any reason on sixty (60) days' written notice to the other party.

**4.3 Termination for Breach.** Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within fifteen (15) days after receiving written notice describing it.

**4.4 Removal of a Site.** Noubin may remove any Site from this Agreement on thirty (30) days' written notice. The fees for that Site shall then cease to accrue.

**4.5 Effect of Termination.** On termination or expiration, Noubin shall pay for all Services performed through the effective date of termination. Sections 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9 survive.

## 5. Insurance

**5.1 Coverage.** During the term, Contractor shall maintain the following insurance with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best:

(a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate;

(b) commercial automobile liability insurance covering all owned, hired and non-owned vehicles and equipment of at least $1,000,000 combined single limit;

(c) workers' compensation insurance at statutory limits and employer's liability insurance of at least $500,000 per accident; and

(d) umbrella liability insurance of at least $2,000,000.

**5.2 Additional Insured.** Noubin shall be named as an additional insured on the policies in Sections 5.1(a), 5.1(b) and 5.1(d).

**5.3 Certificates.** Contractor shall deliver certificates of insurance evidencing the required coverage before starting the Services and on each policy renewal.

## 6. Indemnification

**6.1 By Contractor.** Contractor shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Noubin and its officers, members, employees and agents against third-party claims for bodily injury, death or damage to tangible property, including consigned vehicles, to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Contractor or its personnel, or by Contractor's breach of this Agreement.

**6.2 By Noubin.** Noubin shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Contractor and its officers, directors, employees and agents against third-party claims for bodily injury, death or damage to tangible property to the extent caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Noubin or its employees.

**6.3 Procedure.** The indemnified party shall notify the indemnifying party promptly of the claim, give it control of the defense and settlement and provide reasonable cooperation at its expense. The indemnifying party shall not settle any claim in a way that imposes an obligation or admission on the indemnified party without that party's prior written consent.

## 7. Limitation of Liability

**7.1 Cap.** Except as provided in Section 7.3, each party's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the greater of (a) the fees paid or payable under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim and (b) $250,000.

**7.2 Consequential Damages.** Except as provided in Section 7.3, neither party is liable for any indirect, incidental, special or consequential damages, or for lost profits.

**7.3 Exclusions.** Sections 7.1 and 7.2 do not apply to a party's obligations under Section 6, to amounts recoverable under the insurance required by Section 5, or to a party's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

## 8. Confidentiality

Contractor shall keep confidential all non-public information about Noubin's business, Sites, security arrangements, consignors and buyers that it learns in performing the Services, and shall use that information only to perform the Services. This obligation survives for two (2) years after this Agreement ends.

## 9. General

**9.1 Independent Contractor.** Contractor is an independent contractor. Contractor is solely responsible for its personnel, including their wages, benefits, taxes and supervision.

**9.2 Subcontracting.** Contractor shall not subcontract any of the Services without Noubin's prior written consent.

**9.3 Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, except that either party may assign it to a successor to all or substantially all of its business on written notice to the other party.

**9.4 Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered personally, by nationally recognized overnight courier or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the addresses on the signature page or to any other address a party designates by notice.

**9.5 Governing Law; Forum.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. Each party submits to the jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

**9.6 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only in a writing signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing.

**9.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**9.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.
