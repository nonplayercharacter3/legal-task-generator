# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this **"MSA"**) is entered into as of April 1, 2026 (the **"Effective Date"**) by and between **Noubin Auto Exchange, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with its principal office in Delaware (**"Noubin"**), and **Trupel Lanecast, Inc.**, a Georgia corporation (**"Trupel"**). Noubin and Trupel are each a **"party"** and together the **"parties."**

## RECITALS

A. Noubin operates wholesale used-vehicle auctions for licensed dealers and commercial consignors.

B. Trupel provides an online simulcast bidding platform and inspector-prepared vehicle condition report services to wholesale vehicle auctions.

C. Noubin wishes to engage Trupel to provide those services on the terms of this MSA and the related Agreement documents described below.

The parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND STRUCTURE

**1.1 Definitions.** In this Agreement, the following terms have the following meanings:

- **"Affiliate"** means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party. "Control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of an entity, or the power to direct its management and policies.
- **"Agreement"** means this MSA together with the Order Form, the Service Level Schedule, the Statement of Work and any Side Letter.
- **"Auction Technology Services"** means any technology-enabled or outsourced service used in or in support of the conduct of a vehicle auction. This includes simulcast and online bidding, vehicle condition reports and inspections, transport scheduling and logistics coordination, title processing and document management, and post-sale arbitration management, together with any similar or related service.
- **"Buyer"** means a dealer or other person registered to bid at a Noubin auction.
- **"Condition Report"** means a written or electronic report describing the condition of a vehicle offered at auction, prepared under the Statement of Work.
- **"Consignor"** means a person that offers a vehicle for sale through a Noubin auction.
- **"Consignor Content"** means vehicle information, descriptions, photographs, announcements, disclosures and other content supplied by or on behalf of Noubin or any Consignor for display on the Platform.
- **"Customer Data"** means all data that Noubin, its Buyers or its Consignors submit to the Platform or that the Services generate in connection with Noubin's auctions. This includes Buyer and dealer lists, Consignor data, bid histories, sale results and pricing data.
- **"Fees"** means the fees set out in the Order Form and any Statement of Work.
- **"Noubin Facility"** means any auction site, lane or facility owned, operated or managed by Noubin or any of its Affiliates, wherever located, whether now existing or later acquired or established.
- **"Order Form"** means Trupel Order Form No. 1 executed by the parties and any subsequent order form that references this MSA and is executed by both parties.
- **"Platform"** means Trupel's proprietary online simulcast bidding platform, including all software, applications, interfaces, hosting and related documentation made available to Noubin.
- **"Service Level Schedule"** means the service level schedule executed by the parties that sets out the availability and performance standards for the Platform and the related service credits and remedies.
- **"Services"** means the Platform and all other services Trupel provides under the Agreement, including the services described in the Statement of Work.
- **"Side Letter"** means any letter agreement that references this MSA and is executed by both parties.
- **"Statement of Work"** means the Statement of Work for simulcast and condition report services executed by the parties and any subsequent statement of work that references this MSA and is executed by both parties.
- **"Term"** has the meaning given in Section 12.2.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections are to Sections of this MSA unless otherwise stated.

**1.3 Order of Precedence.** If there is any conflict or inconsistency among the documents that make up the Agreement, those documents govern in the following descending order of precedence: (a) any Side Letter; (b) the Statement of Work; (c) the Order Form; (d) the Service Level Schedule; and (e) this MSA. Accordingly, if there is a conflict between the Statement of Work and this MSA, the Statement of Work shall prevail.

## 2. SERVICES

**2.1 Provision of Services.** Trupel shall provide the Services to Noubin in accordance with the Agreement. The scope, deliverables and implementation milestones are set out in the Statement of Work.

**2.2 Statements of Work and Order Forms.** Each Statement of Work and Order Form forms part of, and is governed by, the Agreement. A Statement of Work or Order Form binds the parties only once both parties have signed it.

**2.3 Service Levels.** Trupel shall make the Platform available and perform the Services in accordance with the Service Level Schedule. Service credits and the other remedies are as stated in the Service Level Schedule.

**2.4 Personnel.** Trupel shall assign qualified personnel to perform the Services, including properly trained vehicle inspectors for Condition Reports. Trupel may replace personnel on reasonable notice to Noubin. Trupel shall ensure that its personnel comply with Noubin's reasonable site safety, security and conduct rules while present at any Noubin Facility.

**2.5 Subcontractors.** Trupel may use subcontractors to perform the Services. Trupel remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors as if they were its own.

## 3. NOUBIN RESPONSIBILITIES; EXCLUSIVITY

**3.1 Access and Cooperation.** Noubin shall give Trupel reasonable access to the relevant Noubin auction lanes, network connections and personnel as reasonably required for Trupel to perform the Services.

**3.2 Authorized Users.** Noubin is responsible for registering and credentialing its Buyers and personnel as authorized users of the Platform in accordance with Trupel's reasonable onboarding procedures.

**3.3 Acceptable Use.** Noubin shall not, and shall not permit any authorized user to, (a) reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble the Platform, except to the extent applicable law permits; (b) use the Platform in violation of applicable law; or (c) knowingly introduce malicious code into the Platform.

**3.4 Exclusivity.** During the Term, Noubin shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, procure any Auction Technology Services from any provider other than Trupel at any Noubin Facility, being any auction site, lane or facility owned, operated or managed by Noubin or any of its Affiliates. Noubin shall not itself develop or operate any service that substitutes for the Services at any Noubin Facility during the Term.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

**4.1 Fees.** Noubin shall pay the Fees set out in the Order Form and any Statement of Work.

**4.2 Invoicing.** Unless the Order Form states otherwise, Trupel shall invoice Fees monthly in arrears. Each invoice shall itemize the number of vehicles sold through simulcast bidding and the Condition Reports completed during the invoiced month.

**4.3 Payment Terms.** Noubin shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receiving it. Noubin may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith, provided that it notifies Trupel of the dispute in reasonable detail before the payment due date. The parties shall work in good faith to resolve any disputed amount promptly.

**4.4 Taxes.** The Fees exclude applicable sales, use and similar taxes. Noubin shall pay those taxes, other than taxes based on Trupel's net income, property or employees.

**4.5 Service Credits.** Trupel shall apply any service credits that accrue under the Service Level Schedule against the next invoice issued after the credit accrues.

## 5. CUSTOMER DATA

**5.1 Ownership.** As between the parties, Noubin owns all Customer Data.

**5.2 License to Trupel.** Noubin grants Trupel a non-exclusive, non-transferable license, for the Term, to use Customer Data solely to provide the Services to Noubin.

**5.3 Security.** Trupel shall maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards designed to protect Customer Data against unauthorized access, use or disclosure. Those safeguards shall be no less rigorous than accepted industry standards for online vehicle auction platforms. Trupel shall notify Noubin without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to Customer Data.

**5.4 Return of Data.** Within thirty (30) days after this Agreement terminates or expires, Trupel shall make all Customer Data available to Noubin in a commonly used electronic format. Trupel shall then delete Customer Data from its systems, except as applicable law requires it to retain.

## 6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**6.1 Trupel Property.** Trupel retains all right, title and interest in and to the Platform and its pre-existing materials, and in all improvements to them.

**6.2 Noubin Property.** Noubin retains all right, title and interest in and to its pre-existing materials, its marks and the Customer Data.

**6.3 Access Right.** Subject to the Agreement, Trupel grants Noubin and its authorized users a non-exclusive, non-transferable right during the Term to access and use the Platform for Noubin's auction business.

**6.4 Condition Reports.** Once Noubin pays the applicable Fees, Noubin has a perpetual, royalty-free right to use, reproduce and disclose Condition Reports in connection with the sale, arbitration and post-sale handling of the vehicles they describe.

## 7. WARRANTIES

**7.1 Mutual.** Each party warrants that (a) it is duly organized and in good standing; (b) it has full power and authority to enter into and perform the Agreement; and (c) performing the Agreement will not breach any other agreement by which it is bound.

**7.2 Trupel.** Trupel warrants that (a) it will perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with industry standards; (b) the Platform will perform materially in accordance with its documentation; and (c) it will comply with all laws applicable to its performance of the Services.

**7.3 Disclaimer.** Except as expressly set out in the Agreement, neither party makes any warranty, express or implied, including any implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY

**8.1 Definition.** **"Confidential Information"** means any non-public information that one party discloses to the other in connection with the Agreement and that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential. Customer Data is Noubin's Confidential Information.

**8.2 Obligations.** The receiving party shall (a) protect the disclosing party's Confidential Information with at least reasonable care; (b) use it only to perform or exercise rights under the Agreement; and (c) disclose it only to its employees, contractors and advisors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Section 8.

**8.3 Exceptions.** Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes public other than through the receiving party's breach; (b) was lawfully known to the receiving party before disclosure; (c) is independently developed without use of the disclosing party's information; or (d) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction. A party may disclose Confidential Information where law or court order requires it, provided that it gives the disclosing party prompt notice where lawful.

**8.4 Duration.** The obligations in this Section 8 survive for three (3) years after the Agreement terminates or expires. For trade secrets and Customer Data, they survive for as long as the information remains confidential.

## 9. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 By Trupel.** Trupel shall defend Noubin, its Affiliates and their respective officers, members, employees and agents against any third-party claim, and shall indemnify them against resulting losses, damages, settlements, costs and reasonable attorneys' fees. This obligation applies to claims alleging that (a) the Platform or the Services infringe or misappropriate any intellectual property right; or (b) Trupel's breach of Section 5.3 resulted in unauthorized access to Customer Data.

**9.2 By Noubin.** Noubin shall defend Trupel, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents against any third-party claim, and shall indemnify them against resulting losses, damages, settlements, costs and reasonable attorneys' fees. This obligation applies to claims arising out of (a) Consignor Content; or (b) Noubin's use of the Platform in violation of Section 3.3.

**9.3 Procedure.** The indemnified party shall (a) notify the indemnifying party promptly of the claim, although a delay in notice relieves the indemnifying party only to the extent the delay prejudices it; (b) give the indemnifying party sole control of the defense and settlement; and (c) provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying party's expense. The indemnifying party shall not settle any claim in a way that imposes an obligation or admission on the indemnified party without that party's prior written consent.

**9.4 Infringement Remedies.** If the Platform is, or Trupel reasonably believes it is likely to be, the subject of an infringement claim, Trupel may at its expense (a) obtain the right for Noubin to continue using the Platform; or (b) modify the Platform so that it is non-infringing without material loss of functionality.

## 10. INSURANCE

**10.1 Coverage.** During the Term, Trupel shall maintain the following insurance with insurers rated A- or better by A.M. Best: (a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate; (b) technology errors and omissions and cyber liability insurance of at least $5,000,000 per claim; (c) workers' compensation insurance at statutory limits; and (d) commercial automobile liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 combined single limit.

**10.2 Certificates.** On request, Trupel shall provide Noubin with certificates of insurance evidencing the coverage required by Section 10.1.

## 11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**11.1 Cap.** Except as provided in Section 11.3, each party's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to the Agreement shall not exceed the Fees paid or payable in the 12 months preceding the claim.

**11.2 Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** Except as provided in Section 11.3, neither party is liable for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits or lost revenue, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

**11.3 Exclusions.** The limitations in Sections 11.1 and 11.2 shall not apply to either party's obligations under Section 9, to a party's breach of Section 8, or to a party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct.

## 12. TERM AND TERMINATION

**12.1 Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for the initial term stated in the Order Form (the **"Initial Term"**), unless terminated earlier in accordance with the Agreement.

**12.2 Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive renewal terms, each equal in length to the Initial Term (each, a **"Renewal Term"** and, together with the Initial Term, the **"Term"**). This automatic renewal does not apply if either party delivers written notice of non-renewal to the other party not earlier than two hundred ten (210) days and not later than one hundred eighty (180) days before the expiration of the then-current Initial Term or Renewal Term.

**12.3 Termination for Breach.** Either party may terminate the Agreement by written notice if the other party materially breaches the Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it in reasonable detail.

**12.4 Termination for Insolvency.** Either party may terminate the Agreement immediately by written notice in any of the following circumstances: (a) the other party becomes insolvent or makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors; (b) the other party files, or has filed against it, a petition in bankruptcy that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days; or (c) a receiver is appointed over all or substantially all of the other party's assets.

**12.5 Service Level Termination.** Noubin may also terminate the Agreement as provided in the Service Level Schedule.

**12.6 Effect of Termination.** On termination or expiration, (a) Noubin shall pay all undisputed Fees accrued through the effective date of termination; (b) Noubin's right to access the Platform ends, subject to Section 5.4; and (c) Sections 5.4, 6, 8, 9, 11, 12.6, 13, 14 and 15 survive.

**12.7 Transition Assistance.** On Noubin's request, Trupel shall provide reasonable transition assistance for up to ninety (90) days after termination or expiration, at Trupel's then-current professional services rates.

## 13. NOTICES

**13.1 Form.** Notices under the Agreement must be in writing. A notice is effective (a) on delivery, if delivered personally or by nationally recognized overnight courier; or (b) on the date of transmission, if sent by email with confirmation of transmission, provided that a copy is also sent by courier.

**13.2 Addresses.** Notices shall be sent to the addresses set out on the signature page, or to any other address a party designates by notice.

## 14. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

**14.1 Governing Law.** This MSA and any dispute arising out of or relating to it are governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**14.2 Forum.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware, for any action arising out of or relating to this MSA. Each party waives any objection to venue in those courts.

**14.3 Escalation.** Before filing any action, other than an action for injunctive relief, a party shall refer the dispute to the other party's senior executive. The executives shall meet, in person or by video conference, within fifteen (15) days after the referral to attempt in good faith to resolve the dispute.

**14.4 Equitable Relief.** Either party may seek injunctive or other equitable relief to protect its Confidential Information or intellectual property rights.

## 15. ASSIGNMENT

**15.1 By Noubin.** Noubin may not assign or transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it, whether voluntarily, by operation of law, by merger or otherwise, without Trupel's prior written consent. Any attempted assignment in violation of this Section 15.1 is void.

**15.2 By Trupel.** Trupel may assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, without Noubin's consent to any person, upon written notice to Noubin.

**15.3 Binding Effect.** The Agreement binds and benefits the parties and their respective permitted successors and assigns.

## 16. GENERAL

**16.1 Independent Contractors.** The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in the Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship.

**16.2 Force Majeure.** Neither party is liable for any delay or failure in performance, other than a payment obligation, that results from causes beyond its reasonable control. Such causes include acts of God, fire, flood, war, terrorism, civil unrest, labor disputes not involving the affected party's own employees, and failures of public utilities or the internet backbone. The affected party shall notify the other promptly and use reasonable efforts to resume performance. The Service Level Schedule determines whether any such event excuses a service level failure.

**16.3 Amendment and Waiver.** The Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing. No failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of that right.

**16.4 Severability.** If any provision of the Agreement is held unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permissible, and the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**16.5 Publicity.** Neither party may use the other party's name or marks in any publicity without the other party's prior written consent.

**16.6 Counterparts.** This MSA may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all of them together form one instrument.
