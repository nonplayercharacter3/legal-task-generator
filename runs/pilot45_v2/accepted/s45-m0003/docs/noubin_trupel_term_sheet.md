# TERM SHEET

## Simulcast Bidding Platform and Vehicle Condition Report Services

**Noubin Auto Exchange, LLC / Trupel Lanecast, Inc.**

**Date:** February 20, 2026

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms the parties have discussed for a proposed services relationship. Trupel Lanecast, Inc. would supply an online simulcast bidding platform and vehicle condition report services to Noubin Auto Exchange, LLC. The parties intend to set out these terms in definitive agreements, consisting of a master services agreement, an order form, a service level schedule and a statement of work (the **"Definitive Agreements"**).

## 1. Parties

| Role | Party |
|---|---|
| Customer | Noubin Auto Exchange, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (**"Noubin"**) |
| Provider | Trupel Lanecast, Inc., a Georgia corporation (**"Trupel"**) |

## 2. Services

**2.1 Simulcast Platform.** Trupel will provide its proprietary online simulcast bidding platform (the **"Platform"**). The Platform will provide:

(a) live lane video and audio;

(b) real-time remote bidding by registered dealer buyers;

(c) proxy and pre-sale bidding;

(d) buyer registration and credit-limit controls; and

(e) integration with Noubin's auction management system.

**2.2 Condition Reports.** Trupel will provide vehicle condition reports prepared by Trupel's own trained inspectors for vehicles that Noubin or its consignors designate for inspection.

**2.3 Lane Equipment.** Trupel will supply, install and maintain the cameras, consoles and related lane hardware needed to operate the Platform. That equipment will remain Trupel's property.

## 3. Sites

The services will be provided at Noubin's three existing auction sites:

| Site | Location |
|---|---|
| New Castle Auction | New Castle, Delaware |
| Elkton Auction | Elkton, Maryland |
| Salisbury Auction | Salisbury, Maryland |

Any additional site will be added only by mutual written agreement.

## 4. Exclusivity

**4.1 Scope.** During the term, Noubin will use Trupel as its exclusive provider of online simulcast bidding only, at the New Castle, Elkton and Salisbury auction sites.

**4.2 Services Outside Scope.** Exclusivity will not apply to condition reports, inspections, transport scheduling, title processing, arbitration management or any other auction service. Noubin's existing vendor arrangements for those services will continue unaffected.

**4.3 Sites Outside Scope.** Exclusivity will not apply to any auction site other than the three sites listed in Section 3. This includes any site that Noubin or its affiliates acquire or establish after signing.

## 5. Term

**5.1 Initial Term.** Sixty (60) months from the effective date of the order form. The target effective date is April 1, 2026.

**5.2 Renewal.** Renewal mechanics are to be agreed in the Definitive Agreements.

## 6. Implementation

**6.1 Go-Live.** The target Platform go-live date at all three sites is June 1, 2026. Before go-live, Trupel will conduct a dry-run sale at each site.

**6.2 Implementation Cost.** Trupel will bear all implementation costs through go-live. Noubin will pay no implementation fee.

**6.3 On-Site Support.** For an initial period after go-live, Trupel will provide on-site technical support during scheduled sales.

## 7. Fees

**7.1 Platform Fee.** $42 per vehicle sold through simulcast bidding. No fee will apply to:

(a) in-lane sales;

(b) no-sales; or

(c) sales unwound through post-sale arbitration.

**7.2 Condition Report Fee.** A per-report fee is to be set in the statement of work. No fee will apply to late reports or to reports corrected for inspector error.

**7.3 No Minimum.** There will be no minimum volume or fee commitment.

**7.4 Invoicing.** Fees will be invoiced monthly in arrears, itemized by site. Invoices will be payable within thirty (30) days after receipt. Amounts disputed in good faith may be withheld.

## 8. Service Levels

**8.1 Availability.** 99.5% Platform availability per calendar month, measured at each site.

**8.2 Service Credits.** Tiered service credits will apply for missed availability, with an additional credit for extended outages during a scheduled sale. Credits will be applied automatically.

**8.3 Chronic Failure.** Noubin will have a right to terminate for repeated service level failures. The terms of that right are to be set in the service level schedule.

## 9. Data

**9.1 Ownership.** Noubin will own all data submitted to or generated through the Platform in connection with its auctions. This includes dealer buyer lists, consignor data, bid histories and sale results.

**9.2 Use and Security.** Trupel may use that data only to provide the services. Trupel will maintain industry-standard security and will give prompt notice of any breach.

**9.3 Return of Data.** Trupel will return Noubin's data on termination or expiration.

## 10. Liability and Indemnities

**10.1 Liability Cap.** Each party's liability will be capped at the fees paid or payable in the 12 months preceding the claim. Customary exclusions will apply.

**10.2 Trupel Indemnity.** Trupel will indemnify Noubin for intellectual property infringement and for data security breaches caused by Trupel.

**10.3 Noubin Indemnity.** Noubin will indemnify Trupel for claims arising from vehicle content supplied by Noubin or its consignors.

**10.4 Insurance.** Trupel will maintain customary commercial general liability, technology errors and omissions and cyber, workers' compensation and automobile liability insurance.

## 11. Governing Law and Forum

**11.1 Governing Law.** Laws of the State of Delaware.

**11.2 Forum.** State or federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware.

## 12. Next Steps

**12.1 Drafting.** Trupel's counsel will prepare first drafts of the Definitive Agreements for Noubin's review.

**12.2 Target Signing.** The parties target signing by April 1, 2026.

## 13. Non-Binding Effect

**13.1 No Obligation.** This term sheet is an expression of the parties' current intentions only. It does not create any legally binding obligation on either party. No binding obligation will arise unless and until the parties execute the Definitive Agreements.

**13.2 Binding Provisions.** Sections 13 and 14 are binding on the parties.

## 14. Confidentiality and Costs

**14.1 Confidentiality.** Each party will keep the terms of this term sheet and the parties' discussions confidential. Either party may disclose them to its advisors who need to know them.

**14.2 Costs.** Each party will bear its own costs in connection with this term sheet and the Definitive Agreements.
