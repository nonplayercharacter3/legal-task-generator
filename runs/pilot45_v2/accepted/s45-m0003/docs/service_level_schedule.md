# SERVICE LEVEL SCHEDULE

This **Service Level Schedule** forms part of the Master Services Agreement dated as of April 1, 2026 (the **"MSA"**) between **Noubin Auto Exchange, LLC** (**"Noubin"**) and **Trupel Lanecast, Inc.** (**"Trupel"**). It applies to the Platform provided under Trupel Order Form No. 1 and the Statement of Work.

## 1. Scope and Definitions

**1.1 Scope.** This Schedule sets out:

- the availability standard Trupel must meet for the Platform;
- how availability is measured and reported;
- the service credits payable when Trupel does not meet that standard; and
- Noubin's termination right for repeated failures.

**1.2 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Schedule have the meanings given in the MSA. In this Schedule:

- **"Available"** means that authorized users can access the Platform, view vehicle listings, Condition Reports and live lane video and audio, and place bids that Noubin's auction lane receives and records in real time.
- **"Covered Sites"** has the meaning given in the Order Form.
- **"Excluded Downtime"** has the meaning given in Section 4.
- **"Measurement Period"** means each calendar month, beginning with the calendar month in which the Platform Go-Live Date falls.
- **"Monthly Availability"** means the percentage calculated under Section 3.2.
- **"Monthly Platform Fees"** means the Platform simulcast fees invoiced for the Measurement Period under the Order Form.
- **"Platform Go-Live Date"** means June 1, 2026, or any other date on which the Platform first goes live at the Covered Sites under the Statement of Work.
- **"Service Level"** means the Monthly Availability Service Level in Section 2.
- **"Unavailable"** and **"Unavailability"** mean that the Platform is not Available.

## 2. Monthly Availability Service Level

**2.1 Service Level.** Trupel shall ensure that the Platform achieves at least **99.5% Platform availability per calendar month** (the **"Monthly Availability Service Level"**).

**2.2 Application.** The Service Level applies to the Platform at each Covered Site. It applies from the Platform Go-Live Date and continues for the rest of the Term.

**2.3 Auction Days.** Trupel acknowledges the following:

(a) Noubin conducts scheduled weekly sales at the Covered Sites.

(b) Unavailability during a scheduled sale disrupts live bidding and Noubin's Consignors' sales.

(c) Trupel shall staff its network operations center and support desk during all scheduled sale hours that Noubin has notified to Trupel.

## 3. Measurement

**3.1 Monitoring.** Trupel shall monitor Platform availability continuously through automated monitoring tools. The tools must check Platform accessibility and bid transmission at intervals of no more than one (1) minute. Trupel shall keep monitoring records for at least twenty-four (24) months.

**3.2 Calculation.** Monthly Availability is calculated as follows:

> Monthly Availability (%) = ((Total Minutes − Excluded Downtime − Unavailable Minutes) ÷ (Total Minutes − Excluded Downtime)) × 100

In this formula:

- **"Total Minutes"** means the total number of minutes in the Measurement Period.
- **"Unavailable Minutes"** means the total number of minutes in the Measurement Period during which the Platform was Unavailable, other than Excluded Downtime.

**3.3 Start of Unavailability.** A period of Unavailability begins at the earlier of:

(a) the time Trupel's monitoring tools first detect the Unavailability; and

(b) the time Noubin reports the Unavailability to Trupel's support desk.

A period of Unavailability ends when Trupel restores the Platform to Available status.

**3.4 Partial Unavailability.** If the Platform is Unavailable at one or more Covered Sites but not at others, the Unavailable Minutes for the Measurement Period are calculated on a pro rata basis by Covered Site.

## 4. Excluded Downtime

**4.1 Exclusions.** The following periods are **"Excluded Downtime"**:

(a) **Scheduled maintenance.** Maintenance that Trupel carries out on at least five (5) business days' written notice to Noubin. The maintenance must be performed outside scheduled sale hours at the Covered Sites, and must not exceed eight (8) hours in any Measurement Period.

(b) **Noubin causes.** Unavailability caused by Noubin's own network equipment, internet connections at the Covered Sites, or lane hardware not supplied or managed by Trupel.

(c) **Misuse.** Unavailability caused by any authorized user's breach of Section 3.3 of the MSA.

(d) **Force majeure.** Unavailability caused by a force majeure event described in Section 16.2 of the MSA. This exclusion does not apply to any of the following:

- (i) a failure of Trupel's hosting providers, subcontractors or data centers that Trupel could reasonably have prevented through the redundancy and disaster recovery measures required under the Statement of Work;
- (ii) a cyberattack on the Platform; or
- (iii) a labor dispute involving Trupel's own employees or subcontractors.

**4.2 Emergency Maintenance.** Emergency maintenance that Trupel performs without the notice required by Section 4.1(a) is not Excluded Downtime.

## 5. Service Credits

**5.1 Credit Amounts.** If Monthly Availability in any Measurement Period falls below the Service Level, Trupel shall issue Noubin a service credit equal to the following percentage of the Monthly Platform Fees for that Measurement Period:

| Monthly Availability | Service Credit |
|---|---|
| Less than 99.5% but at least 99.0% | 5% |
| Less than 99.0% but at least 98.0% | 10% |
| Less than 98.0% but at least 97.0% | 20% |
| Less than 97.0% | 30% |

**5.2 Sale-Day Credit.** If the Platform is Unavailable for more than thirty (30) consecutive minutes during a scheduled sale at a Covered Site, Trupel shall also issue a credit. The credit equals five percent (5%) of the Monthly Platform Fees attributable to that Covered Site for the Measurement Period, for each affected sale day.

**5.3 Maximum.** The total service credits for any Measurement Period shall not exceed forty percent (40%) of the Monthly Platform Fees for that Measurement Period.

**5.4 Application.** Trupel shall apply service credits automatically, without any need for Noubin to request them, in accordance with Section 4.5 of the MSA. If the Agreement terminates or expires, Trupel shall pay any unapplied credits to Noubin within thirty (30) days.

**5.5 Nature of Credits.** Service credits are a price adjustment reflecting the reduced value of the Services. They are not a penalty. Service credits do not limit Noubin's rights under Section 6 of this Schedule or Section 12.3 of the MSA.

## 6. Chronic Failure

**6.1 Chronic Failure.** A **"Chronic Failure"** occurs if Monthly Availability falls below the Service Level in three (3) consecutive Measurement Periods.

**6.2 Termination for Chronic Failure.** On a Chronic Failure, Noubin may terminate the Agreement by written notice to Trupel. The notice must be delivered within fifteen (15) days after the end of the third consecutive Measurement Period in which Monthly Availability fell below the Service Level. Notice shall be given in accordance with Section 13 of the MSA. Termination takes effect on the date stated in Noubin's notice, which shall be no more than ninety (90) days after the notice is delivered.

**6.3 Effect.** If Noubin terminates under Section 6.2:

(a) Noubin owes no termination fee, early termination charge or other amount, except undisputed Fees accrued through the effective date of termination;

(b) Trupel shall provide transition assistance under Section 12.7 of the MSA at no charge; and

(c) Trupel shall pay or credit all accrued service credits under Section 5.4.

## 7. Reporting

**7.1 Monthly Service Level Report.** Within ten (10) business days after the end of each Measurement Period, Trupel shall deliver to Noubin a service level report. The report shall set out:

(a) Monthly Availability, overall and for each Covered Site;

(b) each period of Unavailability, with its start time, end time, duration, root cause and the Covered Sites affected;

(c) all Excluded Downtime claimed, with supporting detail;

(d) the service credits due; and

(e) any corrective actions taken or planned.

**7.2 Review of Reports.** Noubin may dispute any report by written notice within thirty (30) days after receiving it. Trupel shall promptly give Noubin access to the underlying monitoring records. The parties shall resolve any dispute under Section 14.3 of the MSA.

**7.3 Root Cause Analysis.** For any period of Unavailability longer than sixty (60) minutes, Trupel shall deliver a written root cause analysis and remediation plan within five (5) business days after restoring the Platform.

## 8. Incident Response

**8.1 Support Desk.** Trupel shall maintain a support desk reachable by telephone and email twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven (7) days a week.

**8.2 Response and Restoration Targets.** Trupel shall meet the following targets:

| Severity | Description | Response | Restoration Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Severity 1 | Platform Unavailable at any Covered Site, or bids not being transmitted | 15 minutes | 2 hours |
| Severity 2 | Material function impaired (for example, video, Condition Report display or Buyer registration) | 1 hour | 8 hours |
| Severity 3 | Minor defect with workaround available | 1 business day | Next scheduled release |

**8.3 Communication.** During any Severity 1 incident, Trupel shall provide Noubin with status updates at least every thirty (30) minutes until the Platform is restored.

## 9. General

**9.1 Review.** The parties shall review this Schedule at least annually. Any change to the Service Level, service credits or Section 6 requires a written amendment signed by both parties under Section 16.3 of the MSA.

**9.2 Counterparts.** This Schedule may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature.
