# SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Supply Agreement (the **"Agreement"**) is entered into as of March 2, 2026 (the **"Effective Date"**) by and between **Mubart Baking Company, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (**"Supplier"**), and **Zinix Grocers, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (**"Customer"**). Supplier and Customer are each a **"Party"** and together the **"Parties"**.

## Recitals

A. Supplier is a commercial wholesale bakery that manufactures bread, buns and other baked goods, including products packaged under retailer private-label brands.

B. Customer operates a regional chain of retail supermarkets and wishes to purchase private-label sandwich bread and buns from Supplier for resale in its stores.

C. The Parties wish to set out the terms on which Supplier will manufacture and supply, and Customer will purchase, those products.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **"Affiliate"** means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a Party, where "control" means ownership of more than 50% of the voting interests of the entity.

1.2 **"Code Date"** means the sell-by date printed on the packaging of each unit of Product under the applicable Specifications.

1.3 **"Customer Materials"** means all artwork, logos, trademarks, trade dress, label copy, nutritional and allergen statements, marketing claims and other labeling content that Customer or its designee supplies to Supplier for use on or with the Products.

1.4 **"Delivery Point"** means each Customer distribution center or store location designated in an Order Form or Purchase Order.

1.5 **"Losses"** means all losses, damages, liabilities, judgments, settlements, fines, penalties, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees and costs of investigation.

1.6 **"Order Form"** means a written order form that references this Agreement, is signed by both Parties, and sets out the Products, Specifications, pricing, Delivery Points and other commercial terms for the Products ordered under it.

1.7 **"Products"** means the private-label sandwich bread and bun products identified in an Order Form.

1.8 **"Purchase Order"** means a written or electronic release issued by Customer under an Order Form that states quantities and requested delivery dates for Products.

1.9 **"Stale Product"** means Product unsold after its code date and returned to Supplier within 48 hours after that Code Date.

1.10 **"Specifications"** means the formulation, ingredient, weight, packaging, shelf-life and quality specifications for each Product set out in or attached to the applicable Order Form, as changed under Section 6.3.

1.11 **"Term"** means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Term.

## 2. Term

2.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for three years from the Supply Agreement Effective Date (the **"Initial Term"**), unless terminated earlier under Section 15.

2.2 **Renewal.** After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive one-year periods (each a **"Renewal Term"**) unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 120 days before the end of the then-current term.

2.3 **Order Forms.** Each Order Form remains in effect for the period stated in it. If an Order Form's stated period extends beyond the expiration or termination of this Agreement, the terms of this Agreement continue to apply to that Order Form until its period ends or it is terminated.

## 3. Ordering

3.1 **Order Forms.** The Parties may enter into one or more Order Forms. An Order Form is binding only when signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

3.2 **Forecasts.** By the first business day of each calendar month, Customer shall provide Supplier with a non-binding rolling 13-week forecast of its expected requirements for each Product by Delivery Point.

3.3 **Purchase Orders.** Customer shall issue Purchase Orders at least 72 hours before the requested delivery date. Supplier shall accept each Purchase Order that is consistent with the applicable Order Form and does not exceed 120% of the most recent forecast for the relevant week. Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to fill quantities above that level.

3.4 **Conflicting Terms.** Any pre-printed or standard terms on a Purchase Order, acknowledgment, invoice or similar document are of no effect.

## 4. Pricing and Payment

4.1 **Prices.** Customer shall pay the prices for Products set out in the applicable Order Form.

4.2 **Price Adjustments.** Supplier may adjust prices once in each contract year, on 60 days' written notice, to reflect documented changes in the cost of flour, sweeteners, oils, packaging film and fuel. Any adjustment shall not exceed the documented change in Supplier's cost of those inputs.

4.3 **Invoicing.** Supplier shall invoice Customer weekly for all Products delivered in the preceding week, net of any credits issued under Section 5.4.

4.4 **Payment.** Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days after receipt. Customer shall notify Supplier in writing of any disputed amount, with reasonable detail, within 15 days after receipt of the invoice, and the Parties shall work in good faith to resolve the dispute promptly.

4.5 **Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due accrue interest at the lesser of 1% per month or the highest rate permitted by law.

4.6 **Taxes.** Prices exclude sales, use and similar taxes, which Customer shall pay unless it provides a valid exemption certificate.

## 5. Delivery, Acceptance and Stale Product

5.1 **Delivery.** Supplier shall deliver Products, freight prepaid, to the Delivery Points within the delivery windows stated in the applicable Order Form or Purchase Order, using Supplier's own fleet or carriers it engages.

5.2 **Title and Risk.** Title to and risk of loss of Products pass to Customer on delivery at the Delivery Point.

5.3 **Inspection.** Customer may inspect Products on delivery. Customer may reject any Product that visibly fails to conform to the Specifications or is damaged on delivery by notifying the delivery driver at the time of delivery, or Supplier in writing within 24 hours after delivery. Supplier shall replace or credit rejected Product at Customer's option.

5.4 **Stale Product Credit.** Supplier shall issue Customer a credit equal to the invoiced price of each unit of Stale Product. Supplier shall collect Stale Product from each Delivery Point on its regular delivery route. Credits shall be applied against the next weekly invoice.

5.5 **Shelf Life.** Each unit of Product, when delivered, shall have at least the minimum remaining shelf life before its Code Date stated in the Specifications.

## 6. Specifications and Labeling

6.1 **Manufacture to Specifications.** Supplier shall manufacture all Products in accordance with the Specifications and all applicable laws.

6.2 **Customer Materials.** Customer shall supply all Customer Materials for the Products. Supplier shall reproduce Customer Materials on Product packaging as supplied and approved by Customer. Supplier is responsible for the accuracy of ingredient declarations to the extent they reflect Supplier's formulations, but has no responsibility for the content of any Customer Materials.

6.3 **Changes.** Either Party may propose changes to the Specifications or Customer Materials. No change is effective until agreed in writing by both Parties. Customer shall reimburse Supplier for the cost of packaging rendered obsolete by a change requested by Customer, up to a maximum of 90 days' forecast supply.

6.4 **Packaging Approval.** Customer shall approve or reject each packaging proof within 10 business days after receipt.

## 7. Exclusivity

7.1 **Supplier Exclusivity.** During the Term, Supplier shall not, directly or indirectly, including through any Affiliate, manufacture, sell or supply any bakery products to any other grocery retailer anywhere in the United States.

7.2 **Customer Commitment.** During the Term, Customer shall purchase from Supplier not less than 80% of its requirements for each Product category listed in an Order Form for the Delivery Points listed in that Order Form.

## 8. Quality and Food Safety

8.1 **Food Safety Program.** Supplier shall maintain a food safety program that complies with the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act, the Food Safety Modernization Act, and their implementing regulations, and shall maintain a current certification under a Global Food Safety Initiative benchmarked scheme for each facility that manufactures Products.

8.2 **Audits.** On 10 business days' notice and no more than once per contract year, Customer or its designee may audit Supplier's facilities that manufacture Products during normal business hours. Customer may conduct additional audits for cause.

8.3 **Lot Traceability.** Supplier shall maintain lot traceability records for all Products and their ingredients for at least two years after production.

8.4 **Recalls.** If either Party becomes aware of facts suggesting that any Product may be adulterated, misbranded or otherwise unsafe, it shall notify the other Party within 24 hours. The Parties shall cooperate in good faith in any recall, market withdrawal or stock recovery. Customer may decide whether to conduct a recall of Products from its stores, and Supplier shall bear the costs of any recall as provided in Section 10.1.

## 9. Warranties

9.1 **Mutual.** Each Party warrants that it is duly organized and in good standing, has full authority to enter into this Agreement, and that this Agreement is a valid and binding obligation of that Party.

9.2 **Product Warranty.** Supplier warrants that, when delivered, each Product will (a) conform to the Specifications; (b) not be adulterated or misbranded within the meaning of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act, except to the extent of misbranding caused by Customer Materials; and (c) have been manufactured, packaged and stored in compliance with applicable law.

9.3 **Customer Warranty.** Customer warrants that the Customer Materials, and their use on the Products as directed by Customer, comply with applicable labeling laws and do not infringe or misappropriate any third party's intellectual property rights.

9.4 **Disclaimer.** Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, neither Party makes any warranty, express or implied, including any implied warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

## 10. Indemnification

10.1 **By Supplier.** Supplier shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to the Products, including all costs of any recall, market withdrawal or stock recovery and any claim for personal injury, illness or death, regardless of cause.

10.2 **By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Supplier, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and agents from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to (a) any claim that the Customer Materials, or their use by Supplier as directed by Customer, infringe or misappropriate any third party's trademark, copyright or other intellectual property right; (b) any inaccuracy in or legal non-compliance of the labeling content contained in the Customer Materials; and (c) Customer's storage, handling, display or sale of Product after delivery.

10.3 **Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give the indemnifying Party prompt written notice of any claim, provided that delay in notice relieves the indemnifying Party only to the extent it is prejudiced by the delay. The indemnifying Party shall control the defense and settlement of the claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified Party. The indemnifying Party shall not settle any claim in a manner that imposes any obligation on, or admits fault by, the indemnified Party without the indemnified Party's prior written consent. The indemnified Party may participate in the defense with its own counsel at its own expense and shall provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

11.1 **Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** Except as provided in Section 11.3, neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits or lost revenue, arising out of or relating to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, even if advised of the possibility of those damages.

11.2 **Cap.** Except as provided in Section 11.3, each Party's total liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed the amounts paid or payable under the Agreement in the 12 months preceding the claim.

11.3 **Exclusions.** The limitations in Sections 11.1 and 11.2 do not apply to:

(a) each party's indemnification obligations under Section 10;

(b) a Party's breach of Section 13 (Confidentiality);

(c) a Party's gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud; or

(d) Customer's obligation to pay for Products delivered.

## 12. Insurance

12.1 **Supplier.** During the Term and for two years after, Supplier shall maintain (a) commercial general liability insurance, including products and completed operations coverage, with limits of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate; (b) product contamination and recall insurance with limits of at least $2,000,000; (c) commercial auto liability insurance with limits of at least $2,000,000 combined single limit; and (d) workers' compensation insurance as required by law.

12.2 **Customer.** During the Term, Customer shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits of at least $5,000,000 per occurrence and in the aggregate.

12.3 **Certificates.** Each Party shall name the other as an additional insured on its commercial general liability policy and shall provide certificates of insurance on request.

## 13. Confidentiality

13.1 **Obligations.** Each Party shall keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other Party in connection with this Agreement, including formulations, Specifications, pricing, forecasts and the terms of this Agreement and any Order Form (**"Confidential Information"**). It shall use that information only to perform this Agreement and disclose it only to its and its Affiliates' employees, advisors and contractors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 13.

13.2 **Exceptions.** Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes public without breach of this Agreement; (b) was known to the receiving Party without restriction before disclosure; (c) is received from a third party without restriction; or (d) is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's information. A Party may disclose Confidential Information when required by law or court order, after giving the other Party prompt notice where legally permitted.

13.3 **Duration.** The obligations in this Section 13 continue for three years after the end of the Term, and for Supplier's formulations and other trade secrets, for as long as they remain trade secrets.

## 14. Intellectual Property

14.1 **Customer Marks.** Customer grants Supplier a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free license during the Term to use the Customer Materials solely to manufacture, package and deliver Products to Customer. Supplier shall not use the Customer Materials for any other purpose.

14.2 **Supplier Formulations.** Supplier retains all rights in its recipes, formulations, processes and know-how, whether or not used in the Products. Nothing in this Agreement grants Customer any license to them.

14.3 **Return of Materials.** On expiration or termination, Supplier shall cease using the Customer Materials, except to fill outstanding Purchase Orders, and shall, at Customer's election, deliver to Customer or destroy any remaining packaging bearing the Customer Materials, at Customer's cost.

## 15. Termination

15.1 **For Breach.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any Order Form on written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement or that Order Form and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after receiving written notice describing it (or 10 days for non-payment).

15.2 **Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any voluntary or involuntary bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within 60 days.

15.3 **Effect.** On expiration or termination, Customer shall pay for all Products delivered and all Products manufactured under accepted Purchase Orders, and Customer shall purchase at Supplier's cost any packaging bearing the Customer Materials held by Supplier, up to 90 days' forecast supply.

15.4 **Survival.** Sections 4, 5.4, 9, 10, 11, 13, 14.3, 15.3, 15.4 and 18 survive expiration or termination.

## 16. Force Majeure

16.1 Neither Party is liable for any failure or delay in performance (other than payment obligations) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, fire, flood, epidemic, war, terrorism, government action, labor disputes not involving the affected Party's own employees, or failure of utilities or public carriers. The affected Party shall promptly notify the other Party and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. If a force majeure event continues for more than 30 days, the unaffected Party may obtain Products from other sources during the period of the event without breach of Section 7.2, and may terminate the affected Order Form if the event continues for more than 90 days.

## 17. Notices

17.1 Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation copy sent by courier, to the addresses below or another address a Party designates by notice. Notices are effective on receipt.

## 18. General

18.1 **Independent Contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship.

18.2 **Governing Law and Forum.** This Agreement and any dispute arising out of or relating to it are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws rules. Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in those courts. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods does not apply.

18.3 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. Either Party may, however, assign this Agreement without consent to an Affiliate or to a successor in a merger or a sale of all or substantially all of its assets or business to which this Agreement relates, on written notice to the other Party.

18.4 **Order of Precedence.** If there is any conflict among the documents forming the Parties' arrangement, they prevail in the following order: (a) any amendment to this Agreement; (b) the applicable Order Form, as to the Products ordered under it; and (c) this Supply Agreement. The non-binding term sheet exchanged by the Parties before the Effective Date has no effect.

18.5 **Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing, and no failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver of it.

18.6 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, it shall be enforced to the maximum extent permitted, and the remaining provisions remain in full force.

18.7 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.
