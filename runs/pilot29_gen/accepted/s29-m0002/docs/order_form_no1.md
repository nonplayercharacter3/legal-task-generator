# ORDER FORM NO. 1

**Order Form Date:** April 6, 2026

**Order Form Number:** 1

**Agreement:** Supply Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 (the **"Agreement"**) between **Mubart Baking Company, LLC** (**"Supplier"**) and **Zinix Grocers, Inc.** (**"Customer"**)

This Order Form is entered into under, and forms part of, the Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Order Form have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

## 1. Order Form Period

1.1 This Order Form takes effect on the Order Form Date. It remains in effect until the end of the Initial Term of the Agreement, unless terminated earlier under Section 15 of the Agreement.

1.2 Supplier shall make first deliveries under this Order Form in the week beginning May 4, 2026, or on another date the Parties agree in writing.

## 2. Products and Pricing

2.1 **Products.** The Products under this Order Form are the following private-label sandwich bread and bun products, to be packaged under Customer's private-label brand:

| Item No. | Product | Unit Size | Units per Tray | Price per Unit (USD) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ZG-101 | White Sandwich Loaf | 20 oz | 12 | $1.42 |
| ZG-102 | Wheat Sandwich Loaf | 20 oz | 12 | $1.51 |
| ZG-103 | Multigrain Sandwich Loaf | 24 oz | 10 | $1.78 |
| ZG-201 | Hamburger Buns, 8-count | 12 oz | 12 | $1.29 |
| ZG-202 | Hot Dog Buns, 8-count | 11 oz | 12 | $1.24 |

2.2 **Prices.** The prices in Section 2.1 are delivered prices, freight prepaid to the Delivery Points. They are subject to adjustment only under Section 4.2 of the Agreement.

2.3 **Specifications.** The Specifications for each Product are set out in Schedule A to this Order Form. Each Product shall have at least five days of remaining shelf life before its Code Date when delivered.

2.4 **Product Categories.** For the purposes of Section 7.2 of the Agreement, the Product categories under this Order Form are (a) sandwich loaves (Items ZG-101 to ZG-103) and (b) buns (Items ZG-201 and ZG-202).

## 3. Stale Product

3.1 For the Products ordered under this Order Form, **"Stale Product"** means any Product that is unsold at its Code Date or is damaged or unsaleable in store.

3.2 Supplier shall issue Customer a credit equal to the full invoiced price of each unit of Stale Product, without return. Customer shall report Stale Product quantities by Delivery Point to Supplier weekly through Customer's store reporting system, and credits shall be applied against the next weekly invoice under Section 4.3 of the Agreement.

## 4. Delivery Points and Delivery Windows

4.1 **Delivery Points.** Supplier shall deliver Products to the following Delivery Points:

| Delivery Point | Location | Delivery Days | Delivery Window |
|---|---|---|---|
| DC-1 | Customer Distribution Center, New Castle, Delaware | Monday to Saturday | 4:00 a.m. to 8:00 a.m. |
| DC-2 | Customer Distribution Center, Hagerstown, Maryland | Monday to Saturday | 4:00 a.m. to 8:00 a.m. |
| DC-3 | Customer Distribution Center, Lancaster, Pennsylvania | Monday to Saturday | 5:00 a.m. to 9:00 a.m. |

4.2 **Store Deliveries.** Customer may designate individual store locations served from a listed distribution center as additional Delivery Points on 30 days' written notice, if those stores are on or reasonably near Supplier's existing delivery routes. Delivery windows for those stores will be agreed in writing.

4.3 **Purchase Orders.** Customer shall issue Purchase Orders under Section 3.3 of the Agreement through Customer's electronic data interchange system. Each Purchase Order shall state the Item No., quantity in trays, Delivery Point and requested delivery date.

## 5. Customer Materials and Packaging

5.1 Customer shall deliver final approved Customer Materials for all Products to Supplier no later than April 13, 2026.

5.2 Packaging proofs are subject to approval under Section 6.4 of the Agreement. Supplier's packaging lead time is 21 days from final proof approval.

## 6. Governing Law and Forum

Disputes arising under this Order Form or relating to the Products ordered under it are governed by the laws of the State of New Jersey, without regard to its conflict of laws rules. Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state or federal courts located in Bergen County, New Jersey for any such dispute and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

## 7. Contacts
