# PRIVATE-LABEL BREAD AND BUN SUPPLY: SUMMARY OF PRINCIPAL TERMS

**Non-Binding Term Sheet**

**Date:** January 19, 2026

**Parties:** Mubart Baking Company, LLC (**"Mubart"** or **"Supplier"**) and Zinix Grocers, Inc. (**"Zinix"** or **"Customer"**)

This term sheet summarizes the principal terms the parties have discussed for a proposed supply arrangement. It is intended as a basis for preparing definitive documents. Except as stated in Section 12, it does not create any legally binding obligation.

## 1. Transaction

Mubart will manufacture and supply private-label sandwich bread and bun products to Zinix for resale in Zinix supermarkets under Zinix's private-label brands. The arrangement will be documented in a master supply agreement (the **"Supply Agreement"**). It will be supplemented by one or more order forms (each an **"Order Form"**) setting out products, specifications, pricing and delivery points.

## 2. Products

2.1 Initial products are white, wheat and multigrain sandwich loaves, and hamburger and hot dog buns, in the sizes and pack counts to be set out in Order Form No. 1.

2.2 Products will be manufactured to specifications agreed in each Order Form. Mubart will retain all rights in its recipes, formulations and processes.

## 3. Term

3.1 The initial term will be three years from the effective date of the Supply Agreement. After that, it will renew automatically for one-year periods unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least 120 days before the end of the then-current term.

3.2 The target effective date is on or about March 1, 2026. The first deliveries under Order Form No. 1 will follow in the second quarter of 2026.

## 4. Exclusivity

4.1 **Supplier exclusivity.** During the term, Mubart will not supply private-label sandwich bread and buns in Delaware, Maryland and Pennsylvania to any other grocery retailer.

4.2 **Customer commitment.** During the term, Zinix will purchase from Mubart not less than 80% of its requirements for each product category listed in an Order Form, for the delivery points listed in that Order Form.

4.3 Mubart's branded products, foodservice business and other wholesale customers are outside the scope of Section 4.1.

## 5. Pricing and Payment

5.1 Pricing will be set out in each Order Form. Mubart may adjust prices once per contract year, on 60 days' notice, to reflect documented changes in the cost of flour, sweeteners, oils, packaging film and fuel.

5.2 Mubart will invoice weekly. Payment terms are net 30 days from receipt of invoice. Disputed amounts must be notified within 15 days after receipt of the invoice.

## 6. Ordering and Delivery

6.1 Zinix will provide a non-binding rolling 13-week forecast monthly. Purchase orders will be issued at least 72 hours before the requested delivery date. Mubart will accept orders of up to 120% of the most recent forecast for the relevant week.

6.2 Mubart will deliver freight prepaid, using its own fleet or carriers it engages, to the Zinix distribution centers and store locations designated in each Order Form. Title and risk pass on delivery.

## 7. Stale Product

7.1 "Stale Product" will mean Product unsold after its code date and returned to Supplier within 48 hours.

7.2 Mubart will credit Zinix at the invoiced price for each unit of Stale Product. Mubart will collect Stale Product on its regular delivery routes, and credits will be applied against the next weekly invoice.

7.3 Product damaged on delivery or not conforming to specifications may be rejected at delivery, or by notice within 24 hours after delivery, for replacement or credit.

## 8. Labeling and Customer Materials

8.1 Zinix will supply all private-label artwork, trademarks, label copy and other labeling content. Mubart will reproduce that content as supplied and approved by Zinix.

8.2 Mubart will be responsible for the accuracy of ingredient declarations to the extent they reflect its formulations. Zinix will be responsible for all other labeling content and for its trademarks.

## 9. Quality and Food Safety

9.1 Mubart will maintain a food safety program compliant with FDCA and FSMA requirements and a current GFSI-benchmarked certification for each producing facility.

9.2 Zinix may conduct one audit per contract year on 10 business days' notice, plus audits for cause. The parties will notify each other within 24 hours of any potential food safety issue and will cooperate on any recall.

## 10. Indemnity and Liability

10.1 Mubart will indemnify Zinix for third-party claims arising from Products that fail to meet the product warranties, including product liability claims and recall costs caused by those failures.

10.2 Zinix will indemnify Mubart for claims arising from Zinix-supplied artwork, labeling content and trademarks, and from Zinix's storage, handling, display or sale of Product after delivery.

10.3 Neither party will be liable for consequential damages. Each party's liability will be capped at amounts paid or payable in the 12 months preceding the claim. Each party's indemnification obligations, breach of confidentiality, gross negligence, willful misconduct, fraud and Zinix's payment obligations will be excluded from the cap.

10.4 Mubart will carry commercial general liability insurance (including products coverage) of at least $5,000,000, product contamination and recall coverage of at least $2,000,000, and auto liability coverage of at least $2,000,000. Zinix will carry commercial general liability insurance of at least $5,000,000.

## 11. Other Terms

11.1 **Governing law and forum.** Delaware law; state or federal courts in New Castle County, Delaware.

11.2 **Confidentiality.** Customary mutual confidentiality, continuing for three years after the term, and for as long as trade secret status lasts for Mubart's formulations.

11.3 **Termination.** Termination for uncured material breach on 30 days' notice (10 days for non-payment) and for insolvency.

## 12. Non-Binding Effect

This term sheet is not a binding agreement and creates no obligation on either party to negotiate or enter into any definitive agreement. No binding obligation will arise until definitive documents are signed by both parties. The parties are bound only by this Section 12 and by their obligation to keep the terms of this term sheet confidential.
