# AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO SUPPLY AGREEMENT

*Zinix Grocers, Inc. Draft: June 15, 2026. For Discussion Purposes Only.*

This Amendment No. 1 to Supply Agreement (this **"Amendment"**) is dated as of June 15, 2026 (the **"Amendment Date"**). It is made by and between **Mubart Baking Company, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (**"Supplier"**), and **Zinix Grocers, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (**"Customer"**). Supplier and Customer are each a **"Party"** and together the **"Parties"**.

## Recitals

A. Supplier and Customer are parties to a Supply Agreement dated as of March 2, 2026 (the **"Agreement"**). Under the Agreement, Supplier manufactures and supplies private-label sandwich bread and bun products to Customer.

B. The Parties entered into Order Form No. 1 under the Agreement, dated April 6, 2026.

C. Under Section 18.5 of the Agreement, the Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

D. The Parties wish to amend the Agreement as set out in this Amendment.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Amendment have the meanings given to them in the Agreement.

1.2 References in this Amendment to Sections are to Sections of the Agreement unless otherwise stated.

## 2. Amendment to Section 11.3 (Exclusions)

Section 11.3 of the Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

> **11.3 Exclusions.** The limitations in Sections 11.1 and 11.2 do not apply to:
>
> (a) Supplier's indemnification obligations under Section 10.1;
>
> (b) a Party's breach of Section 13 (Confidentiality);
>
> (c) a Party's gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud; or
>
> (d) Customer's obligation to pay for Products delivered.

## 3. Amendment to Section 17 (Notices)

The notice details for Customer in Section 17.1 of the Agreement are deleted and replaced with the following:

> **Customer:** Zinix Grocers, Inc., Attention: Vice President, Private Brands, with a copy to the Legal Department, Attention: Associate General Counsel, Commercial Contracts.

## 4. Effect of Amendment

4.1 This Amendment takes effect on the date it has been signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

4.2 Except as expressly amended by this Amendment, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect. From the effective date of this Amendment, each reference in the Agreement to "this Agreement" means the Agreement as amended by this Amendment.

4.3 This Amendment does not amend Order Form No. 1 or any other Order Form.

## 5. General

5.1 **Governing Law and Forum.** Section 18.2 of the Agreement applies to this Amendment as if set out in full in it.

5.2 **Counterparts.** This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is an original, and all counterparts together are one instrument.
