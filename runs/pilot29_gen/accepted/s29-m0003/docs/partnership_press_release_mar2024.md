# FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

**Poubis Provisions and Gibeis Cold Logistics Announce Multi-Year Cold Chain Partnership**

*Agreement makes Gibeis the exclusive temperature-controlled logistics provider for Poubis's Mid-Atlantic distribution network*

**HANOVER, Md. and BRIDGEPORT, N.J., March 5, 2024.** Poubis Provisions, Inc., a grocery and foodservice distributor serving the Mid-Atlantic region, and Gibeis Cold Logistics, LLC, a provider of refrigerated warehousing, cross-docking and reefer transportation, today announced a strategic partnership.

## 1. The Partnership

Poubis and Gibeis have entered into a Master Services Agreement effective March 1, 2024, with an initial term of three years. Under the agreement, Gibeis will provide refrigerated and frozen warehousing, cross-docking and reefer transportation for Poubis's chilled and frozen food products. These include dairy, fresh produce, meat and frozen goods distributed to Poubis's grocery and foodservice customers.

## 2. Exclusivity

Under the agreement, Gibeis will serve as the exclusive provider of temperature-controlled logistics services for Poubis's Mid-Atlantic distribution network. Gibeis will handle storage, cross-docking and reefer transport for shipments moving through that network, which serves retail grocery and foodservice accounts across the region.

## 3. Service Commitments

Gibeis will store, handle and transport all Poubis product within the temperature ranges set for each product category. It will continuously monitor and record temperatures in its facilities and trailers, and notify Poubis promptly if any temperature excursion occurs. Gibeis facilities serving Poubis maintain third-party food safety certification under a Global Food Safety Initiative benchmarked scheme. Gibeis also operates in compliance with the FDA Sanitary Transportation of Human and Animal Food rule.

## 4. Statements from the Companies

"Our customers count on us to deliver chilled and frozen products that are safe, fresh and on time," said Margaret L. Haverford, Chief Executive Officer of Poubis Provisions. "Gibeis brings the facilities, fleet and temperature-monitoring discipline we need to keep growing across the Mid-Atlantic."

"We are proud to support Poubis's distribution network," said Daniel R. Keswick, President of Gibeis Cold Logistics. "This partnership reflects our commitment to cold chain integrity from dock to delivery, and we look forward to working with the Poubis team for years to come."

## 5. About Poubis Provisions, Inc.

Poubis Provisions, Inc. is a grocery and foodservice distributor headquartered in Hanover, Maryland. Poubis purchases, stores and distributes chilled and frozen food products to independent grocers, regional supermarket chains and foodservice operators throughout the Mid-Atlantic region.

## 6. About Gibeis Cold Logistics, LLC

Gibeis Cold Logistics, LLC, headquartered in Bridgeport, New Jersey, operates refrigerated warehouses, cross-docking facilities and a fleet of refrigerated trailers. Gibeis provides temperature-controlled logistics services to food manufacturers, distributors and retailers. Gibeis Cold Logistics is a subsidiary of Gibeis Holdings Corp.
