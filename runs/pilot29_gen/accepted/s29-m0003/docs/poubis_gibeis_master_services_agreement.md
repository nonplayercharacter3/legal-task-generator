# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "**Agreement**") is entered into and effective as of March 1, 2024 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between **Poubis Provisions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**"), and **Gibeis Cold Logistics, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Provider**"). Customer and Provider are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**."

## RECITALS

A. Customer is a grocery and foodservice distributor that purchases, stores and distributes chilled and frozen food products.

B. Provider operates refrigerated warehouses, cross-docking facilities and a fleet of refrigerated ("reefer") trailers, and provides temperature-controlled logistics services.

C. Customer wishes to engage Provider, and Provider wishes to be engaged, to provide temperature-controlled logistics services on the terms of this Agreement.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**1.1 Definitions.** In this Agreement:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to a Party, any entity that now or hereafter directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Party. "Control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting equity of an entity, or the power to direct the management and policies of an entity, whether by contract or otherwise.

"**Applicable Law**" means all federal, state and local laws, regulations and orders applicable to a Party or the Services. This includes the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act, the Food Safety Modernization Act and the FDA Sanitary Transportation of Human and Animal Food rule (21 C.F.R. Part 1, Subpart O).

"**Cargo**" means all goods tendered to Provider, both chilled (0–4°C) and frozen.

"**Cargo Loss**" means any loss of, damage to, contamination of or spoilage of Cargo occurring while the Cargo is in Provider's care, custody or control. Cargo Loss includes spoilage resulting from any Temperature Excursion.

"**Change of Control**" means, with respect to Customer: (a) any transaction or series of related transactions by which any person or group acquires, directly or indirectly, Control of Customer, whether by merger, consolidation, stock purchase or otherwise; or (b) a sale of all or substantially all of Customer's assets.

"**Confidential Information**" has the meaning given in Section 9.1.

"**Early Termination Fee**" has the meaning given in Section 14.4.

"**Facility**" means each warehouse, cold storage or cross-dock facility operated by Provider and used to perform the Services.

"**Fees**" means the fees for the Services set out in each Statement of Work, together with the Fuel Surcharge.

"**Fuel Surcharge**" has the meaning given in Section 5.3.

"**Services**" means the refrigerated warehousing, cross-docking, reefer transportation and related value-added services described in this Agreement and in each Statement of Work.

"**Side Letter**" means any letter agreement signed by authorized representatives of both Parties that expressly supplements or modifies this Agreement.

"**Statement of Work**" or "**SOW**" means a statement of work describing specific Services, locations, lanes, volumes and Fees, signed by both Parties and referencing this Agreement.

"**Temperature Excursion**" means any period during which the temperature of Cargo, or of the storage area or trailer in which Cargo is held, falls outside the temperature range specified for that Cargo in the applicable Statement of Work or Customer's written handling instructions.

"**Term**" means the Initial Term together with any Renewal Terms.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections are to sections of this Agreement unless otherwise stated.

## 2. TERM AND RENEWAL

**2.1 Initial Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date, March 1, 2024. It continues for three (3) years from the Effective Date, ending February 28, 2027 (the "**Initial Term**"), unless terminated earlier under Section 14.

**2.2 Renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term, this Agreement will renew automatically for successive three (3) year renewal terms (each a "**Renewal Term**"). Automatic renewal will not occur if either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal no earlier than one hundred twenty (120) days and no later than ninety (90) days before the expiration of the then-current term. A notice of non-renewal given outside that period has no effect.

**2.3 Statements of Work.** Each Statement of Work stays in effect for the period stated in it. No Statement of Work may extend beyond the expiration or termination of this Agreement, except as provided in Section 14.5.

## 3. SERVICES; EXCLUSIVITY

**3.1 Exclusivity.** During the Term, Customer shall purchase exclusively from Provider all temperature-controlled logistics services, including refrigerated and frozen warehousing, cross-docking and reefer transportation, required by Customer or any Affiliate of Customer anywhere in North America. Customer shall cause its Affiliates to comply with this Section 3.1.

**3.2 Performance of Services.** Provider shall perform the Services in a diligent, workmanlike manner in accordance with this Agreement, each applicable Statement of Work, Customer's reasonable written handling instructions and Applicable Law.

**3.3 Capacity.** Provider shall maintain sufficient Facility capacity, equipment and qualified personnel to handle the volumes forecast in each Statement of Work. Customer shall provide Provider with a rolling ninety (90) day non-binding volume forecast, updated monthly.

**3.4 Excess Volume.** If Customer's actual volumes exceed the forecast by more than fifteen percent (15%) in any calendar month, Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to accommodate the excess. Any additional charges for excess volume must be agreed in writing in advance.

## 4. STATEMENTS OF WORK

**4.1 Execution.** The Parties may enter into one or more Statements of Work from time to time. Each Statement of Work forms part of, and is governed by, this Agreement.

**4.2 Content.** Each Statement of Work shall specify, as applicable: the Facilities and lanes covered; Cargo categories and the temperature range for each; service levels; key performance indicators; Fees; and any special handling requirements.

**4.3 Changes.** Changes to a Statement of Work are effective only if set out in a written change order signed by both Parties.

## 5. FEES AND PAYMENT

**5.1 Fees.** Customer shall pay Provider the Fees set out in each Statement of Work. Fees do not include applicable sales, use or similar taxes, which Customer shall pay. Provider is responsible for taxes on its own income.

**5.2 Invoicing and Payment.** Provider shall invoice Customer monthly in arrears. Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) days after receipt. Customer may withhold amounts disputed in good faith, provided Customer notifies Provider in writing of the disputed amount and the basis of the dispute before the payment due date. The Parties shall work diligently to resolve any dispute within thirty (30) days.

**5.3 Fuel Surcharge.** Transportation Fees are subject to a fuel surcharge (the "**Fuel Surcharge**"). The Fuel Surcharge is calculated weekly by reference to the U.S. Energy Information Administration's Weekly Retail On-Highway Diesel Price for the Central Atlantic (PADD 1B) region (the "**Index**"). For each full $0.05 per gallon by which the Index exceeds a base price of $3.75 per gallon, the Fuel Surcharge equals one-half percent (0.5%) of the line-haul transportation Fees for that week. No Fuel Surcharge applies when the Index is at or below the base price.

**5.4 Annual Rate Adjustment.** On each anniversary of the Effective Date, Provider may increase warehousing and handling Fees by no more than the lesser of (a) three percent (3%) and (b) the percentage increase in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) for the preceding twelve (12) months. Provider shall give at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice of any increase.

**5.5 Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid when due accrue interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month and the maximum rate permitted by law.

## 6. TEMPERATURE CONTROL AND SERVICE STANDARDS

**6.1 Temperature Maintenance.** Provider shall store, handle and transport all Cargo within the temperature range specified for that Cargo in the applicable Statement of Work at all times while the Cargo is in Provider's care, custody or control.

**6.2 Monitoring.** Provider shall continuously monitor and record temperatures in each Facility storage zone and each reefer trailer. It shall use calibrated data loggers capable of recording at intervals of no more than fifteen (15) minutes. Provider shall keep temperature records for at least two (2) years and make them available to Customer within two (2) business days after request.

**6.3 Excursion Notification.** Provider shall notify Customer by telephone and email within two (2) hours after detecting any Temperature Excursion. Provider shall segregate affected Cargo pending Customer's disposition instructions.

**6.4 Food Safety.** Provider shall maintain all permits and registrations required by Applicable Law. It shall maintain a written food safety plan and comply with the FDA Sanitary Transportation rule in its role as carrier and loader as applicable. Provider shall also maintain current third-party certification under a Global Food Safety Initiative benchmarked scheme for each Facility.

**6.5 Audit.** On reasonable notice and during normal business hours, Customer or its designee may inspect any Facility and audit Provider's records relating to the Services, no more than twice in any twelve (12) month period. An audit following a Temperature Excursion or a food safety incident does not count toward that limit.

## 7. TENDER, CUSTODY AND CLAIMS

**7.1 Custody.** Cargo is in Provider's care, custody and control from the time Provider signs a receipt or bill of lading for the Cargo until delivery to the consignee named in Customer's instructions is documented by signed proof of delivery.

**7.2 Inspection on Receipt.** Provider shall inspect Cargo on receipt for visible damage and temperature conformity and note any exceptions on the receiving document. Cargo received without noted exception is presumed received in good condition and at the specified temperature.

**7.3 Claims.** Customer shall submit written notice of any Cargo Loss claim within nine (9) months after delivery or, for non-delivery, within nine (9) months after the scheduled delivery date. Provider shall acknowledge each claim within thirty (30) days and pay or decline it in writing within ninety (90) days after receipt.

**7.4 Measure of Recovery.** Provider's liability for Cargo Loss is the replacement cost of the affected Cargo to Customer. This includes freight and handling charges already paid and reasonable costs of inspection, salvage and disposal.

**7.5 No Lien Beyond Charges.** Any warehouse or carrier lien of Provider extends only to the Cargo affected and to unpaid charges for that Cargo. It does not extend to other Cargo for unpaid charges on unrelated shipments.

## 8. INSURANCE

**8.1 Required Coverage.** During the Term and for two (2) years afterwards, Provider shall maintain, with insurers rated A- VIII or better by A.M. Best:

(a) commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and $5,000,000 in the aggregate;

(b) commercial automobile liability insurance of at least $5,000,000 combined single limit;

(c) warehouse legal liability and motor truck cargo insurance, each including reefer breakdown and spoilage coverage, of at least $1,500,000 per location and per conveyance;

(d) workers' compensation insurance as required by law and employer's liability insurance of at least $1,000,000; and

(e) umbrella or excess liability insurance of at least $10,000,000.

**8.2 Certificates.** Provider shall name Customer as an additional insured under the policies in Sections 8.1(a), (b) and (e), and as loss payee under the policies in Section 8.1(c). Provider shall provide certificates of insurance on the Effective Date and on each renewal of coverage.

## 9. CONFIDENTIALITY

**9.1 Definition.** "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement, including pricing, volumes, customer lists, supplier information and business plans.

**9.2 Obligations.** The receiving Party shall protect the disclosing Party's Confidential Information with at least reasonable care. It shall use that information only to perform or receive the Services and disclose it only to its employees, Affiliates and advisors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Section 9.

**9.3 Exceptions.** Confidential Information does not include information that: is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party; was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure; is lawfully received from a third party without restriction; or is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's information. A Party may disclose Confidential Information when required by law, provided it gives prompt notice where legally permitted.

**9.4 Publicity.** Neither Party shall issue any press release or public statement about this Agreement without the other Party's prior written approval.

## 10. INDEMNIFICATION

**10.1 By Provider.** Provider shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all third-party claims, losses, damages, fines, recall costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of: (a) Cargo Loss; (b) the negligence or willful misconduct of Provider or its personnel or subcontractors; (c) Provider's breach of Applicable Law; or (d) bodily injury or property damage caused by Provider's vehicles, equipment or Facilities.

**10.2 By Customer.** Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Provider and its Affiliates, officers, directors and employees from and against all third-party claims, losses, damages and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of: (a) the negligence or willful misconduct of Customer or its personnel; or (b) defects in Cargo existing at the time of tender to Provider and not caused by Provider.

**10.3 Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall give prompt written notice of the claim, allow the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement, and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying Party's expense. The indemnifying Party may not settle any claim that imposes an obligation on, or admits fault by, the indemnified Party without that Party's written consent, which may not be unreasonably withheld.

## 11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**11.1 Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** Except as provided in Section 11.3, neither Party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of this Agreement, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

**11.2 Liability Cap.** Except as provided in Section 11.3, each Party's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, will not exceed the Fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

**11.3 Exclusions.** The limitations in Sections 11.1 and 11.2 do not apply to:

(a) a Party's indemnification obligations under Section 10;

(b) Provider's liability for Cargo Loss;

(c) liability arising from a Party's gross negligence, fraud or willful misconduct;

(d) a Party's breach of Section 9; or

(e) Customer's obligation to pay Fees properly due.

## 12. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

**12.1 Mutual.** Each Party represents and warrants that: (a) it is duly organized and in good standing; (b) it has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement; and (c) its execution and performance of this Agreement do not breach any other agreement by which it is bound.

**12.2 Provider.** Provider further represents and warrants that it holds, and will maintain during the Term, all operating authority required to perform the Services. This includes registration with the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration with a satisfactory or unrated safety rating.

**12.3 Disclaimer.** Except as expressly provided in this Agreement, neither Party makes any other warranty, express or implied.

## 13. FORCE MAJEURE

**13.1 Excused Performance.** Neither Party is liable for failure or delay in performance (other than payment obligations) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, war, terrorism, government action, epidemics, widespread utility failure or labor disputes not involving that Party's own workforce (a "**Force Majeure Event**").

**13.2 Limits.** Equipment failure, including failure of refrigeration units or Facility compressors, is not a Force Majeure Event unless caused by a Force Majeure Event. Provider shall maintain backup power generation at each Facility capable of sustaining required temperatures for at least seventy-two (72) hours.

**13.3 Extended Events.** If a Force Majeure Event prevents a Party from performing for more than thirty (30) consecutive days, the other Party may terminate the affected Statement of Work on written notice. No Early Termination Fee is payable in that case.

## 14. TERMINATION

**14.1 Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any Statement of Work for cause by written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it.

**14.2 Insolvency.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or becomes the subject of any bankruptcy or receivership proceeding that is not dismissed within sixty (60) days.

**14.3 Repeated Temperature Failures.** Customer may terminate any Statement of Work by written notice if three (3) or more Temperature Excursions resulting in Cargo Loss occur at the same Facility or on the same lane within any rolling six (6) month period.

**14.4 Early Termination Fee.** If Provider terminates this Agreement for cause under Section 14.1 or Section 15.2, Customer shall pay Provider an early termination fee (the "**Early Termination Fee**"). The Early Termination Fee equals the average monthly Fees invoiced during the twelve (12) months preceding the termination date, multiplied by the lesser of (a) six (6) and (b) the number of full months remaining in the then-current term. The Parties agree that the Early Termination Fee is a reasonable estimate of Provider's damages and not a penalty.

**14.5 Effect of Termination; Transition.** On expiration or termination, Customer shall pay all Fees accrued through the effective date. At Customer's request, Provider shall continue to perform the Services under the existing Statements of Work for up to ninety (90) days at the then-current Fees. During that period Provider shall cooperate in good faith to transfer Cargo to Customer or its designated successor provider. On payment of undisputed amounts, Provider shall release all Cargo to Customer.

**14.6 Survival.** Sections 7.3, 7.4, 8, 9, 10, 11, 14.4, 14.5 and 16, and any other provision that by its nature should survive, survive expiration or termination.

## 15. ASSIGNMENT AND CHANGE OF CONTROL

**15.1 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations under it, whether by operation of law or otherwise, without the prior written consent of the other Party. Any purported assignment in breach of this Section 15 is void.

**15.2 Change of Control of Customer.** Any Change of Control of Customer is deemed an assignment of this Agreement by Customer and requires the prior written consent of Provider. If a Change of Control of Customer occurs without the prior written consent of Provider, Provider may terminate this Agreement for cause by written notice to Customer and recover the Early Termination Fee under Section 14.4.

**15.3 Subcontracting.** Provider may not subcontract any portion of the Services without Customer's prior written consent, except for overflow transportation on a spot basis using carriers that meet the insurance and safety requirements of this Agreement. Provider remains fully responsible for all subcontracted Services.

**15.4 Successors.** Subject to this Section 15, this Agreement binds and benefits the Parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

## 16. GENERAL

**16.1 Parent Guaranty.** At the same time as it signs this Agreement, Provider shall deliver to Customer a guaranty of Provider's obligations under this Agreement, executed by Provider's parent, Gibeis Holdings Corp.

**16.3 Notices.** Notices under this Agreement must be in writing. They are effective when delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission followed by courier copy, to the addresses set out below the Parties' signatures or to another address a Party designates by notice.

**16.4 Order of Precedence.** If there is any conflict among the documents forming this Agreement, they control in the following order: first, any Side Letter; second, this Agreement; and third, the Statements of Work.

**16.5 Amendment and Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both Parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing, and no failure or delay in exercising any right operates as a waiver.

**16.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held unenforceable, it will be enforced to the maximum extent permissible and the remaining provisions will remain in full force and effect.

Each Party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in New Castle County, Delaware, for any action arising out of this Agreement.
