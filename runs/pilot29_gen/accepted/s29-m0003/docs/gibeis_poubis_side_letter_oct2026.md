# SIDE LETTER

**Re: Master Services Agreement between Poubis Provisions, Inc. and Gibeis Cold Logistics, LLC dated March 1, 2024: Fuel Surcharge and Section 11.3(b)**

Ladies and Gentlemen:

This letter agreement (this "**Side Letter**") is entered into by Gibeis Cold Logistics, LLC ("**Provider**") and Poubis Provisions, Inc. ("**Customer**"). It concerns the Master Services Agreement between Customer and Provider entered into and effective as of March 1, 2024 (the "**MSA**"). Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Side Letter have the meanings given to them in the MSA. This Side Letter is a "Side Letter" as defined in Section 1.1 of the MSA.

## RECITALS

A. Since the Effective Date, diesel prices in the Central Atlantic (PADD 1B) region have stayed well above the base price used to calculate the Fuel Surcharge under Section 5.3 of the MSA. The Parties have agreed to reset that base price and the related calculation.

B. On October 1, 2026, Customer notified Provider in writing that Ostrander Foods Group, Inc. will acquire Customer, with closing expected on January 29, 2027.

C. The Parties have also agreed to clarify the application of Section 11.3(b) of the MSA.

The Parties therefore agree as follows:

## 1. FUEL SURCHARGE

**1.1 Revised Calculation.** With effect from November 2, 2026, Section 5.3 of the MSA is deleted and replaced with the following:

> **5.3 Fuel Surcharge.** Transportation Fees are subject to a fuel surcharge (the "**Fuel Surcharge**"). The Fuel Surcharge is calculated weekly by reference to the U.S. Energy Information Administration's Weekly Retail On-Highway Diesel Price for the Central Atlantic (PADD 1B) region (the "**Index**"). For each full $0.06 per gallon by which the Index exceeds a base price of $4.10 per gallon, the Fuel Surcharge equals one-half percent (0.5%) of the line-haul transportation Fees for that week. No Fuel Surcharge applies when the Index is at or below the base price.

**1.2 Calculation Week.** The Index value published on Monday of each week (or, if no value is published that Monday, the next published value) applies to all line-haul transportation Fees for loads tendered from that Monday through the following Sunday.

**1.3 Invoicing.** Provider shall show the Fuel Surcharge as a separate line item on each monthly invoice, together with the Index value and the surcharge percentage applied for each week. Section 5.2 of the MSA applies to the Fuel Surcharge in the same way as to all other Fees.

**1.4 No Retroactive Adjustment.** The revised calculation in Section 1.1 applies only to loads tendered on or after November 2, 2026. Fuel Surcharges for loads tendered before that date remain calculated under Section 5.3 of the MSA as in effect before this Side Letter.

## 2. CARGO LOSS: SECTION 11.3(b)

**2.1 Clarification.** The Parties clarify that the exclusion in Section 11.3(b) of the MSA, which removes Provider's liability for Cargo Loss from the limitations in Sections 11.1 and 11.2 of the MSA, applies solely to the extent caused by Provider's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

**2.2 Conforming Change.** Section 11.3(b) of the MSA is amended to read in full:

> (b) Provider's liability for Cargo Loss, solely to the extent caused by Provider's gross negligence or willful misconduct;

**2.3 Application.** Section 2.2 applies to all Cargo Loss claims for events occurring on or after the date of this Side Letter.

## 3. GENERAL

**3.2 No Other Consent or Waiver.** This Side Letter does not constitute a consent, approval or waiver by either Party under any provision of the MSA, including Section 15 of the MSA.

**3.3 Representations.** Each Party represents that the person signing this Side Letter on its behalf is duly authorized to do so.

**3.4 Governing Law; Venue.** Section 16.7 of the MSA applies to this Side Letter.
