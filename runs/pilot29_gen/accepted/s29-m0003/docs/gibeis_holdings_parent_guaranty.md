# PARENT GUARANTY

This Parent Guaranty (this "**Guaranty**") is made as of March 1, 2024 by **Gibeis Holdings Corp.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Guarantor**"), in favor of **Poubis Provisions, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Customer**").

## RECITALS

A. Customer and Gibeis Cold Logistics, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company and a wholly owned subsidiary of Guarantor ("**Provider**"), are entering into a Master Services Agreement dated and effective as of March 1, 2024 (as amended, supplemented or modified from time to time, the "**MSA**"). Under the MSA, Provider will provide refrigerated warehousing, cross-docking and reefer transportation services to Customer.

B. Section 16.1 of the MSA requires Provider to deliver to Customer a guaranty of Provider's obligations under the MSA, executed by Guarantor, at the same time as Provider signs the MSA.

C. Guarantor will benefit directly and indirectly from the MSA. Guarantor is entering into this Guaranty to induce Customer to enter into the MSA.

Guarantor therefore agrees as follows:

## 1. GUARANTEED OBLIGATIONS

**1.1 Definitions.** In this Guaranty:

"**Covered Goods**" means goods stored or transported by Provider at or below -18°C.

"**Guaranteed Obligations**" means all obligations and liabilities of Provider to Customer under the MSA and each Statement of Work, whether now existing or arising later, to the extent they relate to Covered Goods. Guaranteed Obligations include Provider's obligations relating to Covered Goods: (a) to pay amounts due for Cargo Loss under Section 7 of the MSA; (b) to defend, indemnify and hold harmless Customer and the other indemnified persons under Section 10.1 of the MSA; (c) to perform the Services in accordance with Sections 3 and 6 of the MSA; (d) to pay damages for breach of the MSA; and (e) to pay all costs of enforcement described in Section 2.3.

**1.2 Related Agreements.** The Guaranteed Obligations include obligations relating to Covered Goods under any Side Letter or Statement of Work entered into under the MSA.

## 2. GUARANTY

**2.1 Guaranty.** Guarantor absolutely, unconditionally and irrevocably guarantees to Customer, as primary obligor and not merely as surety, the full and punctual payment and performance of the Guaranteed Obligations when due.

**2.2 Payment on Demand.** If Provider fails to pay or perform any Guaranteed Obligation when due, Guarantor shall pay or perform it within ten (10) business days after Customer's written demand. Customer is not required to make any demand on, or bring any action against, Provider before making a demand on Guarantor.

**2.3 Enforcement Costs.** Guarantor shall reimburse Customer on demand for all reasonable costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees, incurred by Customer in enforcing this Guaranty.

**2.4 Payments.** Guarantor shall make all payments under this Guaranty in U.S. dollars, in immediately available funds, without set-off, counterclaim or deduction, except deductions required by law.

## 3. NATURE OF GUARANTY

**3.1 Guaranty of Payment and Performance.** This Guaranty is a guaranty of payment and performance, and not of collection.

**3.2 Obligations Unconditional.** Guarantor's obligations under this Guaranty are not released, discharged or otherwise affected by:

(a) any amendment, renewal, extension or modification of the MSA or any Statement of Work or Side Letter, including any renewal of the MSA under Section 2.2 of the MSA;

(b) any waiver, indulgence or extension of time granted to Provider;

(c) any change in the ownership, structure or existence of Provider, or of the relationship between Guarantor and Provider;

(d) the insolvency, bankruptcy, reorganization or dissolution of Provider, or any stay or discharge of Provider's obligations in any such proceeding;

(e) any invalidity or unenforceability of the MSA against Provider, other than by reason of full payment and performance; or

(f) any other circumstance that might otherwise constitute a legal or equitable defense of a guarantor or surety, other than full payment and performance of the Guaranteed Obligations.

**3.3 Defenses.** Guarantor may assert any defense that Provider could assert under the MSA against the relevant Guaranteed Obligation, other than defenses arising from Provider's insolvency, lack of capacity or authority, or the matters listed in Section 3.2.

## 4. WAIVERS

Guarantor waives, to the fullest extent permitted by law: (a) notice of acceptance of this Guaranty; (b) presentment, demand, protest and notice of dishonor or non-performance, except the demand required by Section 2.2; (c) any requirement that Customer first proceed against Provider or any other person or exhaust any remedy; and (d) any right to require marshaling of assets.

## 5. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

Guarantor represents and warrants to Customer that:

(a) it is a corporation duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the State of Delaware;

(b) it has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Guaranty, and has taken all necessary corporate action to authorize it;

(c) this Guaranty is its legal, valid and binding obligation, enforceable in accordance with its terms, subject to applicable bankruptcy, insolvency and similar laws affecting creditors' rights generally;

(d) its execution, delivery and performance of this Guaranty do not violate its organizational documents, any Applicable Law or any agreement binding on it; and

(e) it directly or indirectly owns one hundred percent (100%) of the equity interests in Provider.

## 6. SUBROGATION

Until all Guaranteed Obligations then due have been paid and performed in full, Guarantor shall not exercise any right of subrogation, reimbursement, contribution or indemnity against Provider arising from payments under this Guaranty. Guarantor shall hold any amount it receives in breach of this Section 6 in trust for Customer and pay it to Customer promptly on receipt.

## 7. REINSTATEMENT

This Guaranty continues to be effective, or is reinstated, if any payment of a Guaranteed Obligation is rescinded or must be returned by Customer on the insolvency, bankruptcy or reorganization of Provider or otherwise, as though the payment had not been made.

## 8. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**8.1 MSA Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Guaranty have the meanings given to them in the MSA.

**8.2 Guaranty Definitions.** For purposes of this Guaranty, the definitions in this Guaranty control over the definitions in the MSA.

**8.3 Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only. "Including" means "including without limitation." References to Sections are to sections of this Guaranty unless otherwise stated.

## 9. DURATION

This Guaranty remains in full force and effect until all Guaranteed Obligations arising during the Term of the MSA, including any Renewal Term, and during any transition period under Section 14.5 of the MSA, have been paid and performed in full. Expiration or termination of the MSA does not release Guarantor from Guaranteed Obligations that arose before, or survive, expiration or termination.

## 10. ASSIGNMENT

Guarantor may not assign or delegate its obligations under this Guaranty without Customer's prior written consent. Customer may assign its rights under this Guaranty to any person to whom the MSA is assigned in accordance with Section 15 of the MSA. This Guaranty binds Guarantor and its successors and benefits Customer and its successors and permitted assigns.

## 13. MISCELLANEOUS
