# OFFICE LEASE

**Fulcrum Pier Commons, Building B, Stamford, Connecticut**

This OFFICE LEASE (this "**Lease**") is entered into as of March 1, 2022 (the "**Effective Date**") between **Fulcrum Pier Realty LLC**, a Connecticut limited liability company ("**Landlord**"), and **Saltreach Marine Services, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Tenant**").

## 1. BASIC LEASE TERMS

**1.1 Summary.** The following basic terms are part of this Lease. If they conflict with the other provisions of this Lease, the other provisions control.

| Item | Term |
|---|---|
| (a) Building | Fulcrum Pier Commons, Building B, 400 Harbor Point Boulevard, Stamford, Connecticut 06902 |
| (b) Premises | Suite 500, the entire fifth (5th) floor of the Building, shown on Exhibit A |
| (c) Rentable Area of Premises | 9,800 rentable square feet |
| (d) Rentable Area of Building | 112,000 rentable square feet |
| (e) Tenant's Share | 8.75% |
| (f) Commencement Date | April 1, 2022 |
| (g) Expiration Date | March 31, 2029 |
| (h) Base Rent (Lease Year 1) | $46.00 per rentable square foot per year ($450,800.00 per year; $37,566.67 per month) |
| (i) Annual Escalation | Three percent (3%) per Lease Year |
| (j) Base Year | Calendar year 2022 |
| (k) Security Deposit | $112,700.00 |
| (l) Permitted Use | General office use, including ship management, crew administration and related business operations |
| (m) Parking | Thirty (30) unreserved spaces in the Building garage at no additional charge |

**1.2 Lease Year.** "**Lease Year**" means each consecutive twelve (12) month period beginning on the Commencement Date or an anniversary of it.

## 2. PREMISES

**2.1 Lease of Premises.** Landlord leases the Premises to Tenant, and Tenant leases the Premises from Landlord, for the Term and on the terms of this Lease. Tenant also has the non-exclusive right to use the lobbies, elevators, corridors, restrooms, loading areas, garage and other common areas of the Building and the surrounding property (the "**Common Areas**").

**2.2 Condition of Premises.** Landlord shall substantially complete the improvements described in the work letter attached as Exhibit B (the "**Landlord Work**") before the Commencement Date. Landlord shall complete the Landlord Work in a good and workmanlike manner, in compliance with applicable law, and at Landlord's cost, subject to the Tenant improvement allowance described in Exhibit B. Tenant accepts the Premises otherwise in their "as is" condition. This does not affect Landlord's warranty that the Building systems serving the Premises will be in good working order on the Commencement Date.

**2.3 Measurement.** The rentable areas stated in Section 1.1 are stipulated for all purposes of this Lease and are not subject to remeasurement.

## 3. TERM

**3.1 Term.** The term of this Lease (the "**Term**") begins on the Commencement Date and ends on the Expiration Date, unless terminated earlier or extended under this Lease.

**3.2 Early Access.** Tenant may enter the Premises beginning thirty (30) days before the Commencement Date to install furniture, cabling and equipment. Early access is subject to all terms of this Lease except the obligation to pay Rent.

**3.3 Renewal Option.** Tenant has one (1) option to extend the Term for five (5) years (the "**Renewal Term**"). Tenant must give Landlord written notice of exercise at least twelve (12) months, and not more than eighteen (18) months, before the Expiration Date. The option is available only if no Event of Default exists when the notice is given. Base Rent for the Renewal Term will equal ninety-five percent (95%) of the fair market rent for comparable office space in comparable buildings in Stamford, Connecticut. If the parties cannot agree on fair market rent within thirty (30) days after Tenant's notice, it will be determined by the appraisal procedure in Exhibit C. All other terms of this Lease apply during the Renewal Term.

## 4. RENT

**4.1 Base Rent.** Tenant shall pay Base Rent in equal monthly installments, in advance, on the first day of each calendar month during the Term, without demand. Base Rent for any partial month is prorated on a daily basis. On the first day of each Lease Year after the first, Base Rent increases by the Annual Escalation over the Base Rent for the preceding Lease Year.

**4.2 Abatement.** Base Rent is abated for the first three (3) full calendar months of the Term. Tenant's Share of Operating Expenses and Taxes remains payable during the abatement period.

**4.3 Additional Rent.** All amounts payable by Tenant under this Lease other than Base Rent are "**Additional Rent**." Base Rent and Additional Rent are together "**Rent**."

**4.4 Late Charge.** If any Rent is not paid within five (5) business days after its due date, Tenant shall pay a late charge equal to three percent (3%) of the overdue amount. Landlord shall waive the first late charge in any twelve (12) month period if Tenant pays the overdue amount within five (5) business days after written notice.

## 5. OPERATING EXPENSES AND TAXES

**5.1 Definitions.** "**Operating Expenses**" means the reasonable costs Landlord incurs to operate, maintain, repair and insure the Building and the Common Areas, determined in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles consistently applied. "**Taxes**" means real estate taxes and assessments levied on the Building and the land on which it stands. Operating Expenses exclude:

(a) capital expenditures, except to the extent they reduce Operating Expenses or are required by laws first enacted after the Effective Date, in each case amortized over their useful life;

(b) costs of leasing, brokerage commissions and tenant improvements for other tenants;

(c) principal, interest and other financing costs;

(d) costs reimbursed by insurance, warranties or other tenants;

(e) management fees in excess of three percent (3%) of gross Building revenues; and

(f) costs arising from Landlord's negligence or breach of law or of any lease.

**5.2 Payment.** For each calendar year after the Base Year, Tenant shall pay Tenant's Share of the amount by which Operating Expenses and Taxes for that year exceed Operating Expenses and Taxes for the Base Year. Tenant shall pay monthly estimates of that amount based on Landlord's reasonable annual budget.

**5.3 Controllable Expenses.** Tenant's Share of controllable Operating Expenses (all Operating Expenses other than Taxes, insurance, utilities and snow removal) shall not increase by more than five percent (5%) per year, on a non-cumulative basis, over the Base Year.

**5.4 Annual Statement; Audit.** Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each calendar year, Landlord shall deliver a statement of actual Operating Expenses and Taxes for that year. The parties shall settle any overpayment or underpayment within thirty (30) days after delivery. Tenant may audit Landlord's records for any year within one (1) year after receiving the statement for that year. If the audit shows that Tenant was overcharged by more than four percent (4%), Landlord shall pay the reasonable cost of the audit.

## 6. SECURITY DEPOSIT

**6.1 Deposit.** On signing this Lease, Tenant shall deposit the Security Deposit with Landlord. Landlord may apply the Security Deposit to cure any Event of Default. Tenant shall restore any amount applied within ten (10) business days after written demand.

**6.2 Reduction.** If no Event of Default has occurred, the Security Deposit shall be reduced to $75,133.33 on the first day of the fourth (4th) Lease Year. Landlord shall refund the excess within thirty (30) days.

**6.3 Return.** Landlord shall return the Security Deposit, less any amounts properly applied, within thirty (30) days after the end of the Term and Tenant's surrender of the Premises in accordance with Article 18. Landlord shall hold the Security Deposit in accordance with Connecticut General Statutes and shall transfer it to any successor landlord, which shall assume Landlord's obligations with respect to it.

## 7. USE

**7.1 Permitted Use.** Tenant may use the Premises for the Permitted Use and for no other purpose without Landlord's consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

**7.2 Compliance.** Tenant shall comply with all laws applicable to its particular use of the Premises and with the Building rules attached as Exhibit D, as reasonably amended on prior written notice and applied without discrimination. Landlord shall comply with all laws applicable to the Building structure, systems and Common Areas, including the Americans with Disabilities Act.

**7.3 Hazardous Materials.** Tenant shall not bring any hazardous materials into the Premises, except customary office and cleaning supplies used in compliance with law. Landlord represents that, to its knowledge, no hazardous materials are present in the Building in violation of law as of the Effective Date.

## 8. SERVICES AND UTILITIES

**8.1 Building Services.** Landlord shall provide the following services:

(a) heating, ventilation and air conditioning from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. on weekdays and 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. on Saturdays, excluding holidays;

(b) passenger and freight elevator service;

(c) janitorial services five (5) days per week in accordance with Exhibit E;

(d) electricity for standard office use; and

(e) access to the Building and the Premises twenty-four (24) hours per day, every day, subject to reasonable security procedures.

**8.2 After-Hours HVAC; Supplemental Units.** Tenant may obtain after-hours HVAC at Landlord's actual cost, currently $65.00 per hour per floor. Tenant may install a supplemental cooling unit for its server room, at Tenant's cost, in a location reasonably approved by Landlord.

**8.3 Electricity.** Electricity to the Premises shall be measured by a submeter and billed at Landlord's actual cost, without markup.

**8.4 Interruption.** If any service Landlord is required to provide is interrupted for more than five (5) consecutive business days for reasons within Landlord's reasonable control, and the interruption makes all or a material part of the Premises untenantable, Rent shall abate for the affected portion until the service is restored.

## 9. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

**9.1 Landlord's Obligations.** Landlord shall keep the following in good condition and repair, with the cost included in Operating Expenses to the extent permitted by Article 5:

(a) the structure, roof and exterior of the Building;

(b) the Building systems up to the point of connection with the Premises; and

(c) the Common Areas.

**9.2 Tenant's Obligations.** Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good condition and repair, ordinary wear and tear and casualty excepted. This includes Tenant's supplemental equipment and any alterations made by Tenant.

## 10. ALTERATIONS

**10.1 Consent.** Tenant shall not make alterations to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned or delayed. No consent is needed for cosmetic alterations, such as painting and carpeting, that cost less than $50,000 in any Lease Year and do not affect the Building structure or systems, provided Tenant gives Landlord prior notice.

**10.2 Performance.** Tenant shall perform alterations in a good and workmanlike manner, in compliance with law, using licensed and insured contractors. Tenant shall keep the Premises free of mechanics' liens arising from its work. Tenant shall discharge or bond over any lien within thirty (30) days after notice of it.

**10.3 Removal.** When Landlord consents to an alteration, it shall state whether Tenant must remove that alteration at the end of the Term. Tenant need not remove the Landlord Work or any alteration not so designated.

## 11. INSURANCE

**11.1 Tenant's Insurance.** Tenant shall maintain the following insurance throughout the Term:

(a) commercial general liability insurance with limits of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate, plus umbrella coverage of at least $5,000,000, naming Landlord and its managing agent as additional insureds;

(b) property insurance covering Tenant's personal property and alterations for their full replacement cost; and

(c) workers' compensation insurance as required by law.

**11.2 Landlord's Insurance.** Landlord shall maintain the following insurance throughout the Term:

(a) property insurance on the Building for its full replacement cost; and

(b) commercial general liability insurance on the Common Areas with limits customary for comparable buildings in Stamford, Connecticut.

**11.3 Waiver of Subrogation.** Each party waives all claims against the other for loss or damage to its property to the extent that loss or damage is covered, or required by this Lease to be covered, by property insurance. Each party shall cause its property insurance policies to permit this waiver.

**11.4 Certificates.** Each party shall deliver certificates of its required insurance to the other on request.

## 12. INDEMNIFICATION

**12.1 By Tenant.** Subject to Section 11.3, Tenant shall indemnify Landlord against third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage occurring in the Premises, or caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its employees, agents or contractors. This obligation does not apply to the extent the claim is caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord or its employees, agents or contractors.

**12.2 By Landlord.** Subject to Section 11.3, Landlord shall indemnify Tenant against third-party claims for bodily injury or property damage occurring in the Common Areas, or caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord or its employees, agents or contractors. This obligation does not apply to the extent the claim is caused by the negligence or willful misconduct of Tenant or its employees, agents or contractors.

**12.3 Survival.** This Article 12 survives the expiration or termination of this Lease as to events occurring during the Term.

## 13. CASUALTY

**13.1 Restoration.** If the Premises or the Building is damaged by fire or other casualty, Landlord shall, within sixty (60) days after the casualty, give Tenant a contractor's reasonable estimate of the time needed to restore it. Unless this Lease is terminated under Section 13.2, Landlord shall restore the Building and the Premises (excluding Tenant's alterations and property) with reasonable diligence.

**13.2 Termination.** Either party may terminate this Lease by written notice within thirty (30) days after delivery of the estimate in either of the following cases:

(a) the estimated restoration period exceeds two hundred seventy (270) days after the casualty; or

(b) the casualty occurs in the last eighteen (18) months of the Term and the estimated restoration period exceeds ninety (90) days.

Tenant may also terminate this Lease if restoration is not substantially complete within sixty (60) days after the estimated completion date.

**13.3 Abatement.** Rent abates in proportion to the part of the Premises that is untenantable from the date of the casualty until the Premises are restored.

## 14. CONDEMNATION

If all of the Premises, or so much of them that the remainder cannot reasonably be used for the Permitted Use, is taken by eminent domain, this Lease terminates on the date of the taking. If a lesser part is taken, Rent is reduced in proportion. Landlord is entitled to the award for the taking. Tenant may pursue a separate claim for its moving costs and its personal property and alterations.

## 15. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

**15.1 Consent Required.** Except as provided in Section 15.3, Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet all or any part of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent. Landlord shall not unreasonably withhold, condition or delay its consent. Landlord shall respond to a complete request within fifteen (15) business days.

**15.2 Excess Rent.** Tenant shall pay Landlord fifty percent (50%) of any rent received from an assignee or subtenant in excess of the Rent payable under this Lease. The excess is computed after Tenant recovers its reasonable costs of the assignment or sublease, including brokerage commissions, legal fees and improvement costs.

**15.3 Permitted Transfers.** Landlord's consent is not required for any of the following, provided the Premises continue to be used for the Permitted Use (each, a "**Permitted Transfer**"):

(a) an assignment or sublease to an entity that controls, is controlled by or is under common control with Tenant;

(b) an assignment to a successor by merger, consolidation or acquisition of all or substantially all of Tenant's assets, if the successor's net worth immediately afterward is at least equal to Tenant's net worth on the Effective Date; or

(c) any sale or transfer of Tenant's stock or other equity interests, including a change of control of Tenant.

Tenant shall give Landlord written notice of any Permitted Transfer within thirty (30) days after it occurs. A Permitted Transfer under Section 15.3(c) is not an assignment of this Lease, and Tenant remains the tenant under this Lease.

**15.4 No Release.** Except for a Permitted Transfer under Section 15.3(c), no assignment or sublease releases Tenant from its obligations under this Lease.

## 16. DEFAULT AND REMEDIES

**16.1 Events of Default.** Each of the following is an "**Event of Default**":

(a) Tenant fails to pay Rent within five (5) business days after written notice that it is past due;

(b) Tenant fails to perform any other obligation under this Lease within thirty (30) days after written notice. If the failure cannot reasonably be cured within thirty (30) days, this period is extended for so long as Tenant begins the cure within that period and pursues it diligently; or

(c) Tenant becomes the subject of a voluntary bankruptcy proceeding, or an involuntary bankruptcy proceeding that is not dismissed within ninety (90) days.

**16.2 Remedies.** Upon an Event of Default, Landlord may terminate this Lease by written notice and recover possession of the Premises in accordance with Connecticut law. Landlord may also recover from Tenant either of the following, subject to its duty to use reasonable efforts to mitigate its damages:

(a) the Rent accrued through the termination date; plus

(b) the present value (discounted at four percent (4%) per year) of the amount by which the Rent for the rest of the Term exceeds the fair rental value of the Premises for the same period.

Landlord shall not engage in self-help to recover possession.

**16.3 Landlord Default.** If Landlord fails to perform any obligation under this Lease within thirty (30) days after written notice from Tenant, Tenant may pursue any remedy available at law or in equity. This period is extended for so long as Landlord diligently pursues a cure that cannot reasonably be completed within thirty (30) days.

## 17. SUBORDINATION; ESTOPPEL CERTIFICATES

**17.1 Subordination.** This Lease is subordinate to any mortgage now or later placed on the Building. This subordination is conditioned on the mortgagee's delivery of a commercially reasonable subordination, non-disturbance and attornment agreement. That agreement must provide that Tenant's possession will not be disturbed so long as no Event of Default exists. Landlord shall deliver such an agreement from its existing mortgagee within sixty (60) days after the Effective Date.

**17.2 Estoppel Certificates.** Within fifteen (15) business days after request, each party shall deliver to the other a certificate confirming the following, to the extent true:

(a) that this Lease is in full force and effect;

(b) the dates through which Rent has been paid; and

(c) whether, to the certifying party's knowledge, any default exists.

Neither party may request more than two (2) certificates in any twelve (12) month period, except in connection with a sale, financing or Permitted Transfer.

## 18. SURRENDER; HOLDING OVER

**18.1 Surrender.** At the end of the Term, Tenant shall surrender the Premises broom clean and in good condition, ordinary wear and tear and casualty excepted. Before surrender, Tenant shall remove its personal property, its cabling and any alterations it is required to remove under Section 10.3.

**18.2 Holding Over.** If Tenant remains in possession after the end of the Term without Landlord's consent, it is a tenant at sufferance. Base Rent during the holdover is payable at one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the Base Rent in effect at the end of the Term, prorated daily. Tenant is liable for consequential damages caused by a holdover only if it continues for more than sixty (60) days after Landlord gives notice that it has a replacement tenant.

## 19. QUIET ENJOYMENT; LANDLORD ACCESS

**19.1 Quiet Enjoyment.** So long as no Event of Default exists, Tenant may peaceably enjoy the Premises during the Term without interference by Landlord or anyone claiming through Landlord.

**19.2 Access.** Landlord may enter the Premises on at least twenty-four (24) hours' prior notice, except in an emergency, for any of the following purposes:

(a) to inspect them;

(b) to make repairs; or

(c) during the last twelve (12) months of the Term, to show them to prospective tenants.

Landlord shall use reasonable efforts to minimize interference with Tenant's business and shall comply with Tenant's reasonable security and confidentiality procedures.

## 20. NOTICES

All notices under this Lease must be in writing. A notice is deemed given when delivered by hand or by nationally recognized overnight courier to the address below, or to another address designated by notice. A copy may also be sent by email, but email alone is not sufficient notice of default.

(a) **If to Landlord:** Fulcrum Pier Realty LLC, 400 Harbor Point Boulevard, Management Office, Stamford, Connecticut 06902, Attention: Asset Manager.
