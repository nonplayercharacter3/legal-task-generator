# GLILUV MARITIME HOLDINGS, LLC

## ACTION BY UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF MANAGERS

**Dated: March 20, 2026**

The undersigned, being all of the members of the Board of Managers (the "**Board**") of **Gliluv Maritime Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (the "**Company**"), act under the Delaware Limited Liability Company Act and the Company's Limited Liability Company Agreement (the "**LLC Agreement**"). By this written consent, they take the actions set forth below without a meeting. They waive any notice requirement and direct that this consent be filed with the minutes of the proceedings of the Board.

## 1. RECITALS

**A. Acquisition of Saltreach.** The Company, as Buyer, and **Dreisend Shipping Group, Ltd.**, a Bermuda exempted company ("**Seller**"), are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of December 1, 2025 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, the Company acquired all of the issued and outstanding shares of **Saltreach Marine Services, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("**Saltreach**"). The closing occurred on January 15, 2026, for a Purchase Price of $64,000,000, of which $6,400,000 was deposited as the Escrow Amount with **Pelham Trust Company, N.A.**, as Escrow Agent. At the Closing, the Company and Seller also entered into the Transition Services Agreement and the Escrow Agreement. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this consent have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement.

**B. Delivery of Closing Statement.** Under Section 2.4(a) of the Purchase Agreement, Seller delivered its Closing Statement to the Company on February 9, 2026. The Closing Statement sets forth Seller's calculation of Closing Working Capital as $5,420,000, against Target Working Capital of $3,250,000.

**C. Management Review.** The Company's Chief Financial Officer and its outside accountants have reviewed the Closing Statement together with the books and records of Saltreach made available under Section 2.4(d) of the Purchase Agreement. Management has reported to the Board that, in its view, the Closing Statement overstates Closing Working Capital by $1,900,000. Management attributes the overstatement principally to the following items:

(i) the inclusion as current assets of owner reimbursement receivables for crew wages and manning costs that were more than 120 days past due as of the Closing Date and that, under the Accounting Principles set forth on Schedule 1.1, should have been reserved;

(ii) the inclusion as a current asset of prepaid management fees attributable to vessels whose ship management agreements were terminated before the Closing Date; and

(iii) the omission from current liabilities of accrued crew-change, travel and agency charges incurred before the Closing Date but invoiced afterward.

**D. Board Determination.** The Board has considered management's report and the advice of the Company's counsel. It has determined that it is advisable and in the best interests of the Company to dispute the Closing Statement and to seek payment from Seller of the resulting shortfall under Section 2.4(e)(ii) of the Purchase Agreement.

## 2. RESOLUTIONS

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT:

**2.1 Dispute of Closing Statement.** RESOLVED, that the Company disputes the Closing Statement delivered by Seller and Seller's calculation of Closing Working Capital, to the extent of a working-capital overstatement of $1,900,000, as described in Recital C.

**2.2 Authorization of Objection Notice.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Chief Executive Officer and the Chief Financial Officer of the Company (each, an "**Authorized Officer**") are, and each of them acting alone is, authorized and directed to prepare, execute and deliver to Seller an Objection Notice under Section 2.4(b) of the Purchase Agreement. The Objection Notice shall specify in reasonable detail each disputed item, the amount in dispute and the basis for the Company's position, consistent with management's report to the Board. It shall be delivered in the manner provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement.

**2.3 Negotiation and Independent Accountant.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Authorized Officers are authorized to negotiate in good faith with Seller under Section 2.4(c) of the Purchase Agreement to resolve the disputed items. If any items remain unresolved, they are further authorized to submit those items to an Independent Accountant and to agree with Seller on the identity and engagement terms of the Independent Accountant.

**2.4 Collection of Shortfall.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that upon final determination of Closing Working Capital, the Authorized Officers are authorized to take all actions reasonably necessary to collect from Seller any amount payable to the Company under Section 2.4(e) of the Purchase Agreement.

**2.5 Engagement of Advisors.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Authorized Officers are authorized to engage, or continue the engagement of, legal counsel, accountants and other advisors as they consider appropriate in connection with the foregoing, and to pay their reasonable fees and expenses.

**2.6 General Authority.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that the Authorized Officers are, and each of them acting alone is, authorized to execute and deliver all such further notices, certificates and instruments, and to take all such further actions, as they consider necessary or appropriate to carry out the foregoing resolutions. The execution of any such document or the taking of any such action shall be conclusive evidence of that authority.

**2.7 Ratification.** RESOLVED FURTHER, that all actions previously taken by any manager, officer or authorized representative of the Company in connection with the matters contemplated by the foregoing resolutions are ratified, confirmed and approved in all respects.
