# STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

This STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of December 1, 2025, by and between **Dreisend Shipping Group, Ltd.**, a Bermuda exempted company ("**Seller**"), and **Gliluv Maritime Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**").

## RECITALS

A. Seller owns all of the issued and outstanding shares of common stock (the "**Shares**") of **Saltreach Marine Services, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"). The Company provides technical, commercial and crew management services to tanker and bulk-carrier owners.

B. Seller wishes to sell to Buyer, and Buyer wishes to purchase from Seller, all of the Shares, on the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## ARTICLE I. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** As used in this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings set forth below:

"**Affiliate**" means, with respect to any Person, any other Person that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with such Person.

"**Ancillary Agreements**" means the Escrow Agreement, the Transition Services Agreement, and each other agreement, certificate and instrument delivered pursuant to this Agreement.

"**Business Day**" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or day on which banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by Law to close.

"**Closing Working Capital**" means (a) the current assets of the Company, less (b) the current liabilities of the Company, in each case determined as of 11:59 p.m. New York time on the day immediately preceding the Closing Date, in accordance with the Accounting Principles set forth on Schedule 1.1.

"**Environmental Laws**" means all Laws relating to pollution or the protection of the marine or other environment. This includes the Oil Pollution Act of 1990 (OPA 90), the Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, and Liability Act, the International Convention for the Prevention of Pollution from Ships (MARPOL), the International Convention for the Control and Management of Ships' Ballast Water and Sediments, and any analogous national, state or port-state Laws.

"**Escrow Agent**" means Pelham Trust Company, N.A.

"**Escrow Agreement**" means the Escrow Agreement to be entered into at the Closing among Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent.

"**Escrow Fund**" means the Escrow Amount deposited with the Escrow Agent, together with any interest and earnings thereon.

"**Fraud**" means actual and intentional fraud under the Laws of the State of New York with respect to the making of the representations and warranties in Article IV or Article V.

"**Fundamental Representations**" means the representations and warranties in Section 4.1 (Organization), Section 4.2 (Authority), Section 4.3 (Capitalization; Title to Shares) and Section 4.14 (Brokers).

"**Governmental Authority**" means any federal, state, local, foreign or supranational government, court, agency, flag-state administration, classification society acting in a statutory capacity, or other regulatory authority.

"**Law**" means any statute, law, ordinance, regulation, rule, code, convention, treaty, order, judgment or decree of any Governmental Authority.

"**Losses**" means losses, damages, liabilities, deficiencies, judgments, fines, penalties, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

"**Person**" means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, Governmental Authority or other entity.

"**Pre-Closing Tax Period**" means any taxable period ending on or before the Closing Date and, for any taxable period that includes but does not end on the Closing Date, the portion of that period ending on and including the Closing Date.

"**Target Working Capital**" means $3,250,000.

"**Taxes**" means all federal, state, local and foreign income, gross receipts, gross transportation, tonnage, sales, use, withholding, payroll, property, franchise and other taxes, duties and assessments, together with any interest, penalties and additions thereto.

"**Transition Services Agreement**" means the Transition Services Agreement to be entered into at the Closing between Seller, as provider, and Buyer, as recipient.

**1.2 Interpretation.** The words "include," "includes" and "including" are deemed to be followed by "without limitation." References to Articles, Sections and Schedules are to those of this Agreement unless otherwise stated. Headings are for convenience only.

## ARTICLE II. PURCHASE AND SALE; PURCHASE PRICE

**2.1 Purchase Price.** At the Closing, Seller shall sell, assign, transfer and deliver to Buyer, and Buyer shall purchase from Seller, the Shares, free and clear of all liens. The aggregate purchase price for the Shares is $64,000,000 (the "**Purchase Price**"), subject to adjustment as provided in Section 2.4.

**2.2 Payment at Closing.** At the Closing, Buyer shall pay:

(a) to the Escrow Agent, $6,400,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**"), to be held and disbursed in accordance with the Escrow Agreement; and

(b) to Seller, the Purchase Price less the Escrow Amount and less the Estimated Adjustment Amount (if negative) or plus the Estimated Adjustment Amount (if positive), by wire transfer of immediately available funds to an account designated in writing by Seller.

**2.3 Estimated Closing Statement.** No later than three (3) Business Days before the Closing Date, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a good-faith estimate of Closing Working Capital. The amount by which that estimate exceeds or falls short of Target Working Capital is the "**Estimated Adjustment Amount**."

**2.4 Post-Closing Adjustment.**

(a) *Closing Statement.* Within sixty (60) days after the Closing Date, Seller shall prepare and deliver to Buyer a statement setting forth Seller's calculation of Closing Working Capital, with reasonable supporting detail (the "**Closing Statement**").

(b) *Objection.* If Buyer disagrees with the Closing Statement, Buyer shall deliver to Seller a written notice (an "**Objection Notice**") within thirty (30) days after receipt of the Closing Statement. The Objection Notice must specify in reasonable detail each disputed item, the amount in dispute and the basis for Buyer's position. If Buyer does not deliver an Objection Notice within that period, the Closing Statement and Seller's calculation of Closing Working Capital will be final and binding on the parties. Any item not specifically disputed in a timely Objection Notice will likewise be final and binding.

(c) *Resolution.* If Buyer timely delivers an Objection Notice, Buyer and Seller shall negotiate in good faith for thirty (30) days to resolve the disputed items. Any items that remain unresolved at the end of that period shall be submitted to an independent accounting firm of national standing mutually agreed by Buyer and Seller (the "**Independent Accountant**"). The Independent Accountant shall act as an expert and not an arbitrator. It shall resolve only the items remaining in dispute, within the range of values asserted by the parties, and its determination will be final and binding absent manifest error. The fees of the Independent Accountant will be allocated between Buyer and Seller in inverse proportion to the relative success of each party on the disputed amounts.

(d) *Access.* During the review and dispute periods under this Section 2.4, each party shall give the other and its accountants reasonable access to the books, records and working papers used in preparing the Closing Statement.

(e) *Payment.* Within five (5) Business Days after Closing Working Capital becomes final under this Section 2.4, the following payments shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds:

(i) if final Closing Working Capital, less Target Working Capital, exceeds the Estimated Adjustment Amount, Buyer shall pay the excess to Seller; and

(ii) if it is less than the Estimated Adjustment Amount, Seller shall pay the shortfall to Buyer.

**2.5 Withholding.** Buyer may deduct and withhold from any amount payable under this Agreement such amounts as are required to be deducted and withheld under applicable Tax Law.

## ARTICLE III. CLOSING

**3.1 Closing.** The closing of the transactions contemplated by this Agreement (the "**Closing**") shall take place remotely by electronic exchange of documents and signatures on January 15, 2026, or on such other date as Buyer and Seller may agree in writing (the "**Closing Date**").

**3.2 Seller Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer:

(a) stock certificates evidencing the Shares, duly endorsed in blank or with stock powers attached;

(b) the Escrow Agreement and the Transition Services Agreement, each duly executed by Seller;

(c) written resignations of the directors and officers of the Company designated by Buyer; and

(d) a certificate of an officer of Seller certifying that the conditions in Section 8.2(a) and (b) have been satisfied.

**3.3 Buyer Closing Deliverables.** At the Closing, Buyer shall deliver the payments described in Section 2.2 and the Escrow Agreement and the Transition Services Agreement, each duly executed by Buyer.

## ARTICLE IV. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF SELLER

Except as set forth in the disclosure schedule delivered by Seller to Buyer concurrently with this Agreement (the "**Disclosure Schedule**"), Seller represents and warrants to Buyer as of the date hereof and as of the Closing Date as follows:

**4.1 Organization.** Seller is duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of Bermuda. The Company is duly incorporated, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware and is qualified to do business in each jurisdiction where such qualification is required.

**4.2 Authority.** Seller has full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party. This Agreement constitutes the legal, valid and binding obligation of Seller, enforceable in accordance with its terms.

**4.3 Capitalization; Title to Shares.** The authorized capital stock of the Company consists of 10,000 shares of common stock, par value $0.01 per share, of which 1,000 shares are issued and outstanding and constitute the Shares. Seller is the record and beneficial owner of all of the Shares, free and clear of all liens. There are no outstanding options, warrants or other rights to acquire any equity interest in the Company.

**4.4 No Conflicts.** The execution, delivery and performance of this Agreement by Seller do not violate the organizational documents of Seller or the Company or any Law applicable to them. They also do not require any consent under any Material Contract, except as set forth in Section 4.4 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**4.5 Financial Statements.** Seller has delivered to Buyer the audited balance sheets of the Company as of December 31, 2023 and 2024, and the related statements of income and cash flows for the years then ended, together with unaudited interim financial statements as of September 30, 2025. These statements were prepared in accordance with GAAP consistently applied and fairly present in all material respects the financial position and results of operations of the Company.

**4.6 Absence of Changes.** Since December 31, 2024, the Company has conducted its business in the ordinary course, and there has been no event that has had or would reasonably be expected to have a material adverse effect on the Company.

**4.7 Material Contracts.** Section 4.7 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each of the following (the "**Material Contracts**"):

(a) ship management agreement;

(b) crew manning agreement;

(c) charter-party brokerage arrangement; and

(d) other contract involving annual payments in excess of $250,000 to which the Company is a party.

Each Material Contract is valid and in full force and effect, and neither the Company nor, to Seller's knowledge, any counterparty is in material breach.

**4.8 Real Property.** The Company owns no real property. Section 4.8 of the Disclosure Schedule lists each lease of real property to which the Company is a party (the "**Leases**"). Each Lease is in full force and effect, and the Company is not in default under any Lease.

**4.9 Compliance with Laws; Permits.** The Company is, and since January 1, 2021 has been, in compliance in all material respects with all applicable Laws. These include ISM Code, ISPS Code and Maritime Labour Convention requirements applicable to managed vessels. The Company holds all permits and Documents of Compliance necessary to conduct its business.

**4.10 Environmental Matters.** The Company is, and since January 1, 2021 has been, in compliance in all material respects with all Environmental Laws. No discharge, spill or release of oil, noxious liquid substances or ballast water in violation of Environmental Laws has occurred in connection with any vessel under the Company's management. The Company has not received any notice of any claim, investigation or proceeding under Environmental Laws.

**4.11 Taxes** (the representations and warranties in this Section 4.11, the "**Tax Representations**").

(a) The Company has timely filed all Tax returns required to be filed by it, and all such returns are true, correct and complete in all material respects.

(b) The Company has timely paid all Taxes due and owing, including any U.S. gross transportation tax under Section 887 of the Internal Revenue Code and any tonnage taxes, whether or not shown on any return.

(c) No audit, examination or other proceeding with respect to Taxes of the Company is pending or threatened.

(d) The Company has not waived any statute of limitations with respect to Taxes.

**4.12 Employees and Crew.** Section 4.12 of the Disclosure Schedule lists all shore-based employees of the Company and all crew-supply arrangements. The Company is in material compliance with all Laws and collective bargaining agreements relating to employment and seafarers' welfare.

**4.13 Litigation.** There is no action, suit, arbitration or proceeding pending or, to Seller's knowledge, threatened against the Company.

**4.14 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission in connection with the transactions contemplated by this Agreement based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Seller or the Company.

## ARTICLE V. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF BUYER

Buyer represents and warrants to Seller as follows:

**5.1 Organization and Authority.** Buyer is a limited liability company duly organized, validly existing and in good standing under the Laws of the State of Delaware. Buyer has full power and authority to execute, deliver and perform this Agreement and the Ancillary Agreements to which it is a party.

**5.2 Sufficiency of Funds.** Buyer has, and at the Closing will have, sufficient funds to pay the Purchase Price.

**5.3 Investment Intent.** Buyer is acquiring the Shares for its own account and not with a view to distribution in violation of applicable securities Laws.

**5.4 Brokers.** No broker, finder or investment banker is entitled to any fee or commission based on arrangements made by or on behalf of Buyer.

## ARTICLE VI. COVENANTS

**6.1 Conduct of Business.** From the date hereof until the Closing, Seller shall cause the Company to:

(a) operate in the ordinary course consistent with past practice;

(b) maintain its Documents of Compliance and vessel-management certifications; and

(c) not enter into, amend or terminate any Material Contract without Buyer's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

**6.2 Access to Information.** Until the Closing, Seller shall give Buyer and its representatives reasonable access to the properties, books, records and personnel of the Company.

**6.3 Efforts; Consents.** Each party shall use commercially reasonable efforts to satisfy the conditions to Closing and to obtain any third-party consents listed in Section 4.4 of the Disclosure Schedule.

**6.4 Confidentiality.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall, and shall cause its Affiliates to, hold in confidence all confidential information concerning the Company. This includes crew records, charterer and owner information, and vessel-performance data.

**6.5 Non-Solicitation.** For two (2) years after the Closing Date, Seller shall not, and shall cause its Affiliates not to, solicit for employment any shore-based employee of the Company. This restriction does not apply to general solicitations not targeted at such employees.

## ARTICLE VII. TAX MATTERS

**7.1 Transfer Taxes.** All transfer, documentary, stamp and similar Taxes arising from the sale of the Shares shall be borne equally by Buyer and Seller.

**7.2 Straddle Periods.** For any taxable period that begins before and ends after the Closing Date, Taxes shall be allocated as follows:

(a) Taxes based on income, receipts or tonnage shall be allocated on a closing-of-the-books basis as of the end of the Closing Date; and

(b) all other Taxes shall be allocated ratably on a daily basis.

**7.3 Cooperation.** Buyer and Seller shall cooperate reasonably in the preparation of Tax returns and the conduct of any Tax audit or proceeding relating to the Company.

## ARTICLE VIII. CONDITIONS TO CLOSING

**8.1 Conditions to Obligations of Each Party.** The obligations of each party to consummate the Closing are subject to the absence of any Law or order prohibiting the transactions contemplated hereby.

**8.2 Conditions to Obligations of Buyer.** The obligations of Buyer to consummate the Closing are subject to the satisfaction or waiver of each of the following:

(a) the representations and warranties of Seller in Article IV being true and correct in all material respects as of the Closing Date;

(b) Seller having performed in all material respects its covenants required to be performed before the Closing; and

(c) receipt of the deliverables listed in Section 3.2.

**8.3 Conditions to Obligations of Seller.** The obligations of Seller to consummate the Closing are subject to:

(a) the representations and warranties of Buyer being true and correct in all material respects; and

(b) receipt of the deliverables listed in Section 3.3.

## ARTICLE IX. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Survival.** The representations and warranties in this Agreement shall survive the Closing as follows:

(a) the Fundamental Representations shall survive until sixty (60) days after the expiration of the applicable statute of limitations;

(b) the Tax Representations shall survive until six (6) months after the Closing Date;

(c) the representations and warranties in Section 4.10 (Environmental Matters) shall survive until the fifth (5th) anniversary of the Closing Date; and

(d) all other representations and warranties shall survive until eighteen (18) months after the Closing Date.

Covenants that by their terms are to be performed after the Closing shall survive in accordance with their terms. The Environmental Indemnity under Section 9.2(c) shall survive until the fifth (5th) anniversary of the Closing Date.

**9.2 Indemnification by Seller.** From and after the Closing, Seller shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Buyer, its Affiliates (including, after the Closing, the Company) and their respective officers, directors and employees (the "**Buyer Indemnitees**") from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Seller in Article IV;

(b) any breach of any covenant or agreement of Seller in this Agreement;

(c) any liability under Environmental Laws arising out of facts, events or conditions occurring or existing on or before the Closing Date in connection with the Company or any vessel under the Company's management. This includes any oil pollution, MARPOL or ballast-water discharge liability and any related removal costs, natural resource damages, fines or penalties (the "**Environmental Indemnity**"); and

(d) any Taxes of the Company for any Pre-Closing Tax Period, provided that claims under this Section 9.2(d) are subject to the survival period applicable to the Tax Representations under Section 9.1.

**9.3 Indemnification by Buyer.** From and after the Closing, Buyer shall indemnify Seller and its Affiliates from and against all Losses arising out of:

(a) any inaccuracy in or breach of any representation or warranty of Buyer in Article V; or

(b) any breach of any covenant of Buyer in this Agreement.

**9.4 Claims Procedure; Time Limit.** A party seeking indemnification shall give written notice to the indemnifying party describing the claim in reasonable detail. No claim for indemnification under this Article IX may be made unless notice of the claim is delivered before the expiration of the applicable survival period under Section 9.1. Any claim timely noticed shall survive until finally resolved. The indemnifying party may assume the defense of any third-party claim with counsel reasonably acceptable to the indemnified party.

**9.5 Limitations.**

(a) *Cap.* The aggregate liability of Seller under Section 9.2(a) shall not exceed an amount equal to the Escrow Amount. Seller shall not be liable under Section 9.2(a) unless and until aggregate Losses exceed $320,000, after which Seller shall be liable for all Losses from the first dollar.

(b) *Exclusions.* The limitations in Section 9.5(a) shall not apply to Losses arising from Fundamental Representations, Fraud, and the Environmental Indemnity under Section 9.2(c).

(c) *Buyer Cap.* The aggregate liability of Buyer under Section 9.3(a) shall not exceed an amount equal to the Escrow Amount.

**9.6 Source of Recovery.** Any Losses payable to a Buyer Indemnitee under this Article IX shall be satisfied:

(a) first, from the Escrow Fund, in accordance with the Escrow Agreement; and

(b) thereafter, with respect to Losses that are not subject to the cap in Section 9.5(a), directly from Seller.

The Escrow Fund shall be released in accordance with the Escrow Agreement eighteen (18) months after the Closing Date, less the amount of any pending claims.

**9.7 Exclusive Remedy.** Except for claims for Fraud, claims for specific performance or injunctive relief, and the post-closing adjustment under Section 2.4, the indemnification provisions of this Article IX are the exclusive remedy of the parties for any breach of this Agreement.

**9.8 Tax Treatment.** All indemnification payments shall be treated as adjustments to the Purchase Price for Tax purposes unless otherwise required by Law.

## ARTICLE X. TERMINATION

**10.1 Termination.** This Agreement may be terminated before the Closing:

(a) by mutual written consent of Buyer and Seller;

(b) by either party if the Closing has not occurred by February 28, 2026, provided the terminating party's breach is not the cause of the delay; or

(c) by either party upon a material breach by the other that is not cured within fifteen (15) days after written notice.

**10.2 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, this Agreement shall become void, except that Section 6.4 and Article XI shall survive. No termination shall relieve a party of liability for willful breach.

**11.1 Expenses.** Except as otherwise provided herein, each party shall bear its own costs and expenses incurred in connection with this Agreement.

**11.2 Notices.** All notices hereunder shall be in writing and deemed given when delivered personally, by internationally recognized courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission. Notices shall be sent to the addresses set forth on the signature pages, or such other address as a party designates by notice.

**11.3 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of the Laws of another jurisdiction.

**11.4 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, for any action arising out of this Agreement. EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**11.5 Assignment.** Buyer may not assign this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations hereunder without the prior written consent of Seller. This Agreement shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**11.6 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer and Seller. No waiver shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the party against whom it is asserted.

**11.7 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

**11.8 Order of Precedence.** In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and any Ancillary Agreement, this Agreement shall prevail, except that the Escrow Agreement shall govern with respect to recovery from the Escrow Fund and the Transition Services Agreement shall govern with respect to the Services (as defined therein).

**11.9 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the Buyer Indemnitees under Article IX, nothing in this Agreement confers any rights or remedies on any Person other than the parties.

**11.10 Specific Performance.** The parties agree that irreparable damage would occur if any provision of this Agreement were not performed in accordance with its terms. Each party shall be entitled to specific performance in addition to any other remedy.

**11.11 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together constitute one instrument.
