# ESCROW AGREEMENT

This ESCROW AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 15, 2026, by and among **Gliluv Maritime Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Buyer**"), **Dreisend Shipping Group, Ltd.**, a Bermuda exempted company ("**Seller**"), and **Pelham Trust Company, N.A.**, a national banking association, as escrow agent (the "**Escrow Agent**").

## RECITALS

A. Buyer and Seller are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of December 1, 2025 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Buyer is acquiring from Seller all of the issued and outstanding shares of **Saltreach Marine Services, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), at a closing held on January 15, 2026 (the "**Closing Date**").

B. Section 2.2(a) of the Purchase Agreement requires Buyer to deposit $6,400,000 (the "**Escrow Amount**") with the Escrow Agent at the Closing, to be held and disbursed under this Agreement.

C. This Agreement is the Escrow Agreement referred to in the Purchase Agreement and is being delivered at the Closing under Sections 3.2(b) and 3.3 of the Purchase Agreement. The Escrow Agent is willing to act as escrow agent on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND APPOINTMENT

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Claim Notice**" has the meaning given in Section 4.1.

"**Escrow Account**" means the segregated account established by the Escrow Agent under Section 2.1.

"**Escrow Fund**" means the Escrow Amount deposited with the Escrow Agent, together with all interest and earnings thereon, less any disbursements made under this Agreement.

"**Final Order**" means a final, non-appealable order or judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction under Section 10.2, accompanied by a certificate of the presenting party's counsel to the effect that the order or judgment is final and non-appealable.

"**Joint Written Instructions**" means written instructions signed by an authorized representative of each of Buyer and Seller and delivered to the Escrow Agent.

"**Pending Claim Amount**" means, as of any date, the aggregate amount of all claims set forth in Claim Notices delivered on or before that date that have not been finally resolved and paid under Article 4.

"**Release Date**" has the meaning given in Section 3.1.

**1.2 Appointment.** Buyer and Seller appoint the Escrow Agent as escrow agent under this Agreement. The Escrow Agent accepts the appointment and agrees to hold, invest and disburse the Escrow Fund in accordance with this Agreement.

## 2. ESCROW DEPOSIT AND INVESTMENT

**2.1 Deposit.** On the Closing Date, Buyer shall deposit the Escrow Amount with the Escrow Agent by wire transfer of immediately available funds. The Escrow Agent shall hold the Escrow Fund in a segregated account in its name, as escrow agent for the benefit of Buyer and Seller. The Escrow Agent shall confirm receipt of the Escrow Amount to Buyer and Seller in writing on the Closing Date.

**2.2 Investment.** The Escrow Agent shall invest the Escrow Fund in the money market deposit account or U.S. Treasury money market fund identified in Schedule 1, or in such other investment as Buyer and Seller may direct by Joint Written Instructions. The Escrow Agent is not liable for any loss of principal or income resulting from any investment made in accordance with this Section 2.2 or from the liquidation of any investment needed to make a disbursement.

**2.3 Earnings.** All interest and earnings on the Escrow Fund shall become part of the Escrow Fund and be disbursed with it under this Agreement.

**2.4 No Other Rights.** The Escrow Fund shall not be subject to any lien, security interest or attachment in favor of the Escrow Agent or any creditor of any party. Neither Buyer nor Seller may sell, pledge or otherwise transfer any interest in the Escrow Fund before its disbursement under this Agreement.

## 3. RELEASE OF ESCROW FUND

**3.1 Release Date.** On the date that is 18 months after the Closing Date (the "**Release Date**"), the Escrow Agent shall disburse to Seller the then-remaining balance of the Escrow Fund, less the Pending Claim Amount as of the Release Date. Buyer and Seller shall deliver Joint Written Instructions to the Escrow Agent not later than five (5) Business Days before the Release Date confirming the Pending Claim Amount. If they do not, the Escrow Agent shall calculate the Pending Claim Amount on the basis of the Claim Notices and Objection Notices actually received by it.

**3.2 Retained Amounts.** The Escrow Agent shall continue to hold any amount retained in respect of a Pending Claim Amount after the Release Date. As each claim is resolved under Article 4, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the amount payable to Buyer, if any, and then disburse to Seller the amount retained for that claim in excess of the amount payable to Buyer.

**3.3 Joint Written Instructions; Final Orders.** The Escrow Agent shall also disburse all or any portion of the Escrow Fund at any time in accordance with Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order.

**3.4 Method of Disbursement.** All disbursements shall be made by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account of the recipient set forth in Schedule 2, or to any other account designated by that party by written notice to the Escrow Agent that has been confirmed under Section 7.6.

## 4. CLAIMS PROCEDURE

**4.1 Claim Notices.** At any time before the Release Date, Buyer may deliver to Seller and the Escrow Agent a written notice (a "**Claim Notice**") stating that a Buyer Indemnitee has incurred, or reasonably expects to incur, Losses for which it is entitled to indemnification under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement. Each Claim Notice shall describe the claim in reasonable detail, state the amount claimed (or a good-faith estimate) and identify the provision of the Purchase Agreement on which the claim is based. A Claim Notice delivered under this Section 4.1 also constitutes notice of the claim for purposes of Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement.

**4.2 Objection.** Seller may object to all or any part of a claim by delivering a written objection (an "**Objection Notice**") to Buyer and the Escrow Agent within thirty (30) days after Seller's receipt of the Claim Notice. The Objection Notice shall state the amount disputed and the basis of the objection in reasonable detail.

**4.3 Undisputed Claims.** If the Escrow Agent does not receive an Objection Notice within the thirty (30) day period in Section 4.2, the Escrow Agent shall, on the fifth (5th) Business Day after the end of that period, disburse to Buyer from the Escrow Fund the amount claimed in the Claim Notice. If an Objection Notice disputes only part of a claim, the Escrow Agent shall disburse the undisputed part in the same manner.

**4.4 Disputed Claims.** If the Escrow Agent timely receives an Objection Notice, it shall continue to hold the disputed amount until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. It shall then disburse that amount in accordance with the Joint Written Instructions or Final Order. Buyer and Seller shall negotiate in good faith for thirty (30) days after delivery of the Objection Notice to resolve the disputed claim. Either of them may then pursue resolution under Section 10.2.

**4.5 Estimated Claims.** If a Claim Notice states an estimated amount, Buyer may deliver an updated Claim Notice as Losses are incurred. The updated Claim Notice is subject to Sections 4.2 through 4.4 only as to any increase in the amount claimed.

## 5. INDEMNIFICATION CLAIMS AGAINST THE ESCROW FUND

**5.1 Purpose of Escrow Fund.** The Escrow Fund secures the obligations of Seller to the Buyer Indemnitees under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement.

**5.2 Order of Recovery.** Amounts payable to a Buyer Indemnitee in respect of a claim resolved under Article 4 shall be paid from the Escrow Fund to the extent of the balance then available, without any requirement that Buyer first seek recovery from Seller or any other Person.

**5.3 Recovery Limited to Escrow Fund.** The Escrow Fund shall be the sole and exclusive source of recovery for all claims for indemnification by the Buyer Indemnitees under Article IX of the Purchase Agreement, including claims under Section 9.2(c) of the Purchase Agreement. Once the Escrow Fund has been fully disbursed under this Agreement, no Buyer Indemnitee shall have any further recourse against Seller or its Affiliates in respect of any such claim.

**5.4 Insufficient Balance.** If the balance of the Escrow Fund is not sufficient to pay all claims that become payable at the same time, the Escrow Agent shall pay them in the order in which the related Claim Notices were received.

## 6. THE ESCROW AGENT

**6.1 Duties.** The Escrow Agent shall have only the duties expressly set forth in this Agreement, which are ministerial in nature. No implied duties or obligations shall be read into this Agreement against the Escrow Agent. The Escrow Agent is not a party to, and is not bound by or charged with notice of, the Purchase Agreement or any other agreement between Buyer and Seller, except for references to it that are needed to construe this Agreement.

**6.2 Reliance.** The Escrow Agent may rely on any written notice, instruction or signature it believes in good faith to be genuine and to have been signed or presented by the proper party. It need not inquire into the accuracy of any statement in any such document.

**6.3 Limitation of Liability.** The Escrow Agent shall not be liable for any action taken or omitted under this Agreement, except to the extent caused by its own gross negligence or willful misconduct as finally determined by a court of competent jurisdiction. In no event shall the Escrow Agent be liable for indirect, special, consequential or punitive damages.

**6.4 Counsel.** The Escrow Agent may consult counsel of its choice and shall be fully protected in acting in good faith on the advice of that counsel.

**6.5 Conflicting Instructions.** If the Escrow Agent receives conflicting instructions or is uncertain about its duties, it may decline to act until it receives Joint Written Instructions or a Final Order. It may also deposit the Escrow Fund with a court under Section 10.2 by way of interpleader.

**6.6 Resignation and Removal.** The Escrow Agent may resign by giving at least thirty (30) days' written notice to Buyer and Seller. Buyer and Seller may jointly remove the Escrow Agent on thirty (30) days' written notice. The resignation or removal takes effect when a successor escrow agent appointed by Joint Written Instructions accepts the appointment in writing and receives the Escrow Fund. If no successor has been appointed within thirty (30) days after the notice, the Escrow Agent may petition a court under Section 10.2 to appoint one.

**6.7 Indemnification of Escrow Agent.** Buyer and Seller shall, jointly and severally, indemnify the Escrow Agent against all losses, claims, costs and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of its performance of this Agreement, except to the extent caused by its gross negligence or willful misconduct. As between Buyer and Seller, each shall bear one-half of any amount payable under this Section 6.7, unless a court determines that the amount arose from the conduct of only one of them. This Section 6.7 survives the termination of this Agreement and the resignation or removal of the Escrow Agent.

**6.8 Fees.** The Escrow Agent's fees are set forth in Schedule 3. Buyer and Seller shall each pay one-half of those fees and of the Escrow Agent's reasonable, documented out-of-pocket expenses.

## 7. TAX MATTERS; ADMINISTRATION

**7.1 Tax Owner.** Buyer and Seller agree that Buyer shall be treated as the owner of the Escrow Fund for income tax purposes until it is disbursed, and that all interest and earnings shall be reported as income of Buyer for each year in which they are earned.

**7.2 Tax Forms.** Before the Closing Date, Buyer and Seller shall each deliver to the Escrow Agent a properly completed IRS Form W-9 or applicable Form W-8. The Escrow Agent shall file all information returns required of it with respect to the Escrow Fund.

**7.3 Tax Distributions.** Within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar quarter, the Escrow Agent shall, on Buyer's written request, distribute to Buyer from the earnings on the Escrow Fund an amount equal to forty percent (40%) of the earnings allocated to Buyer for that quarter. No tax distribution may be made from principal.

**7.4 Statements.** The Escrow Agent shall provide Buyer and Seller with monthly statements of the Escrow Account showing the balance, all investments, earnings and disbursements.

**7.5 Know-Your-Customer.** Buyer and Seller shall provide the Escrow Agent with all information reasonably requested by it to comply with applicable anti-money-laundering, sanctions and customer identification Laws.

**7.6 Security Procedures.** The Escrow Agent shall confirm any wire instruction, and any change to the account details in Schedule 2, by telephone call-back to an authorized representative listed in Schedule 4, other than the person who sent the instruction. The Escrow Agent may delay a disbursement until it has completed that confirmation.

## 8. TERMINATION

**8.1 Termination.** This Agreement terminates when the Escrow Agent has disbursed the entire Escrow Fund under this Agreement. Sections 6.3, 6.7, 6.8 and Article 10 survive termination.

## 9. NOTICES

**9.1 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing. They are deemed given when delivered personally, by internationally recognized courier, or by email with confirmation of transmission, to the following addresses (or such other address as a party designates by notice):

(a) if to Buyer or Seller, to the address for that party set forth in, or designated under, Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement; and

(b) if to the Escrow Agent: Pelham Trust Company, N.A., Corporate Trust and Escrow Services, Attention: Escrow Administration (Saltreach / Gliluv), with a copy by email to the address set forth in Schedule 4.

**9.2 Effectiveness against Escrow Agent.** Notices to the Escrow Agent are effective only upon actual receipt.

## 10. GOVERNING LAW AND MISCELLANEOUS

**10.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of the Laws of another jurisdiction.

**10.2 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in the Borough of Manhattan, New York, New York, for any action arising out of this Agreement. EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO TRIAL BY JURY IN ANY SUCH ACTION.

**10.3 Assignment.** No party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other parties, except that any entity into which the Escrow Agent is merged or converted, or which acquires all or substantially all of its corporate trust business, becomes the successor Escrow Agent without further act.

**10.4 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by Buyer, Seller and the Escrow Agent. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the party against whom it is asserted.

**10.5 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**10.6 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Nothing in this Agreement confers any rights or remedies on any Person other than the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**10.7 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
