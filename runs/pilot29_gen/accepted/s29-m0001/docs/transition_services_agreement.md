# TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT

This TRANSITION SERVICES AGREEMENT (this "**Agreement**") is entered into as of January 15, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**"), by and between **Dreisend Shipping Group, Ltd.**, a Bermuda exempted company ("**Provider**"), and **Gliluv Maritime Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Recipient**").

## RECITALS

A. Provider, as Seller, and Recipient, as Buyer, are parties to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated as of December 1, 2025 (the "**Purchase Agreement**"). Under the Purchase Agreement, Recipient is acquiring all of the issued and outstanding shares of **Saltreach Marine Services, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "**Company**"), at a closing held on January 15, 2026.

B. Before the Closing, the Company relied on Provider and its Affiliates for certain crew management, vessel-performance data, and enterprise resource planning and chartering-system services. Recipient wishes Provider to continue to provide those services to Recipient and the Company for a transitional period after the Closing, and Provider is willing to do so on the terms of this Agreement.

C. This Agreement is the Transition Services Agreement referred to in the Purchase Agreement and is being delivered at the Closing under Sections 3.2(b) and 3.3 of the Purchase Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein and in the Purchase Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

## 1. DEFINITIONS

**1.1 Defined Terms.** Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the Purchase Agreement. In addition:

"**Company Data**" means all data and records relating to the Company, its business, or any vessel under the Company's management, whether created before or after the Effective Date. This includes crew records, seafarer certificates and payroll data, charterer and owner information, voyage and fixture records, and vessel-performance, noon-report and fuel-consumption data.

"**Recipient Group**" means Recipient, the Company and their respective Affiliates.

"**Provider Personnel**" means employees, agents and subcontractors of Provider or its Affiliates engaged in providing the Services.

"**Service Fees**" means the fees for the Services set forth in Schedule A.

"**Service Period**" means, for each Service, the period beginning on the Effective Date and ending on the date set forth for that Service in Schedule A, unless extended or terminated earlier under Article 7.

"**Services**" means the services described in Schedule A, together with any Additional Services agreed under Section 2.4.

"**Systems**" means the ERP, chartering, crew-management and vessel-performance software platforms, databases and related infrastructure owned, licensed or operated by Provider or its Affiliates and used to provide the Services.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Section 1.2 of the Purchase Agreement applies to this Agreement, mutatis mutandis.

## 2. SERVICES

**2.1 Provision of Services.** During the applicable Service Period, Provider shall provide, or cause its Affiliates to provide, the Services to Recipient and, at Recipient's direction, to the Company and other members of the Recipient Group.

**2.2 Standard of Performance.** Provider shall perform the Services:

(a) in a professional and workmanlike manner;

(b) with substantially the same scope, priority, degree of care and level of service as were provided to the Company during the twelve (12) months before the Effective Date; and

(c) in compliance with applicable Law, including the ISM Code, the ISPS Code and the Maritime Labour Convention to the extent applicable to the Services.

**2.3 Personnel.** Provider shall determine which Provider Personnel perform the Services. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to keep continuity in the key personnel identified in Schedule A and shall give Recipient reasonable prior notice of any change in those personnel.

**2.4 Additional Services.** If Recipient reasonably identifies any service that Provider or its Affiliates provided to the Company before the Effective Date and that is not described in Schedule A, Recipient may request it in writing. Provider shall negotiate in good faith to provide that service (an "**Additional Service**") on terms consistent with this Agreement, with fees determined on the same cost basis as the Service Fees. Each Additional Service shall be documented in a written supplement to Schedule A signed by both parties.

**2.5 Subcontractors.** Provider may use subcontractors to perform any Service that was performed by subcontractors before the Effective Date. Provider remains responsible for the performance of its subcontractors as if the work were performed by Provider.

**2.6 Service Managers.** Each party shall designate a service manager in Schedule A to act as its primary contact for the day-to-day administration of this Agreement. Either party may replace its service manager on written notice.

## 3. COOPERATION AND MIGRATION

**3.1 Cooperation.** Recipient shall provide Provider with information and access to Recipient Group personnel reasonably necessary for Provider to perform the Services.

**3.2 Migration Plan.** Within forty-five (45) days after the Effective Date, the parties shall agree a written plan (the "**Migration Plan**") for transferring each Service, and the related Company Data, to Recipient or its designee by the end of the applicable Service Period.

**3.3 Migration Assistance.** Provider shall provide reasonable assistance in carrying out the Migration Plan. This includes extracting Company Data from the Systems in a commercially standard, machine-readable format, mapping data fields, and answering reasonable questions from Recipient or its designated replacement provider. Migration assistance shall be charged at the hourly rates set forth in Schedule A.

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

**4.1 Service Fees.** Recipient shall pay Provider the Service Fees for each Service for each calendar month (or portion thereof, prorated on a daily basis) during the applicable Service Period.

**4.2 Pass-Through Costs.** Recipient shall reimburse Provider for reasonable, documented out-of-pocket third-party costs incurred at Recipient's written request, without markup. These include crew travel, visa and agency charges and third-party license fees identified in Schedule A.

**4.3 Invoicing and Payment.** Provider shall invoice Recipient monthly in arrears, with reasonable supporting detail. Recipient shall pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days after receipt by wire transfer to an account designated by Provider.

**4.4 Disputed Amounts.** Recipient may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith by giving Provider written notice of the disputed amount and the basis for the dispute before the payment due date. The parties' service managers shall try in good faith to resolve the dispute within fifteen (15) Business Days. Any unresolved dispute may then be pursued under Section 12.2.

**4.5 Taxes.** The Service Fees exclude sales, use, value-added and similar Taxes, which Recipient shall bear where applicable. Each party is responsible for Taxes on its own income.

## 5. SYSTEMS ACCESS AND DATA

**5.1 Access to Systems.** Provider shall give designated Recipient Group personnel access to the Systems to the extent necessary to receive and use the Services. Recipient shall cause those personnel to comply with Provider's reasonable written security and acceptable-use policies provided to Recipient in advance.

**5.2 Ownership of Company Data.** As between the parties, the Company Data is owned by Recipient Group. Provider shall use Company Data only to perform the Services and for no other purpose.

**5.3 Data Segregation.** Provider shall keep Company Data logically segregated from the data of Provider and its other customers within the Systems. Provider shall not permit access to Company Data by any Provider Personnel who are not engaged in the Services.

**5.4 Security.** Provider shall maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards for the Systems and Company Data that are no less protective than those in place before the Effective Date and consistent with industry practice for ship management companies. Provider shall notify Recipient without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to or loss of Company Data.

**5.5 Return of Data.** At the end of each Service Period, Provider shall deliver all Company Data relating to that Service to Recipient as provided in the Migration Plan. Within thirty (30) days after Recipient confirms receipt, Provider shall delete all remaining copies of that Company Data from the Systems, except backup copies retained in the ordinary course, which remain subject to Article 6.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

**6.1 Obligations.** Each party shall hold in confidence all non-public information of the other party, and of the Company, received in connection with this Agreement ("**Confidential Information**"). Each party shall use Confidential Information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement.

**6.2 Exceptions.** Confidential Information does not include information that:

(a) is or becomes publicly available other than through breach of this Agreement;

(b) was lawfully known to the receiving party before disclosure without restriction; or

(c) is independently developed by the receiving party without use of the Confidential Information.

**6.3 Compelled Disclosure.** A party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by Law or by a Governmental Authority. Where legally permitted, it shall give the other party prompt notice and cooperate with any effort to obtain protective treatment.

**6.4 Relationship to Purchase Agreement.** This Article 6 is in addition to, and does not limit, Section 6.4 of the Purchase Agreement.

## 7. TERM AND TERMINATION

**7.1 Term.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until the expiration or termination of the last Service Period.

**7.2 Extension.** Recipient may extend the Service Period for any Service once, by up to three (3) months, by written notice to Provider at least thirty (30) days before the scheduled end of that Service Period. During any extension, the Service Fees for that Service will increase by ten percent (10%).

**7.3 Early Termination by Recipient.** Recipient may terminate any Service, in whole or in part, for convenience on thirty (30) days' written notice to Provider. Schedule A identifies any Service that cannot be terminated without also terminating a dependent Service.

**7.4 Termination for Breach.** Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice describing it. Provider may terminate a Service for non-payment only if an undisputed amount for that Service remains unpaid thirty (30) days after Recipient receives written notice of non-payment.

**7.5 Effect of Termination.** Upon expiration or termination of any Service, Recipient shall pay all Service Fees accrued through the effective date of termination. Articles 5, 6, 8, 11 and 12, Sections 3.3 and 7.5, and any accrued payment obligations shall survive.

## 8. WARRANTIES; INDEMNIFICATION; LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**8.1 Disclaimer.** Except as expressly set forth in this Agreement, Provider makes no representation or warranty with respect to the Services, express or implied, including any warranty of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.

**8.2 Indemnification by Provider.** Provider shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Recipient Group and their respective officers, directors and employees from and against all Losses arising out of third-party claims to the extent caused by:

(a) the gross negligence or willful misconduct of Provider or any Provider Personnel in performing the Services; or

(b) any breach by Provider of Article 5 or Article 6.

**8.3 Indemnification by Recipient.** Recipient shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Provider, its Affiliates and their respective officers, directors, employees and Provider Personnel from and against all Losses arising out of or relating to the Services or the use of the Services by Recipient Group. This includes Losses arising from the negligence of Provider or any Provider Personnel, except to the extent those Losses are caused by the gross negligence or willful misconduct of Provider. The obligations of Recipient under this Section 8.3 shall not be subject to Section 8.4 or any other limitation.

**8.4 Limitation of Provider Liability.** The aggregate liability of Provider and its Affiliates arising out of or relating to this Agreement or the Services, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, shall not exceed the total Service Fees actually paid by Recipient under this Agreement.

**8.5 Exclusion of Certain Damages.** Neither party shall be liable to the other for any indirect, consequential, special or punitive damages, or for lost profits, arising out of this Agreement. This Section 8.5 does not apply to amounts payable to third parties under Section 8.2 or Section 8.3, or to a breach of Article 6.

**8.6 Indemnification Procedure.** The procedures in Section 9.4 of the Purchase Agreement for the notice and defense of third-party claims apply to claims under Sections 8.2 and 8.3, mutatis mutandis.

## 9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**9.1 Provider Property.** Provider and its Affiliates retain all right, title and interest in and to the Systems and any other materials they own or license and use to provide the Services. Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of the Systems to Recipient.

**9.2 License.** Provider grants Recipient Group a non-exclusive, non-transferable, royalty-free license during each Service Period to use the Systems and related materials solely to receive and use the applicable Service.

**9.3 Third-Party Consents.** Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain any third-party consents needed to provide the Services. Recipient shall reimburse reasonable, documented consent fees approved by Recipient in advance. If a required consent cannot be obtained, the parties shall cooperate in good faith to implement a reasonable alternative arrangement.

## 10. FORCE MAJEURE

**10.1 Excused Performance.** Neither party is liable for any failure or delay in performance (other than payment obligations) to the extent caused by events beyond its reasonable control. These include acts of God, war, piracy, terrorism, epidemic, governmental action, and failure of public utilities or communications networks (each, a "**Force Majeure Event**").

**10.2 Notice and Mitigation.** The affected party shall promptly notify the other party of the Force Majeure Event and use commercially reasonable efforts to resume performance. Service Fees for any Service not provided because of a Force Majeure Event shall be abated for the period of non-performance.

## 11. RELATIONSHIP OF THE PARTIES

**11.1 Independent Contractor.** Provider is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or agency relationship between the parties.

**11.2 No Fiduciary Duty.** Provider does not act as a fiduciary of Recipient Group in performing the Services.

## 12. MISCELLANEOUS

**12.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the Laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles that would require the application of the Laws of another jurisdiction.

**12.2 Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.** Section 11.4 of the Purchase Agreement applies to any action arising out of this Agreement, mutatis mutandis.

**12.3 Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be given in the manner and to the addresses provided in Section 11.2 of the Purchase Agreement. Copies of operational notices shall also be sent to each party's service manager.

**12.4 Assignment.** Assignment of this Agreement is governed by Section 11.5 of the Purchase Agreement, mutatis mutandis, so that Buyer may not assign this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations hereunder without the prior written consent of Seller. For purposes of this Section 12.4, references in Section 11.5 of the Purchase Agreement to Buyer and Seller are to Recipient and Provider, respectively. This Agreement is binding upon and inures to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

**12.5 Amendment; Waiver.** This Agreement, including Schedule A, may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless it is in writing and signed by the party against whom it is asserted.

**12.6 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force and effect.

**12.7 No Third-Party Beneficiaries.** Except for the indemnified persons under Sections 8.2 and 8.3 and the members of Recipient Group receiving Services under Section 2.1, nothing in this Agreement confers any rights or remedies on any Person other than the parties.

**12.8 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature. Each counterpart is deemed an original, and all of them together constitute one instrument.
